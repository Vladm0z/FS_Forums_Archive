If you want to do all the three secret bosses in a single run then you need to do the areas in the following order after bringing the first three barrels to the central room.

  1. Water Wheel

    • Take 2 barrels from the central room into the water wheel area
    • Cut the chain underneath to lower the first drawbridge
    • Aim carefully and throw one barrel down to release the second drawbridge
    • Blow up the nurgle wall with the other barrel

  2. Cog Replacement

    • Take the 1 remaining barrel from the central room into the cog replacement area
    • Leave it at the top of this new area, go down and clear everything out so it is safe
    • Do not activate the levers until after you have completed this secret
    • Grab the key from the dead dwarf at the very bottom (needed for the next area)
    • Grab the second barrel from a small alcove along the lower level wall
    • Grab the other barrel left at top of area, and blow up the nurgle walls

  3. Water Flow

    • Stick to the right through the cave so you reach the upper level via a slope
    • You will eventually reach an alcove part with a purple flag, the barrel is inside
    • Take it over the bridge ahead, be careful as a gunner usually spawns on the other side
    • The second barrel is right there behind a door next to lever, use the key to open it
    • Blow up the two nurgle walls across this bridge and complete the last secret

That is pretty much it. I think you have to complete the level to get the rewards though. Don’t forget you can skip the trolls in the central area by activating the lift.

You can do the rooms in any order you like. Each section has a thing you can do that will unlock a barrel in the others. So you need 2 barrels for the first section, whichever section you choose, since nothing is open yet. After the first section, you bring one barrel to the second one since one barrel will be available there. Once you finish the second, the third has two barrels available inside. The things that unlock the barrels are:

  1. opening the dam – this makes it possible to pull elevator levers on the two other sections, which will bring up a barrel.
  2. picking up the key on the dead dwarf at the bottom of the cog section – this makes it possible to unlock all locked doors, which contain a barrel (and sometimes pickups or shortcuts)
  3. moving the water wheel – this breaks the plank barricades, revealing barrels (and sometimes shortcuts).
