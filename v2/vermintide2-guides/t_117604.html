An explanation of the macro game for people moving up to Cata.

Best way to learn Cata?

Play True Duos on VOIP - Two players in a Private Match (hold Tab and click the Private option), and let the Bots die if possible.

What it teaches:

  • Learn to Stack with each other, minimizing damage taken and increasing control of the Horde
  • Learn Positioning
  • Learn priorities - e.g. Special killer kills Specials, while Tank CCs the Horde
  • Learn which Careers have good Boss damage

Gameplay:

  • Hold Block! You should Block as much as possible to avoid getting hit in the back constantly
  • As a beginner always start your combo with a Push for control of the Horde - Push → Light → Light → Repeat or Push → Heavy → Repeat
  • Look for enemies constantly, so you don’t get surrounded

Positioning:

  • Position with your back to a wall, so you don’t get surrounded
  • When a Horde sound starts, decide where you’re going to hold it
  • Defend your Special Killer - if you’re playing solo, they can’t kill Specials quickly, which means Specials will do more damage
  • Stay with the team, so you have more control of the Horde - STOP RUNNING OFF SOLO
  • Hold choke points
  • Let enemies come to you! If moving to them causes you to be surrounded, it’s not worth it
  • Avoid Drop Spots (enemies dropping from the ceiling or a hole in the wall)

Temporary Health (white Health):

This is one of the most important things about Cataclysm, and makes games run much more smoothly.

image566×266 45.1 KB

Firstly; Constantly check your teams health to see if they’re above around 60-70%. If they’re below it, you need to help them get more, by Pushing enemies to make them safe while they generate it.

If they’re below that health:

  • Necklace Trait: Boon of Shallya increases THP generation (this and Barkskin are the two best Neck Traits for most Careers)
  • STOP SPAM SHOOTING EVERYTHING - people cannot generate THP if all of the enemies are dead - If you are low HP you are killing yourself by spam shooting, when you should be generating THP
  • Have a THP Support in your team for safer matches - Sister of The Thorn, Merc Kruber, Unchained

Bosses:

  • Don’t all tunnel (hyper focus) the Boss - Horde can be more important
  • One person should damage the Boss and hold it’s aggro, while the other players kill the Horde and Specials
  • If you have low Boss damage, you might ignore it completely
  • Keep small enemies away from the person holding the Boss
  • Stagger the Boss if the person with aggro gets stuck
  • You can ignore Flame Rat Ogres until the end of the Horde - just avoid them

Patrols:

  • Avoid them if you’re low HP or there’s a lot happening
  • Use Potions and Bombs to clear them - AIM SLIGHTLY HIGHER SO YOU DO NOT BOMB YOUR TEAMMATES
  • Know your role during the fight - Shield users should be Push → Slamming to control them, etc

In-Match Items:
Nobody does book runs in Cataclysm, the fastest way to get Red Items is to do Legend Book runs or Cata Chaos Wastes with premades.

  • If you have been downed, healing SOMEONE ELSE with Bandages will clear your Wounds, meaning you won’t die if you reach 0 HP - this is a good way to use one heal to clear two Wounds - Also recommended if you know you can generate lots of THP
  • Carry Health Pots as you no longer carry Tomes (Zealot, SoTT and Ranger Vet can carry Bandages)
  • If someone HAS to carry the Bandage, give it to someone with an AoE Stagger or Stealth, so they can Heal while using their ability to Stun/Stealth
  • If everyone is low HP just use heals to prevent a wipe
  • Generally try to give Bombs to the Ranged Careers, unless someone can get a lot of Power, like Ironbreaker with certain builds)
