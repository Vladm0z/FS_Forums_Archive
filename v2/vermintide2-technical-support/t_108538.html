Update:

KNOWN ISSUE: Weekly Event Can Crash After Picking Up SkullsTechnical Support
While we work on a permanent fix between the ‘Send in the next wave!’ modifier and the event Skulls, we have swapped the weekly modifier to ‘More specials’ to prevent crashing.

We are aware of and investigating a crash that can occur while playing the current Chaos Wastes weekly modifiers, and picking up Skulls (from the Skulls event).

The error the crash reporter will show for this crash will appear as:
[Script Error]: foundation/scripts/util/error.lua:26: Can't initialize an already initialized mutator (hordes_galore)

We’re working to resolve this issue as swiftly as possible. Apologies for the inconvenience in the meantime.
