  • vermintide2.exe in \Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Warhammer Vermintide 2\binaries
  • vermintide2_dx12.exe in \Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Warhammer Vermintide 2\binaries_dx12
  • EasyAntiCheat.exe in \Program Files (x86)\EasyAntiCheat

Hm, in which case can they try this next:

  1. Log out of their Steam account in the Steam client
  2. Exit Steam completely (End all Steam processes within Task Manager)
  3. Right-click Steam shortcut and choose ‘Run as administrator’
  4. Log back in to Steam account
  5. Try launching Vermintide 2 as normal

Failing this, as this issue seems to be a Steam-related and Easy Anti-Cheat-related mix, it could also be worth checking that a background application isn’t being flagged incorrectly by EAC. Your friend can try a ‘Selective Startup’ which can help to determine this:

  1. Press the Windows key
  2. Type ‘msconfig’ and select ‘System Configuration’
  3. On the ‘General’ tab, select ‘Selective Startup’
  4. Deselect ‘Load startup Items’
  5. Reboot your PC
  6. Try launching Vermintide 2 as normal

If the error no longer occurs after having performed the Selective Startup, try re-introducing programs one-by-one to determine which is the culprit.
