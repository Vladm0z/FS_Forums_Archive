Sounds like this is a result of the Friendly Fire Indicator mod. The mod author is aware of these issues and working on a solution, but in the mean time you’ll need to unsubscribe from the mod in the Steam Workshop:

  1. Navigate to the Vermintide 2 Workshop page in Steam
  2. Hover over the ‘Browse’ dropdown
  3. Select ‘Subscribed items’ and locate the Friendly Fire Indicator mod
  4. Unsubscribe
