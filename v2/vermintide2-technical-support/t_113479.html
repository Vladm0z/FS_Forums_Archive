@Lomary thanks for clarifying! DLC weapons must be unlocked for each character by specific ways:

  • Back to Ubersreik DLC weapons can be unlocked by completing the three DLC levels with each character to unlock their specific weapon blueprint
  • Winds of Magic DLC weapons can be unlocked by completing Dark Omens with each character to unlock their specific weapon blueprint

The blueprints can be acquired by claiming the associated Okri’s Challenge when completed. Hope this helps!
