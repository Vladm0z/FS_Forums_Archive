Sorry to hear that @Gatts

Perhaps an external application e.g. overlay, Anti-Virus etc. could be hooking in.

I recommend ensuring you have appropriate Anti-Virus and Firewall exceptions in place:

  • vermintide2.exe in \Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Warhammer Vermintide 2\binaries

  • vermintide2_dx12.exe in \Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Warhammer Vermintide 2\binaries_dx12

  • EasyAntiCheat_EOS.exe in C:\Program Files (x86)\EasyAntiCheat_EOS

Also, performing a ‘Selective Startup’ to disable startup background applications may help:

  1. Press the Windows key
  2. Type ‘msconfig’ and select ‘System Configuration’
  3. On the ‘General’ tab, select ‘Selective Startup’
  4. Deselect ‘Load startup Items’
  5. Reboot your PC

Lastly, disabling the Steam overlay could be good to try. The Steam Overlay can be disabled by unticking the ‘Enable the Steam Overlay while in-game’ option in the Steam ‘Settings’ menu.

Please let me know how things go!
