We’re aware some players have experienced a substantial drop in performance with the release of the Warrior Priest career. We understand some of these cases are due to the Steam Overlay, and/or mods. Please:

Disable the Steam Overlay

  1. Open the Steam client
  2. Select ‘Steam’ in the upper left-hand corner
  3. Select ‘Settings’
  4. Select ‘In-Game’
  5. Deselect the ‘Enable the Steam overlay while in-game’ checkbox
  6. Save your changes
  7. Launch Vermintide 2 as normal, and see if the issue persists

Disable any mods

  1. Open the Vermintide 2 launcher
  2. Select ‘Mods’
  3. Deselect all mods
  4. Save your changes
  5. Launch Vermintide 2 as normal, and see if the issue persists

I am personally keeping note of all performance-related complaints we receive. So if the above solutions have been unsuccessful, please post here and include your PC’s specifications. Please do not post crashes here.
