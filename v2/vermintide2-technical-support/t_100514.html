I managed to fix the problem by using msconfig and disabling all non-Windows services. The culprits were AsusROGLSLService and services related to Citrix Workspace, AppProtection Service, Citrix App Data Protection Service, Citrix Policy Service, Citrix Workspace Advanced Service, which I sometimes use to access my university computers remotely. I tried also having these separately on, but I only managed to get in the official realm if both were disabled.
