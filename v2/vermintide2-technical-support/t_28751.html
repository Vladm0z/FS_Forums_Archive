Your ISP could do “whatever” to your DNS requests if you are not using some form of validation, such as DNS-over-HTTPS or DNS-over-TLS, so without getting too technical try:

https://simplednscrypt.org

Just follow the instructions for Windows (I’m assuming you are not gaming on Linux, since most peeps who are as crazy as me know their stuff around DNS / all kinds of networking).

Also https://blog.apnic.net/2018/10/12/doh-dns-over-https-explained/ is quite a decent read for the technically interested who hasn’t read all RFCs.

(On a side-note, I’m very surprised that it has taken till 2018(!) for DoH to be standardised, the first really viable alternative to normal port 53 UDP-based DNS, I mean, an evil ISP or state could essentially block or control everything on the Internet from the user perspective…)
