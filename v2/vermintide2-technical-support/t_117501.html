Sorry to hear this. It’s a shot in the dark, but trying a ‘Selective Startup’ to disable background applications may help, in case something external is interfering:

  1. Press the Windows key

  2. Type ‘msconfig’ and select ‘System Configuration’

  3. On the ‘General’ tab, select ‘Selective Startup’

  4. Deselect ‘Load startup Items’

  5. Reboot your PC

Otherwise, a clean reisntallation of Vermintide 2 which involves deleting your AppData can rule out any potential game installation or config issue (please note any custom settings e.g. keybinds, graphics etc. will be reset):

https://support.fatshark.se/hc/en-us/articles/360034118394--PC-How-to-Perform-a-Clean-Re-Installation-of-Vermintide-2-or-Darktide
