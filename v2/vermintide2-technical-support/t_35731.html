FatsharkJuliaCommunity Support • 2019-08-24 15:31:26

If your brother is playing on the same network and router and has no issues, I’d suspect this is due to something running on your PC. Can you think of anything that may be interfering with your connection? An over-zealous Anti-Virus/Firewall, VPN, Proxy? Maybe try Selective Startup:

  1. Press the Windows key
  2. Type ‘msconfig’ and select ‘System Configuration’
  3. On the ‘General’ tab, select ‘Selective Startup’
  4. Deselect ‘Load startup Items’
  5. Reboot your PC
  6. Play Vermintide 2 as normal and see if the issues persist
