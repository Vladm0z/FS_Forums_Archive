Fatshark_Hedge • 2018-10-19 10:04:40

Mods are enabled/disabled in the config file here:

AppData\Roaming\Fatshark\Vermintide 2

in

user_settings.config

I’d suggest unsubbing to all mods and deleting the entire Vermintide 2 folder in App data, then verifying the game files in Steam. This will clear out some local settings, like keybinds and the like and your video settings too, as well as perhaps putting you in the prologue next time you run the game (which you can leave the moment you gain control of Kruber).
