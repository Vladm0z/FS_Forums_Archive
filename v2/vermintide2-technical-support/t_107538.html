FatsharkLevCommunity Support • 2025-05-02 10:31:27

Sorry to hear this @Damanou

The crash log shows what our developers described as an “impossible” crash, with a singular occurrence unique to your system. They suspect that the crashes may be caused by CPU hardware failure. As Vermintide 2 is a very CPU intensive game issues may be more evident.

They noted you are using a 14th Gen i7-14700KF which may have felled victim to hardware degradation which is common with 13th and 14th series i7/i9’s.

For more information on the possible underlying issue, please refer to:

community.intel.com – 25 Sep 24

Intel Core 13th and 14th Gen Desktop Instability Root Cause Update

Following extensive investigation of the Intel® Core™ 13th and 14th Gen desktop processor Vmin Shift Instability issue, Intel can now confirm the root cause diagnosis for the issue. This post will cover Intel’s understanding of the root cause, as...

There are BIOS updates available to mitigate/resolve issues for 13th/14th Gen CPU’s, so I would recommend checking that your BIOS is up-to-date. Though, we advise caution when performing firmware updates, and to do so at your own risk.
