For the sake of completion:

It seems that i have solved the issue - i literally tried everything at that point… - so all good on that front. As for what did it in the end, i’m not entirely sure if only the last step i took was the one that fixed it or all the others (like reinstalling and all that fun stuff with drivers etc.) were necessary too.

The issue was a DXGI_ERROR_DEVICE_HUNG and in my case it seemed to stem from having my CPU and GPU overclocked. Only turning both OCs off fixed the issue with crashing (though i mean to have noticed longer stability even with only one of them turnt off).

Anyhow. I’m looking forward to now be able to complete games and experience the full “Vermintide-Package” finally!
