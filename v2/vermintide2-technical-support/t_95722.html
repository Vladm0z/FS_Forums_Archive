FatsharkLevCommunity Support • 2024-06-13 08:39:55

Hi @applebottombeans,

I’ve spoken with one of our backend developers about this, and they’ve informed me that this error appears to be occurring due to bad timing with backend requests. Currently, when matchmaking begins other requests to the backend e.g. changing career/loadout are paused and resume when matchmaking finishes. However, these other requests can time out when processed due to the paused matchmaking time and result in this error.

To minimise this, please avoid doing any of the following while matchmaking (or a few seconds before):

  • Creating/Deleting loadouts
  • Changing active loadout
  • Changing talents, cosmetics, weapons/gear
  • Changing selected career

Hopefully we’ll have a fix sorted for this issue soon! Apologies for the inconvenience in the meantime.
