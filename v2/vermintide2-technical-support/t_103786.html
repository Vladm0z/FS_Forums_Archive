The background EAC service propably failed to resume on the power event due to timing out and/or didn’t boot properly. EAC is not a steam service and therefore restarting steam would do nothing. If the issue persists or happens often enough under these circumstances you should contact EAC directly and ask them to have a look on your log to determine what is causing the service control timeouts. Assuming it is due to that as that is the most common cause of this on w8 onwards.
