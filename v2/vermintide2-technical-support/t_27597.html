Issue Summary:
Sound muffles when source is behind of the character

Steps to Reproduce:

  1. Select any source of sound. (Such as Okri’s challenges)
  2. Turn 180 degree.
  3. You hear nothing from it.

Reproduction Rate (Choose One): Constant (100%)

Additional Information:

was having this problem, screwing around I resolved it:
reinstalled audio drivers, then within the speaker properties, there was a single checkbox for Dolby 7.1 which was unchecked. Checking it fixed it.
That said, I also had found it was affected other surround sound games. I tested with drills in Payday 2. If you’re having the issue in other games, you could try this, since my situation was exactly as you described. But if it’s only in Vermintide, obviously it would be something else
