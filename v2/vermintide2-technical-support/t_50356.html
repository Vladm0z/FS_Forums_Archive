Tried changing your Steam networking settings to make it always attempt a direct connection first? In Steam → Settings → In-Game → Steam Networking → change from ‘Default’ to either ‘Always’ or ‘Friends Only’ (if the lobbies you join are hosted by your Steam friends, otherwise prefer ‘Always’). With those settings, Steam will attempt a direct connection first ( which should have lower ping and a more stable connection), and only if the direct connection fails, will it relay the connection to Steam servers.

See this Steam Support article for more info on those settings: Peer-to-Peer Networking and Sharing Your IP Address
