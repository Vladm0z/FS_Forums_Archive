FatsharkLevCommunity Support • 2024-04-26 12:01:16

Sorry to hear this @Bemi

As you can launch in Modded realm, but not Official this definitely indicates an EAC issue.

Have you tried performing a Selective Startup and checking if a background application may be the cause?

  1. Press the Windows key
  2. Type ‘msconfig’ and select ‘System Configuration’
  3. On the ‘General’ tab, select ‘Selective Startup’
  4. Deselect ‘Load startup Items’
  5. Reboot your PC
  6. Launch Vermintide 2 as normal

If the error no longer persists, you will need to re-enable your usual background applications one-by-one to determine which is the culprit.

Otherwise, I recommend to try adding Anti-Virus and Firewall exceptions for both Vermintide 2 and Easy Anti-Cheat:

  • vermintide2.exe in \Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Warhammer Vermintide 2\binaries
  • vermintide2_dx12.exe in \Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Warhammer Vermintide 2\binaries_dx12
  • EasyAntiCheat.exe in \Program Files (x86)\EasyAntiCheat
