@senatorarmstrong sorry to hear this.

Firstly, please try a clean reinstallation of Vermintide 2, which involves deleting your AppData, to rule out any potential issue with the game installation or settings (please note any custom settings e.g. keybinds, graphics etc. will be reset):

https://support.fatshark.se/hc/en-us/articles/360034118394--PC-How-to-Perform-a-Clean-Re-Installation-of-Vermintide-2-or-Darktide

If the crashes and instability persist, it may be worth checking for any available BIOS update, as this can often help to improve stability. I noticed you have a 13th gen i7-13700K, which may have fell victim to hardware degradation, which is common in 13th/14th gen Intel CPU’s. Updates have since been released to mitigate and resolve these issues, but if your BIOS has not been updated then it could be a hardware issue. As this is a firmware update, we advise caution, and to do so at your own risk.

For more information on this, please refer to:

https://community.intel.com/t5/Blogs/Tech-Innovation/Client/Intel-Core-13th-and-14th-Gen-Desktop-Instability-Root-Cause/post/1633239
