SneZ • 2020-06-23 12:10:53

Hey Guys

A quick fix that worked for me was to copy the current xinput1_3.dll in system32 directory and rename the copy xinput1_4.dll

So under C:\Windows\System32\ you’ll have xinput1_3.dll and xinput1_4.dll (which is a copy of xinput1_3.dll).

Loads up fine now.
