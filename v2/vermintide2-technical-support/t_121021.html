Here’s steps on how to delete your local save data if you need them in the future:

  1. Go to ‘My Games & Apps’
  2. Highlight ‘Warhammer: Vermintide 2’
  3. Press the ‘Menu’ button (three lines),
  4. Select ‘Manage Game & Add-ons’
  5. Then ‘Saved data’
  6. Then choose your profile to select ‘Delete from console’
