FatsharkLevCommunity Support • 2024-11-16 11:30:30

There are two reasons why this may be occurring.

  1. There is a current backend issue affecting some players opening chests (due to a mismatch in how some chests are stored in account data), though this is more likely to occur when you only have 5 chests remaining and would prevent these from being opened as an example.
    KNOWN ISSUE: Backend Error Preventing Opening Chests - #2
  2. Too many requests being sent to the backend in a short time will result in a Backend Error. This is most commonly triggered when spam opening chests or re-rolling traits.
