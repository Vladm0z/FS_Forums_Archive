  3. Restart your PC

  3. Select the ‘Driver or Class’ option and add the driver that caused the issue - EasyAntiCheat_EOS.sys)
  4. Make sure to uncheck the EasyAntiCheat driver in the list of drivers to be verified
  5. Restart your PC
