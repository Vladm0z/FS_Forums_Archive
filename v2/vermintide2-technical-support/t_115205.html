SOLUTION:

My friend and I had the same problem.

In your PC, navigate to your Vermintide Folder \EasyAntiCheat\. In the EasyAntiCheat, there is a Certificates folder. Delete the folder (or just move it from there.)

Then Go into STEAM and Verify your files [Library, Right-click, Properties, Verify Game files] It will put new certificates into the folder. The game will work.
