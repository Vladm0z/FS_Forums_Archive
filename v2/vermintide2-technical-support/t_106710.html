Issue Description (Required):

We are aware of an EAC connectivity issue affecting players based in Russia:
EAC failed to initialize (EOS auth status = failed result = EOS_NoConnection)

If you are encountering this error, please can you provide us with the following:

  • The name of your internet service provider

  • Your most recent console log from a session the error occurs. Please see:
    https://support.fatshark.se/hc/en-us/articles/360017633918--PC-How-to-Provide-a-Crash-Report-Console-Log-Launcher-Log-or-user-settings-config

  • A zip of your Easy Anti-Cheat folder %AppData%\EasyAntiCheat:

  1. Press Windows + R
  2. Enter ‘%appdata%’ and press ‘Ok’
  3. Right-click on the folder ‘EasyAntiCheat’ and select ‘Compress to ZIP file’
  4. Upload the ZIP file to us
    Please upload files to a reputable host service e.g. Google Drive and share a folder link with public permissions so we can access, thank you!

We are investigating the cause of this error, and if there is anything we can do from our side.

We believe this error indicates connection to Epic’s servers are being blocked in some way. Most commonly, access to https://api.epicgames.dev/sdk/v1/default

Please try the following:

  • Restart your PC and router
  • Ensure you have latest Windows updates installed
  • Check your Anti-Virus, Firewall and network settings and ensure connection to Epic is not being blocked

If the error persists, we suggest contacting your ISP and explaining the situation to them, as they may be able to assist or provide further information on the connection.

Some players are reporting using a VPN as a successful workaround. We appreciate the use of a VPN is not possible for all players, and that it is not a permanent solution. However, for players that do have access to a VPN this may help in the meantime.
