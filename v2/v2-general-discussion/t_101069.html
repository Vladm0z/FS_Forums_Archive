Hello fellow verminslayers!

I’m excited to share the first installment of a series of posts where I will share my ideas for reworking and enhancing most of the rpg aspects of Vermintide 2 (mainly talents, traits and properties). This project has been a labor of love, inspired by my deep passion for the game and its community. Some of you may recognize me from my comprehensive guides, which I’ve shared in the past (you can check them here). The comments I received on those guides pushed me to keep making content related to the game, and I’m hoping we can create even more engaging conversations with this new series.

What to Expect:
Over the next five weeks, I’ll be posting one section per week, starting today with the gear properties and traits, as well as the common talents (stagger talents and THP talents) and a small improvement to regeneration effects. These reworks aim to enhance gameplay mechanics, balance, and the overall experience, drawing on both my gameplay insights and input provided by the community over the years.

What’s Next:

I will be posting each character’s section weekly (ideally on Fridays) where you will be able see my ideas for reworking them. Sometimes it will involve just a few talent adjustments, while other times there will be more significant changes.

My goal is to increase build variety and to address “dead” talents, ensuring every choice feels impactful and meaningful. Your thoughts, critiques, and suggestions are not only welcome but highly encouraged. Let’s build this conversation together!

Stay tuned for next week’s post, where I’ll dive into my boy Kruber.

Thanks for reading, and may Sigmar’s light guide your hammer!

The numbers are probably off, but triggering it on every attack against regular infantry that is attacking (which is almost constantly, unless they are on the ground) AND applying it to all nearby allies could easily make it overpowered, which is why I set it on a 4 second cooldown and limited it to restoring only 2 THP.
It’s true that getting THP is an easy thing to do, but when you want to be a better support for your teammates and they are not super skilled players, I think a talent like this can be super cool.

Frostysir:

Removing scrounger, why in the world?

Because 90% of the time it’s “if you can land headshots, Conservative Shooter; if you can’t, Scrounger”. This opinion is based on (almost) every build that I’ve ever seen being one or the other.
Conservative Shooter at least rewards skill, but the former is just a way to cheese some extra ammo at random. It kinda “blocks” all the other traits.
The replacement was not necessary at all, but it still is better than nothing.

Frostysir:

Inspirational headshots effect is (…)

Frostysir:

The main problem with healing from the likes of waystalker, GK and natural bond is not that they replace THP (…)

I have heard that exact complaint over and over again in discussions about Natural Bond. Because of that, people often consider it “literally useless” (and they are not too far off the truth).
I agree that it still wouldn’t be able to fully compete with Barkskin and Boon, but it is an improvement to its niches (even with the nerf to 10 seconds per tick). I have used NB to great success on squishy ranged-focused builds, on any of Sienna’s careers when using a ranged build, on some support builds and on Ironbreaker and Unchained.
I’ve used it extensively and it’s numbers are good in Official Realm. Nerfing the healing rate might not be necessary (or at least, not such a big nerf), but since having limited healing is a core part of the game, I wanted to make sure it stayed weak.
