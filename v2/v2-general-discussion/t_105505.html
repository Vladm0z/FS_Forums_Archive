I can’t speak on if stagger is applied before or after damage calculation, but I can say the level 15 talent descriptions are misleading. Lightly and heavily staggered enemies will always take 20/40% extra damage respectively no matter what. All level 15 talents aside from Enhanced Power do not affect ranged attacks.

  • Bulwark applies a 10% extra damage taken debuff.
  • Smiter does exactly what it says - if an enemy isn’t staggered, they will take 20% extra damage. This might be evidence that the extra stagger count is applied after damage is dealt, or maybe Smiter is best used on large, unstaggerable enemies.
  • Mainstay makes you deal 15% more damage vs. staggered enemies.
  • Assassin does exactly what it says - if an enemy isn’t staggered but you get a crit or headshot, they will take 40% extra damage without factoring in the stagger.

This info was taken from the Advanced Tooltips mod used in Modded Realm.
