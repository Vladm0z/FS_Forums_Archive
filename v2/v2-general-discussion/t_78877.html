The game is much more enjoyable when playing with a group of people you know and chat with. I was fortunate to be invited to a discord server where friendly players gather to play Vermintide 2, and I even invited some friends from my previous job to join. Now, there are about 5 of us who play semi-regularly, and it’s a lot more fun that way.

I recommend finding a group to play with. The first friendly player you come across, you can start a discord server and invite them to join, or ask to join the server they play on. It’s a great way to connect with like-minded players and avoid the toxicity of quick-play.

However, I also don’t particularly enjoy the friendly fire mechanic in this game; even when playing with friends or in quick play, having adapted to fragile gamer personalities. While some might argue that FF can add a challenging element, not everything challenging is fun. Sometimes, despite our best efforts, friendly fire happens, especially when someone runs in front of you while you’re firing.
