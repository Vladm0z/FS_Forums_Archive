I’d say it’s a hard weapon and class for a newcomer. Huntsman is hyperfocused on headshots, so you had to land them reliably, otherwise class barely works. But it should be much better on legend.

As for long bow. It’s very important to have good damage brake points.
Your goal here is to reach as many one-shot brake points on important targets, without headshots or crits.

Important targets being:
Specials > Storm vermin > Berserkers
Those are enemies, with low enough health to reach those break points, by various means.

Various means being:
Power vs x > ult > Potion of power
Those are means that you have the most control of and can gain by your self most often.
When figuring breakpoints against elites, I recommend always take ult power in to account.

I’d suggest you to experiment on training dummies, or even go on modded realm and spawn actual enemies by mod. That if you want to waste so much time on longbow like me in the past.

Useful info:
Armored training dummy have “Skaven” and “Armor” classes.
Unarmored training dummy have “Chaos”(not sure, mb skaven) and “Infantry” classes.

Longbow have 3 power stages of an attack, you want to shoot from stage 2 (half drawn) and 3 (fully drawn).

Here is a break point calculator that I just found: https://manshanko.github.io
It is lacking some features and info, from the old one. I also Don’t know how accurate it is.
But according to it, having Power vs skaven x2 and Power vs infantry x2 is the most optimal against specials on legend, however you are losing +5% weapons speed on your trinket, which might be important for your melee weapon.
