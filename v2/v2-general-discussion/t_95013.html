CobaltNinja • 2024-05-27 10:37:16

More specifically, after you earn the penance, I would grab one skull and leave it at that. On Legend dificulty, the amount of time between waves results in any buffs higher than that having a severe risk of ticking out and applying massive health drain, with 4 & 5 being basically guaranteed if you don’t have a good pace throughout the map. One skull is a sizeable power boost and lasts for a good long while.

Max skull buffs is 5, granting:

  • Power
  • Attack speed
  • Cleave
  • Movement speed
  • Cooldown

for each stack, top to bottom as you collect more. Picking up another skull or killing an enemy refreshes bonuses, as well as triggering unique event-related CW boons.

If the buff timer runs out, you lose HP per second equivalent to the amount of skull buffs you had when they decayed. Lasts about 15 seconds, I think, and damage taken this way can’t kill you.
