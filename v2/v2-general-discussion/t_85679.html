What "continued support" should REALLY entail

4 Posts • 372 Views
S
Sigmar • 2023-10-30 16:51:46

Like most people, I thought Fatshark would be pretty much done with Verm 2 with the launch of Darktide and fulfilling the last of their career obligations with Necro. I’m pretty happy they retain a Vermintide team and am just happy it’s not going into complete stasis. Similarly, I know requests from Fatshark REPEATEDLY fall on deaf ears, but I’m going to try anyway.

The fact there’s a potential of future content is great, but there’s but a general lack of polish to core aspects of the game that are far more important to me than just new coats of paint.

  • Visual bugs: It seems like if there’s a bug in this game that doesn’t DIRECTLY impact gameplay or performance, they almost never do anything about it. Saltzpyre’s brace of pistols has not appeared as a model on his body for years. Waystalker’s quiver emptied out in the SotT update and never refilled. Now with Necro, the hammer on Bardin’s handgun doesn’t move when shot, for whatever reason. I’ve reported these MULTIPLE times on both Steam and their forums, get an employee reply saying “great, we made a note of it!” and they don’t get touched after years. Little details matter, and the most mind-racking of all is the fact they still haven’t done the easiest thing in the world of adding color adaption between headgear and unlocked body recolors, like helmets on Ironbreaker or Foot Knight matching the red or black armors.

  • Audio bugs: Silent patrols seem to be whack-a-mole with every update. Non-zerker marauders giving off berserker screams. Patrols giving the “alerted” noise even if they haven’t been alerted. Sometimes silent specials, or even non-procing backstab noises. Maybe you haven’t experienced them personally, but it seems like at least someone always has a few of these issues at any given time. It’s not just about immersion, it’s about readability, and it should really be prioritized to function reliably.

  • Class balance: To its credit, it has gotten better, and isn’t even in a terrible spot, but for whatever reason some aspects just seem like they get to sit there for years untouched. Hell, it was close to a year before SotT’s reign of terror was finally ended. Things like Master Engineer being universally being considered pretty mediocre, Grail Knight still being overtuned, and Warrior Priest inexplicably not being allowed any legacy weapons even though they’d fit totally fine thematically (he can have a flail and shield but not a flail???) There’s a matter of basic function too, like how Pyromancer would be so much stronger if her stupid ult was actually capable of targeting what she’s aiming at.

  • Weapon balance: You can’t make every weapon equally viable, and I think some like the Greatsword just deserve a flat rework, but some could so EASILY be made good and they refuse to touch them. Give Flamestorm the primary fire of Fireball/Conflag, boom, infinitely more viable. Up 1h axe damage or give them the mass-ignore mechanic on crit like Necro/Darktide, boom, more viable. Give Kruber’s Blunderbuss some more damage/AP so it can reliably one-shot elites/specials at close-mid range. Boom, more viable. Give the Repeater Pistol more reserve ammo. Boom, more viable… oh, and PLEASE do SOMETHING about the Briar Javelins. It’s been YEARS and no one likes them except the Waystalker throwing them.

  • Mods: I’ve talked to three of the top mod authors who have verified mods, and they all agree that this game is very user-hostile to work with, especially when every update breaks their work. Several have been abandoned at this point. MAYBE that’s unavoidable, but if Fatshark has time to REMOVE mod verification, they could at least do a passthrough and Verify a new batch. Again, it’s been years of stagnation at this point.

  • New weapon skins and hats: Maybe? No, okay, but just consider it?

I’m not suggesting any big changes (and there are plenty I’d love to) because getting Fatshark to work on anything beyond monetized content drops or just make the game baseline functional has been a big ask since I joined Verm 1 beta. Yes, stuff like Necromancer is good work, but the months and years we go between major changes and reworks is not really explained by the company’s limited communication. I’ve given up on Dedicated Servers and PvP long ago, and the core gameloop is in a relatively good spot, but the moment-to-moment little stuff adds up and in games like Deep Rock they get patched within a month of the community shining lights on them.

And then there are cases where you have crowbill which is pretty serviceable still, but nobody will disagree when I say it’s charged attack is completely illogical and pointless in its current state (a completely horizontal swing…with zero cleave?)

And I would certainly hope there’s going to be way more than just some cosmetics, those don’t drive up interests. There should still be enough room for one or more weapon pack I can pretty easily think of. And I do want to see atleast one enemy pack that adds enemies to each Faction, such as a few more specials for Beastmen, slingers for Skaven, forsaken for Rotbloods, etc.
