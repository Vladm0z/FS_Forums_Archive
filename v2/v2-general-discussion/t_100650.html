Flinlock • 2024-10-29 18:08:45

Any pro tips? They’re a modifier on the weekly expedition and they’re annoying AF to deal with.

  • Hordes of them come too frequently
  • Blowing one up doesn’t set off the ones around it
  • Blowing one up DOES put up a big cloud of smoke so you can’t see the other ones
  • Pushing them can save you from getting exploded, but only useful if there’s one or two spread out. Otherwise they way they run in a close line means you push the first one, the second one gets you, then you’re disoriented and they all get you
  • No way to counter for a melee-only character
  • They seem too resistant to AoE damage. A fully charged conflag staff blast will only stagger them
