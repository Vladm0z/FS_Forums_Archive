Ensrick • 2025-11-06 16:31:22

I got 95% vaults even with random players, and for a long time, collecting red items was the main focus. Now, the fun comes from carrying teams and helping others succeed. I’ve solo’d Cata runs and tried modded difficulties, but it was too much of a slog. These days, all I do is drop into random lobbies on Legend or Cata, or even Champion, just to lend a hand. Vermintide 2 has surprising depth. Unlike competitive games that demand 20k hours, after a few thousand hours in VT2, complete mastery is attainable. I spent 5k hours in CS2 and only reached Faceit Level 5. Between hackers and stale gameplay, I eventually quit. With work, family, and other hobbies, I realized competitive games ask too much, while co-op ones like this feel more rewarding and less toxic.

Vermintide 2 fills a rare niche for me. I love team-based games with strong melee combat and a dark fantasy vibe, and this one checks all three of those boxes. At this point, I rarely play anything else unless I’m taking a break. I’ve accepted that any game worth investing in will take serious time, so in my 30s, I only play those that truly meet my standards and fit my schedule. Whether it’s finishing every quest in an RPG or unlocking all the items I care about, I want the full experience. Vermintide 2 hits that sweet spot. It’s deep but not endless, and even after mastering it, I still find joy in improving bit by bit and helping others get their wins.

The grind for red items was one of the most satisfying parts of the game early on. Completing the full collection will probably take a few thousand hours, which might end up feeling like too much depending on your patience and interest. You might burn out before you get there, but if you stay hooked and keep having fun, you’ll eventually collect every red item naturally. I felt very satisfied when I completed my collection, and I still enjoy the game. Meanwhile, most of my friends got busy with life, burnt out long before then, moved onto other games, or some combination of the three, and only infrequently return to this game. So yeah, enjoy while it lasts.
