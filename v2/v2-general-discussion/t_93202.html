So if we get a new Warhammer Fantasy tide game after Vermintide 2 would you guys like the setting of the End Times or The Old World?

The basic differences between these are of course that the End Times is the period of current Vermintide games, the Total War games and the game which was finished/abolished by GW and was followed by Age of Sigmar.

The Old World is the re-released Warhammer Fantasy Game as presently released by GW and set about, I think it was, 300 years or so before the End Times and if I recall just before the Great War of Chaos. So a bit different from what we’ve get in the End Times even if things are mostly recognizible.
