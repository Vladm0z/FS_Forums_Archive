A final wish for Vermintide 2, Truly Custom Games

7 Posts • 258 Views
V
Voidyll • 2025-05-12 17:43:30

As the great game and experience that has been Vermintide 2 ends its development life cycle, I would simply like to make one last request to fatshark. Give us a custom games editor that is truly custom.

To elaborate, me and the rest of my friends are very end game players that have everything and have done everything there is to do in this game, yet we still play it. Why? Because the sheer amount of maps and modifiers makes it so there’s almost always something we can do to keep it fresh. At least, that is almost the case, many of the modifiers that we enjoy are not commonly able to be played together, and we are left waiting week over week for tzeentchian to come back into rotation on the weekly missions board.

And, as console players, we don’t have a way to mod whatever modifiers we want into a mission. So here’s my proposal:

Give us a new custom games mode that lets us pick any of the base game maps, any difficulty (including catas 2 and 3), lets us pick any modifier from deeds, weeklies, weaves, AND events such as the geheimnesnacht or skulls event modifiers, and lets us pick ANY amount of modifiers to stack onto a mission (map and difficulty you can’t pick more than one of obviously, just the modifiers). I would be fine with you excluding rewards system for this mode as calculating what the reward for a mode with this many options would have.

I understand a big fear with a mode like this is how would matchmaking work. I would say that you could give true custom games its own quick play where you pick a difficulty and queue into anyone who is playing a true custom game at that difficulty (would only search for difficulty and not map or modifiers). However I would argue that at least a large percent of the playerbase of this game is held up by groups of friends that simply play private games with each other, and so refusing to add truly custom games for fear that it would split the playerbase even more is not that strong of an argument. This would be a mode made for people who want to try random combos or extra difficult content with friends, or for people who don’t care what shenanigans they are about to queue into.

The literal only reason me and my friends don’t play this game effectively religiously is because the modifiers and difficulties we enjoy are locked behind a random weekly board, weaves, or once a year events. Fatshark my life would be yours if I could simply be able to play gehaimnesnacht skull, tzeentchian, vanguard parting of the waves on cata. The one time that combo was up was the most fun I’ve had in this game period, being able to play that or any other interesting combo would easily let me and my friends put another thousand hours into this game easily.

So that sums up my final wish for this game, I would love to hear a response from the devs on whether there’s any chance of this becoming a reality, and that chance goes up as this post gains support so please comment your own ideas or thoughts as well. Thank you for reading!
