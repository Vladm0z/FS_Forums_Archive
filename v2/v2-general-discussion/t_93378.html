Hello friends.

I am announcing the first Vermintide Community Championship! A PvE-based competition to encourage the community to come together to demonstrate and celebrate the skill of the best rat slayers out there! This is a new community run event started by myself and some other friends.

The VCC will be held with the community-made difficulty mods Deathwish, Onslaught, and Dense Onslaught, and all run on the base game mode Cataclysm 3. The current tiers of difficulty on offer for this event are as follows:

Tiers:

  1. Cataclysm 3 + Deathwish + Onslaught
  2. Cataclysm 3 + Deathwish + Dense Level 1
  3. Cataclysm 3 + Deathwish + Dense Level 2
  4. Cataclysm 3 + Deathwish + Dense Level 3

More detailed information on these mods can be found in the Vermintide Modded Community server [here] (VT Modded Community). If you are new to modded, folks in here can help you get oriented.

The basic idea of the format is for teams to select 4 maps from a list, and run the maps in order. Points are awarded on the completion of maps, and no retries are allowed. Attempts are live-streamed at set times, and once signups are completed a schedule will be made available to all to watch the attempts.

[Sign-ups](VT Modded Community) for the next event are NOW OPEN.

Once registered, teams will then have to reserve their time slot and stream their runs at a pre-arranged time. The form for reserving their time will open on April 19th, and teams are encouraged to register as soon as possible.

Sign-up deadline: Friday, April 26th, 2024

Time Slot Reservation Period: Friday, April 19th until Friday, April 26th, 2024

Competition period: Sunday April 28th, 2024 - Saturday May 5th, 2024
