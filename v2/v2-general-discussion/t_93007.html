Its not a secret the game is in the second half of its lifespan, and in preparation for long waits between content I would love to see introduction of long term grinds for rare or cool items skins and illusions.
This would greatly extend the longevity of the game as players would have something to work to and reach for other than challenges and character numbers, during the extended periods between updates and releases, along with keeping people playing longer.

As examples for what I imagine this would look like, take Sea of Thieves for example, with its ship skins and player model curses. Some skins require players to gather millions of gold for an item many wont be able to have, or having to 100% missions for a rare outfit.
I think this would be applied well in Vermintide and following are a few ideas and examples.

  • Gather all 30 ravaged art for every Helmgart map | Reward: rare frame or something else multicharacter (alternatively could be some new thing that any character can use, like an aura or smn)
  • Reach level 50/100/1000 etc with XYZ character | Reward: Rare color or skin for character, potentially first few small ones like 50 - 100 could be frames including the character, then 500-1000 could be colors or hats.
  • reach level 100 (35+65) with all 5 characters | Reward: A cool frame or multicharacter hat
  • Kill 250,000 (or any other number) Skaven(or other thing) with XYZ character’s XYZ weapon | Reward: A special illusion for that weapon
  • Recycle 500 Veteran items | Reward: ???
  • Complete X missions or amount of missions back to back without being hit or going down or something

These are just loose ideas, don’t knock the concept because you don’t like the one challenge it might not be exactly that.
While this would be a lot of cosmetics and things to design and create for a relatively old game, it would allow for continuous goals that players can reach for and feel proud of showing off, allowing the devs to work on other things and focus less on Vermintide for awhile.

Just an idea.

Agree with the general idea.
Something to keep coming back for (outside of gameplay) - is a contributing factor to player retention.

Deep Rock Galactic has a very good grasp on this idea. From random cosmetic loot in missions, to weekly assignments - that give rare resource (its a primary way of obtaining weapon overclocks).

A similar implementation in VT2 would be a weekly mission that rewards red dust, or occasionally random red weapon itself. Red dust or weapons is something that isn’t too important to miss (if you’re geared enough), but its also something more substantial than shillings. That will also allow you to experiment more with builds and switch to other careers/heroes.

It doesn’t have to be an increased challenge (with a mutator), it could be just pre selected map(s), like an assignment in DRG.

The trick is to make it attractive, not too much RNG (be deterministic), but also not essential.

They failed hard in darktide in this regard.
