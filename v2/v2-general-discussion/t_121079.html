Ensrick • 2026-04-29 04:13:21

The Moonfire Bow is honestly a bit underrated right now. On Handmaiden, Sister, and Shade, it pairs really well with Hunter and crit chance, since all three of these careers have bonuses to crit accessible through talents or their career abilities.

Hunter increases power (both melee and ranged) by 25% against enemies of the same kind for 10 seconds whenever you crit with it. Shade has an extra 5% crit chance baseline, Handmaiden has a talent offering 5% more crit, and Sister can get free crits through her kit. Sister can also take a talent that grants 2 free crits with each Thornwake, plus another that gives +10% crit chance for consuming Radiance.

The Moonfire Bow is a fairly spammable weapon, and the DoT lets you hit all the necessary breakpoints you need for a ranged weapon. Pair this with Hunter and you’ve got a noticeably stronger melee elf. You can also run Barrage, which stacks a 5% power bonus per hit.

The Javelins can use these traits too, but I find Javs are better suited for their stagger, and they benefit much more from the headshot trait that returns 1 ammo, so you can spam them at the head. They’re also a bit slower.

In my opinion, the Moonfire is an ideal choice for any build using crit chance when paired with Hunter. Your uptime should be pretty high when using it, giving you a massive power boost. It’s great for firing into hordes to trigger Hunter and then going ham in melee. Works well against monsters too, and it kills specials pretty fast.

The only sad thing is losing that blue puff effect from when it had AOE. Sure, it was mega OP back then, but they could’ve kept the effect and just made it smaller. It looks a little sad now.
