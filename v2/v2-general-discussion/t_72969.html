Max93 • 2023-04-10 12:08:34

Give it a try with elven spear or glaive. You will have a better block/push angle and these two weaopons are the best weapons with the bleed talent (applying the highest dot damage). Furthermore they synergize better with your increased push strength, due to the longer attack range. You dont need to close up to the enemies after your pushes.

Keep in mind that:

Dual daggers have 40° block-/push angle
Glaive has 90° block-/push angle
And elven spear has 180° block-/push angle

… as base value.

That means if you are good at headshotting with a spear you should go for this one with a repel build. And dual daggers are here in a clear disadvantage

You can try this build:

https://www.ranalds.gift/build/X04NO2HOPqMxioE4DBiH/view

With this build you shouldnt sacrafice any damage potential on your repel build

Just use your ult as often as you can to keep the hunter buff permantly up.

Ult - > 100% critchance on the next 2 attacks - > do one range attack on unarmoured and one amoured units to apply hunter on both armor classes - go into melee - repeat when hunter buff runs out.

On monster ult - > apply hunter buff - - > go melee.

Try to practice headshots on elites because spear has an insane high headshot mulitplier thats why we run smiter

The stagger ult is taken because you want to spam your ult for hunter buff, and with wall you only would annoy your teammates

You can switch staff for jav but you will be more busy with reloading since you run hunter and not ammo regain on headshot.

In another topic i recommend for the hunter build sword&dagger or double dagger but with repel build you are more support orientated with the spear and not a pure elite killer, so staff fits better imo. Thats also why we take thp on crit & headshot and not on kill, because we arent that much of an frontline elite killer than with a pure sister hunter build.

Keep in mind repel is a bit of an offmeta build but with this set up should do very well on cata without sacraficing any damage output, because we compensate the lost bleed and the lost poison stack with the hunter buff. As well we havent sacrificed any stats for an increased block angle by taking the spear which already has 180° block/push angle.
