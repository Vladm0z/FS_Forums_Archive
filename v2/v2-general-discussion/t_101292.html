While the OP could have definitely presented the sentiment a lot better, I can’t help but agree. Versus could be a lot of fun. There’s some balance issues in terms of ranged weapons, and the game mode is inherently restrictive on what talents will and will not see use because it creates a completely different environment from all the other game modes (hordes are irrelevant, elites and armor are few and far between, special sniping is everything) so the balancing of careers will always be a mess.

But that, one could cope with. One cannot cope with the fact the game mode is in a “toiletbowl spiral” because of the nonexistent matchmaker. You simply cannot have a game mode where players are pitted against each other and not account for differences in player skill levels. We don’t need a full on MMR system, or the game to track player’s play time. But one would assume the whole Versus Rank system was implemented for a reason. Not just a cosmetic banner and some chest and coin rewards on lvlup. So use it to assign players into matches. Cause otherwise the spiral continues: Players join a game. One team has 4 lvl 35s, one has two lvl 35s, a lvl 18 and a lvl 6. The two 35s on the worse team have maybe 5 versus games between them. The full lvl 35 team is in green to orange ranks. That’s if you’re lucky any there are no bots to begin with (which also don’t get distributed evenly between the teams for some insane reason). Who do you think will win? The players know that too. So players on the “disadvantaged” team will start leaving, furthering the spiral. And then those people that were left on the disadvantaged team sure are gonna have an awful experience with the bots. They’ll probably leave. Or if they don’t, they’ll seriously consider not queueing for another. And these are the players you should be looking out for the most. You know, the new players, the ones the game should by trying to retain. Those are the ones that will have the worst experience.

Then there’s the poor players just looking for a match. More than half the time, those get thrown into this mess midway through a game, where one team has 0 points and the other 230 after the first round. They’re not gonna have a good time either.

Versus is currently more about fighting the damn lobby system/matchmaker (or absence thereof) than fighting the rats. I queue to get my first win of the day bonus (to farm sellsword marks for cosmetics) hoping the game is as imbalanced in my favour as it can be so I can get it done quick. Cause you can easily spend an hour queueing from one horrible match that’s already decided to another. With long wait times in between, I should mention. Those would be a whole lot more tolerable too if a good game was waiting at the end of it. But 9 times out of 10, it’s an entirely one-sided mess. And it’s a shame, cause as much as I prefer PvE to PvP in most games, Versus really had a lot of potential. And I say had cause I’m pretty sure the horrible matchmaking killed all interest in the mode for a lot of people. You only get to make a first impression once. And Versus botched it hard, due almost entirely to the ridiculous matchmaking.
