Chaos Wastes used to be a nice refreshing breath of air from the normal map grind, but with the recent changes its lost all of its enjoyment, for me and at least the people I regularly play with. While I understand certain traits prior could be abused to make absurdly overpowered builds, the majority of the boons now range from useless to bad.

Half of the boons that could even be considered good are potentially build breaking, so the RNG factor of using boon shrines coupled with the cost of upgrading weapons to Exotic or Veteren status, makes it so for the vast majority of a CW run, you are playing underpowered with 0-no upgrades.

The only somewhat worthwhile things to spend coins till reaching the last map or two (with orange / red upgrade shrines) are, the pit stops along the way. You MAY also get something good if you do the challange shrines, which are typically worth it because they are less of a gamble.

IF fatshark doesnt want to adjust the economy of CW, or improve the utility of many perks (or reduce the amount of useless ones) Boon Shrines should probably give a multiple choice, similar to challange shrines, so you don’t f**k up and ruin your build 10 seconds into a run.
