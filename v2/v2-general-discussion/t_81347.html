Slayer definitely has a lot of bad and/or excessively limiting talents that could use a serious redo. Same goes for most of Bardin’s melee arsenal, which is full of very old weapons that haven’t seen significant love in years. He also has the incredibly vexing issue of being on the ass end of one of the most unfun bugs in Chaos Wastes, where picking up random boons can crater your whole build if you are unlucky enough to get High Tally.

In spite of all that, slayers one build is quite strong in the normal game. Free 30% damage and 7.5% attack speed are both good passives. Leap is a good ult, Adrenaline Surge is by far one of the best talents in the whole game, and the synergy they both have with each other plus Crunch is quite high compared to what most careers get. He’s roughly on par with Grail Knight for non-boss dps while also being tankier and providing more control for the team.
