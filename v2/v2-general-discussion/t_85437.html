AsukasHeadphones:

Meanwhile Huntsman has access to a talent called “Keep it Coming” which is basically made redundant by the existence of scrounger

Keep it Coming is more than ammo sustain; it’s a DPS/reload-mitigation buff.
“Every 3rd ranged hit causes the next shot to consume no ammo”
Essentially doubling your clip size every other reload; assuming you don’t miss.

Give it a try if you haven’t already, it’s a massive boon to Bluntsman.
