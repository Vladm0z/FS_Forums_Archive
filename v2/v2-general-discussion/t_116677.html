SNUFFY123 • 2025-12-16 20:55:41

I agree completely that friendly fire should be removed. It takes more skill to play with friendly fire on, sure, but even the most skilled players mess up sometimes.

It’s not even the horde-clearing weapons that do the most damage to your team (e.g. flamestorm staff on Sienna, or flamethrower on Bardin). It’s the weapons that target elites and single enemies (bult staff, the javelins). Besides, there are already limits on range spam – you have ammo you need to pick up and for certain careers you have to get temp hp to vent.

But another thing is, if friendly fire is a necessary game mechanic, then why does it have to be so darn much? The bult staff takes away like 40 health points or more when it’s fully charged. And the bult staff isn’t even that good anymore because it means you can’t use lingering flames (which is the meta talent right now).

Even if Vermintide 2 players are more easy-going than darktide players, friendly fire still can create arguments between players. “Couldn’t you see I was shooting that enemy?” “Why’d you walk out in front of me?” Sometimes it’s no one’s fault; it can be a combination of factors that make it happen. Why add that game mechanic to a game that is cooperative? It doesn’t make sense.

Overall, I think it needs to be drastically reduced for certain weapons.
