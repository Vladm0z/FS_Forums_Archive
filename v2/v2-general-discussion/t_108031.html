Carius:

Are the DLC’s worth it?

I bought all of them. I personally think they were worth it in general, and worth it to support Fatshark if nothing else. But there are mixed opinions on some of them. Here they are roughly in order of most to least “worth it”:

  • Additional class DLCs (Sister of the Thorn, Outcast Engineer, Necromancer, Grail Knight, Warrior Priest). Sounds like you already have the Grail Knight, the other classes are awesome as well. In addition to the new class you get some pretty baller new weapons that can be used on the base classes.
  • Back to Ubersreik: Really solid DLC. 3 new levels (ported from Vermintide 1), five new weapons, new Okri’s challenge stuff. Plus a secret last-stand-style map called Fortunes of War.
  • Forgotten Relics pack: adds five new weapons, one per character. I like and use all of them frequently except for the Moonfire bow. This DLC gets a lot of hate in Steam reviews, because a lot of people are salty about the weapons being way too OP when first released, and then being nerfed to balance them with existing weapons. I think it’s a bunch of crybabies personally, and if you never played the pre-nerf versions you won’t care.
  • Shadows over Bogenhafen: 2 new levels, weapon illusions (all simple reskins of existing red weapon illusions, that grow purple instead of bluish-white), lots of Okri’s challenges and portrait frames. Good to support the developers, meh on content.
  • Winds of Magic: The least popular DLC. Now, you mostly get Beastmen faction, and Cataclysm difficulty. I think it’s worth buying for Cataclysm alone once you get to that skill level. Technically you also get the Weaves game mode, but it’s dead now. Still fun if you like doing solo challenges, or have friends to run them with.
  • Collectors Edition Upgrade. Buy it to support the devs, though most agree the soundtrack is dope.

Oh, and welcome aboard! Great to have you on team ratstab
