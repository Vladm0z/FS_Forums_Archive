Velsix • 2024-08-05 03:37:17

Boss damage, mainly on Zealot with Smite (every sixth hit crits).

If Repeater Pistol is the hybrid of Griffon-foot and Brace of Pistols, then Volley Crossbow is or could be the hybrid of the Crossbow and Griffon-foot.

Its left click is for some reason extremely accurate while moving.

I made it better in Kite Together (KT) by changing it and Bounty Hunter

Volley Crossbow KT Changes:
  • Magazine: Raised from 15 to 18.
  • Raised cleave by 12.4% (exact value needed to cleave one Cataclysm 1 Marauder iirc).
  • Damage dropoff start distance: Raised by 66.6%.
  • Damage dropoff end (damage stops dropping after this point): Raised by 14.28% (not sure why I made this change, though I probably misunderstood what ‘damage dropoff end’ did at the time).
Related Bounty Hunter KT Changes:

  • Weight of Fire: Gives ranged power equal to double magazine size instead of simply equal.

  • Blessed Combat: ‘Melee hits make up to the next 8 ranged attacks deal 15% more damage; ranged hits make up to the next 12 melee attacks 15% faster and deal 15% more damage.’

  • Salvaged Ammunition: Made to work on special kills and second-last shot, ammunition return raised from 15% to 26%, and melee kill reload raised from one shot to 15% of magazine. Melee kills can reload magazine beyond max capacity, max 3 extra shots, if scored while at least one shot is missing from the mag.

  • Just Reward: Passively makes ranged attacks cleave one more enemy (somewhat humorously, works with Rapier pistol). Cooldown reduction return lowered from 20% to 12.5%.

The above sounds strong (and there’s several more changes to BH), but he’s still tough to play in Kite Together. Wouldn’t have to go as far on Official to make Volley a compelling choice, but the end effect of the changes is a sustained mid-range horde mulcher, boss killer, and close-range special killer capable of accurately picking off already weakened specials at long range, like in this table:

DistanceSpecialsBossesHordes
Close-rangeEffectiveEffectiveAcceptable
Mid-rangeAcceptableEffectiveEffective
Long-rangeOnly if already weakenedAcceptableIneffective

For Official, I’d recommend just the following:

  • Salvaged Ammunition: Raise melee-kill reload from one shot to 15% of magazine (3 volley arrows, 2 bop bullets, 1 repeater pistol bullet, 1 crossbow arrow.)
  • Volley Crossbow: Raise cleave by 12.4% (exact value needed to cleave one Cataclysm 1 Marauder iirc).
