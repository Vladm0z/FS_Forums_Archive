fatsharkrobin:

Damage Numbers, Health Bars and Third Person are three mods that all provide some advantage over unmodded users (and in the case of Third Person, some disadvantages too). We hear you that these are very sought after, but we also don’t want people to feel they have to install these mods either to be ‘viable’ so we need a little bit more time to figure out what is the best way to approach these. Possibly it’s sanctioning the mods, possibly there are other options. It’s not being ignored and this is also why they haven’t been denied either. Currently sanctioning mods also eats from the time budget that also improves the mod tools and makes QoL improvement for the modders (where there is a lot to do) so we’re trying to get as much bang for the buck on our invested sanctioning time doing the most popular of the ‘safe’ bets first.

The bot mods are also a similar case where some of them contain outright cheats (bots can do things players can’t by circumventing player code), some contain disables of intentional inhuman behaviour (aimbotting etc) and some contain things that either just got implemented into the game or are coming very soon. Likely those that get sanctioned will have such parts disabled for Official Realm, but it’s an ongoing process currently.
