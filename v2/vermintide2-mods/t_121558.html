Steam Workshop::Tweaker: Chaos Wastes

A mew mod I made in sort of a beta release for the public. It allows you to tweak various parts of the Chaos Wastes, an experimental feature, you can play campaign missions in the Chaos Wastes. Chests of Trials can appear where Grimoires and books go. Normal pickup locations will have coin and chaos wastes potions, and the remainder are altars for upgrading weapons or getting random boons.

Highly experimental. I found if the number of levels players have doesn’t match, you can’t play with friends. Try turning the “Adventure Missions in Chaos Wastes” toggle off prior to joining, then once you’re all in the same lobby turn it on. I’m working on a lazy initialization, and I pushed an update for it, but if you run into issues, then that’s a good workaround.

Since altars spawn in locations you can find pickups (ammo, potions, bombs, ravaged art, collectibles, etc.), I have settings for how many, might turn it up a bit, like at least 2 to 3 of each. It can be difficult to find them. Really have to explore anywhere where there were pickups.

Expect bugs and crashes, but I played through with a friend, and it was pretty fun, and a very long but satisfying run!

EDIT: If you crash or get a WARNING/ERROR in the log while playing, send me your log

  1. Press Win+R, paste this, hit Enter:
    %APPDATA%\Fatshark\Vermintide 2\console_logs
  2. Sort by Date Modified (newest first).
  3. Grab the most recent console-.log, and if the crash kicked you to desktop,
    also grab the crash_dump-    .dmp with the matching timestamp from
    ..\Fatshark\Vermintide 2\crash_dumps
