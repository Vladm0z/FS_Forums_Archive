You know, I can understand the introduction of the Weave game mode is supposed to be the main selling point and thus is the cause for the naming of the expansion, but after watching the cinematic trailer (great job on that, by the way, it does the Beastmen race so much justice), the title of the expansion just feels a bit… off.

The cinematic trailer obviously puts the Beastmen in the spotlight, but I think it should’ve at least made some hints here and there towards the Weave mode or just magic in general, because without it, “Winds of Magic” wasn’t really the first thing that crossed my mind after seeing it.

A name such as “The Children of Chaos” or something akin to that would line up better with the trailer (and not just because a child was the main character shown), as well as the introduction of the Beastmen as a faction. Winds of Magic seems, to me, like it’s trying to convey that magic is going to play a major role, but it doesn’t really beyond the aforementioned Weave game mode.

I’m almost tempted to say that this expansion pack should have been split in two, one for the Beastmen, and one for the Weave mode. Would certainly have allowed for some of the content to reach us sooner, but that’s just my opinion, of course, and I am fully aware that it’s far too late in the development process to make any changes.
