FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: WHAT IS VERMINTIDE 2 - VERSUS?

Vermintide 2 - Versus is a new player vs player game mode for Warhammer: Vermintide 2. It will be 4 vs 4 gameplay, and you will be able to play as the Skaven, as well as the Heroes of Ubersreik. Take turns playing on the hero or the dark pact side.

Q: WHY VERSUS?

It’s true, we have felt in the past that as a studio we wanted to focus on our proven strengths - making a great co-op game. We like to think Vermintide 2 delivers on that goal.

We have always had PvP as a mode for Vermintide in the corner of our minds, though. In terms of focus, it’s been in the ‘cool to have’ box for a very long time, but in terms of priority, the core co-op experience remained at the very top of our list. This hasn’t really changed even with the development of Versus.

Versus was conceived during special weeks we have here in the studio, known as Hack Weeks. Hack Week is a chance for teams to work on ideas which they believe are powerful enough to impact the company or to focus on a particular product instead of working on the official content pipeline.

Over the course of multiple Hack Weeks, the bones of Versus was born and it turns out to be really good fun. We feel there is an opportunity in it to bring Vermintide 2 to a new audience and introduce a new way to experience the End Times.

We wish to sustain Warhammer: Vermintide 2 for many years and Versus is simply one of the many new features that we hope to bring to Vermintide 2 during its lifetime.

Q: AM I GUARANTEED A PLAYTEST SPOT IF I SIGN UP TO BECOME A PLAYTESTER?

For now, we are running a limited closed alpha, and if you are found to be a match, you will be invited to our current playtest. If you do not fit our needs at this time, don’t fret! You may fit the profile for our next test!

Q: WHAT TYPE OF PEOPLE ARE YOU LOOKING FOR?

We are looking for players from a wide variety of backgrounds, experiences, and abilities. Expert or novice, we’re looking for feedback from you! You must be at least 18 years old and have access to a PC running Vermintide 2’s minimum specs to participate in playtests.

Q: WHAT LANGUAGE ARE THE PLAYTESTS CONDUCTED?

All playtests are conducted in English.

Q: WHAT SPECS DO I NEED TO PARTICIPATE IN THE CLOSED ALPHA IF SELECTED?

It is recommended that your PC meet the recommended system requirements for Vermintide 2 found on this page here.

Q: WHAT IF I WANT TO CANCEL MY PARTICIPATION IN THE PLAYTEST?

If you no longer wish to be involved in playtests, simply write community@fatshark.se with the email address you filled out the form with.

Q: AM I CONSIDERED A FATSHARK EMPLOYEE IF I AM SELECTED AS A PLAYTESTER?

No. Signing up as a playtester does not constitute employment by Fatshark. This is considered a voluntary program. However, if you’re interested in working with us as an employee, head on over to jobs.fatsharkgames.com.

Q: HOW IS MY PERSONAL DATA PROCESSED FOR PLAYTEST SELECTION?

Fatshark cares about the integrity of all we come into contact with. All processing takes part in accordance with the provisions in the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (“GDPR”). This privacy applies specifically when you sign up as a playtester for Fatshark and is supplemented by Fatshark’s general Privacy Policy found here.

Please see our Processing of Personal Data page for more information on how Fatshark uses the data you submit for playtest candidacy.
