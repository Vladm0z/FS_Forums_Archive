Heroes!

Patch 1.2.0.2 is now live and is purely a bugfix patch. We’re actively pouring over all your feedback about the DLC systems and will have news on changes we’re looking to make there. For starters we will be adding more quests for DLC owners to obtain Bögenhafen Strongboxes.

Bugfixes and Tweaks

  • Fixed many issues on both The Blightreaper where players could get stuck on geometry. And also issues where you would clip through geometry, to access out of bounds areas.
  • Fixed an issue where Okri’s Challenges would indicate unclaimed rewards from the DLC-specific Challenges, even if the DLC wasn’t unlocked.
  • Fixed an issue which affected Okri’s Challenges that require 100 missions on specific heroes and difficulties. They now correctly take into account completed DLC levels.
  • Fixed the issue which caused players to be unable to progress at events that require all players, if some players had respawned. This fix solves these issues on all levels, but the notable ones which we had many reports of, where The Pit, and the end of Hunger in the Dark.
  • Fixed an issue where some Skaven horde types spawned less units than intended.
  • Fixed an issue where the Longbow’s spread template was incorrectly configured.
  • Fixed a rare issue where the Bardin Slayers Leap might crash the game if used to do a very short leap.
  • Fixed an issue where the game could crash if a bottle was destroyed.
  • Fixed an issue where the game could crash if another player hit a dead enemy that had been dismembered in different ways between the clients.
  • Decreased the time from button press to fire for Waystalker Kerillian’s Trueflight Volley to 0.25 seconds, down from 0.65.
  • Fixed an issue with training dummies, where headshots were not being registered correctly for certain weapons.
  • Fixed an issue where bots would drop their held Grimoire after getting knocked down.

Sanctioned Mods - Wave 2

The second wave of sanctioned mods is here - a grand total of 14 new mods are sanctioned on the live realm today. Here’s what’s new.

Welcome back from summer vacation and to the second wave of mod sanctioning. This time we’ve reviewed the, at the time, top rated mods from the workshop that had applied for sanctioning. A couple of mods (like Health Bars) skirt the limit of what could be judged as an unfair advantage and we still need to test more before giving a final verdict. For the rest that got reviewed but denied, there is a category in the workshop for it and the denial reasons are posted in the comments section.

We have some pretty cool stuff this time around more than doubling the amount of sanctioned mods, but I’ll let the mods themselves do the talking!

– Robin Hagblom, Fatshark Technical Director

MISSION TIMER
Creator: th-om

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Mission timer

Displays mission time during and/or after mission on the bottom right of the screen. Visibility during mission can be toggled.

MY HERO
Creator: SkacikPL

steamcommunity.com

Steam Community :: Error

This mod enables customization on the title/hero select screens. This means that heroes shown on both screens will reflect your current customization.

PENLIGHT LUA LIBRARIES
Creator: prop joe

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Penlight Lua Libraries

A slightly stripped down version of the Penlight Lua libaries containing all kind of utility functions and classes to speed up mod development.

NO WOBBLE
Creator: SkacikPL

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::No Wobble

With this mod enabled the camera will not be affected by weapon swing animations. classes to speed up mod development.

KILLFEED TWEAKS
Creator: prop joe

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::[OBSOLETE] Killfeed Tweaks

Reposition the kill feed and add a counter for duplicate kills.

NEUTER ULT EFFECTS
Creator: prop joe

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Neuter Ult Effects

Cuts down on visual and audio effects during career abilities.

SCOREBOARD TWEAKS
Creator: prop joe

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Scoreboard Tweaks

Adds a “Friendly Fire” category to the scoreboard. Friendly Fire and Damage Taken now award circles to the lowest score.

PARRY INDICATOR
Creator: Gohas

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Parry Indicator

Provides a color-customizeable visual cue via stamina shields when a timed block for the parry trait is active, allowing you to learn the timing more easily.

THIRD PERSON EQUIPMENT
Creator: grasmann

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Third Person Equipment (Abandoned)

Shows equipped items and pickups on characters.

CHAT BLOCK
Creator: grasmann, blblbl(rus), IamLupo

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Chat Block (Abandoned)

Automatic block with melee weapon when typing in chat and opening menus.

LOADOUT MANAGER
Creator: Squatting Bear

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Loadout Manager

Allows you to save multiple configurations of equipment and talents for each hero career, so that you can restore an entire configuration with a couple of mouse clicks.

ARMORY
Creator: Fracticality

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Armory

Ever wanted to know what the exact stats are for every attack of every weapon? The armory provides just that, full breakdown of all weapons and allows you to check out all the available illusions for each weapon in a preview window with panning, pivoting and rotating camera controls.

PLAYER LIST PLUS
Creator: Squatting Bear

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Player List Plus

On the player list shown when you press the Tab key, shows the equipment of all players. If another player also has the mod installed, it will also show what traits, properties and talents they are using in their loadout.

CROSSHAIR CUSTOMIZATION
Creator: prop joe

steamcommunity.com

Steam Workshop::Crosshair Customization

Change the color and size of your crosshair. Some other options like dot-only on ranged weapons.
