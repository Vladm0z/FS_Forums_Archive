HEROES!

The turn of a new season brings with it a new community event.

Please join us for the inaugural Vermintide Festival April 16–18 streaming on http://www.twitch.tv/fightthetide!

This is a three-day celebration celebrating all things Warhammer: Vermintide—The End Times, featuring gameplay and speedrun showcases, developer and voice actor interviews, community member panels, and more!

Fatshark has kindly provided a wealth of VT1-themed items for this event, which we’ll be giving away at the end of each day of the festival!

Check out the schedule below!

(all times shown are EDT/UTC -4)

PDF SCHEDULE HANDOUT FOR EASY REFERENCE

FRIDAY, APRIL 16

TimeEvent Title
6:00–6:15pmPRE-GAME OPENER: Welcome to the Vermintide Community Festival ft. Vermintide 1 w/ EXAN, Mothmana, and Fatshark Calavera
6:15–6:45pmAN EVOLUTION: BUILDING A COMMUNITY IN VERMINTIDE 1: Sit down with Vermintide & Darktide Community Manager Leo “Hedge” Wakelin, and learn how he became involved in the Vermintide project, how the community started, and where he sees it going.
6:45–8:30pmVERMINTIDE 1 WALKTHROUGH: Gameplay walkthrough of some of Vermintide 1’s most iconic maps; discussing gameplay differences, iconic moments, and what Vermintide 2 can learn from Vermintide 1. Featuring commentary from community veteran AgainPyromancer.
8:30–9:00pmIN NEW HANDS: WHAT IS HAPPENING IN VERMINTIDE 1 NOW? Vermintide 1 still has an active and growing community. Listen to current and past community members speak about what got them into the game, what they’re working on now, and how they keep the player base thriving.
9:00–9:15pmCLOSER: What to Expect on Day 2 & Giveaway

SATURDAY, APRIL 17

TimeEvent Title
12:15–12:30pmPRE-GAME OPENER: ft. Ishka and Grimalackt
12:30–1:00pmWOMAN ON FIRE: A CHAT WITH BETHAN DIXON BATE: Sit down with the well-spoken and illustrious voice actor, Bethan Dixon Bate, as she speaks about how she started in voice acting, and how she formed the character of Sienna Fuegonasus throughout both Vermintide 1 and 2. Viewer questions welcomed!
1:00–3:00pmDEATHWISH ONSLAUGHT SHOWCASE: Gameplay showcase of Deathwish & Onslaught mods ft. Incandescent, Core, BlohBloh, and Typhari. Shoutcasted by everyone’s favorite duo, Ishka and Grimalackt.
3:00–3:15pmINTERMISSION
3:15–4:00pmPREPARE TO CRY: THE BIRTH OF DEATHWISH, ONSLAUGHT & OTHER DIFFICULTIES: A chat with community modder Grimalackt and his creation of Deathwish, Onslaught, and Stormvermin Mutation; and how his mods have evolved over time.
4:00–4:30pmDEVELOPER INTERVIEW: MATS ANDERSSON Jsat sits down to talk to Fatshark’s own game designer Mats Andersson about Vermintide 1’s game design, and what it’s been like being involved in the Vermintide project since day one.
4:30–4:45pmCLOSER: What to Expect on Day 3 & Giveaway

SUNDAY, APRIL 18

TimeEvent Title
12:15–12:30pmPRE-GAME OPENER: ft. Ishka and Grimalackt
12:30–1:00pmNEED FOR SPEED: FULLMAP SPEEDRUNS Gotta go fast! jsat and SneakyPanda discuss the rush of fullmap speedruns, how they got started, and where the format is going now.
1:00–2:15pmNOSKIP SPEEDRUN SHOWCASE: Gameplay showcase of fullmap speedruns ft. Jsat, Zeropathic, Blohbloh, and ObviousTrash. Shoutcasted by Ishka and SneakyPanda.
2:15–2:45pmPLAYING ALONE: THE BIRTH OF TRUE SOLO: Community member, educator, and content creator Jsat speaks about the history of true solos in Vermintide 1, how they’ve evolved, and the pursuit of self-challenge.
2:45–3:00pmINTERMISSION
3:00–3:30pmNEW MODS, NEW CHALLENGES: Community member and modder VernonKun speaks about his current projects and mods to inject new challenges into the game.
3:30–4:30pmNEW MOD SHOWCASE: Gameplay showcase of selected new difficulty mods ft. Vernonkun, Oogy Wawa, inky_blackfire, and Old Joe. Shoutcasted by everyone’s favorite duo, Ishka and Grimalackt.
4:30–4:45pmCLOSER: Closing Remarks & Giveaway
