Happy 4-Year Anniversary!

Heroes, we‘ve come far and slain many a Skaven and Rotblood along the way. The 4-Year Anniversary of Warhammer: Vermintide 2 is now live on all platforms! Between March 8 - March 21 all players can play the celebratory level ‘A Quiet Drink’, and on March 15 the Quality of Life update will go live on PC.

Keep a close eye on our social media, PC launcher, and website throughout this week to learn more about the upcoming Quality of Life update, new Premium Cosmetics, and more!

Thank you from the bottom of our hearts for playing our little rat-smasher, Warhammer: Vermintide 2. We’ve said it before, but it deserves to be said again: your support is invaluable to us and we want to assure you that there are more great things coming!

Developer Stream

This Friday, March 11 at 2 pm CET, Community Manager Asqhy will be taking you through some of the goodies coming in the Quality of Life update on March 15.

Live on all platforms

The 4-Year Anniversary will run across all platforms from March 8 - 21. All future Vermintide 2 anniversaries will happen on all platforms at the same time. This means that the previous Xbox and PS4/5 specific events will happen simultaneously to PC on or around March 8.

A Quiet Drink

Follow the heroes out on the town in search of the famous Bugman’s XXXXXX. Let’s hope that the Skaven and Rotbloods don’t stir up too much trouble.

Quality of Life Update

Throughout the week you will find out more about the Quality of Life update. Which is our 4-Year Anniversary gift to you! The update contains features inspired by some of your favorite mods, new Premium Cosmetics, and more!

Stay up to date with what’s coming by following us on our social channels!
