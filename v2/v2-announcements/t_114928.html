Patch 6.9.0 Patch Notes

Features & Tweaks

  • Added a brand new level: ‘Return to the Reik’.
  • The Well of Dreams - Added more enemy spawn points in open areas.
  • The Well of Dreams - Slightly increased brightness for the entire level.

Fixes

  • [PS4 Only] Fixed the widespread crashes in the Weaves game mode - you can now play safely!
  • Bots now take multiple Area of Effect attacks into account when defending, instead of just the latest.
  • Improved remote player movement prediction on elevators - other players should look less jittery on them.
  • Chaos Wastes - The Citadel of Eternity - Improved terrain collision to prevent players from walking into the plinth.
  • A Parting of the Waves - Added an additional respawn point at a boss wall near the middle event.
  • The Well of Dreams Fixes:
    • Improved bot pathfinding throughout the level.
    • Improved ladders throughout the level.
    • Added missing hangledges.
    • Fixed an issue with enemies spawning above ceiling.
    • Fixed a bug where players joining mid-match could get stuck in the lower floors.
    • Fixed enemies sometimes dropping dead for no reason right before the middle elevator.
