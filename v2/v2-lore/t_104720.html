I was finally able to beat Shadow of the Horned Rat and its sequel Dark Omen.

For those not aware of these, they are 1995-8 RTS WH games, featuring our dear old Franz Lohner in his youth. Well, his identity hasn’t been entirely confirmed, but we do know he was in that outfit and that he has a flaming sword… so if you wanna believe he was just a member of the cavalry regiment and not its leader it’s up to you.

I’ll leave Dark Omen’s exposition to this absolute legend of a guy

It’s just as well, as I didn’t enjoy it as much as the first game. Lohner’s grumpier, possibly because he hasn’t been payed yet, and backtalks pretty much everyone, with the notable exception of his old friends and Karl Franz’s hot cousin (I do wonder why he chose Franz as his go-by name…)
The game is absolutely epic in scope: the Grudgebringers fight whole armies of undeads, and their kill count includes Manfredd von Carstein, one of Nagash’s lieutenants and the Dread King itself.
This time the Grudgebringers travel from the Reikland and the Border Princes to Sylvania, Kislev and part of the Northern Wastes, Bretonnia, Loren and end up in Araby for the final battle.
The mission intros are long and well done, but the game is perhaps too epic and much of the humor is gone (in the previous game whenever the elf would expose the plot someone would yawn or tap his fingers impatiently), and I felt the additon of your “old mentor” - whover that might be considering the detailed background provided in the first game’s manual - to be contrived.
Still, it’s canon, and our Lohner dear makes a casual reference to these events when speaking with Kruber: “I’ve been to Bretonnia. Had a trouble with the Black Grail, a few years back…”

Tbh, the WFBRPG 4th edition module that featured the U5 and Lohner still make me think that Lohner isn’t Morgan, but rather someone close to them.

Lohner having a couple of enemies that are trying to kill him for revenge:

image584×687 49.3 KB

  • Thikad Urgolsson – A Dwarf Slayer, Urgolsson took the Slayer oath after losing his family heirloom — the runeblade, Grudgebringer — in a game of Ranald’s Fingers. He now believes he was cheated by Lohner, and will not stop until the innkeeper is dead
  • Shaderipper - A Skaven Assassin wants the innkeeper’s head. When working for the Grudgebringers in Tilea, Lohner was a constant problem for the ratmen. The ratmens’ auguries suggest now is the time for revenge
  • Carmello Esposito - Carmello Esposito’s Crew work for Marchesa Generosa Caramanici of the Border Princes. Lohner introduced the Grudgebringers to Lord Carl Svennson, the Marchesa’s longtime rival, and for that crime he will die slowly
  • Tah-Ra Mentuhr – A Nehekharan Wraith This ancient creature has emerged from beyond the gates of time. It seeks to possess Lohner’s body, infiltrate the Grudgebringers, then murder the Commander before its master’s future plans are foiled

Here we have 3 interesting points:

  • New origin of the Grudgebringer, old one being meant to be a Magick Sword made by a Bright Wizard.
    → This can still work, by having the Grudgebringer being copied from the Dwarven Runes into the Umgak Magick.
  • Lohner has been active and caused problems to the Skaven (Somewhat confirm that he was in SotGHR)
  • Lohner isn’t the Commander, and the commander seem to be someone who will foil the Wraith plans (I don’t think that’s about DO, but rather the End Time, don’t think we know who the Wraith’s master is, but it’s likely Nagash still)

Hence my theory that Lohner was Morgan’s teacher, and previous leader of the Grudgebringer, and was helping as an aide de camp during the various campaign of the Grudgebringer.
