The archers aren’t supposed to be a threat on their own. They wear you down and interrupt you at crucial moments creating opportunities for other units do take you down. An arrow hitting you will interrupt attacks, mess up your aim, freeze you in place for a moment… all of which can leave you vulnerable to other attacks. The archers make combat more complex and I think that’s a good thing. Also a large group of them do become a serious threat, especially if you don’t have a ranged weapon that can easily return fire.

ZinStonehead:

And it is similar with Standard Bearers

These also add an entirely new dynamic to combat, namely the need to push far INTO the enemy lines to bring down the standard. It disrupts strategies of kiting or hold-position and requires new strategies and often teamwork to take them down.

ZinStonehead:

Not a fan of this either. This would cause contention and make finding multiplayer games harder; I wouldn’t want to join a host that has beastmen banned. Not to mention this opening the door for other people asking to ban the things they personally find annoying.

This mode would probably not have any EXP or chests at the end which would be ok. I think a mode like this would add a lot of replayability, I’d definitely muck around with it.
