Scenario One
Left side of brackets:
Community vs. Community

  • anyone can join but they need to sign up in order to participate

Right side of brackets:
Developers vs. Streamers

Scenario Two
Left side of bracket:
Streamers vs. Streamers

Right side of bracket:
Developers vs. Developers

This is basically just a rough idea. I’m sure Fatshark probably already has some sort of PvP event planned but if this idea gives them a BETTER idea, then I’m all for it!
