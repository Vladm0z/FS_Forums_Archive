I’d never try versus explicitly for the rewards. I gave it a second shot and burnt out within a couple days of spending my free time solo-queuing into quick play versus. We’ll either be squashing the enemy team or being squashed. It’s almost never a fair match-up. I’ve seen 4 stacks against 2 and 2 bots, or accounts with 1000+ hours on one side, be it mine or the other against brand new players. It’s not fun.

I watched a battle wizard brand new player overheat and die repeatedly until they quit within less than 10 minutes. Many new players don’t even know they can call in a Skaven horde, or never can because they never get an opportunity to do much damage since they’re new. You have to be more creative to get damage without a horde, especially on experienced players. Despite experienced hero teams being nearly unstoppable as they rush from objective to objective, there’s many brand new players who go straight to versus and consistently get under 30 points as the heroes, in many cases, 0.

The ceiling and floor for playing well as heroes is much greater than skaven, as a good hero team will steamroll on through with like 30 revives, and a bad one can barely get out the gate. I’d say only one in every 10 matches is a “fair game”. I’m going back to Hitman 3 until they sort this out, maybe I’ll show up for the campaign mode and chaos wastes, but if there’s nobody around, and I can’t find a match in my area, I’m just going to a different game.
