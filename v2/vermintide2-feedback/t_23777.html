I will preface this by stating that i do really like the Challenge part of the Book of Challenges, tasks and rewards present in there are quite fine.

BUT

Then comes the daily part. As we all saw we got 3 quests to start us off, neat stuff really and more loot to work towards. Imagine my surprise when i login today to see that daily “quests” reset has only given me 1 quest out of 3 and that each following quest would be given in another 24 hours.

Let’s be real, that is quite sad and uninteresting and doesn’t really compare to what V1’s daily section of the Bounty Board offered (6 tasks per day that give specific non-RNG rewards, even if overall lower quality ones).

I really wouldn’t mind getting a bit worse rewards for these daily quests as long as they didn’t trickle in at a snail’s pace. Also, what happend to temp buffs as rewards? They were quite nice in V1, IMO
