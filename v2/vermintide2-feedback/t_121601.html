Yes, that’s what inspired me. The Geheimnishnacht event made it night on the mission and gave the light a more gentle and natural color palette. I checked the lighting RGB values and they were all set to a fairly extreme green/yellow. The sun, the shadows, the ambient light, the sky and the fog all set to a sickly yellow green in the extreme.

Now, don’t get me wrong. I understand that Nurgle/Skaven green theme has to be there, but that doesn’t mean turning the dial up to puke green for every light source.

All I did was lower the exposure a bit on the first mission, and then it’s those 5 light sources. I changed the sun, shadows, ambient, sky and fog light colors for all 3 missions. I darkened the shadows and tried to pick less intense colors. A little color theory goes a long way. I used dark deep blue shadows with the greenish haze (or more black on the underground mission), and the green Skaven warpstone light sources unchanged, then a natural sky and sun color with more neutral ambient light.
