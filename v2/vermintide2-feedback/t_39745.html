Something is up with Assassins

12 Posts • 760 Views

zaru • 2020-06-21 17:58:47

Lately, assassins have been behaving extremely strangely. There are so many issues that I’m posting this in Feedback rather than Bugs.
They were always prone to behaviour that didn’t make sense, like super fast and super slow jumps, but the stuff I’ve been seeing recently feels… new?

  • On several occasions, I have seen Assassins move forward and then suddenly skip to a different location further ahead. Like an instant teleport.
  • I have also seen multiple instances of Assassins climbing and then teleporting to another area above that should have required another climb animation.
  • Sometimes I have seen Assassins literally APPEAR on top of players. I am not talking about missing their movement. I mean, literally, “assassin walks behind a wall and suddenly a friendly player is pounced” with only a single assassin in play.
  • Getting pounced the very same second as you hear “Yes Yes”.
  • Assassins randomly either splatting on level geometry (which is funny) or bouncing off it to hit players that should have successfully dodged them or never been jumped in the first place.
  • Way too often, they aren’t even facing you, start jumping and turn mid-flight. There is no way to predict this.
  • Often they fail a jump, hit the ground and INSTANTLY pounce a player without any wait time or a proper jump cue.
  • Sometimes they do their smoke bomb disappear act but stay in the same place, ready to attack again. This one’s definitely an old one.

Most of this cannot be attributed to connection issues. I am quite certain of that.

There are one or two strange behaviours with the other disablers but Assassins are just plain broken on every aspect of their implementation. I’ve played this game for 1600 hours, I can intercept normally behaving Assassins just fine, but no other special screws me over this often with erratic behaviour.
