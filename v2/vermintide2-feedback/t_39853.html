First off, I want to say I love this hero, I think he has some very interesting talents, the quest system is super cool and new, he feels possibly the least cookie cutter out of all of the sub-classes thus far (imo)

Both weapons are very cool looking, the skins on them are pretty fantastic, and they are also pretty darn good especially the Long-sword. The Riposte on charged attack is insanely cool, and honestly I wish more weapons had unique functionality like this.

The sword and shield option is pretty good as well, it has a fairly nice attack pattern and coupled with the crit instakill talent can be devastating in the right situation.

In terms of balance I think he has the potential to be an absolute melee powerhouse, with good sustain, and group buffs which should make him welcome in almost any party. The lack of range is of course a massive downside, but I think the damage output between potential insta kills and his boss damage via his active compensate accordingly.

I think a lot of the talents are viable, although a few stand out as exceptional for higher difficulties.
The double strike ulti can be insanely useful esp cause you can block cancel it, perhaps over the cleave which has sticking issues, and may not be as useful since you already have good melee cleave / stagger options.

His crit kill talent is insane, honestly one of the best talents in the game (stack crit or crit power and just dumpster elites for days)

I think this is a very balanced, well designed and cool hero, if the other careers in the future can keep up, I think this is a move in the correct direction for the game as a whole, and im very excited to see more. Kudos! Great Job this time FS
