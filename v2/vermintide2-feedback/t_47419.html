scribeuntitled • 2021-12-11 15:01:50

I would like to start this off by saying that I appreciate Fatshark incorporating the ability to see talents and weapons when holding Tab.

However, I find it absurd that it is only possible for your steam friends to see this information. In the new “Equipment and Talents Privacy” section of the settings, there are two options: “Friends Only” and “Private”. I don’t see the need for this to be a toggleable option in the first place, but regardless, it is.

The far greater problem is that there is no option for this to be visible globally. I genuinely don’t understand the thought process behind this decision. The vast majority of the time, people are playing with pubs in quick play. It completely defeats the purpose of implementing the ability to see talents and weapons, if you in fact cannot see them.

TLDR: Add an option to make the talents and weapons visible globally and make it the default setting. Or, remove the options altogether, and just make them permanently visible for everyone.
