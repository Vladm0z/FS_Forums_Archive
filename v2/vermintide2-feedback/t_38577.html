Huntsman is already strong in the right hands but has a very specific play style would like to expand on it for more fun and variety… even though not going to be implemented.

Suggestions & Ideas for fun adjustments, improvements and synergies. Numbers and not all talents could be implemented to keep things balanced.

Hunters Prowl:
Huntsman fades into the shadows for 6 seconds or until his next attacks. Gains 1 ammo and next range attack does massive damage (crit / 6x damage slightly lower than~Shades damage, can pass through enemies unnoticed, no long er bobs camera down)

Waste Not, Want Not:
Ranged headshots and critical shots recover 1 ammunition, 2 if both.

[Call Out Weakness] Hunter’s Insight:
Aura, increases critical strike chance by 5% (Double effective range of Auras)

Tier 1:

[Taste of Victory] Burst of Enthusiasm:
Ranged headshots & critical strikes grant 2 temp health, double if both.
(temp health being more temporary think we can reintroduce range temp health in general but based on certain criteria per range class)

Tier 2:

Maim:
First shot maims enemies, undamaged enemies are knocked down , damaged enemies are slowed in movement and attack speed by 50% for 3 seconds, can only occur once.

Tier 4:

Thrill of the Hunt:
After scoring a critical strike next shot counts as a headshot.

[Burst of Enthusiasm] Aim Small Miss Small:
Doubles effects of Waste Not, Want Not.

Tier 5:

[Shot Crafter] Eye of the Tiger
Critical hits grant 5% attack speed & movement speed for 5 seconds, stacks up to 2x.(plays eye of the tiger sound track while buff is active)

[Master Huntsman] Monster Hunter:
Increases monster damage by 10%, Killing a monster reduces damage taken by 30%, last until death.

[Longshanks] Hunter’s Bounty:
Killing enemies with ranged critical shots & headshots reduces cooldown of Hunter’s Prowl by 5%

Tier 6:

Concealed Strikes:
Hunter’s Prowl effects next 3 attacks (melee or ranged) before breaking stealth.

[Head Down and Hidden] Hunter’s Pace:
Hunter’s Prowl last 10 seconds and acts like live version.

…Criticize, Improve, Ignore or Enjoy!
