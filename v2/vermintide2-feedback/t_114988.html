There are often replies along the lines of “not an issue for me,” but that doesn’t really address the design concern. Saying it works fine for one person is anecdotal and doesn’t change the underlying problem.

The pickup hitboxes for items on the ship are very close together, so even when aiming at a barrel it’s possible to pick up a bomb or healing item instead. This makes the barrels harder to grab in the middle of combat, and that’s the design issue being highlighted.
