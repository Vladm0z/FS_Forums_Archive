alsozara • 2022-04-29 13:21:13

Here’s my take on Impetuous Knight. Movespeed is honestly underrated, as is it can already be a pretty valuable kiting tool, but the talent is held back by its awkward condition of having to spend your ult to kill something first to get it. I would flip the condition somewhat. Make it give +20% movespeed while readying the ult (ie holding F) with an extra small burst of speed upon releasing it. This could make it a very valuable burst movement tool for quickly closing distance on targets that can otherwise be quite threatening to GK eg fire rats, and could be used more naturally while kiting, having on demand movespeed at the cost of leaving yourself more open while holding ult.

If that’s still not enough to make people want to pick it it could get another small additional bonus like a small amount of extra damage or cool down. Honestly though I don’t think that would be necessary, it would already have a clearly defined role and give something unique and valuable over the other options. Honestly might be too abuseable if anything so move speed might need to be tuned down further or only proc on release of ult to avoid a situation where GK can just outrun all the enemies by holding f.
