Gorillian • 2018-03-26 12:37:01

A quick shot does less damage - but does it still penetrate clan-rats? That i can spam my uncharged shot into incoming hordes and penetrate a bunch of rats?

I believe so, but I mostly stick to my halberd against trash enemies.

Is it any good, to fully charge my Shot?

No. Always fire before the zoom happens. That said you cant also fire as soon as possible. Go experiment on a dummy and you’ll see that you need to hold it for just a tiny amount of extra time to get the full damage.

How do i deal with the abysmal weapon-swap-speed

As you see in this very clip I get tagged by a marauder when I kill a Chaos Warrior in close range. Just gotta rely on dodging and teammates I suppose. However, if its a critical situation (like stormsorcerer) you can drop aggro by using the active, this gives you plenty of time swap back to melee.

What are you recommendations on the Talents and Properties on Gear?

You need power vs skaven for either the longbow or your charm so you can oneshot globadiers.
Get the decanter trait on your charm (the triple-potion), the strength lets you oneshot chaos warriors to torso, the speed lets you get 4-5 shots off, and the concentration part is just enough to get another active for the same fight.
For longbow trait I use the conservative shooter alongside the talent from skilltree so I never have to pick up ammo.

Whats you personal favorite regarding melee-weapons?

Halberd definitely. Powerful against everything.
