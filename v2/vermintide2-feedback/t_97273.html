ExanimiaCommunity Hero • 2024-07-22 12:00:59

Hey there, I am deaf and have been playing this game for seven years or so.

In the modded realm, there is a mod by Zaphio called “Accessibility Captions”, but in live realm, there’s unfortunately not much that can be done until either a) Fatshark brings back active sanctioning and sanctions that particular mod, or b) implements a native solution, neither of which seems high on their list of priorities. I suspect the only reason spider sense only exists for Darktide is because Darktide has no sanctioning.

Unfortunately, players with disabilities and their needs still aren’t taken as a priority in most studios because we’re a “minority”, never mind the fact that at least 1 in 10 people have a disability, and an even larger portion use some form of accessibility option.

I’m afraid the only real answer is pairing yourself with a trusted hearing player and time. The more you play, the better of a sense you get where things might come from. I’m afraid that’s really the only answer, as well as keeping your “head” on a swivel, which unfortunately isn’t always possible. It is possible to play this game with hearing loss or deafness at a fairly high level, but you will be at a distinct disadvantage.
