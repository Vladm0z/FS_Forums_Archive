Never changed up to to day I think put the video on higher speed or klick around (I got bored).

How to get hyper density

  1. If they are near you they will attack you and keep attacking you no mater what. You can make your own hyper density stack by pushing and walking forward in a horde. (in that case you deserve a punishment of course)
  2. The waiting pools do not distribute mobs only to the ancient pools. While they do try to fill up in that order should the horde be large enough they will be distributed through you causing you to get rushed by a hyper density stack.
  3. They climb up ledges no matter how many are already there (together with their stagger resistance in that case can make them dangerous)

Knowing how hordes move helped me a lot in fighting them (especially in solo). Sticking to a wall will prevent you from being rushed and constantly cycling or moving in one direction will also prevent it (as long as you let them move to you do not jump into an blob you might have created).
