Hi,

I find it kind of difficult to extract any value from this talent when i’m playing with randoms, due to old man hands and reaction times, but considering that it’s dropping ammo on the ground instead of directly refilling Bardin’s ammo, it looks like the intent behind this talent is to be useful for the entire team. So with that in mind here’s my suggestion:

Current perk: after Bardin kills a special, he drops an ammo bag that refills 10% of ammo.
Potential rework: each time the team kills 4 specials, Bardin drops an ammo bag that refills 10% of ammo. Specials killed by Bardin count as two.

This would allow for a more consistent, albeit smaller, trickle of ammo drops, while rewarding good Ranger players without punishing bad Ranger players. Ale and bomb/potion drop chances from level 20 talents can be tweaked accordingly, but i think they would still be alright as they are.
