What hedge wrote is essentially the biggest stuff.

Things I know off hand.

  • enabled cosmetics
  • a lot of work into bots, like making them able to use their activated abilities for instance
  • 9 new maps
  • heroic deeds
  • red weapons
  • a lot of UI work on TLC to the crafting/inventory system
  • full rework/retweak pass on all the talents/passives
  • a lot of work on maps trying to make them more even in difficulty, for instance against the grain has been evened out difficulty wise (less rng)
  • bosses have been worked over
  • a ton of crash fixes, backend error fixes, minor bug fixes
  • it’s mentioned above, but damage to armoured targets has been reworked to avoid big damage spikes and some weapons being useless
  • sienna work (beam staff in particular)
    - spear work
    etc

there’s a lot even without going into minute detail

it is not as powerful anymore, but as a fellow spear user I have to say I think that it’s still strong and really fun to use. it’s just not that over the top anymore. Any weapon where you move faster by spamming wide arcing all-smiting heavy attacks maybe deserves some tuning

The main change now is that it relies more on you carefuly timing your dodges well to reach it’s full potential than mindlessly spamming heavy attacks to kill everything around you.

disclaimer: this is my personal review as a fellow player, I am in no way involved in the weapon balance process.
