Now there seems to be some adjustment in player-skill in Legend, I think one thing that might make things a little easier, without having to adjust massive amounts would be the resilience when a player is downed.

When a player gets downed, the window of opportunity for someone else to clear the area or dash in to help is very very small. Often players go from down>dead in a matter of seconds with little opportunity for others to even help recognise where the player is down let alone make a foray to help them.

I know we’re supposed to lumber around within two inches of each other, but this isn’t ever going to happen.

I think increasing the survivability of players once they’re down might make rescuing them a possibility rather than just dismiss people and move on.
