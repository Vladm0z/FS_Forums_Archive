Ensrick • 2025-11-12 16:32:31

Some rough math:

Each Emperor’s Vault gives 3 rolls at an 8% red chance, so the expected reds per vault are 3 × 0.08 = 0.24. That means about 4.2 vaults yield 1 red dust on average (since 1 red = 1 dust). The claim of “6–8, maybe 10 vaults” per red dust therefore undervalues vaults. The real rate is closer to 4 vaults per dust, making the reward not overly generous.

Of course, this is ignoring the possibility you get an item you want to keep from the Vault. So yeah, 1 red dust, not quite that extreme, but it is a big reward.
