Ensrick • 2026-07-03 22:13:27

I actually have looked at source code on how this works now that I’ve been making mods. The quickplay system uses an average of weights across players in the lobby who have never had a victory on a certain map. For each player that doesn’t have a win on a specific map, it applies a 10x modifier to it’s weight making it vastly more likely that you will get maps players in your lobby haven’t yet gained a victory on. There’s more to it than that, but by far, the biggest factor in getting lots of the same maps is when a player hasn’t yet had a single win on one.
