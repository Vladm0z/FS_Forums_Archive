After playing a little bit more and thinking a lot more, (I’m at the point where I would rather read forums than play the game) I’ve come to the conclusion that this game no longer promotes Aggressiveness.

edit: I should point out I mostly QP legend, so my game experience involves an unknown amount of latency as I am not hosting. Combat 2.0 seems to have exacerbated latency issues.

It used to be that you were encouraged to charge in to melee because that’s where temp HP came from. You wanted to dodge around enemies and swing your weapon because that was a fast and; IF SKILLED ENOUGH, safe way to kill enemies. Now the damage tradeoff is in favor of a loss of life when fighting hordes, the only real source of Temp HP is from Ambient Elites unless you have 1 of 2 weapons that can cheese stagger HP. Damage mitigation talents for Slayer and Zealot are gone, HP on cleave is gone, hell Temp Health for Waystalker Ult is gone, no more Bonus HP from Kruber’s Ult. HP is too precious to use fighting a bugged horde with no more reliable dodge.

The game now Heavily promotes a slower more defensive CC oriented playstyle. I find this considerably less fun. I’ve tried 4 classes and a dozen weapons, the only one that felt really effective was Sword n Board Kruber, but I just don’t enjoy the attack patterns of the shield.

I won’t belabor the dev’s with cries to revert things or give me the game back. But I would like them to understand the reason I loved this game. It used to be that the game rewarded you for going full LEEROY JENK-IIINS!!1!1 and who doesn’t love Leeroy? Fix the bugs then and give me back that feeling please.

I personally think tweaking Several numbers immediately would be better than waiting until the bugs are fixed. Put Dodge value back to near they were before, it was a mickey mouse fix to a netcode issue but it inadvertently led to amazing combat. Set THP on Cleave to what it was before. And give us the tools to endure the current bugged state of combat. I mostly ask this since I don’t expect to have these bugs fixed any time soon.

p.s. I don’t really play Sienna or Slayer, but that is a change you need to revert. The Fix is more broken than the “problem” was.
