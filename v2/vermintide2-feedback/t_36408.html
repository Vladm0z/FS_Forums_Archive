This isn’t about Huntsman being viable. It’s about how terrible and janky some of his gameplay designs are. See the points below:

  • When Huntsman uses his active(and this has been brought up before) he does this goofy duck for your view. You can clearly see though in third person that his character model doesn’t even flinch. It terribly affects a player’s ability to line a shot and make consistent hits. It’s also completely illogical.

  • Huntsman’s longbow is really awkward to use. It has three different stages and going into them is more annoying than fluid. He’s forced to go into his zoom every time you hold it for a short period which creates this irritation because your view is constantly zooming in and out, creating this jarring sensation for the player. If you want to keep looking down your sights, you can’t. Every time you want to fire an RMB arrow, it has to zoom out and then zoom back in. If you want to get more damage(the 1st charge of his RMB) but try to avoid the zoom issue, you have to play a minigame of “beat the zoom” with timing your shots just right.

  • Look at Huntsman compared to Kerillian’s Longbow. Kerillian’s Longbow is so smooth and enjoyable. Each shot just feels great and flows into the next, creating a crisp and clean experience. You can continue to look down the bow and fire charged shots, never breaking that immersion or your lock on the target. It holds what you’re looking at, allowing your eyes to focus and not be annoyed by the jarring yo-yo effect that Kruber’s Longbow has.

  • Kruber has a talent called Maim. It does 30% more damage to injured enemies. What’s an injured enemy? Surely an enemy that is lower than 100% hp? Is that not the definition of injured?
    Nope. It’s if an enemy has less than 50% health. Why is this talent so bad? The headshot talent blows it away.

  • His stagger talents are Bulwark and Smiter. Bulwark and Smiter. On Huntsman. Not even Assassin.

  • The talent Master Huntsman. 3% damage reduction which becomes 30% once the party kills a monster. When you choose this talent you’re gambling that you’ll actually get a monster early enough in the game to get any sort of value out of this talent. That’s if the run even spawns a monster at all. It could be at the end of the level. It could spawn no monsters at all. It’s just not a good design because there’s no sort of guarantee with it.

    • Bounty Hunter gets Job Well Done, which is 1% damage reduction for each elite and special kill. You know there will always be tons of elites and specials throughout the entirety of a level. You will never go a game where you can’t work towards the full power of the talent.

    • If Huntsman had a way to always work towards achieving a larger bonus for his talent, then it’d be fine. But its current iteration is rolling the dice on whether you’ll get a monster or not and is it going to be early enough in the game for it to matter.

I played a ton of huntsman with longbow.

There was a common complaint from the very release - “delayed zoom” for heavy charged shot. You charge the bow, pass normal charged stage, then go into heavy charged stage, some time passes, and only then the zoom kicks in.

It was a blessing for me. Because playing on 100 fov, i no longer was forced to endure sickening rapid fov changes every time i choose to do a heavy charged shot (which gets a lot of use).
Plus, it was fun aiming challenge - to headshot stuff w/o zoom.
Also it felt that hitting stuff is simply easier, especially fast moving stuff. Because with zoom, you have a moment when your fov transitions, during which your target leading is disturbed.

That’s why i didn’t liked handgun huntsman at all. The zoom is so powerful, while stuff you shoot usually hangs in close-med range. W/o zoom headshotting is impossible unless really close.

And at some point, the bow was blessed with a much earlier zoom, ensuring that if you want a heavy charged, you’ll get zoom.

The entire appeal of this weapon was lost for me
Early empire longbow was a breath of fresh air amongst other precision ranged weapons, a weapon that requred you headshots to excel, yet free of zoom nausea. It was a challenge for your aim, with proper reward.
