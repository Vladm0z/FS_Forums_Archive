HungarianPatriot • 2024-07-27 10:09:36

Several of Vermintide 2’s maps are too long for their own good, especially the newer ones and some of the DLC. But length alone is not the issue. Skittergate is also very long but it is, imho one of the best maps of the game. Why? Because the reward scales with the length, and it is pretty much guaranteed that you get an emperor’s whatever out of it even if you miss a book or two. The finale is also hard but not outright unfair BS. The problem is with maps that are not just very long, but also don’t give nearly enough loot dies to justify it, and have finales that wipe 90% of PUG teams, leaving people empty-handed after a prolonged and unfun session, or with a generals-garbage at best. Tower of Treachery, Trail of Treachery, Kaza-kaz -whatever Kazakhstan, Mission of Mercy, are the worst offenders here. Honorable mentions to Enchanter’s Lair, which actually does give good rewards, but the final boss is a guaranteed wipe without a coordinated and build-optimized team, and to Parting of the Waves, which is not particularly hard, but excruciatingly long, poorly rewarded, and plagued with horrid FPS-consuming lack of optimization, and Grudge Served Cold, which although long and poorly rewarded, at least does the courtesty of wiping most teams with the mid-event rather than the final one.
