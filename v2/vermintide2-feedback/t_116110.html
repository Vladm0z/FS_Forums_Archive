I love this game, but its getting harder to play for long sessions. I mostly play Legend, but i can Cata. Cata just hurts my wrist. Im an animation canceling, QQ-ing, bow double-tapping sinner. I have 3000 hours in this game and would like to double it.
But Fatshark needs to really deal with the issues in combat that have been really accumulating.
We have a big pile of band-aid fixes for making the game harder that have assimilated together into a festering abomination that really need addressing. Pull yourself up by your bootstraps!

  • Remove Gutter Runner Pounce Impact damage. 90% of successful reactions against pounces that ISNT dodging it result in a trade where the Gutter Runner just gets free damage due to latency. And the longer distance = more damage is what we call “overdesigning”, its bad get rid of it. plsthankyo.
  • Running Attacks shouldn’t be initiateable within 2 meters of a player. Otherwise the enemies get rewarded for raw input reading cus, for some reason, a running attack at that distance hits faster than a standing attack.
  • With how often Enemies chain running attacks, they shouldnt be tracking. It can be initiated with an arc, but once it begins it shouldnt change trajectory, though the enemy can decide when along that path to end it with a strike. Too often an enemy will slide around an attack or ranged shot mid-running attack and then still connect the strike, or just ice skate tokyo drift around like lunatics.
  • No swapping targets mid-running attack.
  • Keep the enemies from prioritizing players behind other players. Nothing like having a chaos patrol walk through you only to change its mind and now youre inside them and theyre inside you and youre dead.
  • Enemies should NOT be able to attack or running attack through each other. Any attacks that try should be canceled with a quick trample. This makes choke points viable and pushing enemies actually effective again, as they should be. Hordes should NOT have the advantage that they currently have in corridors, going up stairs, and through doorways. Its nonsensical, that’s supposed to be where theyre at their weakest. In traffic.
  • Enemies should complete their animations against a near-death players. Many times the game assumes a player is dead from an attack before giving them the ability to defend, like they would usually. The attack in question skips the latter half of its animation, resulting in a hit much sooner than it should.
  • Enemy’s should not be able to use climbing animations to avoid being staggered. At the very least they should, when they finish, be staggered a little or allow being staggered before initiating an attack. They should be at a disadvantage when they are climbing without a horde to back them up to eat some of the punishment.
  • Elites should not be able to push WHENEVER they want. Ive seen them prevent being staggered by doing a push before the stagger animation.
  • Monsters should be incapable of instant 180 degree turns when attacking. Ive seen some do a whole 360, though not instant in that case but still too fast. That glitching into the ceiling for a second bit also needs to stop, ive had a few headshots stolen because of that.
  • Instead of walking through or into each other, give units that are not horde fodder the ability to trample through lesser enemies. A good idea to make a distinction between slaves/fanatics and clanrats/raiders+ is that slaves/fanatics are not capable of running attacks.
  • Fix the range of enemy weapons. An egregious example is enemies with torches or small daggers. Every rat feels like no matter how their weapon looks, its a spear. Players are using visuals to determine distance so this hitbox consistency is actually bad here.
  • Nerf the butt out of monster sweeping attacks, like troll’s and anything involving chaos spawn’s tentacle arm. Its hitbox is laughably too large.
  • Fix enemies having longer attack hitboxes when on inclines. I know you know. I know you know we know. you know?
  • Fix enemies staggering forward through you when you hit them. Why are they rewarded with an easy potential buttshot when theyre getting whacked? Beastmen are particular culprits of this.
  • Beastmen headbutt is too fast.
  • Gutter Runners need a longer cooldown between chained pounces, as they should at the very least be facing the target when they pounce. Like, have them do a backflip or something that sets them in the right direction and THEN pounce.
  • Don’t let Gutter Runners pounce through monsters. Through other enemies i understand, but through monsters?
  • Don’t let Monster corpses eat melee strikes. Don’t let Enemies stay clipped inside living Monsters. Get em nudged or trampled out.
  • Fix enemies clipping through the floor of elevators? Like, come on gentlemen?
  • Enemies shouldnt be able to attack on frame 0 when spawned, like with Chests of Trials, Belakor, bolt of change, and Twin Blight.
  • Berserker attacks should actually be visually timed with their strikes? Ah yes, just block them. Until its a mixed horde of armour and monks. Until its a stream of 50 monks on well of dreams (yeah. that happened. Not an exaggeration, it was like 4-5 waves of over 10 of em each in that one area you know what im talking about). This ones been needed since release.
  • Enemies cancel their climbing animation into an attack quite often ESPECIALLY when coming out of spawn holes. Black rats frequently cancel it into an overhead.
  • Quit matching a person with 180+ping to a party under 100 ping. Quick way to ruin a perfectly good round and it happens ALL. THE. TIME. WHY.
  • Have parrying do something. Reward the player for parrying elites and above with a little staggering shockwave or something. Like the boon kind in Chaos Wastes. Or a chunk of ult bar. Or refill stamina.
  • Dont let enemies staggering players cancel weapon and item swaps.
  • Reduce the hitbox of Halescourge’s acid ghost bodies. Its only like 20% possible to dodge them. Many times youll weave nicely between and still get hit.
  • Make it so Grail Knight Kruber is immune to damage or something between the first strike of his ult and the second one. The game is too input read happy to not do this. “oh just time it right” yeah you do and then someone slips in a fast and cheeky 2 foot long running attack in a half second. Very funny.
  • Fix Saltzpyres flail heavy spamming, as sometimes it has a doubly long windup, and i dont think that’s intended.
  • Bots should probably do a little bit more than block, push, and get their heavy attacks interrupted. Just saying, im watching them contribute about nothing other than being aimbots and ult mules, leaving enemies alive so they can inconvenience your behind once more in a couple seconds. Id rather have the level 35 players on legend that dont know how to block (i mean it), heavy, or ult (youve seen em).
  • Ability to remove a certain map from your hosted quick play pool (COUGH Skittergate COUGH)

I think that covers just about everything? Id rather the game be difficult by being smarter, and not by simply becoming unrestrained from logic. You want more difficulty? Shuffle in some more berserkers, elites, and have more sinister special spawn placement. Probably keep the shield guys the same number though, oi. Like, just be more evil, not broken.
