Some find the Greatsword boring due to the bland moveset being mostly horizontal cleaves (1, 2, 1,2) or under-powered when playing on difficulties where it doesn’t shine due to a lower density of enemies.

I think I might have an idea that here that could keep it’s role the same and overall efficacy while making it more fun for both players who like the idea of a Greatsword but find it bland and something that will appeal to players who prefer it as is because most suggested changes would significantly alter it’s role and performance.

My idea is to simulate the feeling of momentum with each strike. I think this would pair well with a moveset that alternates between two right and two left strikes (Right → Right → Left → Left) and perhaps vice-versa for the Heavy strikes so that when paired together you have some control over the direction you swing from.

The Push attack combo idea is Pommel Strike (Quick) → Overhead (Normal) → Thrust (Slow)
Going from lower damage with stagger to higher damage with the thrust.

Using the compound interest formula for a multiplier to simulate accelerating momentum when multiplied by Power and Speed:

  • A is the amount modifed.
  • P is the stat value to modify for each (Attack Speed, Power, etc).
  • r is the increase per strike.
  • n is the number of strikes.

Setting P to 1 and r to 0.05 we get a multiplier that can be used to get values that increase per strike as follows : +0%, +5%, +11%, +18%…

This yield’s the following sequence for 4 consecutive attacks: 1.00, 1.05, 1.11, 1.18 – which can be used as multipliers to get a bonus after the first attack, and adjusted if it isn’t balanced.

The heavy and light swings paired with modified animations inspired by use of the Montante or Zweihander could enhance the visual feedback with swings that alternate between Right, Right, Left, Left or vice-versa using the momentum from the previous swing going into the next.

The current weapon’s moveset feels a bit too rigid and the back and forth swings do not feel natural the way they are viewed from first person.

From what I understand, animations aside, it’s probably not too difficult to mod the weapons stats and create an invisible stacking buff applied to Greatsword users to test out the gameplay.

Note: Not going for 100% realism, but if the animations and timing could be made to feel more natural, that alone would be a huge benefit to how the Greatsword feels. In the YouTube short here, you can see at the very start, he opens with a swing twice from the same direction by using swinging the blade overhead.
