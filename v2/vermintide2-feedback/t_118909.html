Darktide includes variants of weapons that with different movesets, stats, special attacks, and it creates a much greater level of replayability and room for experimentation. Just having variants of exisitng weapons could be so much fun. A Sabre and Pistol variant of the Rapier and Pistol, slower and higher damage, more for crowds than single-target.
