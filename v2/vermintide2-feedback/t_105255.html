Ensrick • 2025-02-16 15:00:54

With EAC, there’s probably not much you can do.

TAA is garbage. It can cause input lag and blurry motion. Even if edges look a little pixelated, I’d keep it off and use. There’s really no good alternative either. ReShade is terrible, I don’t know why people like it. A lot of games force TAA, for which there are some workarounds.

If your new PC is good, you can pretty much drag and drop SMAA into your game, and it’s generally much better than TAA: mrhaandi's crypto corner: injectSMAA
Though, this will also probably be blocked by EAC since it’s a binary the game didn’t ship with.

The best would probably be Super Sampling AA, but it’s pretty heavily reduces performance.
