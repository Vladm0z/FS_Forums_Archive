Flinlock • 2024-11-06 15:58:49
ZinStonehead:

There is only one special that need bigger rework and that is Blightstormer because they often cast from places where they can not be reached and players can not fight the storm.

They can always be reached if you stand in the storm circle before it fully forms. The Blightstormer has line of sight to where he is casting. Stand in the circle and look for the green lines leading away from the circle to the caster.

Thus there are two counters to him:

  1. Stay in the storm, find and shoot him during the cast animation before the storm forms
  2. Run like hell

Positioning is an important skill here too. Often Blightstormers seem “impossible” only because players allow themselves to get cornered in a small space with no escape first. Blightstormers play a noticeable span sound long before they begin casting, and characters will often call them out too. If you hear these things and you’re a) in a bad location without room to maneuver, and b) can’t try shooting him (out of ammo, melee only, etc) then do something about it (move to new location)
