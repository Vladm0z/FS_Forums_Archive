You can revive while not crouching. Is it that crouching helps with the hitbox size? I would believe it, but that’s not good design, and I didn’t even know about that.

Ledges have always been a poorly designed part of the game. When you cheese enemies off of ledges, they have no falling animation, and they float just over the edge for a moment before falling like its looney tunes; that, or they magically reappear back on solid ground.

This should’ve been fixed years ago or even before release; that or don’t allow falling off of ledges. As is, it’s not fully implemented.

I recommend allowing players to recover from falling off a ledge without assistance when there are no nearby enemies (who in the Ubersreik 5 can’t do a pull-up) and making the revive hitbox bigger. If there are no nearby enemies, I recommend allowing players to recover themselves anytime they are downed after a brief cooldown period.

For enemies falling off ledges, I recommend allowing them to pull themselves back up by grabbing and using the climb animation, but if staggered with more than one count of stagger, enemies fall off and enter a new falling animation or ragdoll as when dead. If enemies fall on solid ground and do not die from fall damage, they should use the get-back-up animation they do when they are staggered enough to fall and pick themselves up.
