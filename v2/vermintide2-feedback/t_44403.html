Chaos Wastes has host migration as a feature. The map reloads but all accomplishments are saved(boons, weapons, coins, etc.). You reload with the items and health you started with on that level.

As others have stated, everything you’ve accomplished to that point will be saved and all you need to do is rejoin. Ways to do that would be opening the Steam window while in game, View Players, and it should show recent players. The other would be to rejoin via the lobby browser.

For those wondering about abuse? I reckon you could. But the downside of some players trying to cheese is far outweighed by the majority just having their progress not completely erased over a long playthrough.
