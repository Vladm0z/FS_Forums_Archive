I think there’s enough greatsword enjoyers that it’ll probably never be changed.

On the flip side, they did tease a possible weapons dlc. Maybe there will be an alternative two-handed sword with a more dynamic move set. There are some Darktide weapons I feel could fit in Vermintide 2 if adapted.

The Dueling Sword as more of a Sabre as an alternative to the rapier and pistol on saltzpyre.

The Heavy Sword for Kruber and Saltzpyre as a sort of medieval Cutlass

The Tac Axe as a faster lighter war axe for Bardin, Saltzpyre or even Kruber.
