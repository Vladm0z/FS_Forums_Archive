I think the changes Hedge mentioned (different storage solutions) will come sooner or later. Time is passing and the overall number of items that players can own increases with every DLC and every batch of Emporium/premium cosmetics. Simultaneously, the overall amount of players that approach the current item cap grows. You just have to be playing Vermintide long enough to collect a certain ‘base stock’ of items.

In fact, last week I asked Fatshark support to delete all deeds from my inventory since I hit the item cap and couldn’t open chests anymore.

The breakdown of the 920+ items I owned:

  • deeds: 123
  • portrait frames: 131 (all available ones since launch, including one duplicate)
  • armor cosmetics: 96 (including all DLC/premium cosmetics, some Emporium cosmetics)
  • hats: 175 (including all DLC/premium cosmetics, some Emporium cosmetics, and like 10 duplicates)
  • blueprints: 90 (including all DLC weapons)
  • red weapons: 181 (I keep one weapon for each red illusion, and only a few spares occasionally)
  • red items: 125 (I keep 55 charms, 35 necklaces, 35 trinkets; most of them rolled for quick item exchange)
  • chest slots: 2 (one for commendation chests, one for unopened emperor vaults)

So in short, I’ve gotten close to hit the item cap just by owning more or less the base minimum of every item in the game, which is around ~800? as of now (not counting deeds). Only Fatshark knows how big of an issue this really is considering the entire population of players but it’s definitely an issue that gets ever more pressing the more time/items the game sees.
