This is more so me just brainstorming ideas for a hypothetical lizardmen skink character. I am aware this probably wont be added but hey its fun to speculate. Also note that some of these ideas will be rather “loose” as i am a new player so i am not too educated on the game mechanics. I understand if you arent making new characters for the game but you COULD maybe add them in a vermintide 3 release (if that ever happens).

General character concept/lore

The lizardmen in question is Kekton, a pretty standard skink who desired more out of his life. He didnt want to just stay in Lustria and protect the spawning pool, he wanted to take the fight right to chaos! So he ran away in secret, hoping to one day return home with a trophy of some sort proving his power to the slann mage priests and Sotek.

Character personality

Kekton would be the oddman of the group, as he would be extremely blunt, misunderstand metaphors and social cues along with being generally cold and reptilian in their emotions. He would also do things that many of the other cast members find unnerving such as licking his own eyeballs.

Careers

Red crest

The red crest is the typical berserker/melee career, using a mix of a spiked obsidian club and stone javelins for ranged combat. The passives are things that increase melee combat and rewards aggression. The career ability is “beastial rage”, causing the player to enter a state that boosts melee attack speed and movement speed. Killing enemies causes the timer to be extended, but without any kills it drains quickly.

Chameleon

The chameleon is a stealth/ranged career that uses an obsidian dagger for melee and a blow gun for ranged combat. The passives are poisons that can be applied with ranged attack that causes a DOT. The active is called “color change” that makes you temporarily invisible while allowing you to attack, but attacking in melee reveals your position.

Cultist of sotek

My personal favorite, the cultist of sotek would be an aggressive support caster that uses a set of magical claws for melee combat and a golden slab for ranged combat/magic. The cultist uses beast magic, with the the golden slabs´s main fire being launching a swarm of bugs that linger on an enemy while the alt fire launches an amber spear that pierces enemies. The career ability is “consume strength”, which causes all nearby enemies to suffer from a weakness debuff that reduces their attack damage while you gain a damage bonus for the duration. The passive ability is called “sacrifices to Sotek”, which reduces your overheat slightly when you get a melee kill.

Star priest

The star priest would be a sort of support caster, using the lore of heavens to smite foes with lightning and improving the odds of your allys. The star priest would use a wooden club for melee and a star staff for ranged. The ranged combats primary fire would be an arc of lightning that continues as long as you hold the button down, while the alt fire would be a chain lightning attack that hits multiple enemies. The passives would be things such as increasing the critical strike chance of you and nearby allys. The career ability is called “curse of unluck”, which causes the target to take increased damage from all sources for the duration and have a chance to miss their attacks, causing them to deal zero damage.
