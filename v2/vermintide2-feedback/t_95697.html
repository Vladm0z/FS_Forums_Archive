Spike_Nitros • 2024-06-12 07:22:17

In my books dual wielding hammers wouldn’t really fit for either WHC or BH; it lacks a certain refinement and finesse both classes have. Afterall, their whole thing is keeping enemies at an arms length using swift hit and run tactics. One could argue the flail and falchion+axe do overstep this a bit, but what is done is done. Looking at Bardin’s double hammers they definitely boost his ranged career crowd control way more than is appropriate.

1H hammer on the other hand would be (thematically) excellent fit for both. What better way to drive stakes through vampires’ chests for witch hunter or crumple crude bandit armor for the aspiring bounty hunter. 1H hammers are already usable for most other careers, so what’s two more.

Finally, I reckon 2H hammer would tip BH too much in the direction of a melee career. Again, sure, RV and HM have access to those, but Bounty Hunter is already a formidable close-range powerhouse with blessed combat and griffonfoots. Similarly WCH can generate a massive damage surge with the guaranteed crits talent, and combining that with said weapon could easily level a cata sw patrol in seconds. There’s a fine line between extremely fun and extremely overpowered, and the definitions overlap a lot.
