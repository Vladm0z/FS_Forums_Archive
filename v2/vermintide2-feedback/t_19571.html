Quality of Life suggestions to make things a bit easier for all players.

Illusions - keep collection in cosmetics section

  • It’d be great if once extracted we could keep illusions in the cosmetics section to be freely applied to any weapon. That’s easier to organize and less likely to be accidentally scrapped. It’d also make it so you didn’t have to pay 20 scrap every single time you switch it to a new weapon. Finally, it’ll allow you to better manage a large selection of all the illusions you’ve collected.

Melee crosshair - allow customization

  • it’s hard to see just the one white pixel. Allow simple shapes like a “+”

Rerolling Items even while it’s equipped (or allow a “fast” unequip item from all characters option)

  • Allow reroll/upgrade of equipped items (so it doesn’t require you to switch through potentially all 15 class/subclasses using it just to reroll/upgrade one item)

Stats Transparency

  • Show overall Character Stats and show stats for weapons and talents.

Feel free to note your own suggestions or great suggestions you’ve seen!

Edit: Love the suggestions that have been added so far!

QoL Improvements

1- Stats Page

2- More Stats
