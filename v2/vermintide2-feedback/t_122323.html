I’m going to have to hard disagree, because, once again, I played a lot of modded games with a friend who only plays elf and only plays Champion difficulty with my mods

We used my career tweaker mod, which is still in development. I added the Zealot THP conversion from TB as a toggle. Zealot has insanely high THP generation because of the talent giving +15% heal per 30 missing hp, and I had no issues with THP in the least. On Legend and Cata it was also fine. I think people convince themselves too easily of things they have never tried.

Also, what you’re suggesting makes even less sense, because if you’re experiencing THP depleting due to there not being enough enemies, that would be a bigger issue with your suggestion of making him immune to regen. Whether you make him exempt from regen or use the ability to convert the HP, you’re still having to take the green HP down to 30 or lower. The biggest difference I noticed, having actually played a lot with his ability: when I have team regen from Waystalker and GK, it helps when THP gets thin, in a way your idea does not, because the regen can be converted to THP, which actually makes it useful.

Furthermore, the convert on ability use lets Zealot players take advantage of Nat Bond and heal without losing THP. Just making him immune to regen doesn’t do that.

There’s one last thing I’d mention: if you were to force Zealot players to have no regen at all, or to always run the THP convert on ability use, you’d take away Mega Tank Regen Zealot. Getting GK regen and Waystalker regen just converts him into a super tank. In the Chaos Wastes I explicitly go for the regen perks like Nat Bond and regen 1 in 3, along with bonuses to healing, because it makes him damn near invincible.

Taking away regen or green HP forces players to play Zealot a certain way, so whichever way it gets done, I think it has to be something players opt into, whether it’s tied to a specific talent or some toggleable option.
