FatsharkLevCommunity Support • 2025-03-04 16:19:04

@MunKy there isn’t a dedicated/automatic update message, but we can put out a message via the in-game news ticker, which is the message shown on loading screens for important things - in this case alerting to the EAC update and sanctioned mods + Versus QP temporarily not working.

I put the ticker live at the same time as the update dropped, though it can sometimes have a slight delay, which I think is what must’ve happened here

It is also possible to go a step further and have a news ticker across the top of the screen while in the Keep/levels etc. but this is quite intrusive and we don’t like using this unless it’s critical.
