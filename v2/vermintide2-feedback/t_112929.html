Use case:
Solo host/all players are downed → only bot(s) left standing → bot(s) can’t save the mission and the last bot standing goes down → Miracle of Isha resurrects a bot → bot, being as braindead as they are, can’t save the player(s) → mission failed → the whole point of Miracle of Isha is wasted

Now, I’m not here to argue that “you shouldn’t at any costs allow such scenarios to happen” or "good players don’t go down”. All players are prone to mistakes. What I’m saying is, this miracle is supposed to compensate you for such a mistake once. However, it often happens that, if you fall, even three bots in your lobby are unable to save you because they are braindead and they often ignore healing, which seems like will never be fixed. The bots start falling one after another, and when the last one is resurrected, it won’t save the mission, but will only consume the miracle charge.

If instead, a last downed player gets resurrected, and bots are completely excluded from this miracle’s effect, then, even a mediocre player, has far far better chances to save someone else than a bot. And good player has every chance to save the whole mission. The last standing bot, in turn, reduces the chances of good outcome to zero

The Chaos Wastes still has a lot of “really cool” scenarios that for me, make this miracle entirely useless:

  1. You get knocked off a ledge with no ability to recover.
  2. “Striking down enemies” means stagger, but does no damage to them. Surrounded? Good luck!
  3. Activates on a random downed player/bot, not the last downed player.
  4. Isha’s bells toll, and… nothing happens. Literally just didn’t work.
  5. You’re the last player alive, but you’re also wounded, so when you die, you can’t be resurrected.
  6. Due to Morr’s protection bug when two players have Morr’s Protection and are downed together, it simply won’t trigger, and you’re stuck invulnerable and downed.
  7. It activates on a player/bot caught by a disabler, not the last downed player.
  8. Even if it was reliable, how often do you actually need it? How often is this Shrine right before a difficult mission, where the team might struggle? 50/50? And let’s say it might be a difficult mission, how often is the res’d player not going back down immediately?
  9. And of course, it activates on bots, which often prove useless.

There are probably more scenarios I didn’t cover, conflicting boons, etc.

I’ve seen it proc many times, and I can only recall one exceptional case where it actually proved useful, and it was because I went down as zealot to a roaming enemy poking me in the back while I was trying to clutch.

In every other instance where it worked, the player it res’d was unable to do anything. Beast banners, a horde, elites, multiple disablers, a grudge marked mino. Usually when it’s over, it’s over.
