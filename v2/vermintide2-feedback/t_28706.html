Fatshark knows that a lot of people don’t like how Shieldvermin are implemented right now.
So they made this change in the balancing beta.

[PC] Vermintide 2 - The Big Balance BETA
  • Stormvermin with shields should now stay “open” for longer/more hits once their guard is broken.
  • Stormvermin with shields should no longer block attacks from invisible enemies.

This is not how i was hoping they will be changed. But when i was asking in the balancing beta thread why ssv where changed the way they are, Hedge replied this.

[PC] Vermintide 2 - The Big Balance BETA

Not the exact reasons no, but experience tells me it’s an easy way to make them more manageable without diving in to more complex code. If it’s not the right change though in general, we can look further in to it, but this is an ok first step in the journey to balance.
