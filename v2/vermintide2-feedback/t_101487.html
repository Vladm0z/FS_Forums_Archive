First off, congratulations on expanding the game with new content like Versus. I’m sure there will be many players who appreciate this addition. However, I want to express a concern shared by players like me who are solely interested in the PVE aspect of the game.

While it’s great that you’re offering more gameplay options, the integration of PVP elements into the core PVE experience is problematic. Specifically, I don’t want to see any PVP-related features unless I actively choose to engage in that mode. This includes the display of PVP ranks or any HUD elements related to PVP while I’m in the PVE environment. It should be self-explanatory that if a player has not entered the PVP realm, they shouldn’t be subjected to reminders or details of that mode.

My suggestion is straightforward: please ensure that all PVP-related elements are entirely hidden or rather disabled when playing PVE. For those of us who do not wish to participate in PVP, these elements serve as an unnecessary distraction from the immersive PVE experience we enjoy.

Thank you for considering this feedback. I hope you take this into account to enhance the experience for all types of players.
