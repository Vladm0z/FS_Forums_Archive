Enhancement Suggestions for Razer Chroma Support

1 Posts • 206 Views

A.NO • 2023-11-06 09:57:48

Recently, FatShark released an update for Vermintide 2 that includes support for DLSS. This has boosted my confidence in the game’s development. However, I’d like to make a request to FatShark for further improvements in Razer Chroma integration.

Previously, Razer Chroma support in the game was limited to brief lighting effects when using items, with only the WASD keys displaying a constant red hue during regular gameplay. This somewhat detracts from the overall visual experience and can be perceived as monotonous.

Here are some specific suggestions that I hope FatShark can consider:

  1. Expanded Lighting Effects: It would be fantastic to see a broader range of Razer Chroma lighting effects to make the game world more vibrant and engaging.

  2. Game Event Integration: Syncing Razer Chroma lighting with in-game events, such as changing colors when a player is under attack or gaining rewards, would greatly enhance immersion.

  3. Customization Options: Providing players with the ability to customize their Razer Chroma lighting preferences according to their personal tastes would add a layer of personalization to the gaming experience.

We believe that implementing these suggestions can further elevate the gaming experience and demonstrate FatShark’s commitment to delivering a captivating experience to its player base. We appreciate your dedication and your willingness to listen to the community.

We eagerly anticipate your response and future game updates!
