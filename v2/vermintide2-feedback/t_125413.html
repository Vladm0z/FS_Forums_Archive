DOOMWHEEL - Monster
Role: Mobile Siege Engine / Area Denial Tank. It doesn’t chase one player - it controls the whole arena.

Doomwheel - Monster Portrait
Close-up. Inside the warp-cockpit.

A Skryre Warlock Engineer, sealed in. His face is pressed against a cracked, green-glowing glass viewport.

The vox-mask is fused to his snout. One eye is a glowing warpstone lens implant, flickering. The other eye is wide, bloodshot, terrified. Warp-fumes have burned all the fur off the bottom half of his face. Green drool.

You can see his claws gripping two rusted control levers.

Behind him - out of focus - the spinning warp generator, crackling.

The whole portrait is lit from below by sickly green warp-light, with sparks and grease on the glass.

Name under it: DOOMWHEEL

It tells you instantly: This isn’t a beast. It’s a rat who built his own coffin and is driving it at you.

Doomwheel’s theme would be: Skryre Engine Horror.

Base idea: A runaway warp reactor.

No drums at start. You hear it before the music even kicks in.

Intro sting - When it spawns:
Just a high-pitched WHINE of warpstone charging, like a Tesla coil, and the screech of metal on stone. Then a distorted Skaven warning klaxon - WEE-WOO WEE-WOO but broken and warp-distorted.

Combat Loop:

Foundation: Constant, grinding, off-rhythm metallic wheel clank - like a train about to derail. Not tribal drums, but industrial piston percussion. CHUNK-chunk-CHUNK-chunk
Over that: A fast, arpeggiated warpstone synth - green lightning sound made into music. Jesper Kyd’s signature Skryre stuff but faster, more panicked. 160 BPM, like it’s over-revving.

Stats (on Cataclysm):

Health: 2,200 - Between a Bile Troll (2000) and a Minotaur (2400). Tanky.
Armor Type: Super Armor on the wheel + frame, Warpstone (like Warpstone) on the generator. Resistant to melee except from behind.
Speed: Slow rolling speed - 80% of a player sprint when advancing, but 150% speed on a RAM CHARGE.
No stagger: You cannot stagger it by shooting. Only by hitting the generator or killing a driver.
Attack Pattern:

  1. Warp Lightning Beam (Primary - Siege Mode)
    Doomwheel stops, braces, and fires a sustained 4-second beam of warp-lightning from its front cannons. 40m range. It sweeps slowly left to right.

Deals heavy damage over time, drains stamina, warps your screen green. If you get hit for full duration = down.
Tell: Loud crackle + laser sight. You MUST hide behind cover / a pillar. Classic Plague Marine mortar moment.
2. Ram Charge (Movement)
Lowers its front and charges forward 20m, grinding anything in its path. Like Minotaur charge but wider.

If it hits a wall, it gets stuck for 3 seconds - this is your DPS window on the generator.
3. Grind / Crush (Melee)
If you’re close to the wheel itself, the wheel’s warp-blades chew you. Constant low damage + bleed.

  1. Overload - Mis-fire (Death / Low HP Mechanic)
    At 25% HP, the warp generator overloads. The wheel starts sparking violently and zaps everything within 8m. After 8 seconds it EXPLODES, dealing huge damage to hordes and staggering all monsters, but leaving a 15-second warp-poison field.

High risk/reward: Do you kill it near enemies for the explosion, or kite it away?

Weakspots:

Generator (Center): Takes 3x ranged damage. Break it and it stops shooting beam.
Drivers (x2): Kill both Warlock Engineers on top - it loses steering and starts rolling randomly / crashing into walls.
