Ensrick • 2023-04-24 20:19:40

I think I’d like an entire 4th column of new talents added for free. I don’t know that many people would buy new talents.

I second the new weapon packs, and also, weapon cosmetic packs to complement existing outfits and cosmetic DLC outfits. The benefit of a new weapon pack is that it might open up new build options, and synergies and add more variety to the gameplay. Trying out different weapons on different careers and seeing what works well and how it plays is where so much of the fun in this game comes from.

The thing I most want for DLC would be an Undead faction where Sofia makes another appearance and a new Undead boss.

Another DLC I would like is a Chaos Wastes chaos factions DLC that adds new alternative chaos factions. Right now, it’s only Nurgle followers, but seeing Tzeentch and Khorne chaos warriors, monsters, specials, and chaos marauders in areas cursed by Tzeentch or Khorne would be very cool; along with a chance to see them in non-cursed maps, with new rewards, frames, and outfits for defeating Nurgle, Tzeentch or Khorne enemies.

A DLC that takes everyone to another location such as Bretonnia or Lustria with Clan Pestilens-themed Skaven or perhaps in Kislev with Clan Moulder-themed Skaven as well as the other chaos factions mentioned before.

There are really so many cool places, factions, and variety in the Warhammer universe that aren’t really explored in Vermintide; possibly it also takes place in the End Times which to me is one of the less interesting parts of Warhammer. If they want to add something vibrant and new to the game, there’s a lot to choose from. New sights, new enemies, and new plots keep the game from getting stale too. It may even be an opportunity to add entirely new characters, maybe even new playable characters with their own careers and DLC careers.
