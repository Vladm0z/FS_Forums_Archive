Ensrick • 2026-06-03 16:21:04

Javelin (like Moonfire) is genuinely great with Handmaiden, Shade, and SotT: a solid power buff from the Hunter trait, hits the special breakpoints you need, staggers Chaos Warriors, spams freely, and gives you serious mobility plus melee damage. It hasn’t been “nerfed into oblivion”; I swap between Javs and Moonfire on Handmaiden quite often, leaning Javelins only because headshots return ammo and let you spam heads indefinitely in certain boss fights like with trolls.

But this “buff” to throwing axes gutted their best trick on Slayer. They used to benefit from Trophy Hunter stacks, which gave the damage to clear body-shot breakpoints on specials with ease. Take that away and the buffs it was given don’t come close to making up for it, so for Slayer this is a nerf dressed up as a buff. The intent seems to be an unlimited-ammo option for Ranger Vet… except he drops ammo, so what’s the point? I guess to make it easier to use and more accessible to newer players since it has a smoother arc, and reloads a little faster.

Pre-update, I used throwing axes on Slayer 90% of the time. They took effort to master, but with Trophy Hunter they picked off specials effortlessly mid-horde and headshot-spammed Chaos Warriors and monsters just like Javelins. Now they’re slightly worse, still a decent special killer, but the thing that made them shine is gone because they’ve simply made them easier to use but worse at hitting breakpoints on Slayer.

Also, I don’t believe throwing axes and javelins ever had the same damage. Throwing axes always had more damage, lower penetration and a slower arc with greater damage falloff. They also don’t have melee, reload in a cluster with a slow wind up almost all at once rather than one at a time. They’re just different weapons, if you wanted the Javelins to deal the same damage, then you’d have to take away all it’s other advantages and practically make it a clone of throwing axes, and I don’t think anybody would really like that.

The Javelins are still easier to use, and hit headshots well. You can use them on all 4 elf careers, which allows you unlimited ammo on a secondary ranged weaopn which always pairs nicely with Hunter for a decent fairly consistent damage buff.

The throwing axes are only available on 2 careers, and they’re still more of a meme on Ranger Vet. It’s main utility was offering Slayer a way to deal with specials, and that’s been nerfed by taking away it’s synergy with Trophy Hunter. However, it’s a good bit more accessible now to newer players with the extra shots and smoother arc.

While I think that jav was a bit overnerfed, imo Jav shouldn’t have breakpoints of Throwing Axes, because Elf ranged weapons are very different to Bardin’s.

To keep it brief:

On Slayer, it competes with a secondary melee pick, which is a big deal.

On RV, it competes most directly with:

  • Handgun - with 1-Shot BodyShot special + sv/bestigor bps, plus easy long range accuracy and hitscan projectile
  • Crossbow - which matches TAxes for 1 SBS vs ratling/assassin (can’t bodyshot hook), but has better headshot bps vs elites + easier long range

There is a few more things to consider, but I want to avoid creating too much of an essay.

If you compare it to elf, 1 SBS bps vs Ratling/Warpfire/Hook is NOT reached by ANY ranged weapon EXCEPT for Shade’s Volley Crossbow.
That alone imo, would make it a bad (balance-wise) addition to Elf - an INFINITE ammo weapon with high cleave (good vs disablers in hordes, among other things) and BODYSHOT breakpoints vs most specials, I think could contend for the no. 1 common pick, especially on HM/Shade.

So, on RV, you have infinite ammo for all weapons and other 1 shot-bodyshot special sniping weapons. On Slayer, it competes with melee as a secondary.
On Elf, I think it’d become a spam weapon vs specials/elites that’s easy to use and very obnoxious to everyone else (exactly as was the pre-nerf jav).
Current Jav isn’t spammed at everything because it severely lacks cleave and damage vs elites.

Imo Jav has an okay spot in balance right now - in comparison to the Longbow, you get the same special sniping bps on close range, worse vs long range (+more difficult), and much worse vs elites - if you play as a more melee-oriented build, these flaws are somewhat excusable.
In exchange, it’s more spammable (infinite ammo), has high stagger, very good dps vs monsters w/melee and actually good movetech.

I think it’s a solid choice on HM (very good on standard Twitch), and alright on Shade.

Side note: That 20% PvsMonster/Skaven for bps on Jav matches very well with its melee dps vs monsters - that’s +20% damage vs all monsters and +40% vs skaven monsters.
