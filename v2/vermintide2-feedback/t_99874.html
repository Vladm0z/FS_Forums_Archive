  • Extra monster: Mission will have 1 extra monster .
  • Double monsters: Mission will have the double number of monsters.
  • Double HP for monsters: obvious.
  • Max time to end: Finish the mission in X time.
  • Faster elites: Elites are X% faster.
  • No ultimate: Players can’t use their ulti.
  • Auto-exploding bombs: If you carry a bomb, there is a low probabilty of it exploding by itself, damaging you.
  • Vampire mode: Players lose HP with time. Kill enemies to heal HP.
  • Hot potato: There is a special object, players must have it when they end mission to win. But they can only carry it for X time, when that time ends, if player doesn’t give it to other player, it will insta-down.

Those are some ideas Fatshark. Please consider them.
