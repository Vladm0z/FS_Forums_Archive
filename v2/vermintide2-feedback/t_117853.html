Title: Griefing, Rejoining After Kick, and the Lack of Temporary Host Bans

Hi Fatshark Team,

I’ve been playing Vermintide for almost two years now, and I genuinely love the game. It has become normal for me to play exclusively on Legend or higher difficulty, and I really appreciate the challenge and teamwork the game usually offers.

Unfortunately, I’ve encountered a serious issue that can completely ruin the experience: players who intentionally grief cannot effectively be removed from a game.

Here is an example of what happened today:

After a long day, I started the game and joined a random match as Victor, the host was playing as Kruber. After a few minutes, a third player joined as Bardin. As soon as he joined, he ran off alone and died almost immediately. He then became extremely angry at the team and started writing insults in the chat.

He got even more frustrated because we took his potions while he was dead — but at that point, we had nothing left, and we were dealing with a boss and a horde at the same time. After we killed the monsters, we kicked him, since there was no reason to join a game just to insult the team.

One minute later, he rejoined the game and started being abusive again. We kicked him again. He rejoined again. This happened 4–5 times.

Eventually, he rejoined and wrote something along the lines of:
“You choose war, so that’s what you will get.”

After that, he began intentionally killing the team by body-blocking and punching us. During all of this, a massive horde was attacking us, making it very difficult to kick him immediately. We eventually managed to kick him, but it was too late — Kruber was dead, the run was lost, and we failed the mission even though we were almost at the end.

This was extremely frustrating. In the end, we had to set the game to private and start over.

It really doesn’t feel right that a player who gets kicked can immediately rejoin the same host and continue griefing. There should be some kind of protection against this behavior — for example, a temporary timeout that prevents a kicked player from rejoining the same host for 1 hour or even 24 hours.

Please consider implementing a system where, when a player is kicked, they cannot rejoin that same host for a certain period of time. It would greatly improve the experience for players who just want to enjoy the game and play as a team.

Thank you for your time, and for continuing to support a game I truly enjoy.
