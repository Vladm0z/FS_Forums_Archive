Just returned to the game after a fairly long break and was going to play huntsman kruber with the longbow (where I have the most hours in the game) and found that the zoom had been changed away from the right click to aim. I appreciate that many people probably appreciated this change but for me this was really jarring, clunky and feels frankly unplayable. I have a lot of hours using this weapon the way it was before and it felt much more natural, after trying to rebind things I found there is no way to rebind it so both the zoom and the block take up right click without the block taking precedence and the zoom not occurring and my muscle memory is too fixed to be able to use it on another button. Additionally using another button has a couple other issues, 1. you have to right click then press another button which is clunky and annoying. 2. the timing of the old way where it was automatic was very specific and useful and trying to manually time it to have the same effect does not work as a human doing it is too slow for it to help aiming in this regard.

I appreciate many people will have probably liked this change and not want a shift back so I want to be clear I’m not asking for a default change to how it was, but for me and if there are any other players like me I would really appreciate just an additional setting in settings to allow the rebinding of the bow to have the old zoom in on the initial press and hold of right click that could be toggled on. I know it probably doesnt seem like a big deal but for me I genuinely cant play with the new change in the slightest as all my muscle memory and aiming is too tied to how it was. If you have read this far, firstly thank you for bearing with this request and also please consider this change as it would mean a lot to me.
