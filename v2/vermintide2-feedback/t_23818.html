  • To provide an immersive experience which will depict and showcase the warhammer world in a justly manner

  • To provide challenging and interesting gameplay which will push my skills to the limit and make me actually happy for playing it

  • To provide at least 5 weapon skins per weapon per career and at least 5 U N I Q U E skins per each career and 5 unique head cosmetics per career. When I say unique, I don’t mean just a recolor of an existing classic skins.
