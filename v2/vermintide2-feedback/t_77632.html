Adelion • 2023-03-29 21:23:51

No, no skipping necessary. It is soloable and can be done without teammates.

Obviously, do this on Recruit as even playing Veteran gave me a boss each run and one run even twice (on Veteran!). Also, it is 80 % Trolls just to make your time more miserable. Also, it should be doable with several careers. I have read succesful runs for Handmaiden, Slayer and Grail Knight. Personally, I did it with Zealot chosing the two movement speed talents (5 % per stack and 30 % when hit). An additional 5 % on the Trinket helps too. In addition, equip the Billhook and use excessive air-w*nking to get faster. You don’t necessarily need to stop to get some stacks. Latest at Sofia’s dialogue, you can get all six stacks by going into the room on the left with the cirlce (it opens during the monologue). There are some random elements like potions or bosses but it should be reproduceable. Also, in case you didnt notice (because i didnt in the first 10 runs), when dropping down the ledge first time there is a symbol on the right side. Remember it, to not waste time for the light bridge part.

The final itself, takes about 3:30 to 4:00 minutes. So if you have taken already 9 minutes to get there, you have no chance even with a perfect run. Also, pay attention to the blue skulls to not waste your throws.

Other than that, there has been a bug - and maybe it is still active - that joining the game inbetween counts for the challenge as well. So joining shortly before the end makes this a very easy experience.
