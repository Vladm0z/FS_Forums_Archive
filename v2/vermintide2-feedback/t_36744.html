So it appears than some people do like unlocking stuff.
For now, thoses are mostly frames, in the end game.
Now, we could probably see some largers quests to unlock some “meaty” stuff. Something that is not cosmetic only.

Here are some suggestions :

Possible new rewards =>

  • New careers as long term goals >

Yeah, locking careers behind goals would seems a bit too much at first. But it’s a lenghty goal that could indeed keep people interested in, as the reward is a whole new career, which is a very impactful reward. It should not be “too hard” to unlock, but it should be a lengthy quest.

  • Legendary weapons >

Besides the red chase, where we can’t really have an influence on, we could see a new level of rarity, the legendary weapons. Which could be mostly crafted by some of the rarest material found out there during our games.
Some material so rare that it is provided as a thanks during some specific missions or modes.
Not only this would draw people on others modes of play than quick play (weekly challenges or deeds), but it would also make another incentive to chase.

Now what would thoses weapons be “legendary” ? Well, to prevent them from being merely nice skins, or repurposed “reds”, you can make them with a fixed trait that are and usable only for a specific career (not character).
This specific trait would dramatically change the way of play your character, by changing the usability of the ultimate for example (this would be the same ultimate for simplicity purpose, but with a kinda changed effect, for example getting out of “infiltrate” with the legendary dual dagger as a shade would aoe poison instead of single target increase damage).
As it would be an alternative way to play rather than a more powerful one, it would be a desirable goal.

  • Legendary skins >

If the matter proves to be impossible to add, they’re still the “somewhat” easy solution of the legendary skin. But it’s still better than a frame.

Quests improvement (on how to keep the chase interesting) =>

  • This is probably the hardest part. How to provide a quest that is lenghty, and that is meaningful (not some “do 100 games with” ONLY).

  • A lengthy quest is usually an amount of several little quests. By providing littles quests instead of a pretty long one only, you provide milestones. Short term goals that players can focus, instead of something that doesn’t end that will bore players before it’s finished.

  • Of course thoses short time goals should also reward players, but with meaningless stuff, like frame (it’s okay, as long as the “end goal” is meaningful). You did understood that part a bit when you did make the “deed challenge” and “weaves challenges”, but you lacked the meaningful stuff at the end (and you also made bigger gaps near the end, which often make the last part very grindy and boring).

  • A short “Kill this lord” and “Kill 30 chaos warriors” could be done as a milestone. It is okay. As long as the next point is met.

  • Some (but not all) quests should be original, like “Don’t use your ult for a whole level” (which is probably not a good idea, but it’s an example).

  • Quests should not be random like “Kill an assassin when he’s doing a backflip after being pushed and burned by a flamethrower in Convocation of Decay”. This can’t be a goal.

  • Some quest COULD also be “event”, like “You’re poisonned until this quest is done, find some water to heal yourself” (but it could also means it should be toggleable, as to not “block” people until the quest is done).

  • Seasons as in the various Seasonnal pass we can see from f2p could also be interesting, never providing “exclusive content” but at least a way to get nice content (that is made available later in emporium and/or dlc) by doing quests, and leveling up your seasonnal pass. Minion Masters is a good example of this. (but it’s a very generous F2P)
