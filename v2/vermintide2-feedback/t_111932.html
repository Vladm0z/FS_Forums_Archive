Ensrick • 2025-08-27 03:36:19

Oh, I haven’t checked there. I just noticed a few months ago I wasn’t able to find a match, and now it’s completely dead.

The only cosmetic DLC I didn’t get was the Versus rats, because I wanted to see if this mode would even work.

It was fun while it lasted, but they never were serious about making versus good.

There’s a ton of feedback about how unfair the game mode is, especially without matchmaking, and with snowball mechanics. The hero team with recruit difficulty and no weapons to optimize made it so that only a handful of careers/builds were worth a damn.

The worst thing was when teams of blue to orange ranks like a group of friends would curb stomp everyone. When you’re taking damage as the heroes, or dealing more damage as the rats, there’s more hordes, thus making it even harder for the heroes and easier for the rats. Conversely, if you’re unable to deal much damage to a fast moving team of good hero players, as the rats you get longer respawn timers and you get no hordes to call in. This makes it so that the imbalance of skill between teams and between teammates is massively magnified.

Basically, if you’re new and suck at versus, as the heroes, you’ll face constant hordes with elites, and as the rats, you’ll rarely get to play because of respawn timers and the heroes unhindered by hordes will just fly through the mission.

If you’re good at versus, and a seasoned player, you’ll just rush through the mission sniping the enemy players or even ignoring them taking little damage.

Then as the rats, this is where I had the most fun just sadistically torturing new players with rat traps.

Also, people could just drop out and so 9/10 games was just a lobby emptying right away.
