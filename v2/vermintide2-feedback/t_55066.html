Tamren • 2022-11-19 15:40:41

This is mostly an experience issue with players not knowing the items spawns. Levels tend to have many item generation spots and some are closer to the path than others. If your party is in good shape or already has a well stocked inventory you can simply ignore these spawn spots and quickly progress through the level. If you are struggling and need supplies badly it’s up to you to decide if the time to take a detour is worth it.

If people rush ahead, well, you can’t fix stupid. It’s frustrating when I walk five feet off the direct path and find a chest with not one, but two healthkits in it, plus a loot dice, only to see that my entire team has already crossed a one way drop. All you can really do is ping items to show people where they are and hope they remember for next time. If they have the desire to get good at the game, they will.

Regarding the ranger grenade talent, that one is down to pure RNG. Sometimes you get all ammo, sometimes you get all potions, sometimes you get all grenades. There is no “trick” to make it work a certain way, though from personal experience I can tell you that the number of specials rises dramatically on higher difficulty levels and in certain scripted events like boss or finale fights. Especially on CW maps with the +specials modifier. The more specials you kill per map the more rolls you have at getting a grenade.

If your team is experienced they will ask you if you have the level 35 bomb dupe talent, or just assume you have it and pass you the first grenade they find if you don’t have one already. Rangers are also the ideal member of the party to not carry a tome because of the inherent 10% chance to dupe items on use.
