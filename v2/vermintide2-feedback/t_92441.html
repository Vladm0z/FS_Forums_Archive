After the ‘keepsake’ healing potion is used, everything is in phase two of the system, where either a healing potion OR medical supplies (still stackable x2) is equipped, but the slot for the ‘keepsake’ potion is not able to be filled with a healing potion any longer. lmk your thoughts.
