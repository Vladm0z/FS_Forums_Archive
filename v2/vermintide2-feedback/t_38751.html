BighatMarcus • 2020-03-10 09:15:52

Ranger veteran is, in my opinion, one of the coolest careers in the game. Focusing on utility and scavenging consumables displays a stark difference in playstyle from what the other careers offer.

With that said i don’t think it would be controversial to place our ranger rather low on the power ranking of our available careers. While a buff in some respect seems in order i still want to preserve the central theme of a scavenger and not make him a “slayer with stealth”.

The idea that springs to mind is then to expand on his already implemented focus, and give ranger veteran an extra slot for potions and bombs. This would let you better capitalize on his existing strengths, as in the current patch bombs and potions often get saved as a last resort for critical moments, witch in turn makes a majority of the consumables get left by the wayside because of an already full inventory. An extra slot for these consumables allows for spending of a potion or bomb in the natural flow of the game without “wasting” the saving grace in a worst case scenario.

This would make ranger veterans talents actually feel more used during the gameplay, compared to runs where you get just the potion you need for a patrol and save it all map, only to never get a patrol spawn.
