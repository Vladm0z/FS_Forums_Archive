lMarcusl • 2025-11-09 12:13:29

I can attest that the challenge definitely does work, as I helped a friend complete it a few months ago. But it’s not easy to do. Corruscation staff is indeed no longer viable, it was nerfed in terms of shotgun projectiles fired and with it also nerfed in terms of heat generation. We’ve tried the staff many, many times and couldn’t do it. The beam staff shotgun does work tho. It seems like you’re doing all the right things, what with potions, attack speed stacking from talent etc. For things that you didn’t mention (but I’m assuming you’re doing them anyway) make sure you don’t have an overcharge reduction on your staff.

Do you have friends you could coop with? A Ranger Veteran with Ale drops, proxy and the attack speed ult (Exhilirating Vapours) will help you stack more speed and keep it up for longer. That’s the way we ended up doing it with my friend. But I also did it solo back before the Corusc nerf, so an ally shouldn’t be strictly necessary I don’t think.

Worth pointing out, the challenge is pretty…weird in terms of its behaviour. Both times I got the challenge it popped up way later than I would have expected. I summoned Army of the Dead, popped my speed pot, started spamming and sacrificed skellies at the right times as fast as I could. The speed potion lasts 15 seconds with Decanter. My potion was long gone, I had lost my attack speed bonus from having enough skellies up, Bardin ult was gone, proxy ran out, but I kept on spamming and the challenge completion popped up a solid 10 seconds after I was already past my attack speed peak. I seem to remember a similar behaviour back when I did it for myself solo. So it might also be possible you’re giving up “too early" when you don’t see the challenge completing. I think I was down to like 1-2 skeletons (from 12) when it finally appeared.
