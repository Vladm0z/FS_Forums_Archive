As title says, I’m tired of the severe lack of outfits, and variety in weapon designs. Recolours are ok when there’s variety but there currently isn’t even that many recolour options for most careers and none of the DLC outfits offer any recolours. The new glows are nice, but it’s always the same weapon designs. I get that there’s no need to add more weapon TYPES after a career releases (for the most part, new weapons are cool but not as big as new designs at the moment imo) since they have a reasonable toolset already, but it would be nice if as time progresses we could get more designs for those weapons instead of just more glows.

Yes I am aware we’d probably end up paying for them, but maybe we’d also get some free ones from putting in some hard work in game in the long run too. I don’t want more glows for the same like 4 Fire Sword designs for example, I want NEW Fire Sword designs, I want the artists to really unleash their creativity and show us what they can do.

The DLC outfits are nice, but there’s not even remotely enough of them, new ones don’t get released often enough and even when a bundle does come out, it leaves 80% of careers without a new outfit and if you actively play just 1 or even 2 careers, you’re just sat months or even over a year just for a new outfit.
