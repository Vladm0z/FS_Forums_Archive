I haven’t seen one yet, but I’ll give you a quick rundown here, I can grab some screenshots later.

EDIT: Piccies now included.
EDIT 2: More pics of locations.

First 3 barrels:

Just after your first troll fight, there’s three barrels in the halls just before the dropdown point for your second Troll fight (two trolls at once). Sometimes the third barrel will actually spawn in that room right by the level that summons two trolls.

Barrel11920×1080 235 KB

Barrel21920×1080 213 KB

Barrel31920×1080 145 KB

If Barrel number 3 isn’t in the location above, then it will spawn here:

Barrel3ALT1920×1080 173 KB

Barrel3ALTLocation1920×1080 205 KB

Water pump:

I personally go for the water pump first, you can leave one of the three barrels in the main area as you keep coming back round to it. Take two with you dropping them every now and again to kill hordes, SV and gunners/fire throwers. After the causeway with 2 x SV on it and one gunner you can use the two barrels on the blocked off areas on your left, just after the short stairs.

Cog:
I personally go for the cog area next, taking the one barrel you have with you. Deal with all the Gutter runners Blight stormers and leeches first. There’s a dead Dwarf with a key on him just by one of the jumping puzzles down by the pool of water. Make sure you grab it.

If you haven’t activated the water pump he’ll spawn here.
Green line is the path you take with the barrels and also the WORST pathing ever drawn.

CogAreaDeadDwarf1920×1080 159 KB

CogAreaDeadDwarf21920×1080 165 KB

If you have activated the water pump then he’ll be on the rocks here.

CogAreaDeadDwarfWater1920×1080 153 KB

CogAreaDeadDwarfWaterRisen1920×1080 188 KB

Pathway to the Troll boss in this section is a jumping puzzle shown below.

CogAreaBossPathway1920×1080 230 KB

Next activate the lever under the causeway, it’ll send an elevator uptop which has another barrel in it. Take both barrels and complete the jumping puzzle on the far left (far left as you enter the area from up top).

Lever is right down the bottom (not really visible in this image), activate it to send the elevator up and get another barrel.

CogAreaLift1920×1080 164 KB

CogAreaElevatorLever1920×1080 236 KB

Last area:
You shouldn’t have any barrels left now, but not to worry as you enter the area kill the rattling gunner first, make your way across the first bit, but rather than go right and up the stairs, jump on the suspended platform and across to a thin rocky slope, follow it round to the left and you have a breakable chain, this will drop the drawbridge.

DrawBridgeChain11920×1080 134 KB

DrawBridgeChain21920×1080 175 KB

DrawBridgeChain31920×1080 128 KB

DrawBridgeChain41920×1080 122 KB

Double back on yourself and you have another elevator, activate it so it goes up and you have another barrel.

DrawBridgeLift1920×1080 158 KB

With the drawbridge now down on the causeway (you’d normally head left along the causeway), go right on the bit you’ve opened up. You have a similar puzzle to the getting the second grim on Athel Yenlui, aim just above the highlight area for the barrel and you’ll get it.

DrawBridgeBarrelThrow1920×1080 132 KB

You still need one barrel now, so head to the exit like you would normally, but this time there’s a hidden door just on the right (this is why you got the key off the dead Dwarf), it opens to a small stairway which has an ammo crate and two bombs at the bottom (+ the last barrel). Grab the barrel and headback to the drawbridge and across it.

DrawBridgeBarrelLocation11920×1080 239 KB

DrawBridgeBarrelLocation21920×1080 141 KB

DrawBridgeBarrelLocation31920×1080 200 KB

There’s an opening on the right one you’ve gone across it, head there and use the last barrel to get to the last Troll boss.

I’ll grab some screenshots later to help with this wall of text, but it’s possible to access and kill all three troll bosses in one run.
