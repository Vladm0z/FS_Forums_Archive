freqlectic • 2020-06-09 13:21:27
hollerbach:

Probably related to: “Deed owner left game” error message - thing is, I am playing solo, and am the host and the deed owner

This is a general bug telling you that a player has left the game. If it’s the deed owner it specifies that the deed owner left. I haven’t experienced OPs issue during deeds or during normal qp, but I do experience the player has left the game bug pretty much every session.

If you want to replicate it reliably you host a game, get a few players in your lobby and then disband the lobby. The players that were in the lobby when you disbanded will keep being part of a console prompt saying “player x has joined the game” shortly followed by “player x has left the game” at every start and finish of a map. It stays like that for the rest of the session.
