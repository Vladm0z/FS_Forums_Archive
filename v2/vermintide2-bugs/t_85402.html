Foot Knight charge loses one instance of stagger when canceled early

9 Posts • 445 Views
k
kuli • 2023-10-26 19:37:17

As of 5.1.3, Foot Knight charge does not apply stagger anymore when canceled early.

Base ult

  • Old behavior (base ult): You cancel FK charge immediately after activating to not move in place and still apply 1 instance of stagger to enemies.

  • New behavior (base ult): You cancel FK charge immediately after activating to not move in place and don’t apply any stagger to enemies.

Battering Ram

  • Old behavior (Battering Ram equipped): You cancel FK charge immediately after activating to not move in place and apply 2 instances of stagger to enemies.

  • New behavior (Battering Ram equipped): You cancel FK charge immediately after activating to not move in place and apply only 1 instance of stagger to enemies.

Also, from the looks of it, when now canceling the charge (with Battering Ram equipped), some enemies inside the stagger cone do not get staggered at all.
