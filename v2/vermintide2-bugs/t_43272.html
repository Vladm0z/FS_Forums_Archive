Haiken • 2021-01-19 11:31:13

I remember making that thread about the impossible combinations on Charm and Necklace. I was making every possible combination of properties on Charm/Necklace/Trinket.

steamcommunity.com

Here is a list of combinations you can't get for Charm and Necklace ::...

Fatshark support confirmed for me that it isn't possible to get any of the following combinations of properties: -Necklace: Damage Reduction VS Chaos + Damage Reduction VS Skaven -Charm: Power VS Armoured + Power VS Berserkers -Charm: Power VS...

Back in the day, Fatshark made it impossible to get the combination of “Power VS Skaven + Power VS Chaos” on Charms. This caused an uproar, since people that already had a Charm with those properties got an advantage over those that could not make that combination any more. Fatshark reverted the change not long after that.

I think the other 4 combinations became impossible at the same time as skaven + chaos on Charm. But no one cared/knew about them because of how useless they are, so Fatshark never made those available again.

Personally I think it isn’t a good decision to have impossible combinations, especially when there is no indication that you cannot get these combinations. People that try to get them will just waste a lot of their dust and get frustrated. And while the combinations might be useless, I don’t approve of hand-holding that imposes restrictions in a game. It is better for people to figure out themselves what is good and what isn’t.
