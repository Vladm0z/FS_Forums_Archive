In-game description of Crushing Counter-Blow
→ Blocking an attack increases the power of Bardin’s next attack by 5.0%. Stacks up to 5 times.

  • Bug]

This talent increases the damage(not power) of Bardin’s next attack.

  1. 0:04
    → Power vs Chaos / Armoured 30% + Smiter
    Great Axe (w/ Opportunist trait) heavy attack damage : 31.50

  2. 0:15
    → Power vs Chaos / Armoured 30% + Drengbarazi Oath(power +20%) + Smiter
    Great Axe (w/ Opportunist trait) heavy attack damage : 37.75
    37.75 / 31.50 => 1.198412698412698‬ : almost 20% power-increasing effect of Drengbarazi Oath Talent.
    It also can cancel CW’s overhead attack on Cataclysm. This also shows that almost 56% power-increasing effect (1.30*1.20=1.56) is required to cancel CW’s overhead attack.

  3. 0:35
    → Power vs Chaos / Armoured 30% + Crushing Counter-Blow (5 stacks) + Smiter
    Great Axe (w/ Opportunist trait) heavy attack damage : 37.50
    37.50 / 31.50 => 1.19047619047619 : almost 19% power-increasing effect of Crushing Counter-Blow (5 stacks)
    It also can’t cancel CW’s overhead attack on Cataclysm.

If Crushing Counter-Blow really increases the power of Bardin’s next attack, it should cancel CW’s overhead attack on Cataclysm.
I also have tested with Power vs Chaos / Armoured 30% + Crushing Counter-Blow (5 stacks) + Enhanced Power. With this condition [1.3 * 1(0.25 + 0.07) = 1.716 : almost 71.6% power-increasing effect], Great Axe (w/ Opportunist trait) heavy attack can’t cancel CW’s overhead attack.

Conclusion :
Crushing Counter-Blow talent actually work as : Blocking an attack increases the damage (not power) of Bardin’s next attack by 4.0% (not 5.0%). Stacks up to 5 times.

From the code, it is 5 stacks of 0.05 melee power (the talent description does not specify this) , melee power only increases damage and cleave and not stagger, so I would expect 25% boost to damage and cleave from Crushing Counter-Blow at 5 stacks. However, I also know that melee power is additive with power VS. from equipment which explains the damage increase only being ~20% at 5 stacks. 31.5/1.3=24.23, 24.23*(1.3+0.25)=37.56.

bardin_ironbreaker_power_on_blocked_attacks_buff = {
max_stacks = 5,
multiplier = 0.05
}
icon = “bardin_ironbreaker_power_on_blocked_attacks”,
stat_buff = “power_level_melee”
