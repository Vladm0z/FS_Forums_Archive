When you guys created flail&shield, you reused the regular flail’s attacks but shuffled the attacks around a bit. This much is obvious from the animations and damage profiles for both weapons (The regular flail’s light attacks 1&2 became the Flail & Shield light attacks 4&5, and the regular flail light attacks 3&4 became the Flail&Shield light attacks 1&2.):

Warhammer Vermintide 2 Screenshot 2022.04.12 - 14.24.18.692144×521 312 KB

Warhammer Vermintide 2 Screenshot 2022.04.12 - 14.24.22.872162×532 302 KB

But when you guys moved the attack profiles around, you forgot to also move the tank property: thus the regular flail’s light attack 1&2 damage profiles became the damage profiles for the Flail & Shield light attacks 4&5 except for the Tank modifier which remained on the Flail & Shield light attacks 1&2 which I assume is probably an error since Flail & Shield light attacks 1&2 are single target overhead attacks with the lowest damage and stagger cleave limit values a weapon can have (2,24) thus them having the tank property is utterly pointless.

TL/DR

The flail & shield light attack 1&2 shouldn’t have the tank property, the fact that they do is probably a remnant of the copy-paste from the regular flail’s attack profiles. The Flail & Shield light attacks 1&2 should lose the Tank property, which should be on the Flail&Shield light attacks 4 & 5.

Edit: The Skull-splitter & Blessed tome light attacks also have the tank property even though they’re -just like the Flail&Shield light attacks 1&2- single target overhead attacks with damage and stagger cleave limit values of 2,24.

Warhammer Vermintide 2 Screenshot 2022.04.12 - 21.01.26.252150×571 342 KB

So is it also an oversight or is there more to the tank property than the mass modifier effect? It makes sense to have a property like tank on the attacks like the regular flail light attacks 1 and 2 which are designed to cleave through several enemies but for the flail&shield light attacks 1&2 and the Skullsplitter&blessed tome light attacks not really (unless there’s more to the tank property).
