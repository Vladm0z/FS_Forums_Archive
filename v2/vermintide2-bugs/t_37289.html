OenKradCommunity Hero • 2019-12-03 01:47:13

@UveMetUrDoom Gaining THP from torches would be nice.

Don_Kanaille:

… do torches work with Battle Wizard´s lingering flames?

Yes. It’s going to be a bit weaker than staves, melee, or ability burn though. It is also affected by burn related talents, such as Famished Flames or Enfeebling Flames.

Kingseeker:

Can we also drop torches without having to switch to our ranged weapon? That’d be lovely.

This is currently possible. While holding torch either:

  • Press a number (1-5) to change directly to that item(melee, ranged, medical, potion, bomb).

or

  • Scroll mouse wheel to change back to the weapon you were holding before grabbing torch(melee/ranged).

    • If you were holding ranged, swapped to medical/potion/bomb, then grabbed torch and scrolled, you would default back to ranged.
    • If you were holding melee, swapped to medical/potion/bomb, then grabbed torch and scrolled, you would default to melee.
      • Scrolling will always default to the last weapon you were holding(melee/ranged).
