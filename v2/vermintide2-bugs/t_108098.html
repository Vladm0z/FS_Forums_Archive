Issue Type (Required):

Visual

Issue Description (Required):

(These bugs are lumped together because they are all similar in nature)
Bugged textures for Pyromancer’s ‘Burning Head’ ability, the traveling vines of Sister of the Thorn’s ‘Tanglegrasp Thicket’ ability, and many surfaces in the bonus map ‘Fortunes of War’.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

Pyromancer visual bug:

  1. Select the Pyromancer career
  2. Hold the career skill button and look at the ‘Burning Head’ model
  3. Notice how the skull, which should look like bone, is pixelated and multicolored
    (I have linked a photo which displays the issue)

Sister of the Thorn visual bug:

  1. Select the Sister of the Thorn career
  2. Equip the level 30 ‘Tanglegrasp Thicket’ talent
  3. Use the ‘Thornwake’ career skill
  4. Notice how, when traveling to the targeted location, the vines accompanying the thornwall appear multicolored and pixelated rather than the correct green, plant-like texture
    (I have linked a photo which displays the issue)

Fortunes of War visual bug:

  1. Select the Fortunes of War adventure map and enter the Bridge of Shadows
  2. Walk around the plaza paying attention to corpses, barrels, fences, tarps, and brick floors
  3. Notice how the aforementioned items appear multicolored and pixelated instead of matching the correct materials respective to each model
    (I have linked several photos displaying this issue)

Level Name (Optional):

Fortunes of War

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)
