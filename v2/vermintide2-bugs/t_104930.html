Issue Type (Required):

Issue Description (Required):

Some responses to the new dialogue seem mismatched. It’s like this is a new cousin of the mismatched subtitles bug. For example:

  • Kruber: “Look at the elf go! She’s hell-bent on wading knee-deep through blood.”
  • Kerillian: Rude dialogue, ending with “Don’t even think I’ll forget!” (my memory is not perfect)
  • Sienna: “Cultivate a sense of humor, darling. For all our sakes.”

Kerillian’s response seems better-tied to Kruber’s crack about not wanting to be on the naughty list during a spree - both of the Kruber dialogue lines I mentioned did not have a response tied to them before the Devious Delvings update.

It is important to note that the same “response” voiceline can trigger from different “call” voicelines, hence my suspicion that this is a bug.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

Occurs randomly and naturally. Appears most noticeable during combat when the new spree voicelines are triggered.

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, but I haven’t tried disabling them

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Unusual (<25%)

Platform (Required):

PC - Steam
