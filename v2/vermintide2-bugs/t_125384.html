That’s right folks, we’re back with MORE spots to get stuck on this crappy map. In the first picture we can see, just after destroying the last device and heading up the stairs, as you’re waiting for the bomb to knock down the wall there’s a spot on the right (facing the wall with the bomb on your right) where you can get stuck. You literally just have to walk there and you’ll get stuck. The only way to get out is to either die, or get lucky and hope a mob or another player can nudge you out. There’s something even WORSE about this first picture though. And it’s that the wall to the right of my PoV isn’t a full “wall” persay. Ratlings and globadiers can shoot and throw their projectiles through that wall hitting the players while the players can’t do anything. All you have to do is knock them off their platform where the spawn just above it, and bam you can troll your entire team. This one should’ve come up in testing guys cmon, not only the getting stuck since it’s the player’s obvious first guess is to look and see what’s behind the wall thus getting themselves stuck, but also the fact that the specials can still shoot through it without falling to their deaths is ridiculous. Just make a giant invisible wall and be done with it how hard is that? Hand me the keys and i’ll do it.

In the second photo we can see this player got stuck during the final event where you have to fight the empowered rat ogre. The player jumped from the small platform and got stuck on that tiny wooden post sticking out. This map is just full of these and it’s messy design at best, and lazy at its worst. The random bits and bobs of geometry just sticking out grabbing onto players has always been a problem in VT1 and VT2, but it’s extremely egregious in this map. If you don’t want players going somewhere just stick an invisible wall there. No just on the geometry but on the entire plane itself. You guys have done it on other maps so i don’t know why it’s not done here. My best guess is that there’s so much random crap just strewn around that the designers just said “F it” and called it a day after taking too much of their Ritalin. This comes after I discovered I can get under the map on this level near the stairs after breaking the final machine and making the water rise. I’m sure there’s plenty of other spots to get stuck on this map as well considering all the junk just strewn about.
