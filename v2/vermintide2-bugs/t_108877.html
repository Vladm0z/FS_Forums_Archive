Issue Type (Required):

Enemy Mechanics

Issue Description (Required):

For months now (pretty much since the patch that introduced the glitch where Gutter Runners face the wrong way when stabbing the heroes), Gutter Runners have been skipping animations. They can go from their “climbing a ledge animation” instantly into being on top of a hero, without even properly finishing the climbing animation. No pre-leap crouchdown, just instant jump. They can miss a jump, fly past you and instantly rejump without even having to fully turn around to face you first. They can jump from a position where they are sideways towards you (and they will fly through the air still turned sideways). They can be running directly away from the player and instantly can jump backwards onto the player, flying towards the player butt-first, because they entirely skip past having to turn around.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Wait for an assassin to spawn.
  2. Flip a coin to see if they act normally or if they will skip animations, ingore facing directions, etc.
