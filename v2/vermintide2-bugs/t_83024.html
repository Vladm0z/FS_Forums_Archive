Shield Bash Has Less Zerker Stagger on Crits

3 Posts • 315 Views
A
Aether_Blackbolt • 2023-09-24 16:45:48

Issue Description:
The Heavy Shield Bash (Heavy 1) on many shields (Sword and Shield, Mace and Shield, Hammer and Shield, Axe and Shield, Warrior Priest Skull Splitter and Shield) can stagger a plague monk out of all attacks with 0 investment on non-crit on Cata. However, it fails to stagger on a crit on any monk attack, and can only stagger during non-attack).

The shield bash has 3 hitboxes (centre target, inner AoE, outer AoE). Each one has a different damage profile, with different damage/stagger power armor modifier values on non-crit. But they all share the same damage/stagger power armor modifier values for crit. This results in the Zerker Crit Stagger being higher than non-crit for the outer hitbox, but lower than the non-crit for inner hitbox and centre target. Issue is reproducible on C3Deathwish (via mods, not weaves).

2 New crit templates should be added to power_level_templates.lua so that each shield slam hitbox can have their own crit armor mod, then increase the crit zerker stagger of centre target and inner hitbox so that it is at least the same as non-crit, but preferably higher.

Relevant source code below:

  • damage profile templates for shield slams. critical_strike = “critical_strike_slam_tank_M” for all 3.
    https://github.com/Aussiemon/Vermintide-2-Source-Code/blob/71af1489627732309fdc01630c458c9f0a727577/scripts/settings/equipment/damage_profile_templates.lua#L653

  • centre target non-crit stagger mod for zerkers is 2
    https://github.com/Aussiemon/Vermintide-2-Source-Code/blob/71af1489627732309fdc01630c458c9f0a727577/scripts/settings/equipment/power_level_templates.lua#L269

  • inner hitbox non-crit stagger mod for zerkers is 1.5
    https://github.com/Aussiemon/Vermintide-2-Source-Code/blob/71af1489627732309fdc01630c458c9f0a727577/scripts/settings/equipment/power_level_templates.lua#L286

  • For all 3 profiles, crit stagger mod for zerker = 1
    https://github.com/Aussiemon/Vermintide-2-Source-Code/blob/71af1489627732309fdc01630c458c9f0a727577/scripts/settings/equipment/power_level_templates.lua#L252

Steps to Reproduce:
Take a shield with a heavy shield bash (Sword and Shield, Mace and Shield etc.). Have 0 properties or talents that boost Power or Stagger Power or Power Vs Monks (Skaven/Zeker). Perform a shield bash (Heavy 1) on a monk during flurry or final combo attack, then repeat with crit (modded realm allows you to spawn in monks, have guaranteed crits, and be invincible).
