Bots have way more places to get stuck on the Feast of Grimnir map than usual

Normal bot behavior is already pretty bad, they get stuck all over the place and constantly lag far behind the player. But on Feast of Grimnir, it’s on another level.

Players already noticed during last year’s event that bots seem to get stuck much more frequently on this map. There appear to be several specific spots where they get stuck very reliably, sometimes leaving you to continue through the map without them.

It seems like the map hasn’t received any fixes or adjustments since last year’s event before being brought back into rotation this year, because the exact same bot pathfinding issues are still present.

Considering that normal bot pathfinding already has plenty of problems, Feast of Grimnir makes those problems significantly worse.
