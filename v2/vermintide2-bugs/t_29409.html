Not sure if this is a bug or not.

  2. Build up your overcharge; a little under max
  3. Take a hit from something big – but be sure you have enough health so you wouldn’t go down from that hit; it might also be several smaller hits.

This has been an issue even in the versions prior to 1.3. According to this 2018-10-08 post, Why does Unchained insta-explode sometimes?

I believe that it has to do with your remaining health when you lose control of Sienna and she starts to flail about before exploding.

It seems that when you enter the state where she flails about, it also starts to drain Sienna’s health, but if you don’t have enough health to survive one of the damage ticks, it just downs you and the overcharge explosion occurs at the same time.

When you overheat you start rapidly losing HP if you are hit you also loose HP on top of this. Lets say you overheat at 10hp and take a hit at the same time you insta pop. This holds true for all Sienna’s not just Unchained.

I’m not sure if this is a bug or is intended behavior, though.
