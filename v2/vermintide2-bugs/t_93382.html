Issue Type (Required):

Issue Description (Required):

The primary fire on the masterwork pistol for Bardin seems to be bugged on controller for PC, although even when I used it on Xbox it happened. The primary fire will charge up for the shot, and then sometimes it won’t shoot and will charge up again, but in my experience, it only happens on the first shot fired; if you hold it down and keep firing after that it won’t happen, so trying to only shoot one target is basically random. The inconsistency of the weapon ruins the fun of it along with certain builds of the Ranger and Outcast, causing me to really only play his other two careers that don’t use it as much or at all.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Use a controller as input
  2. Play any Bardin career that can equip the Masterwork Pistol
  3. Shoot the primary fire of the pistol one shot at a time multiple times as the issue seems random
