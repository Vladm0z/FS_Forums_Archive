Heyo Fatshark Team,

As mentioned in various other posts, grail knight has 2 quests that require a tome or a grim to be optained in order to be active. However in Weaves are no tomes and grims.

Weave Grail Knight Quests - ConsoleBugs
Grim and Tome quests keep coming up in weaves. It use to only have the 4 other quests as the possible boons. (elites/specials/monsters/kills) This has been ongoing for Xbox since the Be’lakor update
Weaves Grail Knight QuestBugs
In Weaves the Grail Knight Quest is still optained through picking up a tome or a grim which is not possible as there are no books in weaves. Alternatively the quests should be optainable after gaining a certain percentage of Essence or more specific for every weave until one or a few of the mission objectives have been completed. This way the gk quests are soft locked and not usable at all.
Unachievable Grail Knight Duties in the WeavesBugs
Yeah, I know, weaves, ew. But this bug stood out to us because we all remembered it had been fixed at one point. It seems to be back. We only ran a Grail Knight this one time and I believe this is #48 but, obviously, not many grims in the weaves. [20220914021201_1]

This Mod is fixing these issues by adjusting the challenge_settings and adding all quest variations to the same table (of all gamemodes).
The functions to check for the condition if tomes and grims exist in weaves return true either way so grail knight has different objectives to activate heal regen and damage reduction in weaves (collecting essence and killing infantry units).
