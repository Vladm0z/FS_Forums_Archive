Issue Description (Required):

Bretonnian Sword and Shield push attack (shield bash + stab) has almost non-existent reach which forces you to stay very close to the enemy when performing it, which has been reported by other people since the career release (with proof):

Brettonian sword&shield push attackBugs
So I have noticed that brettonian sword&shield’s push attack has shorter reach than sword&shield. Also brettonian sword&shield’s push attack hitbox is aligned more to the right than sword&shield’s. Don’t know if it’s intended or not.
Bretonian Sword and Shield Push-Attack range bugBugs
Doing the Push-Attack on the dummy, in the keep, requires you to be exremely close. The sword doesn’t hit if you’re not standing close enough, even though the animation shows the sword going through the torso. Unsure if this is just a dummy thing. [20220205063600_1]

Someone time ago showed a video with the hitbox visibile at screen. And the push attack hitbox got stuck on the character, somehow it doesn’t move: https://youtu.be/9ayXajin-uE

Combining with low damage of this attack, this makes it one of the worst (possibly, the worst) push attacks in the game. Which honestly shouldn’t be, because it looks more like a bug than design choice. Attack reach is like two times shorter than normal attacks for some reason, and the damage is even worse. It ruins an already not very good weapon with diffucult moveset, essentially depriving you of one of your attacks.

I know this isn’t the first thread about this bug, but it still persists… after 4 years. More to this, this bug has been fixed in the mods already.

I don’t believe it’s that hard to fix, not in four years. So I’d like to clarify once and for all, is this bug just being ignored? Or is it just one of those things that is not a priority right now? Or the previous bug reports just got lost somewhere in the long list of bugs? I really hope for at least some feedback on this issue

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Perform push attack on Kruber’s Bretonnian Sword & Shield in front of a practice dummy
  2. Notice that attack range is much shorter than in apperas, in fact even shorter that normal attacks and mush shorter than regular sword and shield push attach. As if it had zero range
