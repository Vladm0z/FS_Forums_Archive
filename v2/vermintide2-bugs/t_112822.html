Issue Type (Required):

Levels

Issue Description (Required):

Hook rats can spawn behind the gate that leads to the bridge of shadows during the end event of the Tower of Treachery map; these hook rats are able to hook players through the gate.

I saw this more than once but only have recorded evidence of one occurrence.
Video footage of this happening: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fjhq0FI-NgM

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play Tower of Treachery map.
  2. Reach end event.
  3. Have a hookrat spawn behind the gate leading to the bridge of shadows.
  4. Be near that gate.
  5. Get hooked through that (closed) gate.

Level Name (Optional):

Tower of Treachery

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, but I haven’t tried disabling them

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Rare (<10%)

Platform (Required):
