krindi • 2018-12-18 00:42:23

As mentioned in the thread, but for clarity:

Using Waywatcher’s Ultimate applies the traits of the weapon you have in your hand at the time you use it. If you want to receive ammo from headshots then you HAVE to switch to your ranged weapon prior to using the Ultimate.

Melee weapons don’t have Conservative Shooter and thus your Trueflight Volley won’t proc ammo from headshots because the trait isn’t equipped.

(This also means you can have different breakpoints depending on whether you have the Ranged weapon or the Melee weapon equipped when you fire the Ultimate off.)
