Issue Type (Required):

Gameplay

Issue Description (Required):

So, a fundamental mechanic of the Warrior Priest class is his “Righteous Fury” passive, which allows for him to build up Fury, as represented by a metre in the middle of the screen. Once you have reached the maximum number of Fury stacks, you gain a 20% damage boost to your melee attacks, and can be given other buffs through talent choices, such as healing a small amount of permanent health on kill or gaining the benefits of his Shield of Faith ultimate. However, in order to gain Fury, enemies in a certain distance must die around him (usually somewhat near melee range is a safe bet), be it from his own hand or his allies. Lately though, even if I stand shoulder to shoulder with another melee career, killing a horde of rats together, no Fury builds. The only time that I am able to properly earn Fury is through his Level 20 Talents, where another condition, such as taking damage or directly killing enemies, gives additional Fury.

Furthermore, Fury stacks normally degrade when outside of combat, but if you do not have an alternative way to consistently earn Fury, any that you have accrued will dissipate quickly regardless of the amount of enemies around you.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Pick Warrior Priest, and make sure to take the Level 20 Talent “See My Wrath”. (Note: you take “See My Wrath”, so that you can more properly see the lack of Fury generation, as the other two gather Fury through killing and taking damage, thus obscuring the Fury generation problem somewhat during normal gameplay. If you are familiar with the pre-patch regeneration rate of Warrior Priest, however, you can take any Talent you like.)

  2. Hop onto any Quickplay Match. (I’ve heard rumours that Warrior Priest 's passive works on Chaos Wastes, but I have yet to test it, or Versus mode, for the time being.)

  3. Swing into the nearest rat until he perishes, and stick close to a bot or player that does the same.

  4. Neither action will develop Fury.

  5. Keep playing regularly, and if you’re still using “See My Wrath”, use your ultimate. You should see 60% Fury generated, then quickly dissipate, regardless of situation.
