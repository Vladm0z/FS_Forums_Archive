Twitch Mode - Boon of Concentration is not working with Potions as intended

4 Posts • 1006 Views
M
M4X • 2019-06-07 13:10:21

Issue Summary: This bug happens only while playing with the Twitch Mode. If you drink a Potion before that Boon of Concentration kicks in, you will have the effect of a Potion of Concentration reloading the career skill bar for 1 minute straight, unless you drink another Potion in the meanwhile which is going to interrupt this. If you drink a Potion after that Boon of Concentration kicks in, you will have no effect on the career skill bar at all.

It’s important to mention that different traits on the Charm trigger this bug at different conditions. Concoction triggers it with any type of Potion (Potion of Concentration, Speed and Strength). Instead Decanter, Home Brewer and Proxy trigger it only with a Potion of Concentration.

Steps to Reproduce:

  1. Link a Twitch account to your lobby.
  2. Start the portal with everything that can be played with the Twitch Mode and jump in.
  3. Get a Potion.
  4. Vote for Boon of Concentration through the Twitch chat or just wait for it to be dropped by the RNG.
  5. Drink the Potion before or after that Boon of Concentration kicks in. Remember that, if you drink the Potion before, its effect must be active while Boon of Concentration kicks in. If you drink it after, then just do it while Boon of Concentration is active.

Reproduction Rate: Constant (100%)
