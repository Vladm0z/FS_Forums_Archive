Issue Type (Required):

Gameplay

Issue Description (Required):

When playing Sister of the Thorn I always have issues with inputs not registering:

  • Pressing keys 1 and 2 sometimes does not switch weapon
  • Switching using key 3, 4, or 5, sometimes does not switch to the corrosponding item
  • Pressing F to use the ultimate ability often does not register

There are also issues with the DeepWood Staff and ultimate ability:

  • DeepWood Staff often does not highlight or lift enemies I am aiming directly at, even if they are right in front of me
  • Some inclines and declines on the map prevents the Thornwall ultimate from being used

I often find one of two things happen when playing Sister of the Thorn:

  1. I switch from DeepWood Staff to Sword and Dagger but inputs do not register
  2. I spam whatever key I need on my keyboard and eventually the inputs register

In the first scenario, I switch from DeepWood Staff to my Sword and Dagger with a horde approaching close to me, but the inputs do not register and I am hit and downed very quickly (there is no block with DeepWood Staff).

In the second scenario, I spam the key in the same situation and it eventually switches but I get hit at least once from the first input not registering and costing me time.

Additionally, spamming F to use my ult isn’t a good solution either because Kerillian’s Sister of the Thorn has two ultimates that stack, so you just end up using them both.

I would say at least half the times at minimum these issues are present, but they can all happen a lot.

For example in one match it might be around 50% of the time weapon switching does not work (or inputs not registering), but it could also be 70% of the time the DeepWood Staff doesn’t catch what I am aiming at, or maybe 60% the ultimate ability does register when pressing the F key, but these could all happen at different rates in another match.

So the reproduction rate fluctuates a bit but it’s mostly more often than not. So these issues come across as very prevalent and are very disruptive to gameplay.

I play this class a lot recently but when inputs fail to register they often cause me to go down in a situation I was otherwise prepared for

I also do not have these issues with any of Kerillians other classes (I’ve played them all quite a bit), and well as Bardins classes (which I’ve also played a lot).

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play the game as Kerillian’s Sister of the Thorn
  2. Observe the issues mentioned

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, but I haven’t tried disabling them

Reproduction Rate (Required):
