Issue Type (Required):

Talents

Issue Description (Required):

There are 2 bugs related to the Asrai Alacrity talent on handmaiden:

  1. The video uploaded shows the first bug. Pushing, attacking once, and then looping this will eventually only give you 1 stack of asrai alacrity on push. Sometimes this happens almost instantly and sometimes it takes several loops to trigger. This bug only happens when hitting real enemies and never happens on the dummies in the keep which will always give 2 stacks of asrai alacrity on push no matter what. Only the push part of the talent is bugged, blocking always gives 2 stacks. This bug also doesn’t trigger if you attack twice after pushing, it triggers only on attacking once after push and repeating it.

  2. Asrai alacrity power buff doesn’t apply correctly when you’re not the host. My build requires the 10% power buff from asrai alacrity to consistently stagger plague monks out of their attacks with a push (using spear/shield and opportunist trait) but it doesn’t work when I’m not the host. I play with nearby hosts with typically 70-90 in-game ping and have fiber internet. This bug happens with 100% chance when I’m not the host and never happens when I’m hosting. I don’t have more details on this since I cannot test it myself, the uploaded video doesn’t show this part of the bug. I think only the multi-hit attack from the plague monk requires the extra power for staggering.

I used mods to be able to test the first bug but there’s nothing that affects talents or other game mechanics. The bugs have been present before patch 6.6.0

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

For the first bug:

  1. Equip asrai alacrity talent
  2. Push an enemy
  3. Attack once
  4. Repeat from step 2
    At some point, pushing will only give 1 stack of asrai alacrity.

For the second bug:

  1. Equip asrai alacrity talent and spear/shield with opportunist trait
  2. Have 650 power and at least 17% power vs skaven (10% from gear and 7% from enhanced power talent or 20% from gear, both will work)
  3. Play with someone else hosting
  4. Block the first attack from a plague monk to get asrai alacrity buff
  5. Push during the plague monk’s multi-hit combo.
    Step 5 will fail to stagger the plague monk if you’re not hosting.
