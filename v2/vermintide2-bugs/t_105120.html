Issue Type (Required):

Cosmetics

Issue Description (Required):

I noticed that with the Ranger Veteran’s “Varglag” cosmetic armor (paid one), player can see parts of the beard disappear.
The beard can be split into 5 parts - two smaller braids on the left, big one in the middle, two smaller braids on the right (when looking at Bardin’s face). All but 4 weapons cause one of the braids to disappear - mainly, the one on far left. It simply isn’t there.

This issue can be seen with the following weapons:

  • 1h axe
  • 1h hammer
  • Axe and shield
  • Hammer and shield
  • Cog hammer
  • Masterwork pistol
  • Throwing axe
  • Crossbow
  • Grudge-Raker
  • Handgun

This issue cannot be seen with the following weapons:

  • Dual hammers
  • Greathammer
  • Greataxe
  • War Pick

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Acquire the Varglag cosmetic armor for Ranger Veteran
  2. Equip it then go to “Equipment” tab.
  3. Equip DualHammers/Greathammer/Greataxe/WarPick and notice that the beard is full and symmetrical (look screenshots)
  4. Equip 1hAxe/1hHammer/AxeShield/HammerShield/CogHammer.
  5. Observe that one of the braids disappears completely.

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, and I’ve tried disabling them

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)
