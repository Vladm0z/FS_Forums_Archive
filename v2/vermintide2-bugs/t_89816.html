First time playing cataclysm runs in Chaos Wastes and encountered two weird bugs. I did about 5 runs with the same host in the USA. We wiped in all runs until we agreed for a final attempt and won it. But we encountered two sort of game breaking bugs, mentioned in the title.

We didn’t have heavy hitter classes with us. We were a team of a warrior priest, me as pyromancer, huntsman and sister of the thorn. The second monster was a rat ogre, and much tougher to kill (didn’t die suddenly).

One player on the team mentioned that they had seen this issue happen before a few times, that it seems to be an specific combination of grudge marks that instakills the boss after a while for some reason. I had never encountered this in hundred of runs in CW on Legend. So maybe it’s a cataclysm exclusive bug. I forgot to ask this player if this issue happened before the new patch or after.
