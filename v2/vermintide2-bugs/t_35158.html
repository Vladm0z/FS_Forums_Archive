Drakonhammer • 2019-08-13 15:34:57

At least in the past, it’s always been an increase of 50% to the headshot damage bonus (1.5x), not 50% more the total damage. Like how 20% crit power means 20% of the critical damage bonus (also 1.5x), which is 50% baseline, 20% of 50% being a 10% increase.

So with 50% more headshot damage you would expect a 25% to total damage on a HS (from 1.5x to 1.75x), 20% seems a little low.

I’m not 100% certain on the mathematical formula behind the critical headshots (let alone whether it’s multiplicative or additive, I believe it’s additive), but the HS damage bonus applies only to the HS component of the damage, not to the critical damage bonus component. Hence the damage increase is less overall relative to the innate damage of a crit.

So with 50% more damage on HS you get 1.0x + 0.5x + 0.75x = 2.25x damage instead of 1.0x + 0.5x + 0.5x = 2.0x, which is roughly a 10% increase.

I hope that clarifies things! All that said, there have been a lot of issues with criticals and headshots in the past where the damage profiles have accidentally been switched on some weapons or other issues, so it’s good that you’re taking a closer look at them to find out whether they’re working correctly!

Edited for clarity. Also: the executioner’s blade (unlike the falchion) is one of the weapons that have different headshot profiles/better HS multipliers, so the talent will interact differently with that weapon (hence the higher damage increase).

On the falchion vs CW scenario I imagine the difference in the damage increase is due to interactions with armour and the non-armour-piercing vs armour-piercing components of the damage you deal. Falchion is also a little weird in that it was nerfed specifically against CWs, it works fine against SV.
