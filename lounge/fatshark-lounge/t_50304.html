Fatshark_Hedge • 2022-09-15 16:11:42

The builder will let you build one question, but it misses an attribute if you then try to make more in the same post, so you need to add a unique name=XYZ in to the opening declaration of each question; eg

[poll type=number results=always min=1 max=10 step=1 name=turtle]
# Do you like turtles? (1 being hate turtles, 10 being turtles are the finest example of a ninja in a half shell)
[/poll]
