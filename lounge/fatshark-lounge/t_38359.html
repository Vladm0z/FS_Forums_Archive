Long, Irritable Rant about the term "Grind"

Apologies in advance

The word “grind” is getting overused nowadays. The pure mob-killing rampage of level 55-58 to get into Burning Crusade content in WoW is a grind; in order to advance at all in the game, you absolutely had to kill between 100 and 200 creatures to get to the minimum level requirement for the next zones. That is a grind.

Getting cosmetic content at regular intervals by completing quests isn’t a grind, it’s an added benefit of playing the game for any length of time. You can choose to pursue it or not to pursue it and, regardless of what you would choose, it has zero impact on gameplay.
