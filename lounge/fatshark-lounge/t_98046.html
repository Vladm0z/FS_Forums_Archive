Heyooo! To celebrate the Open Beta of Versus with y’all, we wanted to host a small contest where you get to show us the BEST moment you experienced while playing Versus! Whether it’s a funny clip, an epic play or just a very cool and intense moment, we want to see it!

How to participate

Starting from now until September 2nd, post a Versus clip from your own gameplay on either Youtube, TikTok, Twitter, Instagram or Facebook, with the hashtag #versusmoments to participate!

After that, we’ll go through all of the submissions and have everyone at Fatshark vote on their favorites.

Prizes

The top 5 clips will receive the following:

  • 4x keys for base game Vermintide 2
  • 4x keys for the Forgotten Relics DLC
  • 4x keys to a bundle with all the Premium Careers
  • 1x Eternal Serpent Frame!

Use them for yourself, share them with your friends, spread the love around! We want you all to have fun with Versus and with the Vermintide we all know and love.

Rules

  1. Each person may only submit ONE clip to the contest.
  2. The clip submitted must be of your own gameplay.
  3. Gameplay must be of the Versus Beta August Test
  4. Clip must be of a single, unedited moment, not a compilation of clips!
  5. Have fun!
