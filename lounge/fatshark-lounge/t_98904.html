Toss up between v1 and v2 in my mind. In v1 you haver the least amount of options so that alone necessitates that your fundamentals are on point. There are defo some cheesy ways for example to regain HP and ammo nearly limitlessly but other than that mistakes are heavily punished and you will die 1 shot to stuff like overheads.

V2 on the other hand throws way more at you, but you have way more tools like class abilities, temp hp and talent interactions to deal with the added enemy power. There is also something about v2s movement that is just more refined compared to v1. I always notice it whenever i try to play the first game. I guess its similar to people who have started with darktide and try to play v2.

Its different kind of difficulty with the 2 games and i could see arguments for either.
