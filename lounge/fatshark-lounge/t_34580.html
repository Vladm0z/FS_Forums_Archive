If you already have one or two friends, that can already be a group - there’s no real need for more players usually. Just get a set of rules (DnD is one set, but a lot of others are available even for free, and arguably better) and whatever RNG the system needs and you’re pretty much ready to go. I could probably do a long rant about possible systems and advice, but I’ll leave it out for now… But yeah, the only way to really get started is to start. Get rules, get dice, get your friends together. Either read the rules alone beforehand (quite recommended anyway) or just start looking at them together. Whoever has the best story idea can be the GM, and even that isn’t necessarily set in stone.
