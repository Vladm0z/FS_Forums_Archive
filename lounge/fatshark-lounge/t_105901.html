Here’s a simple and delicious cookie recipe! Ingredients: • 1 cup (226g) butter, softened • 1 cup (200g) sugar • 1 egg • 1 tsp vanilla extract • 2 cups (250g) all-purpose flour • ½ tsp baking soda • ¼ tsp salt Instructions: 1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper. 2. In a bowl, cream together the butter and sugar until light and fluffy. 3. Add the egg and vanilla, then mix until well combined. 4. In a separate bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, and salt. 5. Gradually add the dry ingredients to the wet ingredients and mix until a dough forms. 6. Roll the dough into small balls and place them on the baking sheet, spacing them apart. 7. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until the edges are lightly golden. 8. Let them cool for a few minutes before enjoying! Optional: Add chocolate chips, nuts, or cinnamon for extra flavor.
