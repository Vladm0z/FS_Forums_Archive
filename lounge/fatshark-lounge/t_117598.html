We all love the Ubersreik five, but the world of Warhammer fantasy is vast, and there is no shortage of unique cultures and characters.

So, in the name of random curiosity, if you could choose four characters to throw together in a party, for order or chaos, regardless of lore, who would you choose?

Me? I would go with a female dwarf (Bardin’s daughter to be precise), a stranded Skink lizardman from Lustria, a renegade vampire (not all the undead bow to Nagash readily, just ask Settra and Ulrika, mayhaps the one that Lohner met a while back?), and a Cathayan Alchemist.

Why these characters? Because they have both enough potential for different careers and unique weapons. Especially the Skink. Who wants to throw fire-leech bolas?
