Frish • 2024-02-12 03:20:03

It’s been scratching my intense combat co-op itch pretty damn hard. With friends it’s a much better experience imo. Lotta speedrunners in the public lower-level games – once it gets hard enough then it’s good again.

Aside from the various launch issues, a fair few of which have already been addressed, it’s a well polished product that offers a refreshingly simple & effective progression (wouldn’t that be nice). It helps to have played the first one since a lot of the mechanics are the same, so I imagine that there’s a bit of a new player learning curve.

By feeling alone it’s an amazing experience though. The vibe is immaculate, the enemy and level design is awe-inspiring, the combat is robust, and the sheer amount of ordnance at your fingertips is absolutely savage.

I wouldn’t say it’s a direct competitor to Darktide, but in terms of my personal time and interest it’s currently winning out. Is it just that new game feel? Time will tell…
