My ultimate comfort meal is this spicy bean and sausage stew. It’s so easy to make and I’ll never be sick of it!

Nobody asked but here’s the recipe anyway lol

Serves 2 (large portions)
Fry onions & garlic
Add 2 tins chopped tomatoes, 1 tin kidney beans, 1 tin cannellini beans
Add 2 Tsp cumin, 2 Tsp paprika, chilli flakes, salt + pepper
Cook for 10 minutes
Add 6 sausages (I like the Linda McCartney ones. Inb4 “I can’t believe they haven’t run out of bits of Linda McCartney to put in the sausages”)
Transfer to oven & cook for 45 minutes
Serve with garlic bread

Semi-recent development for me but a couple of months ago I got hold of a quick and dirty Quesadilla-type recipe and honestly? It’s surprisingly great. If you want the rundown:

  • Heat the oven up
  • Brown off any meat mince (the beef one you’ve had in the freezer for 6 months that you were definitely going to use will do) in a pan with some tomato puree/ pasata (any form of vaguely-liquid tomato based product should do) and spices, whatever you want
  • Warm some tortillas up
  • Grate some cheese
  • Put all your prepped stuff in the warm tortilla
  • Stick it in the oven for a bit

Cheap, quick and easy.
