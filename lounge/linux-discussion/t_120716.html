If you happen to use Heroic Games Launcher, it can install and update Proton-GE in Steam automatically: Settings → General → set “Default Steam Path” to your Steam directory (the one with steamapps, userdata, steam.sh etc. in it) and enable “Download GE-Proton to Steam directory” further below.
