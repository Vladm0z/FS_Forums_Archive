CobaltNinja • 2024-11-16 13:26:51

And how else would Versus be achieved? It’s a contest of skill and risk/reward, and it would be nigh-impossible to balance earning points as Skaven without creating degenerate tactics that aren’t just “kill the heroes as quickly as possible”.

Left 4 Dead Versus operates under the exact same principle as Vermintide. Players on the Infected team cannot score points - their goal is to make sure the Survivors score as few as possible. So, the team who manages to get further ahead, and is therefore better, scores more points!

The one point where it differs, and I’d argue could make the gamemode worse, is that a single player with a self-sustaining career such as Shade or Warrior Priest can truck on solo for quite a while without the rest of their team and still score full points (disregarding safehouse, which is a measly -10pts per hero dead).

Left 4 Dead’s method of tracking score heavily punishes players for dying, as it means that player is no longer actively contributing points to your total. Thus, a full team of four getting 50% of the way scores more points than a single player managing to make it to the end while the rest of his team wipes early.

Stein:

You kill the entire team of heroes without letting them reach the end of all the matches - and you lose. Huh?!

With a team of intelligent Skaven players, it is extremely difficult for Heroes to make any meaningful progress towards objectives, let alone reach the goal. Again, same thing happens in Left 4 Dead. Scoring points by tracking progress is a way of deciding a match without making constant draws because everyone keeps wiping.
