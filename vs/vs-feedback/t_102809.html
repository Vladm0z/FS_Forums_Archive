The troll needs to be buffed substantially. It gets staggered too easily, has too little damage, and it can’t really do much against people with shields. Even just a non-shielded weapon’s guard stance provides near 100% protection. Light attacks should demolish guard stance in just a few hits, heavy attacks should do a crapload of damage through guard and not just send the player flying unless they guard with a shield. Fire bombs and the trollpedo should not be able to stagger it, neither should shout ults or BH ult unless it hits to the head. Buffing the troll in such a way would also force team compositions to bring (more) boss killers or face an almost guaranteed wipe. More shades and less Handmaidens and Waystalkers, more Grail Knights and less Foot Knights and Mercs would be good for the mode over all as it would make the meta more diverse.
