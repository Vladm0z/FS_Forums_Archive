Versus definitely has potential, but ultimately, V2 is around 7 years old. Coupled with a handful of other game modes to choose from, the game suffers from “choice fatigue.” I’m not too big in the argument of “splitting the player base”, but it’s hard to bring a game mode to a larger population without a) putting the marketing investment into it (which can be difficult to justify for an older game) and b) supporting that game mode when the main draw is adventure mode. I think V2 suffers from trying to be too many things at once, and I think adding Versus compounded that.

Vertraxxx • 2025-08-12 21:15:10

I do think that if Versus had been implemented earlier, the situation might have been different. However, since most players who prefer PvE games generally dislike PvP, Versus would have died sooner or later.
Of course, seeing a game mode that took several years to develop die out in less than six months seems tragic, and I sometimes wish those development resources had been spent on adding more weapons, improving QoL, or enhancing existing game modes instead. That said, from a developer’s and marketing perspective, implementing a new game mode can be a chance to attract new players, as well as an opportunity for experimentation or technical validation to be repurposed for future titles, so I don’t entirely dismiss the idea.
Right now, Darktide is following a similar path, despite the game’s balance being severely broken, they’ve implemented Havoc and Mortis Trial. There are so many things that should take priority, such as adding new weapons and enemy factions, or overhauling the skill tree.
