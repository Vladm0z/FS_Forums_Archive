Classes

image720×477 93 KB

This is the current tierlist I have made after reaching level 50, I’ll consider heroes in A and B tier to be acceptable in terms of power (however, some of those may still get changes only to reduce unneeded annoyingness)

S Tiers

S tier classes are basically the hard meta, the heroes you always wanna have if winning is your upmost priority.

In the past during the Beta/Alpha, the same S tier heroes have remained, with the only exception being Pyromancer, in which now she’s actually balanced since the Fireball QQ nerf

So let’s tackle them

  1. Outcast Engineer:
    While engi got very reasonable nerfs, he’s still capable of dumping over specials while still ruining the troll’s day as easily, this is due to bombs still applying stagger overall, and trollhammer existing

So Engi himself doesn’t really need individual balances, the needed changes will be listed as a mechanical change later on the thread

  1. Warrior Priest:
    His main issue is 2 talents basically super buffing the team for free, which are Prayer of Hardiness and The Comet’s Gift

Prayer of Hardiness snowballs upon other heroes talents that people also deem to be in the toptier (Handmaiden HP increase, and Footknights damage reduction aura)

I wouldn’t exactly know how to tackle them, but in a world without these 2 talents, WP would defenitely become a lot more balanced and tolerable to deal with

  1. Footknight:
    Footknight is more on the “honest” side of things, but his main issue is his aura and It’s Hero Time

However, people do love to have Footknight be centered around the It’s Hero Time, so I think it’s okay to keep it as it is

I do believe however that some of his aura resistance talents should be nerf

Rock of Reikland resistance should be reduced from 20% to 15%
That’s Bloody Teamwork! base resistance should be reduced to 10%, and each close ally should increase it by 5%

A Tiers

There’s only 2 classes I need to talk about in this tier, and this is due most of them being perfectly valid and reasonable to fight, while also bringing good utility to the team and/or themselves

  1. Handmaiden:
    Of course, Handmaiden has been one of the most complained classes in Versus, and this is no shocker, she’s really really annoying to deal with, so while I’m going to propose small nerfs to reduce her annoyingness, her power should still remain practically the same

First of all, the elephant in the room, Gift of Ladrielle needs to be inmediatly banned or entirely reworked. This talent forces the skaven to predict too much on what she’s gonna do with it, to the point that with how often she can get it, it can become really frustrating to fight

Secondly, Ariel’s Benison revive speed should be reduced to 25% faster, and not grant any healing upon reviving someone

  1. Mercenary:
    While I’m still discerning if this is an issue or not, many people have complaining about On Yer Feet, Mates! rendering skaven work futile, so this is just an honorable mention

B Tiers

I will be talking about all classes in this tier except Waystalker, as while she could be “annoying”, I don’t know if there’s anything that could be changed without ruining the character

  1. Bounty Hunter:
    This class is pretty much well executed for versus, there’s only 1 change I would make for him

He pretty much already puts the Bile Troll in shambles, so let’s give the Troll a little bit of wiggle room here:

His Career Skill no longer staggers the Troll, unless it’s a headshot

It makes the stagger feel more fair overall

  1. Ironbreaker:
    His only issue is part of Engineer’s issue, the Trollhammer. It’ll be addressed later

  2. Battle Wizard:
    While VF EP Bolt Staff sounds good, it doesn’t 1 shot ratling gunners at times (Maybe it’s distance related?)

Only change I would really suggest is allowing VF EP Bolt Staff to 1 shot ratlings from any range, and she would be perfect

C Tiers

Here’s the weak of the bunch, and yes I know I’m gonna be bombarded with “WHC is not C tier!”, but he really is once I bring upon perspective

With that in mind let’s just start with him then

  1. Witch Hunter Captain:
    While he can work as a solo carry class when his team is bad, he struggles to find value in good teams compared to his other classes

Bounty hunter does the ranged part way better, Zealot does the survibability / melee part better (especially with Devotion that talent is goated), and WP does the support better

While one could argue “But him being able to do all even in a lesser measure is good!”, and it is kinda true, it just doesn’t do these enough to compensate not going for one of the others instead

And this is not even considering that Witch Hunt got indirectly nerfed due to no longer being able to tag elites

So here are my suggestions:

  • Increase his base ammo capabilities by 15% (So that when combined with black market, you get the same ammo as BH does passively)

This change would allow you to tap the ranged area better if you so choose Black Market, without it being better than BH (No garanteed crit, no dmg resistance won upon killing specials)

This also allows you to diverge in Charmed Life or Cast Away as alternative options, without it being too detrimental to your ability to defend yourself

  • Make his Witch Hunt damage bonus apply passively against Elites

I know I already suggested this in the other thread, but I believe that for simplicity sake it’s better to have it here as well

This would allow you to simply deal with Elites faster, not that big of a gamechanger but it solidifies your role as an “Independent” class that can do a little bit of everything without outclassing the other 3 in anything

  1. Shade:
    Shade might be the only class that could prove me wrong regarding her power level, however even among good Shade players, I really don’t see how they could’ve done worse with something like Waystalker or Handmaiden

Her strength of course comes from deleting bosses, but she requires a Str pot to destroy them quickly, as shown here

And because I’m gonna be suggesting to remove Str pots… we’ll have to see

Even if I don’t have any specific changes for the moment, it is worth noting in my opinion that she’s outclasses by the other two, but defenitely not outclassed by…

  1. Sister of the Thorn:
    Her best attribute is being able to fit in the meta comp by making the other already unkillable classes even more unkillable, but that’s just unneeded overkill

And this leaves her in a bad position, because you could be using any of the other 2 Keris that can fit better in that composition

Only buff I can think for her really is reducing the cooldown of staff to lift specials from 40s to 25s

  1. Necromancer:
    Only good thing going for her is not being Unchained

Her minions being disabled the moment she gets disabled really ruin it for her, the only considerable buff I would give to her is to make her minions actually defend her

And I know that does sound annoying but remember, Ironbreaker still has a free get out of jail free card and that’s mostly tolerable by the lack of complaints about it

  1. Slayer:
    I think Slayer is overall underrated, but he’s still easily fit on C tier

His cost opportunity compared to other classes is inmense, and I believe it really holds him back. However he promotes a healthy and fun playstyle, so let’s try to not overtune him

My main suggestion is to increase the damage of throwing axes, enough to 1 shot assasins/globadiers at any range, and 2 shot hookrats

  1. Grailknight:
    Suffers from a very similar issue compared to Slayer, but I can’t think of anything to make him on the better side of things

Only thing I can think that could work is from this thread:

Grail Knight vs Warpfire throwerGeneral Discussion

Pactsworn

Pactsworn has been as best as it has ever been, extremely fun to play, but they’re defenitely lacking in overall impact, so here’s in order on what I want to discuss about them:

  1. Respawn & Vermintide Mechanic:
    I believe the Vermintide horde changes were perfect, it’s exactly what I could’ve desired out of them, and for this I must be grateful

However, regarding respawn timers… I believe that since the nerf to rat respawn timers, heroes have been able to win too much ground for free, and so I believe this should be reduced once again, by 20-25% on all respawn timers (reducing it to current values that change depending on the amount of rat teammates)

There’s also this change regarding bots, both on Hero side and Skaven, if you want to read

Regarding botsFeedback
I think changes done regarding team size/score difference were good, but it’s still lacking in achieving a proper balance when not having full lobbies First, bot Survivors having no talents: It’s absolutely silly for a hero team to have someone who can’t even generate THP Hero bots should have pre-determined talents that are most compatible with them so that they can actually be a potential ally, and not just something that easily gets dumped at the smallest of inconvenience I also believe …
  1. Hookrats:
    Bringing up the elephant in the room, Instakills (Defined as basically removing a hero out of the game, no matter if they actually die instantly or not)

I have seen 2 types of instakills regarding Hoisting, and I wanna expand on them

A) The type of instakill that puts someone up an edge, this could be the roof of a house, top of a cart, and stuff like that, the end result being something like this:

image1920×1080 413 KB

This results in heroes being unreachable and thus, an instakill

B) The type of instakill that glitches a hero out of the map, mainly done by hoisting someone on a set of horizontal stairs. Once the hero is unhoisted, they’ll fall outside the map

In some situations, you don’t even require a full hoist up to bug them out of the map (This had happened to me, but don’t have evidence sadly)

I believe that type 1 instakills are fair because of the following points:

  • Hookrat must be in a specific place (up an edge)
  • Hookrat must fully hoist someone up
  • Hookrat is vulnerable to easily being killed due to them standing still the moment they hoist someone up

As you see, type B instakills don’t require these points, which makes it feel very bad to be on the end of

In an ideal world all instakills of these types should be fixed, but I think fixing type B instakills NEED to be a priority and asap

Outside of Instakills, Hookrat is still the best Pactsworn, mainly due to the movement speed buff upon catching someone

Very good designed rat overall, no changes needed

  1. Gutter Runner:
    It’s still a very very good disabler, it’s just outmatched by the Hookrat, so only buff I can suggest is to allow him to cancel his invisibility before it expires, so you have a little bit more of control

  2. Ratling Gunner:
    It’s perfect as it is, doesn’t really need changes

  3. Firerat:
    Increase the damage back to where it was before in the past, however, make fire hitbox wider to both help against groups, and to also prevent cheese from firing through tiny gaps

  4. Globadier:
    Experienced teams have resorted into leaving the gas rat alive when he’s in an useless position (heroes went past the chokepoint, no disablers got anything, etcetera)

To the point that even effective globadier players run towards the heroes hoping for them to kill him, but they just won’t

The easiest way to fix is is to add the ability to detonate yourself as a Globadier, this way you can run up to heroes and explode, or force them to kill you so that you don’t explode on them

Either way it’s a win-win for the Globadier

  1. Bile Troll:
    While I already made another thread regarding him (sorry) I don’t think I covered him full enough (as I only stated upon the changes made from Beta to Full Release)

His biggest issue right now is how sluggish and wonky it feels, so unthreatening due to the huge amount of stagger in the game

So how do we solve this? By doing the following:

  • Bombs (and Trollhammer) no longer stagger the Troll
  • As mentioned in the BH change, only headshots from his career ability would stagger him
  • All other Career Skills that stagger, would keep doing so (Shouts, FK charges, and so on)
  • Removing the Str potion

Now troll actually can charge when the opportunity appears, and not get super stunned by the vast majority of his time alive, and would also make extremely dominant classes less dominant (Except FK) because the demand for stagger ults is higher (this would also make less dominant classes a little bit more desireable)

But yeah that’s all, I’m very happy with how fun the mode is, and I would defenitely be way happier if we prioritize making the mode as variable and fun as it can be

Thanks for reading!
