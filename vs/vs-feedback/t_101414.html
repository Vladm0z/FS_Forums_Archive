This post made me think about a couple solo clutches I’ve had where I managed to squeeze out 60 or so more points before I got downed after the rest of my teammates wiped. Thinking about this, I realize that this is kind of absurd. A solo player getting that far scores the same amount of points as a full team of players that go the same distance, even though the team is arguably superior as a whole because they’re all alive? BS.

The safehouse score reduction barely matters as it’s a measly -10pts for each hero who doesn’t make it, maxing out at -30, which is significantly less than completing a single objective gives you, so all it really does is function as a tiebreaker in the rare event that both teams manage to reach the safehouse.

Left 4 Dead Versus did not have this problem - points are scored based on how far in the level each player progressed, and so a player who died early would contribute no points. This rewards teamplay and ensures that players with better teamwork come out on top, rather than godlike lone wolves.

A solo player may earn 100 points on his own track, and 30 points from his team’s track for his three comrades that he left to die in the first 10% of the level, but if the other team stays alive until they suddenly wipe halfway through, then they score 200 points total, and are ahead.

I suggest adding a score multiplier for newly scored points that slightly increases in severity for each dead player - maybe something like 0.9x, 0.8x, 0.7x. This means that YOLOing through the level will squeeze out significantly less points, and make teamwork the greater deciding factor for victory.
