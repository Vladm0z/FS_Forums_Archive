FatsharkLevCommunity Support • 2025-03-06 10:45:00

It can be tricky to pinpoint the cause of performance issues, and micro stutters specifically.

We’ve recently seen stutters linked to high mouse polling rate, so if you use a high poll rate please try lowering this to see if there’s any improvement.

Otherwise, our performance suggestions may be of help, here:
https://support.fatshark.se/hc/en-us/articles/360022719993--PC-How-to-Improve-Your-Performance-in-Vermintide-2

If the issue persists still, you could try a clean reinstallation of Vermintide 2 (please note any custom settings e.g. keybinds, graphics etc. will be reset):
https://support.fatshark.se/hc/en-us/articles/360034118394--PC-How-to-Perform-a-Clean-Re-Installation-of-Vermintide-2-or-Darktide

To note though, after a clean reinstall you’ll experience some temporary stutters as your shaders are recached. After a few games this should go away.
