VERSUS MVP CALCULATION (6.2.1)

Just count the green circles, you fool
Forget about circles (kinda), think about AWARDS. There’s 11 of them.

AWARDS

  • Hero Killer: Most Heroes eliminated as Pactsworn (worth 8 Points)
  • Slayer: Most Pactsworn eliminated as Hero (worth 8 Points)
  • Smiter: Highest Damage dealt to Pactsworn as Hero (worth 8 Points)
  • Damage Dealer: Highest Damage dealt to Heroes as Pactsworn (worth 8 Points)
  • Saviour: Most Revives(?) as Hero Most saves(???) as Hero (worth 7 Points) [most games end with all players having 0 saves and no one getting the award]
  • Hero Napper: Most Disables as Packmaster (worth 7 Points)
  • Assassin: Most Disables as Gutter Runner (worth 7 Points)
  • Horde Killer: Most Total Kills as Hero including AI enemies (worth 7 Points)
  • Bile Troll: Highest Damage dealt as Bile Troll (worth 7 Points)
  • Rat Ogre: Highest Damage dealt as Rat Ogre (worth 7 Points)
  • Monster Killer: Highest Damage dealt to Monster (worth 7 Points)

WHO GETS TO BE MVP

  • Each Player’s points gathered from Awards are added up.
  • Players with the most Award Points are MVP Candidates.
  • Picking MVP from Candidates:
  • If there’s only one Candidate → Candidate gets MVP Award.
  • If there’s more than one Candidate → Number of Candidates in Winning Team is looked at:
    – If there’s only one Candidate in the Winning Team → Singular Winning Team Candidate gets MVP Award.
    – If there’s more than one Candidate in the Winning Team → Winning Team Candidate with the Highest Damage dealt to Heroes AND Pactsworn summed up gets MVP Award.
    – If there’s no Candidate in the Winning Team → Losing Team Candidate with the Highest Damage dealt to Heroes AND Pactsworn summed up gets MVP Award.
