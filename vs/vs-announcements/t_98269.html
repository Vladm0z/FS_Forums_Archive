VS BAT Hotfix 3 - 29th of August

Fixes

  • Fixed an occasional crash when using the Bile Troll’s puke.
  • Fixed a bug where sometimes the Bile Troll’s puke indicator would not go away.
  • Fixed a spot in the first round of Righteous Stand where players could get stuck.
  • Fixed a bug with the cannon in the second round of Righteous Stand that would cause the event to not progress.
  • Fixed the Gutter Runner not having a pounce charge meter while in ghost mode.
  • Fixed a random piece of Tzeentchian geometry that was sticking out in Screaming Bell.
  • Fixed the safe zone in Screaming Bell and Righteous Stand to make it so Pactsworn can’t get in. Sneaky buggers.
  • Fixed an issue that let some Pactsworn spawn before Heroes in round 1 of Righteous Stand.
  • Fixed a bug where the Push to Talk keybind would activate if pressed even while writing in the chat.

Features & Tweaks

  • Tweaked the scoring in Screaming Bell so that it totals up to 300.

Balance

  • Damage of Warpfire thrower reduced from 3 per tick to 2 per tick.
  • Sped up the damage ramp-up of the Globadier.
