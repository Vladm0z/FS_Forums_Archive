You don’t need to change the ragdoll settings or anything, it is just a thing that happens no matter what.

It’s got much worse recently so people are noticing it more. I stream so I get to watch my gameplay back on delay on my second monitor and clip a LOT of buggy sh*t, and I notice the kill feed often fails to match what is going on in game.

It’s not so much the killfeed just not reporting the kills either, its sometimes the game not even registering the kill at all. If you run one of the simple kill tracker mods that just keeps a tally of what the game thinks you killed, you can kill things and it will not tick up and the killfeed won’t say anything.

Almost as annoying as soulblaze stealing every kill even when we get the last hit with Thammer or something.
