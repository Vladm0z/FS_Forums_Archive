Issue Type (Required):

Character Customisation

Issue Description (Required):

When equipping the prison garb (tank-top) cosmetic for veteran, certain helmets or masks remove facial tattoos. I’ve tried all face tattoos and different helmets. They all remove the tattoos. Effects the majority of the Krieg masks, all of the Krieg Commissar’s masks, Armageddon Helmets, Flak Helmets, Militarum Helmets with protectus masks, and Militia Helmet with flash goggles.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Equip prison garb upper body cosmetic on veteran to expose neck and face.
  2. Cycle through different masks or helmets.
  3. The Krieg masks are the most common offenders of this bug.
