Well.. not tiny, but definitely less than it used to be.

So, something is bugged.

In the Psykhanium, using the updated version of the Creature Spawner mod, I tried using the charged, and charging (that’s confusing sorry) attack to backstab each twin, as well as headshot each twin, and backstab a regular captain with its shield down. The regular captain took the expected large amount of damage, while no matter what I did to the twins, they took the same, significantly smaller amount of damage.

I thought maybe it was the relic blade so I tried using a Thunder Hammer instead, and got the same result. I’m not sure if its because the amount of damage the Twins can take is capped, or its some other bug.
