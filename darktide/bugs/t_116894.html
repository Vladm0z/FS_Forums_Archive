Possible to get stuck under staircase in Clandestum Gloriana

2 Posts • 37 Views

Issue Type (Required):

Missions

Issue Description (Required):

You can manage to get yourself irrevocably stuck under a staircase if you crawl under it. The only way out is a ‘sliding ledge’ which prevents you from jumping out again. So the only way out is death or leaving the mission.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play Clandestium Gloriana
  2. Navigate to the opening where the enemies begin, and look to the right.
    See Screenshot ‘1 - Opening.PNG’
  3. Get some height from the staircase, then ‘fall’ into the open area next to the staircase.
    See Screenshot ‘2 - Access.PNG’
    (Note: At this point you can still climb out by using the staircase as a ledge-climb)
  4. Crawl under the staircase.
    See Screenshot ‘3 - Hole.PNG’’
    Congratulations, you are now stuck.
  5. Whenever you try to climb out, the only viable area to get enough height to climb out from, is a ‘sliding ledge’ and so simply causes your character to slide back into the hole.
    See Screenshot ‘4 - Problem.PNG’ for an image of the sliding ledge I’m referring to.

Screenshots ‘5 - Prison.PNG’, ‘6 - Prison.PNG’, and ‘7 - Prison.PNG’ merely showcase the view from within the prison which you are now stuck.

Speculation: Your character height might have something to do with getting stuck/unstuck. Not sure, didn’t bother to test.

Mission Name (Optional):

Clandestium Gloriana
