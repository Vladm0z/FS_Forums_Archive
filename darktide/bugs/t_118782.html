Issue Description (Required):

Stuck spot out of bounds on No Man’s Land last map.
When you are fighting the Abomination and Ogre as the last two bosses before you extract from the campaign, it is possible to get knocked up into the air by the Abomination when it grabs another player and get flung onto one of the rooftops, mainly the one that is “armored” on the left side of the final “Compound” you fight in.
When on the roof, the edges are too high to vault over or jump from as the player is sank into the texture with no escape, unless he disconnects and a bot is joined that teleports to the other players.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play the No Man’s land campaign
  2. Get to the end of it to the double “boss battle”
  3. Stand near the Abomination as it grabs a player
  4. Get accidentally flung up onto the rooftop
  5. Stuck on the rooftop with no way down
