Weapons can either physically hit meaning the weapon impacted the targets hitbox, or cleave into a target meaning you physically hit Target 1’s hitbox, and then cleaved through them into Target 2,3,4 etc. The game will register the first target as a hit - and most talents that trigger off of hits will also include the cleaved hits as a hit even though you didn’t physically hit their hitbox with the swing.

Good example of this is the talent Suppression Protocols - this will trigger if you cleave into 3 or more targets. Which I suspect is not working for Hammer of Judgement. Hammer of Judgement is not triggering unless it is a physical hitbox being registered. Another reason why Brutal Momentum with either the Tac Axe or Combat axe will trigger Suppression Protocols when Brutal Momentum kicks in but Hammer of Judgement does not. Because Brutal Momentum only registered the first hitbox - and cleaved targets register the remaining hits.

The only weapon I’ve successfully got over 10% uptime with Hammer of Judgement is the Crusher - which has extremely wide and long arcs with its swings. Causing the weapon to physically come in contact with the targets hitbox before cleaving. Smaller weapons like the shock mauls and axes rely on cleave and cleave targets to trigger hit X targets with melee hits.
