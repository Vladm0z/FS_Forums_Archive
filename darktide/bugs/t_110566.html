I’ve been trying to figure out what rhyme or reason is behind registering kills, and I think perhaps there is some kind of conflict when multiple Arbites are in the game with Execution Order.

One game I got about 200 kills, and then most games I get 1-20 kills, despite performing about the same.
So I was trying to run my head through what caused me to gain so many more kills on the penance that game… well, I was the only Arbites.
At first I thought it was my ranged weapon only registering the kills, because I primarily used my ranged weapon that game.
But then I tested it the next game, and only got 2 kills registered.
Well that game there were 4 Arbites, and all of us had Execution order, likely trying to get the penance.

I’m thinking that when one Arbites Execution Order is marked on a target, and another Arbites Execution Order also marks that same target, there is some sort of conflict with the registering of the kill.

And because everybody is trying to get that penance now after they reduced the requirement, it has become far harder to get the penance.
The bug was probably present before, just not noticable most of the time, as most people don’t run Execution Order.
Forceful and Terminus Warrant are just so much better.

However, every single time my doge makes a kill, credit is given for the penace.
Of course I had the talent “Not Far Behind” on there, and I’m thinking maybe that might be causing some sort of conflict, especially seeing how the doges were recently nerfed not to give 450% more damage.

I’m also rocking Unleashed Brutality, Canine Morale, Voltaic Mandibles, Remote Detonation, Man & Cyber-Mastiff, Razor-Jaw Augment and will look to see which ones are causing a conflict.
My bet would be Not Far Behind or Unleashed Brutality causing the conflict as they both revolve around the Mastiff and Elites, at least one’s parent talent does.

EDIT 2:
After further investigation, there appears to be no correlation with Not Far Behind/Unleashed Brutality and the Execution Order penance.
