Zealot's Chorus of Spiritual Fortitude ability needs 0.5s delay before it can be deactivated

Issue Type (Required):

Player Mechanics

Issue Description (Required):

I decided to post it here, under Bugs category, instead of Feedback - because I really think it’s a serious usability issue in controls department, not just some kind of improvement that could be just desirable.

Some context: often you need to activate the ability as soon as it’s of CD, usually because you’re in a tight pinch and it’s your getaway card. Often in this game you’ll be stun locked with your ability isn’t triggering until you are out of it; sometimes your button presses may not be even registered (client issues) - all those leading to you often pressing activation button several times in a row to make sure it activates. Or you may start pressing button rapidly half a second before the ability is off CD, to activate it asap.

Current behavior: if CSF is out of CD already, if you press activation button 2-3 times rapidly, it will be activated and then immediately deactivated, effectively wasting your getaway card. There is literally no usecases when you would appreciate this ability to be immediately deactivated like that.

Proposed behavior: there should be a 0.5-0.75s delay window after its activation where subsequent presses of the activation button are ignored, preventing accidental extra presses from deactivating it before it has any effect at all.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Load into Psykarium as Zealot with CSF ability chosen
  2. Wait until it’s off CD
  3. Press its activation button several times rapidly. You’ll see the ability activating and immediately deactivating
  4. Wait until it’s almost out of CD again, and start pressing the activation button rapidly, simulating a case when you want it to trigger the moment it’s out of CD. Chances are high you’ll see it activating and deactivating instantly too.
