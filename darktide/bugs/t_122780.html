Issue Type (Required):

Player Mechanics

Issue Description (Required):

When swapping weapons while sprinting forward as a Skitarri, the weapon swap animation cancels the sprint for the duration of the animation, causing the player to stutter. Rapidly switching weapons causes the player’s sprint to slow to a crawl. This occurs on all weapons.
