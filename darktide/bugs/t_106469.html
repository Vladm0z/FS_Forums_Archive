Heinous Rituals (Havoc) can be triggered through walls and become uninterruptable

5 Posts • 340 Views
B
Bokosatsu • 2025-03-28 06:59:53

Issue Type (Required):

Enemy Mechanics

Issue Description (Required):

Heinous Rituals located in non-linear portions of a map can be triggered by proximity through walls, despite the path to that location being quite far away. This makes the ritual is essentially uninterruptible.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play Comms-Plex 154/2f on Havoc with the Heinous Ritual modifier
  2. Finish the mid-level event and ascend the elevator
  3. Move forward towards the lowered floor. On the left side is a tall barrier/wall (that enemies can climb over)
  4. Heinous Ritual can spawn and trigger on the other side of the wall, despite requiring players to travel past the location and U-turn through the next chamber before reaching the ritual.
  5. Move onwards until reaching the next medicae station
  6. In the room to the right and down the stairs (with the large machines) another ritual can spawn. This one can be bypassed but once you proceed onward and attempt to cross the bridge, it will trigger through the wall and require backtracking to reach the ritual.

Mission Name (Optional):

Comms-Plex 154/2f

Yes, but I haven’t tried disabling them
