Issue Description (Required):

While the Rock is equipped (especially with it’s upgrade node), it seems the game always treats both it’s ‘cooldown’ and the weakspot/miss refund cooldown as being active on the ‘buffs to display’ hotbar. This makes it insanely annoying to keep track of your buffs without mods (is making me consider getting a UI mod now, where as before I didn’t really feel the need for one/could usually see what was ‘most important’ at a glance), where as the other two blitz’s don’t have this issue (for obvious reasons). Just started working on a rock build today and was wondering why I wasn’t seeing Lucky Bullet and all it’s additions along with No Hurt Friends! like at all while I was playing (didn’t even see Bruiser pop up), and it seems to be entirely related to the rocks UI element having way to high priority and just always existing regardless of if it’s in effect or not. It should probably get drastically lowered as it’s CD is not nearly as important as basically everything else (like how resupply on the veteran works).

Bruiser Also seems to have a bit to low of a priority, as I basically never see it pop up on my gun builds, and can sometimes lose it in my melee builds as well. Though this probably isn’t a probbblem as it still effects the CDR and one can notice that by looking at the cooldown working on the CA, but I personally would still like to seee it when I proc it before it gets shoved away quickly.

The root cause for this bug is in HudElementPlayerBuffs._add_buff where buffs beyond the MAX_BUFF value are simply ignored. MAX_BUFFs is 20, with only 15 going to actual buffs and 5 going to debuffs. And that 15 is not buffs that are shown, but all buffs that are tracked - which might include many invisible ones. So you might have less than 10 buffs showing but are already at the max.

Simple fix would be to increase the max buffs in hud_element_player_buffs_settings.lua
A better fix (if you want to keep the current max count) is to change the logic to track ALL buffs in the HUD and apply the max buff restriction only to how many buffs are visible at the same time..
