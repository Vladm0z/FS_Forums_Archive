Issue Type (Required):

Animation

Issue Description (Required):

While sprinting with the special animation (sword held in front), when activating the relic blade in that state, if you stop sprinting right after activation, you are locked into the full activation animation before you can attack.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Equip a Relic Blade
  2. Sprint long enough to get the special ‘sword held out in front’ animation
  3. While sprinting in that state, activate the weapon special.
  4. Stop sprinting right after activating the weapon.
  5. You cannot attack until the full activation animation is done.

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, and I’ve tried disabling them

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)

Platform (Required):

PC - Steam
