Issue Type (Required):

Visual

Issue Description (Required):

When swapping/changing cosmetics on the Skitarii, parts of the model will begin to glow with whatever color lights are attached to that part of the model. Backpacks will have a blue glow, and my chest cosmetic will glow with a blend of my eye colors. I have also noted some rather checkerboard-esque patterns that don’t glow. This does not appear to be limited to the Premium Edition cosmetics, and happens to the underlying body as well. This does also appear to happen randomly, even without changing cosmetics, but I’m not sure how to replicate that case.

My drivers are up to date (updated 6/20 and last checked 6/23), and I have an AMD Radeon RX 9070 XT. This does not appear to happen with other classes, or any other object that is unrelated to the Skitarii.

Apologies for poor video quality, the 30MB upload limit kinda killed it.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play a Skitarius
  2. Open the Cosmetics menu and change any cosmetic, or go to the Barber and alter any portion of your character (changing eye color seems to make it happen faster)
  3. Behold the Blinding Light of the Omnissiah’s Glory
