Heavy Hitter incompatible with No Pushover

8 Posts • 130 Views
B
Berdy • 2025-05-11 09:17:06

Issue Type (Required):

Talents

Issue Description (Required):

While testing out some Ogryn builds in the Meat Grinder, I noticed that I wasn’t getting any Heavy Hitter stacks on either light or heavy attacks. After some troubleshooting, I found that while I had the talent No Pushover equipped, I could not gain any heavy Hitter stacks, but when I removed it, I gained Heavy hitter stacks just fine.

I removed different perks and talents from the build, and it appeared that it didn’t matter what other perks I took, the only thing that could prevent me from gaining stacks of Heavy Hitter was whether or not I had No Pushover.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play Ogryn
  2. Enter any mission (I did not test in a live game, only in missions started with the SoloPlay mod as I did not want to disrupt anyone’s game)
  3. Take any talent build that includes both the Heavy Hitter Keystone, and the talent No Pushover.
  4. Hit things and be sad.

Video shows me in Meat Grinder. I start with No Pushover, and I get no stacks of Heavy Hitter, then swap it out and start getting Heavy Hitter stacks.

Yes, but I haven’t tried disabling them
