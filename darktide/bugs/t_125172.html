Issue Description (Required):

Every time when equipping the Phosphor blast pistol - the first chamber of the revolver cylinder appeares to not properly centered relative to the barrel.
Almost a half of that cylinder is seen rotated out of alignment and so stays offset rightwards in rotation.
After firing a shot - the chamber of the revolver cylinder seems to be perfectly alligned to the barrel.
Until the next use of Phosphor pistol.
Knowing that a great deal of time has been devoted to creating high-quality models of each weapon, I find it unacceptable for the Omnissiah to be aware of such a visual defect. Especially since this would result in a 100 percent misfire and destruction for the revolver
