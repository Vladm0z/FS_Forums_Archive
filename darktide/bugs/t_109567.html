Issue Description (Required):

All decals for the Arbitrator are mis aligned and incorrect for All Chest pieces and the Deluxe Editions Legs.

The decals are correct in some of the Promotional still images and the Deluxe Edition’s Promotional images, but are bugged in the Video Promotion material.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Open Warhammer 40,000 Darktide
  2. Select Arbitrator
  3. Go to chest cosmetics
  4. Inspect selected chest piece
  5. Cycle to Mannequin to view misaligned decal visuals
  6. Go to leg cosmetics
  7. Inspect ‘Arbtrator Protector Fatigues’ (Deluxe Edition one)
  8. Cycle between Mannequin and Player Character

Yes, and I’ve tried disabling them

Constant (100%)
