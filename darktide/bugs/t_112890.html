Issue Type (Required):

Talents

Issue Description (Required):

There is currently a bugged interaction between Scrier’s Gaze with Warp Unbound (SG) / Venting Shriek (VS) and the new Crystalline Will talent. Activating either of these abilities during an overload (when peril is pushed beyond 100%, but the psyker has not yet gone down or taken corruption damage) will immediately trigger corruption damage and prevent the psyker’s AoE damage explosion from taking place.

The Warp Unbound modifier talent for Scrier’s Gaze should prevent overloading from taking place and cancel an overload in progress the same way Venting Shriek does, but when paired with Crystalline Will these abilities instead inflict corruption damage with no upside. Without the Crystalline Will talent active, both of these abilities work normally. Additionally, Crystalline Will works correctly if an overload occurs but neither of these abilities are used. You can have SG or VS in your build and CW will still work, as long as you don’t use them to cancel an overload.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

Take either Scrier’s Gaze + Warp Unbound or Venting Shriek and the Crystalline Will talent.
Drive peril to 100% and overload.
During the brief window between the start of the overload and the damage explosion, trigger either SG or VS. Activating either of these abilities will immediately trigger corruption damage while cancelling the AoE damage explosion.

You can verify that AoE damage does not occur by going into the Meat Grinder and standing in the middle of a group of mobs. Trigger overload by using a staff or blitz, but staff is more efficient. Trigger SG or VS. Look around, you will see that none of the enemies around you have been knocked back or damaged.

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)

Platform (Required):

PC - Steam
