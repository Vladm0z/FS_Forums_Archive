Issue Type (Required):

Player Mechanics

Issue Description (Required):

While working on penances for my Arbites, I did a Maelstrom mission with the Enhanced Blitz modifier while having the Remote Detonation Blitz equipped, the one where your dog can stun enemies around it and it regenerates charges after a short time, with it normally having 2 charges. With Enhanced Blitz you start with 4 charges, but once you reach two charges or less, you don’t regenerate the extra 2 charges from the modifier and are left with the default amount of two Detonation charges, even after dying and being rescued. The only thing is I haven’t tested what happens when you have 3 charges, though I would suspect it fails to regenerate even then.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play as Arbites in Maelstrom with Enhanced Blitz modifier while using Remote Detonation

  2. Use at least 2 of the 4 charges you get of Remote Detonation and observe how they will not regenerate back to 4, staying at 2 for the remainder of the mission.

Constant (100%)
