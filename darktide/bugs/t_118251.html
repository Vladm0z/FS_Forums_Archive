Issue Description (Required):

I have encountered an issue where my character gets stuck when sprinting up different sets of stairs or gets stuck in crouch position when sliding inadvertently under map objects (lamps, boxes, vehicles, statues).

This has specifically happened on multiple runs in Archivum Sycorax. When sprinting up the stairs that lead to the elevator in the final Servo Skull scan at the end of the mission, my character gets stuck in a frozen sprint loop in the middle of the stairs going up.

Also, when sliding around the hallways near the lamps/firepits trying to evade an enemy melee hit or rescue a teammate. I will get stuck under the floor lamp or other object in the crouch position and I can’t move.

I’ve attached photos of the specific areas/objects.

I have experienced this same issue on other maps as well (Refinery Delta, Spireside, Warren-19) with several different classes as well (Ogryn, Psyker, Vet, Zealot).
