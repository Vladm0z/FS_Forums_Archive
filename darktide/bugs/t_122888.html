Issue Type (Required):

Player Mechanics

Issue Description (Required):

When charging a stab or any heavy move with the Relic Blade and then releasing as the Fury of the Faithful charge ends, it results in a completely different move.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Have Fury of the Faithful (+ modifiers if you want)
  2. Have a Relic Blade (either mark works)
  3. Charge any heavy move, and then do a Fury of the Faithful charge fairly close. Release the attack as the charge ends. Repeat at various distances.
  4. See different mapped moves come out. If the Fury of the Faithful charge distance is significant enough for the move to be ‘primed’, the original heavy move will come out. This is most troubling for point blank moves or midrange moves.

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

No, I don’t use mods

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)

Platform (Required):

PC - Steam
