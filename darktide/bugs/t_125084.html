Remove anything and everything in the poxburster behavior settings/parameters related to PUSH, STAGGER, and KNOCKDOWN Resistance against players. From the slightest maximum distance push to an allied Mutant charge or Hound pounce, primed to fall down and explode from ANY source. From barrel explosions to a Psyker passing warp wind.

Whatever system you had before in regards to poxburster stagger behavior, it simply doesn’t work. IT NEVER DID.
