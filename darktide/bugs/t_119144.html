Issue Type (Required):

Text & Localisation

Issue Description (Required):

During the mission briefing for Archivum Sycorax, the spoken dialogue and subtitle text do not match.

Morrow’s voice line says:

“Get in, access the Deep Archives and bring us back something juicy. We’ve got seditionists to root out.”

However, the subtitle displays:

“Get in, access the Deep Archives and bring us back something juicy. We’ve seditionists to root out.”

The word “got” is missing in the subtitle text.

Expected Result:
The subtitle should match the spoken dialogue:

“We’ve got seditionists to root out.”

Actual Result:
Subtitle reads:

“We’ve seditionists to root out.”

Notes:
This appears to be a simple subtitle script mismatch rather than an audio issue.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Start a mission with the Archivum Sycorax briefing.
  2. Listen to Morrow’s dialogue during the loading screen.
  3. Observe the subtitle line when he says the final sentence.
