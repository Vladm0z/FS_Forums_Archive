Now that the game has had the ability for talent trees to be “illegal” (nodes without connecting bridge to the source), I think an update should not cut and forget talents selected after the point of the first illegal lack of bridging.

I suggest that the talent trees will keep ALL selected nodes (based on name) that were previously selected and keep them ALL selected in the new patch, even if this may result in multiple unbridged nodes.

Example of previous patch:

How it worked on the previous talent tree rework, on hive scum, was that the system simply forgot everything after the first illegal lack of bridge due to the movement of the talent selections, causing everything after the blitz choice to be forgotten.

Example of how it ought to work:

If the revamped talent tree layout leads to a, for example, psyker’s skill tree choices of Ability (Telekine Shield, for example) and Keystone being illegal after the update due to the psyker player not having picked Psykinetic’s Aura or One With the Warp in the previous patch (both of which will be new bottlenecks), it should not forget the choices of nodes after Psykinetic’s Aura or One With The Warp in the new layout.

The question(s), which I would prefer the community managers to answer are these:

  1. Is the development team aware of the fact that previously when a talent tree was revamped, that the tree forgot all choices made after the first new illegal lack of connection?

  2. Is this new system (one in which the system does remember nodes even though they would be behind an illegal lack of bridge) feasible to be implemented for the patch?

  3. Is this new system feasible to be addressed with a hotfix after the patch, if the timetable does not allow for it to be implemented before patch release date?
