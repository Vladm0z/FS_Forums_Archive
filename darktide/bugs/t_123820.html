Issue Type (Required):

Blessings

Issue Description (Required):

The newly added Plasma Gun Mk III has no synergy with the “Shattering Impact” blessing. Now, the wording does make it seem like that should be normal, but that begs the question, is having it work with the Ogryn’s Grenadier Gauntlet and Rumbler a bug aswell? The way it functions on those weapons is:
The initial projectile hitting the enemy applies 4 stacks of 2.5% brittleness, then the explosion applies another 4, so this way you can apply 8 stacks of 2.5% to one enemy and 4 stacks to anything hit by the explosion. For the plasma gun, 8 stacks of brittleness are applied to the first target hit. 4 from the hitscan hit and 4 from the explosion. Other targets hit by the explosion don’t receive the 4 stacks.

I am using mods that display debuffs so that I can properly demonstrate the bug. Tested in both the meat grinder and a real mission.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

Without a mod that shows debuffs you would have to just look at the numbers yourself on a carapace enemy, but obviously it’s as simple as just firing a charged shot at a mixed horde with a crusher in it for example.

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, but I haven’t tried disabling them
