Issue Type (Required):

Matchmaking

Issue Description (Required):

A buddy of mine and I want to play Expeditions in a private game.
Through the Mission Terminal Options, one can choose to enable private game.

However, whenever either of us enables private game, we are unable to launch any mission on the Expeditions board. Let me also put emphasis on the fact that we are indeed already in a strike team of two players at this point. This must obviously be a bug, because if private games were not a feature in Expeditions, we naturally would not have the option to enable private game in the Mission Terminal Options.

This is extremely annoying to say the least, and I expect you to iron this out ASAP ZULU as I consider this a bare minimum requirement gameplay-wise.

You cannot give players the authority to enable private game and not let the players launch missions when simply choosing something that is clearly available.

Thank you in advance for ameliorating this issue.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Enter the Expeditions terminal.
  2. In the Mission Terminal Options, opt for private game.
  3. You are now unable to launch any mission from the mission board.
