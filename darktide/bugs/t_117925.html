I would like to start by saying these issues are not because of mods, it seems like that’s the go-to catch-all explanation for every issue, but it’s just not true. Whether modded or not Darktide ALWAYS has the same problems. Consistent disconnects, crashes, and latency. Is this because of the Amazon servers? The other major issues, which never seem to get addressed, are…

  1. Dogs/bursters not reacting to being pushed - This literally happens EVERY SINGLE MISSION. Sometimes when attempting to push a burster or a dog that is jumping at you the game will just not register your push and you will get pounced on/blown up anyway. It’s 100% unavoidable and such a horrible bug. I have over 2,000 hours in the game and this has been an issue the entire time.

  2. Audio-cue issues - This also has been a problem for as long as I have been playing. Silent bursters, trappers, etc. Quite frequently they don’t make a sound until it’s too late (the dogs already on you, net is being shot, etc.). Sometimes there is so much going on the game just abruptly cuts enemy audio altogether, and due to how jarring and awkward the sound cuts are it is without question a bug.

What is the reason why these issues never get resolved? With how much these issues have been reported over the years you certainly know of them. I love Darktide, but the never-ending instability is killing any motivation I have to even play it anymore.
