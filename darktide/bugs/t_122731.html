Pasting an edited post I did in another thread about this penance:

I finished the penance yesterday, and the best way to finish ‘Curse of Malfunction’ is to play Auric Expeditions and run away from your team so you are completely solo.

Wait for two charges, find groups of Gunners/Reapers and aggro them (they need to be shooting at you first or it won’t count), then use ‘Voltaic Emitter’.

The problem is not doing all of these things in itself, but that the penance is so glitchy so even if you do everything exactly right (two charges, wait for enemies to shoot, be solo), you will not always get progress for the penance for every enemy hit even though they get jammed (animation).

Also, keep in mind that if ‘Voltaic Emitter’ kills an enemy, you won’t get credit and progress.

Since it’s glitchy there is never a point where you want to hit only one-two enemies with ‘Voltaic Emitter’ - you always want to hit a big cluster of enemies preferable more than five ranged elites.
