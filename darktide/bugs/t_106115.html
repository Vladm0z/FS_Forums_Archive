HarridasRegular • 2025-03-23 00:04:28

Issue Type (Required):

Enemy Mechanics

Issue Description (Required):

Trapper nets have an extremely small collision point but the actual net hitbox itself is much larger than it, leading to scenarios where trappers can net players through very tight geometry where the trapper is barely or not even visible as it nets the player. And I ran into a particularly nasty sightline where trappers can shoot at players nearly risk-free at the Relay Station mid-event.
This is a general issue though that’s been reported since before the game even launched. The trapper net can no longer ignore certain terrain like airtight fences or stairs since, but it can still go through really tight gaps.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  • Have trapper(s) shooting at you around corners or through tight sight lines.

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)

Platform (Required):

PC - Steam

They would NOT have to re-work the physics to fix this. All they would have to do is increase the size of the projectile for the fired net to a reasonable size so that it would collide with walls and other surfaces instead of going through.

Looking at the source code, it appears that the net is treated a particle (perhaps “0” size?). Which means if the trapper has line-of-sight on the player to start the shot, the net will just go through any surface on it’s path.

You can actually see the net go through floors and other objects, so this is probably an accurate description of how it works.

So, basically, if the trapper has line-of-sight to target you and you are in-range, the net will go through anything in it’s path and will trap you if you don’t pass the dodge-check.
