Issue Description (Required):

Darktide has an issue in which all player inputs and gameplay has to be determined by the dedicated server.

For example actions such as moving, attacking, blocking, special activation, dodging, sprinting, swapping, scanning, reviving, interacting, crouching, sliding, jumping, ability use, tagging, applying stimms, deploying items and giving items are all player actions that the server can and will undo during gameplay. Resulting in jittery gameplay, inputs that are undone or get stuck, hit registration problems, animations being skipped or undone, getting hit by attacks you dodged or blocked, interaction bars that have been completed being undone, being unable to give items or stimms to your teammates, weapon swaps getting botched or undone, projectiles being thrown or shot where you were previously aiming or not even aiming at all, using your ability and everyone hearing the voice line but the ability wasn’t actually used, trying to move around but the server teleports you back, throwing or deploying items and the server undoing that action and more. It’s a constant problem that is present everywhere in gameplay and haunts Darktide. It results in a gameplay experience that feels very weird, and at times very terrible to play. It makes playing with others across regions feel terrible. This is not how a multiplayer game should feel.
I have made a post further detailing what I think on it here:

Server authoritative gameplay is terrible and needs to goGameplay Feedback
