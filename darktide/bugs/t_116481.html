Issue Description (Required):

After the most recent Hotfix #83 - 1.10.2, the collar headlights on Zealot’s “Magistratum Mk V Patrol Armour” were changed, or rather were “turned on” without any notice, warning, or indication in the patch notes.

In all honesty it’s a bit too bright for my tastes, uncomfortably so, and I think it detracts from the aesthetic of the original outfit (it already has the ICONIC Zealot shoulder candles for crying out loud!!!). I cannot recall any other cosmetics with lights that have been released thus far that are as jarringly bright.

I liked the way the armor looked before a LOT better (with the lights off) and that is what I originally paid for! Please revert this change or give us the option to choose between having the lights on or off via a separate torso armor piece!
