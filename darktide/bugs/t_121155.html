Issue Description (Required):

First noted by @sunmelt, filing a bug for this.

When teammates kill elites/specials to proc Survivalist, the aura does not go on cooldown. It visually goes on cooldown when the Vet aura owner kills an elite/special but based on some limited testing it seems like teammates are still able to proc the aura though the Vet cannot.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play Veteran with Survivalist
  2. Have teammate kill an elite/special while in coherency
  3. Aura does not go on cooldown and can be proc’d again by either the Vet or another teammate.

Second Scenario:

  1. Play Veteran with Survivalist
  2. Kill an elite/special as Vet.
  3. Aura goes on CD
  4. Teammate kills elite/special while Aura still on CD.
  5. Aura is proc’d again.

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, but I haven’t tried disabling them

Constant (100%)
