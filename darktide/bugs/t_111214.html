image1304×963 283 KB

You can also easily reproduce it with the Many More Try mod, which can let you start a mission that has already left the mission board(kinda does what quickplay is supposed to do). Literally pick any mission that’s at least older than 20-30 minutes(since thats how long missions are on the board and usually how long a mission can take to finish) and you will USUALLY load into an empty mission and you will not get teammates no matter how long you wait, until the mission expires after ~3 hours.

There is also the chance to load into an ongoing game if you use Many More Try and just so happen to pick a mission with a team that has an open spot(s) available.

The main issue itself, quickplay not joining into ongoing games that have left the mission board, started after the mission board rework/ Battle for Tertium update. Missions went from lasting 24 hours to just over 3. I never did extensive testing but quickplay used to load you into games that were up to ~1-2 hours old, after that quickplay would be disabled and the missions would go on ‘empty’ for the next 22ish hours, unless you used Many More Try to start one. But since the whole mission board rework, as soon as the mission leaves the board, quickplay seemingly get turned off like it used to after a couple hours.

Now there is 1 thing I kinda like about it being this way, its way easier to start solo missions this way since its so easy to replicate, so for any Solo Mode enjoyers this is the best thing they have until an offical solo mode is added if that ever happens
