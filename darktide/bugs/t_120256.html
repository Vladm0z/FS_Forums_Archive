Issue Type (Required):

Penances

Issue Description (Required):

The Data Harvester penance asks the player to extract or deposit 100 reliquaries total with the help of their team. However, this currently has 2 issues:

  1. Reliquaries deposited by teammates inside sanctuaries don’t count (despite the wording implying that it would, since the Tech-Hunter penance uses the same wording and tracks all tech-remnants extracted by the full team).

  2. Reliquaries brought onto the valkyrie extraction, even though they are converted into tech-remnants correctly after the match, don’t actually contribute towards the penance.

As such, the only way to progress the penance right now is to be the person holding a reliquary, before reaching the final zone in an expeditions run, and depositing it inside a sanctuary.

I gotta be owed at least like 5 progress on this penance by now.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Track Data Harvester penance, and get into an expeditions match with at least 2 players on the team (including yourself).

  3. Observe the penance tracker not updating.
  4. Find a data reliquary in the last zone, and bring it onto the valkyrie extraction.
  5. After the end of the match, observe the penance tracker not updating.
