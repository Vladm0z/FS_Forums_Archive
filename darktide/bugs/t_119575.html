Issue Type (Required):

Issue Description (Required):

Channeled Devastation says it’ll give stacks based on Scum’s crit chance (one stack for every 1% crit chance, up to 30). If a Scum has Chem. Dependency with Chem Enhanced, they’re supposed to get +5% crit chance per stack, up to 15% normally, or 20% with Maxed Out Chems, which should translate to 15 stacks for Channeled Devastation or 20 with MOC. However, I see the Scum gaining Channeled Devastation stacks from other crit sources (such as weapon blessings like Shred) but not from Chem Dependency/Chem Enhanced.

Funny enough, I’ve gone into the psykhanium with a crowbar equipped beforehand that has plus 5% crit chance on it. So I see my stacks of Channeled Devastation start at 20, but when I go to use a stim, during the animation it drops down to 15 because I don’t have the crowbar in my hand anymore, but then the crowbar comes back up and it’s at 20 stacks again instead of the 25 it should be, if I understand these perks correctly.

I would check Channeled Devastation’s interactions in general. Assuming Scum has a 10% base crit chance, and I take the aura and passive node to give me another 10%, shouldn’t I be at 20 stacks of Channeled Devastation regardless of my equipped weapon? Because it gives me 15 if I don’t have any weapon with crit effects on it equipped.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play Scum with the Chem Enhanced and Channeled Devastation perks. Have any stim handy.
  2. Observe current stacks of Channeled Devastation.
  3. Use equipped stim.
  4. Observe current stacks of Channeled Devastation again. If I understand how these perks interact correctly, you should have gained five stacks of Channeled Devastation for having 1 stack of +5% crit chance from Chemical Dependency/Chem Enhanced. If you haven’t, you’ve encountered the bug.

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, and I’ve tried disabling them. First noticed issue with mods disabled, mods helped confirm issue.
