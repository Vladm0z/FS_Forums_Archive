isopod2137:

Pounced target receives Bleed occasionally. Unclear which outcome is intended here.

The pounced target receiving bleed stacks is arguably unintended. Explosions have a close radius and a max radius. Each radius has a damage profile assigned. The talent is set up to apply bleed to targets between the close radius (1m) and the max radius (2m) where the specified damage profile applies; targets within the close radius – which naturally includes the pounced target itself – should not have bleed applied to them.

Here’s a crude graphic, highlighted green is the AoE applying bleed:

ExKmdpH300×300 14.4 KB

My working theory how the pounced target can be affected as well is that the target hit zone (center_mass), which is targeted by the explosion, sometimes ends up in the AoE, considering that hitbox size might vary and that hitbox alignments can be janky while enemies are in motion.
