Issue Description (Required):

There is incorrect knife grip (positioning) in character’s hand in Loadout menu.

The character holds it facing inward, not outward so let it be. BUT… the grip is incorrect in that position. The cutting edge of the blade razor faces inward, while the back of the blade faces outward. Should be performed a 180-degree axial rotation to ensure the correctness of holding the knife in character’s hand.

It is clearly seen when performing power attacks in 1st person view. There you can see that the knife grip is proper - the cutting edge of the blade razor always faces outward, while the back of the blade - inward.

Bytheway, there must be checked also 3rd person knife swing animation to ensure correctness there as well. Was not able to check it by myself, unfortunately.
