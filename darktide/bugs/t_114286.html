Since everyone else is being vague or just outright trolling, I’ll give you your actual answer:

This is not a bug. You played a “Starvation Riots” Event modifier mission, which is active in Event missions when the community destroys more rations than recovered the previous day. Among other effects, this will double the amount of ammunition players can carry in reserve.
