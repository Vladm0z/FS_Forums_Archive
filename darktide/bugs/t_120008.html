The "Death's Head" Weapon Skin missing speedloader texture

Issue Type (Required):

Cosmetics

Issue Description (Required):

The “Death’s Head” Weapon skin, which is the one with the golden skull and faux wooden flourishes with gold inlays, misses the proper speedloader that matches it.

Compare this to the “Death’s Head Stub Revolver” Weapon skin, which is the one with the White skull and peeling gold decor on a black metallic base, which has the matching speedloader correctly displayed.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

Equip the “Death’s Head” Weapon Skin for the Agripinaa Mk XIV Quickdraw Stub Revolver.

Reload and see the missing speedloader (the device on which the bullets are mounted which is used to insert them to the chambers) texture, being replaced with the default.

Contrast with “Death’s Head Stub Revolver” Weapon Skin

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)

Platform (Required):

PC - Steam
