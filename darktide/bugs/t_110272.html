Issue Type (Required):

Visual

Issue Description (Required):

Dead enemies that are piled up keep ragdolling/colliding with each other, resulting in constant spawns of new blood decals.

For the evidence video:

  1. Max Blood Decals and Decal Lifetime are set to the maximum.
  2. Notice how the decals from the poxwalker on the right almost immediately disappear after I shoot at it? That means the pile on the left keeps spawning hundreds of new decals every few seconds until the pile sets. Every new ragdoll or ragdoll interaction with the pile refreshes the issue.

Here’s a video from November 2022 where the same issue can be observed: https://youtu.be/4QJqEwCerZU?si=PMVxgfpsMM_vdQ93&t=183

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Set Max Blood Decals and Decal Lifetime to the maximum
  2. Enter the Meat Grinder in the Psykhanium
  3. Spawn >15 enemies on the same spot. I recommend doing it next to a wall or vertical geometry.
  4. Kill enemies to create a pile and observe the issue

Yes, and I’ve tried disabling them
