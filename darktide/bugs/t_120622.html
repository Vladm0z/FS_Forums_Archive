After some playing around it would seem that the vast majority of chestpiece cosmetics in the game don’t have ANY offset whatsoever when it comes to backpack cosmetics and as such most backpack cosmetics clip into the chestpiece like that.

And the backpack offset is most definitely set for each chestpiece cosmetic separately, it is not set for the backpack cosmetic itself. It doesn’t even matter whether the chestpiece cosmetics are shop cosmetics or are earned for free.

My impression is that Fatshark does not have this parameter as part of their QA and it’s up to the artists creating the cosmetic to set it up at all and most artists simply don’t bother and leave it at 0 or whatever the default is.
