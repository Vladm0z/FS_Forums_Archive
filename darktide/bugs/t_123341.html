Issue Type (Required):

Player Mechanics

Issue Description (Required):

There might be an inconsistency between where the server running the game thinks clients are looking and what players see on their screen. This aim lag has been present since the game released. I notice it most when using the Psyker’s staves’ basic projectile attacks & the Voidstrike charged attack.

There’s a delay between inputting an attack and the ball shooting out, which I understand is intentional, BUT if you were tracking an enemy moving sideways or vertically, the attack will be sent where you were aiming when you first inputted for it instead of where your aim currently is. It may be network related as it doesn’t occur in the Psykanium, only real missions, and this didn’t occur with Sienna’s staves in Vermintide 2, which I know uses P2P and therefor different networking.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Bring any Psyker staff that isn’t Purgatus into an online game
  2. Initiate a basic attack and quickly aim somewhere else before the actual ball is fired
  3. Observe the attack being fired towards where you were initially aiming instead of towards your current point of aim.

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, and I’ve tried disabling them

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)

Platform (Required):
