Sorry to hear this @Slaughterhaus. Two things I recommend to try here!

Firstly, please perform a clean reinstallation of Darktide, which involves deleting your AppData, to rule out any issue with your game files or settings (please note custom settings e.g. keybinds, graphics, loadouts etc. will be reset):
https://support.fatshark.se/hc/en-us/articles/360034118394--PC-How-to-Perform-a-Clean-Re-Installation-of-Vermintide-2-or-Darktide

If the crashes persist after doing so, next please check your motherboard manufacturer’s website for any available BIOS updates; as outdated BIOS, especially with Ryzen CPU’s, can lead to instability. With firmware updates we advise caution, and to do so at your own risk.
