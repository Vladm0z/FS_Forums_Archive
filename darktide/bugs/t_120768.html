Ogryn Daggers have wrong weapon charm locations

1 Posts • 36 Views
M
Mavrik • 2026-04-10 09:42:56

Issue Type (Required):

Cosmetics

Issue Description (Required):

All Ogryn dagger marks have their weapon charms linked to the center of the dagger instead of on their designed charm sockets. This lends some of the daggers to have the correct location on the hilt of the blades, while most have them floating inside the blades, instead of their correct locations on the bottom sockets of the guards.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Equip a dagger on an Ogryn
  2. Select a customization option for the dagger marks and choose a weapon charm.
  3. See that all charms attach to the middle of the daggers, instead of being middle or bottom in their proper charm sockets

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)

Platform (Required):

PC - Microsoft Store
