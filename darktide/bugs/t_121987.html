The internal stacks for the damage buff are calculated on shot and apply to the next shot. After reloading, the first shot gets 2% damage by default. The second shot after reload will get 12% damage: the default 2% damage buff plus the damage buff based on current ammo stage; with only 2 shells in clip, the stage threshold is 2 * 0.1 = 0.2; so after shooting one time, you get 1 / 0.2 = 5 internal stacks (rounds down).

Example: left-click

  • base damage vs Unyielding (all 16 pellets): 1013.1165 (rounded on HUD: 1014)
  • 1st shot after reload: 1013.1165 * 1.02 = 1033.37883 (HUD: 1034)
  • 2nd shot after reload: 1013.1165 * 1.12 = 1134.69048 (HUD: 1135)

If you select Ammo Jack, this changes a bit because the stage threshold is now 0.3 and you can shoot one more time:

  • 1st shot after reload: still 1033.37883 (HUD: 1034)
  • 2nd shot after reload: 1013.1165 * 1.08 = 1094.16582 (HUD: 1095)
  • 3rd shot after reload: 1013.1165 * 1.14 = 1154.95281 (HUD: 1155)
