Vror • 2025-05-07 20:03:07

There’s 2 types of level/ranks in Havoc

“Clearance Level” is the highest mission you ever completed, this is what everyone else will see for you. This has no impact in what mission You can host (It used to in the past, not anymore, it’s a fully intentional change)

“Assignment Level” on the other hand is the current mission you can host. You can increase it by hosting a mission, or playing anyone else’s mission that has the same or higher Assignment Level (so if the current mission you can host is 35, you’ll need to beat any havoc 35 or higher to increase it)

Depending on the difference of your current Assignment Level and the host you’re playing, you’ll rank up 1 (if the difference is <5), 2 (if the difference >=5 but <10) or 3 levels (if the difference is <=10)
