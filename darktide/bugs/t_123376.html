Issue Description (Required):

I believe every Darktide player noticed the bad net-code when it comes to hit register especially with ranged precision weapons. I’m talking explicitly about the thing where you aim perfectly at the center of an enemies head and when you shoot, you hear the headshot sound, you see the blood and impact effects but you don’t get a hitmarker and the enemy doesn’t get damaged. It has been a problem since release but now with the Skitarii added there is no way around fixing this issue.

The main problem is that the Skitarii’s Advanced Combat Doctrines ability, aka aimbot, just doesn’t work properly on moving enemies due to most of the shots being not registered by the server. This problem doesn’t happen in the Psykharnium and there you can use the Braced Autogun without missing a single shot in a magdump. In a real round, unless the enemies are not moving or walking straight towards you, you will miss half the magazine or more. Every one of my friends who wanted to unlock the penance for the ability hated using it and I’m not surprised.

I know enough about programming to say this is an obvious net-code issue. It also feels like it is caused by enemy character animation missmatch between server and client, as this bug happens especially often when you shoot at idle enemies that have different animations like sitting or moving in different directions. Once they become aggressive, the animations seem to sync for the most part but still aren’t perfect, but that’s just my theory.

However, this bug is a big issue and now it needs to be fixed rather than a workaround solution for the aimbot ability. If it’s too hard, just outsource a net-code specialist to do so. Every other game can do it properly and it should be possible here too, especially since it is a PvE game that mostly uses hitscan and it could be just client based hit register to begin with. I’m also convinced this bug is the reason why we never got any sniper like weapons, as it happens a lot on long range with the Vigilante Autogun.

Additional notes: This issue is not limited to ranged weapons and can happen with melee too, although it is very rare and needs you to barely miss the target anyway.
It’s less of an issue on ranged weapons that have a wider hitscan, like the infantry lasgun and helbore have. It’s most notably on single shot Vigilante Autogun, as it has the highest zoom and doesn’t have the wider hitscan.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play a standard online match on the servers, NOT a hosted session like Psykharnium or any test session.
  2. Play any class, preferably with the single shot Vigilante Autogun
  3. Shoot at enemies, especially ones that haven’t noticed the players yet and are playing their idle animations. Go for headshots.
  4. Notice that out of 10 bullets 1 or 2 you hear the headshot sound but don’t get a hitmarker or deal damage to the enemy.
