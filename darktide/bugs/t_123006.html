Issue Type (Required):

Talents

Issue Description (Required):

Ability for Voltaic Emitter states “at 2 charges or above, Ranged Enemies, within 30m, have their weapons Malfunctioned for 12s”

Uncommonly when using 2 charges of Voltaic Emitter the gunners infront of the player within 30m will not get malfunctioned and continue to keep shooting the player despite what the ability states.

Several clips of a lot of enemies that should be getting malfunctioned and yet they just don’t. Regardless of if this is intended or not this still means there is an issue with at least the ability text as the only requirement it states is the enemies should be within 30m. The main part of the ability also states “around you” for the aoe so that would only confirm that the malfunction should be 30m around you or it need rewording.

Also the end has a sniper visibly getting malfunctioned but still deciding to shoot. This might also indicate that the actual malfunction debuff isn’t even working when on enemies and this would also explain why people’s challenges are getting a miscount of malfunctioned enemies.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play Skitarii with Voltaic Emitter
  2. Keep shocking enemies with 2 charges until random some don’t get malfunctioned

Yes, and I’ve tried disabling them
