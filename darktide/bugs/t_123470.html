Issue Type (Required):

Cosmetics

Issue Description (Required):

There’re 4 upper body cosmetics here with the exact same glitches in 1st person:

  • Heavy Flak Armour (Relic of Cadia)
  • Heavy Flak Carapace (Sludge Jungle Camo)
  • Stygian Mk III Flak Vest
  • Militarum Battlegear (Sun Scorch Camo)

These 4 cosmetics share the exact same base model for arms & armor, only differing in textures (and for Stygian Mk III Flak Vest, different gloves, as well as a grenadier belt), but the core issues are the same: the arm textures in 1st person are horrendously bugged out.

Below you can find several weapon attack animations where the arm texture just glitches out. These are not all the examples, as they’re quite numerous but these samples should get the point across well enough.

I have reported these 4 cosmetics’ issues in the past and while they were acknowledged, no actual fix has been applied to them, which is a real shame because these are some of the best looking premium cosmetics available to Veteran. They’ve added into the shop for people to buy while having such these awful glitches in 1st person animations that this is simply unacceptable.

When you do fix them, if you ever will, keep in mind that certain attacks (like Power Sword VI’s Heavy 2 attack and Heavy Sword’s special attack, just to name a few) reveal more of the player character’s arm than most other attacks which can lead to an issue of arms appearing cut off & floating midair for many many other cosmetics (I have reported this in the past also, but of course NOTHING has been done to change any of them to nobody’s surprise).

I’d love to buy more cool cosmetics to support the studio for the game they make, if the cosmetic I’m buying actually works properly. None of these 4 do in their current states (and many others more but let’s just focus on these 4 for now), and they ought to be changed.

When this post gets acknowledged, I hope you can at least respond if you will actually work towards fixing them. Because a simple “acknowledged” status is not good enough when there’s been almost 2 years of patches since my first reports on this and nothing has been changed. If you’re not going to fix them, I think I at least deserve to know that.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Equip any of the 4 listed upper body cosmetics
  2. Load into any 1st person environment
  3. Perform melee attack animations with the listed weapon attack examples above
  4. Observe weird texture popping due to arm render model behaving weirdly given these attacks
