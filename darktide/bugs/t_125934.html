Stimm Supply describes it’s tertiary ‘Stimm Dispersion’ effect as follows:
‘Additionally, if you have a Stimm equipped, Stimm Supply will copy its contents and disperse them into the air, granting its effects to any nearby Allies.’

This effect has shown to consume a held Stimm and apply its effects in an area.
When using the Cartel Stimm and using Stimm Supply while Cartel Stimm is ready, the Stimm Supply applies the effects of the Cartel Stimm to the Hive Scum normally.

However, when the Cartel Stimm is on cooldown, the Stimm Supply still applies the Stimm’s statistical benefits but only when stepping on the Stimm Supply. The Hive Scum themselves must step out of the Stimm Supply and re-enter to gain the statistical benefits.
This allows a Hivescum to stack their Cartel Stimm’s Benefits given that they regain their Stimm Supply fast enough.

Is this supposed to be the intended effects of Stimm Supply?
