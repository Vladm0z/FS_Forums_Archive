Issue Type (Required):

Weapons

Issue Description (Required):

The combat blade’s sprint-attack movement speed buff will now briefly turn off if you press 1 (or whatever key you have assigned to your melee weapon.) This was previously used to prevent auto attacking after holding the heavy attack for too long and allow you greater uptime of the movement speed buff. If you spam 1, you will move slower than basic walk speed, even though you are sprinting.

Other weapons like the Deimos force sword also have this speed buff, I have only tested the Combat Blade (both marks.)

EDIT: After testing further I have discovered that this bug only takes place on the DLC Skitarii class.

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)

Platform (Required):

PC - Steam
