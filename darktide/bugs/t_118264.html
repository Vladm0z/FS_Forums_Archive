Plasma Gun weapon clipping during *Inspect Weapon* animation (Veteran – left hand and arm)

1 Posts • 23 Views
L
LaughingBoy16 • 2026-01-30 22:07:49

Issue Type (Required):

Animation

Issue Description (Required):

When using the Inspect Weapon feature on the Plasma Gun as the Veteran, the inspection animation causes visible clipping involving the Veteran’s left hand and left arm, particularly where the arm intersects with the fuel cell.

Observed issues:

  • The Plasma Gun’s housing intersects with the Veteran’s left hand.
  • The Veteran’s left arm clips into the fuel cell during parts of the inspect animation.
  • Clipping is clearly visible from multiple camera angles due to the close-up inspection view.

This breaks visual consistency and immersion during weapon inspection.

Additional Notes

  • Appears to be an animation/rig alignment issue specific to the Inspect Weapon animation.
  • Most noticeable around the fuel cell and left-hand grip.
  • Occurs consistently every time the Inspect Weapon feature is used.
  • Screenshots attached showing the issue from multiple angles.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

Steps to Reproduce

  1. Play as Veteran.
  2. Equip the Plasma Gun.
  3. Enter the Mourningstar (or any safe area).
  4. Use Inspect Weapon.
  5. Rotate the camera slightly while the inspection animation plays.
  6. Observe clipping at the left hand and left arm, especially around the fuel cell.

Expected Result

The Plasma Gun inspection animation should play without:

  • The Veteran’s left hand clipping into the weapon model.
  • The Veteran’s left arm intersecting with the fuel cell.
  • Weapon parts intersecting the player’s arms or hands.

Actual Result

The Veteran’s left hand and left arm consistently clip into the Plasma Gun and fuel cell during the Inspect Weapon animation.
