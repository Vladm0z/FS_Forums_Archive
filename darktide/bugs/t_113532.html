Many attacks (mainly melee) have bleedthrough where a portion of their damage is dealt to the player’s health, and this portion is determined by how much toughness the player currently has in relation to their max toughness.

For example, if you’re hit by a bruiser melee attack while you have 100% of your max toughness, you won’t take any health damage, only toughness damage. However if you have 99% of your total toughness, then 1% of the attack’s damage will be dealt to your health.

This mechanic has been in the game for a very long time and wasn’t newly added or changed in the most recent update.
