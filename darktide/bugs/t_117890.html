The Iron Helm MKIV Thunder Hammer seems to have a problem with its second charged up swing going from left to right (At least that’s the one I notice most). It will charge all the way up, but then when released it won’t swing the attack, it just cancels and returns the character to neutral stance. It has happened both with and without the special activation, and I believe it happens more when sprinting/moving forward rather than side to side movements. I am currently using shroudfield and notice it happening often while sprinting forward. I have not noticed any problems with regular swings.
