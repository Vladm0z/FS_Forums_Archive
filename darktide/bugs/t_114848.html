Issue Type (Required):

Cosmetics

Issue Description (Required):

While in the hub, when my zealot character with darkest skin option switches to another loadout with a different cosmetic set her skin colour on her head switches to a much paler option. Exiting to the operative selection screen and reselecting the character correctly renders the skin colour. Switching to a different cosmetic breaks the skin tone again. In the images below you can clearly see the arms and head have a different skin colour.

EDIT: The skin colour can be different between the inventory screen and cosmetics selection screen. The portrait and operative selection screen seems to be consistently correct.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Load in to character
  2. Change loadout to different cosmetics
