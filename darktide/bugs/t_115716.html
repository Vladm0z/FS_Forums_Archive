Issue Description (Required):

Blackout blitz generation does not work properly with Chem Toxin. Chem Toxin kills does not count towards the 20 kills. I tested without chem toxins and the grenade generates after 20 kills. With chem toxin (Bone Saw), it took more than 20 kills to get the grenade back.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Enter Meat Grinder
  2. Throw Blackout bomb away (not on enemies)
  3. Kill 20 enemies with Bone Saw or with Coated Weaponry talent (let some die to chem toxin).
  4. See when the grenade regenerates

necroposting to confirm this bug is currently still in play and to add specifics to which chem toxins are affected:

Created an account, finally, just to report that this issue still exists when allowing enemies to die at close range with chem toxin and bleed with the Bonesaw and the Shivs (melee applied). It will only work with melee weapons if the kill comes from the damage of the attack. Otherwise the DOT effect steals the kill count.

It seems to work properly when allowing enemies to die at close range with toxin from the Needle Pistol.

Tested as I posted this message in the Meat Grinder.

UPDATE: 7.24.26: Kills from the Needle Pistol secondary fire mode will only generate the regen stacks if the target was hit by the original needle, not if the enemy dies from the spreading chem explosion from the death of the original target. Will generate the regen stacks if a single needle penetrates multiple targets and each die from the resulting chem toxin. They have to be hit by the needle itself to count. Essentially if the explosion effect triggers on them.

As a side note (I can look for the proper thread for this), this issue also extends to the Pickpocket AND Splash Damage talents (probably more). The chem toxin effect will steal the kill on melee weapons and not proc either of the effects.
