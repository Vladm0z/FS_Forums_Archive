Issue Description (Required):

For the Veteran class:

Both the Heavy Flak Armour (Relic Of Cadia) upper body and the Militarum Battlegear (Cadian 802nd) lower body have too light of a color on the tan fatigues. The Tan of all other Cadian fatigues are a much more olive color while the tan on these two pieces are much brighter causing it to clearly not match any of the other Cadian armor sets.

I have attached screenshots showing the color mismatch with descriptions listed in the “Steps to Reproduce” section. Please let me know if you would like more screenshots displaying the color mismatch on the fatigues.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

Equip either the Heavy Flak Armour (Relic Of Cadia) upper body and the Militarum Battlegear (Cadian 802nd) lower body with any other Cadian armor set.

Example 1: Heavy Flak Armour (Relic Of Cadia) upper with Militarum Heavy Battlegear (Cadian 802nd) lower.

Example 2: Militarum Heavy Battlegear (Protectus Pattern IX) upper with Militarum Battlegear (Cadian 802nd) lower.

Example 3: Comparison with any (Sludge Jungle Camo) set’s fatigues. EX3 screenshot shows how the sets should match with the Sludge Jungle Camo’s fatigues used as an example.

Basically, just directly compare the noted fatigues with any other Cadian sets listed or noted in the screenshots to see the miscolor.
