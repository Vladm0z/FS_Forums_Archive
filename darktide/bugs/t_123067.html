Issue Type (Required):

Player Mechanics

Issue Description (Required):

I’ve noticed in this update I was getting different outputs on my mk. II Relic Blade than my muscle memory remembered prior to the update, so I went into the meat grinder to try and resolve if it was just me or if something changed.

I found that when I attempted a dash heavy hold > Shroudfield > release to headstab, I was getting dash lights instead. Testing further with Heavy 3 vertical uppercut > Shroudfield > release, if I activate my Ability while charging the move and then release too soon, I’ll get Light 3 horizontal (diagonal right to left) instead. I tested what would happen if I pressed Shroudfield with this timing while it was on cooldown and unable to activate, and even pressing the key while it was inert reset the Heavy charge timing and produced a light attack instead.

This behavior was found to happen consistently with both standing Heavy attacks as well as the mk. II Relic Blade’s dash Heavy > Shroudfield activation as well, seeming to reset its charge time even on an inert cooldown-state Ability key input.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Make a Zealot with the mk. II Relic Blade and Shroudfield
  2. Perform Heavy attacks until you are charging one of the deeper ones, like Heavy 3 vertical uppercut.
  3. Hold charge on the Heavy attack.
  4. Activate Shroudfield and then release the attack charge almost immediately after.
  5. Rather than a vertical uppercut, a horizontal downward light attack should be seen to happen.

Addendum: This can be repeated with dash Heavy stab attacks, where the result after a quick Shroudfield input produces a horizontal dash Light instead of the thrusting dash Heavy. The Ability key input produces a light attack from heavy attack charge even when the ability is on cooldown.
