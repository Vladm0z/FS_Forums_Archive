Issue Type (Required):

Cosmetics

Issue Description (Required):

Several head cosmetics before and after this patch lack some form of tracking.

For example, the Enlightener’s Shroud for Zealot lacks any form of head tracking and is static, seemingly being parented or locked to the neck. It’s the most “stiff” of the bunch.
(Also, the default Enlightener’s Shroud has a typo, where Shroud is spelled as “Shroudh”)

Then there’s the Votarist’s Pot Helm with Rebreather and the MK IA Skopios Pattern Flash Goggles with Flight Mask for the Zealot, which seem to be “semi-stiff” but still not tracking the head properly.

For the Psyker, the Psykana Moebian 21st Rebreather, the Stygius III Reinforced Psi-Hood and the Adherency MkIV Psykana Collar - Gloamus Camo are also “semi-stiff”.

The “stiffness” is visible when emoting, wandering the Mourningstar or during victory poses, but not when your operator is in the loadout screen holding their weapons. On a side note, your character appears to “A-Pose” when previewing cosmetics.

I would list more headgears but it seems to be quite a broad problem so it likely needs more than a per-cosmetic fix.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Equip any of the listed headgears then emote, view the victory poses or walk around the Mourningstar.
