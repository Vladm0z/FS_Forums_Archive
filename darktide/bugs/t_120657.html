Issue Type (Required):

Weapons

Issue Description (Required):

The No Respite weapon blessing applies bonus damage based on the number of staggers an enemy has received. This is not working at the moment.

In the previous version of the game, this section had target_stagger_count = stagger_component.count, however as of the Expeditions update it is missing and target_stagger_count is always 0, thus No Respite gets no damage bonus.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Load into the Psykhanium with No Respite blessing on a Combat Shotgun
  2. Shoot into a Mauler
  3. Damage does not increase even though he’s being staggered

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, but I haven’t tried disabling them

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)

Platform (Required):

PC - Steam
