After patch, using Zealot stealth during an attack charge (heavy attack, relic blade sprint stab) resets the attacks windup.

Repro: Wind up a heavy (or sprint stab) → stealth → let go of attack
Pre-patch: you do the wound up attack from stealth
Post-patch: the wound up attack gets replaced with light attack

You can circumvent it by “holding the windup” during stealth for long enough, but this was not necessary before.
