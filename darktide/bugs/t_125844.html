fbolduc:

I always understood the fluff text to mean Elite when they mention “Specialists”. Specials like Dogs, Snipers, etc. are not “Shock Troops”, but Crushers and Ragers definitely are.

HISTG was always like this, and on top of this you’re deliberately misinterpreting the description based on what you think it should mean, and not what it actually means.

A crash course: Darktide has two categories of stronger enemies, Elites and Specialists. Elites are bigger, tankier, and more dangerous, like Ragers and Crushers. Specialists are designed to disable players or screw with your ability to navigate combat, like Trappers and Bombers. So you see, when the description of the modifier says “Specialists”, it means the latter category of enemies designed to mess with you. This is what the description means. “Shock Troop” is just fancy flavor text.
