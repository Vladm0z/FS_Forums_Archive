Issue Description (Required):

The Atonement modifier reuses Heinous Rituals spots to spawn altars but, unlike Heinous Rituals havoc modifier, it doesn’t disable normal daemonhosts spawns and sometimes a daemonhost and an altar may spawn in the same place. I don’t know about other maps but it happens in Comms-Plex.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play the Atonement modifier
  2. Find an altar
  3. RNG may spawn a daemonhost on top of it
