Issue Description (Required):

After inspecting certain weapons or armaments and viewing 3rd-person perspective (by pressing “inspect” key and then pressing right mouse button) - a special sound they produce stays there forever.
The hissing and gurgling sounds are persistent and never end strictly at that place where you inspected 3rd person while weilding specific weapon or armament. Even when you equip another weapon.
The weapons/armaments affected are as follows:

  • Chem Grenade
  • Relic Blade when overcharged after using special action (when a red bar overflown)
  • Power Falchion when overcharged after using special action (when a red bar overflown)
  • Others with similar sound effects - normally any weapon/armament that can produce hissing/gurgling sounds, in a common way as for Chem granade or in a specific way after using power action and overcharged as for power blades and other power weapons

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  • Enter the Psykhanium or any mission
  • Equip specific weapon/armament
  • Press inspect key and RMB to view third person perspective
  • Release keys plus (but not necessary) - swap weapons plus (but not necessary) - move to another place - HEAR persistant hissing sounds
