Psyker Head Cosmetic Merging

1 Posts • 33 Views
L
Lazydrones • 2025-06-25 19:02:26

Issue Type (Required):

Cosmetics

Issue Description (Required):

When using Psyker, when switching between various of my head cosmetics, it carries over some of the cosmetic from the previous wardrobe. Also, on same wardrobe “set” - if you attempt to change out the head slot it overlaps the previous cosmetic head piece.
It does clear out and not carry over this overlapping when you exit out of the cosmetics section and enter the game - cosmetics “update” visually properly when exiting the cosmetics section. Changing TABs within the “loadout” / “Cosmetics” / “Talents” - does not update cosmetics and retains issue.

Additionally; when swapping upper body cosmetic pieces - arms will ocassionally ghost out when you equip a piece that is sleeveless or has short sleeves.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

Create multiple Psyker Wardrobes or attempt to change existing wardrobe:
1: Cycle between cosmetic outfits (head / upper body)
2: Observe overlapping from previous outfit or previously existing cosmetic piece
