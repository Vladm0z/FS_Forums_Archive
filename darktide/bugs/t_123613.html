Issue Type (Required):

Visual

Issue Description (Required):

I was playing a couple rounds of Darktide tonight with my beautiful Skitarii when I got a random itch on the PoV. When I am playing with my fellow Unaugmented ones I noticed that my PoV is roughly the same height as the Guardsman next to me(Shortest Height Skitarii, Tallest Height Guardsman) and it got me curious. With nothing to lose but time itself I wandered into the Psykanium to test some of the inconsistencies I felt during my regular missions.

My PoV, again shortest height setting, is roughly the same height as the Specialists. Looking straight on at a Scab Flamer for instance my Skitarii’s Galvanic Rifle while shouldered(Not Aiming) is roughly at the height of the Scab’s Forehead. This is a non issue so far… but then I went into Third-Person using the Inspect, and suddenly my Shoulder where the rifle should be sat is barely cresting the enemies rebreather! This must mean the actual PoV of my Skitarii is just above its Hooded Scalp!

This can’t be! Maybe it only happens at the lowest height setting…?

No! It happens at the tallest height setting as well! But, wait, why does it remind me of the shortest height settings PoV…

What I came to discover, is that the shortest height setting has the tallest height settings PoV when in First-Person. While the tallest height settings PoV is located at the the tallest height settings Scalp. This inconsistency is purely visual and doesn’t seem to change the game-play too much.. although I might be taking damage hiding behind a outcropping even though my head is fully in cover? I could spend another few hours trying a few different things but I figured this would be good enough for a bug report. I hope the pictures give some clarity to what I mean as well!

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Create Skitarii Character
  2. See height difference between Third and First-Person
  3. Cry a little on the inside
