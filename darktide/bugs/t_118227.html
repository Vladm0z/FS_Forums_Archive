Issue Type (Required):

Cosmetics

Issue Description (Required):

The rebreather hose clip intersects the helmet’s side housing (ear/comms module) on the Graia Mk IVE Carapace Warden Helm.
The clipping occurs at the attachment point where the hose clip meets the side housing and is uneven/asymmetrical, suggesting a misaligned attachment or socket offset rather than intentional overlap.

The issue is clearly visible in the Inspect view and persists across multiple camera angles.

Additional Notes:
Occurs consistently and involves hard-surface clipping between helmet components.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Equip the Graia Mk IVE Carapace Warden Helm.
  2. Open the cosmetic Inspect view.
  3. Rotate the character to view the helmet from the side.
  4. Observe the rebreather hose clip intersecting the side housing.

Expected Result:
The rebreather hose clip should sit cleanly in front of or behind the helmet side housing without intersecting the geometry.

Actual Result:
The hose clip visibly clips into the helmet side housing, with uneven alignment between sides.
