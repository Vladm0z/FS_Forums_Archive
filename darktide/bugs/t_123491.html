Issue Description (Required):

during gameplay i noticed that when i use the advanced combat doctrines, it’d occasionally eat a whole stack of capacitance rather than gradually drain it as the intended behavior.

An example of this is, activating the ability at 4 capacitance stacks. thus it displays 3 and the one it’s actively burning. when going to tick down to two, it does so, then immediately seems to consume another, leaving one stack left.

I noticed this during multiple missions during normal gameplay and mortis trials after the patch that happened today. it seems to occur often when holding 2-3 stacks. additionall this was done while using red-line capacitors.

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. load into mission
  2. during gameplay, activate the combat protocols ability (unsure if it may be an interaction from activating the ability while the melee weapon is out)
  3. utilize the ability until a stack is burned, occasionally seems to consume more than one
