Issue Type (Required):

Issue Description (Required):

This has been an ongoing issue for a very long time now, in most(maybe all) maps there are locations of resource spawn points that are either completely or partially out of bounds, and unable to be picked up. I’ve noticed it’s usually on maps with reworked areas, like the Hab Dreyko end event, ever since it was reworked it had out of bounds loot for many many months.

I haven’t been able to go through and find every single location because that would literally drive me insane, but I’m hoping its easier for the devs to find and tweak these spawn locations.

I’ll attach a small handful of screenshots to give a general idea of what I’m talking about, and if I think to I’ll try to upload more in the comments as I remember
(the red P with a circle under it are the spawn points for plasteel/ammo crates/stims/etc)
and there are many locations on many maps, hopefully this can be an easy fix

Steps to Reproduce (Required):

  1. Play on any map(higher chance if in reworked layout)
  2. Look for any old loot spots and notice that its now covered with a box/barrel/etc
  3. (optional) Use a couple mods to see both the resource spawn points and the icons through terrain to easily find out of bounds loot

[PC] Do You Use Mods? (Optional):

Yes, but I haven’t tried disabling them

Reproduction Rate (Required):

Constant (100%)
