FatsharkLevCommunity Support • 2026-06-10 09:08:16

I can’t speak to cross-save, but the account mirroring service will return at a time post-Skitarii DLC launch when we have the capacity.

The aim right now is to improve the mirroring service in a workflow/behind-the-scenes way. We’ve also had some initial discussions regarding whether we can expand what is able to be mirrored - but it’s early days and we can’t make any promises. Currently, we mirror only safe data, and anything additional will introduce risk; and require a lot of testing/resourcing.

I’ll pass your feedback along when I next speak with the backend team and will continue to push for improvements where possible
