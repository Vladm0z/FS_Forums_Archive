We’re aware that some players have been experiencing crashes across all platforms since the release of 1.8.6.

If you encounter one of these crashes on PC, the crash reporter may display one of the errors listed below:

  • [Script Error]: scripts/foundation/managers/package/package_manager.lua:436: Trying to unload resource [FIXED]

  • Access violation (0xc0000005) in build X accessing address Y from Z

  • scripts/foundation/utilities/error.lua:37: [PlayerHuskVisualLoadoutExtension] Profile item mismatch.

The team is actively investigating these crashes and working on a solution. We’ll share updates as soon as possible in the comments below, and link them here for visibility.

Thank you for your patience and understanding in the meantime.

To help keep everything organised, new and existing reports about this issue will be merged here.
