Hi, as you have a 13th Gen Intel CPU, it’s likely you’re impacted by the manufacturing fault listed here: Intel Core 13th and 14th Gen Desktop Instability Root Cause Update - Intel Community

This makes data corruption a common issue in Darktide. You can work around this by underclocking the ‘Performance Core’ speed using Intel XTU, from x55 to x53.
