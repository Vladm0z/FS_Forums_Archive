Soligna • 2026-05-27 22:26:35

Just so you guys know, the 2014 errors I was having had nothing to do with my internet. I checked windows event viewer and noticed that I was having raid errors at every disconnect, specifically “Reset to device, \Device\RaidPort0, was issued.”

Followed by a ton of “The IO operation at logical block address 0x2477b9f8 for Disk 1 (PDO name: \Device\0000005e) was retried.”

So to fix this, I had 2 m.2s in usb drives that I wasn’t even using that I disconnected, then flashed an updated firmware for my SanDisk 2tb m.2 that I keep my games on. Those two things resolved my 2014 error crashes.
