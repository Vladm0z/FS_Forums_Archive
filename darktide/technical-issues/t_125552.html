Curious, what display mode setting are you using? and does the Alt+Tab crash occur for other display modes? e.g. Windowed, Fullscreen etc.

Admittedly it’s a shot in the dark, but to ensure a clean baseline performing a clean reinstall of both your GPU drivers and Darktide may help.
