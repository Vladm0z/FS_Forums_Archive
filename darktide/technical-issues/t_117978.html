Sorry, but it sounds like your i9 is damaged by the voltage issue that has been plaguing the 13th and 14th gen intels.

If you haven’t already, update your bios to stop further damage but the damage is not reversible.

Reducing your clock speeds should help mitigate the issue. You can try increasing your clock speeds a little at a time to see how fast it can go while maintaining stability.
