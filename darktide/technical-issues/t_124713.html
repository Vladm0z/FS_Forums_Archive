KNOWN ISSUE: [Xbox/MS Store] DLC/Aquilas Purchases Not GrantedTechnical Support
We’ve now implemented a fix. Upon launching/restarting Darktide this should trigger any DLC and/or Aquilas purchases to now be processed and granted. If a purchase is still missing, please try restarting Darktide a further time. There also may be a short delay in purchases being granted after logging in. Please let us know if there is any issue still.

We are aware of an issue affecting Xbox/Microsoft Store players, that has resulted in DLC or Aquilas purchases to have not been granted to accounts.

Please rest assured that we’re working to resolve this issue as swiftly as possible. Updates will be shared here.

Thank you for your patience and understanding.
