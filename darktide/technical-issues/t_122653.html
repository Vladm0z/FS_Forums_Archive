Thank you for your patience as we look in to this.

If crashes persist, two shots in the dark:

Check if any background application may be the culprit, by performing a ‘Selective Startup’:

  1. Press the Windows key
  2. Type ‘msconfig’ and select ‘System Configuration’
  3. On the ‘General’ tab, select ‘Selective Startup’
  4. Deselect ‘Load startup Items’
  5. Reboot your PC

Edit: Apparently the drivers have been setting some cards over their default suggested max frequency. My 7900XT was 325 Mhz over the factory boost clock. That may help things. Never fiddled with overclocking before, but just turned it back down to 2450Mhz.

^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^ ^

This fixed the issue for me. For anyone else with the same problem I had, you have to install adrenaline with the driver, go to performance tab, then tuning. Enable custom/manual tuning, enable “advanced control,” and then lower your max frequency to whatever your card’s default max boost clock is. Just look it up. It’s easier than it sounds.
