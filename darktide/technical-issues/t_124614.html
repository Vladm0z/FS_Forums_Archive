Thanks for checking. As of today I’ve finally tracked down the problem after two weeks of losing my mind. The problem was due to an firmware update to my fiber ONT and how it interacts with a specific subset of LAN chipsets (ugh)
It caused some minor packet loss, that was only noticeable on a home network to someone playing a lot of Darktide! In fact, I could only notice the problem while playing Darktide because enemy hitboxes and movement are usually so tight. Other multiplayer games seemed “fine” because there wasn’t enough happening to make 3% packet loss noticeable!
