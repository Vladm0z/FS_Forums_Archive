I found a way to fix it, and I’m putting it here for future seekers who have the same issue.

I clicked the link on my twitch account inventory and got the same error, so I copy and pasted the address into notepad from the browser’s address bar (I think thats what its called anyway).

I then deleted the s after http, and only that one s, from the address and then copy pasted it back into the browser, and it worked. I was able to login with steam credentials, then twitch credentials, and then click the link button.

I now have my drops.
