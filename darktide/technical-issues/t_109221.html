Issue Description (Required):

Crash Report – Darktide

Issue Summary:
The game crashes while in the Mourningstar lobby, typically during loadout adjustments (e.g., changing talents or gear). The crash occurs after approximately 5–10 minutes spend in operative loadout. It happens regardless of whether I’ve just launched the game or have already played missions.

Crash Behavior:

The screen goes completely black without any artifacts or distortion.

In most cases (4 out of 5), the PC fully powers off.

In one instance, the PC remained on, and background audio continued (e.g., Discord or music playback), but the screen remained black and unresponsive.

Post-Crash Consequences:

After the crash where the PC remained on, all AMD graphics drivers were corrupted.

The system defaulted to Microsoft Basic Display Adapter.

I had to use the AMD Cleanup Utility and reinstall the Adrenalin software to restore full GPU functionality.

After doing this clean reinstall, game performance improved significantly, especially reducing the previously near-constant stutter.

Despite improved performance, I experienced one additional crash with the same symptoms (black screen, system power loss).

System Specs:

GPU: AMD Radeon RX 6700 XT

CPU: AMD Ryzen 5 5600X

RAM: 16 GB

OS: Windows 11

*All this happened in the past 3 days. All drivers were up to date both before I had the issue and now. I also wasn’t playing Darktide in months, and while I had crashes before, it never happened so consistently. A crash with corrupted drivers happened to me just once before while playing DT.
