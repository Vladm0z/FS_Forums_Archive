I’ve been playing Darktide for a couple days now, and only today did it fail to boot the game properly past its launcher and no matter how many times I verify my files or reinstall the game, it will tell me the same script error. I’ve played the game with mods for a couple days, and even gone as far as to uninstall all of them to no avail.

I’ve done some trial and error and figured out that Windows Smart App Control deemed file: ‘gRPC_pluginw64_release.dll’ as unsafe, automatically blocking it with no way to undo this decision other than disabling this safety feature entirely. This makes the game playable again with no further issues, but can leave your device vulnerable.

I made this post to inform others how to fix this issue and also ask if this could be fixed, as Windows Smart App Control is a safety feature that helps protect devices against malware. I personally would like to keep this feature activated and still play the game.
