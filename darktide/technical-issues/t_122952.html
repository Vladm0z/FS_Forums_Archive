FatsharkLevCommunity Support • 2026-06-25 11:03:28

Hm, I wonder if an external program is conflicting. A ‘Selective Startup’ may help here:

  1. Press the Windows key
  2. Type ‘msconfig’ and select ‘System Configuration’
  3. On the ‘General’ tab, select ‘Selective Startup’
  4. Deselect ‘Load startup Items’
  5. Reboot your PC

Otherwise, it could also be worth checking your Anti-Virus, and adding appropriate AV and Firewall exceptions for Darktide.
