@korefuji

Aight some more info to add, I just RMAed my AW3423DW real gsync ultimate and they sent me a newer AW3425DW w/ FreeSyncy premium aka gsync compatible as a replacement.

Now I turned on VRR OFC but when I turned on the built-in monitor FPS (aka refresh rate Hz) on-screen display I noticed VRR t wasn’t kicking and neither was RTX HDR.

After some overnight digging I found the culprit and partly thanks to SpecialK info, it was not bypassing DWM for DT or others so it couldn’t use VESA adaptive sync (VRR), and Special K reported MPO disabled as well so no RTX HDR and FPS OSD was showing max desktop Hz of 244 accordingly.

Turns out I had software interfering w/ the VRR (which didn’t with real gsync afaik) and in this case it was Start11’s replacement taskbar which somehow made itself tje topmost invisible window, and on a hunch restarting Explorer fixed it. Disabling autohide on Start 11’s taskbar also fixes it oddly.
Turns out other SW like Asus crap can interfere like this too.

To add insult to injury, and invisible window like the Windows volume OSD which I disabled thanks to an OSS project I forked for Win11STILL disabled VRR while it was invisibly top window for a second or two.

TLDR check for some SW inserting itself as the topmost window disabling all the goodies that come from being a fullscreen application e.g. games+extras, the extra here being RTX HDR (which may not play nice with any overlays as it disables them AFAIK. Check w/ SpecialK to report any interference and if DWM is not beign bypassed and VRR and MPOs idsabled.

TLDR 2 be wary of too many overlays.
