Jls:

and it would gain you more money which is just another benefit along side pleasing the community is there a reason i do not see as to why it is the way it currently is?

the name of the game is fomo, fear of missing out.

if you could just buy whatever you wanted, then you wouldn’t fear missing out. ultimately you would buy less.

it may sound counterintuitive but it’s really just a modern gaming take on artificial scarcity. they wouldn’t be doing this against all feedback if it didn’t work.

if you want fomo to be gone, the first step is understanding that it’s a psychologically manipulative business strategy that is being done because it actually works and results in more sales, it’s not a silly mistake or thoughtless implementation. the steps after are harder, but many countries in the EU did eventually ban lootboxes so there’s hope for this kind of change to happen.
