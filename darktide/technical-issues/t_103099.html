Do you happen to have a 13600K/14600K or higher? If so they’ve been burning themselves out due to a combination of a manufacturing defect and aggressive default voltage. Usually it causes errors during high CPU loads like shader compilation which makes it seem like a graphics issue at first.

Intel has extended their warranty and put out BIOS patches to prevent the effected CPUs from degrading but it can’t save an already defective CPU. They claim it doesn’t effect lower tier or mobile 13th/14th gen CPUs but some people online also claim it’s happening to them if they’re overclocked so don’t count those out either.
