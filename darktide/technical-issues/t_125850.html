Hi @FatsharkLev ,

Quick update: the issue is solved, and it wasn’t on your end. After a Microsoft/Xbox Insider program update to my profile, my Xbox privacy settings had cross-network communication limited to friends only, so the game was muting every non-Xbox player for me. After setting “You can communicate outside of Xbox with voice & text” and “Others can communicate with voice, text, or invites” to Everyone, chat works again. I never thought to look there, since I wasn’t aware of any change.

In case other MS Store/Xbox players report the same thing, especially Insiders: the console log shows this at startup:
You are not allowed to "CommunicateUsingText" with "CrossNetworkUser" because of "CrossNetworkUserMustBeFriend"
followed by “Text muting participant” for almost every player. The Vivox mute errors in my log seem to be a side effect of that.

Sorry for taking up your time, and thanks for the quick and helpful replies!
