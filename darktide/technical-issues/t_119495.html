FatsharkJuliaCommunity Support • 2026-03-18 15:59:06

I notice you have a 14th Gen Intel CPU which is impacted by manufacturing error impacting the the 13th and 14th Gens. This can cause some instability in Darktide and across the board.

You should be able to work around this by underclocking the ‘Performance Core’ speed using Intel XTU, from x55 to x53.

For more information on the underlying issue, please refer to: Intel Core 13th and 14th Gen Desktop Instability Root Cause Update - Intel Community

Reduce the multiplier as far as you can, check for stability, then raise the multiplier back up until it becomes unstable. Here’s a link to the download page for the official utility, you want the 7.x version.

Intel

Intel® Extreme Tuning Utility (Intel® XTU)

The Intel® Extreme Tuning Utility (Intel® XTU) is used to overclock, monitor, and stress a system. This application is supported with unlocked processors (K- and X-series) only. Refer to “This download is valid for the product(s) listed below”...

Also if you haven’t updated your bios to a version that has fixed the bug that is damaging your CPU you should look up how to do that.

Heads up the damage is permanent, best case you can stop more damage from occurring and reduce clock speeds to help with the instability caused by the damage.

Make sure you update your BIOS with the correct CPU & Motherboard specifications (might be a fast tutorial on YouTube on it with your specific set-up, this will correctly set the voltage amounts, etc). I have those same issues & CPU, it fixed everything across the board gaming-wise. I wouldn’t recommend Extreme Tuning when you can fix it directly & permanently. ET is just for a quick fix, like a band-aid, let’s say. But that’s up to you… I’d rather fix is permanently then let any time go by perhaps causing any other unknown issues along the way.

Edit: I know updating your BIOS can be a bit nail biting. If you don’t feel comfortable, I would honestly seek a professional or somebody that knows what they’re doing. But it’s not too bad, you just need a flash drive USB & follow some instructions. It does take some time though, so don’t freak out, lol. I’ve done about 2-3 times; it takes about 10-30 mins or so depending. Then you don’t even have to touch BIOS for probably a year or more.
