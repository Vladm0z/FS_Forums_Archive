Notice about application

Fatshark have been very nice with regards to generally not policing our usage of mods, working on a mutual respect system where we don’t rock the boat with specific usage, and they don’t have to directly intervene. This is especially relevant to cosmetics in the game. Given that a chunk of their revenue is related to cosmetics and the like, they would prefer we don’t affect it in any way. If they detect any loss in revenue from an increased use in a particular mod, they will start banning users using that mod.

So, be cool about it. Don’t cross that line
