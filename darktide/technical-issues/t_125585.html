Hm, I know that Steam can require some additional space for temporary files while installing, but with 232GB free space this seems unusual.

To confirm, is 232GB free space shown when starting the installation? Also, when it fails, how much free space is then shown?

It may also be helpful try repairing Steam library:

  1. In Steam, go to ‘Settings’ > ‘Storage’
  2. At the top, select the SSD you are trying to install Darktide to
  3. Select the menu button (“…”) next to the drive
  4. Then, select Repair Library
  5. Wait for the repair to complete (do not disconnect your SSD during this)
  6. Once finished, then restart Steam and try to install Darktide again
