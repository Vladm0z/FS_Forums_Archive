Sorry to hear about this. Since Darktide runs in protected memory, it wouldn’t directly cause your PC to become unresponsive, but it could be highlighting an existing issue as Darktide is particularly demanding and resource-intensive. You may wish to look in to the corresponding errors in the Windows Event Viewer, as this may point you in the right direction as to what the underlying issue is. We have instructions for how to check this and more listed here:

[PC] How to Resolve PC Crashes, Loss of Responsiveness or Blue Screens (BSOD)

After your stress test (don’t forget mem tests) it may be worth looking at the BSOD/Freeze dumps, IIRC there may be some even without having the blue screen of death appear.

Depending on how the dump files are setup, odds are you have a C:\Windows\Minidump\ and a corresponding dump file at the time of the freeze or BSOD.

Analyzing it with WinDbg (see link) or using Blue screen of death (STOP error) information in dump files. might give you more info as to the culprit.
