FatsharkJuliaCommunity Support • 2025-12-04 09:57:47

Hi all, we’re looking in to whether cFosSpeed, a network traffic shaping application, may be causing this error.

This application is often pre-installed on systems with MSI or Gigabyte motherboards, but it is not required for Windows or normal network operation.

Some users have reported that cFosSpeed can also lead to network instability, such as packet loss or high latency, not only in Darktide but across other applications as well.

Historically this would result in Error Code: 2011, but we’re now looking in to whether it could be causing Error Code: 2020.

Is anybody able to check whether they have cFosSpeed installed on their PC, and uninstall it if so?
