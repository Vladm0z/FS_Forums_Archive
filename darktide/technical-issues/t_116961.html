One thing to be aware of is the temperature sensor location on Zen 4 CPUs and earlier aren’t as close to the CPU hotspot as they could be so there’s a thermal reading offset that’s designed to overshoot the temperature a little bit just as a safety thing. They fixed this with Zen 5.

That said if you’re REALLY worries, AIOs can get gunk built up between the fins on the inside that the liquid is supposed to move through to dissipate heat and there’s a few companies that have had issues with some models. Look up your exact cooler and see if there was a recall or something.
