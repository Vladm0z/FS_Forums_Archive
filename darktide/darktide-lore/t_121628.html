Origin and Recruitment: The Frontiersmen of Pavane

Unlike other Moebian regiments raised from the toxic, hyper-congested hive spires of Atoma Prime, the 13th draws its recruits from Pavane, a rugged, forested frontier world on the edge of the domain. Pavane resembles the ancient wilderness of Old Earth, defined by endless, untamed pine woods, treacherous mountain ranges, and rushing rivers.While Pavane features a robust and heavily populated network of industrial logging hubs, sprawling fortified logging towns, and agricultural settlements across its surface, it completely lacks the massive, planet-choking Hive Cities common elsewhere in the Imperium. Life outside the confines of a hive structure means the people of Pavane are natural woodsmen, trappers, and expert marksmen. They spend their lives tracking game through dense, shadowed treelines and surviving brutal seasonal changes.The planetary tithe selects exclusively from these rugged frontiersmen. They bring a lifetime of wilderness self-reliance and lethal hunting skills to the Astra Militarum.

Combat Doctrine: The Invisible Death

The Stormrangers do not fight in the grinding, mass-infantry waves typical of other Imperial forces. They operate as decentralized, long-range reconnaissance and assassination cells.

Jungle Warfare Specialization: In strict adherence to their canonical mandate within the Moebian Domain, the 13th is primarily deployed to jungle worlds along the treacherous, uncharted Moebian Fringe. Their natural woodsman instincts from Pavane translate perfectly into these dense, choked death-world ecosystems.

The Long Hunt: A standard Stormranger deployment involves being dropped deep behind enemy lines into dense, uncharted jungle canopies. Squads go dark for solar months, moving like ghosts through thorn-choked undergrowth and hazardous alien terrain.

Marksmanship: Every guardsman in the 13th is qualified to a level that would earn them a veteran sniper designation in a standard Cadian regiment. They treat their long-las rifles and specialized, low-flash solid projectile sniper rifles with religious reverence.

The “One Horizon” Rule: A sub-doctrine within the regiment dictating that an enemy should never hear the sound of the projectile that kills them. If a target spots a Stormranger’s muzzle flash, the sniper considers it a personal, shameful failure.

The Haxorn Paradox
The regiment’s most celebrated victory occurred on Haxorn, a planet famed for brutal winters and rusted, irradiated plains. This deployment tested the Stormrangers to their absolute limits. Stripped of the dense jungle canopies they usually used for concealment on their deployment worlds, the 13th adapted their signature camouflage to match the blood-red, rusted snowbanks of Haxorn.For an entire solar month, the Orks attempted to overrun the imperial industrial sectors. The Greenskins found themselves picked apart by an enemy they could neither see nor track. Entire Ork warbands collapsed into infighting and panic as their Warbosses and Nobz were systematically executed from kilometers away by phantom fire. The victory proved the 13th’s lethal adaptability outside of their preferred jungle environments.

Regiment Culture and Equipment

Official “Stormranger” Uniform: As canonically established by the Munitorum, the regiment wears the signature “Stormranger” camouflage pattern across their entire cloth fatigue uniform. Originally designed by the regiment’s own sharpshooters, this exact pattern is optimized to break up a soldier’s silhouette perfectly amidst the thorny undergrowth, high verdant canopies, and shifting shadows of the Moebian Fringe’s jungle worlds.

Standard-Issue Moebian Flak Armor: To maintain maximum mobility and speed while trekking through rough jungle terrain, the 13th is equipped with lightweight, sector-standard Moebian flak armor. While their underlying uniforms carry the Stormranger pattern, their protective flak torso plates, shoulder guards, and web gear retain their standard industrial metal finishes or solid dull tones, heavily overlaid with low-visibility canvas wraps and matte foliage netting to silence moving parts and eliminate metallic glare.

The Mark of Elite Status: Rather than standard Astra Militarum helmets, the Stormrangers proudly wear special-issue, deep hunter-green berets. This distinctive headgear signifies their elite standing within the Moebian Domain and is a point of immense pride.

Ocular Target Acquisition: Eschewing heavy helmets, the 13th relies entirely on high-tech rangefinder goggles or permanent cybernetic eye augmentations. Connected directly to their rifles, these ocular implants and visors feed real-time ballistic data, wind velocity, and thermal signatures straight into the sniper’s mind.

The Looming Shadow: Following the horrific betrayal of their sister regiment, the Moebian 6th, the 13th has been placed under intense scrutiny by the Inquisition. Operating on the isolated fringes of the sector, the Stormrangers must constantly prove their loyalty, often taking on the most suicidal, deeply infected frontline tracking missions to keep the Darktide at bay.
