Moebian 99th

The Elite Mornax Guard.

The 99th have created their own camo known as the “Wastelander” camo, a brown and greyish blotch pattern, this pattern is often adorn on a “Wastelander’s” Autogun along with added tape or field repairs/modifications.

Primary Recruitment from the planet Mornax, They excel at open terrain battles, like wastelands, deserts, shrublands, or Winter wastelands.

The 99th’s gear has adopted a brown and greyish coloration due to the environments they often find themselves. Typically accompanied with Grox leather due to the vicious environmental storms they encounter. Surprisingly the dark brown and grey of the of the 99ths uniforms works well in dark, damp hive cities.

The gear of the 99th is somewhat poor, they use autoguns because of price, some have lasguns but its not universal and their flak armor tends to be self mended or rundown but still effective.

Many “Wastelanders” will modify their autoguns, extended barrels, single shot capabilities, burst fire, add attachments including, scopes, extended magazines, and so on.

Men and women of the 99th may be poorer and less funded then their Moebian cousins but they have proven to be surprisingly resourceful and adaptable on the battlefield, winning battles that many other regiments deemed impossible.

The 99th make for excellent survivalists is austere environments, often being well prepared for long marches and creating makeshift camps.

Due to autoguns running out ammo unlike rechargeable lasguns packs, most members of the 99th take extensive time to hone their melee and hand to hand combat skills.

Other Moebians have given the 99th the nickname “Wastlelanders”, due to the often deployments outside of hive cities.

The 99th are good recon scouts, making them pretty renown marksmen amongst other Moebian regiments, they lack snipers due to funding though, but they can also dig in and hold the line as needed, setting traps for incoming enemies.

The “Mornax Hail” Strategy, made famous by the 99th, They unleash a non-stop wall of low-tech, high-velocity ballistics to tear apart advancing enemy waves, the enemy advance is slow by traps and trip wires that the 99th scouts known as the “Whispers” placed well before the initial battle began.

Recent reports from the 21st indicate that the 99th may have be sighted in the hourglass pushing traitors back.

The 99th have deployed with Tallarn, Valhallan, Vostroyan, and Various Moebian units, most commonly the 21st recon regiment, due to compatibility.

Due to the mining on Mornax they have a abundance of ammo and autoguns stock piled.

Fought on Nox Secundus during the fringe wars.

Helped the Moebian 13th repel the orks on Haxorn.

Sergeant Esta is a well decorated and legendary “wastelander”, she is a fierce combatant and saboteur, often taking up missions with small teams going behind enemy lines, she is known to be rowdy and has a history of talking back to leadership, despite this she always gets the mission done. Esta carries her famous combat knife that she named “Traitor Bane” everywhere she goes, many traitors have been silenced from this blade. Esta also carries her rare modified autogun sometimes adorned with purgation field camo that she earned during the battle of Carara, being a vital part with her squad sabotaging the enemy ammo supplies, and eliminating key leadership. Esta also still has her first ever autogun, painted with a blue from some long ago battle, it is heavily repaired and modified now, but it holds a special place in her heart, and a enemies.

The Night Wolves are the 99th’s commando’s, they are elite recon units, they deploy in small groups training militia’s around the galaxy, they can survive in any environment. They are often left on planets for months at a time, training local pdf or working with the Arbites to investigate corruption on planets and then build a army to fight it. They excel in all aspects of infiltration. Their war gear is not top tier, it is practical, it is highly customized to fit a mission, they will almost always wear a rebreather to conceal their identity. They are the true masters of improvisation, they can find a solution in near any situation.

Small detachments of the 99th have been confirmed conducting raids on the traitorous 6th, aiding the “Deadsider” convoys.

Majority of the regiment continues to deploy to the Moebian fringe. Gaining notoriety recently for devastating a Ork uprising on Gathadra, where the Moebian 81st failed to suppress the Ork’s in the first battle.

The 99th “Wastelanders” share a friendly rivalry with the 13th “Stormrangers”, sharing many common traits, Although the 13th being better well funded primarily recruiting from Pavane.
