Sure, I totally get that, but in the Custodes case I guess I’m just not seeing where it’s the latter instead of the former. The RT era art and models are male, but also a…really leather bar Rob Halford vibe, consisting entirely of 2 models and 2-3 pieces of art (one of which just shows helmets and pikes).

Looking at the Horus Heresy Collected Visions book, the Custodes aren’t described in gendered terms anywhere, aren’t described as a “Brotherhood” or “Fraternity”, or anything like that, that all came in basically 8th edition IIRC, and still didn’t have hard lore like Marines do about their physiology, to me it’s a minor retcon at best in 10th edition, if really a retcon at all (I feel like the first Custodes codex mostly reads basically like copy-pasta Space Marine lore with “Astartes” CTRL-H’d with “Custodes”), certainly much less than the total visual reboot 20+ years later and then several years later suddenly having them be a faction that now leaves Terra and goes out into the galaxy and does stuff.

It’s infinitely less upsetting (to me at least) than the Primaris garbage that affected not only lore and (stupid) naming conventions (intercessor, inceptor, infernus, infilitrator, incursor, OMG STOP WITH THE “I” NAMES) but the whole tabletop paradigm of what Marines were supposed to be (replacing generalist units sporting mixed armaments that could do multiple things with single-purpose uniformly armed units like Eldar Aspect Warriors).
