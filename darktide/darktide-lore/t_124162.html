TLDR - Dans new book HIVE and Darktide have very similar themes/ideas.

is HIVE connected to Darktide?

From a lore perspective, no. Absolutely not. Two different worlds in two different parts of the galaxy. There are some eerily similar threads/plots in the book however leading me to ask. Was this book leftover ideas Dan had for Darktide?

Some key plots in his new grand novel about life in a hive city are…

..not a single space marine, not even mentioned

..it starts about a group of battle-hardened soldiers (4 or 5) who meet once a year at the same pub/tavern to reminisce about the war. Some of them are misfits (rejects) who don’t quite get along together but have a common bond that keeps them together.

..Political intrigue with houses that subvert and plant governors who lean towards their agendas, par for course.

…This is a Hive City (1 of 2) that is rife with rebellion, The upper vs the lower (whoever allowed him to call the lower “the neg” should be fired, poor choice.)

…The first Hive city fell, failed and mysteriously went quiet from an unknown threat. You don’t say? The only notes are from an Inquisitor and those notes are classified. (spoilers.. notes speak of cults by the names of Gene and Brood clan)

… Arbities tasked with stopping the rebellion because “they know best” “this is the will of the lex!”

…Gas/Toxins being released through vents of entire sections of the hive city to immediately change the population

…Vox casters/Hive communication systems being taken over and used by the rebellion, only then to be re-taken by the loyalists so they can use it to transmit critical data to those at the top.

Hmm do any of these plots ring any bells for Darktide enjoyers? Reading this book screamed these were the ideas FS discarded. So Dan literally threw it in 2-volumes and lit it on fire.

My only worry is that this means Darktide will not have a certain faction introduced in the game. The big twist in the book is the main rebellion was indeed a plot by GSC to prepare for The Final Day. It’s a true showing of the horrors from the shadow in the warp and Tyranids/Xenos lovers will enjoy these parts, but the book is honestly a giant mess. Think of Rogue One Movie but none of the emotional plot exists (no prequel or sequel to tie it together) and oh, they do not get the communication to the Princess of Alderaan of House Organa. Everything just explodes. What can Dan say but “you’re Welcome!”

The book was clearly missing an Inquisitorial Prescence. Which shows up in Darktide. There was one mentioned in HIVE and directly involved with the downfall of the other hive city but that’s it. That’s literally the only mention of inquisitor. Like if all the inquisitorial writings were used for Darktide?

Other ramblings and about the book to tie into Darktide

This book does illustrate that Tyranids showing up via shadow will just pulp the brains of all our dear flame staff psykers, navigators, warp enhanced beings (even chaos!) and shut down all vox communication so I can see Tyranids being a no-go for Darktide, but maybe not for GSC?

I have been playing more expedition while reading this book. HIVE does a good job of explaining how GSC communicate. The droning sounds, the tinnitus, chittering that feels like communication is happening similar to machinery but a distant level. This is how those rooms in-between sections in expeditions sound like.

The Tyranid menace are described in the book as being flesh color skin, with bone white armor and translucence teeth. Literally a blank canvas to maybe be replaced with an actual hive fleet? None of the GSC refer or even mention fleet, they are just star children. They stitch red circles on their arm patches.

image648×362 110 KB

Dan has a lot of great set pieces and I kept thinking that each characters journey is a level in Darktide. The defending of the munitions station from both xenos threats. The Tidal Wave of oil after some sections collapse. The burning of sectors, walking over mountains of melted glass. Most of the characters story all progresses the same, they have to escape the horror and get out at the end. This is identical to how a darktide mission plays out.

Thanks for taking time to read, I’m curious if other member of the community have read his new book and what thoughts they have on how this may impact or already impacted Darktide. Maybe even people at FS have read the book and can chime in?
