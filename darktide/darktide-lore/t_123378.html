Myr(pronounced like mire) is a death world.

Guards known as The Myr Marshmen

Very swampy, dark, poisonous flora and fauna.

Swamp monsters known as Gatoras roam and kill anything in sight.

if the vines and mushrooms dont kill you, the Gatoras, Pythors, or the dread Black Stalkers will.

Black Stalkers are large feline beasts with razor sharp claws and teeth who are the apex predators of Myr. Many aspiring Marshmen will try to kill one to attain a elite status amongst their fellow Marshmen.

Myr Boars, dangerous roaming pack animals, their hides are as thick as steel, often used as leather for armor and clothing on Myr.

Toxshrooms are dotted all over Myr, releasing a toxic spore that inhaled is fatal hence the rebreathers for all Marshmen.

The Symbol for the Myr Marshmen is the all white moth that is the most deadly creature on the whole planet, if you take your rebreather off and one calmly flutters past you and your squad, your all dead. This moth is known as the Death’s head Moth.

On Myr the only creature known besides Marshmen to kill a Black Stalker is Myrphants and the loathed Death dancers, large black spider like creatures with yellow lines striping their legs, toxic enough to kill a space marine or Myrphant in one bite from its venom packed fangs.

The Guardsmen from this planet are particularly resistant to poison and disease.

The Guardsmen wear all black armor adorned with candles only for leaders of a patrol to wear to lead lower ranking Marshmen in the the mist and fog, the fog and mist being so dense you can barely see infront of you. The candles also have become somewhat of a good luck symbol for the Marshmen, yes the stand out in dark, damp forest, but it also seems to ward off some creatures.

Marshmen Prefer the recon lasgun to the regular lasgun, although the M36 lasgun is standard issue amongst Marshmen, most Marshmen become excellent shots before becoming veterans, making them adept with the M36 lasgun. The recon lasgun is highly favored and choice fielded weapon of Veteran Marshmen, if using a regular lasgun its usually charged for high power shots that deal more damage. Another favored weapon of the marshmen is the Plasmagun, due to is ability to actually kill many of the beasts on Myr. Marshmen have a unqiue version that shoots a yellow plasma instead of the common blue. If possible Marshmen will field bolters, although its some what rare. Weapon smiths on Myr have also started and fielding their own lasgun, more streamline, all black, white moth symbol, more powerful then recon lasgun but slower to fire. These Lasguns are named the Myr Mark I Patern lasgun, until more verisons are available. The Myr I patern lasgun shoots purple lasers due to the materials found on Myr. The Myr Mark I’s taking a lot of inspiration from the recon lasgun.

The Myr Mark I lasgun has recently been issued to new Marhsmen recruits, it’s also recently been made, due to the induction into the Imperium. Recruits have mixed reviews about the Mark I, they praise its power but not the range it has, its exceptable for sure but they want to be able to shoot further away. The cost is cheap, the design is simple enough, easy to field strip and clean and repair. Like it’s cousin it doesn’t jam ever, the charge packs can be recharged just about any where or any way. It’s a cheap, reliable weapon. The Mark II is currently under production to add a few fine touches. The Mark Ia has the upgrades Marshmen have wanted, which is now being issued to veteran Marshmen instead of the Mark I. A Myr Mark I heavy Laspistol is still being worked out as well. With the introduction of the Mark Ia sergeants are now carring this as well.

Myr Mortars is also being introduced to regiments, due to the light infantry tactics and denseness of Myr artillery was considered but to difficult to traverse through the swamps.

Marshmen have been given Chimeras now that implement into their training now.

Marshmen recruits also have to earn their lasguns and rebreathers by spending one single night in the swamp as is tradition, they will sport autoguns until they return, if they return.

Most Myr milita men highly customize their autoguns for either bigger magazines or retro fitted to shoot bigger bullets, anything really to keep them alive.

The populace of Myr all have Grey eyes due to the lack of natural light.

The Black Armor of marshmen is made from Murk Ore found in the deep murky swamps on Myr.

Murk armor as it’s known, is more resistant then regular flak armor and also has infection resistant properties, aiding in the Marshmens natural resistance to infection and disease.

All uniforms of the Marshmen are meant to be water repelent and seal tight and yet some how still breathable, as well thick enough to keep the Swamp Swarmers out, giant blood sucking insects that can drain a regular human of all their blood in minutes.

Rebreathers are a must on Myr, even with the natural resistance to disease and infection the number of toxic plants and noxious mushrooms would kill a human in minutes.

Marshmen prefer chain swords due to the the thick vines that cover the planet.

Marshmen as profound alchemists making tonics or cures to the many infections or diseases found on Myr, this can be seen by the bottles adorned on their belts. Leaving some to specailize in alchemy becoming the role of medic to squads of marshmen, the alchemist not only cure poisons and infections but will cure battle wounds and similar injurys.

Myrphants are giant elphant like creatures with people living on the back like mobile towns for trading and travel.

Bog berries are the main food source of the people living on Myr, normally served canned, bog berries are packed full of everything required in a normal diet and they surprisingly don’t taste bad,being sweet and meaty in nature.

Notable regiments

113th Myr Marshmen - Most renown regiment of Myr, they deploy more then any other regiment, being elite of the elite. This is the most highly decorated and successful regiment. Combined unit regiment. Known as the “Lucky 113th”. The 9th company being the most famous, known as the “War Born”

1st Myr Marshmen - Light infantry regiment, the oldest still standing regiment, a large portion of the 1st remains on Myr to guard the capital. Known as the “Narwik Guard”

76th Myr Marshmen - Light infantry regiment mixed with mortar teams. Known as the “Widow Makers”

35th Myr Marshmen - Light infantry regiment, deploys across the galaxy on a regular basis. Known as the “Black Lightning”

10th Myr Marshmen - Light infantry regiment that specializes in mountainous or cold weather missions. Known as the “Snow Gators”

20th Myr Reapers - Sniper Reapers regiment, highly decorated, hosting the best snipers of Myr, Known as the “Wraiths”.

88th Myr Ares Troopers - Regiment of Ares Troopers, renown for strict training regiments and success rate. Known as the “War Dogs”.

3rd Myr Marshmen - Specailizing in jungle warfare and recon missions. Known as “Swamp Vipers”

55th Myr Marshmen - Small regiment of sergeants and senior officers who deploy on high risk missions. Known as the “Twice Dead”.

99th Myr Obsidian Group - Special forces regiment mixed with Reapers, Ares Troopers, Veteran Sergeants, Emancipators and Veteran Officers who have all been hand selected to try out for the Obsidian Troopers and passing, Small Regiment of the best of the best Myr has to offer, handles top secret missions across the galaxy, have been known to be able to handle Imperial assassins in a fight, maybe not one verse one, but the Obsidian troopers are highly augmented and highly trained and skilled, working in 4 to 8 man teams, sometimes fighting whole armies, living to fight again. The number of current Obsidian Troopers isn’t known but rumored to only be roughly 5000.

5th Myr Emancipators - Elite jetpack medical troopers, they also specialize in chaos curruption cleansing missions.

-860th Myr Mechanized Marshmen - Mixed light infantry with chimera’s and light tanks.

-999th Myr Marked Regiment - Repeat offenders of rules, given one final chance, be it theft, insubordination, or even attempted murder, these men are given red vests, hence the name “Marked”, the red vest makes them easier targets in the swamp, and minimal gear, a chainaxe, 2 grenades, an autogun and some ammo. They are then sent out on suicide missions, if they come back there is a chance to be forgiven, but chances are very slim. If they die then so be it, it’s a good outcome regardless.

Cities Of Myr

Cities of Myr aren’t like hive cities just yet they’re large but not built like hive cities.

Narwik - Largest city and capital city. Around 50-60% of Myr’s Population lives in Narkwik. It’s the safest city on the planet. A bastion of prosperity to the Myr people. Narwik is the only city with a space port.

Hyram - Swamp city built directly ontop of the swamp using scraps and Myrcrete, port city, high gang population and crime. No secured walls surrounding the city main travel through the city is small barges.

Grynid - Rich well off Large city, Second largest city on Myr, Major trade city, Port city, relatively peaceful city. Has Large PDF manned reinforced wall surrounding city.

The Marshmen have trained with cadian kasrkin and Tempestus Scions.

Ranks of Marshmen

Enlisted

-Recruit-Junior Veteran - Survived a handful of battles.-Veteran - survived atleast ten Battles.-Senior Veteran - Survived many battles.-Corporal - Was a Sergeant in the PDF, Training to become one under a Marshmen Sergeant.-Sergeant - Seasoned Veteran either started as a Corporal in the PDF or was promoted by an officer.-Veteran Sergeant - has led many missions with success.-Senior Veteran Sergeant - has not only led many missions but has proven themselves a great leader, to lead platoons.-Sergeant Major - has been promoted by atleast a Captain to lead companies.-Veteran Sergeant Major - Leads regiments and reports only to Generals.-Sergeant Major of the Marsh - Highest enlisted rank achievable, works with high ranking generals, over sees all regiments of Marshmen. Only one at a time.

Officers

-Cadets - in the Grand Moth Academy still.-Officer Junior - Recently graduated, assigned to a platoon as second in command.-Officer - Leads Platoons, commands platoons and directs Senior veterans Sergeants.-Officer veteran - Second in command of companies.-Captain - Leads companies, directs company sergeant majors.-General - Leads regiments, gives orders to Captains and veteran sergeant majors.-Veteran General - Over sees all other officers in regiments and takes reports from generals only. Reports only to the General of the Marsh.-General Of The Marsh - works with politics, works with Sergeant major of the marsh on all military planning, over all leaders of the military.

Awards - in order of highest achievment.

-PDF Service Ribbon - Given to PDF Soldiers who has served their full two years.

-Marshmen Service Ribbon - Given to Soldiers who make it past Marshmen training and join their ranks.

-Bog Born Medal - Given to off worlders who have passed the Marshmen trials and became a Marshmen.

-Marshmen Officer Survival ribbon - Given to Officers for passing the Grand Moth Academy.

-Swamp Expert Badge - Awarded to Marshmen who return from the one month trial in the swamp alone.

-Hunter Killer Medal - Awarded to Marshmen who make it into the Reapers.

-Hell Born Medal - Awarded To marshmen who make it into the Ares Troopers.

-Swamp Veteran medal - given to Marshmen who survived many missions.

-Planetary War Veteran Medal - Given to Marshmen who have fought and survived on various other planets.

-Marsh Star - given for valor.

-Veteran Sergeant Medal - Given to exceptional Sergeants.

-Black Stalker Slayer Medal - Awarded to Marshmen who have proven they killed a Black Stalker.

-War Leader Medal - only given to senior leadership for many victories on many fronts.

-Hero Of The Marsh - Awarded for unbreakable bravery on the battlefield. Second Highest valor award available.

-Great White Moth Medal Of Valour - The highest medal available to any Marshmen, Enlisted or Officer,being awarded this medal means you went to hell and came back and then did it again, you faced unbelievable odds and came back alive. Every Marshmen who ever gets award this is recorded in history, the total number currently sits at 19, ever.
