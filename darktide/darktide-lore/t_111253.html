…If we disregard the damning evidence…

The Biggest support to Rannick being Swagger are their personalities. They both have a lush way of communicating, a sarcastic conveyance of responces, they mention they will “get on famously” with said person they are communicating with. Swagger tells Hadron and Rannick tells Brahams. This quip seems very specific, but also is a common phrase.

Rannick is not Swagger…

Diving deeper, Swagger comes off as toying with those around him, no denying or admitting to any of Hadrons pressing questions, It simply does not concern him. He also has an “old aquintance” with Grendyl that no one else can tie together.

Additionally, Darktide Locked and Loaded introduced Swagger on the Mourninstar

image705×270 16.4 KB

I failed to see if Rannick was looming above while Swagger was below during that time, pestering Hadron, on the mourningstar but Swagger reaallly likes mobien steel, especially when its being produced.

This leads me to the Overall story and Plot of Darktide.

What is on Atoma that Grendyl needs?

Why would Swagger be leading a mission to recover and produce mobien steel and then hover over Hadron while she fidled with it?

The questions lead me to believe its something very close to Grendyl and very specific to his survival.

The only two persons on mourningstar who would have cause to hide their identify whilts on the field of battle are Grendyl and Rannick. Yes, I still have not counted out Rannick, he has the ability to walk. We still have not seen Grendyls true form. I currently am of the thought that he is glued to the ship like the mutated Radical he is.

So … Who is Swagger?
