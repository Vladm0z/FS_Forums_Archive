There is not really anywhere particularly suitable on these forums to share my recently completed Darktide-related miniature, so I shall post it here. My Veteran is one of my two ‘day 1’ Rejects - alongside my Ogryn - and I have put a lot of thought into ol’ Erasmus Havelock since Oct 2022. As I feverishly awaited the game’s release between the beta test in October and 17 Nov ‘22, I directed my energies into creating my (what was then) Sharpshooter’s backstory. I knew it would be the class I would play most of all as the Guard had been my army of choice since around 1996.

Most of the figure builds and painting I have done over the past 5 years have been more passion projects than building and painting armies. At the beginning of 2025 I went to work on my first diorama, depicting my Veteran in his last moment of glory in the Guard before his fall, imprisonment, interrogation, and subsequent service to the Inquisition. That much bigger piece I await a better camera and cameraman to take pictures of (I know a guy but waiting on them to be free to do it). In the meantime, I had picked up an outfit from the store in-game and decided to recreate that and my Veteran in miniature format.

I have tried to depict Havelock in the flow of combat, with his old sergeant’s sword covered in the disgusting blood and … substances from the mutations which plaguing the heretics.
