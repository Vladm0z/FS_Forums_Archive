The Pride of Rocyria.

Nicknamed “The Chaff” by other regiments.

“The Dirt Provides, The Dirt Protects”

Primary Weapon: Low-Powered Helbore Lasguns

Regimental Lore & Origin: The Levied Harvesters - The 15th is not a standard, elite Astra Militarum regiment, nor are they outlaw rebels. They are a legally raised Planetary Defense Militia swept up into the Moebian Domain’s military structure due to desperate manpower shortages.

When the tithe demands flesh, entire farming communities are drafted all at once.Because they are raised at the local township level, their military hierarchy mirrors their civilian farming hierarchy. A platoon leader is usually the local plantation overseer, and the squad leaders are the senior field foremen.

They are the poorest recognized military units in the sector—treated as clumsy, uneducated rustics by Hive-born soldiers—but they possess a stubborn, legally binding devotion to defending their sector’s breadbasket.

Militia Structure & Low-Tier Funding Locally Sourced Uniforms: They lack the polished, factory-stamped uniforms of the elite regiments. Instead, they wear locally produced, Grox leather or coarse-spun fatigues of Muted Browns, Muddy Ochre, and Corrosive Earth Tones, even using old winter camo’s often, due to the fact they they know they will not stay white long and blend to the color of their environment due to not having paints available to change camo patterns for deployments. They have no produced flak armor, instead they craft their own or find on the battlefield. The only somewhat standard issue item is a thick coarse trench coat, colors may vary. The head gear worn by the 15th is leather caps reinforced with metal.

Low-Powered Helbore Lasguns: The Munitorum funds them with outdated, antique Lucius-pattern Helbore lasguns salvaged from long-forgotten PDF planetary reserves. Because Rocyria lacks high-output plasma generators, the regiment relies on low-grade, locally recycled power packs. The rifles still feature the heavy, high-impact mechanical frame of a Helbore, but the laser output is noticeably dimmer and less stable than standard military-grade variants.

The “Township” Kit: Instead of standard-issue military backpacks, troopers carry their gear in large stitched rucksacks, canvas tool-pouches, and Grox-leather bandoliers. For close combat, they use their own heavy-duty farming implements—specifically like shovels, hammers, or other old tools.

The long walk: All troopers of the 15th are trained extensively for long marches, they do not have the money for transport vehicles, so once deployed to a combat zone, if they are not close to the battle, they will have to march their way there. Their packs are heavy, stored with everything they will need, this compliments the endurance based fighting style they have become known for.

Combat Doctrine: “The Heavy Furrow” Massed Militia Volleys:
Lacking advanced marksmanship training, the 15th relies on massed, synchronized firing lines. Squad leaders blow heavy iron whistles to coordinate their low-powered Helbores, unleashing slow, devastating walls of combined laser fire that can halt advancing enemy infantry waves through sheer volume.

Agrarian Fortification: Spending lifetimes moving heavy soil and handling massive combine harvesters makes the 15th exceptional at manual labor. They can dig miles of deeply fortified trench systems, reinforced with wooden stakes and mud brickwork, far faster than mechanical excavators.

Unyielding Defense: Because they are structured around families, neighbors, and lifelong co-workers, the squads possess incredibly high morale when holding a line. They fight with a grim, unmoving determination, treating the defense of their trench network like a grueling, mandatory harvest that must be finished before sundown.

Family values: The 15th treat each other like one big family, eating chow together, sharing if they have more ammo, food, and so on, then others. This bond makes the 15th have a content persona when viewed by other regiments, as long as they have each other, in the 15th’s eyes, everything will be okay.

Earned many battle honours during the Eve Coronis Campaign, but not significant enough to warrant better funding or gear, but cemented them as a reliable regiment.

The Hero Of Rocyria Chloe is the most famous member of the 15th, she is the epitome of Rocyrian culture, she kind, honest, tough, and unbreakable. Chloe is the glue in the 15th, she has a incredible empathy for the others she serves with, being able to boost moral in the darkest moments, hence helping to bring the 15th to victory. she is small for her size but do not underestimate her in a fight as many others have. Chloe is also the company quartermaster, she provides small gifts for the others she serves with. Chloe is a kind, humble soul, but unshakeable on the battlefield. Chloe is strong for her size, and crack shot with her Helbore, named Ol’ Reliable, she is the heart of the 15th.

They have deployed for defense missions before with the krieg, at their base they are very similiar but the 15th view the Kriegers are cruel, and have little commaradery while the 15th are kind and family based.

The 15th do share a good standing relationship with 99th due to them both be poor planets, and sharing a resilence mentality.
