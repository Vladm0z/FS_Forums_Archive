Nothing personal against anyone, but out of respect for our post-Soviet and current war zone members, no Russian nationals (sorry Russians).

And just to clarify things, this group is meant for older people, so don’t bother if you are not old enough, or don’t have enough hours in the game.

Thank you for reading this, take care and have a nice day!
