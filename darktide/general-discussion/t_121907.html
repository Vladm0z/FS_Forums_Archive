@Pirx
Yup, you can very easily adjust it, but the issue is going above the official limit Fatshark set (8 was the hidden before and they increased it to 9 after releasing hive scum) breaks it, so you want to set it to 9 currently. Increasing it above works seemingly but the game breaks when you try to create the first above limit one.
Find your mod folder and go to…\mods\more_characters_and_loadouts\scripts\mods\more_characters_and_loadouts

And change the following line to this:
MainMenuViewSettings.max_num_characters = 9
It should give you the proper and currently active official limit of 9 operatives.
