Greetings, everyone!

I’m a long-time fan of the Tide series — I absolutely love Vermintide 2, and I’m deeply enjoying Darktide. I have enormous respect for the developers at Fatshark. However, I feel that Darktide could benefit from more visually distinct and thematically unique locations.

So I wanted to share one of my own ideas for a potential new mission location — the Oxygenarium — along with a concept for a final boss encounter.

In a vast Hive City there are countless zones that the Inquisition could deploy Rejects to cleanse of heretics and cultists. One such place is this Oxygen Production Complex — a forgotten and overgrown bio-laboratory responsible for generating breathable air for the hive. Visually, it could add a very refreshing tone to the grim darkness of the setting.

I’ve also prepared a short description of the location and some visual concepts.
All renders were created using AI, but I put effort into making everything feel as close to Darktide atmosphere and visual style as possible — it took quite a bit of time.

Boss Concept

(Image 10)

Abilities & Mechanics:

  1. Leaping Crush
    A delayed leaping attack. A glowing area appears on the ground before impact, dealing massive damage.

  2. Double Claw Strike
    Charges up and performs two very fast slashing attacks in front of him.

  3. Tentacle Grab
    Grabs a player with its tendrils and throws them a great distance.

  4. Feast of Gluttony
    The boss enters a defensive stance and unleashes a roar.
    All nearby mobs ignore the players and rush toward him.
    Each consumed mob restores the boss’s health.

  5. Wide Swings
    Sweeping attacks with tentacles and claws, knocking back players.

As an additional feature, the boss could use its vestigial wings to perform short dashes or pick up regular mobs and throw them at players.
