TLDR: I’m going to rant a bit about why I hate scoreboard, and because it’s being getting worse lately, so ignore this if you don’t care.

Removing the scoreboard was one of the best decisions FS took with Darktide IMO and I LOVE them for that.

I think a scoreboard does encourage competitiveness amongst strangers, and what’s worse is, it does not belong in co-op focused games that the tides series are.

Neither the classes, their weapons, the random spawns, player positioning, using an ability that saved the run at a critical point during the mission, or the combat mechanics (as a few examples) can be measured in such simple, arbitrary numbers.

You are not shooting at a single dummy with different weapons and comparing the results, you cannot even compare your own scores from various missions using the same setup, because each mission has so many immeasurable variables that cannot be studied and make sense, ever.

You can however use the psykhanium and test breakpoints, that gives you results that can be measured and compared reliably, in short, socreboard is in reality useless.

Let’s be honest here, scoreboard is attracting to players who want to boost their ego by topping the numbers, it’s the green circle chase from vermintide, monkey brain wants gold stars from teacher to feel special and that did good.
