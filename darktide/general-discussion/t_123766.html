zaygr • 2026-07-08 05:13:33

Vanguards were not fun because any way you could mechanically counter them and not just brute force through them was systematically neutered for reasons.

  • Their stagger thresholds were so high they wouldn’t react to hits, shield or not.
  • There was no mechanical way outside of a handful of weapons’ special attacks to open them up that isn’t just brute forcing their shield.
  • They had an insane amount of non-indicated, uncounterable universal damage reduction to punish you for daring to aim and go for their weakspot.
  • They have almost 3 times the health of their bruiser counterparts without any real indication for why they have so much health, and it also counters flanking or hitting them from behind because of how tough they were even if you actively attempted to mechanically bypass their most salient feature, their shield.

It’s an enemy design that doesn’t reward you for making good decisions; they were an unresponsive, visually uninteresting HP sink that did nothing but further punish weapons that were already struggling and did absolutely nothing to the current cream of the crop weapons.

The first set of changes made them more bearable, because at least they respond a bit more to your attacks now, but the implementation still sucks.

I don’t mind the shield damage cap, but I feel that if they have that, they should have a max damage threshold too so it doesn’t mess with slower, harder hitting weapons. For example, the shield has 500 HP, damage is capped to 250 damage meaning it requires 2 hits minimum to break but if you can hit the shield for 750 or more, it would ignore the cap.
