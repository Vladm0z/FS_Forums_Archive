Seriously, CDR spam is one of the biggest issues with the games balance rn, it’s exacerbating every unbalanced thing in the game.

Zealot’s Invocation of Death letting you spam Fury gives you full uptime on the attack speed buff (which is half of what lets zealot use the dueling sword comfortably into hordes), and gives zealot 50% toughness every 5 seconds which means you can play like an idiot and barely try to avoid dmg and get off scott free (esp with gold toughness spam). It also enables chorus to be used multiple times a minute, leading people to crutch on gold toughness and giga stagger spam.

Vet’s Tactical Awareness lets a vet spam full knockdown stagger and stack giant blankets of triple digit gold toughness on the team, enabling everyone to take tons of free hits for no consequences. Gold Toughness is op on its own but it wouldn’t be nearly as bad if it only had 50% percent uptime as its designed to do (15s Duration on a 30s cooldown). If you actually had to choose when to have it up it would at least be a little more reigned in and engaging. And of course this is multiplied by vet having the plasma gun, letting you snipe all the specials and proc the CDR.

Ogryn’s bruiser lets you use charge to knockdown all the armor ever on repeat, but honestly the bigger problem is CDR taunt. Light spam on the club mk3 with all og ogryn’s new attack speed and dodge stagger means you can have basically full uptime on taunt, making you functionally immortal since you can stagger all the armor you grab, and making your team immortal cause nothing is attacking them.

Then finally the biggest elephant in the room, Psyker’s Psykenetic Aura. The fact that this applies to your team is ABSURD. They’ve nerfed it barely this last patch but it needs to be kneecapped. At the very least it needs to work like Bruiser and IoD, and it needs to not affect teammates, but honestly it should just be removed. They said they’d consider looking at that talent more and I’d say they gotta destroy it. It enables the entire team to do all of the things outline above but even harder. It also enables psyker to spam venting skriek, putting giant 30+ stack dots on bosses and melting hordes before they can ever be a threat. It also enables staves to be even more broken because of how much you’re bypassing the intended resource management limitation. (Peril is supposed to limit the power of an infinite ammo staff! that’s why they nerfed quelling in havoc!)

I’m honestly worried Arbites will have something stupid to do with CDR on its own, especially since they already highlighted that its foot knight charge ability will have the shortest CD in the game. Fatshark has started to realize the toxicity of CDR talents by giving small nerfs and reworks to IoD, Bruiser, and Psykenetic’s Aura, but we need to go further. They’re overcentralizing talents that restrict build choice and are giant fun dampening crutches that should be removed.
