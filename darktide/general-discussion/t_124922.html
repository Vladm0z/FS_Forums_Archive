DeathLD • 2026-08-16 19:24:25

I think the class only needs some relatively minor adjustments rather than a complete rework.

The main thing I would change is the way its bonuses are distributed. A lot of them could be made more universal so that more weapons and builds can actually benefit from the tree. For example:

  • Add more fixed Impact bonuses instead of bonuses that are tied to very specific weapons or situations.
  • Add more ways to scale damage from attacking from behind, especially within the Stealth tree.
  • Replace some of the short-duration and highly specific damage bonuses with more universal damage increases.
  • Most importantly, add some kind of universal ranged Finesse modifier or ranged damage bonus to the Stealth tree. Ranged weapons should actually be viable for the class instead of feeling completely useless for most builds.

I would also rework the Blitz abilities, because some of them have become outdated:

  • Stun Bomb: It’s simply terrible at the moment and needs a significant buff or redesign.
  • Fire Bomb: The concept is good, but its current effect is just not impactful enough.
  • Knives: Of the three, knives are the only ones that haven’t become completely obsolete. They are still useful, but they definitely need some love to keep up with the current state of the game.

Overall, though, I don’t think the class needs a complete 180-degree rework. Its core identity is fine. It mainly needs its bonuses adjusted to be more universal, more weapons to be properly supported, and its Blitz abilities brought up to the current state of the game.
