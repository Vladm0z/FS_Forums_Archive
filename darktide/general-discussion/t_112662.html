I feel like I’ve been needlessly pessimistic lately so I want to try and reset my perspective by making this post and hopefully get some other folks to cool it. This forum has been rancid lately and it’s dragging me down.

First, a few acknowledgements:

  1. Fatshark doesn’t talk nearly as much as they should.
  2. Fatshark’s development is slower than it feels like it should be.
  3. Darktide is an increasingly unstable video game.

Got it? Ok, good. Here’s the main thing I wanted to bring to attention:

Most of the major updates have been pretty good. The game as a whole HAS gotten better over the years. It takes a lot of yelling and complaints, but Fatshark IS listening, even if they don’t speak out loud. We’re getting THREE tree reworks in the next update, all of which look really good, and I would consider this the main focus of the update since nobody really cares about Maneater Trees. I honestly expected just the one for Veteran or Zealot, but instead Vet, Zealot, and Psyker, all of which have been somewhat stale meta-wise, are getting reworked.

We are getting the new Veteran weapons we promised, even if they aren’t really all that cool. We got the locks broken last year, allowing for freeform experimentation and buildcrafting. We got the ultra sweat 3000 gamemode for ultra sweats, for all of its ranking system flaws. There IS something interesting going on here.

S’all. See you tomorrow, depending on performance issues.
