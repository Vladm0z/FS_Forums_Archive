Gimmick: The Blitzs and abilities interact with each other.

Blitz:

Rosarius: target a player to bless them, granting 10% strength, lasting 12 seconds and has 2 charges recharging every 40 seconds.

Rules of Sororitas: channel a prayer granting 25% toughness damage reduction and regaining 5% toughness per second for all players in coherency. Cast based on resource ( resource is gained by killing enemies) Max resource: 150 10 resource per second Basic enemy: 5 Elite and specialist: 15 Monstrosity: 25

Phosphor Grenade: A incendiary grenade with a small area of effect, direct impact inflicts 8 stacks of burn on contact and causes suppression in a large area of effect and leaves a small pool of fire. The pool of fire inflicts 1 burn stack every second and removes goo. Holds 3 grenades

Abilities:

Blessed Standard: Place a banner down at your location, The banner acts like a player for coherency. Players within the banner’s coherency are stun-immune and suppression-immune, regen 10% toughness per second and reduced stamina cost. Banner can be picked up like a battery and placed down. Last 30 seconds cooldown 80 seconds.

  • Rosarius can target a player within the banner’s coherency to apply the effect to one other random player in the banner’s coherency.
  • While using Rules of Sororitas, Enemies in the banner’s coherency have 15% brittleness.
  • Phospher grenade’s fire pool effect applies in the coherency of the the banner when thrown in the banners area of effect.

Nodes:

  • No surrender: Cooldown reduced by 20 seconds, but the banner can not be picked up.
  • Emperor’s wrath: While in the area of effect of the banner, the player and all allies get 15% rending.
  • Death March: After carrying the banner for at least 3 seconds, placing the banner down creates staggering shock wave. Activating this ability also creates a shock wave.

Death march and No surrender are mutually exclusive.

Fire and Fury: Enter a stance where you gain 25% move speed and 25% toughness damage reduction when moving towards enemies. Range and melee attacks deal 4 burn stacks. When you kill burning enemies, restore 5% toughness and 15% toughness for elite enemies. Last 15 seconds cooldown 45 seconds.

  • During the Fire and Fury ability, Blessing a player with the Rosarius also grants the range and melee burn effect.
  • The resource for Rules of Sororitas is not consumed during Fire and Fury.
  • During Fire and Fury, you can throw one free Phospher grenade.

Nodes:

  • Flash Fire: Burning enemies have a chance to explode when they die during Fire and Fury.
  • Sear the soul: Increase the Burn effect from 4 to 8.
  • Steel and flame: Range attacks against burning enemies deal 2 bleeding stacks

Judgement: Pull out a inferno pistol and fire a high power shot. The shot pierces through enemies and can crit. Cooldown 30 seconds

  • If judgement kills a elite enemy, the Rosarius is applied to you.
  • If Judgement kills a elite enemy, restore 15% of resource for rules of sororitas
  • If Judgement kills a elite enemy, gain one grenade

Nodes:

  • Double tap: increase the cooldown by 15 seconds, but fire the inferno twice.
  • Perfect shot: Weak spot kills with judgement refund 20% of the cooldown.
  • After firing judgement, gain 15% melee damage and 25% stagger for 8 seconds

Notable Talents

Faith in Fire: Inflicting burn heals 1.5 corruption

Heating up: 15% damage against burning enemies

Salamander: When using flamers, Killing elite enemies with damage over time restores 5% ammo, cooldown: 5 second

Stand with Me: 5% toughness damage reduction per ally in coherency.

Shared Hatred: 5% critical hit chance, When you critically hit a enemy, allies in coherency gain 10% critical hit chance.

Fortify Faith: Using a blitz restores 15% toughness over 5 seconds

Condemned target: When a range attack hit only one enemy, gain 10% critical hit chance, stacking up to 3 stacks. Stacks decay one by one every 10 seconds.

Righteous fury: Range critical kills grant 15% damage for 5 seconds
