  1. 50 extra toughness = you don’t lose Iron Will as easily, especially against Ragers.
  2. 50 extra toughness = great for Exe Stance and Stealth builds which don’t instantly restore all toughness like Voice of Command, and rely on maintaining toughness pool in the highs with 30% ranged damage reduction from Exhiliratng Takedown and Tunnel Vision.
  3. 50 extra toughness = instead of going all toughness, you can use stamina and health curios in Havoc without having 100 toughness and less than 100 HP @ havoc 40.
  4. 50 extra toughness = easier revives on teammates without abilities, gunners can’t zero you in an instant, synergy with Leave no One Behind without relying on having VOC for revives.
