deusvult6 • 2025-09-02 06:17:04

New levels take a considerable amount of work. And even then, you’re just fighting all the same things in a new arena. I could be wrong, but I’d think the best bang-for-the-buck in game variety per dev effort terms would be new units.

A whole new faction would be a lot of work all at once, so I get if they don’t want to do that but there are obvious niches that could be filled. Niches that were filled in VT2 but have never been here. Scab sergeants and Dreg priests for local buffs and elite variety. Chaos psykers with specialized abilities (a la life leech and blightstormer) and chaos sorcerers could be super-elite/monster class (or even assassination target) and have more varied ability set. Could even do funky things to play havoc with players comfort zones. Psykers that prevent ult use or project their own shields. That could be diabolical. Ogryn beserkers, plague bearers, plague toads, etc. etc.

Name dropping means nothing in this game any more than it did in VT2. It’s just lore drops. Stuff to tie it into the wider setting and get 40k newbies interested in the whole thing. DoW1 did that with loading screen lore tidbits and it got me hooked. The only banter “hints” I would give any credence to would be the ones about the immediate setting. Stuff to do with Tertium, Atoma, the Moebian Domain, and the Fringe. And even then, it may be a red herring. Or they might change their minds and do something entirely different leaving the old, now-nonsensical voicelines in.

If they did do a new faction though, I know a lot of people seem to be quite keen on genestealer cults. I have personally always found them quite bland but to each their own. For myself, I’d like to see the “Noxians” or whatever they are actually called, the primary xenos enemies of the Fringe War and the main contingent of the eponymous “Darktide”. Since they are supposed to be gobbling up poorly defended planets one after another and headed straight toward Atoma, the setup is all there for them.
