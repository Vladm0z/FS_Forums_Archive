If I’m picturing the situation correctly, Havoc difficulty has been at its absolute peak since patch 1.9.4 (October 20th last year), which nerfed the Arbites, up until the release of Hive Scum (December 2nd, 2025). And those one and a half months were the ultimate pinnacle of Havoc difficulty. I remember numerous threads here complaining about Rotten Armor with clown-car spawns of nearly invincible Crushers, and about Contaminated Stimms—especially Dreg Ragers, whom the blue stim combined with toxic gas made (literally?) unkillable. And then there was Encroaching Garden, which also painted absolutely everything pink while restoring their health. All this while Hive Scum and Skitarii weren’t out yet, and Arbites had already been nerfed

It looks like Havoc had some truly legendary difficulty back then (not that I find the current one lacking)

I remember my runs even on Rank 21 usually ending quickly and decisively. After that, I’d just sit in a corner and watch the sole survivor being chased by… 14? (I think they spawned in batches of 7) Rotten Armor Crushers at the same time. I suppose it would end just as quickly for the present-day me, since I still don’t know how to kite Crushers properly. I constantly catch an overhead the moment I turn my back to them (which I repeatedly demonstrated in my matches yesterday)
