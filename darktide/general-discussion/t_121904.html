derpherp • 2026-05-25 13:38:49

For new maps I’d want them to use some of the procedural tricks that they used in expeditions where they have spots on the map that can be switched out with different map chunks.

A core issue that Darktide has always had is that map creation takes Fatshark forever even when they were focused on it.

So it would be really good for Darktide if the new adventure maps had some of that changeability that expeditions has making them more replayable, it would also cause something good to have come from the miss with expeditions.

For example they can have a set of rooms or buildings at one point on the path that are from a random pre built selection. Or they could chunk out entire sections of the map, sometimes a building has collapsed and you need to cross the mounds of rubble, other times the ground has collapsed revealing a cavern you need to go down into to cross. If the system is robust enough it could even have layered tricks like the cavern that is revealed has random ruined ancient buildings in it. They could also do an expanded dynamic version of the open battlefield area in No Mans land, each time you cross it there are different buildings and craters and cover.

The possibilities with this tech could be so good for darktide’s main mode, I rly hope it isnt left to languish.
