While the steam comments are the typical cesspool of “how dare you nerf the the broken thing I’ve relied on,” it’s actually really encouraging to see that the dual problems of “the things that are good against carapace trivialize the entire game” and “the things that are not good against carapace are functionally useless” are at least being recognized.

Effigy • 2026-05-09 03:29:43

I think the big question now is whether this the start of a new trend toward releasing regular balance updates. This is a good start, but there’s more work to be done in terms of buffs, nerfs, and general reworks. Fatshark needs more consistency in the timing of these updates so things can be brought into reasonable balance on an acceptable time scale without making massive changes in a single patch.

I’d like to see more reworks of uninteresting and generally-ignored weapon blessings. Things like Thunderstrike and Vicious Slice may as well not exist in their current form. These could be reworked into fun new blessings to give more variety to weapons beyond basic stat increases. I’d also like to see nerfs or reworks to some of the popular blessings that just give damage/cleave bonuses with easy triggers and high uptime, because those will generally be chosen over “fun” blessings for pure efficiency.
