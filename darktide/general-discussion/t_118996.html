For the sake of our fingers and mice, can we please get an official “macro” function in the game? Some players have certain physical pain after playing.

Does anyone else feel like their index finger’s about to fall off after a run with Lasguns or Lucius on their Veteran?

The tech is clearly doable — there’s already a mod that lets you hold left click for full-auto, and it doesn’t interfere with melee charge attacks. That proves it’s not hard to implement, and people clearly want it.

But it’s not just semi-auto weapons turning fingers into cramped claws. Think about it:

  • Repeated interactions — carrying batteries, opening loot crates, grabbing ammo. It all adds up to a lot of pointless clicking.

  • Melee weapon combos — some weapons rely on fixed rhythm patterns for optimal DPS. Manual timing is inconsistent; macros could deliver clean, reliable output.

  • Item spamming — throwing multiple grenades quickly or chaining consumables. Why does this require frantic key mashing when it could be one smooth input?

These issues either wear out your hands or distract your focus. But mods have already proven the concept: hold left-click for full-auto exists and doesn’t mess with melee. So why stop there?

The logic can go further:

  • Give certain weapons a “combo assist” mode where rhythm-based attacks loop automatically while held.

  • Even introduce a “quick-use chain” for items — toss grenades without counting clicks.

This doesn’t break balance — fire rates stay the same, damage remains untouched, skill ceilings aren’t lowered. It just takes the “you must mash this button repeatedly to achieve normal effectiveness” nonsense and gives our fingers a break.

This isn’t cheating. It’s letting veterans keep playing for years and not scaring off new players with carpal tunnel syndrome.

Fatshark, hear us out. All classes, all players — we’d all benefit.
