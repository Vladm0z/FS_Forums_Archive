I think there’s a missing role in ogryn weapons with their lack of ‘power state’. They should definitely add a power weapon but I’m here to sell something else. In general moves kinda come out chunky and slow.

But what if an ogryn weapon had a special that activated a grab bag of buffs like a relic blade active, but also some general buffs like a scum stim? And had a drawback like old exhaustion?

Enter the slab fist knuckle dusters. By default they have terrible range and weak ADMs, but down a ration can for a 12s buff window of 15% attack speed, armor ADMs set to 1.0 or greater and 20% move speed. When you use Special you consume a can of rations, which has an animation similar in duration to stimming where you can’t block. On consumption, you gain a stack of full stomach, lasting 25 seconds. At 1 stack there’s no problems, but 2 and above start bringing in movement penalties, 3 and above taking 15% attack speed away.

Basically its a strong burst option, but if you continuously abuse the can you end up suffering a large debuff window from being bloated. Like most active/inactive weapons the balance would be in dealing with not being able to access the can for periods for fear of debuff. But because it has that debuff angle, it would always be avaliable. Bit of a buyer beware.
