WHY A REWORK ? : Because the Veteran is one of the very first classes added and probably conceptualized when the game launched. Each year, with new classes and updates, those older classes are starting to be left behind the new ones. So I believe a small rework could really help Veterans with a more distinguishable playstyle. He will obviously still focus on range combat, having the most range weapons variety and more toughness than any other classes to play around it with lots of toughness resistance buffs. However, to counterbalance this, he would be less efficient in melee combat than range. Vulnerable against melee enemies, very fragile when toughness breaks since most of his resistance buffs are TDR and Lacks of staggering potential outside blitz. Also, adding new weapons to furthermore specialise his playstyle could a be a huge plus for him.

  • HARDENED GRUNT : Hardcore Guardsmen blessed with the strength and will to survive the nightmarish battlefields of the 41st Millennium. These Hardened Grunts are experts in all aspects of warfare, from marksmanship, close combat specialist, to demolition work, and carry a variety of non-standard weapons and equipment. Invariably tasked with leading the charge against the enemy and of engaging the largest, most unpleasant foe they can find. Those grunts often end up performing the most physical, dangerous, and demanding work of ground combat. This archetype focuses on the most polyvalent playstyle you can theoretically perform with this class, buffing their melee combat capabilities, grenades and having to often swap between their range and melee weapons when the situation demands it.

  • SQUAD LEADER : Their primary role is to ensure the squad gets the job done. It is their duty to ensure that everyone under their command fights effectively on the battlefield with the most effective strategy. They are often given additional training and better equipment, enabling them to function as combat medics, vox-operators and other orderlies. This archetype focuses on granting a lot of diverse buffs, either offensive and defensive, to you and allies in coherency, making the whole team stronger if you fight as one with good coordination.

Veteran Squad Leader1920×922 439 KB

  • SHARPSHOOTER : Imperial Guard sharpshooters are specialist troopers of the Astra Militarum who possess deadly skills that enable them to kill the enemies of the God-Emperor from afar. These men and women are experts in locating an enemy, drawing a bead on him, and unleashing the killing round at the most opportune moment. Sharpshooters are master of the doctrine of “one shot, one kill” and can be relied upon to send a single bullet, bolt, or blast towards the heart of an enemy where hundreds, even thousands of rapid fire weapons have missed their mark. This archetype focuses on range weakspot hit and the ability to enter stealth to better position themselves for delivering the perfect shot.

AURA : Survivalist could get a buff, replace Fire Team with a new defensive aura and replace Close and Kill with a talent. You now have 3 viable auras and each one of them focuses on very specific situations such as utility, defensive, and offensive.

  1. BUFFED SURVIVALIST : Replenish 1% ammo for you and allies in Coherency whenever any of you kill an Elite or Specialist enemy. This can only occur once every 5s. The cooldown debuff only affects your allies.
  2. NEW UNDER PRESSURE : +20% toughness resistance against ranged attacks for you and allies in coherency.
  3. MOVED AND REWORKED PRECISION STRIKE : +25% range weakspot damage for you and allies in coherency.

-

BLITZ : Frag and Krak grenades remain exactly the same but the Smoke grenade got two new unique tweaks.

  1. SAME Frag Grenade : Throw a frag grenade that explodes after a short delay. Applies 6 Stacks of Bleed to all Enemies Hit, causing Damage over time.
  2. SAME Krak Grenade : Throw a grenade that deals devastating Damage. Sticks to Flak Armoured, Carapace Armoured & Unyielding Enemies.
  3. BUFFED Smoke Grenade : Throw a grenade that creates a lingering smoke cloud for 15s. The cloud blocks line of sight for most enemies and reduces the sight range of enemies inside it.

The explosion of the grenade will grant stealth for a very short time, like 5 seconds, to you and allies hit by it; attacking during the Smoke stealth will dismiss it. Also, a very popular idea from a lots of people would make the smoke grenade cloud to extinguish fire on the ground from barrels, scab bomber, and flamers and prevent it until the cloud disappears just like the smoke from Counter Strike.

ABILITY : All abilities remain the same in their core and get new modifiers. EXECUTIONER’S STANCE receive some tweaks and focus on chained killing priority targets. VOICE OF COMMAND has its cooldown nerfed and focuses on team support. INFILTRATE now grants a single buffed ranged attack, it focuses on mobility and burst damage.

  • 1 REWORKED EXECUTIONER’S STANCE :

    • Enter Veteran Stance for 6s. When in Ranged Stance you instantly equip your ranged weapon, deal +25% Ranged Damage, +25% Ranged Weakspot Damage against highlighted enemies, replenish 10% Toughness per highlighted enemy killed, and your Spread and Recoil are greatly reduced.

    MODIFIERS :

    • NEW UNWAVERING FOCUS : +50% toughness damage reduction against enemies’ ranged attacks, grant stunt and suppression immunity during Executioner’s Stance.
    • SAME ENHANCED TARGET PRIORITY : Executioner’s Stance now outlines highlighted Enemies for Allies in Coherency for 6s.
    • SAME THE BIGGER THEY ARE … : Executioner’s Stance now outlines all Ogryn, Captain, and Monstrosity Enemies and has its duration increased to 9s.
    • NEW HARDENED BRAWLER : Change the bonus damage against highlighted to be affected by both range and melee damage, you can now swap to your melee weapon without dismissing the stance but melee kills wont refresh the duration of the bonus.

    The Counter-Fire modifier is now in the base ability so it will highlight for 6s Specialist Enemies, Human-sized Elite and Ranged Enemies. Killing a highlighted enemy refreshes the duration of its bonuses for 6s as usual. No longer replenish 10% Toughness per second but per highlighted enemy killed to reward the player for killing valuable targets instead of regenerating toughness for doing nothing. The damage bonus is now only effective against highlighted enemies.

-–

  • 2 REWORKED VOICE OF COMMAND :

    • Shout to replenishes all Toughness for you and allies in coherency. Staggers all Enemies within 9m. Voice of Command now always replenish toughness to allies in coherency and can revive close downed allies but increase its cooldown from 40s to 60s.

    MODIFIERS :

    • SAME DUTY AND HONOUR :Provide for you and allies hit a +50 toughness that can exceed maximum toughness for 10 seconds.
    • REWORKED ONLY IN DEATH DOES DUTY ENDS :On ability activation, freely drop one of your current grenade on the ground at your emplacement.

    To me, Voice of Command is a great and useful ability but in its current design, it suffers from being overused to simply play the game safer. I think that by increasing the cooldown from 40s to 60s would balance it and the only way to reduce the cooldown would be with new interactive talents, engaging you more in a Team Support leader role. I’m always baffled when I play with Veterans that choose this ability but never take the revive modifiers, whilst it has the potential to save the entire run, but they would rather put one talent point elsewhere. So I believe that the revive modifier should be in the base ability. It would make Veterans players to think when it’s the best moment to use this ability, either to save an ally or to make you and your allies to tank more enemies’ attacks, instead of spamming it.

-–

  • 3 REWORKED INFILTRATE :

    • Enter Stealth for 8 seconds, gaining +25% Movement Speed and replenish 10% toughness per seconds while stealthed. Leaving Stealth Suppresses nearby Enemies around you. While in Stealth, and for 8s afterwards, you have +30% Damage. Attacking makes you leave Infiltrate if this attack was a ranged one, it will get +100% weakspot strength and +100% crit chance.

    MODIFIERS :

    • NEW BLEND IN :+30% damage to ranged backstab while stealth in 8s afterwards. Ranged attacks no longer display the stealth. Only the first shot is a buffed guaranteed crit.
    • REWORKED HUNTER’S RESOLVE :Infiltrate now provide +30% toughness damage resistance during and 8 seconds afterwards the stealth end.
    • SAME OVERWATCH : +1 ability charge but +33% cooldown
    • NEW AMBUSHER KILL TEAM :On ability activation, apply Infiltrate and its modifiers to allies in coherency.

    Replenish toughness the longer you stay in stealth rather than fully replenish it on ability activation, because its way to similar to Voice of Command. Greatly increase your next ranged shot damage with a guaranteed crit and more strength damage if it’s a headshot. Making this ability more like an adapted Shroudfield for this class, while the Zealot stealth consists of an empowered Melee Backstab attack, INFILTRATE becomes the opposite with an empowered Ranged Headshot attack.

-–

KEYSTONE :

  • 1 MOVED AND REWORKED WEAPON SPECIALIST :

    • +50% weapon swap speed. Killing enemies in melee grants 1 stack of RANGED SPECIALIST up to 5 for a maximum of +25% ranged damage, +20% reload speed, +20% range attack speed and +100% critical chance only for your next range shot, all for 8 seconds. Killing enemies with range weapons grants MELEE SPECIALIST +25% melee damage, +15% melee attack speed, -25% stamina cost, and +10% dodge speed and distance. Both buffs are separated and only one is active depending on the weapon you are wearing.

    MODIFIERS :

    • BUFFED ALWAYS PREPARED : Melee kills replenish your ranged weapon clip from your ammo reserve by 3.3% per melee kills only when MELEE SPECIALIST is active.
    • ON YOUR TOES FUSIONED WITH INVIGORATED : Activating MELEE SPECIALIST or RANGED SPECIALIST replenish 20% toughness and stamina back.
    • MOVED GRENADIER : Kills during MELEE SPECIALIST and RANGED SPECIALIST grant 1 stack of GRENADIER SPECIALIST up to 30, granting 3.3% chance to throw an additional grenade per stack that will still only consume a single grenade. So the effect is guaranteed at max stack. Consumed all stacks when throwing a grenade even if this effect didn’t activate.

    Change this Keystone to fusion both AGILE ENGAGEMENT with ON MOTION, which makes more sense for the playstyle this keystone focuses on, and keep the original buffs of Weapon Specialist. The stamina cost reduction from the modifier is now in the base keystone. Reduce by 5 the number of melee kills required to fully charge RANGE SPECIALIST but reduce both buff duration from 10 seconds to 8. This keystone focus on a polyvalent and aggressive gameplay that forces you to consistently swap between melee and range weapon to benefit from those huge buffs. Also, I put the Grenadier talent in the modifier for extra layer and removing the randomness of the effect.

-–

  • 2 REPLACING FOCUS TARGET BORN LEADER :

    • FOCUS TARGET! This keystone is a fusion of multiple talents in one BORN LEADER, MARKSMAN, CLOSE QUARTER KILLZONE and RENDING STRIKES. Allies in coherency replenish 20% of any toughness that you replenish, and you have a +50% Coherency radius. On ability use, grants for you and allies in coherency +20% weakspot strength, +15% damage, +20% rending to all weapons, and +25% damage reduction for 10 seconds. Increase the duration by 3.3 seconds per allies affected, up to +10 additional seconds. So you can double the duration of those buffs if your entire team is rallied around you when you activate your ability.

    MODIFIERS :

    • NEW READY FOR ACTION : On ability use, grants additionally for you and allies in coherency +50% ability cooldown regeneration.
    • NEW STRIKE TOGETHER : On ability use, grants additionally for you and allies in coherency +15% attack speed, movement speed and reload speed.
    • NEW PLATOON COMMANDER : On ability use, grants additionally for you and allies in coherency +100% aura efficiency.

    Totally replacing FOCUS TARGET which is now a talent fusioned with COVERING FIRE. This makes this keystone a team supportive tool. Forcing the whole squad to have good team coordination and rewarding all players with powerful defensive and offensive buffs when using your ability, making you a real squad leader.

-–

  • 3 MOVED AND REWORKED MARKSMAN’S FOCUS :

    • Ranged Weakspot kills grant 1 stack of Focus and 3 stacks for elites and special weakspot kills. Up to 15 Max Stacks. Lasts 5s. Stacks are refreshed on Weakspot Hit and decay one at a time. Each stack of Focus grants +7.5% Ranged Finesse Strength, +1.5% range weapon stability and +2% Reload Speed. For a maximum of +112.5 range finesse strength, +30% reload speed and +22.5% range weapon stability.

    MODIFIERS :

    • MOVED LOW PROFILE : -6% threat per stack. So -90% at 15 stacks. When you reach the max stacks, enter stealth for 5 seconds; range attack won’t break the stealth. 20 seconds cooldown.
    • NEW CONCEALED POSITION : +1% range strength and range attack speed per stack. So +15% range strength and range attack speed at 15 stacks, +10% range crit chance and suppression immunity at 10 stacks.
    • BUFFED TUNNEL VISION : +1.5% toughness damage reduction per stack. So +22.5% toughness resistance at 15 stacks. At 10 stacks, grant you +40% toughness replenishment.

    The changes are that the modifier Long Range Assassin that increases by 5 the maximum of stacks is now in the base keystone. Change the way of gaining stack by 1 for regular enemies weakspot kills and 3 for elite and special. Increase the reload speed buff from 1% to 2% per stack. Add a new buff of +1.5% range weapon stability to improve your precision to more easily land your headshots. This keystone would force you to focus on landing precise headshots and rewarding you for being good at it.

TALENTS : Completely replace all Operative modifiers into talents. 46 talents in total, buffed the worst ones and some have been reworked to synergize better with the rest of the talent tree. 4 Unskipable talents placed before and after Aura, Blitz and ability so you can now swap them before a mission without having to destroy your actual build.

Just like the Skull-Splitter and Blessed Tome special action of the Warrior Priest of Sigmar from Vermintide 2. You can overcharge this weapon by maintaining heavy attack or the special action after a short delay to greatly empower your next melee attack with an AoE shockwave explosion around the target hit.
