Just have the psyker deal 75% of its maximum HP with Brain Rupture (No killing blow required to get the penance) I find it baffling why you need to be the one to deliver the final blow in the first place when during a very hectic mission, your teammates will likely take the killing blow because its just common sense to turn your attention to said monstrosity, robbing you of the penance (While I wasn’t there for when the penances were first added, I’ve heard stories of psykers holding their teams hostage while they try to do this particular penance.)

These are my proposed changes, because for the other classes; I have completed Ogryn, Veteran, Arbitrator, and Hive Scum - but these two in particular, haunt me with their 57/60 completion.
