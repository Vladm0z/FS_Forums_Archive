Well, here are the reasons why I believe Veteran struggles to perform in Havoc:

  1. The inherent ammo scarcity due to Havoc’s debuff makes it hard to maintain ranged pressure.
  2. Compared to skills like Exhilarating Takedown or Infiltrate, temporary toughness generation abilities are generally much more valuable.
  3. While not currently present, debuffs like “Gunk” (which reduce ranged damage) would be devastating for Veteran.
  4. To truly reach high damage potential, you’re pretty much forced to run the Weapon Specialist keystone.

I think I may have caused some misunderstanding by describing the class as too weak.
That said, I still believe Veteran has a hard time competing with Ogryn or Psyker in terms of ranged DPS when playing the pure damage role.

Taezzang • 2025-07-21 22:44:55

The Arbites class is certainly viable, and there are already plenty of Arbites players in Havoc parties across the Asia region.
That said, most Korean players tend to describe the class as “somewhat awkward” in the current meta.

A common opinion is that its role overlaps heavily with the Veteran. From my experience, it offers similar elite and special-handling capabilities, and thanks to its many damage-boosting skills, Arbites can feel very powerful.
It also has excellent crowd control thanks to multiple AoE CC abilities. Personally, I’d describe the class as something like a hybrid between a Veteran and an Ogryn.

However…

Despite its intimidating appearance, Arbites lacks reliable damage reduction. Aside from Castigator’s Stance, it has very few damage mitigation tools compared to other classes, and the ones it does have tend to be conditional. As a result, when exposed to ranged fire, it can feel as fragile as a Psyker.
On top of that, aside from Nuncio-Aquila, Arbites doesn’t offer much in the way of direct team utility. This makes it difficult to justify as a replacement for one of the existing core classes.

In order to play Havoc effectively, Ogryn and Psyker are considered nearly irreplaceable due to their unique roles.
Meanwhile, both Zealot and Veteran offer overwhelming utility, so most players still believe sticking with the original four classes is the optimal choice for Havoc.
