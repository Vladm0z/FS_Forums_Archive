40k isnt like other franchises where everything that is written down is canon or canon adjacent. the reason for this is that its a vast IP with dozens of writers that contradict each other at times.

the in universe explanation would be that of the unreliable narrator. most information is written from a flawed perspective of imperial citizens.

anyway what does that have to do with felinids?

well 40k operates on something akin to a redstring an overarchin narrative all these writers orient themself divert a little and then come back to, and to know canon of 40k is basically to see the red string not what individual writers say.

and for felinids they havent been part of the red string for quite some time, and thus are very unlikely to make an apearance, for all intents of purposes they dont currently exist in the setting.

we have seen this many times before in 40k where races quietly phase out of the debate, and maybe or maybe not make a new appearance when GW decides they can make money by bringing them back

examples of this are:

Squats (the biggest example ironically) (reintroduced as Votann)

zoats

Slann

beastmen

jokairo (not gone but leading up to 5th edition werent mentioned much) now part of lore again

all these are races that were mentioned in official 40k novels or rulebooks (rogue trader) and quietly were phased out and some made reappearances.
