Khorne_DawgRegular • 2026-05-09 09:17:31

Power Falchions, in my opinion, need improved ADMs against both Maniac and Unyielding, as well as a SLIGHT Finesse buff.

As for why?

Finesse:
Power Falchions’ current Attack Speed just makes them feel a teensy bit too slow to swing compared to similar options. Having slightly more rewarding Weakspot Damage wouldn’t be a bad thing either, in my opinion, considering the nature of such a Weapon.

Maniac ADM:
Better Breakpoints against Mutants and Dreg Ragers would specifically go a long way with making Power Falchions feel better to wield.

Unyielding ADM:
Seeing as Power Falchions don’t really have a lot of Boss Damage nor any reliable way to break open a Bulwark’s Block, which even Chain Weapons do, the instances where you DO hit their “fleshy” spots should be better rewarded.
