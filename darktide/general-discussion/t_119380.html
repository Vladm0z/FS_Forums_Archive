Why are “Voc with Duty and Honor” and “Bubble Dome” so powerful in this game?

Any reasonable person knows that the answer lies in the fact that these skills don’t just benefit the caster—they also give the other three teammates a moment of respite in the face of endless threats.

It’s the same with Chorus: carrying these skills inherently reduces a player’s own damage output and flexibility. Any other skill allows you to easily deal more damage and enjoy more kills.

But this is a four-player cooperative game; we rely on each other’s help to survive, especially on Havoc difficulty.

Given that players are already making a sacrifice by carrying these skills, further weakening them and undermining cooperation among players is utterly ridiculous.

Complaining that these skills are overused is like complaining that players prefer to fight together as a team of four rather than using stealth to solo the entire game—it fundamentally goes against the game’s core premise of four-player cooperation and defies basic logic.

If Fatshark wants to convey that this game was always intended to be a single-player experience rather than a four-player co-op, then please release a true single-player mode—instead of forcing us to face monsters and difficulty levels scaled for four players.
