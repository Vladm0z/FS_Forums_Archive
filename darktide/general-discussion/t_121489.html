Hello. I post here only rarely, and indeed haven’t been playing dark tide much in the last 3 months or so, but I have been a player since the second round of beta. Reading some of the discussion around the recent (and sorely needed) balance update, a particular dynamic crystallised in my mind. Something like this:

  • The intent for high Havoc is ostensibly “Absurd difficulty for the most rarefied of players”. That is to say by extension, not the difficulty the game should be balanced around.
  • An astonishing number of players feel that high Havoc is where they belong. I’m not going to say that they don’t, but perhaps they shouldn’t expect to win so often as they do, but for certain weapon/build power outliers.
  • Auric/maelstrom is presumably intended to be the peak for difficulty for average players, otherwise why separate the player pool into different buckets?

So what exactly am I claiming to have realised? Havoc is responsible for part of our long-lived balance woes that are likely to remain entrenched until either

  1. Havoc is nerfed to the point of losing it’s identity
  2. Fatshark make an explicit commitment to not balance the game around Havoc
  3. Havoc goes away

Now I can tell you which of those most likely. As for which one I’d want? Option 2 seems ideal for the largest number of people, although I’d bet there’s a substantial demographic that would be perfectly happy with number 1. Other more nuanced possibilities are also likely to exist, but that’s almost another whole discussion.

Havoc exists. The negative review brigade feel entitled to play on the highest provided difficulty. In some ways FS may struggle to unscrew that pooch.
