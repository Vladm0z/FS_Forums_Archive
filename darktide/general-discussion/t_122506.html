There isn’t really a hard set META as of late. Some people say there is but there are plenty of very viable team linups you can play. It’s better to think of it more as there being general roles that many classes can fill multiple of rather than there being any set roles. Yes bubble+chorus+shout+taunt is a good way to just brute force your way through havoc with literally no skill required but plenty of people enjoy playing more interesting things.

Better to think general roles like for instance you probably want someone who is a good frontliners with good crowd control and damage. For that you can run taunt ogryn, arbitrator, charge zealot, melee psyker. Etc you can apply this to basically every slot in a 4 man team with the basic ideas being something like.

  • Enough hoard clear
  • Enough specialist/shooter removal
  • Enough carapace removal
  • Enough defense/crowd control
  • Enough toughness generation
  • Enough boss damage

Every class brings varying amounts of each of these to a lineup and as long as your lineup ticks all these boxes it’s plenty viable. And if you just want to know who is the best at what and just in general who is the best: https://youtu.be/dndum4eUdc4?si=DDGc05kXoK9DTKEj

For Arbites, I don’t think much has changed. I prefer Shield Mk 6, but Arbites Shock Maul is still the top weapon. For grenades, still just take Shock Mines. These might be even more meta with Skitty coming. Dog is very useful, but the grenade regen is just even stronger. For ranged weapon, I like to run Bolt Pistol, the one with the bigger explosion. Put Bleed on direct hit blessing on it - this is how you damage bosses, fire four shots to stack up the bleed on it and then keep it topped off. If you want dedicated anti-boss, use the full Bolter.

At least, that’s how I’ve run it and had success and a lot of fun.
