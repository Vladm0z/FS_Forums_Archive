@FatsharkQuickpaw

Hi, everyone! A bit over a year ago, I made a Topic about Infantry Lasguns and how Fatshark should buff them. Infantry Lasguns still haven’t been buffed, and my views on how to buff Infantry Lasguns have become a bit more nuanced than before.

I want to thank @Epic_Cole and @Reginald for their contributions to this. So, let’s get to it! Here are my suggested focuses on buffing them:

  • Allow the Player to fire them continuously whilst holding the trigger down. This wouldn’t be a buff but a QoL adjustment, specifically focused on preventing carpal tunnels and repetitive strain injuries. Your mouse will thank you, too!

  • Buff their Damage against Flak to account for the Flak buffs introduced in the “Bound by Duty” Update. They weren’t anywhere near overperforming against Flak prior to the aforementioned Flak buffs; therefore, this’d basically just be a spot check.

  • Buff the Mark IX’s DPS to match it with that of the other two Marks. The difference between the Mk IX and the other two Marks in the area of DPS is day and night.

  • Change their Dodge and Movement Profiles from Marksman to Assault.

  • Give them a small amount of Cleave on non-Armoured Enemies, to help with both horde clear and hitting priority targets within hordes. They currently struggle too hard to account for the Enemy Density changes that’ve occurred since the last time Fatshark are known to have really looked at them.

  • Reduce their Hipfire Spread. When you account for the ridiculously high Visual Recoil and slight tunnel vision caused by ADSing with them, the insane Hipfire Spread is just borderline miserable to deal with. Hipfire shouldn’t be so inaccurate on such a Weapon.

  • Reduce their Visual Recoil. It’s ridiculous how much the Sights bounce around on Infantry Lasguns, which causes the Sights to effectively obscure your targets. Same as the Hipfire Spread, it’s just borderline miserable to deal with.
