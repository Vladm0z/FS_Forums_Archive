Hey everyone,

What builds do you find most successful for carrying or clutching games in Auric and above?
(Not looking for a balance discussion here, just what you are more comfortable with.)

My Take

For me, Stealth Zealot with Combat Knife feels like a near-guaranteed carry 99% of the time. If I fail, I know I messed up badly. Or maybe it was just server shenanigans .

Best Loadout:

  • Combat Knife + Whatever carry-worthy ranged weapon suits your style:
    • Flamer: Reliable in all situations, as long as you can aim your knives for specials/shooters
    • Bolter: Solid and impactful
    • Revolver: Underrated powerhouse (used to be the go to “nerf this” weapon)
    • Agripinaa Braced Autogun: Less flashy but effective and reliable.

Ogryn feels unstoppable across most builds, although he suffers from disabler vulnerability. And of course, gunlugger is now a dead meme build (RIP Twin Stubber Gunlugger).

Veteran can clutch, though it’s not as “safe” as Zealot in my experience.

  • Shout builds are strong but semi-team-dependent if I have to clutch with one, I need to play WAY more tactically. Fun challenge though!
  • Infiltrator builds can be hit or miss. Veteran’s melee just doesn’t compare to Zealot’s “space marine” power level.

Best Veteran Weapons:

  • Bolter – Super strong
  • Plasma Gun – Great damage but hitreg can feel clunky with hitreg shenanigans.
  • Las weapons – Especially las pistols, a true jack-of-all-trades, underrated asf.
  • Autoguns & Agripinaa Revolver – Solid choices.

Psyker… Feels Rough

I’ll be real, I struggle the most clutching with Psyker. I’ve seen absolute madmen/gals pull off insane solo plays, but it’s rare. In 1800+ hours, I’ve only seen it happen once or twice.

They just feel like “all glass, no cannon when it really matters.”
Yeah, I know, I’ve seen the 1M+ damage Psykers in Auric/Havoc lobbies. But in those cases, they had ideal team setups covering them, once they’re on their own, they usually flop.

Weirdly enough, I started playing VT2 recently and have the opposite experience there I feel more confident clutching as Sienna (Necro) than Shade for instance.

What’s your go to clutch build?

What builds are you absolutely confident in when carrying bad teams or soloing tough content?
