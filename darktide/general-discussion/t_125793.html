ramrod_ikarus • 2026-09-22 11:51:48

Some answers to your questions:

Head taker has a duration of 3.5s and slaughterer has a duration of 4.5s. Hammer special + full thrust charge takes roughly 3.5s. That means you have a high chance of loosing all head taker stacks before landing your big hit. If you ask me that is a trap.

Slaughterer with it’s higher duration is far more reliable. The problem is you always must be killing horde before going for a delete. Thus it works far better with FotF, as that ability allows a quick repositioning.

Skull crusher and thunderous have the problem you already described; you must be hitting your enemy some before going for the kill. That is also working right against the Thunder Hammer idea.

The hitmass blessing also does not grant any utility the 14 cleave heavy attacks cannot solve themselves. It does not even allow the iron helm to hit two elites…

So the problem is not thrust being too good or too strong but rather all other blessings working contrary to the design philosophy…
