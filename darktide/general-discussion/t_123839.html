ILikeFatShark • 2026-07-10 19:22:30

For short, Redline capacitors and Static Capacitor Drain only affect the base 2% capacity
generation.

Detailed, I tested some bug which I read in Reddit: Reddit - Please wait for verification

Redline capacitors and the static capacitor drain augment node for the power overload keystone have an effect that states “+25% capacitance generation” or “+5% capacitance generation ~per stack~”. Based on this wording, one would expect it to enhance all forms of capacitance generation, which would be quite nice. However, that is not the case.
Your capacitance generation occurs in several ways.
Firstly, you generate 2% capacitance every second. To simplify, this is the ONLY form of capacitance generation that these abilities influence.
They do not impact:
The 2% capacitance generated from a kill
The 4% capacitance generated from a specialist or elite kill
ANY OTHER TALENT that generates capacitance.
This includes the Augmented Power-Cycle talent, which generates 1% capacitance per second, similar to the base 2% generated per second.

Test with mod cowldown analysis in psykarium and public games.
