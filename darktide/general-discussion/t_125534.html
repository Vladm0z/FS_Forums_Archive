As I understand it, the way Rotten Armor works now is that rotten enemies are infested armor type, but carapace center mass, so your melee attacks will do the same damage as it would into a poxwalker (minus the DR effect from Rotten Armor that still exists), but dots and the like will be hitting carapace, and thus do way less damage than normal (even before the Rotten DR effect is applied).

The TLDR is that Rotten enemies feel way better to fight and it’s not a boring slog to deal with a huge pile of them, but they are a reasonably effective counter to stuff like inferno psyker and PBB ogryn and whatnot that rely on dots to do their damage.
