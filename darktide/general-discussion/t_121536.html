Taezzang • 2026-05-13 02:59:22

While it’s good to listen to users’ opinions on balance discussions in the forums, I’d prefer that Fatshark base its balance patches primarily on its own statistical data.

Everyone participating in these balance debates here is discussing the issue without knowing various metrics, such as which builds have high or low win rates, which weapons have high or low win rates, which skills have high or low uptime, or what the average DPS is. Of course, I’m no exception.

Personally, I found it hard to understand the recent nerf to the Krak Grenade because, in my experience, Smoke felt like it had better performance and a higher win rate. However, if there had been official data showing that Krak actually had a significantly higher win rate, I would have readily accepted the nerf without any questions, even if it contradicted my personal feel. Having access to these metrics would eliminate unnecessary doubt and help players align with the developers’ intentions.

As such, the meta likely varies by player, and on a larger scale, by region, and everyone has different opinions. So, what I’d like to suggest is
I’d like to ask if Fatshark could release the various statistical data they have.

If we look at the actual statistics generated by players during gameplay, we might find that certain builds, weapons, or skills have surprisingly high win rates—contrary to what we expected—or that some we thought were strong actually have low win rates. We could also see how much the average match duration varies by region and how meta builds differ, which would make it easier to understand the differences in perspective between players from different regions.

I think this could lead to more meaningful discussions rather than users simply arguing based solely on their own experiences.

What do you all think?

I think numbers are only useful as a rough reference point.

For example, imagine two Veteran players joining Auric with DS, Krak grenades, and VoC equipped.

One player only has Malice-level skill. Even with a fully optimized meta setup, they still struggle heavily in Auric and end up with an awful winrate. (Everyone has seen the phenomenon of players with Havoc 40 titles constantly going down in regular Auric missions.)

The other player actually has the skill level required for Auric. With the same meta setup, the game becomes trivial unless an accident happens. Their winrate would probably exceed 80%.

That’s why statistics don’t mean much unless you also account for player skill, and player skill varies massively from person to person. Realistically, it’s impossible to fully account for all of that.

Ever since the game’s balance started falling apart, the average skill level of Auric players has dropped dramatically. Most players are honestly below Malice level now, and the constant quitting is another major issue.

So in summary, data should only be treated as a reference. I think the most reliable balance discussions come from players who genuinely understand the game at a high level.
