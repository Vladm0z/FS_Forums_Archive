I keep reading about how strong the Arbiter is and how he needs to be nerfed. But he’s far from strong in every situation! Monsters are basically some kind of curse — the Arbiter can’t do anything against them, neither in melee nor at range. Shooting them with the Mk III shotgun is like picking at a concrete wall with a nail. Melee is the same, except with the bonus that they can throw you off the map or swallow you. Grenades don’t stun them (at least it seemed that way to me), and mines don’t stun them, and Breaking the Line doesn’t stun them either. Total uselessness, basically.

I guess in some ideal scenario the Arbiter is supposed to distract and stun other enemies while the other three deal with the monster. But it usually ends up being the opposite — everyone else is dealing with other enemies, while the monster goes after the Arbiter as a priority target. Especially fun when you’re in a tight space where you can’t escape.

The most anti-monster build is probably the one with the dog: Remote Detonation at least stuns monsters, Castigator Stance so you can focus fire on the monster while ignoring other enemies trying to disrupt your shooting, and a bolter with an extended magazine. But right now I’m trying to play as a Lone Wolf with the Mk III shotgun, and it feels like there are no tools against monsters at all.
