Molonious • 2025-11-21 16:15:05

The unfortunate problem isn’t that these pennances are private, it’s that they’re fundamentally poorly designed in the first place for a 4 player coop game and Fatshark shouldn’t have introduced them in their existing forms in the first place. They’re not the sort of thing that can really be organically attempted and completed through normal play typically (train mission and flesh wound being a much later development), rather they require a dedicated run with a supporting team that’s willing to play along. They’re the sort of thing that belongs in a single-player or solo experience, not trying to rope 3 other people into helping accomplishing your single Pennance, which is what led to them being made Private only so people would stop spoiling quickplay runs trying to complete them. I’m not sure why Fatshark didn’t design (or redesign) these in such a way as to be more suited to the game they actually made, but their fundamental design is the real problem, they’re inherently badly designed achievements for Darktide. Making them requirements for other pennances was even more inexplicable and someone at Fatshark should be genuinely professionally embarrassed over that.
