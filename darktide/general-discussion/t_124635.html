ZoroDaOtter • 2026-08-03 21:35:28

Behaviour.

Dreg behaviour should reflect crazed berserkers. All horde units should have decent melee and should be fast: high movement speed, fast attack speed, and should look like they’re just swinging wildly. They should feel “unescapable” and “endless” to fight. The faction should be resistant to ranged in general to encourage melee. Horde units should be more numerous, harder to swing thru, but die quickly to melee. Higher teir units should be more tanky. The ranged should be spray and pray with the ability to stun for longer or even push players back when getting hit.

Scab behaviour should reflect an army of trained elite guardsmen, and they kind of already do. Scab Shooters prefer to take cover and throw down mass accurate volleys, to the point that they can be more dangerous than one or two gunners with enough mass. The problem is the nature of the game. Against large ranged threats players can always pull back to deal with the melee then return to deal with the ranged. Thus for scabs spawn behaviour should be changed. Scab patrols composed of light elites/horde units should be spawned from behind the player team and flank during horde events or if the team is sitting in one place for too long. This would make it seem like you’re fighting a group using actual tactics.

The best way would be to add more elite units to Dregs that behave differently from Crushers/Maulers.
