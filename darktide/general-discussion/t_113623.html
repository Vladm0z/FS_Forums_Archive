During the Introduction stage in late 2022, the game launched with significant anticipation due to Fatshark’s prior success with Vermintide. However, technical and performance issues at release limited adoption. Marketing efforts at this time focused heavily on generating excitement through cinematic trailers, closed beta testing, influencer previews, and Steam promotion, leveraging the strength of the Warhammer 40K brand to attract early adopters.

In the Growth stage throughout 2023, Fatshark released patches and major updates that improved gameplay stability and user experience. The game’s availability on Xbox Game Pass helped expand the audience. Marketing priorities shifted toward community-building—through transparent patch notes, developer updates, and direct engagement on social media—to rebuild trust and strengthen the player base. Influencer campaigns and community events were used to maintain momentum and increase visibility.

By 2024 and into 2025, Darktide entered the Maturity stage, where the player base stabilized into a loyal but smaller niche audience. Fatshark’s focus turned to retention strategies typical of live-service games, such as introducing new map, useless modes and cosmetics.

Looking ahead, Darktide may approach the Decline stage unless it receives major expansions like a new faction or biome. As newer co-op shooters enter the market, engagement may gradually decrease. Fatshark could respond by extending the product’s lifecycle through DLC but they seem to clearly be all about redirecting resources toward developing a new IP and offering bare minimum updates.
