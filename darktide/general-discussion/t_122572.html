Intro

I think it would be very interesting to gather and chart a decent sample size of how the community performs on various classes. Of course this will be biased due to these stats coming from obviously invested players, but honestly I think that is better since it is closer to seeing the actual performance of classes without it being skewed by people who completely don’t know what they are doing.

Im just putting this at the top here due to misunderstandings. All data will be anonymized, this is just trying to see how each class stacks up against each other.

Additional Details

I will primarily be charting your damage and percentage of team total damage. Yes damage isn’t the only thing that matters obviously so I will also chart completion time in my best attempt at a metric to represent how easy a given class makes the game. Also because why not as a fun bonus thing I will chart stagger.

But yes primarily this is about damage, Id like to see how each class stacks up to each other on average across all (or most) builds and amongst experienced player in terms of the almighty big number go up. Perhaps if there are enough people interested I could run a proper study using set builds to get more granular with what build a class is using, but for now this is the best that I think is feasible.

I will take people’s submissions of their scoreboards and build a full chart of the relative performance of each class at Auric Maelstrom and Havoc 40 (if I get enough scoreboards to work off of anyways). I will also be adding my own stats from the current patch.

How to access your scoreboard history

It has come to my attention that many people dont actually know that the mod automatically saves your scoreboard history. So simple guide

1: Open Options
2: Click Mod Options
3: Click Scoreboard
4: On the right side of your screen, the first thing should be “Open scoreboard history” with the button beside it being the bind. It might be unbound, in that case just bind it to something

If you want to submit your scoreboards:

Do a full screen screenshot of the scoreboard mod scoreboard history page (if you open it from the character selection screen you get a nice black background).
