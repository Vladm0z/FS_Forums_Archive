The build that I think has been performing best from what I tried in Damnation+ has been no dog, far right line melee build using the XI Maul and Shield.

Very solid tanky / high dps / control build.

Arbiter von Mezmorki - Arbites Build for Darktide

Arbites build for Warhammer 40k: Darktide, using the Branx Mk III Arbites Shock Maul and Exaction Mk III Exterminator Shotgun. Created by Mezmorki.

I’m actually really enjoying having the dog around, even if not many points are invested in it. Being able to use to it stagger mutants and pox bursters, or pin down trappers is pretty awesome. Also, it can do a a decent job killing the random kraking enemies that spawn behind you, allowing you to shoot from a somewhat safer feeling position.

As far as skills go, I like the Terminus Warrant Keystone. It’s similar to Vet’s Weapon Specialist in that it encourages regularly swapping between ranged and melee, but it works in a more forgiving manner with the non-decaying stacks and lets you stack some nice buffs.

The build plays around with suppression synergies, which with Powderburn blessing on the new shotgun seems to throw out a lot of damage. Also plays with impact synergies, and the multiple stagger-damage stacks seems to work well.

The shock mine blitz and aquila ult give pretty amazing crowd control and lockdown potential.
