Vertraxxx • 2025-08-17 21:26:31

The reason Auric can become either extremely difficult or surprisingly easy is that there is a huge gap in the skill levels of participating players, and performance varies greatly depending on the builds and weapons they use.

For example, consider a group of four players who are qualified to attempt Auric. Of course, there will be some variation in skill, but these are players who at the very least can clear Damnation. If these four players use Gold Toughness, Ogryn, Arbites, or similar strong setups, the game essentially turns into a walking simulator, and unless an unlucky accident occurs, the run will succeed, probably with a win rate of over 90%.

On the other hand, if the same four players attempt a run without using any meta setups such as Ogryn, Arbites, DS, or Gold Toughness, the game becomes reasonably challenging, with a probable win rate of around 50%. This is how the difficulty is supposed to be.

But the reality now is that a large number of players below Heresy are gathering in Auric. You might not realize it, but Auric is the highest difficulty. Let me say it again: it is the highest difficulty.
