alwaysoveronepointow • 2025-07-20 00:29:20
SamSlade:

Only RoF weapons?

Generally yes, and that’s mostly due to the interaction with Empathic Evasion. Automatic guns with good uptime can use it to engage shooters and gunners without getting ripped to shreds, which is one of psyker’s main weaknesses. While he absolutely can get around it in other ways, those won’t have the same uptime nor versatility - Force Dome will often fail to provide the protection if you’re being pushed out of your position, Soul Drinker is kill-dependent and so on.

It’s not impossible to play gun psyker with low RoF weapons, but you’ll need to either be much more defensive or make heavy use of the Force Dome, which obviously limits your gameplay options.

So in general, yes the Recon Lasgun, Laspistol and Infantry Autogun are best options here (since Lasguns just lack the firepower against massed elites and Braced Autoguns don’t synergize with your talent tree in a meaningful manner).

One option that is good enough but rarely played is the Vigilant Autogun. It’s not as good as the high RoF options mentioned simply due to more limited uptime on Empathic Evasion and relies more on weakspot hits, but it synergizes strongly with Disrupt Destiny which lets it gain enough firepower to decimate massed elites. The damage on weakspot crits is so high that it can even deal with Crushers.

If you’re using SG you want something synergizing with Tranquility Through Slaughter to help maximize Gaze uptime, so to that end your best contenders are the fastest-shooting variants of the Recon Lasguns and the Infantry Autoguns.

The difference between the two is a matter of taste - Recon deals more total damage through ignite stacks at a lower rate than IAG which does direct damage, and I personally prefer IAG because killing things sooner is better IMO. Ultimately it’s your choice.

If you’re using shield (why? wut r u, casul?) you have more viable options, but the DPS on a fully cranked SG IAG is insane.
