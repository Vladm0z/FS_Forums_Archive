Psyker Staff Rebalancing

5 Posts • 265 Views

TwistedGuy • 2026-08-08 12:26:24

I think the psyker staves as a whole are obviously in a kind of bad place. Besides trauma staff (which I have used maybe once in 1600 hours of playtime because I already used it in vermintide 2 too much) the inferno staff, my baby the elektrokinetic staff, and the bowling ball staff all have clear problems.

The inferno staff has been discussed to death for being overkill for this game but I really think its main problem is the minimal peril gain combined with the ease of dispelling peril in this game compared to v2. Mix those in with no friendly fire to worry about and its no wonder it became the most brain dead weapon of all time. If they never want to add friendly fire in as a mechanic then I think peril gain on both firing modes need to supersede the elektrokinetic staff. And a small nitpick I’ve always had is I find the primary fire kinda janky for some reason. The animation for spamming it doesnt seem like something a human would naturally do with their hand and doing it is also about the most powerful/least costly/quickest attack in the entire game so that really should be looked at.

As for the ek, I mentioned in another post its my most used weapon in the game because I will always be a lightning wizard when given the opportunity, but it has been underpowered since its inception. The obvious buff it needs in my mind is to have its peril generation on secondary attack reduced, probably significantly if nothing else was changed. But more importantly a more interesting buff would be to add one more arc from the original target and each of those arcs can arc up to maybe once or twice more each so long as they keep criting on those bounces. Given how weak the bounces are anyway, it would mostly help with crowd control which every psyker staff seems to have in spades on top of good damage besides this one. The skitarii arc rifle is also already stronger than both of these ideas combined so I think it would be fair to let psyker have some lightning fun. Especially when as it stands we can somehow shoot far more lightning out of our hands than we can manage to summon straight out of the warp.

I’ll admit I havent used the bowling ball staff as much as the elemental staves but mainly because I think its so slow and boring. If it was just a little quicker to charge and maybe leaned into being a stagger powerhouse more by getting more shots off when enemies are not currently funneled in a hallway I think it’d have a better place.

Other than that I think each staff would also majorly benefit by having a unique primary fire that complimented/contrasted the secondary mode like how each of the staves in v2 were designed. All besides inferno defaulting to the 5 pound bowling ball has always been a bit disappointing especially for the bowling ball staff.
