I reached level 30 on my PermaDeath Zealot without ever dying and carried a HiSTG on the last game!

MedalTVWarhammer40000Darktide202506131349131920×1080 203 KB

It was a self-imposed challenge that I found pretty fun since low-level Darktide is such a different game compared to level 30.
My self-imposed rules were:

  • Any death results in deleting the character no matter what, even when backfilling into a losing game
  • No disconnecting during a mission
  • Weapon mastery for said character was 1-to-1 with character level, if character was level 10 I can craft a weapon with the limitations of level 10 mastery. In hindsight maybe I should’ve gone with 1-to-0.66 so I’d get max mastery at level 30 rather than level 20.

It was pretty fun playing low levels and working up to max level, I played on each difficulty besides Sedition and I’ve seen lots of players at varying skill levels. I’ve had to struggle and carry regular heresy missions a couple of times too, the most perilous game was one I had to clutch 3 separate times at level 13 since the other players kept dying and another kept losing connection. Most games became a lot more tense when dying meant actually losing that character! And elites are a lot scarier at low level too, Reapers in particular are absolutely terrifying at low level. I’ve lost 2 Zealots doing this self-imposed challenge and got to level 30 on my third.

I’ve also learned that it’s still possible to get a HiSTG mission on the regular mission, when’s the last time you’ve seen that? It’s effectively the same as an Auric HiSTG.
