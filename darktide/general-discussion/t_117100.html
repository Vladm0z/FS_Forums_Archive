My ideas are a bit more standard.

The Archetype: The Enginseer. The average Cog who keeps the machines of the Imperium running. The most humane of the personalities, yet a bit disconnected due to Cult Mechanicus dogma. I’m thinking of Felicia Tayber from the Ciaphas Cain books, or Faustinius from Mechanicus, or Abel and Opticon-22 from Rogue Trader.

The Caricature: The Lex Mechanic. They have undergone the Rite of Pure Thought and as a result are very literal minded and almost robotic. I see them being more a springboard for the rest of the classes to banter on.

The Cynic: The Reclaimator. Having dealt with the underworld of the lower Hive and the disparaging attitudes of higher status Magos has left them with a chip on their shoulder. Very sarcastic. Aye, lemme forward this thing from yore:

Mechanicus Theorycraftin'

Lest I forget the three personalities:

  • Nice: The Alchemist: Someone who’s always looking to try the next little addition, they eagerly trade away flesh and bone for the future. They encourage their teammates to try it out, often to disgusted decline, but never really lose their spirit.
  • Mean: The Purist: Absolutely despises the meatsacks for unjustly prosecuting them without having a word with the Mechanicus first. Virulent hatred for anything remotely resembling meat, and thus a purifying hatred of Nurgle. Only tolerates their teammates because they see them as tools to manipulate.
  • In-Between: The Miscrafted: Someone who wanted to be part of the Mechanicus but hadn’t the standing to be recognized, they took it upon themselves to do DIY cybernetics, with predictable results. Their malfunctioning technology often results in rapid mood swings, even multiple times in the middle of a sentence, but their combat prowess is not to be underestimated. Maybe they also have a Beloved? The Omnissiah?
