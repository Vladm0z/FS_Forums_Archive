The idea is every grenade node is split into two, you have the choice between two options that offer different advantages, I made this for fun, no balance was taken into fun, I don’t think this will be added ever.

Veteran:

Shredders counterpart: (Radiation Grenade) 2 Charges | Fuse 3.0 sec
Applies stacks 4 of radiation on impact, while also leaving a long lasting radiation cloud, that will apply 4 stacks on any enemy that passes, the cloud can only apply stacks once per enemy (8 stacks on enemies hit by the initial impact).
Radiation deals damage overtime, while also inflicting a slow that goes up with stack count.

Krak counterpart: (Melta Bomb) 1 Charge | Explodes on impact
Deals devastating damage in a small radius, while also applying up to 16 (based on distance) burn and brittleness stacks to enemies within a larger area.

Smoke counterpart: (Flash Grenade) 4 Charges | Fuse 3.5 sec can be cooked
Blinds enemies in medium sized radius, causing a small stagger to human size enemies, and forcing all ranged enemies to switch to melee for 15 seconds.

Zealot:

Stun counterpart: (Stasis Charge) 2 Charges | Fuse 3.0 sec
Completely freezes enemies and makes them damage immune within a small radius, you can also walk through them during this.

Immolation counterpart (Thermite Charge) 4 Charges | Fuse 2.0 sec
Sticks to enemies on impact, dealing damage per seconds, while also applying brittleness and burn stacks per second to them, also applies burn charges in a small radius around the main target.

Knives counterpart (Throwing Hatchet) 5 Charges | Impact | charges restored on ammo pickup
Hurl a heavy hatchet at an enemy, dealing heavy damage and stagger to them, high armor penetration, the arc is more curved than the the knives.

Ogryn

Rock counterpart (Smashed rock!) 4 Charges | 35 second recharge time | Only restore charges overtime
Crush a rock into tiny pieces and then throw it in a wide pattern, has high cleave, damage, and stagger at close range.

Nuke counterpart: (Tank Shell) 2 Charges | Explodes on impact
Throw a tank shell like a spear at an enemy, dealing extreme damage to them, and causing the shell to explode in a cone shape behind them, dealing heavy damage to all enemies behind them.

Box of hurt counterpart: (smuggled Rations) 1 Charge | 120 second recharge time | Only restore charges overtime
Hurl a box of rations at an enemy dealing heavy damage and stagger, that box then becomes a makeshift healing kit that lasts 30 seconds max, or after healing a certain amount of health.

Psyker

Brain burst counterpart (Mental link) 10 charges | 3 seconds charge restore time
Main fire: link elite enemies’ brains together, can link up to 5 elites enemies at once.
Secondary fire: spend charges equal to the amount of linked elite enemies to cause a damaging brain burst on all of them, also causes AOE damage around the elites.

Smite counterpart (Lightning Bolt) 6 charges | 15 seconds charge restore time
Main fire: fire a lighting strike at a single enemy, causing them to take damage and become electrocuted (stun and ticking damage), costs 1 charge.
Secondary fire: Fire a lighting strike that jumps between up to 10 enemies causing them to take damage and become electrocuted, costs 2 charges.

Assail counterpart (Wrap Spear) 3 Charges | 60 seconds charge restore time

Main fire: Stab with the spear for a combo of melee attack, costing no charges.
Secondary fire: Throw the spear in arc, dealing heavy damage to multiple targets as the spear passes through them, if the spear hits a weakspot, reduce the next charge restore time by 30 seconds. (This almost kerillian’s spear, I know)
