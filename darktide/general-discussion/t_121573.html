The Power Sword is strong, but “league of its own” is exaggerated because Darktide melee isn’t judged in a vacuum spreadsheet. It’s judged by survival, consistency, stamina economy, mobility, control, and how often a weapon actually lets you recover bad situations on Auric/Maelstrom.

Power Sword excels at burst cleave after activation. That’s its identity. But it also has downtime, activation management, mediocre mobility, weaker defensive flow than several alternatives, and a rhythm that can absolutely get you punished in high-pressure mixed hordes.

Meanwhile:
• Combat Axes still mulch elites with absurd consistency.
• “Chainswords and Chain Axes bring single-target deletion and stagger utility.”
• Shovels have ridiculous control/survivability value.
• Devil Claw enables defensive playstyles Power Sword users would fold under.
• Knife builds still turn Veterans into caffeinated sewer rats with infinite repositioning.

People keep treating “highest visible damage numbers” as synonymous with “best overall melee weapon,” which is how every balance discussion turns into Reddit discovering DPS calculators for the 900th time. Darktide is about uptime and survival under pressure, not just vaporizing three Crushers in a highlight clip.

If the Dueling Sword gets nerfed, that doesn’t magically make every other melee weapon irrelevant. It just means one outlier stopped overshadowing weapons that already perform perfectly fine in actual gameplay.
