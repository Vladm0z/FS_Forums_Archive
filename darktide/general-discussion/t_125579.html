Literally on my knees, praying to the Emperor himself, give this thing sound design on par with Vermintide 2’s Greathammer. The sound design for all blunt weapons in Darktide just sounds so WEAK when hitting flesh. A wet paper towel would sound more impactful.

Ogryn was the only exception. He stood out with his blunt weapons, as they all sounded strong and adequate to their size.

So I beg of you, FatShark, don’t screw the Hammer up with weak sound design. Make every hit MEATYAF when hitting flesh and absolutely CRUNCHY when hitting armor.

Edit: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kjWXIsB3LdY

The video gives a preview of the swinging sounds, and so far it looks and sounds great. Slow and heavy swinging from left to right strongly reminds me of Vermintide 2 – a very good sign. But I need to hear it in action. It’s about the meat (hitting unarmored Dregs) and bones (hitting armored Scabs) – how it’ll sound there will ultimately decide if the Hammer was properly blessed by the design team. Fingers crossed, everyone!

Hammer_hold • 2026-09-16 15:31:24

To me in terms of ranged weapons, combat Shotguns and Autoguns (Braced and not) sound the worst.

Shotguns’ shooting sounds too quiet for a Shootgun and the sound when moving the pump to end the reload sequence makes them sound exactly like nerf guns.

The same goes for the reload sound of Autoguns. The magazine reload and the sound of pulling the charging handle makes them sound like they’re toy guns (, though Braced Autoguns give me that feeling more).

Left 4 Dead made the guns sound super loud and when I played it for the first time (after Darktide) it actually added to the chaotic experience for when the horde came and you had to start unloading all you have to survive.

It really made me wish that Darktide did the same for all their guns. It would allow to make the experience feel more chaotic and rely less on things like balance-breaking enemy spam to achieve it.

Actually, this feeling extends to Dreg enemies who also use Autoguns. The weak sound design is in their shooting sounds. I think that sound design plays a major part in making enemies feel dangerous. So even a weak enemy with proper sound design could make you percieve them as a threat.

I imagine that every encounter with Dreg enemies should be super loud. It would feel more chaotic, which would fit the unorganized nature of the cult compared to more tactical Scabs.

Of course, with the current enemy spam, that would be an audio-breaking experience, I know. So right now I wouldn’t want that added without adjusting enemy numbers first.

I’m of the firm belief that in Tide games, less is more, so there should be less enemies on the screen at all times, but said enemies need to communicate that they’re deadly or tough to take down (so either buff their stats or give the more intense sound design/animation/voice acting/etc.)
