Too often the bad gets conflated with the good and people get stuck on what isn’t or could have been and lose sight of what is. I have my gripes but I’m going to try to keep my criticisms to a minimum and celebrate what is.

Darktide despite many of its issues is an epic love letter to the 40k community. The initial release was rocky but they managed to create addictive visceral combat and give you first person access to the universe, which is not an easy ambience to create. The voice acting is top karking notch. The feel and aesthetic of the weaponry is great. The sound effects and music teams havr blown it out of the water and have managed to create a distinctive world. The customer support team has been attentive and have worked hard to build confidence. Catfish worked hard to restore broken community trust and while change has been slow, in a lot of cases fatshark has listened to feedback and tried to improve. I think they did a phenomenal job on the arbites release and I can only hope a tech adept is in the cards for the future!

Kudos fatshark. This won’t stop me from calling out the bad, but you’ve been redeeming yourselves. Thank you for all of your hard work and effort!
