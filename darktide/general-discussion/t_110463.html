BizarreSalp • 2025-07-06 07:53:45

So, the Arbites Shock Maul has a really neat special. It does the light combo, but “charged”. If the enemy has been Shocked, this causes them to become frozen and take a very strong DoT effect. This is so strong that you can H1 a Rager in his head, then use the Special Light follow-up, and he will become frozen and die from the DoT after a few moments. You can even do this to MAULERS.

But

Why bother? The attacks on the weapon are already so strong that you can just bash away, never touch the special, and kill the enemy in 3 total hits. So what’s the point?

This weapon is one of the most egregiously overpowered the game has ever seen. It is stronger than even the Bully Club on Ogryn. That weapon can stagger and control and kill anything with lights, but this weapon controls/staggers and kills even quicker.

I don’t want to see it nerfed to the ground, but its base damage against certain armor types certainly needs to be toned down so that its own unique qualities can actually be relevant.
