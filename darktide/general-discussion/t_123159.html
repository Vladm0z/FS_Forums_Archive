I’ve honestly had no hope for something like this for the past few years until seeing the Skitarii’s release, which has reignited some hope enough for me to make a profile here and write this, but t.l.d.r.; Darktide would be so perfect for some kind of Psker/Witch hunting debuffer Pariah class and I’ve been so desperate to better get to play out that character style in this game.

For context, and a bit of yap, the Sisters of Silence are my absolute favorite faction in Warhammer, but my tabletop stuff tends to include various Blank forces (Culexus assassin(s), a pariah/Onmyodo Necromunda gang). I’ve mostly ran an anti-corruption Zealot up until the Arbite came out, largely built around the one node Beacon of Purity for my own narrative satisfaction. I’m not the best at Darktide, I’ve dabbled in Havoc but tend to play for the fantasy of the Warhammer setting, simply because an anti-corruption build how I’d hope is just too annoying to optimise for late game and beyond, so I don’t know much about balancing and why everything works how it is, but I have played all the base game classes to level 30 (except Ogryn) just to get a feel for the gameplay. So, something like this would likely be niche in its appeal, but I think mechanically could actually be really interesting for the game, as I don’t think there’s much interaction with Corruption outside of Zealot, and it would cool to see a class designed around specifically debuffing the enemy in ways other than brittle.

Some lore-points I was considering prior to this were:

  • As much as I would love it, it’s really unlikely this could be a true Sisters of Silence class - Darktide’s just kinda anti-genderlocking for some reason, so I’d imagine we’d get a true SoB before SoS, hence titling this as a Black Sentinel class, basically the militia of the Astra Telepathica.
  • Psykers and Blanks can exist in tandem! That wouldn’t be a lore plot-hole, in fact, there’s an entire Necromunda household, House Ty, who’s whole gimmick is using them together. They both wear special collars that can inhibit their psychic abilities, which as far as I know is similar to the ugly neck piece most of the Psyker’s cosmetics have in game, something to keep the Reject in check.
  • Blanks have unique/trainable abilities, which’d be a neat thing to explore in game - they’re not as versatile as all the different -mancies Psykers have, but pseudo-invisibility and aura-projection are among them.
  • The Arbites and Skitarii really opened up the game narrarively to more than just prisoners. The Arbite may still be a reject relative to other Arbites, but they stand above the other rejects in station enough to yap about in gameplay, and the Skitarii technically exist way outside whatever incarcerates the normal rejects.
  • The Null Rod would be a cool weapon in game. In lore, the Black Sentinels aren’t all blanks, in fact most use anti-Psyker tech similar to Kill Team’s Celestian Insidiants team, with anti-Psyker rounds, Null Rods, and other Null Field Generators.

With all this in mind, I think an Astra Telepathica (but not the Psyker) class would be super cool. Mechanically, it would be a cool way to explore things like multiple stacking aura effects in a single class like slows, accuracy, and a targeted effect to stun enemies like Mutants and Beasts of Nurgle, and Corruption negation. This doesn’t have much lore supporting it specifically for the Astra Telepathica to my knowledge, but the Clade Culexus Assassins use what’s called an Animus Speculum to kinda condense their null energy into a beam that can delete stuff from a distance (very fun in Chaos Gate:Daemonhunters). The Animus Speculum is a super controlled and rare device, however, the Sisters of Silence have a tiny version that’s almost a digital weapon in scale called a Huntress’ Eye, which could be small and simple enough to give the player character as an active ability to do a single-target nuke. And the Null Rod, which really, if any devs managed to read all this, I’d be content enough if the Zealot had access to. A big black bonk stick that debuffs an enemy. I don’t know a ton about the balance of the Skitarii, I’ve heard it’s broken and weak, but as someone who’s enjoyed their and the AdMech’s lore a good bit, all the options and design of it really made the class show a passion for the setting that makes me desperate to see what other aspects of the universe y’all could explore. Blanks, Ratlings, Gun-Servitors, Rejuvenats, any new way to play the world of Warhammer is awesome.
