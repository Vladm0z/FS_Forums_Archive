Rvl • 2026-07-07 17:29:48

Don’t run pure ranged build, Hive Scum is the only character who can do full ranged build in Havoc without sacrificing his survivability in some way. Both Vet and Skitarii require a proper frontline on the team and still need to hide from shooter packs while picking them off, if Reapers catch you in open space they will hit you through the dodges even if you dodge spam.

The auto-aim ultimate is not that good on Skitarii currently aside from lightning gun, it has a noticable number of ghost hits on revolver/garand and it barely buffs the damage against inflated Havoc healthpools, get the Emitter ultimate with toughness regen node and lightning arcs, it will increase your survivavibility against elite packs which push you and will give you stuns on hordes so you can shoot freely.

It also gives higher damage in moment to moment gameplay, BUT only on electrified enemies.

Ryken has a video about this Combat Doctrine-less ranged build if you want some actual gameplay reference, I tested a variant of it in Havoc 40 and the ranged Skitarii legit feels better to play with Emitter, although I have swapped some talents around and I take no blitz on ranged build.
