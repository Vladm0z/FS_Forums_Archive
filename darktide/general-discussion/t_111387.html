I dipped my toe in Heresy, as I’d been away from the game for a few weeks besides the odd mission here and there, and found how nasty it was immediately with the packs of Crushers suddenly appearing on the battlefield, not like they’re there by magic, more a how in the name of the God-Emperor did I not see them?!

When you’re trying to comprehend everything that is happening, with all the quick animations from multiple enemies, the cacophony of noise my brain is sorting through, keeping an eye on the team members and so on, whilst there are explosions of green across the screen, it becomes really hard to understand what I need to do. It is like the brain is swimming through thick slurry and the sudden appearance of brown-green Crushers in a horde cause just a little pressure and a spot of panic.
