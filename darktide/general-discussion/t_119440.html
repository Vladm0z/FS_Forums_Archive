What are peoples thoughts about pushing enemies ?

I know it can be useful in certain situations, it staggers a bit, can cancel enemies attacks.

I do use it, sometimes, but when I do it doesnt feel like it perform as well as just hitting stuff with weapons that already have good enough cleaves.

And yet there’s situations where you ended up getting swarmed by so many enemies at once, and even specialists or armored one, that cleaves doesnt become that helpful and get stopped by the armored enemies, and pushing just doesnt give you time to breathe.

In a way I wish pushing would at least actually really push away poxwalkers and just any basic enemies like that, so you can still have a potential way of getting out of those situation where you will just end up dead because you absolutely can’t move or cleave enough to clear around you, especially if you’re not using an action skill that can push away enough for you to escape, like Zealot’s stealth or dash, Psyker scrying gaze etc.

I know such situations can be rare, or just bad movements to begin with by getting stuck in a corner or something, but I want to think that for any person that often find themselves as last man standing like I do, focusing on dodging, keeping an eyes on the enemies and trying to figure out where you are in the map with all the clutters in the way, they sure have came up in that situation where they get stopped in their dodge by a random barrel and by the time it took them to vault over it or go around, they’d already be surrounded by the swarm.

So to resume everything, I wish pushing would be a bit stronger at least against poxwalkers like how L4D pushing just really push away zombies enough for you to move.

In general pushing lost its value since Vermintide 2, as you can usually manage enemies with pure aggression and mobility. In DT pushing is also noticeably easier than in VT2, where you only push enemies in a certain angle in the front of you, which varies depending on your weapon & perks. In DT you can push away enemies without actually facing them. Whatever that is good or bad, that’s probably up for a debate.

There are few playstyles and weapons which make pushing / push attacks worth it. Slab shield, Crusher and Mk XII chain axe come to mind straight away. I also love launching pox bursters into enemies with force pushes, and enemies in general off map edges
