For those who don’t know: Once you reach Havoc Assignment Rank 40, failing missions will not cause you to lose your rank. And now all you have to do is enter Havoc 40 mission once a week, even if you get wiped out by random hordes just a minute later, you can still keep your Assignment Rank for next week.

This has given rise to countless issues, many Havoc 40 players don’t take a match “seriously,” as failure comes at no cost to them.
What’s more, players have less incentive to play Havoc, since there is no risk of losing their rank, there is no way to keep them engaged in taking on challenges.

The lack of a drive to victory is definitely a major design flaw.
If Fatshark has even the slightest care for this mode, the minimum requirement should be changed to: You must successfully complete Havoc 40 once a week to retain your Assignment Rank.
