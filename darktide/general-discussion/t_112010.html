Veteran ammo problem solution: side arms

20 Posts • 672 Views
T
Torimori • 2025-08-24 20:19:00

So, the other thread on issues with Veteran’s ammo economy and how people are not satisfied with current situation (ammo shortages at higher difficulties, over-reliance on las weapons limiting your options etc) made me think: what if the solution could be with vet being allowed to carry TWO range weapons instead?

  1. You still can choose what to put in your Primary range weapon slot freely
  2. There is also a Secondary range weapon slot now you can put only pistol-sized weapons into; they have their max ammo capacity halved when carried like that. So it’s a side-arm effectively
  3. When you press “2” button during a mission, it selects your Primary range weapon, same as Q button switches from Melee weapon to your Primary range one. If you press those buttons two times rapidly, you are instead switched into your Secondary range weapon. If you hold “2” button for 1.5+ secs, it will set your current range weapon as default one (so if you have run out of ammo in your Primary, you set Secondary as default for all the switching).
  4. When picking up ammo during the game, it will replenish your Primary range weapon’s ammo exactly as it happens now - but the Secondary range only will get 1/2 of its normal amount replenished.

So, not only we give veteran some extra ammo to use on those missions, but also we make it a truly unique class with its own special mechanics.
