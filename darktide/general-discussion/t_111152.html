They track well if consistent speed and consistent angle. i.e. Running at straight line 30 degree towards them, they still track just as well.

Shift + W + A or Shift + W + D, rotate adjust facing towards gunners as you do this.

It causes you to sprint in semi circle fashion towards gunners and I noticed harder for them to track this non straight line movement.
