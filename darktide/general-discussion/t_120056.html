Parrying bulwarks is so annoying when they refuse to attack and just bash you with shield instead or just stand there with shield up not doing anything. I like using it against bosses and Twins killing themselves by hitting parry all the time but to be honest it’s all “looks”, the actual damage is on the lower (lowest) side.

I would never put Hammerblow on it, you either put two damage blessings or Wrath + Skullcrusher, Rampage + Skullcrusher and maybe Shred with something else.

Like, if you want crowd control just take crowd control weapon like Shock Maul.

I think on Psyker Devil’s Claw might seem good because of the autobrainburst. You hit rager or mutant and it poofs away because of that brainburst proccing, Devil’s Claw even in Damnation doesn’t always kill Ragers in one parry with Vet or Zealot.

For this weapons to be truly good we’d need to have mission type with no armored enemies at all, like Dregs Only, or “more hordes and specials, almost no armored enemies”.

I personally see it as “horde clear only” weapon or “a tool to reposition”. It’s safe against ragers and you can spam parry waiting for your dodges to reset (which is good). But in terms of pure DPS it’s very lackluster.
