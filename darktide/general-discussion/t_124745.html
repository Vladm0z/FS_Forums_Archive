There are two ways one can go about the enemies we face in Darktide:

  1. They’re just lunatics. A ton of crackheads who have killed a ton of people; no personality to be found here.
  2. Every enemy we face has more depth than we think, and it can be seen in their voicelines, their behavior, and design.

I choose the latter, and I encourage you to do the same, even as a little exercise, because it can get really fun.

Take the Scab Shotgunner, for instance.

At first, they seem just like another specialized soldier in the regiment; the Shotgunner one. But with how they are always positioned in the front of small squads and patrols roaming around the map, we can deduce that they fill the role of leaders, commanding the troops on the battlefield.

To add to them being commanders, from the sheer aggressiveness and delivery of their voicelines, we can assume that they are very hotheaded, constantly on edge (similarly to the Cutthroat Veteran Personality) and possibly prone to violent outbursts. Pretty fitting for shell-shocked veterans from a death world now pledged to a God of Chaos and Death. Knowing how, as a heretic, you can be murdered not just by loyalists but also by your fellow Chaos followers, it’d make sense why you would obey someone like that. You wouldn’t want a shovel in your head, would you?

That’s all and dandy; the heretics are very aggressive, so what? Well, the thing is I don’t think the heretics only follow Scab Shotgunners’ lead out of fear only. I think they also obey them out of sheer respect for them. Because even in such a degenerate state, these heretics still find humanity and care for their fellow soldiers, not just charge at any loyalists they see, but instead warn their troops to “Look out!” because enemies are running in their direction*.*

Scab Shotgunners strike me as someone who will beat your ass if you aren’t listening to them but will also literally die just to save your life, and it checks out. After all, the old saying goes all the same: You watch my back, I watch yours.

To add to the respect factor, Scab Shotgunners will switch to melee when you have your melee out while close to them. They have a clear sense of honour, even if they hate our guts for being loyalist scum. We want to fight them in melee? So be it. Even if they have us at point-blank range, they will still put away their shotgun in favor of flaying our skin with their shovel; Eye to eye. Say what you want, but that takes some next-level guts and honor.

They also scream out, “You’re not even real soldiers!” which makes sense to me. After the nightmare that was the Death World, it’d make sense for them to believe themselves to be the true soldiers and defenders of humanity. One’s who have literally gone to hell and back. Anyone else who didn’t go through what they went through doesn’t deserve the title of a “soldier”, especially those who still serve the Emperor after hearing the truth about the Imperium’s lies and cruelty.

Last but not least, the skulls.

Every Scab Shotgunner has skulls adorning their armor. Just a sick tribal type of trophy? Maybe, but I see a different possible reason why they are with them.

Back on Nox Alpha, the Moebian Sixth have suffered greatly, their troops being forced to fight and die in the mud forgotten by the Imperium, who couldn’t be assed to support them in any way. I bet many soldiers wanted to just go back home to Tertium, away from this hell, but never got the chance because death claimed them too early.

But what if there was still a way to fulfill their wishes? Once turned traitor, the Moebian Sixth have made their way back to Atoma Prime to spread the truth they have learned in the darkness of this Death World, and among them are their old friends who died on Nox Alpha. Their brothers in arms are the skulls. They got their wish. They’re finally home. And they will accompany their enlightened friends as they kick down every door and butcher every loyalist who won’t accept their truth.

A sick and twisted way of remembering the friends you lost to the brutal war you all were thrown into. I guess it’s better than abandoning their bodies in the mud like the Imperium did the entire regiment.

They were together in the hell of a Death World, and now they will be together until death takes them.

.

That’s my interpretation of the Scab Shotgunner’s personality/character. I’m curious as to what you think and also what other ideas you have for the rest of the heretics. Do you think they have depth as I do, or do you think they’re just some fodder with no personality other than “Destroy the Imperium, raaaaaggghh!”? Let me know because I am curious about your possible takes on the rest of Darktide’s enemies.
