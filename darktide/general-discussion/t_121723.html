And that’s an issue because that’s not what Darktide was supposed to be. You were never supposed to be able to just walk into a horde and mindlessly spam light attacks with a shove every few seconds.

Vermintide 2 shows really well how easily you can die from a horde if you don’t play carefully and deliberately engage with both push and dodge mechanics. Darktide’s combat system is just pure onga bonga compared to that. Why would you even use a shove if you can spam dodges and then spam slides as well. Why time any of these actions if you can just infinitely spam them without consequences that are felt by the player? Why bother to think what weapon to choose if some stuff just one-shots enemies?

That’s something that Darktide’s devs need to approach and it can go two ways:

  1. They will make changes that will anger the current playerbase, but it won’t make them revert it, changing Darktide into a game that it was intended to be before the balance broke after patch 13.

  2. They will make changes that alternate between pleasing and inconveniencing the current playerbase leading to a hybrid between what the players want it to be and what the devs want it to be.

Personally while I wish for the former, I think the latter is the one that’ll end up happening.
