Spagett • 2025-06-30 14:53:07

I agree that the Arbites talent tree is over-tuned. They seem to be able to do many things quite well - they may not be able to do any one thing as well as one of the base classes, but still too well imo. Tanking, melee dps, ranged dps, crowd control, special killing, etc. They just don’t feel like they have a palpable weakness in the way that the other classes do. My zealot, for example, feels like he is noticeably not that good at ranged dps and countering shooters/gunners. And there’s a lot of those in this game, so that can be a significant weakness.

I think the dog is over-tuned as well. He is so effective that I actually picked the no-dog talent just because I felt like the game was playing itself (in my favor) with the dog active. I think it should require a significant point investment for the dog to be as effective as he is by default.

Regarding the Arbites weapons, I think that the only one that feels truly overpowered is the arbites shock maul (without shield) - it does a ton of damage, has good horde clear and single target, can kill crushers VERY quickly, has stun, fast swing speed. No real weakness. As a zealot main, I can’t help but compare it to the thunder hammer, which has very noticeable weaknesses to balance its strengths. The arbites shock maul can kill a crusher in almost the same time, with none of the risk of the thunder hammer.
