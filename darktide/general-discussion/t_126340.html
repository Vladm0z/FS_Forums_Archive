Dear SE7ENFALL,

The only issues I have is trappers being too quiet, trapper nets going through entire enemy hordes and walls sometimes, and generally the game’s soundscape sometimes having problems processing specials… plus, you know, the sudden trapper hordes on auric and auric malestrom (I don’t even want to get into havoc).

Beyond that… nah, trappers are fine. I might hate them sometimes because of the above reasons, but like… they are designed around the game just fine outside of those minor issues.

The main problem I see here with your post is that you want trapper nets to be easier so you can solo more and not have to focus with your team. Aka, you don’t want to play the game the intended way. You want to focus on playing by yourself rather than working with your team, which is already breaking a lot of aspects about the game which it just wasn’t designed for.

Now, we can get into semantics on how Darktide isn’t Helldivers 2 with how the game is designed around the player vs designed around the enemies, but with Darktide… yeah even with the issues it has, it’s extremely balanced. Specials are supposed to not only get you to work with your team more, aka stick together and watch each other’s backs, but also get you to better work with the game’s mechanics. A lot of elements about the trapper force you to improve your skills as a player.

You know, there is a way to fight against trappers and express skill, without needing your team to help you… and there is one obvious solution (you know, other than STICKING WITH YOUR TEAM):

Get this… the best solution to trappers, team or not, is… dodging.

That’s right. You can dodge trapper nets, even as the slow Ogryn. They are easy to dodge too, if you see or hear it coming. And most of the time, trappers will make it obvious they are about to trap you. They talk out loud, they make a small sound before they shoot out, and the distance of the trap can easily be dodged if you keep an ear out. Not only that, but their glowing bits and their silhouette is easy to tell apart from most other enemies. You can easily dodge a trapper’s net if you pay attention.

Again, the only issue I see with trapper is a general issue I see with most other specials, plus trapper nets going through enemies easily. With those fixed, trapper wouldn’t be so hard. But, regardless, work with your team and get better at the game. It isn’t that hard.

Regards,

A guy who played 340 hours of the game and knows what he is talking about.

P. S.

Remember, this is a team game, not a solo splatfest. You aren’t supposed to go alone (and even if you do, remember you are putting yourself more at risk of enemies rushing you down because you are alone.)
