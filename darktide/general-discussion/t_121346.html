SirFailsalot • 2026-05-07 19:53:34

As the title says, I think we have too many different modes that don’t share the same queue.

Standard mission board: 5 difficulties + 2x Maelstroms (Heresy and Auric)

  • Quickplay only queues for each difficulty (as it should). Maelstrom must be queued seperately. 7 queues, or 1 to 2 queues per difficulty (5 quickplay)

Mortis Trials: 5 difficulties

  • 5 quickplay queues

Expeditions: 4 difficulties.

  • 4 quickplay queues

Havoc: 40 difficulties. No quickplay. Special case (uber leet hard mode for the sweaty gamers who want to push things to the limit)

If we ignore Havoc, we have 14 different quickplay queues, or approximately 3 different quickplay queues per difficulty.

Why not add a quickplay that queues for all of the non-Havoc modes together? Choose your difficulty and end up in a regular mission, a Mortis Trial, or an expedition?

Perhaps an additional queue for people who want to aid newer players that allows you to queue for all difficulties and all modes at the same time?
