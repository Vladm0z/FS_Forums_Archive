Good defense:

Enemies Within, Enemies Without is very powerful as it’s a constant flow of toughness with the game’s easiest proc condition.

Retaliatory Defense let’s you block and push a lot more. Handy for clutch res too.

Restoring Faith can help mitigating some chip damage.

Taking Bacon of Light in Havoc means overheads no longer leave you at 99% corruption. Combined with Restoring Faith means you get at least half a wound to one wound worth of HP back.

Offense:

You don’t need Punishment. You don’t need more CC, the anti-interrupt is too short to be taken advantage of, and TWbS already covers anti-stun.

Desperation is pretty good if you notice you constantly sit at low Stam.

Unseen Blade is really only good for boss killing. You do enough damage in Aurics to constantly have high threat so you aggro things.

Here is a build you might like that focuses on Attack speed, Movement speed and Toughness : Relentless - Zealot Build for Darktide - Darktide WH40k

Obviously make changes as you see fit, but Relentless Fervor is amazing for speed, I take this instead of Thy Wrath be Swift. Note that I take mobility stat on the Relic Blade. Stacked with the 5% movement node you will usually be the fastest on the team. If you decide to put stamina on your curios you should take separate perks for more value overall. For me at the moment 15% Sprint Cost Reduction is enough.

Also it is subtle but personally I find Unfaltering to be game changing for Relic Blade’s mobility.

With this build I’m able to easily sit at 1hp pretty much all the time, while zooming around to wherever I need or want to be. Health curios and nodes are not necessary, as long as you are able to dodge of course.
