Combat Shotguns and the Double Barreled Shotgun in DT are really strange weapons. Due to minimum pellet counts, they can be surprisingly okay at taking out soft targets at long range, like snipers. But at the same time they feel underwhelming at close range. One can make them deal a lot of damage by using Manstopper+Bleed and firing into hordes, but I feel like their design is a solution missing a problem. There are so many other ranged options that fulfill their same roles, but better, at nearly all ranges.
