Now I cant remember where I heard this but I believe it is confirmed we are getting another class. The natural question is what will be the next class. Now I have seen a lot of speculation but after seeing the hive scum + arbitrator bundle it does make me think. These two are opposite ends of the setting, law vs outlaw, tank vs glass cannon, big weapons vs fast weapons etc. What if that will be the case with the next class.

Origionally I was thinking deathcult assassin is sort of the obvious shoe in for next class. Other then that the other options seem unlikely, I know I have seen people mention ratlings but (again cant remember where I heard this) I believe one of the devs said they aren’t doing that because snipers and very short characters both would cause issues with the game. However there is one possible class that perfectly fits as the exact polar opposite to skitarii and would make sense to exist on tertium, perhaps even also found due to expeditions.

Beastmen. I initially dismissed this as I am not sure if GW would like that, they haven’t really included loyalist beast men anywhere in a long time, but with them again giving some more attention to the guard it may be possible that they are looking that way (Beastman penal regiment would be very cool for tabletop if you ask me).

Beastmen are the exact perfect opposite to skitarii in any way. One is refined machinery the other is mutated flesh, one is using highly advanced weaponry the other is using scraps, one is emotionally reserved and chaste the other is loud and brash. It is such a perfect contrast in every way that if they don’t do this I will actually be pretty disappointed.

But that is just a pretty random prediction, I do wonder if anyone else has any compelling cases for what the next class will be.
