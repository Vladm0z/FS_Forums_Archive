Hello everyone! I’d like to share and discuss my concepts for new specialist enemies that could enhance the gameplay experience.

  1. The Laugher (Heretic Fanatic Assassin)

This specialist is a professional assassin and a devout heretic fanatic.

Arrival Signature: The unsettling jingle of tiny bells on his tattered clown hat signals his approach, followed by a burst of hysterical laughter right before he strikes.

Ability: The Vanish (Ичезновение): He instantly turns invisible and leaves behind a gas mine at his starting location, forcing players to quickly relocate.

Combat Style: Highly agile, capable of quick dashes and evasive maneuvers. He attacks not only with a close-range dagger but also with a chain/flail for mid-range damage

2. The Bloat Croaker (Mutated Specialist)

A creature so hideously mutated that it barely resembles a human anymore.

Attack Mechanism: From a distance, the Croaker can grab a player with its long, powerful tongue. It slowly reels the player in, rendering them temporarily defenseless.

Counterplay: To save the grabbed player, the squad must quickly sever the tongue (by focusing fire on it). Alternatively, a single, precise headshot will cause the Croaker to recoil and retreat immediately.
