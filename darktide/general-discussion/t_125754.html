The new changes are exciting. I hope the chastise the wicked bug(or feature?) with not playing the voiceline/warcry gets fixed so we can live our dreams of shouting the emperors name while charging at the tide repeatedly. What is sad to see though is the lack of changes to the Blitz. And a couple node changes could be better.

Starting with the nodes,

The new Risen keystone seems kind of pointless, +40 toughness is great, but for 5 seconds is kind of whatever. A little buff to make it more attractive could be “Can not be disabled upon triggering Until Death”. So now when you get trapped or jumped by a hound and trigger until death you can get yourself up instead of just dying 8 seconds later. Similarly you can gain temporary immunity to mutant hordes and boss grabs like beast or spawn. turns the keystone from meh to holy emperor this can save a run.

I personally feel Unfaltering should get combined into Thy wrath be swift. These 2 nodes do about the same thing, provide mobility and stun immunity. Given there are more nodes to grab, would be a bit nice to declutter and be rid of essentially a redundant node.

Similarly Good Balance could be combined into Dualist to potentially free up an extra talent point to invest in the new nodes, although this one could be a bit much.

Now onto the Blitz,

The Stunstorm Grenade is impeccable, saved me many runs and helped many revives. It’s the best Blitz Zealot has.

My only complaint with it are the visuals, wish they lingered as long as the actual stun, and spread as far as the actual radius, would make it easier to tell if enemies are stunned or not.

The Immolation Grenade in contrast, is plainly crap. it’s a terrible area denial tool that barely kills chaff. Worst of all doesn’t even apply burn stacks. So some ideas to improve the flame nade-

  • The radius should match the Stunstorm Grenade.
  • The initial blast should apply max burn stacks
  • Each tick of damage while in the flames should add a stack of burn
  • Should do more damage to armoured enemies not less, maybe adds increasing stacks of brittleness
  • Burning enemies should be suppressed, not just walking through fire as if nothing’s wrong
  • Add Blue gradient to flames to help differentiate from enemy flames

A large AOE armour reduction, even a slow one would be much better than what we currently have.

The Blades of Faith are slightly underpowered in my opinion. They could use more cleave, it’s quite hard to use them in crowded spaces. The blades should also add a few bleed stacks and more stacks on headshots.

Quite annoying trying to hit a trapper while there is a horde in your face and then when you land a hit she lives with like 10 hp. Bleed would also help handling flak armoured enemies like ragers and plasma gunners from a distance.

Lastly, 6 ammo tins to fill up on knives is too much, each ammo tin should give 3 knives instead of 2.
