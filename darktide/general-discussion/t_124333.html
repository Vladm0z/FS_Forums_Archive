Agayek • 2026-07-23 08:45:15

First, I would add:

  • Dreg Maulers, infested armor type big burly elites with large swords that are squishier than scab maulers but much more aggressive with longer reach
  • Ogryn Ragers, Unyielding ogryn that dual wield crude hand axes and look like a Packmaster minus the armor. They would be extremely aggressive with high stagger resistance and spawn in packs of 3-4 at a time. They deal bonus stamina damage to blocks, but also have generous attack timings that are relatively easy to dodge, even with negative dodges.
  • Scab Commander, flak armored elite using the Radio Operator model that would spawn relatively rarely and never more than one in any given pack. He has a gun that works similarly to the plasma gunners, in terms of charge up and shooting, and does very little damage. But if his target doesn’t avoid the shot, they get 1 stack of the Houndmaster’s debuff that hinders stamina and dodge regen, and at the same time it taunts every enemy in the general vicinity to fixate on the player who got shot, forcing the team to react to protect them and/or the shot player to have to play like a god for the next ~10s or so.

After that, I wouldn’t really change anything else about any individual enemy. I think they’re all in about the right spot.

But then I’d go into the spawn system and get to work. Main thing I’d do is go in and dramatically lower the pace and volume of spawns, to bring the amount of stuff going on at any given time significantly lower, with fewer enemies spawning and some amount of additional minimum time between the various varieties of spawns. Bring everything down to a level where the engine is teetering on the brink of collapse and things, especially audio cues, function as they are supposed to reliably.

And then I’d go into the weapons and talents available to players with a hacksaw and just have fun. I would nerf most sources of player power significantly. Biggest thing would be to delete infinite cleave from everything that isn’t a non-regenerative blitz, spend a year rebalancing abilities and talent synergies on several levels, and just generally toning down player power to match the lowered tempo of the game.

Then I’d playtest the hell out of it and probably spend some time iterating and refining enemy behaviors and stats to find new and interesting ways to challenge players that don’t lean on ramping density and spawn volume.
