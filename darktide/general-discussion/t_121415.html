Shield Ogryn is getting out of hand

20 Posts • 1115 Views
A
ApollOgryn-Creed • 2026-05-09 17:48:33

I know that, for many of you, damage is king and in the forefront of what you consider to be priority when discussing balance. However, there is a growing problem with how absurdly strong Shield Ogryn with Loyal Protector has become. This class is about to be on the receiving end of many indirect buffs in this next patch and Shieldgryn was already, arguably, the easiest class to win the game with on Havoc 40.

Less Crushers = Shield Ogryn buff. One of the only things slowing this big guy down was walls of armor and the threat of taking an overhead. Any other melee unit simply gets pushed to the ground and instantly invalidated.

Uncanny nerf = Shield Ogryn buff. His massive push radius and applying brittleness in a cone to groups of armored enemies will become more valuable than ever.

Flame staff buff = Shield Ogryn buff. Way too many players are completely dependent on the flame staff to succeed in havoc. Shield Ogryn puts a set of training wheels on your crutch so you can be completely oblivious and still be safe in his mighty, taunting arms.

There once was a time where Shieldgryn required skill and finesse. It costed a tax node to get bracing toughness regen. The taunt only had the initial pulse and provided no toughness regen. You had to be very certain when diving gunners as there was no panic button other than a micro stun. You had to center yourself in groups of enemies to get maximum taunt value rather than just popping it and turning your brain off while it pulses and grabs the attention of every melee enemy in a 100m radius.

I love Shield Ogryn, I’ve been playing it since before Auric Maelstroms existed. Lately, I’ve seen more and more Shield Ogryns in the party finder and it’s only a matter of time before it becomes considered as the same tier of broken as flame staff. Fatshark, please slow down the big man. Bring honor back to the only real tank class in the game!
