Force Greatswords added an interesting mechanic to the game, by requiring players to charge up a meter with kills for a stronger AOE attack. The same charge up the power meter concept would be interesting applied to Electrokinetic and Voidblast staves, and Blaze Bolt would be removed from both of these staves completely.

Voidblast

Staff Primary - Would be Secondary as is currently. This would focus Voidblast on being a CC staff and require Psykers to use BB or Assail for longer ranged attacks or adjust attack strategy for the weapon, same as other classes must do when using DB Shotgun or Flamer. Blaze Bolt not only doesn’t fit the theme of Voidblast, but is also far too strong (Surge), suppressive and spammable.

Staff Secondary (Singularity) - the opposite of Voidblast Secondary. When fully charged by primary kills Singularity becomes available. After a short charge, a tear in the fabric of reality opens, then enemies within the radius of Voidblast are violently pulled into the singularity for 5s. Enemies take 1 tic of (TBD) amount of damage per second spent in the singularity. Each tic of singularity damage would count towards Rending Shockwave. This offers an ability to condense enemy groups into a tight area, opening the door for high damage teamwork combos like Ripper/Rumbler/Flamer/Barrel (drop singularity on top of Barrel) into the tightly packed singularity.

Electrokinetic

Staff Primary (Voltaic Bolt) - After a brief charge period (same as now) a lightning bolt erupts from the left hand of Psyker, targeting one enemy Weakspot for significant Electrocution damage over 3s. Targeting visual could be the similar to Brain Burst, but with electrical flair. Flurry would enhance the speed of this charge period and would effectively turn Electrokinetic into a Specialist Popper. Voltaic Bolt wouldn’t have the same range as Brain Burst, but would keep the current range of Secondary attack.

This attack would NOT chain, enemies hit would get stunned on initial hit, then just a reduction in attack speed on succesive hits for a period of time until the enemy’s CD for stun finishes. The removal of suppression from Blaze Bolt, additional charge time for attack (compared to Blaze Bolt), removal of chain lightning and the reduction in stun against enemies would balance out the additional single target damage given.

Fully charged, this attack would deal 1700 Weakspot Damage - enough to deal with a Gunner/Spec quickly, but not enough for Mauler without a Crit + Carapace.

Staff Bound Smite (SBS) - When fully charged by primary kills Secondary becomes available. After a short charge up, a powerful bolt of lightning erupts from the head of Electrokinetic staff, Electrocuting and stunning up to 3 Ogryn level enemies (intial target + 2 chains) struck by the intial bolt or chain lightning for 8s.

This bolt would deal 7000 damage on intial impact, 1000 on Chain lightning. Chain would debuff up to two nearby Ogryn level enemies with High Voltage equipped or 4 with Conduction Field. SBS would not stun a boss but would give it the Electrocution status debuff for 8s.

This could open the door for an Electrokinetic “support build” focused around isolating HVTs and debuffing with High Voltage. SBS could be preceeded by 5x Bolt (w/ Flurry) for an Empyric Shock, High Voltage anti-boss combo.

New Blessing: High Voltage - +25% Damage taken by Electrocuted enemies.

New Blessing: Conduction Field - Your primary attack now chains twice, but takes twice as long to charge. Your Staff Bound Smite also chains two additional times for a total of 4 enemies affected.
