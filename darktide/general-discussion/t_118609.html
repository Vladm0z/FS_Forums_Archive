Molonious • 2026-02-12 18:04:05

Alas, much like the Krieg set, this isn’t what the actual Cadian art or tabletop models look like.

The set is a weird mash-up of both Kasrkin and basic Shock Trooper stylings from different eras, with a healthy helping of “creativity” on top. This is basically looks like something ChatGPT would hallucinate, with elements from the actual models and art, but distinctly and unmistakably…different.

The cosmetic has Shock Trooper chest armor, but no Cadian sets have ever had an armored collar/gorget.

It has Karskin style vambraces (but bulkier/more trim, and without their armored gloves) and Kasrkin style hip tassets and groin protection.

The shoulder pads look like a strange halfway between the current Shock Trooper and Kasrkin versions, but still tylistically different (particularly with the trim/rolled edge flare) from both the 2003 (which are really big and bulky) and 2022 iterations of each.

Leg armor being mismatched on the legs doesn’t appear on any Cadian models in any edition, while the kneepads and leg armor don’t actually look like the Shock Trooper or Kasrkin equivalents, it’s too curved for Kasrkin (which have flatter/angular panels, a different cosmetic has those legs), and the armor is much larger/more extensive than the small kneepads the newer Shock Troopers get (older ones didn’t have any leg protection).

The helmet is distinctly the old 3rd edition style Forgeworld-kit Hostile Environment type, not the current version helmet/mask.

Between this and the Krieg sets, it’s why I have a hard time believing GW interferes all that much in what FS puts out, if they’re cool with Chinesium like this, then it’s hard to believe that they’re particularly concerned with most of the stuff that gets chalked up to their meddling.
