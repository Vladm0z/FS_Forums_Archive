Ralendil • 2026-08-07 21:14:31

Playing zealot and Scums

I disagree this is the same.

For me zealot is fast, the melee frontliner that always have a card “get out of jail free card”
Melee is the best thing he will do… ranged is a support. (sidenote: I begin to love the assault shotgun… just because this is a close combat weapon)

The Scum has stimms (usually) that can transform him. That’s a sort of second ability. If you play it melee, then you have rampage, then maybe the keystone if you have picked adrenaline frenzy.
If you have picked chemical, basically this part is really interesting for the critical chances bonus. This is also the part that is clearly linked to toxin.
I skip ranged scum, not relevant to compare with zealot.

As a result, Scums is about dealing a max of damages in a min of time. Zealot is about consistently control the mass of enemies (I refuse to say horde control, we don’t have horde since the port to consoles). That’s a different role.
However, zealot is the one that, how I use it, has the fastest gameplay between both. I play it aggressively, using a lot Fury of the Faithful (FoF). FoF is increasing your attack speed (melee and ranged), this is also a way to move really fast. Also, zealot has several movement bonuses (node +5% movement, Retentless favor, Thry Wrath be Swift, Providence (less interesting - Why Fatshark moved this from veteran to zealot???), Unfaltering (interesting for heavy melee weapons)).
You can also use it as assassin, dealing a max of damages in one hit. I don’t like this gameplay.
Perso, I use a lot martyrdom. I don’t feel that the 2 others keystones are good, at contrary. I use fury only with weapons that have low critical chances, or not enough, and need it because they shine only with critical. Or I can use it with weapons that shines better with it. But the left part is about being untouchable with lot of DR.
However, with martyrdom, you can achieve that with a lot of wepaons, and get also a big attack speed bonus.
The right part… I don’t see how it can be interesting.
