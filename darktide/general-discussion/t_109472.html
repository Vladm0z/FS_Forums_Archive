I’ve been contemplating how to introduce the Tyranid ‘Genestealer Cult’ faction into the game. While every hive city harbors Genestealers, they cannot be added like the Beastmen in ‘Vermintide’—fighting alongside Nurgle factions against players—because the Tyranid Hive Mind and the Chaos Gods are mortal enemies. A Genestealer-Nurgle alliance would be lore-breaking and absurd. Thus, integrating Genestealers poses a challenge. I believe players would welcome a new enemy faction. So why not make them descend from the skies? Like in Warhammer 40K: Deathwing, an unidentified immigrant vessel emerges from the Warp above Tertium Hive. It ignores all hails from the Mourningstar. With Tertium’s relations with the Inquisition strained, supplies are dwindling. To secure resources, Inquisitor Rannick and Captain Brahms deliberately dispatch a strike team to board this silent ship. Unbeknownst to them, the vessel teems with Tyranid xeno-mutants stirring restlessly."
Upon accepting the mission, players will board the immigrant vessel for investigation. The ship contains multiple compartments where combat ensues—similar to Vermintide 2’s Chaos Wastes, but adhering to Darktide’s claustrophobic close-quarters combat. Completing objectives in each compartment dynamically impacts subsequent missions:

Destroy Tyranid mutant armories → Next mission has fewer ranged aberrants but more melee swarms

Sabotage incubation chambers → Drastically reduces special/disabler spawns

Restore power in electro-conduit vaults → Restore the light to the dark spaceship

Activate comms-relay → Enables Mourningstar vox-hail support (supply drops/intel)
Players must conquer the entire mission chain amidst ever-shifting combat parameters.
