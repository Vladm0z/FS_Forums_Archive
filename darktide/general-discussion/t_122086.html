AI slop, as far as I’m concerned, should be considered a form of spam and removed as such.

What I’m referring to is content that is clearly AI generated, typically images or videos but also posts that are entirely written by LLMs (large language models) such as this post.
Posts that entirely or mostly consist of AI slop are in a sense, offensive. The human effort, person-hood and experience completely absent, replaced with content that a bot posts through a user’s account with their consent which effectively bypasses automoderation efforts that normally prevent bots from posting here. AI slop posts usually contribute very little to nothing to a post’s subject or discussion and at times may instead throw a thread off-topic from its original discussion. It is low-effort, offensive and counterproductive to the desired ends of having a discussions forum.

So I suggest that, when it is obvious that the contents of a given post consists of AI slop, for it to be within the forum’s guidelines or rules that such a post can be flagged by users and removed with moderator approval.

Unless of course, its original contents/meanings are also entirely written by LLMs.
