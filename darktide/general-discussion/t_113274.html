Rvl • 2025-10-06 17:46:15

If you want truly optimized Havoc build you need Concussive, Razor Jaw Augment and Drive Them Back. Concussive is just a staple talent for every Arbites build, and using dog without both Razor Jaw and Voltaic is not worth it. Drive Them Back is also a staple that gives that additional cleave for Havoc hordes and stuff like multiple ragers.

In Havoc 25% against elites dog keystone is useless. Elites have such large HP pools that dog deals peanuts damage to them without blitz’s explosion, it takes like 10 seconds to kill just one Mauler. Bulwarks and Crushers, which are both elites, don’t get pinned down at all– the dog just pounces them every couple of seconds, so this talent buffs damage against Ogryns even less so.

In my opinion, in Havoc you either run Lone Wolf or Go Get Them, or you don’t use mid keystone at all to save a point for pure damage talents down the line. Go Get Them is great keystone to have if you don’t have Vet on the team or your Vet is a Zealot wannabe who doesn’t kill Gunners. With that keystone you can just send dog after packs of Gunners in the enemy backline; even if you marked just one Gunner, as long as there are enemies left in the backline and you don’t ping anything else, the dog will keep on killing shooters next to the initial target.

If you’re using Rashad, you need attack speed from either Hammer of Judgement or Lone Wolf. Rashad is kinda slow at base and it’s possible that enemies hit you through your swings, because they don’t come fast enough to stagger enemies (since you complain about low survivability).

But your perks and blessings on both shotgun and the axe are both on point for Havoc.

Shockmaul is better in Havoc, but you need to use the special attack that electrocutes the enemies inbetween normal attacks. It applies DOT which can also (as far as I know) be buffed by Thunderous blessing.

I also believe Serrated Maw (6 stacks of brittleness) is also a weak talent not worth a skill point. I don’t remember the reasoning behind why exactly it’s not good (I think it’s not worth it unless you always attack the same enemy your dog targets), but it buffs your dog’s damage the least, compared to bleed and electrocute talents.

Castigator’s Stance is also the worst Havoc ultimate on Arbites (debatable). It doesn’t provide team value like Nuncio and it limits your movement unlike dash forward. Most Arbites who play Castigator’s Stance just use it as a heal, which is bad use of the ability. I’d get Arbites Vigilant (near the Forceful keystone) which buffs your damage upon ultimate use. This will at least let you use your Castigator’s offensively, rather than a simple heal that also limits your kiting potential.
