Darktide Design Director Victor Magnuson was just interviewed in new video posted by Jeficus. There are some interesting nuggets in this conversation but I wanted to focus on one piece which starts at 15:42.

Q: Right now it feels like the story in Darktide has hit a little bit of a lull. It feels like we’re in the calm before the storm. Could players expect a climactic escalation soon, such as in the new operation?

A: I don’t know if I would call it [referring to the operation I believe] climactic but I do know and I would dare to say that we will be in the near future going to places that maybe you would not have expected it to go.
A: Another thing you can expect is that, with the Arbites update we added the … Battle for Tertium … The Battle of Tertium gave us tools to be able to do little stories like that so I think you can also expect more things of a similar type than that as well.

Two things stand out. The first part of the answer suggests that new main story missions are definitely in the works and coming Soon^tm which is something I think many suspected but confirmation is good here. The second part of the answer seems to suggest that we will see missions that are unrelated to the main story line, sort of like how VT2 has things like “The Curse of Drachenfels”, “Karak Azgaraz” and so on. Timeline for those is a little more opaque but definitely interesting nonetheless.
