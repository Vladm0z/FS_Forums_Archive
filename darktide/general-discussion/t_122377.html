Fatshark mentioned they want Skitarii to be a “Jack of all trades” type class

Stamina regeneration timer

Notice how all DLC class, regardless of role, have 0.25s stamina regeneration start up

A frontliner and a “glass cannon”, both if these being DLC and both having the lowest stam regeneration time

Please Fatshark I hope this jack of all trades ends up as it, I’m excited for the solo potential of this class without needing to cheese the game, but if it ends up just getting free benefits over non DLC classes (like faster stam regen) while still being able to do everything a non dlc class does (or Even better) I’ll be seriously disappointed

DT doesn’t have traditional roles like Tank, DPS, and so on, but I think there are roles unique to this game.

Anchors, characters who can clear lots of space and are frequently tanky - Chorus Zealot, Taunt Ogryn, multiple builds of Arby

Sniper, picking off key targets at range. Plasma Vet is a common example.

Horde Clear, pretty obvious.

Boss Killer, who can delete major single-target threats, like Zealot with Thunder Hammer.

Skitarii does seem to be able to do all of these, depending on how you build it, but doesn’t seem quite as good at a lot of them as other classes. I mean, there’s no meta right now, but I see claw builds that can chunk bosses, chain lightning, chordclaw, or transonic blades builds can horde clear, the stun ult can anchor, and the shooting build can snipe.
