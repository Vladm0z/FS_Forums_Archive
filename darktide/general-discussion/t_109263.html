This patch buffed the older ‘standard human’ weapons. But FS didn’t say it like that because noone would believe them despite the numbers being there. Good job by some of the testers. I think they shouldve marketed this update like that moreso than they have, but heyho:

Better melee choices (But Shovel is still king, depending on the horizontal heavy swords rework). Swords that can damage everything reliably, Tac Axes that are able to reposition much more easily. Zealots taking Eviscerator so Vets don’t have to do that job and can focus on other things…
Buffed Shock Mauls which do more st damage, more aoe on the heavy ‘relentless’ and more reliable stun dmg for enemy Ogryns, not to mention overall melee boss damage on all weapons.

Helbore. Already was a ton of free ammo to the team and could kill anything with enough space. Now it has zoom, a fatter hitscan shot, and more damage. It’ll be hard to miss Gunners heads let alone Crushers or the top of Bulwark shields… It can now do everything and is a really tempting Alternative for Lasgun players. Especially combined with the new melee options, but Lasgun got a huge indirect buff from the increased melee too. This Lasgun Sword Enjoyer is happy.

The metaslave Preachers now taking more Eviscerators (which personally I never stopped runinng because why would I) will help the team with their increased prevalence owing to more aoe stagger and damage allowing more actions by support classes who technically dont need to defend themselves in melee as much due to less DS users. On my Vet almost every game I’m having to babysit a DS Preacher in melee with my Shovel because theyre constantly overrun and even then most cant weave through a fight to kill Crushers fast enough before I have. They were becoming really obsolete and in those games I’d easily rpefer a second Ogryn, Psyker, or Vet. With the Combat Shotgun buff they can be taking a lot more options for dealing with hordes and CRUSHERS alike. The Agripinaa especially really relegates the Bolter to only bursting down Ogryn blobs and Bosses. The shotgun ammo buffs are a huge buff to Preacher.

Combat Shotuns got the ammo increase they needed. And damage buffs on top for some reason. I think this was an appeasement change but thats me.
Zarona got even better cleave, spamming special shells with crit+cleed will be nice, as ever, but the increased damage may turn them into Elite killers which, if so, I dont agree with.
Agripinaa got its hitfire fixed, thanks for this, it was prett janky having to ads to kill soemthing you knew you should be able to hipfire but couldnt. I dont think it needed a damage boost, but this plus the increaed ammo will allow it to reliably delete ranged blobs.
Accatran got its damage ranges increased allowing for a much more accessible skill floor, this weapon also needed the ammo increase on its critbleed build to synergise with spaming the flame shot as well, so I’m expecting this on a lot of Relic/DS preachers…

Psyker already fine but the buffed human melees will give more options for current builds, but not by much. i expect more DD/bubble builds with Tac axes, which I’ll be running more as GS got boring.

Ogryn not touched. Solidifies him as the tank class with double toughness blessings and a Club. I’d like to see a Vet explosive weapon as an indirect nerf to Rumbler spam, especially now that he can afford that role with increased melee options that isnt the King Shovel. If this were to happen, The weapon draw speed talent could also give 15% Aoe stagger radius (as it’d undoubetedly have to be smaller than Rumblers) and one of the Stealth abilities nodes changed to add burns on crit, making this side of the tree more attractive and potentially lessensing the synergy with VoC spam.

Arbites first ‘universal’ class, able to cover all playstyles ‘dps/tank/support etc’ on a single class will be interesting to see how this shakes the meta of ‘Follow the Ogryn’ which many dislike. I will be playing him offtank and pet ‘dps’.

S short mention to the DBS. This was already fine, the miniscule ammo icnrease is a token buff, as is the adm vs Unyielding. The only way to appease the forumcrowdTM is increasing the range of its osk (one shot kill) altfire ability and to be honest while this may make it mroe attractive I dont think this would be beneficial. Tac shotguns got the love, keeping this things osk ability in a nice niche a melee backup that can delete Mauler blobs when being overrun.

Everything got buffed, keeping the difficulty level the same. Its clear FS now want the game to be 4 people going through a map at the same time rather than 4 ppl going through a map together. However, with more melee viability perhaps this will shake the meta enough for people to consider their team. perhaps. For a while I was worried we’d see a universal reduction in difficulty, if forum voices are anything to go by. I’m looking forwards to you still shadowbanning my Lasgun/Helbore/Sword loadouts even more now. My throat is dry.
