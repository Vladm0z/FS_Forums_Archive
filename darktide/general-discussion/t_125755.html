The basic idea is to add Dark Eldar raid as an event however ive also put some restraints on myself to keeps things realistic in a development sense to keep it viable.
these restraints are:
Keeping it within existing framework, no new systems etc.
Keeping it within Lore, not just 40k lore but also in regards to events within Tertium, so no contradictions to existing plots but able to plausibly exist with it, to add but not change anything.
minimize development time and resources as possible, though I know first hand that somethings that need to be don are labor intensive
but using as much of the existing assets, rigs, materials and texture as possible.
To add fresh approaches to gameplay with simply through number tuning, and staying within existing frame works of unit behavior, weapon functions and overall game mechanics.

what I have below is just my idea and my suggestions are not set in stone but more of a starting point that could be tuned to taste.

So the premise is that the activity of the system has piqued the curiosity of Haemunculi of the Hex Coven, for reasons beyond human understanding, could be trying to make a name for himself or just bored, however its at least known there is an interest in the pathogen, infected, Karnak twins and or Nurgles blessings. As such is conducting small abduction raids in Tertium for specimens to take back for study. crossing paths with rejects in the Haemunculi’s eyes is just another sample, of something to test on. perhaps a worthy slave to sell off
point being these are small detachments not full invasion. simply popping in via webway, get what they can and leave. they have no interest in the heretics or Imperium and just taking advantage of the situation
happy to expand on this further in more detail if desired.

For now I wanted to keep the event simple in that is a normal mission with variables.
so it may start and end the same but in the middle the rejects stumble onto a raid taking place
where webay portals are present and spawn the enemy you could also at this point have some interference caused that cuts comms, so eliminates any comms chatter inconsistencies with HQ to get around any need to record new voices. or if desired, new scenarios or make use of the expedition maps.

As for the Eldar themselves, I restricted to what is offten lead by a Haemonculi coven.
so would consist of
Wracks, Grotesques, Mandrakes, Pain engines. with specialized units like a Beastmaster

These units could simply just be skin swaps of existing units with numbers tweaked to suit
what they are based on i can leave up to you but you can simply see what could work, like a Wrack with a liquefier gun is just a pox flamer that uses a recolored beast of Nurgle breath attack visual effect etc. a Talos takes the place of a Chaos Ogrin or Spawn.
its the numbers here that would do the most heavy lifting, increasing movement speed and agility. more hit an run behavior if able. the goal being, while still attacking in numbers its not the endless swarm. making up for it with speed, and pressure to not simply be gunned down.

Other systems like corruption still exist and function the same, it’s still corruption, just toxin corruption instead of the nurgle corruption.
the majority of work would come from making the models themselves but if they can be rigged to existing rigs it can cut animation need or time.
it just comes down to how much extra one wants to put into it.
I think i have gone into enough now but just curious of the concept and happy to expand on things here or not mentioned or considered. not expecting this tomorrow if at all but perhaps could spark something interesting to add to the game whatever final form it takes.
