Why on earth an ork!?
This is exactly why! I don’t think there has been a SINGLE video game where we get to play as an ork protagonist. For some reason, our favourite comic relief greenskins have always been taking the backseat when it comes to the narratives of video games. I think it’s high time we change things up. Orks just like space marines have been the meat and bones of the series and I sincerely believe it would be crazy to get to play an FPS from ork perspective. Each time a new Warhammer 40k game releases it’s a coin toss if it’s just another “here check out these space marines” game. How crazy fun it would be if once in a while we had something other (of high quality) to play that is not exclusively based on astartes or imperium of mankind. How often do you get to play as an ork from the first-person perspective? Never. In Darktide a huge opportunity arises to test new things and what concept is better to test than a Vermintide style FPS with an ork pov! Imagine the Waaagh! Imagine the unique and fun voice lines! Imagine getting hands-on experience of ork arsenal which like I said before, you probably never had a chance to test yet. This idea would surely not only get lots of support from the wh40k community but definitely the ork players and even any greenskin lover who haven’t even heard of Warhammer before but would like to see more ork stuff in video games! For me, this would be revolutionary. Just imagine testing all the cold steel at your disposal while calling out the chaos baddies! Similarly to Bardin from V2, Wargad would make use of his extraordinary vocabulary and language. No matta if talkin to Iz fighting bruvvaz or sum pansy grotz! This might come as heresy to some of you but honestly, ork culture has been a great benefit to the Warhammer universe. Even if you dislike playing them personally. They always make fun allies, sidekick and enemies. Orks are always cool and versatile. I don’t think I have ever met a single person who truly believes orks are a boring and stupid faction. You simply can’t go wrong with orks and why!

What possible ork careers could we expect?
Because it’s never been done before I was going to say the sky is the limit. However, after doing some research I realized the options would be somewhat limited. For starters, we need to make Wargad equal to the rest of the party strength-wise. Just like we get the imperial guard and not space marines. This means no Warboss stuff that would make our ork character ridiculously big. Another thing to remember is there will be no Nob stuff, the party of Darktide makes use of available resources. This means Wargad can’t lug everything he found to this moment and bring it here. No supa-deff-gun-killas. Sorry. Weirdboy is also out of the question because of the rank and forces the posses, and that’s okay though because we still get plenty of careers to choose from. Shoota/Slugga/Burna Boy, Tankbusta, Kommando, Runtherd, Mekboy, Painboy. Anything goes really. A couple of cuts here and there and we are left with a diverse and interesting variety of careers that proudly represent the power of greenskins I chose the Slugga Boy, Kommando and Tankbusta. All of these careers are fairly similar in strength and cover different archetypes or classes. Here’s a few examples of how could these classes fit into the gameplay side of things.

Ork arsenal put short
Orks are the trashmen of the Warhammer universe. They make use of everything they find. Every ork knows how to turn something he just found into a weapon. They also upgrade their arsenal by adding spiky bits and more metal to stabilize their crazy weapons better. Their guns also work great as melee weapons (all the spikes and stuff). Because their list of weapon is so great I am not going to list every one of them and instead, I will just send you to this page so that you can discover all of them for yourself. Their weapons are big, loud, cheap and expendable in a nutshell. You’d think that space marines get the coolest weapons but it’s the ork faction that has the highest number of weapons I like the most. Such as a melee weapon that is a metal rod with a faulty rocket welded at the end that can explode with each hit. Also, orange sparking beamy weapons, those are super fun!
