Recently as you know, the game is kinda stale because lack of new maps, absolutely awful Havoc state with the devs spamming the same modifiers and not fixing longstanding issues, and the fact that most of new content gets locked behind time limited events, so the games you play in auric barely change from game to game if you play the game often. I’ve decided to install solo mod to switch things up to see if I can complete auric true solo as every single class, just for the fun of it, and to see if I’m skilled enough to do it.

I don’t have any experience doing solos, since I previously considered the solo grind to be a waste of time in a highly randomized game with lots of BS situations.

I decided to play one map only (Smelter Complex), so I could compare my experience with each class more accurately. Smelter map is really well designed for solo gameplay and there’s always room to kite enemies, with the exception of one poorly designed area towards the end (room before last airlock door).

I started the challenge on Vet, but quickly realized it’s not as easy as I expected and decided to move to other classes before returning to Vet the next day. Despite Vet being my main (I guess) who I enjoy playing the most, it took me two days to get my win. I lost every game trying to play builds that I wanted (recon lasgun, stealth power sword, bolt pistol grenadier, boss-buster with bolter), I only won when I brought Plasma Gun. I also didn’t want to use Duelling Sword or Knife, because it makes running away from enemies too easy. I’m happy with Vet’s current power level in the game overall, but not gonna lie, in solos they were pretty mid even with a weapon like Plasma while requiring lots of effort in melee compared to some other classes…

Ogryn was a walk in a park mostly, even though the win took average time, it felt like it went in a blink of an eye. The first Ogryn run I died to some BS early in the map, but then the next game I instantly got it after I decided against running Ripper Gun and switched from Heavy Hitter to Feel no Pain to reduce pressure from basic shooters. While Ogryn kinda lacks DPS and struggles against large number of shooters in open areas and fears Dogs/Trappers due to shortest dodges, I think Ogryn was amongst the easiest runs. To be fair, I think not having rock for mutant/disabler instakills and Rumbler for bosses will make it much, much harder.

I was surprised to get what I consider an easy win on Zealot. I died once right at the end to burster doing something stupid (video), once to first boss Burgle eating me in the first half of the map and bringing me to Flamer, and once to Plague Ogryn bumping into me which sent me flying to lava pool (after that game I pre-blocked every charge he did XD). If it wasn’t for my lack of experience against Packmaster, I would play throwing knives, but I had to play fire nades so that I reduce the chances of getting pounced.

Despite Hive Scum taking 3 runs to finish, I almost won on the first try, but at the end of the map during the final event I ledged myself by accident, I was so shocked by my stupidity I couldn’t even be mad (video lower). The second run ended somewhere at midpoint to a dog pounce while I had no Rampage active with mini-VOC shout effect. But despite Hive Scum taking 3 almost full runs, he was the easiest class by far to do it and the fastest to progress through a map each time. After doing solos I now consider Hive Scum the strongest class in the game above Psyker, because melee Psyker took WAY more effort and required to be careful at every turn. And that’s funny, because I don’t even use +1 blitz talent in my builds, meaning I could clear crowds even faster…

Psyker might be the top DPS in the game with flame staff and a few other builds (the one I used), but that’s when they don’t have much heat on them, because teammates are covering them and distrubuting aggro and supporting them with abilities. But to me personally, playing them solo was an excruciatingly infuriating experience, and I kept dying for a different reason every run, and I still don’t understand what’s the best solo curio setup for Psyker (3 toughness, 2 health + 1 toughness, 2 health + stamina etc…) and I had to get every single toughness regen talent for comfortable play. Thankfully I exploded from peril only once in the ~15 games that I played, that’s something positive. I also didn’t use perilous combustion, because I want to see enemies in melee without blinding myself, and I didn’t want to play a shriek solo build which require running away from enemies all the time.

I’m not too familiar with the spawn logic of the game engine, but it felt like the game just wanted to kill me as Psyker the most ruthlessly, it’s the only class where I saw squads of 5+ Crushers spawning across multiple runs (which shouldn’t be the case anymore?), 3 bursters at the same time multiple times per mission, and getting the worst luck on bosses (Packmaster twice per mission every other mission, or DH + boss in the cramped space). In one of the runs I died to a single sniper shot right at the end, because I was low on HP…

Speaking of Packmaster, he was the scariest boss to face BY FAR with any class, because of his dogs. I never thought much of them playing the game normally with other people because their attention is spread across multiple players and people keep them in check with stagger ults by using them in turn, but playing solo made me respect Packmaster for how imposing he truly is.

I figured out that the best way to solo Packmaster is to hold either A or D (so move in a single direction continuosly, without letting go of the button) and circle them around, pressing dodge on every swing. When you have the slow debuff on you and he just keeps reapplying it because there are no other teammates around, it’s very painful to fight him, considering the game doesn’t stop spawning specials and random enemies, so it’s never a 1vs1 (video above).

For countering his dogs in solo I found that it’s best to find a room with a pillar or a large box in the middle, because less space means dogs can only jump from one or two directions. By the way, Packmaster doesn’t have collision for the dogs, so they can pounce through his model, which I think is kinda silly and overpowered on the enemies side… This I noticed really fast in my runs when I’m the only person who dogs target, and he spawns 3+ of them on each summon.

90% corruption resistance on Skitarii is a lifesaver-- if you get pounced by a dog, you can wait for minutes for a poxburster to spawn so they will free you by exploding on top, which prevents a failed run. Flame skull with 5 charges is insane in non-speedrun true solos where you will have to fight every Vanguard by yourself. Skitarii is probably the easiest class to deal with them, while Vet without shredder grenades is the worst.

Dog Arby is just fun. After playing 3 classes on my own, it was something special to play them with a dog and have someone you can rely on. I know some people might consider that playstyle cheating, since it’s not a “solo”, whatever.

I’ve spent around 5 days on all these runs. Next time I’ll try to go for even more off-meta builds, although I’m not sure I want to go back to Psyker solos anytime soon.

Now that I played the game in solo extensively, I have to say, it’s a completely different, refreshing gameplay experience, because unlike in normal games you can’t recover from stupid mistakes easily by the help of teammates. Losing too much HP means that you might end up fighting the boss one hit away from going down. And you truly have to master dealing with every type of enemy on your own to maximize the chances of success.

I started to respect solo runs (not speedruns with knife through the map though) a lot more even that use some cheat mods, because either way they require a lot of focus, reacting quickly to each possible situation and never messing up game inputs.

Maybe some of you thinking the game is in boring place should try this “every class solo challenge” yourself. And if you think it’s too easy you can avoid meta weapons entirely.
