Vertraxxx • 2025-10-12 19:00:19

Yes, I believe Havoc should either be removed entirely or rebuilt after a proper rebalancing of Darktide’s core systems.
The biggest mistake was creating Havoc without first addressing the elements that make the overall difficulty far too easy, such as Gold Toughness, Smite, and overpowered weapons.

As seen in VT2, having too many game modes is never a good thing.
It splits the player base and makes the game confusing and uninviting for new players who are unsure where to begin.

Currently, Darktide offers five base difficulty tiers, plus Maelstrom and Havoc.
However, both Maelstrom and Havoc serve the same purpose: they are modes designed for players to test and improve their skills.
In particular, the lower tiers of Maelstrom and Havoc overlap almost completely in terms of challenge, which makes one of them unnecessary.

The difficulty consistency within Havoc, especially between levels 30 and 40, also feels very unstable.
Because of the AI Director’s randomness, I have often found level 30 missions harder than level 40 ones.
Without pressing TAB to check, it is almost impossible to tell whether a mission is level 30, 33, 36, or 40.

The game should first return to the basics.
The standard five difficulties should be balanced to cover around 95% of the player base.
Then, for the top 5–10% who find Auric too easy, Havoc can serve as a true endgame challenge.

However, Havoc does not need 40 different tiers.
A simpler five-step structure would be more than enough.
Having too many difficulty levels does not add depth; it only creates confusion and divides the community.
