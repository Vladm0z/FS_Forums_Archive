Thus far, Fatshark seems to keep the weapons to a relatively limited level. Veterans for example get weapons you can find in a basic Infantry Squad (which can include bolters and plasma guns and power swords), but not heavy weapons or special-issue equipment like Hot Shot weaponry or particularly exotic bolters. Within that space, about the only thing we’re missing is plasma pistols, Grenade Launchers, and Meltaguns, and with the types enemies we have in game a meltagun largely feels like it’d just be a slower firing and shorter ranged charged plasma gun. Maybe powerfists? That’s something not available to a basic Infantry Squad but is available to many common officers. My issue with a Long-Las is that we already have the Helbore, and most players won’t take a shot over 50m in most games, so where is a Long-Las going to get a chance to really shine?

I’d love to see something like the Gravity Gun from Half Life for a Psyker Stave

Zealot needs a more classical Eviscerator, something that’s hilariously huge and absurdly unwieldy but with huge sweeps and devastating overheads.

That said, we have triple digits worth of weapons in the game already, that will forever have balance issues, adding forever more won’t help on that front
