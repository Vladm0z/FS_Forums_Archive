anon63448941:

so what situation calls for Special attack?

the special originally was just a combo resetting attack, it could be used at any point in the combo to reset you back to the first attack which is notable on the mk9 due to the overhead only being available from a neutral state which the special put you back into. it also is notable with its much higher stagger, and an actually decent carapace adm which is why its used in those armor combos. as of this latest patch though, they added 2 features to it, the special now applies a unique 10% brittleness for a few seconds (meaning it stacks with other sources) and also makes the light that immediately follows it bypass the carapace armor cleave override and ignores 75% of their hitmass for that swing (basically what perfect strike does, but built into that one attack.) is that part really that useful for damage? no, its still dealing sword light damage to carapace armor, but it does inherently let you proc that rending blessing even into a wall of armor, which gives it some niche value.
