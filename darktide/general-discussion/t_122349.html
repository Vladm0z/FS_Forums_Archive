Concerns that the skitarii data interrogator function will neuter many finales and events

20 Posts • 1064 Views
e
eatingsand • 2026-06-13 19:47:25

finales and events are fun because people need to do tasks that distract them from fighting. in interrogation events, players need to render themselves defenseless to solve the minigame. the team then needs to protect these players. it’s a fun dynamic that adds pressure to the event and forces them to coordinate, pick their timing to do the event, and not unimportantly, have less people available to do the actual fighting, making pressure higher

by allowing one player to send a servoskull to do the event mechanic, the entire event is neutered and turns into a 4-player survive-anywhere event, while being designed and balanced around players needing to do an interrogation task, i.e. a 3-player hold your ground event where you need to protect a specific zone, the interrogator location. this neuters the event from two different angles. you can now just make the skull solve it while you hold out in a choke.

i think mechanics that neuter game mechanics and directly solve a gameplay task for you are boring, and ultimately bad design. please reconsider letting servoskulls do this. it’s thematic, but it will make the game less fun by removing complexity and decisionmaking.

if i had to suggest an alternative, why not allow the skull to hack into the minigame for the skitarii and alter it to become easier to solve? the compromise still keeps the dynamic intact while still being a cool thematic benefit. for example it could show the upcoming letters on the letter minigame, or show which circle must be pressed on the hab dreyko minigame

this has been pointed out by other people before, but i’m a hopeful type of guy so i decided to make a thread for visibility
