Try using Feel no Pain, it reduces the damage from basic shooter and with the node the restores 50% toughness it prevents losing too much HP if you toughness gets broken ocassionally. Also this node removes dog from you as long as you don’t get pounced twice in a row. Playing shield Ogryn on havoc made me like Feel no Pain, it’s just that in normal auric 4 man games there was never a reason to use it previously. Feel no Pain for auric is only good when you play with really, really bad teams who keep dying all the time, so you need extra survavibility to go for the revives constantly.

The difference between Heavy Hitter and FNP in auric is only 1-2 hits on most enemies, and if you’re gonna use Rumbler for solo run, it also doesn’t matter if you don’t run Heavy Hitter, since Rumbler doesn’t benefit from any of the left side skills.

The only reason to use Heavy Hitter over FNP in auric solo runs is additional attack speed in my opinion, maybe cleave node for pickaxe to maximize the damage with 2 strength blessings + cleave node.

It’s just that, obviously, Heavy Hitter is better in good teams of 4 players. More damage etc But in solo you’re gonna kill everything by yourself, so there’s no need to race other people for kills trying to stack everything into damage.
