Firstly, Adepta Sororitas is not the same as Sister of Battle, as Sisters of Battle refers only to those from the Orders Militant, the term “Adepta Sororitas” refers to the other Orders as well, such as the Orders Hospitaller who are well known for being attached to the Inquisition.

Secondly, when we do look at Sisters of Battle, they also use heavier weapons such as the heavy bolter, multi-melta and heavy flamer in their Retributor squads.

If fatshark leaned into the Retributor angle, it’d be an interesting counterpart to the Ogryn in that it would be single gender and also using heavier weapons.

A Sister Hospitaller also has potential to be an interesting support class.

More broadly the Adepta Sororitas also use hand flamers, inferno pistols and storm bolters.

A key distinction between the Zealot and Adepta Sororitas, is that Zealots represent wandering preachers and frateris militia and other members of the ecclesiarchy which are inherently poorly disciplined and less organised.

Adepta Sororitas on the other hand are highly disciplined and well trained warriors, who despite their common portrayal in memes do not purely rely on fervor, in their books they’re often depicted as being tactically proficient.

Additionally, there’s a good justification in the story for Sororitas arriving on the Mourningstar as:

  1. The second world in the Moebian Domain is Crucis, a large shrine world is likely to have a contingent of Sororitas.

  2. The theory that Admonition includes a fallen Sororitas in Urmal Skarkal. The Sisterhood is well known to dispatch teams to destroy those who stray from the path.

  3. Rannick messed up his intelligence and allowed Admonition to attempt a summoning inside a cathedral, if the Arbites can show up because they ran out of patience, the Sororitas can show up because Rannick allowed Admonition to desecrate a holy cathedral.

So yes, I believe they’re distinct enough to warrant inclusion as their be all and end all isn’t religious fervour like the zealot.

McJager • 2026-09-26 05:07:01

You raise some fair points regarding standard tabletop rules and the extreme ends of the lore, but I think you might be looking at my concept through an “Officio Assassinorum from Terra” lens rather than an Inquisitorial Operative framework.

You mentioned that the Temples don’t mix and are separate organizations. While true for the grand bureaucracy on Terra, it doesn’t apply to a high-ranking Inquisitor like Grendyl. Inquisitorial warbands regularly recruit, train, and utilize acolytes and death-cultists trained in the distinct philosophies and tactical doctrines of these schools. A multi-clade skill web represents an agent pulling from a library of secret Inquisitorial training templates—not seven different Grand Masters crammed into a single body.

Saying a Blank node would break a friendly Psyker assumes we are copying the tabletop 100% literally. Darktide is a video game where mechanics are routinely adjusted for fun team gameplay. A “Blank-inspired” talent node wouldn’t kill your teammate; it would function as a game-balanced Blitz or Aura that actively suppresses heretic, and pox-corruption without turning off friendly mechanics.

As for you saying they don’t fight hoards. Neither do standard Skitarii Alpha Primuses or isolated Astra Militarum sharpshooters under standard military deployment, yet here they are in Darktide. When an entire Hive City falls, everyone has to fight through hordes of Poxwalkers to get to their high-value target. An agile assassin utilizing stealth and speed to dance through a crowd and decapitate a Scab Captain is Warhammer at its core.

Darktide already stretches weapon lore to create an engaging sandbox. Realistically, Ogryns don’t walk around with customized folding shovels or twin-linked heavy stubbers in baseline tabletop lore—Fatshark designed those weapon patterns to fill out a fun gameplay pool. An Assassin class would easily share access to existing finesse weapons (like power swords and tactical axes) while introducing a few exotic highlights like Needler rifles or poison-infused blades.
