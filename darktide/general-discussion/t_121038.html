silentbob515 • 2026-04-22 19:04:10
brosgw:

Wouldn’t it have the opposite?

Don’t look at the QP as something that starts but as something that fills. If less people are joining then every Expedition in the QP is a glass that is waiting to be filled. It takes just one person to start the Expedition for the QP to create a pull.

In the QP case less people is actually a bad thing, because it means that there are always slots to fill. And that is before we account for QP being a filter on its own, because QP can only work with people that QP in the first place!

And while the ability to opt out of it is certainly a solution for people who wants none of it, toggling it On also means there are less people in the pool for QP to draw from in order to fill the Expedition. So that’s another potential filter to pass thru!

Which means that its statistically plausible to see a tilt toward the QP favoring the Expeditions, nothing dramatic but at the same time capable of creating a vicious cycle of people having a QP streak with Expeditions and getting sick of it, opting out and shrinking the available pool even further!

I mean we don’t have to go too far for an example, everytime there’s an event the willingness to QP drops sharply with each passing day in my DT social circles, which indicates that deep down people are aware of certain processes that diminish the randomness of QP and act upon it by avoidance
