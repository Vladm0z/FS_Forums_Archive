KnotForSail • 2026-06-11 02:11:53

I’ve been playing again after a roughly six-month hiatus, and I’m quite disheartened with the feel of the game. There are only three difficulties right now: Fading light 2 > Fading light 1 > Everything Else. Anything outside of havoc 20+ feels like playing Diablo 3. There are enemies on the screen, they probably do different things. Maybe they have different armor types or resistances. You don’t care about any of that though, because they’re all going to disintegrate in the next few seconds. Maybe you contributed to their destruction, maybe not, but there’s three more players in the match than there needs to be, so don’t worry about it.

This is not a fun place to be. Havoc matches are unreliable, and still frustratingly confined to a subset of the total map pool. But currently it’s the only place to go if you want a match that actually benefits from having a full team. Auric and below are now just competitions to feel relevant.

And just to preempt any “it’s always been this bad” comments: I’m six months out of practice, coming into a version of the game that has had at least 2 major balance passes, and I’m more bored than I’ve ever been. It’s never been this bad. I’ve read a lot of overly dramatic hyperbole on this forum, and been guilty of it myself, but there genuinely isn’t enough fight to entertain four players anymore.

The easiest solution in my mind is to just make havoc more accessible. You have a game mode that’s still fun and challenging, utilize it. Make two new difficulties that you can quick play into and have them correspond to havoc 29 and 40 in terms of difficulty (or whatever the ceilings are for fading light 1 and 2). Put a big fat banner on them that says, “these difficulties are hard for the sake of being hard, and offer no unique rewards”, and let players have fun.
