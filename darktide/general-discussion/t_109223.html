Norrisjpackard • 2025-06-22 10:09:59

I’ve been skipping around through different videos and streams put out by people who have the ability to use the Arbites currently, and I noticed a worrying trend.

Unless they specifically were attempting to show off a particular weapon, functionally every single one of them has been running the regular Shock Maul and the Bolter. And more than a few have said the regular Shock Maul (not the Arbites one) is the best weapon the class has, and while none have (to my knowledge) outright said that the Bolter is the best ranged weapon choice… They’re all using it over the Arbites Shotguns.

And I know why that is after watching the footage, and had one of them explain why the Arbites Shock Maul is inferior. The regular Shock Maul has better overheads, and stun capacity, but more importantly: it has Skullcrusher as a blessing. Currently, almost every single Ogryn is running Skullcrusher, which means that although the base damage of the Arbites Shock Maul is impressive it is left behind the moment that Skullcrusher stacks start falling into place, and everyone is aware that the Bully Club using Ogryns (functionally all of them) now have a friend who will push the damage up by another 40%. The end result is that the Arbites Shock Maul will never approach best in slot consideration.

Several have also talked about how the shields (both versions) are meh, for different reasons. Defensively they are good (a sentiment I have also heard and seen repeatedly) but that seems to be about the only thing they’re good at. The mixture of wonky move sets, and mostly bad blessings hurts their damage immensely.

Needless to say, I am not particularly enthused by this result. But I can live with it.

However, what really gets my goat is that the Bolter just bodies every single other ranged weapon the Arbites has. The two Arbites Shotguns are already inferior to the Zarona, which I wasn’t particularly happy to see. But worse, the Bolter is so vastly more effective than any of the shotguns (especially since it paves over the Arbites biggest weakness [again, another sentiment shared by basically everyone], Monstrosities) that no one is using the shotguns unless they’re just messing around. When it comes time to “lock in”, they’ll stop futzing with any other ranged weapon and pick the Bolter. And even when they are messing around with things that aren’t the Bolter, they still are taking the regular Shock Maul to make up for the fact that they are using a weapon that isn’t best in slot.

I have no hope that anything will be done to the Bolter because the class truly does lack for boss killing potential, but for the love of God, Fatshark, can you please do something to make the weapons this class is supposed to be known for actually worth consideration? It’s not a great sign when the people who are supposed to be convincing us to buy the class just aren’t using the weapons you made for the class because they’re saying the quiet part loud.
