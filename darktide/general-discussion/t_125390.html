In my mind, there are six categories of triggers:

  1. Strictly guaranteed triggers — someone crosses the trigger line, and a boss drops right on their head; it’s impossible to avoid while progressing through the level.
  2. Time-dependent triggers. Hypothetically: if the trigger is crossed less than ten minutes into the mission, a boss spawns to slow down a rushing team; if it’s past the ten-minute mark, no boss spawns.
  3. A trigger based on the player’s geometric position relative to the team’s center. If you break too far ahead, crossing the trigger spawns a boss; if you stay close, it doesn’t. The same mechanic might apply if you lag too far behind.
  4. A trigger for “deserters” — if you retreat too far back from the point you’ve already reached, you get a boss.
  5. A periodic time trigger — if there hasn’t been a boss on the map for too long and no other triggers have been activated, a boss spawns.
  6. A trigger right before the player respawn point. If all players are alive, the trigger is inactive; if someone is waiting for a rescue, the trigger activates when you try to run up and save them.

Which of these triggers actually exist, and which are just a figment of my imagination?

This feels especially relevant during clutch attempts. It’s as if: you rush forward — boss, you run far back — boss, you almost reach the team’s respawn point — boss. Long story short: don’t even hope to avoid showcasing your clutch skills against a boss, even if your teammates are just three meters away
