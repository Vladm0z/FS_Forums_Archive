I’m being serious. I’ve seen many posts on forums from new players who just bought the game and have never played Vermintide or similar games before. As veteran players, we always say that the final boss fight is the easiest mission for kills. But for new players with zero talent bonuses and terrible gear, many say they can’t even make it to the final boss—just a few regular enemies are already tough to handle. Even if they manage to reach the boss, it’s too hard. They say the boss deals massive damage, can’t be staggered, has attacks that are almost impossible to block,making it unbeatable.
