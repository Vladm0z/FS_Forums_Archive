Congratulate me. Today I managed, on my 19th attempt (actually even more — the 19th is just since I started keeping track at the end of December), to complete the H21 mission while playing as a Zealot. This is only the second time I’ve managed to do it. The first time was when I played an Ogryn, even before the Hive Scum update.

I don’t know whether it’s a coincidence or not, but this was the first attempt where I took Chorus instead of FotF and Relic Blade instead of DS4. By the way, when I played as an Ogryn I was using a Bully Club and the Loyal Protector ability. It’s possible that team-oriented abilities influence the success of the mission more than I previously thought.

I was only third in damage (and the last Hive Scum — which disappointed me quite a bit; I brought him into the team expecting him to be a strong damage dealer). Besides him, the team had a Veteran with Commanding Voice and an Arbites with a dog. The Arbites was probably the MVP of the match — not only first in damage but also first in the number of operatives revived from knockdowns.

I was knocked down at least twice but wasn’t killed a single time. In total, players were killed three times during the run, and the last player who died was never revived — we evacuated without him.

Only 9 bosses during the mission, which is pretty merciful. In my previous H20 mission there were 12 bosses. However, there was one very difficult moment, and we created it ourselves. When we had already run into the elevator, a Captain appeared. I tried to jump out to fight him, but someone pressed the elevator button and we left. The result: literally right after the elevator doors opened we had to fight the Captain and the Twins at the same time. I don’t recall my team ever surviving similar situations in H21 or even H20 before. But this time the team handled it. Was it thanks to the golden toughness from my Chorus? Would the team have survived if I had used FotF as usual?

Anyway, I’m happy as an elephant. And now I’ll proudly stroll around the Mourningstar with the rank-21 badge (at least until my rank gets reset to 20 at the start of the new Havoc season).

Experimentally it has been established that it’s better not to bring Psykers into the team, no matter how cool they may seem. They love to be the first to go down, especially when the Twins appear on the map. Maybe at higher difficulty levels — where the team protects them well, or they themselves play like gods — this isn’t the case. But the Psykers who try to get into rank-21 lobbies just ruin the runs
