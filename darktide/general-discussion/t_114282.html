oh interesting, guess it might just be bugged then, ill see if i can hop on the game and do a little testing.

Ive done a little testing in the meat grinder, and i think the talent is working properly. On my build i get 9 toughness per melee kill without any +toughness gain talents and at max health

if i then grab restorative verses, i still only get 9 at max health, but if i drop to 1 hp, i get 11 toughness back on melee kill

25% of 9 is 2.25 which would round down to 11 in the hud ui.

if i then grab blood redemption, it instead becomes 19 toughness on melee kill

if i grab both blood redemption and vicious offering and get a heavy melee attack kill, i then get 40 toughness

and finally, both blood redemption and vicious offering, but no restorative verses i get 33 toughness

so it seems as if the talent is working properly, its just that 25% of a small number is an even smaller number that might not even round properly in the ui
