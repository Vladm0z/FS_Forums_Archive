  • Option 1: Doing it normally.
  • Option 2: Pressing and holding the interact button exactly during the slide animation.
  • Option 3: Pressing and holding the interact button exactly during the block animation.

Do Options 2 and 3 offer any advantages over Option 1?

  • Option 1: Doing it normally.
