Obone:

What’s the argument there, is it that the gauntlet hypothetically does more DPS against a densely packed chunk of meat?

The reason to take GG over the kickback is that a) the GG does significantly more damage at mid to long range, which is where you generally want to be killing specials, b) the GG explodes so even if you miss your target or a poxwalker mr president’s the shot, the explosion will generally stagger the special or elite or whatever and prevent it from doing anything until you can line up a second shot, c) the GG’s special attack is extremely good at dealing with densely packed armored enemies piling up on you and for clearing space to move through dense horse, and d) the GG fires explosives, which are infinite cleave, which means damage can rack up quickly in dense enemy hordes.

The kickback is a perfectly valid gun to be running; it shreds unarmored enemies at close range and performs reasonably well as a special sniping tool at longer ranges, but the nature of shotguns and its damage falloff mean it can struggle to fill that role in higher difficulties, and it ends up not being very ammo efficient as a direct result, forcing you to use it with relative caution or bring the Rock blitz to deal with enemies at long range. And many builds would just rather have the more versatile option that doesn’t force build decisions to maintain effectiveness.
