Mister E here,

Just wanted to put this out here with the intents of positively reinforcing fatshark. I see the new event as a HUGE win for all parties, it’s not only a decently fun event, it has a reward players will actually want (500 aquilas). Since the in-game events only happen about once a month, Fatshark is in no danger of spoiling the economy for themselves by overindulging players. There are plenty of things to spend on, AND having a little change in your pocket might make you want to buy a little something extra. My favorite game economies are Warframe and Helldivers 2, as they both allow pathways to generate currency albeit slowly just by playing.

Darktide should do the same. The +1000 melk bucks (for completing all weekly tasks) should be converted into an account-wide reward of like, 100 aquilas. HAVOC should reward Aquilas based on the highest level completed for the week or the average level you completed for the month. It’s literal fake money to Fatshark, and pennies to players; but for many, it’s an actual, REAL incentive. Do I NEED 500 aquilas? No. Am I going to play the event for 8 hours to get them? Damn straight.

Also please enlarge the selection at the premium shop, or at least keep it as large as it is since skulls update.
