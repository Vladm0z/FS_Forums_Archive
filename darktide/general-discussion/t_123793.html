Double-barrel / CAxe stealthy fragger - Veteran Build for Darktide

Veteran build for Warhammer 40k: Darktide, using the Rashad Mk III Combat Axe and Crucis Mk XI Double-Barrelled Shotgun. Created by tangodown7854.

As you can see, this build has a lot of hoard clearing capabilities via Frags and Double Barrel, so I only use caxe for that when hoard is thin. When fighting I throw in push attack from time to time (a wide swing), to just keep trash at bay and “condense” them a bit - then I chop them almost individually with light attacks. I don’t do push attacks too often as they do little damage and burn stamina like crazy, though.I take out singular more armored (scab) chaff with heavies to the head too.

I kill reguar elites by just chopping them with light attacks, mostly, throwing in targeted heavies when possible (if I can afford staying in one place charging and aiming it).

When it comes to Crushers, I observe what it does, if it targets somebody else, I attack him with heavy hits trying to aim at the head, after a couple of such I shoot him with dbarrel to the head which either heavy staggers or knocks him down, then finish him. If there are a bunch of them I drop Frags under their feet to disrupt them, and then target the ones that still keep charging. If it charges me I start with the dbarrel shot to the head to throw them off balance, and then chop them as usual.

Also note I’m using different bless on caxe, the one that gives mobs brittleness, it helps with killing heavy armor too.
