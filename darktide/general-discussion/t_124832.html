2.) Skill. There’s a very large skill gap, at several stages between players. The players stuck at malice, the players doing Auric duos, and the people doing havoc 40 solos and such. Obviously the people with lower skill would want the nerf and people bored with difficulty would not.

A very large group of them could cover each other to some degree and make clearing them take longer, but it was not difficult, just slightly time consuming. All they needed at most was a slightly lower spawn rate.

What’s bad design for a shield enemy in a 'tide game is threefold;

  1. Them creating situations the player cannot escape, ie a group of Bulwarks crowding you in, and the blocking ones preventing attacks on those who open up to attack.

  2. Only being countered by certain weapons.

  3. Be so weak as to not serve their function of forcing a change in behavior in players.

To look at some of the prior shield enemies in 'tide games, all of them have one of these problems.

Shield Skaven and Shielded Marauders were so weak as to often be ignorable in VT2, so they fall into 3. Shieldvermin had a bit of 1, if a group of them were in your face, and Bulwarks do still. 2 also is something that affects Crushers, since certain weapons just don’t deal much damage to Unyielding in large amounts and others just bypass shields completely.

Vanguards are probably the best shield enemies they’ve made, because they solved all three problems - their shields can be broken, it just takes awhile, so you are never fully trapped by them and every weapon has the potential to kill them. All while they have a distinct counterplay that is executionally very simple and logical, ie “moving behind them”. Much moreso than “push twice to open them up”, which is a functional if unintuitive and slightly stupid counter.

Like, I don’t know what to say here; literally this was a case of people being bad at the game and dragged it down because they wouldn’t adapt at all. Now the Vanguards are basically ignorable and you can m1 spam with near impunity into hordes again. It is 100% a regression.
