It runs better on Win10, though you can adjust a lot of stuff in Win11 that makes it run basically like Win10. In my experience this game is rather poorly optimized overall. I have a mid-range setup and I have to lower a lot of stuff to get decent fps. I don’t play with frame generation on as it adds too much latency and will get me killed surprisingly often in a game already suffering from latency issues.

Then there’s all the weird crashes. Failing to load into mourningstar after end mission, textures that fail to load in or only load in their most washed-out form etc.

Overall the engine is still full of bugs and runs poorly. The ratio of eye-candy to performance is poor. It looks great at max settings, but performs abysmally imo.
