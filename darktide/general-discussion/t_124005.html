It could also use some additional forced objectives, like grabbing a certain number of remnants to ‘buy’ the cost of fuel to even make a trip and back worth it for the warband. It’d also be nice if additional caches could be found from killing stuff, and I’d appreciate a rework of the roaming boss mechanic.

The ones that could be hunted down should be announced by the mission handler as an additional objective, since they drop remnants equal to an objective (with a streak bonus for monsters killed in the same zone, up to 250).

But for me on Auric I’d like even more tempo, since I use this to assauge my want of a genuine 6th difficulty. There’s way too many garbage enemies and gunhands in Auric, so fighting like 30% elite enemies and constant bosses is nice. I think Auric should have even faster patrol size growth, 3 monsters roaming max instead of 2 and much more frequent terror spawned monsters. Like to the point where you get a forced boss fight every objective cleared on the timerless one.
