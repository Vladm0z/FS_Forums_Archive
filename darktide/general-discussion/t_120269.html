prolegitman • 2026-03-25 16:46:35

The melta gun would do exactly what it says on the tin. Melt things. They may have similar jobs but it’s role is suitably different enough to a plasma guns to warrant implementation.

Using the SM1&2 cone example gives you an anti carapace weapon with a large aoe. Alternatively a beam variant or weapon special would focus the melta gun for a narrower and condensed shot with more stopping power and cleave. In a game where the armoured carapace enemy set that exists is spammed constantly within that LoS of a few dozen meters that you mention, the melta gun becomes an obvious choice. The higher up in difficulty you go the more return on investment it provides.

Maulers and rangers? melta gun.
Crushers? melta gun.
Bulwark shields? melta gun.
Rotten armour? Believe it or not melta gun.

Regarding the tabletop I can only speak from the viewpoint of someone who worked at GW so I might be uniformed on some things. Against such a list the weapon you’d likely be bringing is neither the plasma gun or the melta but instead the cheaper and more versatile grenade launcher ( Which is also missing from Darktide) Nonetheless, melta guns have been universally good at cracking armour across the editions. Depending on the edition played a case could be made for the melta gun. Being str 8 the melta gun would be instant deathing all those multi wound renegade ogryns and characters, certainly very useful.

If you’ll allow me to be slightly tongue in cheek here regarding the Long las…The eternal question for the Long las is what would it do that a Helbore, boltgun or revolver doesn’t already do with twice the rate of fire and comparable ranges?
