lMarcusl • 2025-12-05 11:30:23

The Stimm Supply ability does not count as stimming yourself currently, so you won’t get any Chemical Dependency stacks out of it. You need to just straight up jab yourself with that stimm for it to work, so you need to either reduce the cooldown with Sample Collector if you have a long cooldown stimm or you need to make a faster stimm. With a 75 s stimm and 15 s duration you’ll miss the 90 s Chemical Dependency window. If you increase your stimm duration by an extra 5, you need to take that into account in your stimm cooldown for Chemical Dependency too, cause stimms don’t start cooling down until they run out. So 5 s of extra stimm duration is 5 s of extra cooldown, for the purposes of Chemical Dependency stacks.
