Honestly, despite all this, the MK VI is still my favorite melee weapon. The recent change is actually a big deal for me personally - canceling the energized state on stun used to be a death sentence.

But it’s definitely off-putting to see other melee weapons performing same or even better without needing any activation, or having other penalties.

There are three things I’d like to see improved:

  • Performance of regular attacks - The sword should feel usable even when it’s not energized.
  • Blessing variety - Reducing the heavy reliance on energized attacks would help, but Power Cycler would still be the obvious choice. I think the sword should have three energized attacks per activation by default, and Power Cycler could add a fourth, along with some bonus scalable with the blessing level. I believe Blessings should support different playstyles, but not be the main factor in a weapon’s overall power.
  • Mobility - To put it simply, I hate relying on Weapon Specialist. I don’t want my primary weapon’s survivability to depend on ranged kill, that makes no sense. It needs to be consistent. Otherwise, I don’t care if it’s native behavior or requires investment in the talent tree.
