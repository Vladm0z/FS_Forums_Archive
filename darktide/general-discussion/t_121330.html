Hi all,

I just recently started playing, but I had a few ideas I wanted to share in case the devs see them on the forums for new classes to be added.

  1. Pariahs - As Blanks/Nulls/Pariahs are ideal against psychic threats, of which the situation on Atoma is definitely one, going by the presence of Daemonhosts and the like, getting a playable Pariah would make a lot of sense. This could either be a Blank of somewhat lesser power, or, ideally, a Sister of Silence novitiate (at the point where she could, but has not yet, taken the Vow of Silence. For the banter). A Culexus would also be an option, but getting a full Omega Minus in there might be a tad overkill for the way the game is meant to function. Not to mention, they might be a bit too dangerous for allied psykers to just be around, since a Culexus likely will not care in the slightest.

  2. Sanctioned Xenos - As the player characters are working directly with the Inquisition, a playable sanctioned Xenos would be amazing to have. This would most likely be an Aeldari - Rangers, Corsairs and Harlequins ought to be the most logical candidates, but I could also see Drukhari wyches (particularly if they are of the Ynnari faction by now) that just relish the challenge of showing the filthy Mon Keigh how it’s done.

  3. Sisters Hospitaller - Not only could they serve as a dedicated healer class with powerful buffs, they could additionally deploy medical countermeasures to Corruption, or weaponize their medical knowledge in various ways. As Sisters Hospitaller are known to research all sorts of pathogens, them being present would also be very sensible and reasonable.

  4. Genestealer Cultists (Magos or Kelermorph) - Now, I know what that sounds like, but hear me out. Genestealer Cults have been known to rise up in defense of their world in cases of Chaos incursions on several occasions, and them engaging in a temporary truce with the Inquisition might be possible. If Zola can strike a deal with underhive gangers, why not tap into every possible resource a hive such as Tertium might hold?

  5. Hive Spyrers - While originally mentioned for Necromunda, Spyrers with their unique armors would make for highly mobile gameplay by comparison, bringing in another speeder like the Hive Scum. Let the nobility shoulder the burden for once!
