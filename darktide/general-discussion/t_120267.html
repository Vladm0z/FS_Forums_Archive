KJeraDRegular • 2026-03-25 14:19:42

My list would be:

  • Adventure gamemode is identical in both games,
  • Mortis Trials is an equivalent to VT1’s Last Stand and Vermintide 2’s Fortunes of War modes, with a spin in terms of featuring randomly picked modifiers for players to alter their experience throughout the length of one encounter,
  • Havoc is means of implementing a challenging type of content without needing the modded realm, effectively being an equivalent of VT2 Weaves in terms of featuring a casually competitive format,
  • Expeditions is a short-form indirect equivalent to VT2’s Chaos Wastes (also more diegetic, i.e., with no interface intermissions between gameplay segments), only without rogue-like elements in form of on-the-go build-making and otherwise focused on Adventure fundamentals, with more scattered terror events and no “main” objective to fulfil,
  • VT2’s Chaos Wastes doesn’t have a true equivalent; not yet, maybe.
