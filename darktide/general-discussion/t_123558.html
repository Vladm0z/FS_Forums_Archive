JDLS • 2026-07-03 08:27:59

I have tested this extensively and this is the fastest clear time on that test group. It is also the highest consistent DPS for a few reasons. (Also the weakspot damage does help a little but yeah its not a big deal, the auto aim is also pretty helpful for clearing out shooter groups, you just rake your fire across their general direction and end up killing all of them)

  • Crit damage is actually pretty good on the arc rifle. 1.6x this is a lot when your crit chance is 55-60%.
  • +30% fire rate is a massive boost.
  • +25% damage is also a very good boost and quick to build.

Yes on the surface arc rifle does not seem to work well with ACD but it does I promise you. This is the best build for maxing overall damage on the arc rifle.

A few reasons why VE doesnt work out very well:

  • One and done, you spend all 3 stacks at once, where as ACD can be toggled on for only a few seconds spending tiny amounts of capacitance for a pretty good boost. This saves you from running much capacitance talents.
  • The 3rd stack on VE is useless for arc rifle so every time you activate VE on 3 stacks your just wasting a stack.
  • VE is short range and isnt a active effect. You activate it and only the enemies near you are effected. If there are many enemy groups coming in you will end up with no bonuses at all and only +15% fire rate. Meaning it just rapidly drops off compared to ACD if you have a large amount of enemies coming in.

Arc rifle also only arcs 3 times. If you just rake your fire across a group you electrify all of them and get your full arcs after only 4 shots. So even with bad luck and no random arcs happening off the start, your really not getting much extra from VE (Really just a buff to your first 4 shots). Really the biggest boost from VE is the direct damage it can deal, but that get rapidly out paced from the much longer ACD boost.

Overall VE is better with arc maul and a more melee + ability spam focused build. ACD works out better for min-maxing damage with the arc rifle.

VE is a good ability, just not better for arc rifle.
