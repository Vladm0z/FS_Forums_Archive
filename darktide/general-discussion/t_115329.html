Hi all, first post, been playing for close to a few hundred hours now.

I just wanted to share, and solicit, important lessons that may not be obvious to new/intermediate players. Lessons I mostly learned by helpful folks in game after I made awful mistakes.

  1. It’s typically best to push poxbursters, slide back, and let them explode. I used to just shoot them before they got too close to the group, but sometimes when they were already too close to the group, to predictable and disastrous effect.
  2. Deployed ammo boxes only have 4 “refills”. If you shoot half your ammo, and pull another chunk of ammo, you just took what could have been another full ammo pull for someone else.

Any other little lessons, pet peeves like this you can think of? Things that drive you crazy when others do it, but they are probably just ignorant of unexplained mechanics, and not actually malicious trolls.

  1. Burster can be pushed twice on faster weapons, which will push it further. Though it still would be best to additionally dodge/slide back.
  2. Don’t rush ahead, open crates for ammo pick ups, med and ammo crates. Rushing triggers patrols, bosses, hordes.
  3. Don’t revive under heavy fire, you just die. Don’t revive with many enemies around and low stamina, you will likely die.
  4. Use stimms, poorly used stimm is better than not used at all. Except green one, peak of it’s use is when one wound fully corrupt, it clears it completely. But it’s not always beneficial to wait till someone’s wound will corrupt, not going down at all is generally better, not always though. Overall if there’s stimm to hold, it’s this one, others need to be used actively and preferably appropriately.
  5. Kiting back helps in hard situations. Use barrels. Red barrels don’t do direct damage but push away when you close enough, use them for movement. Same can be said about pox ogryn charge, it does damage but not a lot, but pushes far, can be used to advantage, but also can kill by dropping you into abyss.
  6. Red barrels fire damage health directly, bomber flame damage toughness 1st. Jumping helps slightly against it, but best way is to climb some crate, terminal it will likely save from it.
  7. Be extra mindful of blowing up barrels when ally nearby them.
  8. Don’t try to heal martyrdom zealots,the ones that got many empty or corrupt wounds.
  9. Most dangerous enemies are specials area deniers, flamers, bombers, kill them 1st. Many even in aurics ignore them entirely or prioritize something else. Among elites only reapers have a slightly similar danger.
  10. Trappers are worst disablers, players usually know that. What they don’t do is save ASAP trapped ally, it takes just a second without revive speed and faster with it. No need to kill trapper 1st after she used net, if possible, kill enemies attacking trapped ally, while you running to save him.
  11. Mutant second most dangerous disabler, but many ignore them. It can be useful, like save from dog, but also really easy can start chain of events the will lead to defeat. When he grabs you in open space, he beats you then throw you. When he grabs and hits obstacle, with t exception of medicae, he will just throw you, use it to your advantage. Between pox hound and it, kill mutant, because if they both go on you and dog gets 1st, mutant can save you, but like I said, get grabbed by mutant and there’s immediately various possibilities for you to die. Bosses also save from dog, burster may save form dog, but none of the enemies save from mutant, while it enables them to end you.
  12. Bosses very weak, so often it’s best to leave them for last, though not always, big exception can and often should made against burgle, because it’s another area denial enemy.
  13. Use field improvisation talent on the veteran.
  14. Check what is behind you, during horde spawn there’s always something.
