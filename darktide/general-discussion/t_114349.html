this is what brave browser said on the subject:

The Kroot Kill-Broker is a character archetype from the Warhammer 40,000 universe, specifically part of the Farstalker Kinband kill team, which is a mercenary unit of Kroot warriors. These Kill-Brokers are leaders of farstalker Kinbands, tasked with breaking off from their war spheres to find potential genetic stock to return to their kindreds. They are known for their role in tracking down targets, often using their keen senses to detect genetic material, and are frequently associated with the Imperium’s Inquisition, which employs xenos mercenaries like the Kroot for specialized missions.

In the context of Warhammer 40,000: Darktide, speculation has arisen that the upcoming second class may be a Kroot-based character, potentially a Kill-Broker, based on datamined codenames indicating a “broker” class. This theory is supported by Owlcat Games’ emphasis on a Kroot lady companion in promotional material for The Dark Heresy game, suggesting a possible joint release. The Kroot Kill-Broker’s abilities in the Kill Team game include strategic ploys like “Prey,” which enhances ranged weapons, and “Bound,” which improves mobility. Their lore aligns with themes of scavenging, tracking, and genetic acquisition, fitting the game’s narrative and the “rations event” hinted at in recent developer commentary.

While some community members speculate that the class could be a Kroot Kill-Broker due to the “broker” codename and thematic connections, others argue against it due to technical challenges like height class issues. Despite this, the Kroot class remains a popular theory, with potential for unique weapons such as Kroot rifles, scatterguns, and tanglebombs, which can be used in melee. The class is also speculated to be released around December 2, 2025, based on the game’s patch schedule and the official release date of Darktide on November 30, 2025.
