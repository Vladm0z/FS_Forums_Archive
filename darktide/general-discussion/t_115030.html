and I for one think it would be extremely cool to get personalities based on the modes of persuasion, ethos, pathos, and logos:

-
Logos: the more cruel and spiteful type akin to the arbites maul, the typical mechanicus personality of despising organics and more devoid of any other type of emotions, and showing a tiny bit of more respect to the arbites for being more logical to handle the situation of tertium but doesn’t show that much respect to them due to being organics, and see rising through the ranks quite easy as they believe they are more logical than anyone else.

Pathos: the more emotional and kind or maternal type, kind of similar to the savant psyker, those who didn’t remove/restrict their emotions by their augmentations, and believe they’re outcasts from the Mechanicus for being too empathetic, especially towards organics, or not living up to the admech’s standards, and they view the rejects akin to a worried mother or a upbeat father having to deal with a bunch of jolly/unstable crackheads for children.

Ethos: a more jack of all trades, not too emotional, or spiteful or devoid personality, quite akin to what the arbites fatalist is like, they have to focus on trying to fix the outbreak of the blight on tertium and joined the mourningstar to show they can be worthy of rising through the ranks of the mechanicus into higher tech priesthood, and are willing to take any necessary steps to achieve it, even if it means to be as devoid or rational in their actions to see these goals achieved.
