Introduction

Introducing the Mobian Domain Ordo Pronatus (Non-Militant order), “Daughters of Messelina”, like most Imperials from the Mobian worlds the local Sister Conclaves keep the Imperial Saint Messalina as one of their major figure, but like other people from the domain, they also aren’t knowledgeable on what Saint Messalina fought for, nor what they truly was (A Psyker)

As a minor ‘non-Militant’ Order the Daughter of Messalina do not have Repentia Squads or Mortifier and Anchorite Engine, instead they’re becoming Sisters Oblatia, considered dead to their Order, but in the manner of a Dwarf Slayer, seeking it, in service of the Emperor and the Imperium.

From this point they would then be treated as disposable agents, but their past training make them suitable to further training under the guiding hand of Hestia.

Forced into seclusion and near mindless worship of the Emperor let them manifest lesser miracles, miracles that the Emperor bestows to the ones that are to face his enemies.

Why not …

  • Repentia: No ranged weapons allowed; very restricted in weapons; quite close to what Zealots can do
  • Celestian: Higher ranked, too valued
  • Seraphim: Map issues and higher ranked
  • Hospitalier: Too much emphasis on healing

Tree

Going back to the ‘traditional’ tree style with the only unique point being the order of the exlusive nodes:

  • Aura → Blitz → Abilities and finishing with Keystones with Aura possibly having side nodes.

Faith

Resource similar to Peril on the Psyker, used to power Blitz, talents, weapons but also the Aura.

Aura

Base: 2 tiered Aura, with weakened continuous effects and effect tied to specific state of Faith level

  • Repent
    Constant: Increase melee attack stagger
    Low Faith: Increase melee attack cleave and damage
  • Light of the Emperor
    Constant: Grant stagger resit
    High Faith: Grant health damage resist
  • Pure Faith[3]
    Constant:
    High Faith:

Blitz

Like the Psyker, cost Faith, unlike the Psyker, instead of venting Peril, the Sister generate Faith.
With 2 different things that are new:
→ Burn: Sister are able to use their talents without Faith, but will generate Corruption[4]
→ Martyrdom: Generate more Faith per missing Wounds or health percentage (could be better tied to a talent ?)

  • Blessed Strike
    Switch to your melee weapon, imbuing it with Faith making it glow, one next attack, increase the attack’s impact and damage
  • Spirit of Martyr
    Work like the Warrior Priest’s ability, creating a shield around the player, or around one of its allies.
  • Purge the Unclean
    Highlight an area on the ground, holding it increase the size, but make it cost more Faith, on release cause fire damage that deal increase stagger on Infested and Unyielding enemies.

Abilities[5]

Like usual, tied to Cooldown, could also have the option to use them faster at the cost of a wound

  • Leap of Faith: Movement ability highlight an area before leaping upon it and causing damage and stagger in an AoE
  • Banner: Plant a banner that buff allies and debuff enemies around it until it’s destroyed.
  • Catechism of Fire: Release a whirling firestorm that tagger and burn enemies while moving away from the player
