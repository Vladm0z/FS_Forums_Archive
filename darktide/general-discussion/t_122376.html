Since apparently private, or solo matches ain’t going to happen, I’d suggest as an alternative that mission initiators can kick any joining players without vote required.

Either rushers and rambos, who are doing their own thing, not maintaining squad cohesion, etc. while I myself want to look for specific items (i.E. grimoires) to collect.

Or pillagers, who pick up any ammo they find after having only one, or two shots fired, often very obviously purposefully with the intention to keep myself and others from restocking, and thus running out of ammo eventually, so they can hone in all the specialists/elite kills.

As a matter of fact, my block list becomes larger, and larger every day. At other games I very rarely blocked someone, respectively did not ever block anyone at all. But something like in this game I never have experienced before, at least not in that intensity.

While I am aware that this could be abused in a very arbitrary way, imho this is better than what is currently happening on servers right now.
