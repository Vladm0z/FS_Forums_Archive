FrankJack • 2025-07-21 03:23:28

7 enemies is.. actually really easy

keep levelling up, the hordes will become a lot bigger than in the early difficulties.

there are several places you can do it, you’ll find ledges here and there but here’s a few notable ones:
-Chasm logistratum has a bridge to the left of the first room that’s really really narrow, its extremely easy to do it here.
-Chasm station HL-16-11 has a good ledge near the end of the first bridge, you can throw off enemies here and get it.
-Clandestium gloriana’s whole first room is a massive ledge where you can even throw enemies down with some of the maps barrels in addition of your powers or even melee weapon, again, its only 7 enemies, push once and you’ll have it.
-Refinery Delta-17 has spawn points around the last room where some enemies come from, if you’re at a slight angle, you’ll push them off and get the penance.
-Silo cluster 18-66/a has a ledge in the.. ‘‘first room’’, just before the first medicae station, especially to the left and right are ledges (enemies will often spawn inside of the left’s side structure) you can use your ability if a horde comes out and bam, ez penance.

All of this becomes easier and eaaaaaasier the higher the difficulty.
