Taranok • 2026-07-17 21:02:45

I’d say rather than weapons having unlimited ammo being broken, weapons that have limited ammo just aren’t satisfying.

The game exists in 3 states. Completely unlimited ammo (which is fun.) Horrifically limited ammo (to the point where using it is worthless) and limited ammo but functionally unlimited (where most ranged builds exist, and if you can’t get here it’s not worth using.) But there’s a lot of talents that pretend to give ammo (Veteran and Skitarri both have one) but they provide such little ammo it’s just generally not worth it (though there’s still a good case for Vet because it’s 1% to up to 4 players per proc, not just personal.)

Grenades are in a similar but overall worse spot. And it’s truly the difference between ‘Ammo system’ and ‘Stamina system.’ And I’d much prefer ammo (and grenades) operated on the rules of stamina systems instead of ammo systems because it just, generally, opens up more design space for interesting decisions. And also not getting mad at that one guy on the team who constantly goblins up the ammo or accidentally stealths the horrifically limited grenades. Or, to put it another way. Res bot with 4 charges? Potentially OP until you run out. Res bot with a 1-2m cooldown but only 1 charge? Interesting and not as overpowered while also being consistent in expected power over time.

Hell, most builds on any class with access to grenade 3d printers will typically run it if they can, and the grenades when balanced around it are fine without being overpowered.

But within the current context of the game? I kinda disagree on flame staff being overpowered. Sure, it’s powerful. It’s certainly annoying if the flame staff psyker doesn’t give you the option to have fun because they’re constantly looking all directions. But I just don’t see it as fundamentally more powerful than something like a relic blade, or an arc rifle., Even a good shotgun/autogun build will rival it just fine and not run into serious ammo issues while demolishing just as many enemies. But in the greater context of ammo-limited classes? Well, I already gave my opinion. I’d rather every class had some way to reasonably sustain ammo at all times, especially if it’s unique to the class, than tear down the only truly good staff Psyker has. (Trauma is okay since it provides good CC, voidblast and electrokinesis are generally considered bad outside of electro’s primary attack crit builds.) I’d like to see more ranged ogryns that don’t vomit all their ammo and then be starved for the rest of the game, and find that a more interesting solution.
