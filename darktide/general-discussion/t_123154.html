Noospheric Command is a Sleeper Hit

20 Posts • 483 Views
J
JDLS • 2026-06-27 08:38:41

TLDR:

Infinite ammo aimbot infantry lasgun vet in your back pocket that costs practically nothing to summon whenever you want.

+Killing elites/specialists without switching to your gun.
+Saving massive amounts of ammo.
+Free bons damage for doing practically nothing.
+Staggering dangerous enemies.

-Slightly less ability use.

Basically blades of faith but 10x easier to use, way more damage output, works on maulers/plasma gunners, with way more ammo, and you still get to bring a pretty good grenade.

Details:

In all of my auric games so far I have only seen maybe 2 people running Noospheric Command. I feel like people are seriously under valuing the damage this does considering it is almost entirely free to use. Now yes if you just want to spam Chordclaw as much as physically possible its not ideal, but I dont feel like you actually need to spam chord claw as much as possible generally.

Noospheric Command turns your servo skull into a infantry lasgun veteran for 2 seconds. Dealing ~300 damage per shot with a good fire rate and applying fire stacks. It also penetrates certain enemies hitting up to seemingly 3. Oh and it has very high stagger able to knock gunners out of attacks and even slow down ragers. Oh and it seems to prefer targeting elites.

For this the cost is 25% capacitance. This seems like a lot if you are used to normal abilities, but then you realize you can easily generate that back in 2 seconds under many circumstances.

To compare this to Arbitrator Dog, well, arbitrator dog can kill one enemy in ~2 seconds plus travel time, meanwhile the servo skull can easily kill 3+ elites in one activation, and all you need to do is click your ping button every few seconds while doing melee. The capacitance cost is negligible and unless you are using it fully on cooldown you will barely notice the hit to your capacitance.

And all that by itself would already make it a useful thing to consistently use. Essentially spawning a infantry lasgun vet for almost free. But then you realize it doesn’t use ammo. You have a entirely infinite ammo infantry lasgun with aimbot sitting in your back pocket and all you need to activate it is spam the ping button while looking vaguely in the direction of any elite you want deleted.

This is incredibly useful when using a plasma gun or even a arc rifle or galvanic rifle. Saving ammo and time switching to your gun when you just need to remove a sniper or bomber, or even just getting a gunner to stop shooting at you. If you consistently use Noospheric command instead of switching to your gun you will save at least a ammo bag worth of ammo on the low end. I really cannot think of a situation where not taking Noospheric command is a good idea.
