I don’t really have a point to make here, but I hope this data might help other players make theirs. This is going over every skill tree and rating each node based on how easy their effects are to get.

  • Passive (Blue): The effect is always active.
  • Simple (Green): The effect is easy to trigger and will either happen naturally or can be accommodated for without too much fuss.
  • Complex (Yellow): The effect is rare under natural circumstances or requires the player to adjust their playstyle significantly to get the full effect from it.

Your view of the category talents fall in may disagree, and I might not even be consistent with how I categorize them - this is simply intended to be a discussion starter (and an analysis on why nobody takes the Veteran keystones (and also, in other words, please don’t yell at me)). Type indicators will be dots on the top-left of the node. Also remember that Complex doesn’t mean bad - complex nodes can be really powerful, if trickier to use.
