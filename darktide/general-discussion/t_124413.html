ADS, Fanning but isn’t there still potential for a 3rd one ?

One of the strength of the Stub Revolver is that they’re quite well animated, having both staggered reloads (one for each missing round) and Speed Loaders.

image1043×1087 76.1 KB

An idea:

Special Round:

  • Easiest to animate (all of the components are already in game)
  • Slightly weaker stat wise than the Zarona
  • M1: Remain the same
  • M2: ADS
  • M3: Lose the revolver bash and instead load a special round into the Revolver (using the reload used when only 1 round is missing, can be done multiple times to load multiple rounds, but take time to do so.
  • R: Reload using the Speedloader.
    Regarding what they could introduce as special round for this new Stub Revolver (Going by special ammunition from Necromunda, DH and RT):
    • Rad Ammo: Debuffing ?
    • Static Rounds: Shocking rounds, causing the enemy to be stunned in place (Would fit extremely well on Skitarii and Arbites)
    • Phosphor Round: Apply the Phosphor debuff (the one that the Skitarii brought
    • Dumdum: Anti-Flesh
    • Manstopper: Anti-Armour
    • Warp Round: Cause the target to become insane, but illegal for the most part.

Otherwise, which would require new animations but could be interesting ideas

Dual/Paired:

  • Require a lot of animations
  • Axed more to having more ammo but weaker damage wise
  • M1: Shoot alternatively each Revolver
  • M2: Braced, shoot both Pistol more quickly
  • M3: Bash
  • R: Speed-loader reload
    Would require more work, to the point of feasibly being considered a separate weapon outright

Revolver and Power Fist

  • Require new model, but without asset being able to be reused
  • M1: Shoot
  • M2: Braced
  • M3: Power Fist hit, holding to Charge the fist more
  • R: Speed-loader reload
    Would require most work, to the point of feasibly being considered a separate weapon outright; but could share some animations with other potential weapons that use the Power Fist
