As the title says. Imagine we the Traitor Guardsman had some vehicles? A Sentinel walker springs to mind first, perhaps modified to be more Nurgley and lob out infectious blobs. From a gameplay standpoint a heavy cannon could be oppressive, so I’d suggest it work like this boss attack from Heroes of the Storm:

AKA, highlight an area, warm up, and then blast away, so players can see where it’s going to hit. And yeah, if caught maybe only Ogryn with heavy shield isn’t splatted.

I’d imagine it has less health than most monsters, but is mostly carapace armor.

Light tanks could also be plausible enemies.

While these could be planned encounters, I’d much rather see them added as a type of enemy who can simply spawn in certain types of areas, perhaps already patrolling so the players can decide how they want to take them down.

The tank could have a crewman appearing out of the hatch, so if you’re sneaky you can pick him off and perhaps deny the tank one of its abilities (perhaps a burst-fire lasgun or something).

If players have no heavy weapons, then have it so you can plant detpacks on the back of the tank! The rest of your team distracts while one player sneaks behind it.

Just spit-balling here. Working totally on theory-craft for fun. Figured it’s better than arguing over Veteran.
