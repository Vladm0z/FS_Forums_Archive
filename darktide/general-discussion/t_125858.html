BongoSkaggs • 2026-09-24 22:09:34

Seems like you’re asking for merely bad talents rather than uninteresting ones? The categories are pretty distinct. About 80-90% of all of the talents in the game are boring, regardless of their power level. And for much the same reason that 80-90% of blessings are boring. They don’t really define or alter your game play in any great way outside of just doing more of what you’re already doing.

For actually bad talents, coherency regeneration effects tend to be pretty awful. Coherency radius increases are also a perennial loser, with the exception of born leader. Same for “toughness replenishment on melee kills” talents, suppression talents, and backstab talents on classes that aren’t zealot.

This talent needs to give each weapon a unique effect, similar to Vet’s nade talent, like “extra crit damage for heavy stubbers, 20% long distance damage with ripper gun, 20% reload speed with kickback, rumbler nades now stick to any carapace armor, not just Ogryns etc”

Protectorate Protocol on Skitarii, because of “in coherency” requirement and “downed teammate” requirements. Even though I use 20% revive speed 20% damage reduction while reviving talent in the same part of the tree, but I have no idea who’d use this garbage talent.

GOD, Covering Fire on Vet, I don’t know how this talent stayed in the game for this long, it’s about time it gets outright deleted and replaced with something worthwhile, I don’t mind a new support talent instead of damage talent.

Covering Fire
“When you contribute to killing a ranged elite enemy,allies around you get 15% damage reduction against shooting enemies”

Vicious Offering on Zealot, because it requires a kill by heavy attack. It might sound like the right talent for Zealot, but if you check builds even most of competent people ignore it. On most weapons you have heavy into light (and viceversa) loops as the best way to deal with most targets (relic blade mk2 is light into heavy overheads, same with Crusher heavy into light or push attack). Meaning that every other kill gets no benefit from this talent, since you might kill the target with the light attack while USING THE CORRECT COMBO. It’s just stupid.

Ramping Backstabs on Hive Scum which only barely works with competent Taunt Ogryn on the team. The fact that it needs to be stacked without any misses makes it worthless. With Rampage active you’re already killing enemies in 1-2 shots, meaning you get 10% bonus damage most of the time, if any at all. If enemies target you face to face, you get no extra damage at all. If you hit a stray enemy who’s turned sideways or a random poxwalker walks into the cleave, you lose all stacks.

It’s like this talent belongs on Zealot but they gave it to Scum for no reason.

I could go own, but meh.
