Inferno staff, Flamer, Voidblast, Voidstrike, Eviscerator, Relic Blade, Chem Grenade, Shotguns with manstopper, Ex3 shotgun, PBB Rumbler, Mk3 Plasma, Immolation grenade & Force Greatsword are all strong options to get the penance. The most important thing is getting the opportunity for the penance since you have 3 team mates that are all just as capable of killing enemies as you are.

There are some maps with spots to get this penance more easily:

  • Mercantile HL-70-04 at the beginning event of the map
  • Hab Dreyko at escape sequence
  • Power Matrix at escape sequence
  • Warren 6-19 at escape sequence I think?

That’s just top of my head, you could probably do this penance on any map if you’re playing solo using MMT otherwise you’d have to get your team to cooperate to give you the opportunity for it.
