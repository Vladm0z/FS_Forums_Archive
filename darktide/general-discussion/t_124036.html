Cosmophase • 2026-07-14 00:03:11

These are in no particular order:

NoSkull: Removes the Skull icon from tagged enemies so it’s just the outline

Numeric UI: Displays actual numbers for ammo and such on the UI

Chat Block: Makes blocking automatic when typing in a mission

Emperor’s Lantern: Gives a small circle of light around you for lights out missions rather than it just being completely dark

Log Me In: Automatically skips the startup screens and will put you into the hub all in one button press

Killfeed Improvements: Instead of seeing the spam of a rager dying 20 times, it will combine the deaths of specials/elites into one - such as dreg rager x8 etc

Kill Tracker: Tracks kill combos on the hud - if you’re killing tons of enemies in a horde it will track the amount of enemies you kill as long as you keep killing enemies within an allotted time.

Red weapons at home: When you fully max out a weapon with the best possible stats it can be, it will turn red instead of staying orange

Less Annoying Ping: Turns the normal tag sound for enemies into the eye marker sound which will make tag spam much less annoying.

My Favorites: Enables you to mark favorites in your inventory, making it easier to find specific items and making sure you won’t accidentally delete one.

Recolor boss health bars: Gives each boss a unique colored name rather than them all being the same

Display Ping: gives you an idea of your latency

Crosshair remap: let’s you change/adjust the shape of the crosshairs depending on what weapon you’re using. For instance on my Psyker I used it to adjust the crosshairs for the voidstrike - when I hold the button down it turns into a circle, and when I press it normally it’s a small aimer

Player Deathfeed: let’s you get an idea of wtf just killed someone

Alternate Grenade Icons: changes grenade icons to reflect what blitz someone is using in your mission

Scoreboard: gives you a rough idea of how many enemies you killed in a mission

RingHud: Gives you a ring style hud around your reticule that you can adjust

Custom Hud: Let’s you adjust/turn off hud elements

Guarantee weapon swap: sometimes the game will bug, this helps

Guarantee ability activation: same with the mod above
