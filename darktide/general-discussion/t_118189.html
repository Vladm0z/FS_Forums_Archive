Molonious • 2026-01-28 18:58:57

Overall?

Mixed.

The game has a great core gameplay loop, magnificently captures the atmosphere of 40k, is visually stunning, and has a wide array of toys to play with.

Unfortunately just about everything outside of that, all of the game’s ancillary systems, are…bad. Not just in terms of implementation, but in terms of basic inherent conception, often built upon wreckage of poorly conceived and abandoned ideas but carried on with in some form anyway.

RNG based gear and items built off a failed Gacha implementation that doesn’t seem to serve a purpose, weapons with “80%” stats with no explanation as to what “100%” would mean and no plan to implement anything, a weekly Contract system that’s both frustrating to complete and largely worthless in terms of payoff resulting in it not fulfilling its intended purpose, painfully overcomplicated talent trees, byzantine and arcane game mechanics that are explained little-to-nowhere within the game or the game’s support sites and materials, multiple “harder than hard” modes that just split the playerbase and infuriate people with annoying gameplay and differing metas, all coupled with a terrible Cosmetics rotation all sourced from a Chinese E-sweatshop (with some truly terrible outcomes, like the Krieg cosmetics that look like a comic-con cosplay instead of the actual art and models) and somehow basically nothing for the new DLC classes.

Most damningly, for a Coop game, it’s relentlessly single-player in feel, with no coop elements like one might find in DRG or HD2 (no bar to gather round for minor buffs and psych each other up with, no pre-mission planning beyond maybe checking party member builds in 60 seconds, no Bring Doretta Home traditions, etc), players in the same party will be in different instances of the Morningstar, etc. all while the game doesn’t actually have a Solo mode.

It was released in an early alpha state at least 2 years early, seems to have stubbornly stuck to stuff it should have ditched and over-complicated things that should have been simple, and it shows.

The game has slowly morphed into something that is no longer recognisable as the franchise I fell in love with. I adore VT1 and VT2, and Darktide started off with a similar vibe but has become something distinctly different and, to my mind, infinitely inferior.

Pacing, atmosphere, the feeling of being a group of relative nobodies facing oppressive threats they have no right to survive, it’s all been sacrificed at the alter of turning the game into some high octane super hero power fantasy, as you wade through an absolute ocean of enemies who are completely inconsequential individually and bleed together into an amorphous mass of flesh for you to cut through.

Personally I FEEL the loss of the previous combat director. The current gameplay feels like it’s slowly turning into a cheap imitation without the weight or thought put behind it.

I’m not saying they’re wrong for doing that, it’s probably been a rather lucrative choice, and I’m sure I’m in the minority for absolutely loathing this direction.

So while I recognise at this point the game will likely never be what I hoped for, and that what it is now is probably preferred by most, I mourn what was lost.
