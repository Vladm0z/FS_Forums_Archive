KJeraDRegular • 2026-04-21 11:37:49
VonHeex:

Why is it possible to strike through walls, doors, and obstacles?

Because that’s how the game operates - how enemy attacks operate. Walls or other obstacles are never a consideration for melee attacks - these are mostly distance checks for players presence within a certain cone of attack and reach. If you are not dodging attacks, that cone/reach check is respectively wider and longer.

As to why can players attack through walls etc. - because it would feel immensely bad to get your weapon stuck on everything in game that’s action-driven first, then “realistic” to an extent.
