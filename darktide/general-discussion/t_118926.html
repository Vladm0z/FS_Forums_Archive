It probably feels like that to you because psyker has access to the most unique weapons.

Aside from that psyker feels very rigid in its tree choices. Left keystone is the best by far, top and middle section always look very similar to me. The only unique build is I occasionally run is an assail + improved psyonics setup.

Zealot has a lot of variety in its keystones and midsection. You have agile as a skill, soap and damage reduction for your team. You have a keystone which makes you nimble as a dagger on the clunkiest weapons in the game, one that allows you to go down as often as you want and reap benefits and the one fits all plain crit chance to everything you do. All equally good, all with their own set of nice befitting adjacent nodes.

This by the way is no comparison to arbitrators, with the craziest choice on keystones: exe stance in good, unkillable mode, and the one that sounds so bland in comparison I never tested it.

Vet used to have different styles but that is no more, now the only real choice seems to be what grenade you take.

Scum has a lot of number buffs. The only interesting thing I see on it is the possibility to generate multiple medi crates but I have yet to come across that or make it myself work

My time has come…

This is my bootleg Ogryn Weapons Specialist

It takes advantage of Ogryn Power, where you don’t need that many perks to perform well in melee and most weapons can kill hordes very easily. Sure you sacrifice some optimal perks, but you make up for it with a super dynamic smash n’ blast experience with free salvos and quick feet. And any build you never have to reload is a winner in my books!

The build is super flex, just take these nodes and a (ripper) gun of choice:

image184×305 62.7 KB

  • The Ripper fires bursts, which are free whenever Lucky Bullet triggers (3 for the price of zero!). You could use any weapon though (>1 bullet:clip recommended), I’m just Ripper-brained.
  • When you kill something in melee, you are more likely to trigger Lucky Bullet (and in fact you should do so regularly – both things).
  • When you kill something with ranged, you get a 3s movement speed boost which feels great on the big man. Places to be, rashuns to eat!
  • My melee weapons of choice are Paul/Branx as they are strong, versatile weapons even without perks to back 'em up. Bonus for anti-carapace since the Ripper is not armour-piercing.

Playstyle is mostly melee, with regular (free) bursts to give you some reach in the thick of things. I’ve taken it up to Auric, and while it’s not my top performing build I do bust it out a lot because it feels special and I like builds that don’t need ammo/reloads.
