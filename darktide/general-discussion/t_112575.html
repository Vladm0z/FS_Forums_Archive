Well, this is what I think is the best (in terms of melee)… I know they vary from build to build, but this is a general outline, IMO. I feel it’s one of the cheesiest Ogryn builds so far. CS if you struggle with toughness, though, so instead CS + SC or Thunderous. I feel that you don’t need Hardknocks, because the extra Rending nodes, plus the Brittleness blessings, really wrecks everything from armor to bosses & more. No need to waste tax nodes for just an extra 15% DMG, not worth it. In Havoc with enemies inflated health pools, so the extra rending & brittleness helps after some testing between having the perfect balance for armor & non-armor types. Great Cleaver, I found is better to get through everything instead of Impact, you get enough Impact with Slam for everything. In terms of Melee, this might be the strongest I’ve tested. Obviously, the Rumbler spam builds with BLO is better damage overall for the real cheese, but this is still very solid. Another side note is that Carapace on the club 100%, because I’ve seen people with flak & that is okay, but we all know Maulers have Carapace heads as well as Crushers, but that’s personal preference of course. Another obvious tip is always hit the head with everything light-spam-wise, that’s when your True Strike talent comes alive too for that extra punch. You could also go with Bullrush too, just personal preference as well. I just find Shout more helpful in Havoc’s myself. All-in-all, this is what really dominated games for me with killing power.
