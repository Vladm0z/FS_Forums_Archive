Jord • 2025-12-06 10:39:31

I do agree that it needs a change, but not in the way you suggest. In fact, I think the approach you are taking is part of the reason the game ends up with so many balance problems.

A better way to handle this, and honestly the entire game and its classes, is to give every option some form of regeneration and then balance them by how many charges they can hold and how fast they regenerate.

For example, take the gas grenade. Give it a maximum of 2 charges with a 120 second cooldown per charge. It stays very strong, but now you have to use it smartly.

For the rocket, give it 4 charges with a 45 second cooldown per charge. That alone would make it much more reliable against crushers. ( you could argue for it to add brittleness on hit )

For the flame grenade, 3 charges with a 60 second cooldown makes sense and follows the same logic.

Ogryn grenade, 2 charges 80s Cooldown. , you get my point…

I am not saying they should never be changed. Adjustments are fine. But every time again and again, enemy need a buff, weapons need a nerf, bugs happen when they change a damage number etc. It has created so many problems for the devs, us and the overall powercreep in the game.
