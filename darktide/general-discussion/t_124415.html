Please reduce the amount of new character grinding in time for the new class

20 Posts • 483 Views
F
Forblaze • 2026-07-25 13:03:08

So I recently came back to the game after a multiple year break, which meant I had three brand new classes to level from 1 to 30. Frankly, it sucked. I already know how to play the game and the low difficulties are extremely boring and it takes way too long to slog through. While many classes have nuance to learn, that nuance just doesn’t really matter in your climb to 30. Even when it does, the ability to slide, dodge, and push to protect yourself from getting hit is Darktide’s most important skill and is universal between the characters.

Obviously we don’t want to just cut out the leveling process entirely and hand new players a level 30 character, so my solution is simple: Every level 30 character you have gives you an additive 1x XP multiplier. If you’re on your second character, you get 2x XP. If you’re on your third character, you get 3x, and so on. This should dramatically increase the speed that experienced players get to 30. I suppose you can lower this if it’s deemed too extreme, but I think it’s appropriate.

Now, maybe we don’t want to flood the high difficulties with untested babes, so there’s one more piece to this. Playing a difficulty X number of times is a pretty poor way to gauge someone’s readiness for the higher difficulties. Darktide isn’t the most intuitive game to learn, and just because your team can scrape you off the floor enough times to get through the mission doesn’t mean you’re ready to move up. So my next solution is change the to gate moving on the next difficulty with a simple “Complete the previous difficulty without ever going down” penance. Not foolproof, of course, but nothing will be. This will allow players to climb the difficulties at a more appropriate rate and not arbitrarily lock the higher difficulties to grinding.
