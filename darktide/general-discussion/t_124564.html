Hello everyone,

I would like to suggest an accessibility feature for Warhammer 40,000: Darktide.

I have a friend who plays Darktide on PlayStation 5. He was born deaf and genuinely enjoys the game, but unfortunately he cannot experience it the same way as other players because so many important gameplay mechanics rely on audio cues.

He specifically chose to play on PlayStation because the DualSense controller provides excellent haptic feedback and vibration. Those features are extremely important for him and help compensate for the lack of hearing. Unfortunately, vibration alone cannot replace the many audio warnings used throughout the game.

Warnings for Trappers, Mutants, Hounds, Bursters, Snipers, and other special enemies are primarily communicated through sound. As a result, he often has to react much later than other players, even though his gameplay and awareness are otherwise very good.

On PC, there are community-made accessibility mods, such as Spidey Sense, that provide optional visual indicators for nearby threats. Console players, however, do not have access to these kinds of accessibility tools.

I would like to ask the developers to consider adding a similar optional accessibility feature to the PlayStation version, or even to the base game for all platforms. A visual warning system for incoming special enemies and attacks would make a huge difference for deaf and hard-of-hearing players.

This would not be about making the game easier. It would be about giving players with hearing disabilities access to the same critical information that hearing players already receive through sound. Players who do not need this feature could simply leave it disabled.

Thank you for taking the time to read this suggestion, and thank you for continuing to support Darktide.
