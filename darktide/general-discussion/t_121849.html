​With the new Skitarii class coming out, I’m honestly a bit worried. I know Skitarii are high-tier units in the lore, but I’m really scared Fatshark is going to make them completely busted in-game just to be “lore-accurate” or simply to boost DLC sales.

​We all remember the nightmare with the Arbites and the Scum. They were made ridiculously strong at launch. Sure, they eventually got nerfed, but those months of them being completely overpowered in every lobby seriously ruined my gaming experience. A new class should be balanced from day one. So when designing the Skitarii, please, absolutely no golden toughness. To be frank, the golden toughness mechanic itself is already broken and needs a massive nerf across the board anyway. And please, no infinite ammo BS like the Scum either. Even though they tweaked the Scum so you have to use melee to regen ammo, it’s still completely overpowered. Since they are a melee-focused class, it basically still means infinite ammo, which is just super unfair to every other class.

​You guys have the Arbites and Scum as clear examples of what not to do. Please learn from those mistakes and don’t repeat them. We want a fun, balanced Skitarii, definitely not another game-breaking OP character!
