I like the Crusher on Zealot because it gives me a lot of control. Unlike with most other weapons, I don’t need long attack combinations - I just charge, hit, charge, hit, and repeat.

It doesn’t do a lot of damage, but its stagger is really great, even against specialists. That lets me stay safe long enough to survive the horde around me. It also pairs well with the bolt pistol since it deals good damage and can be switched to quickly, letting you finish off a staggered enemy when needed.

I mostly play on Damnation, though, so I’m not sure how well it performs on Auric or Havoc. Does the Crusher still make sense there? Is there a way to play it similarly to how I do, or am I just wasting my time?

It’s much better on Arbitrator than on Zealot. Reduced Crusher (enemy) spam helped it alot, but if you want to use it on Zealot you have to use Grevious Wounds, otherwise it won’t stagger Ragers with heavy attacks.

For Havoc in particular staggering enemies can be problematic because many modifiers (Purple, Red, Stiims) give enemies stagger immunity or massive stagger resistance, there is also a lot of bosses and Crusher is not good against them. Spamming charged attacks will just get you killed. Overall a weaker choice for Havoc that locks you into spamming push attacks for clearing and has mid elite/boss killing potential.
