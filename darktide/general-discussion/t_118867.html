Folks are all abuzz about that under construction area in the Mourninstar and how it’ll lead to a new game mode like Chaos Wastes or something pertaining to the 2nd Hive City or Nox Alpha etcetera etcetera. But as neat as those things seem, I gotta say I don’t think I really want that stuff right now. Not that it’d be a bad addition but it seems like since Havoc and Mortis Trials were added, things like new standard maps and new weapons for the existing classes have dried up considerably as more resources/manpower are focusing on supporting the new gamemodes and even classes.

Back in 2024 we were eating fairly well when it came to new maps and weapons, particularly with the Grim Protocols and Blessings of the Machine God. But in the following year with Havoc getting further fleshed out since it’s debut in December of the prior year and Mortis Trials coming out in March, we’ve only had one single map throughout that whole year and we only got it at December, literally the last month. New weapons haven’t been all that great either, with 2025 only having a Relic Blade copy for the Veteran in the Falchion and another Bolter/Bolt pistol mark that scarcely get used - Not to mention how Veteran was left out of the beefier weapon update in Grim Protocols and they had to wait 8 f***ing months later.

I don’t dislike additional game modes or characters in totality, after all I’ve enjoyed the s**t out of Arbites/Hive Scum and Mortis Trials is kinda neat. But if you don’t care for Mortis/Havoc (which is a lot of people, especially for Havoc) or having to pay extra money for the classes (A valid reason for skipping them tbqh) then you’re pretty much stuck waiting even longer than usual for Fatshark to hopefully add something like new weapons/maps or even more enemies to the base game.

And I don’t know about y’all, but I’m getting a bit tired of having to wait for such things while ancillary game modes and classes get all the focus.
