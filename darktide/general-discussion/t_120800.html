I’m not sure whether I’m in the minority, or part of a (very) quiet majority, but yeah, I really enjoy Expeditions. It’s a nice change of pace compared to the usual missions, the equipment you can buy are pretty fun (I wish you could find some, like the mines or the voidshield generator, in normal missions as well).

But the last few days, almost every single game was the same: people rushing through the map, each on their own, ignoring sites of interest, and only picking up salvage. Once we reach 400 (sometimes “giving up” at only 380 or 390, so at least most of them are probably not using cheat mods that show you the location of all that stuff) they just beeline to the sanctuary door, buy everything and repeat, until the last sanctuary where they just quit.

That 100.000 salvage penance has truly become the bane of Expeditions. What’s even the point of grinding it at this point, when you can’t even get the one for 18 grid points yet the first place?!
