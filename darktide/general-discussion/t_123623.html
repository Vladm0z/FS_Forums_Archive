lMarcusl • 2026-07-04 12:15:17

That’s where all the good melee stuff is IMO. If I’m using an electrocute/arc weapon, I go top first, bottom second. If I’m not using an electrocute/arc weapon and I’m not going ranged, I go bottom first. How could you not? 12.5 % attack speed, 15 % damage, cleave and rending aura (to synergise also with any DoTs you apply), uninterruptible heavies with free 15 % power on them (great for transonics or arc maul where heavy spam can be your horde combo and your single target combo), plus built-in synergy with Chordclaw builds (cause chordclaw is considered a heavy attack). Also built in synergy for ranged builds with weakspot kill toughness gain and weakspot damage to combine with capacitance on weakspot kills. There’s all of 3 nodes in the bottom branch that I’d call weak: damage resist on sprint and slide, damage resist on toughness break and impact on pushes above 75 % stamina. Everything else is chef’s kiss for a melee-focused Skitarius.

If any branch is full of questionable nodes, it’s left side. Excess toughness sharing. Incapacitation buffs. Revive speed buff on a branch that already has a medicae skull. Conditional 3.3 % toughness replenish per second. 10 % stamina on dodge on a node that leads nowhere.
