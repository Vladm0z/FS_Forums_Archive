MeatShield01Regular • 2026-07-28 22:41:18

My 2 cents… Pickups should be shared, grenades, and ammo. Whenever any player picks up ammo, or grenades, they get shared to everyone.

Ammo caps should be removed, instead it’s just the amount of ammo you start a mission with, and same for grenades.

When a player grabs a small ammo pack, everyone gains X% of their starting ammo. X can be tuned for balance, as well as the frequency the packs appear in missions. No more ammo goblins, and no more weighing the effectiveness of everyone’s gun as to who should get it. No ammo cap makes it so there’s no waste, no real reason why someone can’t hold more than they started with.

Grenades would work similar, whenever anyone picks up a grenade pack, everyone gets X% of their starting grenade count. Keeping track of fractional grenades. If X is 25%, a HS with 2 rockets would get an extra every second pack, an ogryn with a big bomb would be every 4th, etc. Again, no cap so an ogryn who doesn’t use their first grenade, gets a second after 4 pickups. Balance is tuned by frequency and percent. No more who’s grenade is best grenade…

The only hitch I see is the regen talents, for ammo it’s not a big deal, but for nades it’s a bit trickier. Maybe cap it at 2x the starting amount, or once you hit the starting amount the regen stops, or slows down…
