Vet Observations:

Skill Tree Rework: “A”, Good Flow, Few Taxes, Forced Tax nodes not quite as generous as Ogryn or Arbites, but not terrible, lots of merged talents

New Weapon: “C”, not what I was hoping for and it probably won’t replace my daily driver, but not terrible. Calling it a “new” weapon is a reach, but it probably had the most existing resources to reuse. Not what I was hoping for after 8 months, but I’ll definitely try it out. I’m a bit worried that the weapons we were hoping for will end up class exclusives on new classes.

CDR Change: “F”, Comparing Tactical Awareness (specialist player kills) to Bruiser (elite player or team kills) and Psykinetic Aura (elite or specialist player or team kills) and the Devs moved Vet CDR firmly into last place among all classes. Best guess, it’s an attempt to combat shout, without dealing with shout. The end result is punishing Infiltrate users.

Overall: “B” Lots of consolidation. No real answers for Survivalist. Failure, intentional or otherwise, to alter trigger condition for Tactical Awareness is going to hurt Infiltrate disproportionately. Having three of the class CDR talents have wildly varying trigger difficulty, but the exact same effect was a bizarre choice.
