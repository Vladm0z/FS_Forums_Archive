As much as I love the Skitarii, some of of its weapons/abilities reinforce the trend that i have been observing in Darktide, a trend thats imho detrimental to its fun. Namely there seems to be less and less benefit for precision playing and melee engagement, and more and more emphasis on ultra fast spam of ranged AOE, what i think MrE in his youtube videos calls sloptide.

What im talking about is weapons that dont really benefit much from precision, and have such AOE power that the game must spam endless hordes , so in return if you dont have that AOE , you are cooked. Inferno staff, rumbler are some of the examples… But even with weapons like recon lasgun you are nearly as good just spamming the burn stacks without headshots.

For Skitarii its the advanced doctrine ablity and arc rifle. Why do we have an ability that auto headshots for us? Make it so it boosts the benefits from headshots, but dont remove the need for precise aiming alltogether. The arc rifle similarly is a spray and pray weapon that deals insane AOE dmg to everything, it mows hordes, mows elits, mows crushers/armored.

For some poeple it might be fun, i find it detrimental. I play and I find myself in that conundrum that i have most fun fighting close range with crushers/maulers but at the same time realizing i am putting myself at a high risk of insta death, that i kill them slow, and the best thing is just to back off and annihilate them with arc rifle.
