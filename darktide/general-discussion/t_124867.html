I Play on xbox and lately my good controller broke leaving me to use one with a left stick potentiometer thats going out (REALLY bad stick drift as well as a 12 to 1 o’clock dead zone). So i figured why try plugging in a keyboard and mouse and see if that works?

It did!

Kind of. . . . .

Are you bored with DT and want a bit more of what could be considered a “hard-core challenge”?

Look no further, just play on xbox with KB and Mouse!

You now have the added challenge of having to play the game while remembering to press a button on the controller, lest it punishes you as it turns off and freezes your game with the “controller disconnected” prompt.

Because you have your KB&M plugged in you aren’t able to use the controller to unfreeze the game and because fatshark is fatshark only roughly 80% of game prompts work with KB&M. So you have to use the controller to go to your xbox dashboard, quit the DT app and restart it.

I get actually dizzy with rage when it happens to me but I have seen some folks on here complaining about not having enough challenge?

So i figured there ya go guys! A new challenge for you difficulty seekers! Having to remember to press a button every 10 min doesnt SOUND hard till you are playing on havoc or high intensity maps, it can be easy for it to slip your mind.
