Vror • 2026-08-21 16:07:28

Zealot has 3 specific talents that have massive synergy with each other as well as stacking HP on top of it, that I like to call “Bermuda Triangle”, a.a uses this for massive safety on the Wholesome Zealot build he made (Fatmangus)

Screenshot_20260821-125508~2720×976 114 KB

The way it works is:

1- Restoring Faith regenerates 20% of the HP lost, so it benefits from more HP since it’ll be more HP to generate in the long run

2- Bleed for the Emperor creates insane burst DR opportunities, so things like bursters that deal a 100 garanteed damage will have it reduced down to 60 dmg, and so and so

These 2 already have synergy, since Bleed from the Emperor reduces the hp dmg taken, and Restoring Faith might regenerate just enough HP to put your HP about your wound, meaning that the next attack is most likely also gonna be reduced by 40%

Now we add

3- Faith’s Fortitude, which makes sure Bleed for the Emperor procs a lot more, not only because there’s 2 new thressholds, but also because it’s more likely that Restoring Faith regenerates hp over them

So essentially these 3 constantly proc off each other and help Zealot have a lot more HP dr, which means that when maxxing out on HP, You squeeze most juice out of it

Good for Havoc builds, makes you nearly unkillable on Auric

It’s normally better to not build like this if you’re playing with fast weapons (since you shouldn’t be taking that damage with fast weapons) or want to use Martyrdom (since you don’t benefit from being super tanky)
