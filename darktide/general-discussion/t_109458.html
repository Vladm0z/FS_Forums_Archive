TheRabidOgre • 2025-06-24 07:26:40

When the Arbites were announced I was excited, but a bit nervous about the Cyber-Mastiff. When I heard about the Lone Wolf talent I was seriously considering taking it.

Here’s the thing - I tend to hate pet classes. Not because I hate pets, but because I rarely see them implemented in games in a way that’s fun. Usually they fit either of two extremes where they’re really fiddly and require extra dedication no other class has to worry about just to be on the same level, or they’re just plain unreliable and have you frustrated at their AI more often than not.

My expectations were blown away. Right away the Cyber-Mastiff actually felt like my companion. The power of the class isn’t even the point here. It’s that I can direct my pet when I need to, but the thing actually plays smart when I’m not directing it. The first time I heard the “you’re about to be hit” sound effect only to turn around and see my Cyber-Mastiff pounce the guy who snuck up on me all on its own, I actually felt like this dog was my friend, not just a sloppy game mechanic.

Dog does solve issue of enemy dogs. Players love to scatter and run in different directions in quickplay for some reason. Even with trappers, even though your dog can’t untrap you, higher chance it will intercept.

With full arbites teams, the dogs are like multipoint defence shield around team. Noone gets trapped/netted now.

Liking a combo below:

  • Agni Shock Maul - Carapace, Unyielding, Skullcrusher and Opportunist.
  • Heavy bolter - Just reserve for monstrosity.

Zealot has like 7-8 damage reduction nodes, Ogryn has massive constant instant toughness regen. Arbites toughness regen is more progressive for only few secs, so it is quite tanky.

Bonuses tied to stagger so favours maul.

Bottom right of skill tree compliments it well and some others

  • 15% damage if enemy above 75% health
  • 20% damage to ogryn/monstrosity
  • Enemies staggered take 15% damage, 5 secs
  • +15% rending on staggered enemies
  • +15% melee damage first hit
  • Gain 15% damage for 5 secs after heavy hit
  • Weakspot guarantee 4 sec stagger.

So this class has somewhat Ogryn’s style of constant stagger and bonuses for it and keeping up mobility. Also similar TTK of Ogryn bonk of around 3 hits to Crusher heavy vertical of maul.

Pretty solid
