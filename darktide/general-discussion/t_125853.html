Flame staff psyker is the most egregious outlier in terms of power balance and seems to have the greatest (negative) impact on my games. Infinite ammo, infinite cleave, works on everything. Clutters up the screen with annoying flames for the entire game (I know there’s a mod for that, I don’t want to use mods). I can’t believe this weapon has been allowed to persist in this state for so long.

Rumbler ogryn, desperado hive scum, and arc rifle skitarii also come to mind, but they’re not quite as egregious as flame staff psyker imo. At least those are theoretically ammo-limited.

Stealth zealot is powerful, but I list it here more because it just breaks the intended flow of the game and the AI. This is also applicable to stealth veteran, but you don’t see that nearly as much probably because VoC exists.
