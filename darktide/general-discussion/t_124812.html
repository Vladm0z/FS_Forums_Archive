Perhaps, in a way, Fatshark no longer has devs who have the sense for what makes a game genuinely fun.

I believe the quality of their graphics and visuals, as well as the feel of character controls, animations, and weapon movesets, are all at a level that surpasses that of many AAA studios.

but, when it comes to actually making a game fun, that’s a different story.

Take Havoc, for example. There are modifiers that offer little to no enjoyment and that a large portion of the player base dislikes, tedious systems such as the Party Finder, and game modes like Mortis and Expeditions that quickly lose their appeal.

Looking at these things, it makes me think that being able to make a high-quality game and being able to make a game genuinely fun are not necessarily the same skill.

Fatshark undoubtedly has developers who excel at the former. But they seem to be lacking game designers who truly understand the latter, what players actually find fun, and what makes them want to keep playing a game over and over again.
