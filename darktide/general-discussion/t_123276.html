Alright boys, the DLC dropped. The gameplay is solid, and the melee weapons are tasteful (although I’ve seen some complaints about the balancing).

I do have a few pet peeves with the Skitarii ranged weapons, though. To keep it short: there are no variants, the weapon specials are boring, and there are too many small differences from the artwork and tabletop models for my taste.

First, I think everyone can agree that the special attacks for the Skitarii ranged weapons are disappointingly boring. We got three different weapons, and all three just use a weapon bash.

To be fair, a lot of ranged weapons simply have a bash or a flashlight as their special action. Still, I thought Fatshark was moving toward making weapon specials more interesting and unique. For example, the Hive Scum dual autoguns have a fairly boring special, but the dual pistols have a unique animation tied to a specific gameplay mechanic (a massive finesse multiplier that rewards accurate aim). Likewise, the needle pistol lets you switch toxins. While that isn’t the most exciting thing either, changing toxin types has a much bigger impact on gameplay than another generic weapon bash.

My second point:the lack of weapon mark qnd some aesthetic point.
For each weapon , i will go through my aethestic complain then propose one or multiple mark with a suggestion of special to it

Some of you might remember the thread I made before the DLC released. The points I raised there still stand. I understand that the weapons in-game are Moebian-pattern variants, but I’d still like to see weapon marks that are one-to-one recreations of the tabletop models or the official artwork(mars pattern if you like).

Galvanic Rifle

So, the Branx Pattern is basically a Garand in space. The drum magazine/revolver cylinder on the Galvanic Rifle is actually a power coil. Alright, why not? The Galvanic Rifle has always been something of a mix of different historical rifle influences(lee enfield, johnson rifle for exemple even maybe a bit of brown bess), and there was definitely some Garand inspiration in the original design plus I do like the blue light accent on the rifle.The weapon looks almost entirely right

However, I think they leaned too heavily into the Garand. To make it look more like a Garand, they added a lever on the left side of the rifle that wasn’t present on the original design, and they altered the breech, the rear receiver block and the iron sight embelished “stud” so they more closely resemble those of an M1 Garand. I am not a big fan of these changes even though I know it is a popular representation

I don’t even think that what we see on top of the tabletop Galvanic Rifle is meant to represent a breech at all. Here are side views of the galvanic rifle and a model who is closer to the tabletop model.

For these two, my main problems are again the aesthetic changes
For the arc rifle.
The branx pattern is a battle rifle pattern with a removing power battery.

image1650×928 130 KB

What I don’t like is the simplification of the artstyle (the simplified grip , the sightly less baroque look for the handguard, the stock), the lack of the oversized nozzle lever, and the removal of the stud on top of the receiver, which is now sort of part of the action that the Skitarius needs to operate while reloading. After that, it goes down into the receiver tough it is farther back than in the artwork.

I would like a way to keep the stud visible on the gun most of the time. If we decide that it is part of the reloading mechanism and that it usually stays up because the reload mechanism is disengaged when the rifle is used with a backpack, I would still like a slight alteration to the reload animation for the new variants.

Either the stud stays visible at all times and the reload mechanism for one of the variants becomes a front-to-back movement,

OR, to reuse the in-game animation, the stud would stay up while the gun is loaded, then drop down once the battery is empty. After reloading the battery, the Skitarius would raise it again using the existing animation, but the final part where it is slapped back into the receiver would be omitted.

image362×139 63.5 KB

Finally, since we already have the Voltaic Backpack in-game, when you look at it, it’s simply the addition of a Voltaic Power Unit to a standard Skitarii backpack, and there is no possibility of not wearing a backpack as a Skitarii. In the end, there are fewer technical hurdles to implementing a backpack-fed Skitarii rifle than I originally thought.

In terms of gameplay, there is already a way to imitate the effect of a Skitarii backpack with the ammo regeneration perk and the perk that reloads your weapon when it isn’t in use.

For a variant without a backpack, I could see a Mars Pattern Arc Rifle with heavier damage and a weapon special that charges a lightning bolt in the rifle, similar to the Plasma Gun.

For a variant with a backpack, I could see an ammo regeneration passive combined with a huge ammo reserve, lower damage, and a higher fire rate, while still allowing the battery to be reloaded by disengaging the nozzle with the lever.

As a weapon special, an overdrive mode for the Arc Rifle’s power feed, activated by using the nozzle lever (if it isn’t already used in the reload animation), could temporarily boost the fire rate at the cost of adding an overheat mechanic.
