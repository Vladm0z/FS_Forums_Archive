talents that give some weapons stagger and cleave that weren’t intended in their original design are an easy way to make weapons “meta”.

the devs’ plot is clear: want stagger, take the crusher; want cleave, take the relic; want a one-shot, go Hammer Time.
It’s fun to have a relic with cleave, stagger and one-shot capabilities, but it’s not fair, as I see it.

and of course, there’s a difference between a talent from the tree and a weapon blessings.
With blessings, you sacrifice some of the weapon’s efficiency to smooth out its flaws.
With the tree, you just add on top of everything, with no trade-off.

so I’d prefer to have no cleave or stagger modifiers in trees at all, for any class.
i still remember ogryns with the Mk III club that had colossal stagger, cleave and damage all in one package.
