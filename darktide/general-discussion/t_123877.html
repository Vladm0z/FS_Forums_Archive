I really like the new class but I feel like one of the primary factors driving me back to the old classes is the way their cosmetics function aesthetically.

With no way to match all the lights you either suffer looking like a rainbow robot or some sort of toucan, otherwise your best bet is to go all blue which is fine except it doesn’t feel like Me to me. Which is a shame when they give us so many cosmetic options to choose from compared to the other classes. My main build ended up using the las pistol to match my Stygies green eyes but that feels like a little less Skitarii than ideal imo (wish we had atleast one rad weapon.)

My other issue is the robes basically hang on your body like bell-bottom jeans which sorta works if you go for a 70’s disco vibe with the rainbow lights but it’d be so much better with some sort of physics flow when you move around. Plus even while walking in the mourningstar, but especially in game once you crouch or do anything, you can see that the whole booty clips through the robes since they don’t hang naturally. Very unideal. Looks like it smells.

I also think it’d be nice for some forge world alt colors for the deluxe and shop items since, as it stands now, you pay extra to limit your forge world options which Feels Bad.

I know they’re on summer break and more pressing issues like the combat doctrines capacitance bug need to be fixed, but I feel like if at least the first two of these changes were implemented it would make the class look a lot more put-together and aesthetically coherent.
