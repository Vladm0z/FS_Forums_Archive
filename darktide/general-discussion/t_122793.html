Destiny 2 is a great game. The problem lies with the people making the decisions.

  1. The thing I could never accept was the removal of story content and entire destinations. I’ve honestly forgotten whether it was Europa or another planet, but some of those locations were simply deleted. There was also a particularly memorable NPC who ended up dying. Completing his storyline rewarded you with a rapid-fire hand cannon, which was probably my favorite weapon in all of Destiny 2.
    I still don’t understand why they couldn’t structure the story as a progression system where, after completing content, players could freely revisit earlier chapters whenever they wanted. Something similar to how Final Fantasy XIV handles its story would have been much better.

  2. The grind became too much. I just couldn’t keep up with it anymore.

  3. The progression design often felt backwards.
    If I remember correctly, some of the best PvE weapons required completing PvP objectives, while some of the best PvP weapons required grinding PvE content. Maybe I’m misremembering some details, but I remember that design philosophy being incredibly frustrating.

Destiny 2 should have been one of those games remembered for decades. It’s a real shame that it ended up this way.
