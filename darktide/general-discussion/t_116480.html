I personally think that Hive Scum is a lot stronger than people say, and comparing boss kill times alone and in a vacuum ignores a lot of nuance of gameplay. Vet killing things with full focus target stacks and a melee with very poor mobility is perfectly fine. The prevalence of CDR and blitz regen sort of homogenizes the game a little bit in my eyes. It’s becoming less and less of a skill check to keep track of your cooldown rotation on certain buffs, abilities, and blitzes because you either have access to insane CDR, insane Blitz regen, or both. So people can just use these things without much thought because the uptime is too good. This is another part of the powercreep issue that no one really talks about.
