To clarify for OP:

If it says Hestia’s Blessings - need to reach the point requirement on the penance track

If it says another bunch of words, that’s the specific name of the class penance you have to do to unlock that cosmetic (they can be nested, as in some penances are only completed by doing other penances).

If it says Commissary - buy it with in-game dockets at the commissary counter by the barber shop on the MStar.
