Inumuro:

In fact, the very concept is confusing in the first place. How does a weapon being good, lessen your experience? You’re not on the receiving end. In fact, it’s quite the opposite. Your teammate having better gear can only benefit the team.

Let’s take the most extreme scenario. Say you’re joining a co-op game and you want to kill some bad guys with your sword, right? Then someone comes in with the Super Kill Everything Weapon 9000 and deletes everything before you get to hit it. Not fun, right?

Same scenario if your sword happens to be made of foam. You can’t kill anything with it. Also not fun.

This requires balance to achieve. Yes, balance is necessary in a PvE game. Dueling Sword was (and honestly still is, not even really addressed) overpowered because it was best-in-class at melee elite damage and still very good at crowd control, mobility, and defense. It’s a master-of-all weapon when it was supposed to be (in my eyes) a single-target killer that was mediocre at CC.

Overpowered weapons and characters are dangerous because if left unaddressed they can shape the meta and cause the whole game to be balanced around them. This results in a lot of power creep and eventually the numbers can stop making sense. A wide variety of weapons in Darktide are serviceable at least, some are just outright garbage, and a few are overpowered. Buff the garbage weapons, nerf the OP weapons.

I won’t hold up pre-Patch 13 DT as a paragon of balance because it very much wasn’t, but it had weapon stuff down very well. You’d mostly see lasguns and autoguns, alongside 1-2 anti-armor ranged options, balanced against solid melee choices, and while the classes were busted, weapons weren’t completely unhinged. When Boring Yet Practical dominates the lists it’s usually a sign of good balance where all-rounder basic kit is taken barring specialist builds for specific encounters, and that’s what we had.

We’ve since had that replaced with ds and knife hyper-mobility meta where it’s no longer the boring basics that can handle most threats but insanely cracked outliers that allow for nigh-brainless play at zero risk if you’re even moderately competent.

This is a bad thing. PVE does not excuse it.
