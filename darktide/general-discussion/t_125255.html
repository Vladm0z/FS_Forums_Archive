Dizzy721Regular • 2026-08-30 15:30:05

The decision to move away from the careers/subclass model and move to the tree model is one part of it. It’s not that picking 6 mutually exclusive talents with pre-defined career skills, auras, blitzes, and passives is inherently better than the tree model we have now. But as best I can tell, this decision was made after the game was released, after the game had been designed around the careers model and not something they were planning all along. To compensate for this major shift in how the classes play, enemies have been buffed in HP and density. And that’s really it.

It seems possible, if not likely, that if trees had existed since early in development that they would have made different decisions in enemy design (other than just c/p from VT2 and put in 40k setting) or overall combat design. You can somewhat see this in the plasma gunner, vanguards, and Packmaster, i.e. enemies added after the introduction of trees. The other good thing about doing it while the game is in development is that players aren’t used to a certain level of power or way of playing the game yet so when attempt to make changes you don’t provoke reactionary blowback that gets your game review bombed because it’s not out yet.
