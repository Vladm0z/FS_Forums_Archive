Well VT2 has 4 Expeditions

Fortitude, Courage, Determination and Citadel of Eternity

They’re essentially the same gameplay wise, 4 random maps + their final specific Arena (In Citadel of Eternity, it’s 3 random maps + 1 fixed map being the Citadel itself + the final arena), with 2 shops in each Expedition

One of the expeditions will also be affected by Belakor, changing one random map into a harder more rewarding map if their sidequest is done. This also rotates to different expeditions with time

The weekly run is just one of these 4 expeditions but with Boons and Curses from the get go
