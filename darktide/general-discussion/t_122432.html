This is a talent that seems to be completely slept on really any melee psyker build I have seen in a while and I really struggle to understand why exactly. That and the wildfire talent as well to an extent though that one is much worse.

Perilous Combustion

Applies 3 stacks of soulblaze on killing a elite or specialist in a ~5m radius. This is incredibly good at high difficulties especially on any mission that spawns waves of specialists, even without it, it turns shotgunner hoards that spawn on anything auric or above into tactical nukes.

Perilous combustion does not have a cap, its capped at the soulblaze cap and triggers off of soulblaze infinitely. This quickly gets out of hand when your dealing with large groups of elites/specialists.

  • Kill 1 elite = base 9dps to everyone within 5m
  • Kill 2 elites = base 32 dps to everyone within 5m
  • Kill 3 elites = base 65 dps to everyone within 5m
  • Kill 4 elites = base 106 dps to everyone within 5m
  • Kill 5 elites = base 152 dps to everyone within 5m

Etc. And that is base DPS not counting other buffs that can add up to around +75% damage. This is insane when you consider you can easily kill 5 elites with one good swing of the force greatsword, and then enemies that die from wildfire retrigger it and can cause it to go infinitely with no additional input potentially spreading across a entire hoard from one good attack. You can just go into the meat grinder, go up to the group of specialists, kill 4 of them and watch as the soul blaze continues forever.

Plus soul blaze is buffed by malefic momentum, deals additional damage to some targets and gets other buffs that melee psyker just naturally has while also providing buffs to melee psyker sword through malefic momentum.

From my play Perilous Combustion adds about ~100k-150k through a auric maelstrom game (currently returning from a long break and don’t have a havoc team).

Now yes switching to and using the inferno staff between using the force great sword can also basically do similar if not much higher damage. But running perilous combustion gives a very big damage buff and lets you not run the inferno staff.

I personally run Voidblast as it lets you stagger crusher/bulwark hoards along with just good infinite cleave damage on choke points. Its just a great staff to do crowd control and trivializes otherwise deadly situations. Sure this is less impactful in a proper team composition but in solo play or even just suboptimal team lineups? I wouldn’t give it up for anything.

Wildfire

Wildfire is definitly the weaker of the two soulblaze blessings but I still think it is under rated. Primarily just because of Soul Drinker and Maleific Momentum. Wildfire basically just means once enemies have soulblaze they have it forever as long as you are doing melee. I would be more impressed if you could somehow manage to lose soulblaze on any hoard you are fighting if you are using melee psyker with this talent.

With wildfire souldrinker is permanently up with no additional input required, and all you need to also just have +25% damage forever is for 4 stacks of soul blaze to do a final hit on a few groaners every now and then.

Yes taking wildfire is debatable as that last talent point is a really tough choice (at least on the build Im running), it probably means giving up warp speed if you want to be optimal, I just give up vulnerable minds because I love warp speed too much. Could give up Psykinetics aura or empathic evasion instead, but both of those are such crazy good utility that its a tough sell. Giving up warp expenditure could make sense just sitting on souldrinker toughness regen instead as it has basically 100% uptime. Giving up Vulnerable minds is definitely not the optimal choice but it is the most comfortable choice for me.

Im not saying its a easy or probably optimal choice but its definitely a very solid choice

Conclusion

Running Perilous Combustion and Wildfire gives you basically 100% uptime for Malefic Momentum and Souldrinker, meaning +5% crit chance, passive toughness regen, and +25% damage to your sword. And also gives you huge amounts of passive wildfire damage for just swinging your sword, arguably being a reasonable substitute for running the Inferno staff, allowing you to take Voidblast to make dealing with crushers/bulwarks easy.

I am just curious what people think of these two talents and of course the supporting talents of Malefic Momentum and Souldrinker. I do feel like basically everyone runs Malefic Momentum on melee psyker, though souldrinker is pretty useless if your not taking Perilous Combustion and Wildfire, I guess its probably still good if you regularly swap to inferno staff.
