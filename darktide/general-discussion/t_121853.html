i genuinely think game design might play a role in the decision to remove autoblock when chatting.

in vt2, there’s a few legitimate reasons to type ingame for strategic reasons, like “does someone know grims here” or “do we want 2nd grim” or “come carry tome” or “can i have that purple pot” or “heal me please i have nb”, “sorry the rat ran behind you” after ff, “come through basement” to take a path on an event, etc
in darktide, scriptures are a meme, grimoires are a meme, co-op mechanics have largely been streamlined and made less significant, heals are at large less important cause of how wounds and toughness work and heal stimms werent even in at launch, no ff to apologize for, events streamlined etc.

i’m not saying there shouldn’t be autoblock when chatting. but i can sort of see what their thought process was. people often only type to flame in darktide and they didnt wanna encourage that. i know i have seen people die while typing angry sentences many times

it’s also just a bit of a modern multiplayer game thing, devs want to discourage chatting to discourage toxicity in general. look at kf3, it didn’t even have text chat for years
