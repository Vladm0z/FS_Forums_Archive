CobaltNinja • 2025-07-21 22:39:32

My word of advice is to only hand out advice if someone is actively being terrible. Build critique will often be either ignored or maybe get you flak - I see it as a minor enough detail to ignore most of the time.

A few months ago there was a Veteran in Carnival 1 who kept running too far away and dying. Gave him a quick tip of “you’re moving too far away, it’s ok to be ahead but stick closer to us”, and while I didn’t get a response, I noticed his positioning drastically improve, and he didn’t get downed for the rest of the game because we were close enough to back him up.

I would also not give advice more than once a game, even if there’s more than one player. Appearances are a big part of handing out tips ingame and you throw too many (even if two total is too many) can make you seem like a powergaming backseater.

A little advice goes a long way, but use it sparingly. I often do not comment on 5-wound Ogryns and the like, since they tend to be competent enough anyways.
