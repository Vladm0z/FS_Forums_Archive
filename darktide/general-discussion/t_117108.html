So “cryptic” got leaked so now my mind’s running again. Can’t wait for the inevitable class reveal where we play as a brand new Explocator eager to prove themselves in the field (lol). But seriously, I’ve got high hopes this time.

Innate Passive: Machine Whisperer

If a Tech Acolyte is on the team, Data-Interrogator objectives progress 50% faster.

ARCHETYPE: TRADITIONALIST

This is the class’s default archetype, and focuses on area control and weakening enemies.

Active:Reawaken/Holy Recall

You target a square area on the field. Enemies hit with this ability using non-primitive weapons (e.g. ranged enemies, Maulers) cannot use Ranged Attacks and have their damage reduced by 50% for 7 seconds. Upgrade increases radius by 50%.

Blitz:Nanite/Scrapeater Grenade

Throwable grenade that applies stacks of Brittle in a wide area, dealing no damage or stagger. Upgrade increases Brittle applied.

Aura:Well Oiled/Killing Machine

You and allies in coherency gain 2.5%\5% attack speed.

Keystone:Administrative Access

You can tag an additional enemy. Killing tagged enemies grants +10% Cooldown Reduction for 4s, stacking indefinitely.

ARCHETYPE: BIOLOGIS

This archetype bolsters teammates and sows chaos in enemy ranks alike.

Active:Overclock

You target yourself or a nearby ally. Target receives a large boost to damage and speed for 8s.

Blitz:Neurological Override

Target an Elite enemy to mind control. They will fight for you for 15 seconds before dying immediately. Mind controlled enemies cannot take damage, and can be targeted by other enemies. Recharges every 60s.

Aura:Hotfix

You and players in coherency regenerate 1% toughness whenever you kill an enemy. (You do not regenerate toughness from teammate’s kills.)

Keystone:Optimizations

Gain a minor boost to most stats.

ARCHETYPE: SKITARII

This archetype focuses more on regular combat and boosting your own power to crazy heights.

Active:Quarantine

Deploy a heavy turret to fight for you that lasts for 20 seconds.

Blitz:Taze

Quick Throw. Your mechadendrite releases an electric pulse that momentarily staggers and knocks back enemies in front of you. Recharges every 15s.

Aura:Serrator

You and allies in coherency gain 7.5% Rending.

Keystone:Ablative Armor

Gain a stack of Ablative Armor whenever you kill an Elite enemy. At 3 stacks, the next time you would take health damage, nullify that damage and remove all stacks.
