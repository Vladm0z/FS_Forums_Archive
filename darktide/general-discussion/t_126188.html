The design is gorgeous, as usual. The design aesthetic is hands down the best part of this map (and game), second only to the combat. Clearly this team is incredibly gifted when it comes to map design, which is a shame considering there are so few of them.

The story: no one cares. I don’t mean this ironically, or even insultingly - the story in this game is offensively mediocre and forgettable. The cast of characters are poorly written self-contained wit boxes that do not amount to anything, none of them have any arc, and they get REAL boring very quick. "“oOoOoOOo I can’t wait to learn what Sister Prine is hiding!” - said 5 people in the Youtube comments while queuing on Uprising.

Then, when you finish the map; you get to fight a World of Warcraft raid boss. “You heard Brunt! KILL HIM!” said Sister Prine, comically, as the boss becomes invincible for the 10th time as you have to fight off maulers, Crushers, annoying balls of Nurglesnot/Pink Goonwave and do the hockey pockey until you die. And much like a Dark Souls 2 boss, the run-back is you have to play the whole map again! That’s what it’s all about!

Please stop making “boss fights”. At least in the Karnak Twins mission the “run-back” to the boss once you inevitably die is relatively short - and that was a chore as well. Having an “invincibility period” doesn’t it make it “more challenging”, it makes it more idiotic, when you can’t shoot in a game about shooting.

Much like Expeditions and Mortis Trials, I’ll play this map for the penances , and then move on and happily forget it ever existed.

zircuits • 2026-10-02 23:46:00

Yeah the boss fight is very mid but I think the map design itself is pretty neat until the bit before the end. Having a pathway loop back to the start of that path was very surprising. Having the players actually use their eyes to notice a symbol was something very bold. I think both of these statements disappoint me but here we are.

I think the colors would be fine if they didn’t purposefully blend them in. Darktide’s darkness is everywhere but the floor, and no one needs to look at the floor to know where it is, they look at the objects that visibly lift off the floor in a contrast to indicate things, like walls indicating places you can’t walk, or cover indicating you have to go around or vault. bright glowing flames and big clouds of gas to indicate places to not stand in, enemies indicating something to hit.

mmorpgs can put visual indicators on the floor, because it’s 75% of the screen, with the hud being the other 25%, and the hud tells you everything else including timers, boss health, team health, etc. Even so, they still do intense visual indicators, such as big shadowy objects, or bright glowing orbs, to indicate where danger is, places to stand, where not to stand, etc.

Darktide can’t reliably do this because the floor is a very tiny fraction of screen real estate. It’s especially noticeable in the final phase of the boss fight, he teleports back into the air, with players still capable of dealing damage to him (boss healthbar at the top of the screen btw), and then places all of the indicators on the ground and activates them. That’s the type of design that will cause pubs to wipe, especially first attempts.

My personal fix would be a visual wall that separates each zone with a screen filter applied to indicate when you’re in the danger spot, having it intensify when the danger becomes active. Everyone sees the thin dark pink mist, no one can tell what is going on with the bright green floor.
