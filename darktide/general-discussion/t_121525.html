I’ve seen some people that are not happy with the game’s difficulty going down now that there are fever Crushers in the game with not much to compensate for it aside from nerfs to the outliers.

This post is less of a question and more of a suggestion for players who want to have a challenging experience as we wait for more changes to come.

And that is to play without a full talent tree and with worse weapons. Don’t click off, let me cook here now.

One thing I learned from Darktide is that leveling a new character shows you a much different side of Darktide. The AI director doesn’t spam enemies and not only is the game still hard, you now have quiet moment where you can take in the environment and the atmosphere.

This was before the balance patch was even announced by the way.

Lower difficulties feel like what Darktide is supposed to beGeneral Discussion
TL;DR If anyone hasn’t tried making a new character and playing with them from scratch I highly suggest you do so. It shows you a completely different Darktide. A better Darktide. I could even say that this is the Darktide experience that the devs envisioned. I’m currently leveling up a new Veteran character and let me tell you, the gameplay has never felt more engaging and intense. Every game was super fun but the best was probably the Consignment Yard in Meltafab (“Secure the train” mission).…

But going back to the difficulty, basically if you play Auric with at least 10 unused talent points and blue weapons with around 300 power, you can bring that feel of challenge back because now enemies will take longer to kill and they themselves will hit you harder.

I know that this suggestion has a chance to be frustrating because it could turn Darktide into a walking simulator as everyone kills everything before you can. However, from my experience, as long as your team doesn’t have a Psyker with the Flame Staff/Soulblaze talents or Hive Scum with a Boom Bringer/Chem Toxins you will be fine in having a harder experience without other players getting in the way.

I just wanted to give people who now find the game boring an out because it’s pretty clear that any type of balance changes won’t come until the next update rolls around. Until then, you have to work with what you have to make the game harder.

Let me know what you think. Do you have an idea on how to make the game harder for experienced players? Do let me know in the replies as this can be a good place to suggest solutions, so people can try and see what works for them.

Cheers!
