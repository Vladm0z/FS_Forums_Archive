Doing this I’ve learned a tiny bit of online Chinese slang.

“List them up on the stock market” being said to mean “Kill them” or “Make them disappear” is a funny instance of Chinese internet slang.

It originated from Battlefield 4’s Campaign where Admiral Chang Wei’s voice actor mispronounced “Make them disappear” or something similar in Mandarin but it sounded like “List them up on the stock market” instead to native speakers and it became a kind of meme.
