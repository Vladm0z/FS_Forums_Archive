Ha, that’s a very open subject. In general, I use knowledge of the game and personal themes. By “knowledge” I mean understanding of the weapon stats and how this could interact well with the available classes, their abilities and talents.

For example: is this a finesse weapon, meaning it does good crit and weakspot damage or does it scale better from pure Strength and damage buffs? Then you take it from there, and pick talents which could complement those choices.

“Theme” is just down to my own personal preferences, class fantasy and often self-imposed restrictions. For example, I know force greatsword is better than lets say Obscurus force sword, but nothing is stopping me from making that my weapon of choice and making a melee psyker build around it. Or make it chainaxe, or devils claw on zealot. Duelling Sword Mk IV is the meta? Pick Mk V and make a build around it

This is where the game longevity comes from for me personally. One of the main reasons why I’m fairly vocal about the state of Havoc. I don’t like how the current Havoc difficulty modifiers push players towards very rigid metas which “solve” the current Havoc “puzzle” in the most optimal way.
