I don’t always want to deal with swarms of Hounds or Specialist conga-lines keeping me from straying just a little off the beaten path, nor do I want to always deal with Blackout/Fog giving me near-sightedness or repenting for my transgressions via St Messelina for the Nth time long after I already finished the event. It wasn’t until I got all the way down to Uprising that I saw a single standard mission. Literally the only way you can escape these damn things without playing on the lowest difficulty is to play the Battle for Tertium missions, and that’s not too ideal given that the amount of people queuing up for those tend to be lower compared to regular play, not to mention that BoT missions always have the same personalities narrating/commentating the same dialogue.

I realize that prior to this, some modifiers were so rare that they were practically non-existent and there are plenty of folks that like this added variety so I don’t necessarily want that to go away.
