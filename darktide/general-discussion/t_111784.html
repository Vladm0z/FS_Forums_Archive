I wish there were a few more earnable Titles that are more for aesthetic purposes. Fatshark’s done a bang-up job of making Titles that indicate skill or dedication, but their naming conventions are also a bit pragmatical. Thusfar, it’s mostly the I Came, I saw, I Conquered Titles that have some thematic value, and I feel a few more in such a vein would be fun.

For example, Titles could have some relation to playstyle or build. Perhaps akin to

Demolitionist Title, for getting Situational Awareness (3), The Enemy of my Enemy is my Friend (3) and Bestiary: Grenadiers/Poxburster (5).

Bodyguard Title, for getting Untouchable, Discipline & Teamwork (4), and Leave No One Behind.

Executioner Title, for getting Serial Killer, Dodge This (2), Frenzied Killer (2) and Time to Die (2).

Apothecary Title, for getting Cleanse and purify, Liquid Wrath, No Time to Wait, Redline and The right tool for the job.

Think it’d be fun : )
