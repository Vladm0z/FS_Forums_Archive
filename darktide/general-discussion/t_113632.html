Ranged Gameplay is largely fine. The issue at hand is how ridiculously capable we are in Melee against SPECIFIC Enemies. For example, we have ZERO business being able to just spam Dodge Slides up to Ranged Enemies and then bap them to death, like what cats do to mice.

For Gameplay purposes, being able to Dodge Slide in order to avoid Enemy Ranged Attacks is fine to an extent… but it should be HEAVILY limited. That way Fatshark could reduce the AI Director’s spam of specific Ranged Enemies while making Ranged Gameplay more relevant and viable.

Also, Scab Shooters need their Secrets of the Machine God buffs back:

Secrets of the Machine God - OUT NOW - Pt.2

Shooter Changes

  • Scab Shooters now have a tighter shot spread, making them more threatening at range. While they still have ranged damage falloff, they will be more consistent in how they hit players at range.
  • Additionally, Scab/Dreg shooters will now stay in cover longer (and remain exposed longer) with a reduced cooldown between sprays, making cover-taking enemies more of a threat but also enhancing the viability of player weapons that apply suppression.

zircuits • 2025-10-15 03:46:19

A lot of problems with how ranged are fought would be fixed if fatshark added shielded horde and elite variants that actually forced players to fight hordes instead of bumrushing past them. Give elite shields a charging shield bash that interrupts sliding/dodging, dragging the camera towards them and put 10 of them in the frontline and you’ll never see players rush at a gunner patrol ever again in their lives. Give the shield bash to an ogryn bulwark and have it launch players upwards in the air to set up some skeet shooting for local shotgunners and now they’ll be more annoying and scary.

Another idea would be to deplete stamina while under suppression, and, to add to this, the current effects of suppression should be tied to stamina as well (0% stamina would be current effects of suppression, and above 0% would be like no suppression or minor debuffs per 25th or whatever percentile). Make sliding only provide a damage reduction buff to ranged attacks that depletes as stamina depletes (from 100%-0% or whatever seems best balance-wise). Then just decouple the currently cumbersome ties between stamina and dodging and now suddenly you have a gameplay loop that requires you to cycle from your dodges to using your stamina in a more efficient and rewarding manner. You’d end up having to watch your angles against shooters more often, and they’re more likely to bleed you out during kites in a meaningful way.
