Now that the new class has been revealed, can we get some buffs for the non ganger classes that clearly need some love? For starters, here are some ideas of new/reworked talents:

Psyker

  1. New talent: Lingering flames - Soulblaze now does 2x damage and lasts until the enemy is dead

  2. Empathic evasion - Now also gives immunity to melee attacks

  3. Scrier’s gaze - Now gives 10 gold toughness per second (max of 100)

  4. Malefic momentum - +10% Strength and +15% cleave to all Attacks for 8s after a Kill. Stacks 5 times.

  5. Telekine shield - Now saves you from overloading on peril, cooldown reduced to 15 seconds

  6. Creeping flames - Now applies maximum (31) soulblaze stacks

  7. Brainburst - Can now be charged for 30 seconds to do up to 100k damage

Zealot

  1. Until death - Cooldown can now be reduced with CDR talents
  2. Holy revenant - Now gives 100% HP back
  3. Fury of the Faithful - Bring back the ranged rending
  4. Duellist - Now gives 150% extra finesse damage
  5. Invocation of death - Returned to the old version that applied CDR for each enemy hit
  6. Shroudfield - Now doesnt remove you from stealth when you attack

Veteran

  1. Power cycler - Returned to its original version that allows for 8 swings
  2. Exe stance - Auto reloads your gun since Hive Scum will have it
  3. Shredder grenades - Apply max stacks of brittleness to all enemies hit
  4. Serrated blade - Now applies 8 stacks of bleed per hit
  5. Iron Will - Now gives 90% DR if above 90% HP
  6. Voice of command - Grants 100 gold toughness to teammates, range doubled

Ogryn

  1. Point blank barrage - No longer consumes ammo during the duration
  2. Feel no pain - All effects now spread to allies in coherency
  3. Local protector - Grants 300 gold toughness
  4. Batter - 4 stacks of bleed on light attack, 8 on heavy
  5. Frag bomb - capacity increased to 5, sets ground on fire for 30 seconds
  6. Passive 33% DR

Arbites

  1. Dog explosion nades - charges increased to 5
  2. Castigator’s stance - Can now run and slide
  3. Execution order - All specials and elites now visible through walls 100% of the time, enemy list configurable in settings
  4. Cyber mastiff - Can now loudly bark to scare enemes and suppress them for 10 seconds
  5. Voltaic shock mine - Electricity now spreads to cover a wider area, lasts longer

Thoughts?
