Guess which one is better? No respite specifies that you gain “up to 20%”, while execution is straight up 20%, no up to, so which one is better?

No respite, by a huge margin, no respite increases damage by 20% for each instance of stagger, meaning if a target is already staggered and you stagger them again, they take 40% extra damage, until they regain their balance, this bonus can reach up to 140% damage.

Why is it not ok? any shotgun that is not equipped with it will be missing out on tons of damage, making it mandatory pick, effectively making shotguns a blessing slot weapon which I think prevents shotguns from being more diverse build-wise.

My suggestion would be to completely remove no respite and replace it with execution for the affected weapons, while also adjusting the damage of these weapons to account for the change, I’m not saying increase the damage by 140%, but it will need to be increased to account for the missed although inconsistent power provided by no respite.

Check this steam guide for more info about how blessings work.
