The Glitchling is a fast moving Disabler who targets a player and disables either their Combat Ability or ranged weapon for 8s upon gaining line of sight of that player. If a Combat Ability is disabled while it’s active (like Chorus), the Ability will be refunded when the glitch is over.

To spice things up, Glitchling could even prefer players leading score with ranged kills and/or players that have the most Ability activations (since that data exists buried in Darktide somewhere)… that could add exciting risk to running strong ranged/CA focused builds

How Glitchling Would Work:

Glitchling will shout: “Bite, bite, chew, chew, your gun won’t work and I’m coming for you!”, followed by his bright eyes flashing. If the player dodges while his eyes are flashing, they will not be disabled by his glitch attack. Effectively his glitch attack would work like the Scab Sniper shot, as a hitscan attack. If hit by the invisible beam, the player would be affected.

Players can also end this effect prematurely by killing the Glitchling, which targets and rushes them for a melee attack after attempting to glitch them.

Much like the Pox Hound, the Glitchling will leap at a player, followed by a chaotic flurry attack with his claws. Dodging this attack can leave the Glitchling exposed momentarily, allowing the player to quickly finish him as the Glitchling finishes his leap.

Thoughts?

Darktide is in need of balancing changes around the Auric difficulty. One way this can be done (in addition to targeted weapon adjustments) is by the addition of new Nurgle specific enemies and/or existing Elite trait adjustments.

Nurgle’s Rot has progressed, the infestation in Tertium worsened. New sightings of lesser Demons are inevitable.
