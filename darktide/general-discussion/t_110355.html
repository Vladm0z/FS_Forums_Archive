Margeret-Dayhoff • 2025-07-04 20:20:01

I envision smite having a charge-time similar to brainburst, and once you reach the end of the charge you send out a branching and jumping chain lightning attack which momentarily stuns the enemy (in the same way Electrostaff 2ndary “stuns” an enemy for like a second) that bifurcates once for every 20% peril or thereabouts (for a maximum of 32 hit enemies at >90% peril). Could also be 25% for 16 enemies at >90% peril or something along those lines. Make the bifurcation jump random but biased towards elites/specials, and without the ability to loop back on the same enemy.

Dmg should be strong but not absurd. Would need testing to find what is a reasonable amount of damage.

Giving it a charge-up time and making it a shoot-once when finish channeling like brainburst (or electrokinetic 2ndary) prevents the stunlock we see from current smite which you can just continously channel. And a maximum number of bifurcations prevents stunning entire walls of enemies. I’m thinking perhaps stun duration should max at 2 seconds for groaners/pox walkers, and diminish with armor class (1.5 sec unarmored, 1sec for flak, 0.5 carapace, and unyielding 0.25 sec, with monstrosities/bosses being immune to the stun).

Values would need testing of course. What ruins current smite is the complete stunlock. Already stunned enemies could get a buff/debuff that makes them increasingly resistant to the stun effect (diminishing returns on stun duration to prevent infinite kiting), but makes them take more damage from consecutive electric attacks.
