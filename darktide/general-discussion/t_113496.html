Scrier’s Gaze Suggestion —

Scrier’s is doing a bit too much, don’t get me wrong the TDR boost is nice especially for melee Psyker but getting the whole package Scrier’s provides at once is a bit with the new enhanced Toughness regen Talents… well it kind of encroaches on Zealot’s class identity as a melee specialist that outright facetanks tons of damage.

Here’s a suggestion:

Scrier’s Gaze
Triggers Scrier’s Gaze. When entering Scrier’s Gaze you Quell 50% Peril as well as gain +10% Damage, +20% Critical Chance, +10% Weakspot Damage, and Suppression Immunity.

When not in Scrier’s Gaze or under the protection of Warp Unbound, you gain +20% Toughness Damage Reduction and +2 effective Dodges.

For every second in Scrier’s Gaze you gain +1% Damage up to a maximum of +30%. This effect lingers for 10s after leaving Scrier’s Gaze.

While in Scrier’s Gaze you build up Peril. Build- up is temporarily slowed down by enemy Kills. At 100% Peril, Scrier’s Gaze ends.

There - to access the impressive damage Scrier’s provides, TDR goes away. It seems like a fair trade given the enhanced Toughness regen options Psyker has access to and everything Scrier’s provides. Also it fits - during Scrier’s Psyker becomes more reckless, defenses are neglected for the maddening power of the Warp.

Also keep in mind Empathic Evasion uptime would still be increased throughout the duration of Scrier’s so there’s that.
