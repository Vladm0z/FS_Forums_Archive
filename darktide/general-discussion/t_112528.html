Everyone who played Vermintide and tried the chaos wastes mode knows how good it is, in short, it’s a “rogue-lite” mode where you go in with all your talents, and the basic version of your equipped weapons (no blessings or traits, and lowest power level possible), you can build on that by spending the mode’s currency (obtained in the run itself) to obtain unique talents, or to upgrade your weapons.

It is extremely fun and replayable, and I really wanna go back to vermintide for it, but I got real bad at the game due to how different the combat feels and don’t have enough time to retrain.

I think the mode will be a perfect fit for darktide, especially in the map design department, since most maps in darktide feel really similar to others.

TLDR; Chaos wastes is a great casual mode, and darktide map design could make it an even better mode in darktide than in vermintide.

There are a lot of similarities between the two, but I don’t think Mortis Trials means something else that borrows from Chaos Wastes couldn’t come down the pike.

If anything, FS have demonstrated that they’re pretty-well committed to building off of what they’ve laid down as a foundation. I think all the level tweaks/expansions we’ve seen show that, and of course Havoc & Mortis Trials do too. And there are loads of possibilities for other game modes that build off the core experience.

Mortis Trials reminds me a lot of Remnant 2’s Boss Rush Mode; they’re both spiritual successors to quite popular modes in each’s preceding game…and I think they both kinda miss the mark. By condensing the gameplay loop, they both kinda cut out the parts of the roguelike loop I liked most in Chaos Wastes & Survival mode (from Remnant 1).

I think it’s definitely possible FS could make something interesting out of what they didn’t bring to Mortis Trials from Chaos Wastes: level traversal (regular, maybe more varied, gameplay), some level of narrative/decision-making (like CW) and a weapon upgrade/trading system. More grounded than CW or Mortis Trials, more methodical. Could be totally rad. And with solo mode? Shoooo.

Ultimately, these are things that would increase retention. And they’re on the scale of Mortis Trials or Havoc, I’d reckon.
