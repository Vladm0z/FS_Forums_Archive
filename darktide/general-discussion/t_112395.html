BizarreSalp • 2025-09-08 17:53:56

There’s a mod called “true level” that shows you a player’s level beyond 30, so you can essentially see how much experience they have with a class. It can also show the highest level Havoc they’ve cleared. I’ve seen people with a True Level into the thousands.

From what I’ve seen, people don’t want to take someone into their Havoc 40 who hasn’t already cleared Havoc 40 lol. That said, going for slightly lower Havoc levels like high-30s will let you onto some teams. For any lower-level Havoc, having cleared that level or something near it is a good sign.

Also, picking a class that is not present on a team already is a good method of getting picked. Some classes are also more popular than others; Zealot (for Chorus support), Ogryn, and Psyker (for bubble). Psyker is probably the #1 desired member for a comp, but they’re also so squishy that a good Psyker is hard to come by, and not everyone enjoys the playstyle (I don’t).

There are a lot of Arbites floating around, so while they’re good in Havoc*, it’s common for a lot of them to be competing for space in teams. Veteran has nice utility and support but is generally just too squishy for Havoc, and can only somewhat carry himself by Plasma Gun spam, which also means he eats all the ammo.

*Can be hit or miss, though, I find that there are a lot of lower-skill players who just play Arbites to crutch on its strength and end up going down a lot in games and/or doing pitiful damage when it’s the most damaging class in the game. My point is not whether this is right or fair, but that people putting together teams are going to see an Arbites and consider if they will be a help or a hindrance.

Those are just my observations; I rarely run my own games and just try to join others. I have a lot of success getting on teams if I’m on my Zealot, reasonably on my Ogryn, can sometimes find a team as Arbites, and it’s kind of difficult as Vet.

Depends on a multitude of things.

  • Havoc Lvl
  • Class and builds
  • Portraits and titles
  • True level(Kind of)

The first one is a big one. It’s quite surprising the amount of invites I get from fresh lvl 30s and havoc level 20 players trying to join my lobby. At havoc 35+, having to babysit someone who folds at the sight of 10 poxwalkers makes for a miserable time for the rest involved. This could also be the case if your clearance is 40; then people can assume that you know what you’re doing and would be willing to take you in. It’s also that people value their time, and they don’t want to take a chance on a Havoc lvl 35 player where it would be better to take the 40 guy(I’m not saying this is correct behavior, but it is what it is). Also people vary, I give leeway to folks about four havoc levels below me to enter my lobbies, where as others kind of want people to be on the same level.

Second is builds as some mods allow players to see your load out and if you’re using FotF, Exe Stance, Infiltrate, PB, Shroud, venting shreik, Scyer’s Gaze then you will be denied as the emperor’s fading light is brutal, having gold toughness helps or bubble for pushes. I’ve seen Arbitrators get denied as they don’t provide Gold toughness that a team wants.

Flashing your portraits and titles could also help get you into havoc lobbies as it ‘can’ indicate that you at least have some knowledge(Granted this isn’t always the case as some titles are easily cheesed)

Last being true-level, as being under 100 will likely get you denied.

In conclusion, it’s better to host your own havoc games as you can dicate what you want in a squad.
