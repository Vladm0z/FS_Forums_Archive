Hark ye scunners, I’ve come to b****.

Tl;dr FOMO is a manufactured damper on customer satisfaction. Players would be more engaged with the item shop if you gave them they ability to buy whatever they wanted instead of forcing them to wait 2 weeks for new stock.

Since I’ve started playing Darktide last year I’ve had my eyes set on the Tempestus Scion helmet. Scions were my first set of tabletop minis so I’ve been wanting to play as one in an official 40k game.

The only problem is that I haven’t had the opportunity to actually play as one because Fatshark keep rehashing the same handful of cosmetics. So every other Thursday for me this past year has been “Darktide Disappointment Day”.

I understand that the developers don’t think there’s a problem because they can just grant themselves cosmetics whenever. But for players, this FOMO monetization is just an annoying waste of time.

TenCent money means they don’t have to worry about customer satisfaction, just that they have an ever-revolving door of customers. So they use the most iconic and marketable detachments to key jangle at the new players.

I wouldn’t be as pissed if there weren’t so many duplicates and reskins being rotated. But in the year I’ve been waiting for this one item to show up:

  • Not a single NEW cosmetic has been designed.
  • DKoK have been brought back 4 different times.
  • Vostroyans and Euclidian Starstriders have been brought back 3 times each.
  • Commissars, Steel Legion, Hive Gangers and Rogue Traders have all been brought back 2 times a piece.
  • There are currently 9 different skins for the generic “Goggles and Rebreather", not to mention the dozens of other Inquisitorial cosmetics with at least 6 reskins each.
  • The Tempestus Scion helmet hasn’t shown up once.

I’ve accepted the fact that the helmet will likely never be brought back to the game again, but the idea that there IS a chance will compel me to log in every 2 weeks like a conditioned rat in a cage. Which deep down just means that this Chinese "turn-everything-into-a-content-mill” system is working as intended. Dissatisfaction by design.
