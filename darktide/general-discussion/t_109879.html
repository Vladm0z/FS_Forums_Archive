All my suggested numbers are place holders, no need to discuss them.

  1. All or nothing:

Current functionality: Gain you Up to 25-40% Strength, as Stamina depletes, meaning you will never see that 40% increase unless you are at 0% stamina, so it should be written as 20-32% instead of the impossible 25-40

My suggestion: Gain 25-40% strength while at or below 50% stamina, this will give you a nice instant boost while low on stamina without the ridiculous requirement of being dangerously low on stamina, making the perk more usable.

  1. Limbsplitter:

Current functionality: 60% Strength on your First Attack every 3.5-5 seconds, The timer restarts if you damage an enemy before it’s finished, this makes it extremely hard to benefit from this blessing.

My suggestion: I have two suggestions, the first is keep it as is, but increase the strength to something ridiculous, like 120-200% (maybe also increase the cooldown a bit), making this a potentially good pick for playstyles that use ranged weapons most of the time, only whipping out a melee weapon to kill a stray enemy that attacks them. my second suggestion would be to change it, so that this affects only heavy attack, meaning light attacks neither restart the timer nor benefit from the strength boost, of course the strength would need to go down from 60% to compensate for the increased availability.

  1. Devastating Strike:

Current functionality: 65-80% Cleave for 5 seconds on Critical Hit, why would this exist when you have Savage Sweep and Wrath? all three provide cleave, with Devastating Strike being the lowest.

My suggestion: in addition to the cleave, gain 10-20% strength for the duration, this will effectively increase the cleave to 75-100% (65-80 cleave + 10-20 strength = 75-100 cleave), making this blessing a good pick if you want some cleave with the added benefit of increased damage and stagger, you still get the least amount of cleave compared to the other two blessings.

  1. Run ‘n’ Gun:

Current functionality: You can Hipfire with this weapon while Sprinting. 6-15% Close damage while Sprinting. Also reduces weapon spread at all times by 30% (10% for DB shotgun), this blessing sounds cool, but i could never justify taking it over anything else, and also I hate the fact that I can’t use it will bracing the double barrel shotgun.

My suggestion: close damage increase is now 10-25%, you can also reload while sprinting, but you move 15% slower, this blessing is not worth the slot, so why not give it some power.
