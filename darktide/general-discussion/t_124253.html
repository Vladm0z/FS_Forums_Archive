This isn’t in any way trying to be pessimistic. This is more of a call to attention about something I’m genuinely incredibly concerned about. Darktide may be my favorite game ever, and I know a lot of people who feel the same way. Yet I’ve come to realize something.

Is this game designed to die at some point in the future?
I don’t just mean the developers stop updating it, and player numbers drop. This eventually happens to the majority of games. The thing is, many of these “dead” games can still be played, even in multiplayer.

My concern is that Darktide (as far as I’m aware) is not like this. If the game’s servers were to ever be shut down, the game could no longer be played. At all. Ever.

Like I said, it’s not the most comfortable thing to discuss especially after Skitarii dropped not long ago, but I think this issue needs to be addressed. To be entirely honest, I don’t even know if this is an issue that CAN be fixed.

Like I said, not trying to be all doom and gloom, just something I wanted to bring attention to.
