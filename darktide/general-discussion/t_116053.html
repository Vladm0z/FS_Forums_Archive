eatingsand • 2025-12-09 05:46:29

I always felt that VT2 chaos warriors struck the balance between beef and numbers perfectly. I think that the issue with Darktide balance is that it’s so easy to blow beef off the playing board without engaging in it. Compared to how plentiful Crusher-b-gone ranged options are in Darktide, VT2 didn’t have THAT much of it, and they often had severe drawbacks in VT2. Player power level issue basically.

The result from that dynamic is that if you have few crushers, they’ll just get solo’d by the kraks veteran (for example, plenty of other things to pick from) and if you have many crushers everyone now has to build for crushers or the game meatwalls the players.

I think the only feasible solution to actually solve this is to rebalance all crusher-deletion methods to actually have risk vs reward to them, and to get rid of the no-risk-all-reward options. I used kraks as an example so I’ll continue to, but if they didn’t oneshot multiple crushers per regenerating grenade (and nobody else could do anything equivalent either), you could have 3 strong crushers and they’d actually be challenging. With these options, you have 3 strong crushers and they’re on the killfeed the moment they come into load distance.

Unfortunately, the bottom line seems to be that Fatshark is unwilling to go that far because of fear of community backlash. But I think if they put in some effort and actually came up with good alternate gameplay for this that DOES involve risk vs reward ratios that aren’t off, it would be more fun for everyone involved. For example, krak grenades once more: What if crushers picked them up and flung them away unless you hit them during an overhead animation, or in the back? It would force you to actually engage with the crusher in a meaningful way, while still acting as a distinct anti-crusher tool. I don’t think anybody would hate such a change if it went along with a crusher rebalance.
