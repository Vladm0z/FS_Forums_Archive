Idea to make enemies more challenging without health buffs. This mission modifier and the following suggested enemies would add more combat variety to the game, potentially altering how players approach situations and encouraging more tactical thought to engagements.

The Moebian Domain

The Traitorous Moebian 6th are sabatoging communications in the area, expect higher Moebian presence, and treacherous conditions.

Treacherous Conditions: Poxwalkers replaced by Moebian 51st.

UW50gSlWvbkgUBnEOx133CxFo4R4ppMGxgvkhYiJ1200×675 710 KB

Scab Only Elites/Specialists. Bosses replaced by Captains. Capsules are randomly rigged with explosives that detonate a short period after being opened, damage can be avoided by dodging backwards. Power Supply Interruption on Elite horde ambush, along with music cue:

Captains:

Aj93z1Ng7kHseaPyU4gNS5WJBEfBajRjdOWdIcwm1139×641 166 KB

Captains now have two phases, phases are indicated by the two wounds their healthbars have. Captains have different behaviors in each phase.

Phase 1: Tactical, prefers ranged combat. Periodically calls in reinforcements (same style as Vox Operator below). Breaking Void Shield staggers Captains, but stagger duration is much shorter. Void Shield regenerates if Captains are ignored for too long during this phase.

Phase 2: Captains become enraged and switch to melee. They gain increased attack speed, are immune to crowd control, and gain increased damage. Captains cannot call for reinforcements while enraged.
