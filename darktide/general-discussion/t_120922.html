Hi Fatshark team and fellow Rejects,I’ve been advocating for a Space Hulk / Boarding Action mode for a while, and I’d love to share a full concept. I believe it has way more potential than the current Expeditions mode.Lore Tie-in (Fits Perfectly):
With the Mourningstar’s relations with Tertium’s local nobility deteriorating, resupply is becoming harder. Suddenly, an Imperial migrant ship that broke through a Warp storm appears in orbit above the hive — no response to hails. Inquisitor Rannick has no choice but to send teams aboard to scavenge supplies and intel.Why This Would Be Better Than Expeditions:
Expeditions’ open wastelands are a fresh idea, but they lack meaningful progression and get repetitive quickly. A Space Hulk mode would return to what Darktide does best: dark, narrow, claustrophobic horror.

  • Tight corridors, flickering lights, leaking compartments, zero-G sections — pure tension and dread.

  • Different decks offer wildly varied combat: fiery engine rooms for melee, frozen cryo bays with slippery fights, luxurious (but corrupted) noble quarters for gunplay, overcrowded migrant holds for massive hordes. Every run feels unique.

  • Natural fit for roguelike elements that Expeditions is currently missing.

Core Mechanics Idea:

  • Procedural Ship Layouts + Chaos Wastes-style roguelike: Random deck connections, enemy placements, and Warp corruption levels every run.

  • Warp Corruption Timer: Longer stays = stronger mutations, hallucination events, and bigger rewards (more terrifying and meaningful than radiation).

  • In-Run Progression: Collect “Warp-tainted Relics” to buy random boons/perks/temporary weapon upgrades at makeshift altars — real “get stronger as you go” feeling.

  • Risk/Reward Extraction: Quick scout & extract, or push deeper into the heart for massive loot (with higher chance of death).

  • Permanent Progression: Successfully returned supplies permanently upgrade the Mourningstar (new weapon mods, hub features, better vendors) and tie into the main story.

  • New Enemies/Events: Infected migrants, Genestealer hybrids, daemonic incursions, gravity flips, etc.

This mode would deliver incredible atmosphere, variety, and long-term replayability. Many players have already asked for “Chaos Wastes but Space Hulk” — this could be the perfect execution.What do you think? Fatshark, any chance of considering something like this in future updates? Looking forward to your thoughts!
