JanZamoysky:

In general instant toughness regen on few other talents has been replaced by time based regen, which is also a clear nerf.

I wouldn’t be so sure, honestly. In general, over-time toughness restoration is a good thing, this is part of the reason why vet’s Confirmed Kill is so good. Because, normally, you operate with your toughness around 100%, at least you should struggle for this all the time, it’s a core design principle in this game for all classes/builds. And all that extra toughness you replenish through kills and hits while being full on it, is lost. Over-time replenishments turns it into regen effectively, and if your toughness will take a hit after you triggered one of those talents, this damage will be repaired and your tougness gain won’t be lost.

It’s important to have both instant and over-time replenishment talents.
