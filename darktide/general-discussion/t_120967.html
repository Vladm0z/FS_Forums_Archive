TLDR: No, I am not saying to actually do this. But I am saying there is currently zero defense against it. I brought up the lack of proper matchmaking in a suggest post but now I am drawing attention to something more problematic.

Starting the gate or calling the valk will cause massive waves of enemies to spawn. Currently, there are almost zero ways to establish what kind of game the already existing players are running. If me and my friend want to do a salvage run, we either have to do it with just the two of us and the idiot bots or let randoms in who can at ANY POINT just decide “I am going to start the gate.” This legit just happened. We were fighting a boss and some dingus ruined everything by pulling the gate, so we lost nearly all our tech. Same dude then got left behind because we were fed up with him and called the valk to end it early, and he didn’t notice.

But it did make us realize something funny. There is nothing stopping anyone from joining a game and ruining it right away aside from reputation loss. There are no host kicks, the vote kick is nearly useless. The party finder is a joke and doesn’t block randoms unless you set it to private, but then you have to put up with bots. You can’t play solo. You can TRY to talk to people, but legit me and my friend got radio silence the ENTIRE time. Few people communicate or bother to read or respond in chat until they’re mad about something. Gate puller dude was quiet the entire time, not even a “my bad” or “sorry” until he realized he missed the valk and then suddenly he found his voice.

Part of me is legit considering it. Just expose how awful the current system is by exploiting it for all its worth. I imagine if enough people suddenly just started preventing people from playing expeditions, then Fatshark might actually care enough to fix the issue and add some proper matchmaking and let us start a game and choose how that game goes, kicking included.

EDIT: Also, an easy fix would be that the gate/extract alarm does not go off until majority of the players are there. Kind of dumb that a single dude can mess with everyone by starting the massive waves, but the bar won’t even progress for him. Even if you kick them, damage is done.
