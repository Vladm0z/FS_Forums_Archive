Here’s my personal Psyker build for the Surge Force Staff.

The core idea of this build is to stay at high Peril as much as possible to maximize damage output. It also has very high Toughness regeneration, so I personally think its survivability is excellent. The only downside is Peril management — you’ll need to vent pretty often by pressing R.

For the staff, I use the Flak perk and +5% Critical Chance. The blessings are Warp Nexus and Warp Flurry (I used ChatGPT for the translations, so hopefully those are the correct English names). For curios, I run triple Toughness. The secondary stats are +4% CDR, +5% Toughness, and +20% Gunner Resistance. You can swap the resistance stat depending on the Auric Maelstrom modifier to improve survivability. I haven’t tried this build in Havoc 40 yet, but at least the damage is enough for Auric Maelstrom.
