Sure! My karsolas play style is very twitchy and more about timings and positioning than combos. I almost never use light attack on karsolas. Basically I use dodge as part of the swing attack, which allows me to charge a heavy for a big swing that travels further and hits more of the crowd. It’s very effective. I rarely use block in this style as I simply don’t need to.

Run towards a horde with a charged heavy then dodge sideways in the direction of the swing attack exactly the same time as your pick starts swinging. If you turn slightly whilst doing it you can get a full 180 (or more!) arc if needed for maximum reach. Then instantly charge the next heavy and dodge the other direction as you release the next heavy attack that way. You can usually do it with a short span between attacks to regen the stamina, unless there are ragers then you do them quicker and pick the right stagger moment to regen stam.

With this method, almost nothing can reach you as they’re all knocked over or dead lol. (Except rotten armour enemies that have crazy stagger stats).

For bigger enemies I often switch between the above method to stagger them, then special followed immediately by charged overhead to finish (unless the insanely OP psycher has melted the target first lol…a 7/10 chance these days).

Something else I find effective is running towards an enemy, but then sliding to their side whilst releasing a heavy swing back towards them, hitting them in the back. It opens them up really easily.

Not sure if all of that makes sense but let me know if not.

The karsolas has 10% weak spot damage, 25% carapace, thrust and head taker.
