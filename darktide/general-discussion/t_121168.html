I disagree that the force swords are almost 3 completely different weapons. I would even argue at the loosest definition of difference that these weapons are near identical. Their optimized blessings and perks are the same across the board, and their damage combos are all heavy spam or light-heavy repeat. Their push attacks are the same. The only differences are pretty much that illisi has a fun special attack while the other two have a dumb sawing swing, and breakpoints.

Which is part of my greatest problem with weapon families or weapon marks. They don’t really contribute anything to the game other than padding out the experimental phase of something new coming out. Either you end up with relic blade, where both marks are practically identical in both damage and tech, or you end up with (insert ranged weapon here), where one mark outperforms the others in every single way you want a weapon to perform well in, and the others are used by people who don’t know or don’t care and contrarians.
