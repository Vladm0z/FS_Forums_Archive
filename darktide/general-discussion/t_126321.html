Few days ago, I had an extremely successful, flawless Havoc 40 Spillway with my wife, a friend just met on other Spillway match, as well as a random. Made me appreciate the charm of cooperation all over again. So aside from the itself, I thought I’d share some more tips that I’ve noticed most randoms don’t really do.

First, role assignments, I recommend that even when playing with randoms, at least try to discuss before entering the boss area who will focus on destroying orbs in the air, who will focus on taking down elite specialists and hordes, and who will focus on dealing damage to the boss. Then focus on your own role.

Next is resource management, don’t waste any ammo crates along the way. Normally, you can bring 2 into the boss area. If you have a vet, everyone should have plenty of grenades to create a safe environment for dealing boss damage.

Finally, just like in the twins, players can respawn in the boss area over and over again, so try to keep your party fighting near the respawn point as much as possible.

Extra tip, if you’re playing on Havoc and don’t have a well coordinated squad, definitely avoid rotten armor. I’ve only ever succeeded in premade team.

Hope we get more interesting maps like this.
