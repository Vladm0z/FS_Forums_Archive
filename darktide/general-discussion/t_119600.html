This is my opinion on the timer, but I don’t claim it’s the right one.

In Helldivers 2 the mission timer is 30 minutes, so you don’t feel pressure as much. The game is designed in a way where one person can clear an objective by themselves in many cases by using stratagems, except for big enemy bases or core mission objectives which are guarded by lots of enemies or objectives like Bunker which requires 2 people to open the door.

Most maps are way more open in Helldivers and do not force you to go around level geometry to reach objectives on most planets. Most of the time you literary see sights of interest in the distance without anything in your way. Not to mention you have vehicles (2 for now) that help you to traverse the zones quicker.

Yes, the few planets in Helldivers 2 have a lot of foilage or canyons or rivers everywhere that can split the zone into 2-3 separate islands, but let me be honest here, most people don’t love those planets, and most people like the more open maps. If you want to copy Helldivers 2 to attract new audience, at least copy the things people actually enjoy about it.

The best planets in Helldivers 2 have a straight way forward to any objective.

With random players you lose a lot of time in Expeditions, because people don’t go straight to objectives to speed up the process of hacking or capturing the zone, and most of the time people stick around on finished objectives way too long instead of moving forward.

Darktide’s Expedition map has a lot of chokepoints and you’re more likely to encounter enemies, unlike Helldivers 2 where you can circle the edge of the map to play stealth and avoid enemy patrols. Some of the Expedition’s objectives absolutely require you clearing off enemy groups near them. Also most objectives have data hacking and power cells, so it means that it’s unlikely to finish those objectives alone, or at least 3x times harder than in Helldivers 2.

Helldivers 2 revive/teamwide “lives” system also compliments the open world design. You can summon dead teammates anywhere on most maps (except for bug planet Oshaune with underground caves and other “unique” planets). In Darktide when your teammates die you have to navigate the terrain, avoid enemies and find the respawn point in the distance which wastes a lot of timer. You can also meet bosses on your way to respawn beacon in Darktide, which punishes the players that already stayed alive the longest (I’ve had Scab Captain spawn near player respawn point, thankfully I had stealth to revive).

The timer resetting in Sanctuaries is a decent idea and makes the gamemode at least playable to some degree. But I don’t think the timer is the right call in the gamemode that’s based around exploration. In my opinion the gamemode should be balanced around resource management and health management, rather than time management.

The cool thing about Darktide main maps is that you can either go slow or fast, it all depends on player skill and preference. Forcing people into the same frantic pace just gets tiring. There needs to be freedom to approach.

I have 3k hours in Darktide and 300 in Helldivers.
