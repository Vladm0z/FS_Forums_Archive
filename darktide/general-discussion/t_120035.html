I don’t hate it. New Biome, new items, new combat puzzle not seen often in the base game because of long range combat and guaranteed resupply points. All good stuff.

I think there’s real points of criticisms that need to be made, but I’m enjoying myself.

Speaking of, rest of this post is going to be criticisms. Sorry.

I can’t realistically see a dev cycle that’s juggling 4 or more different game modes for Darktide. Expeditions is getting a much better reception than VT2’s Weaves, but it’s a departure from the main game. The only way I see Expeditions getting more dev time is if the whole player base jumps from the main mission board to this, and I don’t see it. Before I say anything else, I think the single best thing you can do here is to streamline the best parts about Expeditions into a mission type for the base game map rotation.

Enemy spawns and AI needs another pass. It really feels like half of the team gets to have all of the fun while the other half gets to kick sand and be all alone the whole map. As if enemy spawns are seeded closer to one player and not another. Maybe it’s a quirk with the POI’s, but patrols probably need to be more aggressive in their placement and pathing, have a wider aggro range (maybe?), and re-think how to use a horde timer to make sure everyone gets to be under pressure. 8 out 10 of my games are borderline Maelstrom levels of chaos that I never saw during the press release week. The streams I watched looked boring as hell and I am at this moment convinced it’s because enemy spawns actually break when 4 competent players do all POI’s. So far I’ve had more fun being with 3 bad teammates who have no idea what’s going on or how to read the auspex map.

Also, it’s time for enemies to treat stealth differently. I personally don’t mind aggro dumps, but you have to know people are soloing POI’s, stealthing, and having all of those spawns sprinting across the map at the rest of the team. They need to do something, anything, before aggro switching. Run in a circle, be suppressed, anything that isn’t an immediate dead sprint towards a new target 300m away.

Ogryn Pack Master, way overhyped. I still believe he can be buffed, but at the moment he just comes off as an overachieving dreg rager. Y’all had me hoping for some kind of anti-player monster that’d deny you easy wins or cheesed out strategies while accompanied by a deadlier version of the second most hated enemy in game.

I’m all out of juice for auspex minigames. There’s so many. I know the primary inspiration for Expeditions had hundreds of similarly annoying minigames, but they tended to end in an explosion, unlike ours, which just opens chests for currency without an explosion. Please tighten those minigames up and let me get back to chopping heretics.

All my other issues with Expeditions is just bug related. I’m lucky I don’t crash a ton, but I see people drop in and out a couple of times a match, plz fix.
