Just an idea of mine! Veteran needs some LMGs. Heavy Stubbers are definitely an option, but why stop at that? Ballistic Weapons are great and all, but the Astra Militarum are known for their ENERGY WEAPONS first and foremost.

This niche would be applicable to Hot-shot Volley Guns, what the Scab Gunners use, but they’re difficult to balance with the rest of the current Las Weapons. Therefore, I’ve got a more grounded and lore-friendly alternative in mind…

Support Lascarbines. They’re Lascarbines with Drum-shaped Power Packs. The Tempestus Aquilons have access to a Hot-shot variant:

image342×399 37.3 KB

So how would it work? How does it compare to the current roster of Las Weapons?

Aiming:
The ADS function would be similar to the Ogryn’s Heavy Stubber, but with increased Spread to make up for their increased Ammo efficiency. Support Lascarbines should be focused on Suppression.

Ammo:
Compared to Infantry Lasguns and Recon Lasguns, Support Lascarbines would have increased Magazine Capacity at the cost of reduced Ammo Reserves.

Rate of Fire:
Between that of Infantry Lasguns and Recon Lasguns, with the same applying to their Damage Per Shot.

Reloading:
Support Lascarbines would be subjected to a lengthier Reload than the other Las Weapons in order to give them a drawback to their massive Magazine Capacity.

Special Attack:
To further differentiate them from the other Las Weapons, they’d have neither a Flashlight nor a Shove. Instead, the Special Attack would be a Bash.

Suppression:
This Weapon would be excellent for keeping Enemies down so you and your teammates can advance, move to cover, or retreat.
