Hello all! I’m having a good time with DT at the moment. And despite all the angst over the Hive Scum decision, I AM having a lot of fun with the class (although I do want to acknowledge that it’s stepping into a playstyle that the used to be in the vet’s wheelhouse).

Anyway, that all said, I wanted write out what I’d love to see for DT over the next year:

  1. Enable solo-mode. Bonus points for having the game actually use your other characters for bots (you know, like in Vermintide 2….). Allow building custom missions (for solo or party finder) with modifiers of your choice. Honestly, just being able to go into a mission solo and mess around / experiment would be really great.
  2. New Geographic Region + Set of Maps. Would love to see a fully new region to explore with a different look and feel. Ideally we’d have a set of three “maps” released over the year to explore. Maps that add onto to the new battlefield location would be good too.
  3. Havoc Improvements - Remove de-ranking (maybe just drop to level 30 when a season resets?) and add quick-play options. Remove the requirement to use party finder (that can stay an option if people want to use it).
  4. New weapons - especially some more ranged options for the Vet, more Ogryn weapons.
  5. Weapon Rebalance - DS and plasma need tuning down, autoguns and lasguns need some buffs outside of really specific builds. Flame staff tune down, etc.
  6. Expand penances - need more penance main track rewards given all the new penances that were added. Obviously adding DLC penances complicates things - maybe those need to be kept on their own track?
  7. Commissary improvements - need some new sets of stuff in the commissary - especially for the DLC classes.
  8. Rework the FOMO / MTX / Premium cosmetic shop - just open up the whole catalog. Rotate items that are on sale for a discount instead of rotating what’s available.
  9. Expand + unify modifier pools across game-modes. So many interesting modifiers are locked behind havoc, or maelstrom, or mortis trial game modes or events. Coming up with a system for using different amounts of these modifiers on normal maps depending on the difficulty level would be great to keep the gameplay variety up.
  10. Expand pool of secondary missions/objectives and give real reasons to go after them. Tie into events more directly.
  11. Always have an event running (or maybe 1-2 week gaps between them). Always provide a final event reward that is Aquillas. Add event modifiers/effects as a possible mutator / modifier for normal missions even after the event is over.
  12. Last big wish - turn Mortis Trials into a proper roguelike / Chaos Waste mode. Seems like it was going in that direction and got cut short. Suggesting that mortis trials be re-worked instead of adding yet more modes. Although if it needed to be a new mode entirely, that’s fine.

What else would you add? What of the list above is most important to you?
