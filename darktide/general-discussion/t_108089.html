Thats not the definition of overpowered. Its the definition of a meta-game in which players take generally balanced weapons but when applied to a specific style are considered strong, thus defining a metagame.

PG is meta because it doesnt require aim and deletes problems. There’s very few people taking PG because they want to be a Guardsman Special Weapons player…

Lasgun does nothing in these players eyes but in the hands of someoen who enjoys running a Lasgun and just. plays. a PG is kinda useless. A boltgun is alright but still meh. A lasgun does everything these weapons do, better, except killing things that require burst damage like Ogryns and Bosses. Everythnig that a Lasgun cant handle instantly can be handled in melee by the teams Ogryn or psyker anyway if the Lasgun is allowed to put shots on the field which requires a screen.
A BG and PG spend ammo (metaphorically not literally) to ignore this, and struggle with range hordes as a tradeoff. As such, since the meta is to DISCOURAGE teamwork, people prefer BG’s and PG’s so they can focus on playing individually rather than skillfully. Skill is required and defined by teamwork, not solo, in a PVE game where all enemies do the same thing over and over.
