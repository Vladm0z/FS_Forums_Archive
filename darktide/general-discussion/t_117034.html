So I was thinking about if a Ratling class came into the game how it could be made to not step on the toes of the Vertan and now Hive scum. And also how to give it some build diversity other than simply “sniper”

The core mechanic could be something I’m calling “cousins”. Mind you I don’t know if this would have any basis in existing lore, or could be folded into lore for ratings on this particular planet. The “cousins” would be like a large extended family network that help each other out. This feature would have the ability to spawn helpful temporary NPC Ratlings, a sniper, or trap layers, or scouts, or maybe ambushes that would attack enemies within spawn points that players can’t get into. Like the tab for hive scum stim cocktail the Ratling could have a “Family tree” they can invest in to build their network of helpful kin.

I imagine that the class regardless of specific path choices would all be fairly ranged focused, in the sam way that Zelot is always atleast somewhat melee focused. So having a kind of standard, ranged, melee, support/defense paths I don’t think would work.

The three major talent path concepts would be:

Scouting: On the left side. Giving visibility and information to the squad. This would also probably have the most sniper focused talents.

Traps: Down the center. Maybe have things like a dead enemy detonating after a short delay. Maybe seeker mines that you can leave behind that help against elites spawning behind the team.

Family: On the right side. Increase frequency, gain buffs for or even provide additional points to spend on the family tree.

lastly, like the Arbitrator, have something like a “Loan Gunman” aura, this would disable cousins from spawning, and give some major buffs so you can be that ultimate lone sniper.
