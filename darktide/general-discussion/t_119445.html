Nice to meet everyone here.I am willing to discuss the balancing strategy and try to give some personal suggestions.You can criticize my suggestions as u want,but plz no personal attack.

1.Enhance some weak weapons and skills,then await.

I’m pleased to see that in this update, many relatively weak (or even trash) weapons and skills have received some buffs, and I’m looking forward to their future performance.However, along with that came the nerfing of some mainstream powerful weapons and skills, which seems to have sparked many debates.

I think the core reason is that

Buffing A while nerfing B seems like telling players how to play the game.

Especially when A and B are in some kind of competition.For example,Psyker’s shield skill.

I speculate that the developers’ intention is to give both sides room to shine, but making adjustments this way may make players feel uncomfortable. So, why not first buff the weaker side and then observe players’ reactions, such as pick rates and other data, rather than nerfing its competitor while buffing.

2.Nerfing should be done carefully

Darktide is a PvE game,it is about loyalist against heresy.I think the best(or better) way to give players challenges is creating New enemy,New map,New element, rather than nerfing players.

Of course,it is OK to do nerf.But we should be careful in doing this kind of things.Because it may represent taking away strength from players.Though it might be helpful to the game environment, it also could be risky.

I read many message about that Fatshark is just nerfing equipment with high pick rate.If that’s the case it could be awful.What about having a deep consideration before nerfing them.

They are too powerful so players are addicted to them**.Or players have to use them.**

Hope darktide to be better and remember to kill more heresy.

Thank u for reading.
