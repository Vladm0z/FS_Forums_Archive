The combat shotguns got a nice buff to their stats, namely:

  1. sprinting doesn’t sap all your stamina now.
  2. base cleave increase against trash enemies.
  3. damage increase.
  4. ammo reserve increase.
  5. Slightly faster reload speed.

All sounds nice, but after trying all the shotguns, I can confidently say excluding the slug shotgun, they still suffer from major flaws, that are now even more apparent after the addition of the Arbites shotguns.

Agripinaa Mk VII (Slug) is now much better at picking targets from range, the ~20% damage increase was really effective, and the finesse boost while not aiming is good too, I think this shotgun is really good now, the slug needs a little bit more damage IMO.

Zarona MK VI (Wide shot) is better than before, but still suffers from the frustrating situation where you feel like the enemy should have died, but they still take 1-2 more shots than what feels right.

Accatran Mk IX (Fire shot) this one is sad, the special ammo does nothing, the ammo economy is pathetic, you can spam the special into a horde and they will still only take minor damage, you are better off never using the special attack on this one, also aimed shots are not that good beyond 20 meters, which is to be expected of a shotgun, but also you would expect close range devastation.

I would suggest lowering all the ammo of the shotguns and increasing their power, making them closer to the arbites shotguns in terms of power, they still should have 2-3 extra shots per clip, but the current 9-13 clip size IMO is the main cause for the shotguns sad state.
