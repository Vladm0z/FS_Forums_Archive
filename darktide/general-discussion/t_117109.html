For the longest time I always believed that if Darktide were to ever see a third faction that wouldn’t just fight us in the Inquisition but also the Moebians and Admonition, a good contender that wouldn’t be as resource intensive as something like Genestealers would involve hostile Hive Gangs - Most notably the Water Cartel as they played a large role in giving Admonition the tools it needed to start the Uprising. Now the release of the Hive Scum class presented a bit of a roadblock with this idea since the gangs affiliated with the Conclave are now allied with the Warband in taking Atoma back from the heretics, but what about the gangs that are outside the Conclave? Those criminals that flout the Conclave’s authority over the seedy underbelly of the Hive and openly reject Rannick’s offers of truce and see us as the very people that would shut them down, especially after seeing Arbitrators within our ranks?

That’s where these unaffiliated gangs come in. These are the Crooks: The lowest of the low within Tertium, ranging from typical Hive Gangers to Crooked Enforcers on the take, who take up arms against us just as readily as they would against any Heretic.

In game, groups of Crook footmen and elites spawn sporadically throughout the maps and are often found fighting against any Heretic enemy that they come across. Being on the bottom of the Atoman food chain during the war, they lack the rugged discipline of the Moebian Sixth or the sheer numbers or tide of Poxwalkers that Admonition uses to bolster their cult followers, nor do they have as many designated specialists or any Monstrosity-tier units. To make up for this, Crooks enjoy a fairly robust roster of Elites that take full use of Stimms in a manner identical to the Blighted Stimm Havoc modifier - Meaning that players encountering these ensuing firefights may not always have the luxury of sitting back and letting the enemies thin each other out lest they contend with a mob of Stimm-addled gangers barreling down on them. Furthermore, Crook patrols tend to be more numerous with rank-and-file units to compensate for not having true horde units.

In appearance, Crooks often resemble the playable Scum/Gangers of the Conclave but without any Inquisition seals or memorabilia, save a few notable exceptions - Furthermore the appearance of the Crooks will be zone-dependent and reflective of gangs seemingly outside of the Conclave. For example Crooks in the Torrent and Hourglass will be represented by Ragged-King loyalists within the Water Cartel and keep their purple colors, while Crooks in Chasm Terminus will be represented by the Chasm Railers with motifs of green and black, Crooks in the Metalfab will be represented by Abyss Stalkers adorned in red and metal-grey, and Durkham’s Raiders clad in blue and white can be found in Throneside as well as the Carnival as they attempt to muscle in on The Show’s holdings while fighting off any Heretics that take notice.

Faction Units:

Name: Crook Thug

Role: Melee rank and file of the Crooks, usually armed with Crowbars or makeshift Clubs and Axes.

Armor/Appearance: Flak on shoulders, Unarmored everywhere else. Very similar in appearance to low-tier Hive Scum armor.

Mechanics: 30% chance to utilize Yellow Stimms, otherwise unremarkable.

Name: Crook Shooter

Role: Ranged rank and file of the Crooks, armed with Autoguns

Armor/Appearance: Flak on shoulders, Unarmored everywhere else. Very similar in appearance to low-tier Hive Scum armor.

Mechanics: 30% chance to utilize Yellow Stimms, otherwise unremarkable.

Name: Crook Gunslinger

Role: Ranged Elite, armed with twin Autopistols

Armor/Appearance: Flak on arms and torso, unarmored on legs and head. Armor most similar in appearance to dead Water Cartel bodies in the Torrent, ammo bandoliers wrapped around the torso, sports spiked hairstyle.

Mechanics: 40% chance to utilize Yellow Stimms. Fires in salvoes of 10-20 rounds, less individual damage per shot compared to Dred/Scab Gunners but boasts hire fire rate. Also able to move freely and far more quickly while firing compared to other ranged enemies, favors medium to close range combat.

Name: Crook Shotgunner

Role: Ranged Elite, armed with Double-Barreled shotgun

Armor/Appearance: Flak on shoulders and torso with arms bare, unarmored on legs and head - Sports a mohawk hairstyle. Armor most similar in appearance to dead Water Cartel bodies in the Torrent.

Mechanics: 40% chance to utilize Green Stimms. Fires in 2 round bursts with quick succession, but longer delay between bursts due to reloading the weapon. Favors medium to close range combat.

Name: Crook Scalper

Role: Melee Elite, armed with twin knives.

Armor/Appearance: Carapace on head and shoulders, Flak on torso, unarmored on legs and arms. Armor resembles highest tier Hive-Scum armor with Gladiatorial helmet and shoulder coverings.

Mechanics: 50% chance to utilize Red Stimms. Prone to feinting/dodging melee swings mid-combo, more easily staggered compared to Scab/Dreg Ragers.

Name: Crook Grenadier

Role: Ranged Specialist, armed with a Grenade Launcher.

Armor/Appearance: Carapace on head, Flak on torso, arms and legs. Armor most similar in appearance to dead Water Cartel bodies in the Torrent but with a welding mask/blast shield for a helmet. Also wears grenade bandolier akin to Scab Bomber.

Sound Cue: <Sound of spinning grenade chamber> <gruff voice. > “Gonna liquify ya!”

Mechanics: 20% chance to utilize Green Stimms. Fires 2-3 grenades identical to standard grenades from Light the Fuse event. Especially strong against Groaners/Poxwalkers, favors long range combat. Prone to fleeing if approached by enemies.

Name: Crook Stimmer

Role: Support Specialist, armed with Stub Pistol

Armor/Appearance: Flak on shoulders and torso, Unarmored everywhere else. Very similar in appearance to mid-tier Hive Scum armor, wears refurbished stimm packs/medipacks on back.

Sound Cue: <Sound of escaping gas>. <Shrill voice> “The doc is in!”

Mechanics: Occasionally lays down refurbished Medipack to apply Green Stimms to allies within range, this effect overrides any existing Stimm effect on allies. Favors long ranged combat and fires Pistol in 2-3 round bursts, prone to fleeing if approached by enemies.

Name: Crook Legbreaker

Role: Melee Elite, armed with Suppression Shield and Shock Maul.

Armor/Appearance: Carapace on head and torso, Flak everywhere else. Wears looted Enforcer Armor with insignias/decor replaced with Gang affiliated logos and color.

Mechanics: 25% chance to utilize Red Stimms. Most similar to Bulwark in behavior but with less stagger resistance, less health than Scab Mauler. Suppression shield able to absorb bullets/las fire but staggers when hit with Bolt rounds, can be pierced by Plasma/Flamers/Warp energy. Arbitrators will have particularly vitriolic callouts when dispatching these enemies.

Name: Crook Kneecapper

Role: Ranged Elite, armed with Suppression Shield and Stub Revolver.

Armor/Appearance: Carapace on head and torso, Flak everywhere else. Wears looted Enforcer Armor with insignias/decor replaced with Gang affiliated logos and color.

Mechanics: 25% chance to utilize Yellow Stimms. Will fire intermittently from behind Suppression Shield, prone to planting themselves between enemies and ranged allies for additional cover. Suppression shield able to absorb bullets/las fire but staggers when hit with Bolt rounds, can be pierced by Plasma/Flamers/Warp energy. Arbitrators will have particularly vitriolic callouts when dispatching these enemies.

Name: Crook Bully

Role: Melee Ogryn, armed with two-handed Spudjacker.

Armor/Appearance: Carapace shoulders and arms, Unyielding everywhere else. Wears makeshift armor similar to Bulwarks/Reapers but sporting gang affiliated colors.

Mechanics: 25% chance to utilize Red Stimms. Moveset is almost identical to Crusher, particularly adept at clearing out Groaners/Poxwalkers with sweeping attacks.

Additional Notes

  • Crook Specialists have separate timers/spawning rules from Dreg/Scab Specialists, they will only spawn when Crooks are on the field.

  • Dreg/Scab Specialists will either prioritize the player or Crooks depending on their roles. Flamers/Bombers/Poxhounds will target both while Trappers and Muties will prioritize players. Crook specialists meanwhile readily target either players or heretics.

  • Crooks are less likely to spawn during mid-mission/final Crescendo events that call a horde to reduce immediate confusion and challenge.

  • Crooks are completely absent during Clandestine Gloriana, Dark Communion, Rolling Steel, No Man’s Land, and Orthus Offensive. Gloriana is too cold for the likes of them whereas the other missions feature notable heretic strongholds/operations. Not even the most reckless of Crooks would openly challenge Wolfer or the Karnaks!
