Cosmophase • 2025-12-30 01:41:56

One thing to keep in mind is that some days will just be worse than others. I have days where I lose 4 or 5 games in a row to just crazy RNG hitting the team or just playing bad. Other days I will win games with little to no issue.

Truth of the matter is, if you wanna have an easier time surviving you need to practice. Practice makes perfect and this game has a much larger learning curve than most other games. Normally, hitting “max level” means you are at end game or somewhat close to it and have grasped most mechanics. In Darktide, hitting level 30 means you passed the basic tutorial.

If you can’t consistently survive Damnation difficulty, you should be in Heresy. If you can’t consistently survive Heresy, you should be in Malice. If you can’t survive in Damnation, you should be no where near a Maelstrom or Hi Shock. I remember spending at least 10 hours in Heresy and another 20 hours in Damnation before I actually felt competent enough to not be a burden on Hi intensity. Guess what? Another 10-15 hours in Hi intensity before I moved to Hi Shock.

Way too many games now give so much instant gratification that it’s no wonder people expect to be the best of the best as soon as they hit max level. You aren’t going to get that here though - enjoy the journey of becoming a measly reject to a veteran capable of carrying entire teams.
