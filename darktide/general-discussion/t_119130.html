Scoreboard mods and PowerDI are known to affect performance, but I assume PowerDI is actually disabled in your case. I don’t think anyone is going to give you a definitive answer. The game is really CPU and GPU heavy. If you are experiencing fps drops, and suspect mods, disable ALL mods, and enable them one by one until you identify the culprits.

I’ve a pretty good PC, Ryzen 7800x3d, RTX 4080 Super, 32GB RAM, a M.2 drive. Even myself I can feel the game stuttering at times, especially after patches. I bet NVIDIA and Microslop DirectX might be too blame too, even if NVIDIA GPU drivers situation is still far better than with Radeon GPUs.

I assume you tried optimising your graphical settings, checking GPU drivers, deleting shader cache, setting the shader cache to “Unlimited”? I heard game crashes can apparently corrupt shader cache creation, so doing this from time to time might be a not bad idea anyway if you crash. I wouldn’t close the game using Alt+F4 also. It’s enough the game often does NOT close correctly by itself…
