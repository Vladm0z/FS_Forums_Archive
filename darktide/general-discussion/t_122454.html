the 2 simple tricks to fighting chaos spawn are

  1. do not be afraid to block, blocking trivializes their fast combos. a friend of mine struggled with them really hard and when he was asked “are you blocking it at all?”. they werent, and when they stopped trying to dodge every single attack suddenly they were pushovers to him, they just cant do anything about it get blocked bozo
  2. never let your right hand side get blocked off. thats the side you need to dodge to to avoid a grab. this means you do actually have to pay attention to the geometry and your general location as you move around with it, but if that side is open you are perfectly safe. also worth noting the grab has a cooldown and it’ll try to use it the moment its available, so youll start to notice that he’ll only do it every 3-5 attacks so you can be more prepared to avoid it when you know its been a bit since the last one

Margeret-Dayhoff • 2026-06-19 06:37:57

As others have said the issue is you’re almost never fighting only the spawn. It’s true threat is the constant knockback into other enemies, over ledges, pools of fire etc.

Ideally you want to fight it with your back to a wall with a little space to dodge sideways, but close to it’s body. Keep up block as it has very low stamina consuption on hit, wait for it to finish the left-right spam attacks, and once it starts the slow rights put an attack of your own in between every hit while blocking the right-arm hits. Oh and dodge into/under the tentacle grab, never backwards. If you get too far away from it’s mouth you trigger the grab.
