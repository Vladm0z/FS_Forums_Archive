I haven’t seen many appreciation posts on the forum, so I thought I’d make one—even if it’s just me.

I’ve played Darktide for a few years, always on console. I know that alone limited my performance a lot, and for a long time I was stuck playing on Heresy. I used inverted look controls and ran builds that were far from the meta—like melee Psyker, which only now gets praised.

The turning point for me was modifying my controller to add paddles and finally switching off inverted look. That alone allowed me to survive Damnation with my atypical builds.

I knew that playing on console meant I couldn’t realistically expect to run Auric or compare myself to Storm Survivor players. But everything changed with the big update that reworked class talents. Suddenly, my weird, off-meta builds actually worked. I stopped dying. I started experimenting with multiple builds across all weapons—even those considered bad. Without following guides, I was finally able to play competitively in Auric.

I was probably never the best player in the group, and I don’t really have the tools to objectively judge my performance—but I felt it, and I saw it: I was actively contributing and helping my team.

I want to thank the Fartshark team for the countless hours of fun this game has given me. I love and play all the classes. I’m genuinely happy with the changes and with being able to be creative with my roleplay builds.

I went from being a total scrub to… a not-so-total scrub.

About a couple months ago, I earned my Auric Storm Survivor title, and the highest I cleared in Havoc was Rank 30.

Dear Fatshart, from a very non-elite Reject who achieved something I once thought impossible:

Thank you.

P.S. If I may make a request: more content—just like everyone else truly wishes.
