I was thinking recently about the new Skitarii class, and in particular their little servo skull buddies. You ever notice how when we get bots they’re only ever Veterans? Having disposable guardsmen is a nice thematic choice, them appearing when a downquitter leaves and picking up a real player to salvage a run is always an heroic event, but they have a tendency to take Medicae pips and get left behind by the players. “Is it really worth it to pick up the bots? They’ll just die again.”

Imagine if instead of an absent player being replaced by a CPU Veteran, they were replaced by what is essentially the Skitarii servo skull. Zipping around, occasionally firing off a few shots from their laspistol, never enough to make a real impact and occasionally picking up a downed player, not taking resources, not taking damage and thus never really needing to be picked up or ignored and generally not getting in the way of things while their spot on the team awaits an actual player to show up. Give 'em those big blue thruster trails like the servo skulls on the Mourningstar have so they’re easier to see. Just something I’d been thinking about.
