zircuits • 2025-05-29 16:39:22

accatran is a high stagger/suppression powerhouse, gets really good value with no respite and it has incredible pellet damage at close range. On zealot, with the dance of death talent, it becomes accurate enough to snipe with. The high suppression means you can reliably push into gunners and fire a shot towards them when your dodge count starts to go into the negatives and you don’t have the stamina or skill or positioning to run perpendicular from them for reset.

Zarona on crit headshot with its wide sweep shot is really good horde damage, and has the best output from manstopper/flechette, making it a pretty good compliment to a weapon’s specialist build on vet, but wouldn’t take it on any other class. The suppression is mid, but the stagger is decent enough to stop anything from shooting at you, but its weak base damage and unimpressive finesse on normal shots make it unreliable for getting quick kills.

Agripinaa is the best shotgun for sniping, rivaling (and, in my opinion, far better than) helbore with its finesse breakpoints. In a marksman’s focus build in havoc 40 you can reliably oneshot everything with the slug except for carapace, which will require a crit headshot for a oneshot breakpoint. You’ll also be able to oneshot headshot most small elites with the normal shot. The largest effort is shooting into dreg ragers, unless you can reliably headshot them as they move they don’t take much damage from body shots. Fullbore and no respite on a marksman’s build with exe stance can magdump a full health beast of nurgle in its back on havoc 40 and leave it at little less than half hp, and if stagger sources it does similar damage to any other boss, but requires some coordination or, most reliably, krak grenades. You can also use manstopper and full bore/no respite, but I don’t find searching for good angles to multi-kill can outdps just shooting normal shots or slug shots.

double-barrel requires speed-load to get the most value out of it, or you end up spending a lot of the time it creates from its high stagger and reliable suppression reloading instead of pushing or dps. Weapon’s specialist on vet can ignore this and crit farm with manstopper, but I don’t find it particularly interesting as a playstyle. You have enough crit chance available with every class to take manstopper, so scattershot is mostly a wasted blessing spot.

aroth68 • 2025-05-29 17:12:48

Manstopper + Scattershot + Veteran’s Deadshot talent makes these things good at clearing hallways.
Aim down sights with 2 stacks of Scattershot or more to get an almost guaranteed crit.

The shotty with the best Finesse multiplier (the Agripinaa) has the best crit damage and sniping potential but also the tightest spread when not aiming. So you always need to try and line up as many targets as possible in a conga line. But when you get a crit headshot, everything in your crosshair gets annihilated.

The ammo economy, as always, is trash, and these babies need some specific-ass talents to make it work, whether you’re using them on Zealot or Vet (and probably at least 1 Stamina trinket, because Deadshot). So it’s a meme-build, essentially.

The Combat shotties also suffer from terrible mobility, even though they’re made to be switched to and from very often (weapon switching to them is extremely quick). It’s the final nail in the coffin for them, IMO.
