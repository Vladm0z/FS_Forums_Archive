No thanks. I like my crusher where it is. In fact, I don’t even think Arbites should have had it (even though it is admittedly a thematic fit).

In general I’m a fan of keeping weapons compartmentalized. The more classes you spread a weapon to, the harder it becomes to balance that weapon, because weapon balance is dictated by the best performing class. Once the dueling sword finally gets the nerf it deserves, every psyker out there will understand this first hand.

I’d much prefer just creating new weapons straight up. Or if you really want a crusher, implement a different mark that’s only available on other classes.
