Setting aside the odd hit reg of the Scab Trapper’s net, it ignores any and all physics that the game sets up in the first place.

What doesn’t is the fact that the Scab Trapper’s net will ignore any and all masses of friendlies in front of them. It can only hit so many heretics before it snags and traps the wrong target. I reckon it would also decrease the amount of rage quitters too.
