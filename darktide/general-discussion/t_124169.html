Before starting the event, clear all the ambients from the area. Then you need two people* to go to the rows of synchronistors. They turn on one by one from left to right until one of them each row fails. You have like ten seconds or so to fix it before it causes a sequence break, which means once it’s fixed you have to repress the button and it will start over from the beginning of that row.

The one that fails has a subtly different electricity effect so you can tell it will fail a couple of seconds before the red synchronistor pops out. Follow the path from left to right and once you complete a row, move up to the next to do the same thing in the next row. As a group of two, one person fixes while the other creates/maintains space.

Some reasons for failure are starting the event before everyone else is ready and in position, before clearing out enemies, and generally just not paying attention to the synchronistors.

Optimally it should take about 90 seconds to get through this event.

Skitarii with servo skull, stealth vet and zealot, and bubble Psyker can do that objective solo without relying on the team.

Ogryn can somewhat do it without teammates covering you and Zealot with stun immunity talent (you don’t get interrupted while interracting with the objective).

Enemies mostly spawn in the far left corner, dropping down from elevation and exiting from the door. It’s a good idea to camp the area near the respawn totem, it’s the safest spot in this room.

Teammates are more likely to help you with the objective if you push first. Most people are scared to push the objective first, someone else needs to be there already. Just stay near the highlighted pylon and clear the enemies, pinging the objective.
