I’m generally asking because due to Vermintide’s lack of enemy spam, there are a lot of quiet moments and last time I played, I realized just how empty the maps are compared to Darktide. On Legendary difficulty you can walk into an entire square and see that it has only a few chaff enemies, one or two elites and that’s it.

Of course what allows for such small spawn rates is things like not being able to run and in-turn never being able to outrun enemies, having very sparce ammo so it’s no use immediately killing the few roaming enemies you see but in the end it creates a very good atmosphere that fits the entire “Everyone’s dead and only rats and heretics remain” feel that the game is going for. It also makes it that much more effective when the horde spawns in, and we’ve gone from quiet to chaos in an instant.

There’s no such feeling in Darkitde. The game never lets player breathe, and it works to the game’ss detriment.

I was hoping the event would make it so we are allowed to breathe and take in the environment, character dialogue etc. but unfortunately this event was just another “fill the quota of requried dev activity on the game” rather than an actual test to see how fewer spawns will affect the gameplay’s experience.
