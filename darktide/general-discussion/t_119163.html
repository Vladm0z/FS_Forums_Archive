Very neat concept and I’m all for seeing additional bosses, but I have a few potential concerns.

First are the armored poxhounds and what kind of armor type they’ll be using. Flak is a-okay but Carapace seems too excessive/durable for a fast disabler, even if they’re supposedly slower than the standard kind. That and with all the criticism of the Plasma and DS meta that’s out there, the very last thing we need is another annoying Carapace enemy that pushes people to those same metas we’re supposed to be moving away from.

Second is how the Packmaster hard counters Cyber-mastiffs. It’s one thing if players are punished for siccing their mastiffs on the Packmaster but what happens when our dogs auto-target the Packmaster while we’re shooting him? At that point it’s more of a punishment just for bringing the dog as opposed to using it incorrectly, and that doesn’t exactly scream good game design.

Time will tell and I’m definitely happy to be seeing more enemies, especially new monstrosities, I just hope that Pack master won’t just bottleneck players into a certain style of play.
