Arbitrator released and is very strong, but other then some minor value tweaking I don’t think he should be changed, in fact I think all classes should be brought up to his level, but the game should become harder to compensate for there power, but not by increasing health/damage just horde makeup and tactics. The enemies should be better equipped tactically and kit wise. Also the game should encourage the players to group more by making enemies force them to slow down.

List of change suggestions:

Add an enemy that forces the players to work together and punish solo fighting.
There should be an enemy (maybe call them the Duelist) that: Cant be stunned/staggered at all, That cant be killed at range(or really inefficient to) until they are in melee combat with another player. An enemy with two states, one where they are guarding, where they take reduced ranged and melee damage and one where they are exposed but the player they lock onto cant dodge there attacks and are forced to block instead. Like the sword Karnak twin, but less aggressive/fast and have carapace on the front of their body and flak on the back. The player engaged with the enemy should be able to kill the enemy if they know there attack patterns and cooldown periods, but it should still take some time as that would let them be overwhelmed by enemies if they don’t get assistance form their team. The enemy should have a moveset that clearly shows when the enemy is guarding/fighting/cooldown. The enemy should never attack the same target that is already engaged by another duelist (i.e. they should have honor) and should wait until the other is dead or target another player. This enemy addition if executed properly should incentives groups to work as a team to take down the hordes of enemies and not just plow on past them as solo warriors.

Make existing enemies change there tactics to group players and not separate them.
Right now most of the disruption enemies in the game force players to spread apart and not fight as a group. For example, the fire bomber throwing a fire grenade in the middle of a group of players will force them all in different directions due to the size of the AoE and how fire works. This disincentivizes players to engage in group play as it makes standing near your ally if they get targeted by a bomber dangerous leading to be trapped into corners or separated. What I would propose to change would be, make fire/tox bombers target entrances and exits to try and box the players in to areas. The first thing a fire/tox bomber should do is target the entrance to the next area the players are headed, then when they reload they should throw the fire/tox behind the players to prevent there escape and funnel them closer to the other enemies, then they should begin re-upping their fire/tox. This would disrupt the players by: separating players who run off ahead, box players in and shrink there area of movement, and allow enemies a better time fighting the players as they are more grouped and less spread out.

Ammo should not be scarce and the limit should be how long players take to get through the level.
By making ammo scarce it discourages player from making gun focused builds and makes them create melee focused builds that favor guns that target only elites/gunners (revolver/boltgun). As it stands, horde clearing with a gun is sub-optimal at best, and griefing at worst. If you make an ammo hungry build that mainly focuses on using guns you deprive other players from ammo, making weapons like autoguns effectively pointless. Also if you load into a game with another player who also focuses on gun, you either have to make them play as a sub-optimal melee build or you have to do it yourself. So my opinion on how to fix this while also not letting ranged become too powerful, is to reduce the ammo reserves everyone has, but make dedicated ammo chests/depots at set intervals that force players to progress through the area to upkeep there ammo needs and punish passive gun play.

Player skill trees should be wider not longer.
The Arbites skill tree layout is very good, most of the perks are good and impactful, where the problem becomes (which is a good problem to have) is you want everything but only have so much you can get making builds more about personal preference and not needs over wants. So what should be changed from the other classes tree’s to make them better. Well first would be to remove/condense the tax nodes. For psyker to go from the scriers gaze ability to the warp siphon keystone, you have to spend 7 skill points to just reach the keystone. Most psyker builds will have to spend about 1/3 of there total perk points on just operative modifiers, while Arbites for comparison only has to spend 2 whole points in total. The second change I would do is remove/condense weak/useless passive nodes. Again for the psyker (I main psyker so I’m more familiar with it) there are nodes that do nothing or actively hurt you for taking them. Perilous Assault, the talent you have to take before you can reach the staff perks Empyric Shock or Channeled Force is useless since staffs and melee weapons already have near instant wield times, so if you want to focus on staves you have to waste a perk on something that doesn’t help. So in conclusion for perk trees, their tax nodes should be condensed, and their should be more freedom to go left and right in the trees, and some perks should be changed to better serve the character.

Minor complaint.
When a barrel blows up don’t send the player flying, they can push the player back on the ground and slow them, but don’t let them send players flying so they cant outright full kill you.
