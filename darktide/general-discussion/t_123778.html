Gdek • 2026-07-08 21:31:19

If we fully commit to the idea and implement ammo replenishment options in talent trees then Survivalist can read something like “Increases ammo replenishment by x%”. Then we can preserve the idea of Survivalist being a tool that helps groups with ammo management, but in a way that isn’t game altering as it just enhances whatever base option they are using.

IMO, each class should have a few different ammo conservation and replenishment options sprinkled around their tree. Melee builds should have ways to replenish ammo through melee gameplay but it should be fairly slow to the point that melee builds have to be pretty conservative with where they are spending ammo.

All ranged builds should have ammo replenishment that operates around a conservation/burn/replenish cycle. IE typical gameplay would involve trying to be conservative and optimal with ammo expenditure and only use ammo as fast as you are regaining it. The goal of which is to have enough reserve ammo that you can then dump when you need damage now in a burn phase. Afterwards you would need to be focusing on actively replenishing your reserves which creates times of vulnerability that others in the group will have to cover.

Actual mechanics could vary to different degrees based on class. For instance Ogryn Gunlugger might have more access to burn phases by having free ammo during his ability, but might have weaker than average replenishment.

Overall, I really wish that Fatshark was a bit more creative with the base gameplay. There’s so many holes I think in the basic combat design and balance that has allowed combat to become too spammy and simplistic and there’s a real lack of need of real group tactics or synergy outside of Havoc (which I dislike the implementation of for other reasons).
