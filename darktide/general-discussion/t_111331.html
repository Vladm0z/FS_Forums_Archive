I think the game could use some new enemies, and I would love to see Ogryn specials, here some random ideas I have:

  1. Ogryn bomber: Loud and big, he fiddles around with his hand held nuke till he figures out how to pull the pin (giving players time to react and some sound cues to spot him), when thrown, the bomb has a long fuse, but has a bigger AOE and deals high damage (doesn’t go through toughness though, or maybe it should?) and launches all enemies and players inside the AOE back.
  2. Ogryn supply mule: an ogryn with a packback filled with goodies, will idle around till he spots a player, then he will start sprinting away from them knocking enemies down similar to a mutant charge.
  3. Ogryn berserker: A bigger tougher berserker unit that doesn’t come in packs, will charge a targeted player and attempt to mow them down, he knocks both non-target players and enemies away with the charge and with each attack, will get tired after a flurry or two, giving players a chance to safely take him out.
  4. Ogryn sniper carrier: an ogryn with a platform strapped to his back with a sniper perched on it, the sniper hides behind cover, and can only be killed by killing the ogryn himself.
