There is a common misconception that IX is the best mark, people use, find out that it’s trash and say heavy swords are bad. They used to be pretty bad due to Crusher spam and low boss damage, while it’s true to this day, mark VI is amazing on crit Zealot with great horde clear and elite/special bullying. You have to use a very specific crit build (left tree essencially), and Deathblow + Chained deathblow. Heavy attack slashes have very generous hitboxes and Deathblow crit allow you to decapitate multiple ragers with one hit.

I haven’t used it on Hive Scum, so no comment on it.

TL;DR: IX is trash, VI and VII are great on crit zealot.

It’s horizontal heavies deal less damage than the diagonal heavies of the other marks while having less cleave (IX heavies have 9.5 vs 11.7 of the others) despite being classified as a vanguard attack.

Meanwhile the IX has a profile that is almost identical to the devils claw on its horizontal heavies, including their cleave damage distribution, allowing you to damage many more enemies in a single swing.

This however doesnt make the IX any better sadly, due to the existence of Deathblow.
