brosgw • 2026-01-31 15:15:21

You’ll see the indicator at the bottom right where your other indicators are like slaughter stats. It’s a red square. Each stim permanently adds 10%. It maxes out at 100%. It doesn’t make you immune to pox gas but it lowers the toughness damage quite a bit (unsure if it does anything else).

From there it depends how you build your character. I played melee focused and usually 3 toughness curious, and just got toughness back spamming melee into enemies while in the gas.

If there is no enemies I would try harder to leave.

I did stealth zealot with beacon of purity and 3x health curios and that made the gas pretty trivial to me honestly. Way easier than scum I just didn’t care if my toughness was gone.

Edit:

Stim 10x as quicky as possible and then stim only when needed/desired. Using this build really makes the event easy mode^ (personally for me, even on auric).
