Vet Shout Needs Changing

20 Posts • 617 Views
M
McJager • 2026-09-24 13:06:59

To make it clear. I’m not here to complain about gold toughness a being removed.

Vet Shout caught two nerfs before they removed gold toughness. These nerfs were targeted at reducing gold toughness uptime.

They reduce gold toughness, lasting from 15s to 10s. They also and increased the cool down on shout from 30s to 40s.

With gold toughness being completely removed, it make zero sense to keep the 40s cooldown. Also, the longer you make cooldowns, the more of a requirement it is to have ability cooldown reduction.

Even if the do give shout the 30s cooldown, I’m not entirely convinced the ability is gonna be even a fraction of how strong it used to. So maybe making it more of a team centered buff would be nice. Giving your teammates faster stamina regeneration, and melee attack speed. Those could potentially make it better without make the ability completely busted.

Altogether, though, I think we need to wait and see how shout is going to be handled and played after the update. Maybe the additional passive nodes, you can pick up on the tree will offset this issue. But the 40s cooldown has got to be changed.

TLDR; Vet Shout has a 40s cooldown, because of Gold Toughness. But Gold Toughness is getting removed. Bring shout back to a 30s cooldown.
