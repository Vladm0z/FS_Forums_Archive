This update (like many others) highlights the massive disconnect between Fatshark and the community on what should be one of the most important topics : balancing.

Fatshark seems to balance the weapons and classes only using spreadsheets showing pick rate so obvious outliers get buffed and nerfed but those buffs and nerfs rarely feel sensible because the people in charge of balancing at Fatshark don’t seem to play the game and don’t seem to understand the reason why some options are way more popular than the others.

Most if not all of the balancing problems in the game right now come from two things, the absurd carapace spam in high difficulty and the ability for enemy bullets to completely ignore the mass of the rugby team of Ogryns currently pushing your team back. I will take the plasma gun’s nerf as an example because it seems to be the hottest topic right now in term of balancing and is pretty much the embodiement of the problem.

Is the plasma gun weaker and more unwieldy now ? Yes, obviously. Is the charged shot better ? Statistically yes. Is the plasma gun pick rate going to go down significantly in high difficulty content ? Extremely unlikely because plasma gun is still the best answer Veteran has to deal with gunners hiding behind carapace clown cars and people are still going to spam uncharged shots over charged shots because the charged shot buff doesn’t change the fact that ammo in high difficulty is the rarest resource and wasting 8 shots worth of plasma to only deal slightly more than twice the damage is not worth it. The only way plasma gun would ever fall off the current meta in high difficulty content would be to bring it back to pre talent tree state with no cleave on the uncharged shot and that would make the weapon unusable exactly like it was back then.

Right now the game in high difficulty feels like a bad run of Vermintide 2 Cataclysm but instead of only having to deal with Chaos Warriors patrols with a bunch of trash mobs around you are always dealing with full patrols of Chaos Warriors somehow backed by 10 ratling gunners that can shoot through them and will kill you in 0.2 second. The priority of the next big balance patch should be to tone down the carapace spawn heavily (you know, the thing that was promised when Fatshark massively increased the health of Crushers) because balancing will never work as long as the enemies with the highest amount of HP are completely immune to 90% of the weapons and you fight dozens of them at a time in high difficulty. If you look at difficulties where you don’t have to deal with a 50/50 ratio of carapace you can see diversity, you can see people running shredder autopistols, autoguns or even infantry lasguns but all that diversity disappears as soon as you reach the carapace spam tiers because not preparing for carapace clown cars is a recipe for disaster.
