So first of all let me say I think it’s great you went through and updated so many enemy damage locations to more accurately reflect their 3D model. Cheers for that.

However, I can’t help noticing a few seem to have slipped through the cracks. The scab flamer for one, while dressed almost identically to the scab shooter, they’re Maniac type all over while the scab shooter is prominently Flak in many locations, as is the signature for scabs. I submit you could do worse than simply copy the damage location type data of the scab shooter over to the scab flamer, and perhaps adjusting their hp to make up the difference if desired.

*MAYBE the trapper could receive a similar treatment, but in that instance I think it’s mostly fine. If you do introduce a dreg trapper variant, however, I think differentiating between them with one being mostly Flak armoured while the other is mostly Maniac could be desirable. I.e. change the current trapper’s model and name into the hypothetical dreg trapper model, and create a new scab trapper enemy that is mostly Flak armoured, assigning it the current scab trapper model.

Likewise, it feels a little strange that the dreg tox bomber is Unarmoured all over while the almost identically dressed dreg tox flamer is Maniac all over. I submit, again, you could do worse than making them maniac all over, like the dreg tox flamers. I’ve always thought they die a little too easily, almost as easily as basic groaners. Though with how annoying their tox bombs are, maybe that’s a good thing.

Not a huge deal by any means, and you wouldn’t even notice it outside of the psykhanium, but something to consider, perhaps.
