This

As a Destiny 2 Refugee, Raids where one of my most hated experiences. ‘Get 5 other random chuckle ducks together and watch them run around like chickens with their heads cut off until finally enough right happens that you get the drop you don’t need and the dude that did the least amount of work gets the super wonder drop and everyone hates them and you leave’. They are a puzzle game crammed into an FPS shooter, they suck, pure and simple. This isn’t to say they HAVE to suck, but every single one is designed like said puzzle game first, instead of like an interesting challenge to enhance the combat. This results in ‘put the round peg in the round hole’ gameplay (sometimes even literally), and makes the whole experience a exercise in patience and learned hatred for your companions, verses an actual fun team based experience.

This game was liberating coming to after finally quitting said Destiny 2, and realizing ‘wait, you’re telling me I don’t have to subject myself to social torture every week for a drop of a weapon that doesn’t even matter because the game’s power crept to heck and back and abilities are the only thing that actually does anything anymore? And all I have to do is carry some brainlets sometimes and honestly just feel better about doing that? WOOW!’. As most have stated, a vast amount of the community already has trouble holding the interact button in areas that require it, much less having to actually do complex ‘mechanics’ (forced puzzle solving) while juggling 10 crushers, 5 bulwarks, and 8 gunners with a trapper running around.

People play FPS shooter games to shoot things, there’s a reason even in Destiny 2, raids are played by like 2% of the player base, and even Dungeons (which are strictly better designed as at least they can be completed by just one person knowing what to do) are mostly ignored by the wider population because they don’t have quick matchmaking. People don’t want to do forced puzzle solving (that isn’t even puzzle solving past the first few runs because the puzzles don’t change, so it just becomes ‘solving’ at that point and again, an exercise in patience not enjoyment), they want to shoot the funny men and get dopamine from watching heads pop and VFX on their screen, and adding a ‘you wipped’ screen every 5-10 minutes because you/your team can’t put the round peg in the round hole and keep trying to put it in the square hole actively hinders that enjoyment. It isn’t fun for basically any of the their target audience outside the streamers that are so bored with the solved combat that they think doing extra puzzles on top of it that actually slightly activate their brain is fun, when if any of them went to play an actually good game they’d probably swap off D2 in a heartbeat (or if their livelihood wasn’t now tied to it, mostly the later).

So, no, no I do not think we should have a Raid in this game, and ‘to answer the question’, if they added one it would be completely ignored, a massive waste of dev time that could have went into a new map (like Clandestine Gloriana, give me more Clandestine Gloriana), and probably also be just strictly unfun given the only template FS has to work off of is the failed experiment that is Destiny 2. I don’t need more ‘Witness raises his hands, shoot the right sha- and someone already shot the wrong circle…welp there goes 15 minu- dies’ in my life, I left it for a reason. (well, I left it for many reasons but that was one of them xD).
