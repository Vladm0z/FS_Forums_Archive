NERFS CAN MAKE THE GAME MORE FUN

When arbites first came out, it filled a niche that had poor representation in darktide at the time, that of a stagger heavy frontliner that wasn’t jank like ogryn. A foot knight type instead of a bardin type you could say. The problem was, that arbites was soooo strong and durable that he wasn’t fun to pilot. I would play him and literally zone out of the game because i never felt any danger or stakes that would actually get me to engage with the game on a satisfying level. Since then, they’ve nerfed his toughness generation (the main thing making him feel invincible) and the increase to elite hp and buffs to other classes no longer make him kill everything and top damage for free every game. This has lead to him being in a sweet spot where he is still quite strong (especially so with outliers like the Buddy Shotgun) but you do actually have to engage with the game to do well, and that makes arby more fun and satisfying than he was on release. The nerfs made him more fun.
