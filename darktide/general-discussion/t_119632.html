Regardless of opinions on balance, there’s a lot that can be agreed upon with darktide’s approach to content. Expeditions seems to have fallen flat, mortis trials is just an abandoned game mode for now and ever, and the main game mode/adventure mode content has had little to no content, the base game classes themselves have received little to no content (most likely in favor of content being designed for the current dlc classes and the future one[s]), and balance is incredibly stale. The dlc classes seem to be receiving no appreciation whatsoever in cosmetics, and that’s only the surface level series of problems that the game has.

The recent balance discourse was over a mere cdr change to a couple abilities that everyone recognized to be incredibly potent, and that inspired people from an entirely different part of the world from the majority of people common on the forums to go out of their way to engage the community.

That, I think, is healthy. Two completely different parts of the world in the same game with completely different opinions on the outlook of the game. We rarely get that type of engagement, and I hope fatshark doesn’t see the initial reaction from this and assume that it would necessarily repeat itself, and continue to instead invest way more time into critically designing the game with these multitude of opinions in mind rather than just what development has felt like, ignoring the current state of things while moving forward to new ideas that seem extremely rushed or unfinished when released, weaving a complicated web of problems that plague the game, and just touching one strand ensnares its players.

The HK community has given us a great chance at the moment to make a statement, and give the game an honest review with our opinions of what we think about not just this development cycle, but the current state of the game. The devs listen to the numbers and the feedback, we know this from launch when they issued an apologia, and we know this kind of thing works from looking at helldivers 2, or Total War. You don’t get much in results from just going with the flow or arguing on a forum, even big names in both the test servers and on youtube largely go ignored, but this is a wonderful time to make a big impact on the game as a community worldwide.

I know you hate this idea, but you can, in fact, read the reviews people are making for a game.
