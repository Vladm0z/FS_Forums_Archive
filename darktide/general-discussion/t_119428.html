Fat Shark’s approach to weapon updates has fallen into a kind of “Helldivers”-style balancing trap—whenever everyone is using a certain weapon, they nerf it. They think the weapon is overpowered, but in reality, it’s just that all the other weapons are too weak.
Compared to the Plasma Gun, Laser and Headhunter Guns lack penetration—even a single zombie can block shots meant for Gunners and Specialists. The high headshot requirement also places greater demands on players’ accuracy (coupled with the lack of penetration, it’s like trying to hit a target in an amusement park with an air gun through a pile of obstacles). The low damage multiplier against armor means we simply can’t threaten Crushers and can only polish their armor.
The same logic applies to the Heavy Sword, Catachan Sword, and Shovel. I can’t imagine how a new player, armed with the Inquisition’s worn-out shovel, is supposed to deal with Crushers on even N2 difficulty. When the Catachan Sword clinks and clanks against hard armor, I even feel like I’m here to perform music for the heretics.
That’s the problem—too many weapons are just too weak, leaving us with no real choice.
We want a diverse arsenal where every weapon has the potential to turn the tide, not having to sift through junk.
