I will write some of my suggestions for the next updates for darktide, I’m open for every criticism so let me know if you agree or disagree with my suggestions.

1) Suggestions for new type of level:

I have noticed that the community likes levels that have a different design from what we are used to (example: the train that is a linear timed level not our typical ‘‘room to room’’ level), so I suggest the addition of a level that starts like a normal mission, say the team is sent to destroy or investigate something and then the heretics deploy something that the team simply isn’t capable of destroying and thus they have to rush to the nearest extraction point while being followed by this undefeatable extremly slow enemy and the team will also have to clear waves of enemies coming from the front and the sides trying to slow them down for the undefetable enemy to kill.
My suggestions for this undefetable enemy are 2: either some tank like the scylla or the chimera, lorewise we can just say that the 6th moebian brought them with them when they deserted because they are light tanks easy to move and that it’s moving so slowly because they don’t really have the means to do proper maintenance, or a team of 3 plague marines, lorewise we know that space marines are extremly fast compared to humans but we also know that the ones corrupted by Nurgle turn instead into slow tanky masses of meat so we can use that as an explanation to why they are so slow and tough.

2) Suggestion for new enemies:

Dartktide could use a couple of new enemies, the first that comes to my mind would be an easy to implement one, a rocket launcher enemy, just an easy reskin of the specialists that we already have, give him a sniper like behaviour; it keeps his distance, takes ages to aim, the rocket is very slow but it does immense damage if it hits, even if the player has some kind of shield (a version that shoots the green gas that boosts the enemy could even be implemented like you guys have already done for the grenadier).

Also a new boss enemy came to my mind, the toad dragon from warhammer fantasy.
I’m not sure if in the lore of 40k we have this beasts but we already share the beasts of nurgle, the great unclean ones and the soul grinders with both lores so I don’t see why we shouldn’t interchange this one too.
It would be basically a big hound that is just a boss instead of a regular enemy, so a very fast moving boss that jumps around a lot, something that we really don’t have at the moment.

I would also suggest the addition of a new type of mini-boss like enemies:
Plague toads and plague drones, packs composed by 4 units of extremly durable low damage dealing, slow moving units that would really give that nurgle like vibe to the hoards we are fighting.
Now I realize that if a tanky enemy without any real damage dealing potential was to be added like this the players would simply ignore them and go for the surrounding hoard only clearing them ones the real threat has been killed thus making them a useless enemy but I thought of a way to fix that problem, we give to the beasts some kind of area buff that improves the rest of the enemies near them forcing the player to target the monsters firts, something like ‘‘swarm of flies’’ or ‘‘putrid aura’’.
Lorewise we can just say that the corruption of atoma from the cults have been going on for so long since the start of the war that new breeds of monsters have started growing in the forgotten back alleys and sewers of the hive.

3) My last suggestion for improving the game is about the expeditions outside the hive, the open world ones:

Now as now the community has been complaining about the fact that they are useless (and they are right, there is no reason to play those missions) I thought of a way to change that.
Turn salvage into a resource that can be extracted out of the mission and add some gadgets that can be bought with salvage and tech remnants.
Those gadgets should add minor bonuses in order not to unbalance the classes by changing what makes them unique, so far I thought of these potential gadgets:
1) Pistol holster: gives you a third weapon that you can bring into battle, this weapon will be a new small insignficant low damage dealing pistol with maybe like 30 shots, it should be given the same weapon progression system that we already have but with maybe half of the points to put into progression, it would just be a small insignificant prize that people would buy out of curiosity and that wouldn’t really change anything in the game.
2) Syringes holder: It allows you to bring one extra stim with you, nothing that crazy but still somewhat meaningfull enough for people to farm it.
3) Grenade pouches: increases max grenades you can bring by +1, arguably somewhat op, but again it would definitely give people a reason to farm the expeditions
4) Light scout armor: increases max toughness by like 5% or something along those lines, possibly op so maybe change the number to like 2-3% if 5 is too much.
5) Mortar strikes/Artillery strikes/Air strikes: flares used to call in strikes, only usable in open maps, you give them like a 7 minutes cooldown so you can only use it like 3 times per mission and it can be bought with salvage and tech but it needs to farm kills in order to be upgraded (with salvage and tech of course).
6) Like 3 different scopes you can put on your weapons: just a visual change to weapons, nothing crazy that changes the stats of the weapons and thus dosen’t unbalance the game, would make the player feel like some weapon personalization was added to the game.
If we lack any meaningfull scopes in the lore just use like a real life red dot, holographic sight and some weird russian pattern like the okp-7.

The lore reason why we got this could just be something along the lines of ‘‘The 21st and the 53rd Moebian are starting to have some problems resupplying themselves on Atoma, thus do to our warbands latest success Commissar Dukane allowed some limitated trade between the warband and the guard, exchanging salvage and tech remnants for some exccess meaningless scout gear’’.

Also I have a suggestion for a new ‘‘event’’ for the expeditions, add something along the lines of ‘‘emperors shrines’’ or ‘‘emperors ruined abbeys’’ or something else similar in the desert and give the team the secondary quest of defending them from the traitors while servo skulls inspect them, if the team manages to win the mission they get one favour point to use in a new shop managed by Sister Hestia Prine.

In this shop you buy 1 time use buffs per mission, something like prayer books that cost 1 point and give you either an increased damage/defense/hp.
And for 3 points a 1 time use auto revive when you go down, something like ‘‘The emperors blessed icon’’.
Of course you can only have one of these per mission so no spamming defense, attack and hp in one run by one player, but maybe make them have a timed effect on the whole team so people playing in stacks can if the properly organize have an easier time.
