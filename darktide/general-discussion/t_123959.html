Hi there,

I have many hours in this game and, while I am generally pleased with the current state of the game, I do have some recommendations for it.

To start, I believe that the Skitarius talent tree set up is clearly superior to all other skill trees and allows for more interesting and varied builds than the other classes–all classes should be changed to this format.

Next are bullet weapons, which suck and have done so for a very long time, specifically with regards to the autogun style weapons. I’m not sure why this hasn’t been addressed, but it has been a nagging issue for I want to say a year and a half. Even if there were talents just for the Veteran, it would at least allow one class to make good use of them. Perhaps giving them an innate (and noticeable) damage bonus against flesh, monstrous, unyielding, etc (basically all non-armor) would help, as well as increasing ammo reserves. I also believe they should cause bleed, perhaps via a talent in the Veteran tree (or crafting). Something to help these weapons–I really want to use them, but they are simply not viable end game. Also, we absolutely need Heavy Stubbers (and hellguns)–why do the enemies of the Imperium get more fun and powerful weapons than we do?

Lastly, something I have always wanted for the Psyker is a true Blitz build, where you actually feel like a Psyker instead of a staff wizard who occasionally can use pyschic abilities. To this, end, I think a fun keystone (or talent, in general) would be to prohibit the use of any ranged weapons but, to compensate, empower the Psyker’s inherent abilities (blitzes). This would basically be the equivalent of a lone wolf Arbites, for example. I was thinking the following:

Brain Rupture: changed to Crush–basically implode an enemy (and cause area damage around it due to its body parts/gore flying around like shrapnel). This power was demonstrated in an Astra Miltarum novel I have previously read (I forgot the exact novel, but if pressed I could find it.) This would be fun, cool, and impressive.

Smite: changed to Electrocute–could have the same effect as Smite, but maybe it could actually do damage (yes I am aware smite technically does damage). It could also have a much smaller forward arc and only hit in a straight line, but massively increase damage, especially to armored targets.

Assail: changed to Shardstorm–constantly release a torrent of the shards in a forward cone with shorter range and increased cleave.

This keystone would also come with a reduction to their cost or, in the case of rupture, remove the charge limit and reduce the cost.

It could also affect the abilities:

Venting shriek to psychic scream: reduces range, but vents all perils and deals massive AOE damage around the Psyker, also causing bleeds–could have a longer cooldown to compensate.

Telekine Shield–could create a smaller bubble that moves around with the Psyker. Would be fun for melee-focused builds.

Scrier’s Gaze: Not really sure how to go about changing this, honestly. More creative minds than mine could think of something.

Anyways, I just wanted to throw in my two cents. Hope they are welcomed.
