Body: Hey everyone,

With the recent debates about Fatshark nerfing certain player mechanics, I’ve noticed a specific narrative popping up. A vocal minority responded by claiming Asia players take the Havoc 40 title way too seriously. The accusation is that we rely on “broken” or “crutch” builds to clear it, have zero self-awareness of our actual skill level, and act like we’re invincible just because we got the title.

I want to set the record straight on how our community actually views this.

First off, no one here thinks clearing Havoc 40 makes them a god. While it is legitimately requires solid teamwork, the reason so many players want to keep their Havoc 40 title has nothing to do with a massive ego. For a huge chunk of the player base, maintaining Havoc 40 is just a weekly routine. Think of it like a weekly challenge in a mobile game—it’s simply a standard, structured goal people log in to complete with friends to unwind after a long day at work.

Secondly, regarding the claim that we lack self-awareness and rely on “crutch” builds: let’s be real. Yes, Havoc 40 is genuinely difficult, but absolutely no one in our region clears Havoc 40 and thinks they are suddenly a god gamer. That mindset just doesn’t exist here. Instead, the community looks up to the players who pull off Duo and True Solo runs. There is a unanimous agreement among us that only these players have earned the right to claim they are genuinely skilled. And frankly, we have no shortage of players doing exactly that.

So, to the very small group of people acting superior and claiming we are “crying” over an ego-boost: you are completely misreading the room. We aren’t upset because we think we’re invincible.
