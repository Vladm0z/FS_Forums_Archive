I was quite excited for the Skitarii Ranger archetype, as I love the look/sounds of the Galvanic Rifle and Phosphor pistol. It is disappointing, however, that the ranged-focused ability turns out to just be an aimbot. This has nothing to do with power or balance, purely fun-factor. I don’t want the game to aim for me, I’ve been doing that in FPS games since the days of Quake, and though this may come as a shock, I do actually enjoy it.

My hope is that this ability can be changed to something that supports the ranger archetype, but doesn’t remove a fundamental level of interaction from the player experience. Don’t have my computer do the work, let ME fight for the glory of Mars.

There are plenty of less powerful ranged weapons in Darktide, and their underperformance stems from an inability to reliably land weak-point hits—especially against bosses. In truth, these weapons boast substantial weak-point damage multipliers; they would be devastating if every single shot connected with an enemy’s head. I once hoped the Skitarii’s auto-aim ability could breathe new life into these underwhelming arms, yet its performance in actual combat left much to be desired. It might work far better if the auto-aim skill treated every hit, regardless of body part struck, as a weak-point hit.
