  • Snipers
  • Mutants

Why Big Boom?

  • Panic Button, when players start to drop like flies, my BB give me small respite to get sh%& done before its too late. Sah!
  • Rager/Gunner spam delete button
  • There are a surprising amount of Grenades being un-used. And lobbies are getting better and better in recognizing who needs what, they will ping Grenade for Ogryn and ensure I have their back.

Rock - If mutants, snipers & gunners annoy you that’s what you want. The downfall is that you cannot deal with tough situations of hordes overwhelming you unless somebody else has crowd control then you’re golden. It’s cool they regenerate though.

Box - Stirring up chaos if there’s hordes of elites it also buys you some time & it also procs Skullcrusher to kill things a bit faster within the horde because of the stagger it produces. It’s also great for picking up teammates when they’re down. Not too many downfalls, honestly… unless you don’t see any nade pickups for a bit. This is one of my personal favorites only because it’s gotten me out of those hard situations & in my humble opinion the strongest choice out of the three. Like somebody else mentioned, you can stagger bosses & DH killing them in one-go without much consequence.

Nuke - This one is a hit or miss for me personally. It’s really good if there’s a dense horde you can kill tons of elites in one go, but you can only carry one unless it’s one of the game modified matches. Otherwise, it’s mid for me. Admittedly, it has its fans for sure. A cool thing is that it’s really good in Havoc-40 when you need to wipe-out a cult in a rush.

It all depends on what you’re going for with your load-out.
