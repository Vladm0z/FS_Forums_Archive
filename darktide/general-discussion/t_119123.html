Kinda pointless and unfair poll, tbh. As I don’t see any enemy in the game “unfun”. I mean there is nothing wrong with their design in general, it’s just certain decisions, certain touches FS made to some of them which often make them extremely frustrating.

Like, Trappers and dogs are fine, but the fact they can disable you from inside of a crowd is absolute frustrating bs that has no justification whatsoever, except catering to a few bored sweats out there who are not “challenged” enough by properly designed enemy which you can realistically avoid.

And there are actually better ways they could implement it if they would like for trappers to still be a danger even when they are in a midst of a hoard. Like, add a new animation for them allowing them to raise their hands with the gun above the crowd, or teach them to climb smaller objects, like boxes or rails, to try to shoot at your from an elevated position; and add some lighting effects to that move, making it clearly visible to you, so you could have a fair chance to react etc. Or make the dog in the crowd to pounce not a you directly, but sort of to a nearby wall or pole above the crowd - then bounce off that object towards you; would be bad-ass and again would give you a fair chance to react. But here we are, stupid reasons and lazy implementations.

But if I would have to choose one the most irritating thing in DT I would like removed for good, it’s freaking barrels. It’s absolute random, unfair, stupid crap that has no place in a hoard shooter with so heavy melee component. Or at least their kickback must be reduced several times, and there must be a hardcoded behavior which ensures you NEVER will be thrown to some pit for insta-death by a barrel explosion, EVER. Like, always will be left hanging on some rails or edge, eligible for saving. A random crap that insta kills you is a straightway reason to just quit the match immediately for me, I can accept I did something wrong and got downed so often I got captured, fine. I won’t accept somebody just hitting a barrel accidentally which then insta killed me out of random, nope. That is the top most unfair and stupid mechanic in the game rn, for me. Doesn’t have any right to exist.
