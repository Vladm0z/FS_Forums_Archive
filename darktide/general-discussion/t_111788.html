GrimGearheart:

Is there a reason I should use the rock?

Because Rock.

Rock..is Rock. Rock is life. Rock do Rock things, make Ogryn happy.

Rock has high damage, incredible accuracy, extremely long range, and is available in greater volume than other grenade options, doesn’t use your ranged ammo, and has an extra node to really help throw them out with greater confidence. It’s amazing for dealing with Trappers, Muties, Snipers, Flamers, and the like, at literally any range.

If you really like the grenade launcher and run it routinely, you may not see a whole lot of need for Rock. But Rock is glorious in and of itself, and pairs wonderfully with other weapons like the Ripper Gun or Stubbers.

MarxistDictator • 2025-08-16 02:01:16

You gotta run an extra talent to get rocks back for headshots or misses. I usually do for memes but in general ogryn power 1 node to meme is expensive.

The rock advantage is in that it draws much faster and doesn’t burst on hit, in addition to the obvious functioning outside the resource economy of grenades. When the rock hits something it doesn’t go away, it can hit another target like a half second later for full damage. With this knowledge and perfect timing and spacing you can set up very funny double kills.

Box does more Unyielding damage, but without a weakspot hit it’s not a wide margin and with the sluggish delays on box it’s hard to pull off. It also does no on hit stagger even if you do hit a weakspot - only the inner radius of the grenade explosions can stagger monstrosities. Rock I routinely dash into a spawn and then feed it a couple rocks to stagger lock it…the team usually kills it before it recovers or I run out.

Rock removes mutants and specials quicker than some of the Ogryn’s ranged weapons which might require multiple shots or waiting for stuff like Surgical stacks in case of Heavy Stubbers, it’s can even oneshot enemies across the room where stuff like Kickback loses its punch.

For example, if you’re using knife or karsolas pickaxe, in Havoc you can’t oneshot mutants and ragers without full buffs (fully charged Thrust hit etc), but with rock you can do it if you land headshots.

It’s the best blitz for kiting enemies, since the other two grenades you don’t always use optimally or you just won’t have them at the right time. There’s a good chance you will sometimes waste nuke/box on situations where you didn’t really need them, unless you only use them when somebody is down and you’re surrounded by dozens of enemies. But in that case, you can sometimes go whole games without using your blitz, if your team is good enough.

In Maelstorms with mutant squads a single rock can kill 2+ mutants at a time when they’re running right behind each other.

I think rock is the best if you plan to use your blitz all the time without fearing to “waste” it.

Ogryn is the only class where I use all three blitzes constantly.
