Darktide taught me what a toxic relationship feels like.

I love this game.
There is no other game like this one. I love the lore, the atmosphere, the characters, the enemies, the banter, the gameplay, the dodging, the blocking, the fighting, the challenge of modes like havoc40.

But it doesn’t love me back.
There are soooo many bugs and they keep coming back. Once you think they’re fixed there’s a patch and that old bug from 3 years ago is back. Or they just never get fixed. Or new ones appear and you try to work your way around them. But you die sooo many times just to the bugs. Silent ninja-crushers sneaking up and oneshotting you out of nowhere. Pox Bursters ignoring your push or running through your kinetic shield. Bosses deathpopping you. Or just the gamedirector going nuts and throwing everything everywhere all at once at you.

But still you try to make it work.
An then it hits you with lags, errors and disconnects. You fight through all that bullsh°t for over an hour and suddenly the enemies start sliding over the floor… Error, disconnect, reconnecting and game session ended.

This game could be the goat, and I’d honor it by delving into Fashiontide, but after years of Fatshark not managing to tame the beast, I just can’t spend another dime on flowers.

Long story short: New update and we’re back at graphic problems, bugs, errors and disconnects. Great work Fatshark!
