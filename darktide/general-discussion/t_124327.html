Every 'tide game has some variant of daggers that invoke all of the eldritch powers of the Rule of Cool to be WAY more effective than they have any right to be. I get it; feeling like that nimble character who darts between the enemies, striking them down with their immense skill fits perfectly with the series. And, of course, a dagger slid into the gaps is one of the most definitive ways to kill something in heavy armor.

But really, can’t this be reigned in? Like, I feel like these types of weapons don’t actually need massive weakspot modifiers on top of massive crit modifiers. What if they had great crit mods but not amazing weakspot mods? Or vice versa? Something that can preserve the coolness factor, keep them viable, but not always make them incredibly powerful choices.

Or at least just nerf the shivs in particular.
