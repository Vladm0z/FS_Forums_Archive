The Infested counterpart to Crusher:

t40k-15-plaguebearer-of-nurgle1920×2269 366 KB

Where the Crusher draws strength in devastating overheads and protection through heavy Carapace armor; the Plaguebearer draws strength in melee attacks that apply Corruption when health is damaged and protection through gifts of Nurgle.

Gifts of Nurgle

Vile Presence:Periodically, Plaguebearers grant nearby allies Cranial Corruption .

Plaguebearers are very resistant to stagger, yet slow and lumbering.
“When strikes do land upon Plaguebearers they seem to have little effect, for the minions of Nurgle feel no pain, and shrug off what should be lethal strokes”

Melee attacks from Plaguebearers inflict Corruption damage.
“Each Plaguebearer wields a rusted blade called a Plaguesword that corrupts flesh in an instant”

Plaguebearers are very resistant to ranged attacks, headshots give no bonus damage.
“Should a foe endure the stench and not be struck down, they will find their own blows hampered by swarming flies.”

When not attacked for a period of time, Plaguebearers slowly regain lost health
“The body of a Plaguebearer appears weak and fragile, but their constant exposure to the vilest concoctions of Nurgle’s cauldron has made them unnaturally resilient”

Molonious • 2025-06-07 17:58:04

I think in terms of gameplay Plaguebearers can work fine. I think we’d need a significant lore shift and for things to get a lot worse to justify their presence however.

Thus far we’ve got Spawn, Plague Ogryn, and Daemonhosts, but these are all corrupted material creatures. The only actual Daemon we have is the Beast of Nurgle, which is essentially a large Warp puppy, the type of thing a cult might choose and be able to summon in some small numbers.

Sentient Daemon infantry however would tend to imply a dramatically worse situation. In the Siege of Vraks for example, Daemons don’t bother showing up until untold millions of guardsmen and much of the planetary population are dead, and not until many years after the arrival of both loyalist and traitor Astartes and even Titan Legions. For them to openly walk Tertium, they’d probably be owed some story advancement and the situation would have to worsen dramatically.

In term of mechanics, I don’t think a Plaguebearer should be immune to CC and stagger, they may not feel pain, but they can certainly have their incessant counting thrown off and get distracted trying to remember where they were at, maybe resistant to these things but not fully immune. Corruption damage from melee attacks is perfect and absolutely fitting. Being non-corporeal damage sponges, no headshot bonus sounds good, but I don’t see the purpose of the 3x melee rear damage, if a Plaguebearer can take a noggin shot no problem, I don’t think a knife to the back should do any better, I’d just go full sponge. Regaining lost health is a solid concept.
