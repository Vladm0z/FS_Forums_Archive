This is one of the most well-made and easy to grasp guides I’ve seen for Darktide.

I’ve been playing a version of this build for a very long time, even H40 every now and then, but I’m not as good with adding the pushes inbetween attacks like you are and there are some tips I never knew/never noticed in gameplay, such as force push being able to hard stagger captains and throw them down the abbyss when they have no shield.

My favorite blessing combo on FGS is double crit blessing so I have max uptime on Mettle move speed and Empathic Evasion and also nearly every hit restores 10% toughness. I’ve tried all kinds of setups with Wrath, with Unstable, but I enjoy double crit blessings the most.

I play the version with Kinetic Flayer, as I find assail a bit weak for horde clear to my liking, esp at base without the keystone. You’re gonna kill most hordes with warp slice, so I find using Assail redundant, especially now that Vanguards reduce its value further.

Kinetic Flayer is just too good, because it acts like an extra fully charged hit on Crusher, so you go from 4 hits per Crusher to 2-3 even in Havoc. Also it often oneshots specials/disablers on EK staff.

I also find By Crack of Bone to be good talent to always reach 30 stacks of Scrier’s Gaze. You don’t always have enough enemies on the screen for full duration/full buffs of Scrier’s Gaze. Again, I imagine for you it’s not a problem, considering your high mechanical skill and also the fact that you play hyper density mode with only two people, but it’s a handy talent to have so that you stay in Scrier’s for as long as possible inbetween cooldowns.
