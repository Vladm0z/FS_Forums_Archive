All the blitz’s can fall under this category (with many of them having ways of regenerating, so I’d refer to them as abilities more than strictly grenades most of the time).

Beyond that though, for Combat Abilities the only thing you have with direct damage is Psyker’s Shriek (with them also having the best blitz’s in terms of ‘things that do damage and can more often than not always be ready to use’). You got a few pushes with Veteran’s Shout, Arbitrators Shove, and Ogryns Charge (which can also do damage if you get the bleed pip), and a good many damage amplifications with Executioners Stance, Castigators Stance (with an upgrade pip and a few kills), Scrier’s Gaze, Fury of the Faithful (it applies 100% rending to your next strike on top of the damage and guaranteed crit, while giving attack speed afterward), Shroudfield, and Ogryn Charge again (but with Trample this time).

Beyond that, Psyker Staves are basically ranged abilities with no ammo cost, so I’d look into that too. In general, ‘Psyker’ is the main thing you’d likely want to look into, or Zealot with a Knife, Arbitrator with Remote Detonation/Lone Wolf, Veteran with Demolition Stockpile, or Ogryn with Pulverize and Rock.

Don’t think they’ve said anything about why most abilities don’t hurt directly, but likely something to do with wanting to encourage range weapon usage and swapping back and forth between the two, not sure beyond that though.
