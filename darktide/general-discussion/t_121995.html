Since we now have an in-game image of the Arc Rifle, I have a few nitpicks with the model when compared to the official artwork.

image559×276 39.1 KB

Arc_rifle362×139 63.5 KB

First one the grip

The in-game grip is shorter and more blockier than the one shown in the artwork and on the tabletop model. Personally, I prefer the original design. The artwork grip is a visual element shared across a lot of the newish admech weaponry, which helps create a consistent aesthetic and help the baroque look. I also prefer the flintlock or revolver-like profile it gives the weapons,particulary the pistols.

I’m not sure why the Darktide team seems to favor blockier grips. It was already something that bothered me with most of the laspistol and lasgun marks, whose artwork features noticeably more curved handles than their in-game counterparts(Admittedly it’s most true for the laspistol and accatran lasgun).

image250×144 29.1 KB

image300×150 23.1 KB

It also appears to be missing the distinctive grooves present on the original design, with only the darker texture pattern hinting at them.

Second: the lever in front of the capacitor.

The lever looks noticeably shorter than it does in the artwork and tabletop version. It’s a small detail, but it contributes to the weapon’s silhouette.

It also lacks the completely useless metal spur thingy on top of the receiver.

I’m still hoping we’ll get a second pattern or mark variant—perhaps a heavier Arc Rifle—that stays closer to the established tabletop and artwork design.

Maybe it’s just my tism talking, but I’m a little disappointed that the weapon doesn’t adhere more closely to the classic Adeptus Mechanicus look.i could understand for the lasguns as most of the artwork depicting them are a bit old but these are quite recent.

I think i will have the same nitpick about the phosphor blaster from what I saw of the model on the deluxe edition package. It seems to also go for a different grip compared to the official artwork

I understand the choice of not having the arc rifle backpack. It is what it is for gameplay reason and clipping reason
