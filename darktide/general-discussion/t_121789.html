Agayek • 2026-05-22 07:54:43

Attacking the totems triggers spawns that continue until the totem is destroyed and, I think, the servo skull collected. In addition, there’s an aura around the totem that gives surrounding enemies significant DR.

Once you kill a totem, it gives the team a stack of a buff for the rest of the mission. This buff gives you significantly increased movement speed, attack speed, fire rate, damage, and toughness damage reduction for as long as you are in coherency with at least one other teammate (and I believe it scales by number of people you’re in coherency with but not 100% certain of that).

As far as I’ve been able to tell, this and giving enemies a chance to drop a skull when killed that need to be collected for the community wide event challenge thing is all the event mutator does.
