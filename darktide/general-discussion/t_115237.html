I’ve cut my list down to ~70 and I am trying to get it lower. Here are some of the QOL mods:

QOL:

  • BornReady – to skip the after-mission screen.
  • Default to Strike Team Chat – haven’t had the broken chat bug since I installed this. Might be placebo but it’s been a year without the bug.
  • Talent Tree UX – lets you change individual talents without resetting the whole tree.
  • All the various Guarantee mods to fix dropped inputs.
  • Valkyrie Blitz Ingress – based on ZipIt, shortens mission loading screens by turning off the audio briefing.
  • Weapon Filter – provides buttons for filtering your inventory by weapon type.
  • Auto Backup – backs up my settings every session.

I also made Ring HUD, MoarDots, Better Loadouts and YesTakeCandle so I am probably one of the few mod-users that does not have Numeric UI or More Characters and Loadouts installed.
