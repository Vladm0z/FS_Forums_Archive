I’ve observed that the quality and visual appeal of the base, non-premium cosmetic items (earned through in-game time and achievement/penances) are currently very low. This significantly diminishes the sense of progression and makes the player experience feel unrewarding/stale. This affects the enjoyment in the game quite a bit for me and my friends, many of my friends have stopped playing the game because they feel like they are not getting rewarded for their time spent. The Darktide franchise fans overall are really lore-invested people and really like to roleplay, I just think this makes the game stale and boring if we don’t have anything to play around with. The cosmetics that you can buy with gold are in my opinion very boring compared to the premium ones.

To address the current cosmetic dissatisfaction and simultaneously incentivize spending, I would propose running a limited-time promotional sale on the Aguilas premium currency. I believe this would be an excellent short-term revenue strategy.

Implementing this promotion would likely lead to a significant, immediate boost in revenue and improve overall player goodwill by giving a wider segment of the community access to desirable premium items.

I really hope you will consider these points as you plan future updates, seasonal promotions and monetization strategies. I wish to see this game strive and this in my opinion would make a lot of people happy and stick to the game for a lot longer.
