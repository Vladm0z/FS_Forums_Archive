Why?

The audio queue is often overloaded on higher difficulties, leading to very inconsistent specialist sound cues and much more likely to be a negative experience. The pressing need for specialist sound cues (mostly disablers) means Jesper’s music is constantly interrupted and much harder to appreciate.

By doing structured waves, the need for constant sound cues for Specialists would be reduced, players could focus on strategizing around the different waves and Jesper’s music can get more appreciation without it being drowned out by constant Specialist/Elite audio cues.

These waves would ideally be triggered by map progression points, and all waves would disable elevator/airlocke activation. These progression points would not be at Auspex hacking event locations, but rather after dropdowns and airlocke/elevator door openings.

NOTE: These waves would not replace all waves during a mission but would act as moments of repreive from the constant Specialist spam.

Upon triggering the wave, the game will check for an active Boss. If there is an active Boss, the game will trigger the wave after the current Boss is defeated.

There would need to be a code that checks for active waves while players are waiting to be rescued, and relocates any captured players to an area within the current active zone due to the elevators/airlocke being disabled during wave.

Spawn Pool Restriction - Bulwarks, Crushers, Reapers, Poxwalkers, Scab Bruisers, Scab Bombers, Scab Flamers, and 1 Plague Ogryn.
