Can someone explain why the DLC classes still receive so few cosmetics?

I am not only talking about Commodore’s Vestures. Why are Arbites, Hive Scum and Skitarii also mostly ignored by Hestia’s Blessings, the Commissary, Havoc rewards, events, and other cosmetic tracks? We paid for these classes, but compared to the original four, there is very little to unlock or buy after their release.

There are so many easy ways this could be handled. For example, Skitarii could receive evolved versions of their existing Forge World armour.

  1. A more advanced Branx Minor set could be added to Hestia’s Blessings (its blue!! so it fits),
  2. a darker and more decorated Stygies VIII set could be earned through Havoc (its black, fits current havok cosmetics),
  3. and Mars or Ryza could be used for another achievement or event.

Not simple recolours, but proper upgraded versions that show where they came from and what the player achieved.

The same could be done for Arbites and Hive Scum. Give them improved versions of their class armour through penances, Havoc, Hestia, or the Commissary. These classes have very strong themes, so there should be plenty of room for meaningful progression instead of giving them a few launch sets and then almost nothing.

And yes, give them paid cosmetics too. I genuinely do not understand what is stopping Fatshark from making new premium sets for classes people already paid to play. They have already shown that DLC-class cosmetics are possible, so why are these classes still left out of most cosmetic releases?

Cosmetics are a large part of the long-term progression in Darktide. When three entire classes cannot take part in most of that progression, playing them feels less rewarding. Please start supporting DLC classes across all cosmetic sources, not only with the small number of sets included at launch.
