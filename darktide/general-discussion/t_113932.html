Error CodeDescriptionPotential Fixes
2001Often indicates a server-side problem or an issue with your system.Restart your PC and router. Verify game files. Update graphics drivers.
2003A server disconnection error.Restart your PC and router. Verify game files. Change your DNS settings to 1.1.1.1 (primary) and 8.8.8.8 (alternate).
2004Appears alongside errors like 2001 and 2014.See fixes for 2001 and 2014.
2007An “internal error” that may be related to the 2003 error.Apply the same fixes as for error 2003, or wait for developers to fix a potential server-side issue.
2014Another common disconnection error, often caused by server overload.Wait for the server to resolve the issue. Restart your PC and router. Test your connection by disabling a VPN or using a different network, like a mobile hotspot.
3013Can be related to specific network configurations or ISP issues.Check if you have a VPN or proxy active. Try a different connection, such as a mobile hotspot. Some users report issues with specific ISPs like ‘Free’ in France.
4001Typically points to a server-side problem with no fix on the user’s end.Wait for the developers to address the issue.
4008“Failed handshake timeout” error, preventing connection to the server.Restart the game and Steam. Check your internet connection. Consider temporarily disabling your firewall or VPN to see if that resolves the issue.
9999Appears to be the same as error 4008.Apply the same fixes as for error 4008.
