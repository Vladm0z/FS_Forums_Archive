My thoughts are: This enemy is the equivalent of a skavenslave mook from VT2, but has the tankiness of a stormvermin with shield. It’s not an elite unit and it just feels wrong, like it’s too tanky for how common it is despite being a normal mook that can be one shot.

Nowhere is this more exemplified than with Chordclaw, an attack that can one-shot Crushers, and the shield user just ignores it (or feels like it.)

I’m not opposed to shield users in mixed hordes. My proposal would be:

Reduce shield health to something like 2-3 standard hits to kill from most weapons capable of 1-2 shotting the same enemy without a shield. Reduce the health to ‘can be one-shot if flanked.’ Look at it again.

Then, add a new shield unit that takes the place of the Stormvermin with Shield. Say, mauler. It’s about as tanky as the current shield users, give or take (balance to taste.) It can be just as common as current small shield users.

Basically, my problem is that they don’t make sense being as durable as they are and they’re disruptive in a way that feels spongy and kinda awful to engage. It has nothing to do with the raw gameplay intent of mixing up horde engagement, but I fear it’s just going to make flamethrowers even more popular because they, of course, bypass shields.
