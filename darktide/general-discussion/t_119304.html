Ideas for balance changes

Fatshark is apparently picking up the tempo with balancing the game, which is nice, though even after hearing the patch notes for the Beyond the Hive update, I can’t help but feel there are some glaring outliers that remain unaddressed. They may be in the works and taking longer than I anticipate. Regardless I’d like to make this as a sort of compendium of ideas from the community of hopeful balance changes.

Classes Balance Thesis: I think the real problem facing the classes is that they have lost their identities over time. These diminishing niches have been exacerbated by the release of new classes and the talent tree reworks to the point where personal preference is the only reason you’d play certain classes not because they can do something well or unique. I think it’s great classes can spec into alternative gameplay styles through the tree but I feel a problem has arisen where 3 specific classes (Psyker, Vet, Scum) can do everything well at this point with little to no drawbacks, and other class identities have been completely eclipsed as a result (mostly Ogryn but Zealot a bit too)

The balance I’m suggesting has these niches in mind. If at all possible I’d love for classes to keep being good at what their class fantasy suggests they should be good at instead of the reflexive gutting that usually happens.

Psyker: This is really the only class I have the in depth knowledge of to propose any specific changes. It’s easily my most played class and I have to say I was not a fan of how it was changed last year. I think:

  • Perilous combustion should be changed to only proc on direct hits to limit the infinitely stacking soulblaze shenanigans
  • Smite should be reworked to have a charge system like assail but have it do its cc through the application of Electrocution so the caster can do something actively while the blitz is affecting targets.
  • Mind in Motion should be replaced
  • Channeled Force and Empyric Shock should be reworked to not be focused on the primary fire of staves at least and at most changed completely to offer different bonuses while keeping the theme as being staff psyker nodes
  • Kinetic Presence should be changed to range damage
  • Psykinetic’s Aura should be changed back to the 100% CDR for 3 seconds and made to only proc of the players specialist or elite kills not the teams’s.
  • Just a Dream and Immaterial focus should be reworked to something other than DR and TDR.
  • Scrier’s Gaze should have its 20% TDR removed and its cooldown increased to 30-35 seconds.
  • Venting Shriek’s Creeping Flames augment should reduce the amount of peril quelled to 20-25% and substantially reduce the range of the ability.
  • Electro Staff should just get a rework to focus it as either the premier single target staff or the CC Staff. In the process the primary fire should be replaced with something like a short arc of electricity.
  • Voidstrike staff should just get some minor tweaks to not make it feel so anemic when not on a crit surge build, possibly a boost to its charge rate.
  • Voidblast should have its stagger profile reduced on lower than fully charged secondaries.
  • Flame Staff should get a substantially reduction in how fast it applies soulblaze and compensated with the plume of flame doing substantially more damage so less DoT shenanigans and it still feels like a flamethrower. Its peril generation should be increased so a fully charged plume fills you up most of the way to 100%.
  • Staves as a whole should get some kind of touch up to help dissuade the degenerate playstyles that come with having infinite ammo such as putting a hard cap on primary fire rates and incentivizing fully charging secondaries instead of just spamming half charged ones by making below full charge less impactful across the board.

DoT Effects: I think DoTs need some reasonable adjusting. Across the board 16 should be the max stack count and the concept of overcapping should be removed. In lieu of this a rock paper scissors approach to their balance and to help differentiate them should be added.

  • Bleed should keep its short stack time and should have the highest damage per stack to compensate. it should excel against unarmored and maniac armor types, be neutral infested and unyielding, and be inefficient against flak and carapace armor.
  • Toxin should have a medium stack timer and medium damage. If should excel against flak and carapace, middling against unarmored and maniac, and bad into infested and unyielding.
  • Fire should have long stack times and lower damage. It should be good against unyielding and infested, ok at flak and carapace and bad against unarmored and maniac.
  • Soulblaze should retain its enormous stack timer and have even less damage than flames with the idea that its used as a tool for psyker class synergies as much as damage. To compensate it should be neutral against all armor types.
  • Electrocution can remain mostly unchanged having a similar neutral against all damage profile as soulblaze but be focused on cc with a short timer to differentiate.

Weapons and Blessing: Weapon balance has never been worse. With a scant few outliers dominating in every category while others feel like they haven’t been touched since the beta. In keeping with the theme of opting for giving downsides as opposed to gutting powerful things entirely I think these powerful outliers could use some tough love. And then some regular TLC for some of the stinkers.

  • Uncanny Strike should have its max rending cut to 60% and make it so it and every other blessing can only affect direct damage from the weapon it’s equipped on (barring things that apply a debuff to the enemy which common sense would exclude anyway).
  • Slower swinging, slow movement speed, low crit/finesse weapons need substantial buffs across the board, obviously more so for weapons that fit into multiple of those descriptors. My initial thought is in addition to more damage (like a lot more) these weapons should have much better cleave profiles, so when the dust settles the niche these larger melee weapons bring are the ability to deal with large groups in exchange for their reduced mobility.
  • Faster swinging weapons that should be focused on single target damage should be adjusted to have much worse cleave even with blessings and talents backing them up. The extreme end of this is the dueling sword, combat blade and shivs should be able to hit like 2 enemies maximum with blessings and talents active to give them a weakness and by extension a niche as single target assassin weapons that struggle in hordes.
  • Ranged weapons are bit too diverse in their woes for me to care enough to list them individually. As is they also run into a niche problem. Relatively minor tweaks to damage, ammo, and ADMs for weapons so that the amount of overlap isn’t so staggering.

Enemies: The enemies we face have been molded by the mostly stagnant meta of apply dot use fast melee weapon and blast with high cleave/high stagger abilities to the point where the majority of enemies don’t even get to engage with you. This has led to modern darktide. Armor conga lines as far as the eye can see, a downright ridiculous specialist spawn rate at times, and a dircetor that seems at times to just not have a budget for how many of a particular enemy he can throw at you. And it needs to be that way because if the slightest mistake can’t snowball into a wipe we as players have too much power to ever be meaningfully challenged outside of high Havoc missions. I don’t think Havoc should be the norm and it shouldn’t be balanced around as to retain its CBT nature.

Enemy spawns would need to be reined in alongside these other changes and while they’re at it, I’d love to see fixes for the jank that comes with a game being warframed like darktide has such as:

  • Specialist sound cues being drowned out or not playing.
  • Enemies being able to clip into each other and the player.
  • Enemy spawn patterns and seeds being tweaked so that the majority of a mission isn’t just one enemy type.
  • Carapace needs its damage reduction reduced and every melee weapon needs at least one move that can bypass a reasonable amount of armor in base. ranged weapons just need more weapons that can do anything against carapace.
  • Enemy behaviors like poxbursters not exploding, general dog and mutant jank, and trappers firing nets through geometry and large hit mass enemies that completely block line of sight need to be fixed.

Obviously, there’s plenty more that could be changed. So I’d like to hear the community’s thoughts on things I either did or didn’t cover, especially for the classes I don’t have the know how to speak on.

SkibidiFortnite:

Faster swinging weapons that should be focused on single target damage should be adjusted to have much worse cleave even with blessings and talents backing them up. The extreme end of this is the dueling sword, combat blade and shivs should be able to hit like 2 enemies maximum with blessings and talents active to give them a weakness and by extension a niche as single target assassin weapons that struggle in hordes.

That’s not a functional model in a heavily-melee game. Not a single melee weapon needs to be rendered close to useless in any given situation. Like, neither it must be completely useless for hoard clearing, nor for anti-armor. The baseline effectiveness must be sufficient for you to protect yourself and do your share of work. So if you prefer to use devilclaw, you’ll be really good at mixed hoard clearing, but you should have some kind of special strike hidden inside its moveset that allow you to ignore carapace armor partially - it’s just to execute it you would need to spend some time (like it would be activated after a push attack only) - making your dps vs a Crusher kinda low, but still allowing you to kill it in a reasonable time. And such approach must be applied to all similar weapons (ogryn’s cleaver, heavy sword, tactical axe, certain range weapons which are notoriously bad vs carapace armor etc).

Same in case of duelling sword and the combat blade, they both must keep a reasonable cleave allowing you to not be overwhelmed by chaff, but DS in particular need to lose huge part of it’s anti-armor capabilities, making usage of fully charged Thrust on it mandatory to do the same amount of damage it does now with simple pokes if you are after the single target damage vs carapace armor. Otherwise having both insane single target damage vs all foes AND insane mobility, coupled with it being available to half the classes now outshadows all other similar niche weapons, like the heavy chain weapons. Combat Blade is though already in a good spot, its tiny reach and crappy anti-hoard capabilities balance high mobility and high single-target damage. You need to be really good to utilize its benefit fully and not die while at it.

And ofc all this only makes sense if all other insanely overtuned anti-armor abilities are nerfed (probably followed by nerfing Crusher’s HP back to reasonable level). Like it is totally unfair to make a psyker or a zealot to poke some Crusher for 7-10s with precise charged DS headshots while a typical meta yolo build of vet or HS insta-removes packs of them, or an arbitrator just slaps pack of helpless Crushers with a club to death while rolling their face on their keyboard.
