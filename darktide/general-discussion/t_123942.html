SES-X71A “Scribe John”

John died exactly as he had lived:
hunched over a mountain of parchment, completing Form CXVIII-44/B
before the filing deadline.
The document’s true purpose was known only to his superiors,
but its timely completion was deemed sufficiently important
that his remains were sanctified as Servo Skull SES-X71A.

Now serving the Imperium as he always has, John continues to organize records,
carry dataslates, archive reports, and ensure that not even death excuses missed paperwork.
He has since been equipped with a regulation-pattern laspistol for the protection of Imperial records and authorized personnel.
His marksmanship is considered adequate, though his primary function remains the preservation of data rather than the elimination of the Emperor’s foes.
His service record, both in life and death, remains exemplary.

SES-P65Y “Zealot Laurenzius”

Laurenzius was a fanatical Zealot who fought throughout the Fringe Wars,
cleansing worlds with his beloved flamer, “Clara the Cleaner.”
To him, hesitation was heresy—better to burn ten innocents than let one heretic escape.
He fell while leading a final cleansing charge into enemy lines,
still laughing as the flames consumed friend and foe alike.
His remains were consecrated as Purgator Servo Skull SES-P65Y,
so his holy fire might never be extinguished.

SES-M44K “Medicae Lazarus”

Medicae Lazarus served with the Moebian 12th during the Battle of Nox Secundus
in the Fringe Wars, refusing to abandon the wounded even as the regiment was overwhelmed. Mortally wounded himself, he used his last remaining combat stim to save another Guardsman’s life instead of his own.
He died knowing another servant of the Emperor would fight on.
Revered as a quiet martyr by the Moebian 12th,
his remains were enshrined as Medicae Servo Skull SES-M44K,
where he continues to preserve the lives of the faithful.
