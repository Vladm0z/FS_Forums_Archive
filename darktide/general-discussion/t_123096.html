GMG_Pie • 2026-06-26 19:20:50

The sweeping moveset has higher adms against everything except flak and carapace, which has a negative cleave multiplier (unyielding also has this penalty despite the boosted adms.) The opposite is on the single target moveset: higher flak and carapace adms and a cleave penalty against every other armor type except unyielding. This means that the sweeping moveset actually has decent single target damage against maniac ragers and unyielding targets.

Is this explained in game? Of course not.
