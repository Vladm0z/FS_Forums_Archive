The strip at the top is a compass.

The icon that looks like a vault door is the trader’s bunker.
The icon that looks like an up arrow is the evac point.
Any other icon is a “point of interest” (resource cache).

You can see how far each one is from you using your auspex (5), and also mark them with your slot colour on your auspex.
I would generally recommend starting with the closest cache but the most critical thing is to stay as a group.

There are three types of icon:

  1. The door icon, which progresses you to a safe zone where you can heal/restock/buy toys, and then sends you to the next area (also adds 5m to the timer)
  2. The up-arrow icon, which is the extraction icon and ends the mission
  3. Everything else, which are POI’s that have loot you want to extract

The gameplay loop is: Hit as many POI’s as you can (health/ammo/time permitting) to acquire tech salvage, then go to a safe zone to restock/heal, then do it again x2, then extract.

Everyone has access to marking icons via auspex [5] (IDK what it is on controllers, but 5 + ASDW navigate then select to add to top compass) - so you can tell people you want to go to a given POI, or a safe zone, or extract using your auspex.
