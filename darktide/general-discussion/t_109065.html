In its current state survivalist gives 1% ammo with a 5sec cooldown. if we conservatively assume an average match is ~25min, that is ~300% ammo restored per player over the course of 25min. normally a small ammo pickup is 15%, so each player currently gets ~20 small ammo pickups from survivalist alone, or 80 in total.

from what i can find, at most havoc reduces the ammo pickups to 40% of their usual amount, making a small pickup give just 6% ammo. this means that in havoc, survivalist is worth ~50 small ammo pickups to each player on your team, or ~200 in total.

i hope this ballpark math gives you an idea that not only is survivalist already very strong (even post nerf), but what you are suggesting would barely even increase the ammo your team gets because it is already so much
