Its a bolter build with focus target and frag/bleed grenades

you sacrifice the cool kid’s damage perks like skirmisher or agile engagement to maximize your quality of life and defensive capabilities, you will be a tank amongs terracotta pots

you can still get your 2million damages if you game hard enough so it hardly matters, with the caveat that you won’t die at the first poxburster that your team mate will pop on your face

The crux of this build was to work on a bolter build that had both +25 toughness nodes, you’ll end up with 152 hp and 141 toughness on level 40s

but if you are a spergie like me you can lower your toughness to 140 by using a 16% blessed curio

when you are swarmed by rotten armors elites you apply your bleed stacks with grenades (and most importantly you stun them) and finish them off with your duelling sword, its pretty simple
