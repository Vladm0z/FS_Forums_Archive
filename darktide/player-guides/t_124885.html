This is a template I’d go for - I went for Flensing keystone, but this can also work well with Redline. I wouldn’t play this with Power Overload, since that one kind of wants you to commit to picking the Servo skull from the skills you grab on the way to it. There’s probably some other good skills options to pick, but some I outlined are:

  • Pick either orange option - both are good and have valid usecases (Probing strikes is just extra boss damage, spreading electrocution across multiple elites via Electro-strike conduit (electrocution on melee crit), and shafting Bulwarks without much effort; Axial is effortless damage into multiple enemies in front of you - Probing strikes also uses any attack speed increases more than Axial does, since you can perform the three blows quicker),
  • Pick some combination of pink skills that you like/think will contribute to damage, surviability or provide some team or personal utility. This isn’t an exhaustive selection and I could see arguments being made for some skills, but it’s a bit too much to go into without writing a wall of text. Experiment, see what works for you.

KJeraDRegular • 2026-08-15 10:12:14
Metaphage:

so is it good or . . . the servo skull is too good?

It’s not a dychotomy of good/bad - both can be good. Let’s put it this way:

  • IRE requires little support and is branching from a part of the tree you are almost always taking anyway. The higher shooter density on the map, the more use you will get out of it, which is kind of RNG-dependant, because it’s not as good in melee seeds. Its supporting nodes are mediocre at best, which means that speccing into utility, more ammo or else, is worthwhile,
  • Servo skull at minimum wants Noospheric, and its two active use nodes cost extra skill points, which you could spend somewhere else. In return, you get a blitz that can trigger your “on ranged kill” skills (also Superiority blessing for melee), and can allow you to become a jack-of-all-trades in area denial/capacitance generation (Purgator) and provide good team utility (remote revives, rescues, at a distance), while still being capable of doing hacking objectives. It all costs extra points, however.
