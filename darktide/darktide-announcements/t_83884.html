Hotfix 1.2.1 just went live on Steam and Xbox Live, and will be out shortly for the Microsoft Store. Here are the notes:

  • Fixed a crash which could happen when navigating the Social menu
  • Fixed a crash which could happen when navigating the Commodore’s Vestures menu with a controller
  • Fixed a crash which could happen when rapidly changing wargear on HDD
  • Fixed a server crash which could happen when inflicting a large amount of stagger to a Beast of Nurgle
  • Fixed a case where the shoot action could cause mispredicts for clients
  • Fixed an issue with keyboard button mapping in the End of Round screen which prevented users from proceeding
  • Minor localization fixes
  • Fixed an issue where the “Recently played with” section of the Social menu was not correctly populated
  • Fixed clipping issues and added feather physics to Psyker “Trader’s Hat” premium cosmetic
