Hey Everyone,

Summer is here again!

Just a quick reminder that during the month of July, many people in Sweden take time off to enjoy the summer weather and spend time with family. This includes a lot of the team here at Fatshark, too.

That said, while not everyone will be away, the team will be working at a slightly slower pace than we have been during these past few weeks. We expect one Hotfix in July, and then the next scheduled Hotfix is set for August.

Thank you to everyone who has supported us and gave us feedback during the launch of our first DLC for Darktide. We’re looking forward to sharing more news about what else we have planned this year after returning from the summer break!

The Darktide Team
