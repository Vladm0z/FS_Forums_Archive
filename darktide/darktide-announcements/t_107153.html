ENTRIES ARE NOW CLOSED

Rejects!
‍
Suffered greatly under the watchful eyes of Commissar Dukane or Sefoni since the release of Nightmares and Visions?

Excellent! Let your suffering be a source of entertainment for your fellow Rejects (and us) by joining the Nighmares and Visions clip competition!
‍
Upload a video with footage from your Mortis Trials or Havoc mode sessions with moments that are: Glorious, clutch, humorous, flat out fails or anything in-between! Edits are allowed, but please keep the submission under the 1 min mark. We don’t have any hard requirements apart from that, so get creative!
‍
We’ll be picking 5 winners who will each win 2900 Aquilas and a pair of socks, obviously. The winners will be picked by a jury of devs from different disciplines.
‍
The competition will run until the stroke of midnight on April 30, the winners will be contacted as soon as they have been picked. We will also be posting the winning submissions (and perhaps some honorable mentions) on our social channels and the launcher with credit to the uploader.
‍
The finer details - PLEASE READYou can upload your submissions to your own social media profile (e.g. Twitter / Instagram / Bluesky / TikTok) using the hashtag #DarktideClips, or submit it as a YouTube clip in the appropriate thread on the Fatshark forums.Only videos that are Havoc or Mortis Trials themed are valid. You are allowed to get a bit creative and edit the video.

You can submit only one entry!

‍Notice to new members on the Forums: Your post will have to be approved before it appears here, which may take up to 12 hours if posted during the night. Please be patient, it will be approved! We may use your gameplay clips on social media or the launcher to promote the competition before it ends, but that doesn’t necessarily mean you’ve won!
