New In-Game Event: Light the fuse!

Things are getting explosive on the streets of Tertium. Wyrmwood agents report an unusually high presence of explosive ordnance in the drop zone.

Light-The-Fuse-Cover800×450 36.8 KB

To make progress in the event, complete missions with the Explosive Uprising condition.

This event will start on Thursday, August 21. The rewards will be Ordo dockets, Plasteel and Diamantine. Those who complete the last tier of the event will receive a cosmetic insignia.
