Warband!

Patch 1.13.1 is now being rolled out on all platforms, and while you wait for it to download you can read the notes below!

This hotfix includes a few tweaks to the Prophet of Decay boss fight, as well as a couple of bugfixes.

Hotfix 1.13.1 patch notes

Features & Tweaks

Enemy Balance

  • Rodin Karnak , Prophet of Decay:

    • On Damnation and up, the minimum amount of tooth pillars have been increased from 2 → 3 during the second phase of the boss.

    • On Damnation and up, some health thresholds have been slightly adjusted to allow more melee damage to be done when he’s exhausted during the first and last phase.

Fixes

  • Fixed an issue where crafting materials were not being awarded when coming back to Sefoni’s Reverie after completing Mortis Trials.

  • Fixed a crash which could happen when entering the Meat Grinder.

Known Issues

  • New Penances related to Spillway mention finishing missions on Damnation on the Auric mission board, when it should instead mention finishing missions on Auric difficulty.

  • New Penances related to Spillway mention the Sedition difficulty that no longer exists.

That’s all for today!

We’ll see you on the Mourningstar.

– The Darktide Team
