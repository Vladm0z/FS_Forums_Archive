Cover text_newsfeed_ru800×450 115 KB

After the clamour and chaos of the Rumbling Giants attack, the streets of Tertium feel eerily quiet. Don’t let your guard down, though, the Heretics may be low in number, but Wyrmwood agents report the troops present show unnatural resistance to injury…

Complete missions with the Indomitable Incursion condition to make progress in the event! These missions will see a much lower number of enemies… because you will be facing nothing but Elites, Specialists and Monstrosities!

This condition will only be available in Heresy, Damnation and Auric difficulties.

This event is LIVE NOW until May 10. The rewards will be Ordo dockets, Plasteel and Diamantine. Those who complete the last tier of the event will receive a cosmetic insignia.
