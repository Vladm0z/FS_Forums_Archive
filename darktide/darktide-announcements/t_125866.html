Krourk Mk IIa Cruncher

image2048×1107 424 KB

“We’re gonna need a bigger bonk, sah”

Overview

An industrial sledgehammer of immense weight and size, this weapon can only be wielded by equally enormous Ogryns.

While each swing is ponderous, it also carries huge stagger, above average cleave potential, and amazing reach.

The moveset of the weapon is straightforward, allowing to chain attacks in a pleasing, destructive rhythm.

Additionally, if the windup of an Heavy or Special attack is maintained for a longer period of time, the weapon will accumulate up to 4 stacks of +10% Melee Strength for the following attack.

This weapon is available to the Ogryn class.

Weapon Special

Pressing the Special input initiates a unique windup animation, raising the Cruncher above the Ogryn’s head and giving access to devastating Strikedown attacks.

If the Special input is released immediately, the weapon will perform a Light 1 or Light 4 attack (depending on the combo chain); if the input is held, the weapon will release an Heavy Special Strikedown attack.

Attacks

  • Light 1: Moderately fast, vertical Strikedown attack with good Impact and Damage.

    • Chains into Light 2 and Heavy 2, or Light 4 and Special 2.

  • Light 2: Moderately fast, diagonal Relentless attack with good Impact and Cleave.

    • Chains into Light 3 and Heavy 1, or Light 1 and Special 1.

  • Light 3: Moderately fast, diagonal uppercut Relentless attack with good Impact and Cleave.

    • Chains into Light 4 and Heavy 2, or Light 4 and Special 2.

    • Also accessed while in a Slide stance.

  • Light 4: Moderately fast, vertical Strikedown attack with good Impact and Damage.

    • Chains into Light 1 and Heavy 1, or Light 1 and Special 1.

  • Heavy 1: Slow, horizontal Relentless attack with great Cleave and Impact over secondary targets.

    • Chains into Light 2 and Heavy 2, or Light 4 and Special 2.

  • Heavy 2: Slow, horizontal Relentless attack with great Cleave and Impact over secondary targets.

    • Chains into Light 3 and Heavy 1, or Light 1 and Special 1.

  • Push-attack: Fast, horizontal Relentless attack with good Impact and Cleave.

    • Chains into Light 2 and Heavy 2, or Light 4 and Special 2.

  • Special 1: Very slow, vertical Strikedown attack with great Damage and Impact.

    • Chains into Light 2 and Heavy 2, or Light 4 and Special 2.

  • Special 2: Very slow, vertical Strikedown attack with great Damage and Impact.

    • Chains into Light 3 and Heavy 1, or Light 1 and Special 1.

Balance Changes

As part of the Depths of the Damned Free Update, we have a balance update. Our focus has been on improving the base classes talent trees, overall class balance, weapon balance, and addressing Golden Toughness and its impact on both player builds and our overall design space for the future. Since we are making changes that will affect all players, regardless of class and loadout, it’s important for us to go through our thought process.

The original four Classes are receiving several changes to their talent trees, with a particular focus on reducing the number of stat nodes required to complete your build, and improving the paths through the trees with the intent of providing more interesting choices and improving buildcraft. We are also looking to reinforce what makes the Veteran and Zealot stand out from the other Classes, by taking a closer look at their themes and mechanics. The Skitarius has now had some time to settle, so we are also going over its talents and abilities and making some targeted adjustments. NOTE: Since the changes affect the layout of the trees, your loadouts may need slight rearrangements to amend broken connections or reapplying of points to make up for removed talents. Rest assured that these are only partial resets.

Several Melee Weapons are also receiving changes, with the Thunder Hammer in particular getting some extra attention.

Finally, we are taking a closer look at Golden Toughness. In May, we outlined our intention to improve combat balance by addressing some of the outliers in player power, and took the first steps by adjusting Carapace Armoured enemies and the players tools to deal with them. You can read that balance blog here.

Our commitment is still to preserve the intensity of the combat, while making it more stable, more readable, and more supportive of different builds and loadouts. Adjusting Golden Toughness is another step towards that long-term goal.

Golden Toughness

Golden Toughness is one of the more disruptive mechanics in Darktide. By changing the way damage and toughness interact, it can obscure some of the core rules of combat.

Many players are naturally guided towards abilities that provide Golden Toughness, and these abilities can be highly effective even before a player has developed a strong understanding of the mechanics they are intended to learn, e.g. positioning, and how to engage each type of enemy. This can allow difficult situations to be bypassed or diffused without engaging with the underlying threats, making it harder for players to build that understanding through the intended learning curve.

When Golden Toughness was first introduced to Abilities, Darktide was in a very different state. Since then, the overall difficulty and complexity of the game has increased, and the value of Golden Toughness has increased alongside it. As its value has grown, it has also become increasingly difficult to balance abilities against one another without making Golden Toughness a prerequisite for competing with its level of defensive power.

We want Toughness to have consistent and understandable rules regardless of how it is obtained. To that end, we are removing the Spillover Immunity effect from Golden Toughness. It will now follow the same damage calculation rules as regular Toughness.

This means that Golden Toughness will continue to provide additional protection, but it will no longer allow a single point of Toughness to fully absorb the damage of an otherwise lethal attack. The result should be a more consistent combat system, and greater room for us to balance defensive abilities against one another.

  • Spillover immunity - Removed

  • Voice of Command - Duty and Honour

    • Toughness Bonus 50 → 75

  • Chorus of Spiritual Fortitude

    • See Zealot Changes

To accompany these changes we’re also making adjustments to the enemies where Golden Toughness gave players the largest benefit.

  • Poxbursters

    • Explosion Damage now falls off based on distance

      • 20% of full damage at the edge (6m)

      • 100% of full damage within 3m

  • Snipers

    • Damage Spillover reduced by 50%.

      • Dev Note: this is the damage that is applied to Health if a Sniper’s shot completely removes Toughness on the target.

  • Ogryn Crushers

    • Lowered chance to target players that are already overwhelmed

  • Scab Mauler

    • Slightly reduced the rate at which they utilize their overhead attack

We know that these will cause changes in the meta and balance of higher difficulty play, and will keep a close eye on how the affected classes perform.
