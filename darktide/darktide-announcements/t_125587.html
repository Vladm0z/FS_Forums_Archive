Warband!

As part of the upcoming Depths of the Damned Free Update we have a balance update. Our focus has been on improving the base classes talent trees, overall class balance, weapon balance, and addressing Golden Toughness and its impact on both player builds and our overall design space for the future. Since we are making changes that will affect all players, regardless of class and loadout, it’s important for us to go through our thought process.

The original four Classes are receiving several changes to their talent trees, with a particular focus on reducing the number of stat nodes required to complete your build, and improving the paths through the trees with the intent of providing more interesting choices and improving buildcraft. We are also looking to reinforce what makes the Veteran and Zealot stand out from the other Classes, by taking a closer look at their themes and mechanics. The Skitarius has now had some time to settle, so we are also going over its talents and abilities and making some targeted adjustments. NOTE: Since the changes affect the layout of the trees, your loadouts may need slight rearrangements to amend broken connections or reapplying of points to make up for removed talents. Rest assured that these are only partial resets.

Several Melee Weapons are receiving changes, with the Thunder Hammer in particular getting some extra attention.

Finally, we are taking a closer look at Golden Toughness. In May, we outlined our intention to improve combat balance by addressing some of the outliers in player power, and took the first steps by adjusting Carapace Armoured enemies and the players tools to deal with them. You can read that balance blog here.

Our commitment is still to preserve the intensity of the combat, while making it more stable, more readable, and more supportive of different builds and loadouts. Adjusting Golden Toughness is another step towards that long-term goal.

Golden Toughness

Golden Toughness is one of the more disruptive mechanics in Darktide. By changing the way damage and toughness interact, it can obscure some of the core rules of combat.

Many players are naturally guided towards abilities that provide Golden Toughness, and these abilities can be highly effective even before a player has developed a strong understanding of the mechanics they are intended to learn, e.g. positioning, and how to engage each type of enemy. This can allow difficult situations to be bypassed or diffused without engaging with the underlying threats, making it harder for players to build that understanding through the intended learning curve.

When Golden Toughness was first introduced to Abilities, Darktide was in a very different state. Since then, the overall difficulty and complexity of the game has increased, and the value of Golden Toughness has increased alongside it. As its value has grown, it has also become increasingly difficult to balance abilities against one another without making Golden Toughness a prerequisite for competing with its level of defensive power.

We want Toughness to have consistent and understandable rules regardless of how it is obtained. To that end, we are removing the Spillover Immunity effect from Golden Toughness. It will now follow the same damage calculation rules as regular Toughness.

This means that Golden Toughness will continue to provide additional protection, but it will no longer allow a single point of Toughness to fully absorb the damage of an otherwise lethal attack. The result should be a more consistent combat system, and greater room for us to balance defensive abilities against one another.

  • Spillover immunity - Removed
  • Voice of Command - Duty and Honour
    • Toughness Bonus 50 → 75
  • Chorus of Spiritual Fortitude
    • See Zealot Changes

To accompany these changes we’re also making adjustments to the enemies where Golden Toughness gave players the largest benefit.

  • Poxbursters
    • Explosion Damage now falls off based on distance
      • 20% of full damage at the edge (6m)
      • 100% of full damage within 3m
  • Snipers
    • Damage Spillover reduced by 50%.
      • Dev Note: this is the damage that is applied to Health if a Sniper’s shot completely removes Toughness on the target.
  • Ogryn Crushers
    • Lowered chance to target players that are already overwhelmed
  • Scab Mauler
    • Slightly reduced the rate at which they utilize their overhead attack

We know that these will cause changes in the meta and balance of higher difficulty play, and will keep a close eye on how the affected classes perform.

Ogryn

We are making changes to the Ogryns Talent Tree with the intent of making it easier and more intuitive to create builds. We have made changes to the top part of the tree to make the choices feel more in tune with the build you’re going for. We have also made the higher pick rate talents a bit more available, so that you spend less points to get the nodes you need, and more on the nodes you want.

  • Stat Nodes Removed
    Dev Note: Most stat nodes have been removed. We’ve added back some Toughness to the Ogryn’s Base Toughness. We have increased the value of Keep Shooting and Dominate to make up for the lost Rending and Reload Speed.

    • Now Baseline
    • Coherency Radius 75% → 50%

Zealot

We are making changes to the Zealot’s Talent Tree with the intent of solidifying its strengths and expanding on one of the features that is unique to it. We have also made some smaller pathing changes to the layout in an effort to give you a couple extra points to spend on the new additions.

    Dev Note: We are pruning a few nodes to make space for larger changes. The toughness we are adding to your base toughness pool. The Movement Speed you will instead find in ‘Faithful Frenzy’.

    • +25 Toughness
  • Base Toughness
    • 100 → 125

Weapon Balance Changes

  • Ogryn Slab Shield & Battle Maul
    Dev Note: the Ogryn Slab Shield relied a lot on its strong Heavy Shield attacks, while most of the Light attacks were neglected. We are updating the properties and adding a new combo path for all Light attacks, to allow the whole moveset to be used in interesting ways.

    • Increased Light Relentless profile damage on 2nd and 3rd targets
    • Updated the Pushfollow attack profile to be aligned to other Light Strikedown attacks
    • Added Melee Block properties during Heavy 2 windups
    • Slightly lowered the chain time from Push to Pushfollow attack
    • Added chain to Special Shield plant from Pushfollow attack
    • Changed chain out of Light 4 from Light 1 / Heavy 1 to Light 3 / Heavy 1
    • Light 3 and Light 4 will now loop between themselves, gaining +10% attack speed
    • Tweaked hitboxes and ranges for some attacks

  • Relic Blade Sprint Heavy Stab
    Dev Note: The Relic Blade Heavy Stab from Sprint is one of the most powerful single target attacks of the family, but was also too easily accessible when already in close melee combat with enemies.
    We are rebalancing the profile by lowering the baseline damage and the Armour Damage Modifier against Unyielding enemies by around 20%, and conversely adding a stacking Melee Strength bonus that is gained by charging the Heavy Stab while Sprinting for a small amount of time.
    The current full power of the attack can still be obtained, but only after Sprinting for a little while and not from super short Sprints performed just to enter the Heavy Stab attack windup.

    • Lowered the baseline performance of the Heavy stab attack on Sprint, and added a Melee Power bonus stacking up over time while Sprinting
      • Up to 3 Stacks of +8% Melee Strength (max +24%)
    • Lowered the Unyielding ADM for the attack profile
    • Normal from 1.2 to 1.1
    • Special Activated from 1.5 to 1.33

  • Thunder Hammers

    We are adding a large amount of buffs to the weapon family, with also specific tweaks for each mark, to make them both more reliable against larger groups of enemies and more decisive against single targets.
    The “Thrust” Blessing, which was often a key part of the Thunder Hammers gameplan, centralized too much power into itself. We are rebalancing the Blessing by adding part of its bonus as an inherent property of the weapon, to also promote experimenting with other Blessings and build styles.
