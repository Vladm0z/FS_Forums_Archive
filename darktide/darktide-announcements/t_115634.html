Hey Everyone,

Hotfix #82 (1.10.1) is currently rolling out across our platforms. In the meantime, you can read the notes below.

General Bug Fixes

  • Fixed an issue with talent trees being wiped for some classes (will unfortunately not fix the trees that have already been wiped)
  • Fixed an issue where some players would not get access to their Hive Scum Deluxe Edition items until entering the hub with another character.
  • Fixed an issue where the Special Assignments menu would not close properly while using a gamepad.
  • Fixed an issue where the Play button could disappear after entering and exiting the Special Assignments menu.
  • Fixed an issue that could cause a crash when warpfire from the Psyker Talent ‘Wildfire’ spread to other enemies
  • Fixed a crash that occurs when you have Stimm Field and +5s Stimm Duration talent and pick up any street Stimms other than the medical Stimm.
  • Fixed server crash that occurs when a player who places down a Stimm Supply despawns before the Stimm Supply duration ends.
  • Fixed multiple instances of missing translations in the Psykhanium.
  • Fixed an issue with a Penance description mentioning melee attacks instead of critical strikes.
  • Fixed multiple instances of missing translations in the Hive Scum’s talent tree.
  • Fixed an issue with loadouts being unequipped after a Personality Scourge.

Cosmetic bug fixes

  • Fixed some visual issues with implants on the Hive Scum “Cartel Casual” cosmetic upper body when using specific body types.
  • Fixed an issue with unnaturally smooth skin around implants on the premium store upper body for Hive Scum.
  • Fixed a minor clipping issue on two faces for the Hive Scum’s Deluxe Edition headgear cosmetic.
  • Fixed a clipping issue with a specific hair style and face when having the head plate augment equipped.
  • Fixed an issue with skin around implants not transitioning smoothly from the rest of the body.

We’ll see you on the Mourningstar.

– The Darktide Team
