Warband!

Today’s hotfix includes a handful of fixes. The patch is now rolling out on all platforms, and you can read the notes below!

Hotfix 1.12.5 patch notes

Fixes

General Fixes

  • Fixed a crash occurring when Zealot players disconnect mid use of Fury of the Faithful.
  • Fixed Chordclaw ability being able to be used without consuming charges.
  • Fixed a crash occurring when Veteran hotjoins a game with Leave No One Behind talent.
  • Fixed a rare crash occurring when the Servo Skull is reviving an ally but the player being revived dies.
  • Inviting your Steam Friends from the Steam Overlay into your Strike Team now works again.
  • Fixed an issue where newly created characters could not opt out of the campaign.

Cosmetic Fixes

  • Fixed visible hole on Cadia’s teeth Chainsword.
  • Fixed ammo meter and broken reload on Resplendent Fury Laspistol.
  • Fixed hair clipping into headgears.
  • Fixed Hair clipping with Scryer’s Eyewrap with Psykana Collar.
  • Fixed wrong colors being displayed on Pistolero’s Duty Uniform & Gun-Hand’s Duty Uniform.
  • Harsh Environment Flak Helmet with Psykana Collar (Montar Camo) with orange emissive light renamed to MkIII Harsh Environment Flak Helmet with Psykana Collar (Montar Camo) to avoid name duplication.
  • Fixed issue with powered VFX being offset on Pyre Blade.

That’s all for today!

We’ll see you on the Mourningstar.

– The Darktide Team
