Something that I found that may help you guys that has resolved the majority of my lags.

When looking for a game, go in the parameters of connection.

I have servers very far away from me like hong kong (I am in Latin America) that falsely show up at 1 ms so the game selects it as the best server (when the real ping must be 4000ms).

I select latin america (around 100 ms for me) and I can play without the majority of the lags I encountered before ! Hope this helps !
