Issue Fixed: Reason still unknown.
1: Saw a post about someone managing to play well on Bazzite.
2: Installed Bazzite on my old Drive “F” which somehow held the boot of Windows “C”.
3: Ran a match on Bazzite, well over double my framerate. Good usage of the CPU and such.
4: Had to provide a new “boot” partition for Windows, on a slice of “C”, named “Z” or whatev.
5: Finally boot Windows 11 again, try Darktide to compare performance just to surprisingly see it finally run at 100+ FPS where it should.

So somewhere along the line, whatever was wrong got sorted.
(In the process I had to disable and enable once again Secure Boot).
