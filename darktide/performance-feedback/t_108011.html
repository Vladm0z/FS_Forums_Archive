Hello!
Your Darktide FPS drop from 140+ to 40-60 after one match, even after reinstalling everything, strongly points to thermal throttling (CPU/GPU overheating) or a memory/VRAM leak. Crucially, monitor Dog translation app your CPU and GPU temperatures and clock speeds using tools like HWMonitor during gameplay; if temps hit 85°C+, it’s likely throttling. Solutions include improving laptop cooling (pads, cleaning fans, repasting thermal compound), monitoring RAM/VRAM for leaks, ensuring all Darktide settings (especially Volumetric Fog/Ray Tracing) are truly at their lowest, and verifying Windows/NVIDIA power settings.
