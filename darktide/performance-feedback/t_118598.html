Headhunter • 2026-02-12 22:56:47
Danubian93:

I start the game with 4.4/3.9 gb usage - still 60 fps

That’s when it starts using system RAM and slows down after your GPU VRAM 4GB limit.

Texture Quality and Resolution has biggest effect on VRAM usage.

As a test to show you. Even 1080p and Texture Quality Low. Then it will still hit 4GB VRAM. All I did was load into training ground and back again.

Switch to 720p and Texture Quality Low. VRAM usage drops slightly 3.7GB

Put all your graphics to lowest. Leave FOV as it is.

Go 720p for resolution

DLSS - Doesn’t matter here. Upscaling options at low resolution it will look the same from Performance to DLAA. So might as well put on Performance.

Play a mission, then check your VRAM usage.

Now if VRAM limited still, normally a person would suggest going lower resolution. But there is a mod that allows Texture Quality “Very Low”.

More Graphics Options - Performance boost at Warhammer 40,000: Darktide Nexus - Mods and community

So then could try 720p and Texture Quality Very Low. VRAM usage goes up slightly from initial load, but tends to “settle” a bit higher.
