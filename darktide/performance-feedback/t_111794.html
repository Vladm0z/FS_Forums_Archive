The shader cache builder has been broken for the game for a while so those going from green to red (and even the other way around) will have big micro stutter issues. We’ll have to see what the Tuesday patch will bring us.

In the meantime, I recommend turning ambient occlusion down to low and textures to medium. I don’t know why but for some reason the AO is disproportionally performance intensive and medium textures have less stutter for little graphical change.
