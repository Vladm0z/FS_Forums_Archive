I’ve been thinking about how the current Curio system works. Since Fatshark is likely looking at ways to improve trinkets and curios, I wanted to share a suggestion to make them more impactful and balanced.

The Core Change: No More Stacking

In this system, you could no longer stack three of the same Curio type. No more running three 17% Toughness curios, for example. Instead, every build would have three distinct slots: 1 Defensive, 1 Offensive, and 1 Utility.

This sounds like a big change, but it makes way for much more powerful individual items:

1. Defensive Curios (Health, Toughness, Wounds)
Since you can only pick one, these would receive a buff to compensate:

  • Health & Toughness: Buffed to +25% at maximum rating.

  • Wounds: These would now scale like Stamina (e.g., +1 to +3 wounds based on rating).

  • The “Wound Fix”: To make Wounds viable, they would now increase your bleed-out timer (longer time before capture) and increase Health Stim efficiency. At max rating, a single Stim could clear up to 3 wounds of corruption for the user.

2. Offensive Curios (New Category)
These would introduce entirely new ways to customize your combat style:

  • Ammo Capacity: Increase total max ammo (e.g., +10% to +25%).

  • Grenade Capacity: Increase total grenade count (e.g., +1 to +3 extra grenades).

3. Utility Curios (Stamina, Movement Speed)

  • Stamina: Moved to this slot and buffed to include Stamina Recovery Speed alongside the extra bars.

  • Movement Speed: A new Curio type that increases total max movement speed (e.g., +5% to +10%).

Why this works:
Right now, the meta is often just stacking one stat. This rework forces players to make meaningful choices: “Do I want more grenades or more ammo?” or “Do I want to be tanky with Health or fast with Movement Speed?” It turns Curios into a true extension of your Talent Tree.

(Please keep in mind that any numbers provided are just examples and would be subject to balancing!)

If you have any questions or thoughts on these numbers, let me know!
