Do current high-safety builds still have enough real weaknesses?

10 Posts • 154 Views

Strong builds should exist. The issue is not that these builds are powerful, but that they may be combining too much safety, reliability, recovery, and role coverage without enough meaningful weakness.

Flame Staff Psyker is the clearest example after the recent changes. Flame Staff already has excellent horde clear, area denial, stagger, and mixed-wave control. If it also becomes reliable into harder armored targets, especially carapace-heavy pressure, then its role becomes unclear. At that point it is no longer just a control or horde-clear tool — it starts becoming a general answer to too many situations.

This can make high-difficulty waves feel less interactive. Enemies enter a choke, get staggered, burned, controlled, and often die before the team has to make many decisions. Psyker should have answers to armor, but Flame Staff may be losing one of its main tradeoffs.

Veteran’s Voice of Command has a different issue. The ability has been adjusted several times, but the revive branch still makes it extremely difficult to replace in high difficulty. Instant ability-based revives change how much punishment a team can absorb.

Reviving a downed teammate should be one of the most dangerous and meaningful actions in Darktide. If that risk can be bypassed by pressing one ability, then numerical nerfs to cooldown, toughness, or radius may not solve the core problem.

I do not think Voice of Command should be made useless. Veteran should have strong team utility. But the revive branch may need a redesign, such as speeding up manual revives instead of instantly picking teammates up, or adding a heavier tradeoff for taking that effect.

The goal should not be to make strong builds weak. The goal should be to make sure strong builds still have real weaknesses.

408075015 • 2026-05-26 08:07:11

I agree. The biggest issue with revive shout probably isn’t something that can be fixed by simply lowering the numbers, because the effect itself changes how people use the ability. As long as it exists, teams are encouraged to treat it as an emergency insurance button rather than a normal team-support tool.

But that is also exactly why I think it needs to be addressed. The problem is not just that the revive node is “strong”; it is that, on higher difficulties, it has become close to the default answer. No matter how good Veteran’s other abilities or branches are, instant revive is so valuable that not taking it is often treated as the wrong choice, and in some groups it can even get you blamed or kicked. At that point, Veteran technically has options, but in practice there is not much real build tradeoff.

So I am not saying it should be nerfed into uselessness, and I do not think Veteran should lose strong team utility. My point is that revive shout should not be so dominant that it crowds out the rest of the class’s choices. Whether that means removing instant revive, changing it into faster manual revives, or attaching a much clearer cost to the effect, the goal should be the same: not taking revive shout should still be a reasonable choice, rather than looking like you are deliberately weakening the team.
