Lactamid • 2026-10-04 15:10:43

Talked about Melk before in some post/thread here and how his Melk-bucks become rather useless after a while when you have the weapons and curios you want.

The same of course goes for every resource - gold, plasteel, diamantine - so I thought to myself: what could be something that we could spend it on?

Naturally I thought about something that goes away, consumables, but I don’t want massive power creep, and ideally it should not be too much developer effort either.

The first two things I thought of were:
Flares - throw them on the ground for a bit of light, and possibly mark something (different colors).

Auspex batteries - so you can use the auspex to find stuff (ammo, stimms, books etc)

Those things have relatively small impact, and could be sold by Melk for a few Melk-bucks. Not so much as to that you don’t use them, but enough to take away a little of your horde.

You could also have more “cosmetic” stuff, and here I do not mean skins, but rather small stuff like a bottle of what ever “moon shine” is called in 40k.
Maybe you can have a little medal you can give a team mate? A purity seal that shows up when you inspect a player? Or a bit of paint to mark someone.
Yes I do see the issue of the players turning this into the opposite of the positive reinforcement it can be and go “ok yellow paint means this person is someone who quits the game” or what ever. And maybe therefore we only spend these little things at the end of the mission?
Maybe you make your own purity seals with a group?

  • We swear to kill 3000 heretics in this mission - done? - ok you get a little seal/stamp on your profile when inspecting.
    It’s just for show, it doesn’t do anything - but maybe it helps a little with roleplay or the “feeling” of doing a mission together.

These are just ideas, and yes there are of course ways to make elaborate “end game systems”, that is not what this is about.

Do you have any ideas?
Remember:
Try to avoid power creep
Try to keep in “low effort” (not bad, just small)
