After what they plan to do to Ogryn’s Frag Bomb in the coming patch, I think it’s time to revisit vet’s Frag Grenades which was struck by the same “illness” and became basically obsolete with buff to kraks (which imo went too far).

I see two problems with them currently:

  1. Prior to increasing HP pool of gunners in particular, on a build fully dedicated to frags (with a bunch of nodes improving base damage taken etc) you could be sure that dropping two frags one after another would remove chaff and gunners in a vast area almost certainly. That was what made Frags competitive, comparing to kraks which removed Maulers and Ogryns with a targeted throw. It doesn’t happen anymore, there is a rather small gap (50-200 HP) now that prevents gunners from dying consistently even when they are rather close to the epicenter of the explosion

  2. Grenade Tinkerer isn’t really that useful when it comes to Frags. It give enormous +75% damage buff to kraks - and just +25% Damage (explosion damage only) and Radius to frags, seriously? Frags don’t deal much instantaneous damage in the first place, they deal almost all their damage via bleed stacks

Suggestion:

I see two possible ways to improve Frags’ balance without changing anything else:

  1. Simply increase amount of Bleeding stacks they apply, to compensate for the increased HP pools
  2. ..or change Grenade Tinkerer so that it would add extra Bleeding stacks instead of the explosion Damage (still should keep the +25% radius buff); kinda sub-optimal solution, as it increases talent point tax for frag users, to just get back what they had before
