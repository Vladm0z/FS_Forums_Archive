The BbD Update Feels Microwaving Chicken and Rice

9 Posts • 163 Views

LungsMD • 2025-09-29 21:06:10

The granular details of the game have been updated, and are piping hot, but the meat of the game is practically cold.

I want to be clear; I don’t dislike this update, but despite my initial excitement and my overall appreciation for the changes , I’ve found myself disappointed with the update overall. It’s my understanding that at least the Veteran and Psyker will be getting further updates, and I desperately hope that Zealot is a part of those further changes. I’ve posted a lot of my thoughts on all the classes, because I play all the classes, and my frustration isn’t that the changes aren’t perfectly reflective of my own suggestions. It’s more so that out of the three major tree updates, I definitely feel like Veteran has walked away the best, but not great exactly. Psyker feels enjoyably playable into high tier content for the first time in a while. And Zealot doesn’t have a mono-build anymore. But for all of this, the three classes really only feel like they’ve reached the starting line.

As a Zealot main specifically, I feel like there’s definitely a difference between Build Variety and Variety of Builds. It’s a lot easier to create different builds, but those builds don’t feel especially different from one another. And the class feels more guided by the overall weapon balance of the game more than any other. Their skill tree doesn’t come with enough benefits to ranged weapons to empower the weaker ones enough to be worthwhile, and despite all the bonuses to melee, they also can’t make the weaker melee weapons viable into high tier play, which means you’re playing much the same way as you did before the update, with Bolters and Dueling Swords reigning supreme. Psyker, despite having an easier time building for melee and guns, still has a tree that can’t resolve the sheer utility and survivability that staffs offer up over the other two playstyles. Worse yet, some of their options (like Crystalline Focus) only feel realistically tuned for Havoc (as a competent team will probably kill the target you want to explode on before the animation can finish). And Veterans melee potential is far less contrived, but still feels like a bit of an afterthought. And these problems feel so bad because this is a game where theoretically melee and ranged prowess is important for every class.

And then there’s the other aspects of the update; Sefoni’s new map and the new Weapons. And to be clear, I really like these additions. But Sefoni’s is a sidemode to Darktide, just like Havoc is. They aren’t the reason I come back to play the game; just a distraction when I feel like I need a break from the typical mission structure and difficulty. And while the New Bolters and Power Falchion are welcomed additions, The Bolters only further reinforce the weapons overbearing meta, and the Power Falchion does nothing to address the deficiencies w/ the Power Sword. And don’t even get me started on Plasma Gunners. An enemy that feels like it does twice as much damage as a sniper, at twice the rate, with twice the health, and twice the spawn rate, moving through a hoard while firing and giving very little feedback on when they are going to shoot or where they’re positioned? Yeah; Not sure who’s bright idea was that but they unironically drag down the entire experience.

Ultimately, it all feels a bit frivolous, like a distraction from the greater problems that continue to rot away at the reasons that I had fallen in love w/ Darktide in the first place. I want to be hopeful for the future, but this update feels like far too little far too late, when what the game truly needs to hold me is something more substantial. A month before this update, for the first time since the game released, I had uninstalled Darktide. And this update hasn’t, for me personally, felt like it’s been worth the reinstall. It’s a novelty place holder at best.

In this update, there were essentially no real balance changes.
The main focus seemed to be the expansion of the so-called skill trees.
What Fatshark should really be doing is fundamental balance adjustments to the base game itself.

I think the adjustments to CDR and such are a step in the right direction. While overall elite health was increased, the existing weapon and class balance remains in a pretty bad state. As you all know, even after the update, many players are still using the same old meta, with almost no change in abilities or weapon usage. The gameplay patterns are nearly identical every time: spreading Gold Toughness with Chorus or VoC, spamming Bubble one after another, and burning everything with Inferno. I see this exact scene in practically every single match. The game loop has become so formulaic that it’s honestly not fun anymore.

I also tried playing Havoc, but it was exactly the same experience.

Gold Toughness, CDR, the overall roles of enemies… all of these need to be taken into consideration when making balance changes. Otherwise, like in this update, adjusting a few aspects in isolation is completely meaningless if the related systems remain unbalanced.
