I like ADSing because it allows your bullet slinging to be more accurate at longer ranges.

that is probably due to balancing. i think the idea behind not allowing ADS is precisely so you are forced to close the range, and cannot simply interrupt i.e. a sniper at long range with such a weapon.
think about it this way: if this purposefully close-range weapon now also can do some utility at long range reliably, it would further decrease the incentivize to use infantry-guns and other such weapons, which are a lot worse at close range than the shredder autopistol.

i’m not saying the weapons are actually balanced this way, because the game fundamentally lacks weapon balancing due to power-creep of “fancy new toys” and other stuff, but i guess that was originally the idea behind it.
