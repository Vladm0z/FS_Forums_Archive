First of all, this was translated by software, so please forgive any parts that may be unclear.
My main source of enjoyment in the game comes from completing Havoc 40 and the high-difficulty Golden Vortex as quickly and effortlessly as possible (meaning with as little health loss and as few deaths or knockdowns as possible), and my comments are based on these challenging missions. (Although after playing through them, I’ve completely lost the courage to use it in Havoc 40.)
Regarding the Skitarii, let’s start with the weapon stats. The stats for all three unique melee weapons are downright ridiculous. Maybe the hammer is slightly better, but not by much. As a Mechanicus member specializing in machinery, the Arc Hammer’s stats are worse than the Inquisitor’s Intimidation (Intimidation?!) The hammer and dual swords are practically on par with the dual daggers (a pair of steel pipes?) used by the Nest scum, and the power sword is completely outclassed by the one a veteran picked up on the battlefield (is it some kind of sacred relic?). Honestly, this is quite baffling. Similarly, when it comes to ranged weapons, aside from the Arc Rifle, the other two are basically weaker than or on par with weapons of the same type from other classes. Even the Arc Rifle suffers from severe limitations in terms of ammo endurance. As for active skills, aside from Claws (which is indeed very powerful), the other two are just playable—but that’s about it. There’s almost no difference between using these skills and not using them at all (again, excluding Claws). While the issues with Blitz and Cornerstone are minor, they still lack distinctiveness. As for passive skills, to be honest, their effectiveness is surprisingly low. If you don’t want to use active skills and only want to rely on the class-exclusive weapon, this class’s melee damage is nothing short of a disaster. For the first time, I actually found that Power Scimitar (a crude imitation of the Relic Sword) to be a decent option.
As for the mechanics, they still start with melee weapons. The implementation for the two weapon types other than dual swords is excellent and quite fun, but when it comes to dual swords… well, this is the first time I’ve realized that changing your attack path—or rather, your grip—makes such a huge difference for sonic weapons. Am I wielding some kind of walking stick? Is the handle too soft to hit people with, so I have to poke them with the tail end instead? That said, the idea of switching slashes is genuinely interesting, but its terrible stats (I have to mention this) completely overshadow the fun. As for skills, I do find it a bit of a shame that there aren’t any radiation, explosive, or thermal effects. The arc mechanics are passable, though—even if they do seem to copy the Magi’s electric shock mechanic—but the fact that arc weapons are blocked by “metal” shields and armor is just unacceptable. Sonic weapons, which behave exactly like regular bladed weapons, are even more disappointing (Yes, I know it shakes violently in the animation, but that’s completely useless.) That revolver could have had a more interesting effect instead of firing a red-hot iron ball (even though it isn’t one, the effect really looks like it). As for the Blitz abilities, I think the shield-consuming grenade is a terrible idea—it makes no logical sense. Why would a non-throwing projectile consume a grenade instead of charging up? The other two Blitz abilities are actually pretty fun, though.
All in all, I find this class very disappointing. The melee aspect completely fails to deliver a mechanically precise combat experience, I don’t understand why so many people on the forums are calling for its melee power to be nerfed—all I see is a maggot writhing on the battlefield, swinging a scrap of scrap metal (its signature weapon) once it’s separated from its claws. As for the ranged aspect, setting aside the issue of sustain, even if its signature weapon’s power is barely passable, it’s still unsatisfying. Mechanically, it’s barely passable, but I feel the synergy between the minor skills and its charge mechanism is still too weak. That’s all.
