Idea: Partially replaceable talent trees.
Not full trees, but parts of it. Adds new blitz, abilities and keystones.

dllccftrees615×920 268 KB

Benefits: New class mechanics are time consuming because they typically only come with a brand new class. That’s new voice lines, animations, weapons, etc. Lots of work.

Adding only new talents significantly reduces amount of work while still providing many similar benefits.

Risk: Adding new talent nodes as DLC paid or free. If paid then risk is backlash due to perceived “P2W”.

Also possible concerns from community over getting “lazy” content over more meaningful stuff such as fully packaged new classes. Idea is to get both.

We all know there is a notoriously long amount of downtime between updates.

This method should let meaningful content drops appear more frequently.

Just remove auras, abilities, and blitz options from the talent tree and instead tie them to curios as blessings.
