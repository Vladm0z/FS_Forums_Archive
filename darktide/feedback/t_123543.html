You reduced the Crusher and Bulwark spam, and I commend you for it, FatShark.

I do, however, have a huge issue that now said enemies either die immediately or get staggered into oblivion. Either way, they do not get to even swing at you unless you are completely focused on something else for a couple of seconds.

I guess what I think would help make Ogryn enemies have more of a presence is:

  • Further reduction of player power (which is already in the plans)
  • Buff to their stagger resistance so they just do not get perma-staggered and get to try and hurt us. I’d say increasing it to the point where explosions no longer throw them onto the ground would work very well.
  • Increasing the amount of stamina that blocked attacks of Bulwarks and Reapers take away from the player would also be very good at making them a bigger threat (Cushers are fine in the amount they take, which begs the question as to why the other two didn’t get the same treatment).
  • Bulwarks being capable of moving forward while being shot at. I think the only weapons that should stop them at bay are Ogryn guns and explosives.
  • Making the Ogryns capable of knocking the players into the air like the Captain bosses can. I’m referring specifically to Cursher’s and Bulwark’s upward attacks and Reaper’s uppercut.

These buffs could also warrant a further reduction in their spawn rates if the buffs prove to be too much for the players, by the way. I just wish for them to not be total pushovers in the game.

Also, if we already reduced Bulwark and Crusher spam, we can do the same for the Reapers now. What do ya say?

Would love for Bulwarks to get some QoL. Currently they are a bit uninteractive for my taste due to their shield absorbing everything no matter what. Anyways, introducing more counterplay is a good vehicle in my opinion for making them hardier along the way. Currently I don’t think Bulwarks should have more HP. But if they had a more engaging moveset I could see the value in having them stick around for longer!
