The problem was never queuing into dying squads. It was how long it took to do so, how long it takes for the end screen, respawning in mourningstar, and then getting back to mission select. The first fix was avoiding the actual issue of the end game screen and load times, and this fix would avoid the issue of fixing the queue and add another problem on top of it. Buffing them when they’re solo incentivises them to never pick their teammates up because they can just keep playing as is. You’ve just added another problem, not solved the initial one at all.
