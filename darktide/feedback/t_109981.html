So, I have a lot of hours in VT2. Darktide always lagged my PC, so last time I left feedback I was only really playing tanky/support stuff until I got bored of the lag. Now I can play the game properly.

These are mostly negatives, but I am enjoying the game, but want it to improve.

General:

  • I’m not a massive fan of lower HP characters having too much damage and Corruption going through Toughness - one tiny mistake or just bad luck can make you have to play more defensive, so getting chipped down is a bit annoying - WITH THE CURRENT STATE OF OGRYN AND ARBITES AS THE TANKIER MORE FORGIVING CLASSES THAT DO THE MOST DAMAGE THERE IS NO REASON FOR THIS DAMAGE
  • Rotation in shop is slow - I feel like you could attach the gun skins to the gun cosmetics sections in the inventory, and then fill those gaps on the shop with actual masks
  • Faces are all really ugly and it makes me avoid skins where the face is showing completely
  • Gearing and Mastery was pretty good
  • Things like Dodge caps, and certain buffs that you need to see still rely on mods. not sure why the builds are being designed around specific buffs if there’s no official way to outline those buffs
  • Making certain builds heavily reliant on Crit stacking ruins build variety
  • If you’re not adding gun customization, in terms of scopes. I think it might even be better to not have ADS, but just zoomed hip fire, for visual clarity. Helbore has been ugly for too long!

Visual Clarity:
This was in my last review too and it seems like very small changes have been made (Psyker peril option), but nothing has been done for ADS or ironsights, where the guns look like cars driving across the screen.

Scopes/Sights also add even more distortion and don’t even show where the bullets are actually going (without like 3 mods lol).

I’ve never had tired eyes or eye strain more in a game than this.

Someone made the argument before that it’s to mitigate range spam, but most of the best ranged weapons in the game rn are hip fired, so?

  • Arbites Shotgun (Vet replacer)
  • Ogryn guns
  • Plasma Rifle
  • Psyker Staffs

Mods I’ve had to use:

  • Clean Force Blocking
  • Invisible Shields
  • Invisible allies if they’re close
  • Disable Screen Effects
  • Weapon Customization (using barrels and ironsights that don’t obstruct)
  • No Scope Plugin for Weapon Customization

Curios:
While I like the choice, it’s not really a choice, because some enemies and some stats seem required, like 3 Stam, Toughness x2.

The other stats aren’t reflected in build choices and it would be nice to get like a separate Talent tree with just 3 Talents, but multiple options with specific things, so that you could bounce these off the Curio and make the stats more meaningful. Arbites gas Talent is a good way of doing it too.

Psyker:

  • Lacking a Tracking Staff with high A-D strafing, where you beam heads while sliding about

  • Can feel really squishy and have to deal with blowing up from Peril, where other Classes can just have passives up for a lot of the match

  • Best damage (outside of unlimited cleave DoTs) always relies on Scriers Gaze, and on an already squishy class, I just wanted an Unchained(Vt2) playstyle, tanky/Support - I think that should be done and replace bubble spamming, as it’s boring and too good

  • Talent tree is a chore to figure out and requires way too much outside information on what’s good or pointless - you’re either doing 0 damage or over 1 million

  • No access to a melee build that isn’t Crit% - Mostlye due to health pool or Peril required for Toughness Generation - Also staffs are slow and require ramp up - wherease on VT2, you could shotgun spam during melee

  • Staffs on lower difficulties can feel slow with a high TTK (I get that they’re mostly insane on Havoc)

  • Staffs and Brain Burst (even with the build) feel soooo slow. I get that you get unlimited cleave, and can slide while doing stuff, but it just feels slow

  • Staffs Special Attacks should be other spells, not a Staff hit that bugs out or does nothing. E.g. self shield

  • Brainburst is really slow - You can go for the most out of the way enemy in the world and someone will 360 headshot it in one second, while you’re stood like this:

    man-showing-framing-hand-gesture-BAABK0-35610782971300×954 79.5 KB

  • Brainburst doesn’t scale well with higher difficulties, especially with the TTK vs Elites

It just makes me want to pull out a gun and dome the enemy in less than a second. Faster Brain Bursts relying on Career Skill usage is also kind of weird.

  • Smite should just become VT2 Beam Staff shotgun blast with Hunter, so you can fish for a melee damage buff (becomes the Horde clear + burst CC choice of Blitz)
  • Majority of the Melee and Range Weapons that aren’t Psyker specific don’t have any real synergy with the Talents
  • Every build requires high Peril to do more damage, and it really limits what you can play, as you always need access to Peril Generation
  • Requiring Elite kills for the bottom left Keystone messes with build diversity

Veteran:

  • Lacking Dual Wield guns
  • Simply selecting Arbites Shotgun is better than the entire Class???
  • Lacking in access to 1shots, without heavily building into them, and then lacking in Horde/Elite damage - even Ogryn has easier access to Special 1shots with Rock and Heavy Stubber (AND VISUAL CLARITY)
  • If you can’t get any melee damage in your build, you have to just sort of wait for Hordes to be dealt with or run from them
  • Talent tree is like ‘If it’s 5 o’clock on a thursday, the glass of water is half full, the wind is blowing the chimes for 10 seconds straight, you’re upside down facing at a 90 degree angle from the fountain - your gun will reach the headshot breakpoint’
  • On Vt2 you just took a weapon that could one shot headshot and get ammo back while doing it and you was the Special killer. On Darktide it’s an inconsistant ramp up that never ends and doesn’t get you where you want to be. You also run out of ammo while doing it - other classes just 1shot the enemies
  • No Horde clear Weapons
  • No access to ammo outside of Laser/Survivalist
  • Scopes/Ironsights are blinding
  • Support build doesn’t make your build any more fun to play, and feels a lot more passive than the Support builds on VT2 - WHC with Tag and AoE stun was still killing everything
  • Marksman’s Focus is really bad

Over all it feels like Vet’s Talent tree was designed by the enemies to stop you doing damage, while other classes criteria for getting buffs is a lot easier to reach, and they’re more versatile while doing it.

Zealot(My least played):

  • Lacking in build diversity
  • Lacking in playstyle diversity a bit
  • Lacking a big leap with Thunder Hammer that slams and AoEs everything
  • Grenades feel kinda meh. Should be renewable or changed for something else imo. I’m thinking speeeeeeed boosts, Stealth as a Blitz
  • Chorus is insanely good, but also quite boring. If you could nerf it or change it to blinding everything, and then give Zealot the Warrior Priest 1H single target mace at the same time, it could be more fun. You literally just stand there
  • Loner should become something like ‘inspiring kill - gives you and teammates x when you land a kill, but no need to be in coherency’
  • I feel like dagger builds that still give a lot of team support somehow should be a build, as that playstyle is insanely fun - you should draw a lot of aggro, but gain access to lots of passive Dodge
  • Lacking Dual Daggers that do single target light attack damage with downward stabs and high move speed

Ogryn:
hit bad man

  • The more I play Heavy Stubber with Rock and Shield, the less I want to play Vet at all

Arbites:
hit bad man (he broke the law)

Enemies:
Hordes and Elite density can feel too small on Auric.

Even if you can add a less deadly Elite that’s just there to let you use your heavy cleave build, please give us more to whack that doesn’t die instantly.

Cover:

  • If you’re slightly obscured from Gunners, I feel like their accuracy should be lowered, as sometimes cover just straight up doesn’t work - I think this because the cover can sometimes be either lacking or strangely shaped
  • Veteran’s thing where you can peak from behind waist high cover doesn’t stop you getting domed
