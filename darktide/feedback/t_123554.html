Se we all know Expeditions are not really good as of now, as there is genuinely absolutely no reason to do them, besides “trying out the new thing”. If all they were meant to be, was a lead up to the release of Ad Mechs, that genuinely could have been a single mission under the Operations tab, right next to the Trenches (which is a very cool map to be honest)

I have read a couple of ideas how to improve them, and i have come up with a single idea, that might actually just fix the replayability issue.

So you know how the stats of our weapons lock-out at 80%? How about we use the currency we collect in Expeditions, for no apparent reason, to overclock a single stat of our weaponry?

Ex: My damage stat of this sword is capped out at 80%. I use an unspecified amount of the Expeditions currency to overclock that stat to 100%, reaching the maximum potential for that specific stat. Or maybe, my Mobility stat is maxed out at 60%, so i’m feeling a bit whimsical today and decide to overclock the Mobility stat to 80%, for a nice, clean balance of 80% across the board. This could also be a nice segue to turn our Yellow weapons into the long coveted Red, veteran tier weapons.

The reason i’m thinking this way is because i believe there are multiple weapons in the game that lack that critical breakpoint for one-shots by a minimal amount, and this could help breach that breakpoint, opening an interesting amount of build variety with weapons simply forgotten by either time, or power-creep.

Lemme know what you think. Cheers.

So “spend Expeditions currency to increase a stat bar above the normal cap” has a bunch of design issues with how the game of Darktide works, not least of which is making Expeditions functionally “mandatory” for high level players who want the best gear, and the resentment that would breed would be legendary. There’s a slew of other issues introduced by such a system, mostly in the realm of balance and player incentive, and it’s kinda very nasty.

That said, the core of the idea, using Expeditions currency rewards to modify gear is not in and of itself a bad one. And there’s a very obvious way to leverage it that does fit into the greater game. Specifically, “spend Expeditions currency to move stat points from stat A to stat B”.

So for example, if I have a, say, power sword with 80% Damage, 78% Finesse, 72% Cleave Damage, 79% Cleave Targets, and 71% Mobility, I could run a few Expeditions and take the Tech Remnants to a vendor located next to that elevator by the queue map. There I could use Tech Remnants to move points out of Mobility to get 80% in all other stats and end with 60% in Mobility.

This gives a reason to do Expeditions, as you could then just take any weapon from Brunt’s Armory and use the currency to create a godroll with exactly the stat spread that you want. But it also doesn’t make it feel mandatory, because long term players with a reasonable bank of Dockets could just random roll from Brunt’s Armory until they get the exact roll they’re after. It’s a win-win all around, really.
