There’s not much challenge left in the game now. Overall, everything feels way easier. Even outside of Havoc, in Auric, the game barely feels threatening anymore. A lot of matches feel closer to regular Damnation or even Heresy instead of the insanity it used to be. H40 basically feels like Auric V-E-G with blight or rituals slapped on top. It doesn’t have that same fear factor anymore where you’d get rushed by death balls of 8+ Crushers and maulers stacked behind Bulwarks, while hitscanning Reapers, Flamers, and Trappers were all layered into the chaos at once.

I can understand how the old enemy spawns probably affected performance or stability, but now it feels like weenie hut jr’s compared to what we used to deal with. Back then, you could end up fighting 2 to 5 bosses on top of all the other instant kill threats happening at the same time. Yeah, sometimes situations were borderline unwinnable, but the fact that there was still a possibility of clutching it made the mode exciting and worth playing.

Maybe I’m just too used to the chaos or have Stockholm syndrome from old H40, but come on. I don’t know if this is them prepping for H50 or what, but the current difficulty just doesn’t hit the same anymore. Ty for joining my Ted talk.
