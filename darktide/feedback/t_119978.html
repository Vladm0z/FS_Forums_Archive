Hello, I wish to explain a big issue I have with one of the new penances for the Expedition mode. I hope these concerns can be taken seriously and hopefully adjusted in the future.

The new penance “Data Harvester” is arguably one of the more frustrating challenges in the game, and in my opinion, for all the wrong reasons. The penance have 3 stages of completion and is described as followed.

“Harvest or extract with (X amount) of Data Reliquaries with the help of your teammates.”

The first stage of completion requires 10 reliquaries.

The second stage requires up to 50 reliquaries.

And the final stage is a total of 100 reliquaries.

This penance did not look like much on the surface, until I realised that the penance only tracks the reliquaries that you specifically harvest or extract, not your team. Realising this, I could immedietly see how this penance could create some serious problems. Generally, only about 1-4 reliquaries are found during a full three-zone Expedition run. The mere fact that it only tracks individually, will create issues within the team, and cause players to fight over who carries and moves the reliquarie to extraction or the sanctuary.

If it were me, I personally would love to see this penance be based on teamwork and remove the individual progression. By making the penance progress based on the amount of reliquaries, harvested or extracted by the entire team, this challenge will immedietly become more enjoyable. Otherwise, you would have to do a total of 400+ runs just for four people to aquire this objective, which seems a bit ridiculous.

Thank you for reading and I hope this penance can be adressed.
