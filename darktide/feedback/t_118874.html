So, I had an idea and wanted to hear thoughts of other players about that one. May be start a discussion that will drag attention of FS’s CMs, making them to consider this option? Who knows.

Let’s assume FS has some kind of homebrew map editor for DT (it’s usually the case, it simplifies future content development; we also know for sure they have internal tools for recoloring / reskinning cosmetics). So what if they’ll decide to release it to the community, allowing us to create custom maps with it - and would implement a new mode of play that would allow us to play those maps online with friends?

Then may be once a month they could allow some of the most popular maps to be nominated for becoming a part of the game by voting? Then we would be able to see them on rotation on a special section of Missions Terminal in the game - with everybody (not just your friends) be able to play them, like any other “vanilla” mission in the game.

To me it looks like something that could literally save this game, which is - let’s be honest - is stagnant now, starving for content. It’s harder and harder to find any interest in replaying the same builds on the same maps with the same foes for yet another 100hrs, for me and many others I believe. Influx of fresh ideas from the community could be that difference that would spark a new interest to the game again among the “older” playerbase.

Share your thoughts?
