Suggestion: Skitarii's Resurgence aura should increase coherency radius too

7 Posts • 212 Views

Effigy • 2026-06-21 00:30:08

Resurgence aura for the new Skitarii class has an interesting concept, but I don’t think it will be a popular pick due to the niche position of coherency toughness regen in the modern metagame. The other auras for this class are just more generically useful.

Rather than change the existing function of Resurgence (which I do think could be build-enabling for some team builds), I’m recommending that the aura also grant +X% coherency radius to self and allies. That would make it more generally useful and fits the theme of the existing effect. I don’t know what number is appropriate here, but +10% radius seems reasonable.

EDIT: My initial estimate for the increased radius was based on Psyker’s Puppet Master (+75% coherency radius), but divided among party members. After further consideration, I lowered the recommended bonus since Resurgence has other effects in its value proposition.

Agayek • 2026-06-21 23:44:15
Dizzy721:

How much coherency toughness regen do players receive in the average game?

With zero investment, it’s a 1.5s delay after taking damage before regenerating 3.75/s with 1x other player in coherecy and scaling up to 7.5/s with 3x players in coherency. With 3x toughness regen speed curios, this goes up to a 0.77s delay for 7.125/s to 14.25/s.

The aura plus investing into the regen means, with everyone in coherency, you’ll potentially regenerate a minimum of 3.6-7.12 toughness per second with a 0.77s delay after taking damage. This is roughly equivalent to the regen from Auto-Repair Doctrines, depending on your keystone and how aggressively you spend capacitance.

It’s not as good, because it’s interrupted by damage and relies on teammates being in coherency, so it most likely will average about half to 2/3 of the regen from ARD, but it’s not terrible sustain on paper. I’m really curious to test it and see if it’s maybe finally worth investing in toughness regen speed.
