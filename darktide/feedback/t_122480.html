Grenade etiquette is quite simple:

If there are multiple grenade users then grenades ALWAYS go to Scum and Ogryns, in that order if either one is present on the team. Everyone else either has an alternative, has regenerating grenades or doesn’t pick up grenades unless they are the last man standing.

Scum: Bazook and Chem nades are downright filthy. Want to clear out a never ending horde of ragers? take chems, want to stagger a boss and kill the crushers around it? take rockets. Want to kill one of those bugged rituals that Tubby Tuna hasn’t fixed for over a year before Hexbound Michael Phelps swims out to kill your whole team? One shot it with a rocket from across the map. The only exception to this is if the Scum has blackouts, in that case they don’t get grenades because they regenerate. However when both rockets and chems are exceptional there is no reason not to take either.

Ogryn: The upgraded box is a powerful CC tool that lets you stagger bosses, create breathing room and damage enemies all at the same time. And you have 3 of them allowing you to do it multiple times. The nuke on the other hand like rockets is your one stop shop for clearing the screen or a ritual. Unlike the rocket however you only get a single use which is a big shame, and if thrown on stairs it won’t even tickle enemies.

Arb: If you are running lone wolf then you regenerate grenades, there is no reason to pick them up unless you are the only one with grenades. If you take grenades and the dog instead of remote detonation then you need to go home and rethink your build.

Vet: You regenerate grenades, You do not need to pick up grenades, don’t even think about it.

Zealot: Don’t you dare take grenades. Both options are abysmal so don’t even bother, just take knives. Flame grenades are bad chem grenades and stun grenades are bad ogryn boxes. Seeing a zealot pick up stun grenades when a Scum could instead have 3 rockets is just painful. If this is you then I hope the other side of your pillow is always warm.

Psyker: Not picking up grenades so that Scum and Ogryns can have all the fun, 10/10, no notes.

Skitarii: TBD, however they don’t seem like they will outshine Scum or Ogryns. Skitarii’s non grenade blitzes using grenade pickups is also something that doesn’t sit well with me.

To recap on grenade priority:

  1. Scum
  2. Ogryns
  3. Skitarii
  4. Arbs
  5. Vets.
  6. Psykers.
  7. Zealots.
