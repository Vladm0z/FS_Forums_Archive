Here’s an idea for new psyker staves: Staves that primarily buff players and debuff enemies rather than do damage.

Biomancy staff.

It buffs your teammates.
Primary: Puts a buff on a teammate that lasts maybe 10 seconds, that buffs their melee strength and attackspeed (Iron Arm/Warpspeed biomancy powers). Gives a 10 second debuff afterwards during which it can’t be reapplied, so you have to select another.
Secondary: Continously Channel biomancy into a teammate to significantly increase their toughness regen, make them immune to stun, possibly even give them some dmg resist (ala the biomancy power “Ferrocrete Flesh”). Generates peril of course.

Telepathy staff:

Primary: Makes target enemy halluscinate, lowering the damage they do (because they have difficulty focusing properly on you, so their shots and melee attacks don’t hit well). Works on all enemies but good on gunners and reapers in particular. 10 second debuff that can’t be reapplied for 10 seconds (or something like that). Alternative function could be to just make a single enemy stand there holding it’s head (like it’s about to be brainbursted) for a time, during which it’s particularly vulnerable. Should work even on monstrosities but have like a 1-2 second duration at most, giving your teammates time to land a single big strike.
Secondary: Buffs your teammates willpower giving them strong corruption resistance (you bring this staff on a pox gas or blight mission of course).

Divination staff:

Primary: Alters allies perception of time, giving them time to aim and therefore land more deadly blows. Again a buff on teammate, significantly increasing their finesse/weakspot, and crit damage. Also giving them time to dodge attacks. In effect that means it gives more dodges, or lowers cooldown between dodge regen, or increases dodge distance/dodge duration, or some combination of all of the above.

Secondary: Channels the ability to sense enemy presence, enemy future movement, and enemy weaknesses to a teammember, so they basically gain the Vet ability to see enemies even through walls/fog/tox gas, and also increases allies cleave and armor pen(rending).

Edit: All of these staff effects have to be strong enough to compensate for the fact that when you’re playing ally buff-psyker you’ve mostly removed yourself from doing damage. You sacrifice your own damage to make allies stronger in return. You could even make a staff take effect through coherency for example.

InferusIcornix • 2026-09-17 15:40:43

For a long, long while, I’ve had an idea to rework the Electrokinetic staff in a way that makes it less of just an M1 headshot spam weapon, while also getting rid of the awkward special attack and overused M1 in its entirety.

So, it goes something like this:

M1: A quick chain-lightning shot that can chain to up to two additional targets besides the initial one hit. It doesn’t deal much damage, but applies an Electrocution debuff to enemies. These debuffs can stack up to 3 times per enemy. By itself, the Electrocution debuff does nothing.

M2: The current charged shot, but its damage scales depending on how many stacks of Electrocution the enemy has.

Special: A quick, low-range punch that releases 2 chain lightnings onto the hit enemy instead of the regular M1’s 1 chain lightning. It can be held to perform a heavy attack, which releases 3 chains instead. Has good stagger, so you can interrupt enemies attacking you without changing to melee.

Each chain hits a random target after the first one, but has a preference for chaining onto carapace enemies. So, if a carapace enemy is within the radius, there is a guarantee that it will be hit instead of any other base-type enemy. This makes the weapon better against armour, since, you know, lightning and such.

The basic gist is that you use M1 to set up a big boom with a charged M2. The Special is essentially a better M1, but it requires the psyker to get up close to the enemy, making it more situational.

But, yeah…the current state of psyker staves is very stale and uninspired, unfortunately. FS could have done so much more.
