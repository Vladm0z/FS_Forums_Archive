Voltaic Emitter's hit scan is awful

11 Posts • 350 Views
n
nitemarez44 • 2026-06-24 16:35:52

Problem:

As far as I can tell, Voltaic Emitter’s radius scan to check for enemies originates at the player’s feet, and only scans horizontally. The result is that ankle high geometry, or even gentle inclines, will block the ability. It also means enemies below you, but still within clear LOS, also aren’t affected. You can’t use it to fight up staircases because of this, and I’ve even run into issues where I was fighting between two train tracks, and enemies on the outside of the tracks were not affected by the ability.

Simply put: the ability is wildly inconsistent in what enemies it affects, and it feels awful to use because of that.

Proposed solution:

Just have the damn thing scan in a spherical radius from the player’s head instead of a 2d circle from their player’s feet.

Benefits:

  • It’s not blocked by random geometry your literally able to walk over without mantle/jumping
  • It just works.

Drawbacks:

  • It makes the ability stronger because of its increased consistency
  • A spherical hit scan is more computationally demanding

Compromises:

  • Cap the vertical height of the scan radius to +/- 6(?) meters. Kinda un-intuitive, but it also means it can’t shutdown galleries of shooters way above or below you.
  • There are many ways to optimize a spherical hit scan function You are smart devs, I’m sure you can figure it out.
