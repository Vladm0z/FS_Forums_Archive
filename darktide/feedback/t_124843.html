My suggestion is the option to hide enemy pings.

The reason is that I enjoy running Arbite Dogs and focusing servo skulls on Skitarii. Whenever I highlight an enemy for my skull or dog, people will do exactly what they are supposed to do. They make the highlight their main focus. Usually highlight = problem that needs to be fixed.

But what that sometimes ends up doing is distracting them from what they were currently doing. So if they were dealing with a horde or another elite, they will switch gears.

When I’m running arbite or skitarii, I’m usually trying to deal with elites so my teammates don’t have to. I don’t want them to try and focus on the gunner way down the hall especially when we have a horde right in front of us. But the highlight says “Fix this problem!” Even though technically I’m already working on it.

The ping should still work as is with items and medicae. But the ability to turn it off so I’m not distracting my teammates with distant threats I think might help reduce confusion and increase efficiency.

In the event there IS an enemy that is a problem, there’s also the backup marker (the skull) in the social wheel you can put down.
