Overall I’m enjoying the mode and can see huge potential but it’s certainly got some kinks to iron out. I keep wanting to play the mode despite the kinks, so that is a good sign imo.

Some idea/notes from my playing so far:

  • Give a height indicator to pings on the compass

  • Reduce grid size of new mini game - I like the style of the new minigame but the glyphs make it too difficult compared to other minigames. I suggest reducing the grid size to help this.

  • Too much waiting - I get that waiting is part of the game but there is an excessive amount in this mode. Especially around minigames. Wait on timer, then minigame, then wait again (?!), then press button, then wait again, then collect. It’s too much. I suggest that chests always immediately unlock once the first timer or minigame is complete. Solving the minigame should give the gratification of reward. The timer can be longer if need be, but just lock the waiting to 1 singular timer per objective.

  • Tornado - please block it from going directly over objectives. It’s just cruel and a real fun sponge and it always miraculously spawns at every single objective so it gets old really quick. It should be a nuisance for the journeying, not the objectives where you’re forced to wait in the same tiny spot for minutes.

  • Hounds modifier - I don’t have a good thing to say about this. They are too many, waaaaay too frequent (do they even have a cooldown??), and the openness of the map means they’re extremely OP as a modifier. It feels like 3 times per zone should be the max. Dealing with 4+ packs of hounds during a single zone means you can’t focus on anything other than dodging an infinite stream of dogs for 3/4 of the time. Not fun.

  • Holding the cannister - make the push attack actually push things. It barely moved a pox walker so it needs a buff, especially as it uses stamina. Give it the strength of a snipers kick!

  • Sunset/Dusk - could we get some variations in time of day aside from night/midday? A huge red setting sun with long shadows, and turning into a deep purple dusk would fit the desert style of map and would look gorgeous.

  • The extraction is a bit too messy. I get that messy is probably intended for brown pants moments but the way the valkarie pushes you away feels very jarring on that tiny little landing platform. I’d suggest you change the mission success to automatically trigger at the end of the timer and then it just plays a quick cutscene of the characters entering the valkarie after it’s gunned down remaining enemies. A bit like the cutscene endings after boss fights.

  • Allow 1 player to finish the mission if all the other players are downed. Not allowing this is deeply, deeply unfair for everyone. It doesn’t make any sense.

  • Some extra environmental hazards could be cool and could spice up the journeying between objectives. Minefields, toxic waste puddles, steam/acid geisers, toxic flora etc

  • Fix the distances for jumps - constantly failing to make jumps that look/feel like I shouldn’t have failed, is both frustrating and common on this mode that has loads of jumping required.

  • Please fix ogryn vault height (pleeeeease).

  • A few interior POI could be interesting - I’m still finding loads of cool new parts to the map/environment but I’m sad we don’t get to go into much of it. Perhaps some old bunkers could be interesting…like the end of the trenches mission.

  • Different biome/enemy - this mode feels like a perfect opportunity to try some new biomes and enemies out. Orks and jungle would be very cool!

  • Different styles of objectives - I do like that the objectives have quite a few variables but they still feel a bit samey at times. Perhaps we could have to escort a servo skull from point A to point B etc,

  • Give some rewards on a failed mission - just like materials on regular missions. It feels too deflating to lose a run on the 3/3 zone because your teammates flake out or the server flakes out. Losing every shred of painstakingly gathered resources does absolutely nothing for replayibilty. It just deflates me so much that I turn the game off.

  • Medicae in the safehouses should be free imo.

  • Joining an already started mission should always be with full health and zero corruption. As there is no healing, it’s too unfair to start in the middle of a clusterkark.
