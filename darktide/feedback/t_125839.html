I realize this post is going to be a total nothingburger but it’s been bugging me nonstop since they dropped and I wanna rant about it.

Every class has a unique nickname for every other class. This was true all the way through Hive Scum. You’ll always hear HS call Veterans “Killers”, cops always call Zealots “Firebrands”, etc. And then Skitarii came out and ruined everything.

Skitarii will always refer to every other class equally when it comes to random combat barks, so they interchangably use whatever was assigned most to their personality. Degradable, Bio-Matter, Unblessed One, whatever. This is breaking the established convention for dialogue and means that Skitarii don’t discriminate between each class. Additionally, Savant Psykers will refer to Skitarii as Tech-Warriors, which is the Zealot term, instead of Rust-Brains, which is the Psyker term.

Why is it like this??? It was fine with everyone else and then you activated my OCD!! UNFORGIVABLE
