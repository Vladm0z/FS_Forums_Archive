The current meta goes that if you take damage resist perks on curios, it has to be resist vs gunners, with them being the most numerous kind of elites/specials you will meet, and often the most obnoxious. And as players normally have very limited number of slots free from more important perks on their curios, other damage resists are almost never used.

Suggestion: make it so bomber/flamer damage resist also would reduce damage from regular environmental fire (from barrels mainly). The environmental fire resist effects should stack even across different kinds of such perks, so if you take one +20% vs bombers and one +20 resist vs flamers, you get effectively two stacks of +20% resist against environmental fire (stacking multiplicatively)

Won’t make them your default perks choice ofc, but at least they’ll be more worth considering.
