Torimori • 2025-07-21 21:38:46

you need to explain to me how failing to add something basic they’ve already made to the store that exists to provide earnable cosmetics already is going to evaporate their cosmetics profits.

I can. It’s a common marketing trick. So, look, you are relatively new player and you open a store from time to time. You see a particular item that looks not bad, it’s also seems to be “on sale” which ends in a day, then it will be gone for some time, until available again. And may be you won’t notice it next time, or it won’t be on sale? Tough decision, and so you decide to buy it.

Then you check the store next day - and see even better item which effectively (you think) is just ideal to your planned look, you need it now! it also makes the item you bought a day before obsolete, because it takes the same slot and much more attractive (to your eye). You are sad and angry - but still decide to buy the 2nd item too, because it will rotate in 2 weeks and who knows when you see it again?

Now what would happen if you could browse their full stock freely? Right, you wouldn’t buy the 1st item at all, you would only need the 2nd one. Even if at the moment only item 1 would be available (or on sale) - you still would decide to give it a pass, and wait for the item 2. That’s less profits for FS.

P.S.

It also helps to spread expenses more evenly across their stock. With looks, you never know if what you create will be a hit or miss. Some customizations will be much more popular than the others - while still requiring about the same effort to create them. But you still waste some time of your visual artists to develop the later, which don’t sell that well if people know there are better options they just need to wait for. If they don’t know though, they will be more prone to buy the worse ones (because they are still better than free ones) - and thus RoI will be better for those. You just could make the more good-looking one to be more expensive - but price can only go so and so high before it will cause outrage or people will stop buying it. Instead, you just trick them into buying the worse one, problem solved, they’ll still most likely buy the better one later too, because it’s, like, better.
