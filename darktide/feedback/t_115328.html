I heard people claim that FS does not want to nerf overpowered weapons because the Asian players will review bomb the game and as proof they mention a leak, that happened on the Chinese Steam which had nerfs for the Dueling Sword and the Plasma Gun. They suggested that a mere suggestion of nerfing the outliers caused a very impactful reaction from the Asian playerbase.

So I checked the number of negative reviews for DT on Steam days later after the leaks to the DS and Plasma Gun nerfs happened to see what really happened, and here are my findings:

obraz932×434 15.2 KB

Now, I found about the leaks through Mr E and his video was posted on July 19, so I assumed that’s when the leaks happened. But now, after seeing the sudden spike in negative reviews on Jun 18, I take this is where the leaks occurred.

  • On Jun 18 DT got +173 new, negative reviews.
  • On Jun 19 DT got +51 negative reviews,
  • On Jun 20, +25 negative reviews,
  • On Jun 21 +24 negative reviews,
  • On Jun 22 +8 negative reviews
  • On Jun 23 (Arbites release) we got +188 negative reviews (probably caused by people being angry with new class being premium DLC and not Asian gamers, upset that FS suggested DS nerfs).

So to sum these numbers up:

After the leaks that FS plans to nerf the DS and the PG, all the way up to the release DT got +281 negative reviews (excluding the Arbites release). And I must add that I haven’t read through all the reviews, so I do not actually know how many were left because of the leaks.

The only negative review that I found, that could be referring to the leaks, was this:

obraz597×205 38.9 KB

Translates to:

“Instead of making pointless adjustments, Fat Shark, you’d be better off increasing production capacity to rescue the servers and your mother in warp space.”

In conclusion:

While Jun 18 saw a huge increase in negative reviews being left on Steam for the game, the Arbites release has seen the same, if not slightly bigger, increase in negative reviews and the Arbites was a successful addition to the game that was a huge net positive for FS and DT.

Taking all of this data into account, I believe FatShark does not have to fear being review bombed by the Asian players, over them nerfing the Dueling Sword, the Plasma Gun and even others like the Flame Staff and the Rumbler.

While it is quite a huge spike in negative reviews, I don’t think it would run the game into the ground or affect the sales that much. I am saying this based on the fact that Hive Scum reveal trailer also saw an influx of negative reviews yet when I look at the game now, days later after the spike, before the class is even released, it says “Mostly Positive” in the Recent Reviews section.

In other words: the consequences from these kinds of player reactions do not last longer than a few days, and the game doesn’t really suffer from it.

Additionally, applaying these changes to DT will be a net positive for the game in the long run (nerfing the outliers will make it so enemies no longer die immediately, aka. Enemy spam can be reduced).

But what do you think? I wish to hear what people have to say to this post and the data within it.

Cheers!
