I personally prefer a high-paced combat style, so I enjoy the current state like AuricHISTG.
In Havoc, enemies spawn at an even faster rate, and with modifiers and rank-based difficulty scaling, they become even tougher.
As a result, breakpoints have shifted, and even with headshots, many weapons can no longer one-shot enemies.
The increase in bullet sponge enemies, combined with reduced ammo pickups, has drastically worsened ammo efficiency.
This has changed Darktide’s fundamental combat style, which was a hybrid of melee and ranged combat, into a game mode more like VT2, where melee is dominant.
All players are forced to kill Carapace and hordes as quickly as possible, leading to almost complete weapon meta-locking.
The skill tree was originally implemented to encourage diverse playstyles, but the game balance in Havoc renders that meaningless.
It’s almost been a year since Havoc was released. What has really changed during that year? The balance has only worsened, and modifiers like PoxGas and Encroaching Garden, neither of which are fun for players - have been added.
Alongside this, the combat tempo has steadily deteriorated, and combat times have only lengthened, sometimes exceeding an hour in extreme cases.
Many players are exhausted by this situation, and matchmaking has not been implemented; instead, we’re still stuck using outdated party finders.
Fatshark, it’s time to end this ridiculous cycle. Please stop wasting time on unnecessary things and fundamentally reexamine Darktide from the ground up.
