So. I’m gonna just say it right from the start, I LOVE the revolver, I love its design, action, and execution, It sets off my cowboy dopamine just right… But I really, REALLY hate how its so obsolete compared to every other weapon in the armory. Even after 117.4 hours of playing this incredible game, I feel like the revolver deserves some sort of redemption.

I am a COWBOY at heart, so when I had heard that Darktide has a revolver that you could fan the hammer into an incoming hoard, It peaked my interest immediately and once I was able to acquire one, I was met with the heartbreaking discovery that this revolver… Kinda sucks.

First issue I had was it’s reliability, If you don’t have the EXACT build for the revolver, the thing is next to useless. When I was able to use one for the first time, the next 5 missions almost made me wanna trash it immediately. I understand it’s a weapon you keep on a particular melee build, but it feels like it intentionally makes the player want to use THE GUN rather than the melee, I can not stress enough how satisfying it is to crouch slide toward a crusher and dropping it in a hammer fanning fashion. It just feels unfair that it doesn’t stand out on its own like the other weapons. I hate to say it, but The Shredder Pistol feels more reliable than The Revolver.

Second issue is the ammo, my god the ammo… Look, I can get it that its a heavy hitting weapon, so your not gonna have a lot of ammo for it, but how is that fair when you give the player the ability to spamfire it, and wastes all their ammo 10 minutes in cause the game just sent you through 5 hoards in one session. Now the same thing can be applied to the single-shot mark, now that makes more sense that it would be more crucial to make your shots count on the big boys rather than the hoard, but why, oh why, is the reload so slow and only one bullet at a time? I feel like the reload animation got swapped between the two marks, cause having the slower animation for the fan hammer mark and giving the single-shot mark the moon clip would make it feel more fluid. I’m sure there’s a gameplay explanation for that, it just feels off for me is all.

sddefault (1)640×480 34.4 KB

Last issue i have is it’s blessings, I feel like even if all of the blessings are at max, it offers very little advantage in it’s gameplay, at least from what I’ve experience. It feels so neglected compared to The Bolt Pistol, now I know I am comparing two completely different weapons here, but they both sorta operate in the in the same category, Slow, Heavy Hitting, Limited Ammo. What differs is the blessing The Bolt Pistol has, it gives it a massive advantage in it’s collateral and weak spot damage. It makes The Revolver feel puny compared to it, why wouldn’t you use the rocket pistol instead of the 5 cylinder pea-shooter. That just doesn’t seem fair, cause I REALY want to have fun with the revolver and not feel like I HAVE to give it up for a weapon that’s meant to out-class it, if that makes sense.

Now someone who has more lore knowledge and gameplay experience can more than likely provide me with an explanation as to why The Revolver is the way it is, but it just feel so bittersweet that you give this revolver so much love in its design and animation, only for the trade-off to be that it is stunted in it’s reliability and is to be treated only as a secondary last resort weapon.

Now I know the Mortis Trials are different from the normal missions, but I actually have more enjoyment using the revolvers because of this perk alone.

THAT ONE’S FREE: No ammo consumption on critical hits.

hordes_buff_no_ammo_consumption_on_crits288×288 25.3 KB

I’m sure there are many reasons why that wouldn’t work for etc. reasons, but in the veteran talents they give a passive ability for The Lasguns called the SHOCKTROOPER, which does the same exact thing. The only way I can see them incorporate this without it being too complicated is in the blessings, maybe you could make it a 75%, 50%, or at least a 30% to not consume ammo on critical hits with the blessings on max. If you can’t do that, maybe swap the penetration modifier for the fan hammer mark with an extra ammo capacity modifier, that way people can decide if they want to use the mark with better damage, penetration, reload speed, and limited ammo or a mark with a higher fire rate, better crowd control, slow reload, but with extra ammo. If you can’t do that, just give the revolver itself more ammo… Maybe like 150 for the first mark, and 100 for the second mark. But that’s just me being generous.

I’m a simple man, I don’t ask for much. You can keep the damage and everything else the same, but all I really want is to feel like a cowboy who’s got a chance at fighting back the hoards for the Imperium. And maybe with an extra bullet to spare…

My top problems with the revolver:

  • Runs out of ammo very fast at auric hi-shock
  • doesn’t reliable kill most specials one hit with headshot if you don’t have enough ranged talents
  • basically useless when farther away than 8 meters when suppressed
  • doesn’t register chest shots if the arms are in the way despite over-penetrating the target (bolt pistol does, so the system exists)

It definitely needs more ammo and without killing a special one hit to the head, it will always be bad. At least against maniac and infested it should deal slightly more headshot damage to make headshots to flamers and dogs a guaranteed kill.

Since ranged enemies are very likely to cover their chest with their hands due to them pointing a rifle at you, it NEEDS to register chest shots through arms so you don’t waste two bullets for one small shooter. It would still be considerable worse to use against ranged thrash mobs than most other weapons, but at least reliable.

Even with all this improved, it would still be considerable worse than the phosphor pistol that is a guaranteed one-hit kill to basically everything weaker than plasma gunners due to the burn effect, even when shot in the foot. Phosphor also comes with much more ammo, despite one bullet being 10x the size.
