I am not a frequent guest on the forum, so please forgive me if something is not formatted correctly. Perhaps in the future I will correct it. Perhaps…

Today I would like to express my feelings regarding the update from June 23, 2025.
I played a couple of evenings, in total for about 10 hours, and this is what I saw:

In general, the update with the rebalance was not bad. The policy of increasing the power of weak weapons is really what is needed now. There are no frankly overpowered guns in the game, but there are those that feel absolutely useless. Especially at high difficulty levels.

Regarding the reworking of weapon balance, I would continue the topic with shotguns:

• The pump-action shotgun still needs to be improved, but the alternative attacks need to be improved. They are too weak even against the background of the main attack.

  • The first modification (with a solid cartridge) needs to have its damage and penetration increased. Even if it means increasing the amount of ammunition used (make it so that half of the magazine is used for a shot, for example), but then it will become usable. Right now, there is no point in it.
  • The second modification (with an incendiary cartridge) needs to have the number of fire stacks increased.
  • The third modification (with a fan) needs to have penetration, so that clearing out a large crowd becomes really possible.

• The double-barreled shotgun is really a pretty good weapon anyway, so the minimal buffs it received are quite justified, but I would still add 10-15% damage, simply because it is not a popular melee weapon that cannot boast of either rate of fire, or range, or anything special.

• As for melee weapons, I was more or less satisfied with the balance. Yes, there are a number of not very useful guns, such as axes or a claw sword, but you can also play with them if you want.

New class - Arbiter:

It turned out interesting. Many call it imbalanced, but it’s normal that it looks like that against the background of old characters. It is not imbalanced. It is just new. For some reason, everyone forgets that the fanatic can clear out a giant crowd of enemies in a couple of seconds, the ogrin has incredible survivability and huge damage, the psyker has excellent defense abilities and pinpoint attack skills, and the veteran… More about the veteran later.

• Dogs are a great innovation. But outlining so clearly both our dog and allied dogs is unnecessary and greatly knocks out the immersion. In order to avoid the blurriness, it is enough to show the dogs’ nervous system, as it works for allies, and for your dog, when it is out of sight, add a pale green or pale yellow dot above the dog itself to understand that it is yours. This way, you can distinguish allied dogs from enemy dogs in battle and at the same time improve the visuals. (I also went around the streams on Twitch and asked people about this - not a single one spoke in favor of the current situation with the outline, and many confirmed that it needs to be changed)

• As for the referee himself, I have nothing to say yet. I haven’t played on him enough, but you probably have an idea of ​​what needs to be changed, if it is really necessary.

General impressions:

• The new quest terminal is terrible. Colorful, with strange fonts. The old terminal was much more canonical and fit into the atmosphere of the game better.

• The new system with difficulties feels bad. Artificially delaying the process of opening new difficulties is annoying. I have also collected a large number of concurring opinions. Especially for old players who have spent more than 500 hours in the game, and when creating a new character are forced to sit on easy difficulties, where you do not get enough experience to level up and the game does not challenge you. It feels like you, a thirty-year-old guy, when applying for a new job, are forced to mold sand cakes for the first month, “to make sure that you have basic skills.” Don’t do that. Players who already have at least 1 character of level 30 should be given the opportunity to choose for themselves which levels to play at, opening access to difficulties depending on the level of equipment and character level:

  • 1,2 difficulty levels will be available with weapons <300 ilvl or character level up to 10
  • 3,4 levels >300 ilvl or for characters above level 10
  • 5 level >400 ilvl or for characters above level 20
    where the main indicator for entry will be the character level, and only then the weapon.

What was missing:

• Veteran. As promised earlier - we are returning to it. Now it feels like the most useless class in the game, without any special features that could hook you. It is not unpleasant to play, but you don’t get any pleasure either. Yes, a rework of talents with buffs can help it, but what it really needs is a couple of new unique guns - a machine gun and a sniper rifle.

  • Sniper rifles are what this class should have had by default. Without them, the veteran feels completely incomplete. The universe has a large number of these weapons: Space Marine Rifle, Long Lasgun, Needle Sniper Rifle, Exitus Rifle and others. The key features of this weapon should be colossal damage, low rate of fire, average reload and the presence of a sniper scope. Since the game has the ability to change weapon models, the characteristics can change in favor of the rate of fire or in favor of damage, including changing the magnification of the sights.
  • I would also like to see light machine guns in the hands of a veteran. A large number of rounds in combination with a high rate of fire, average damage and a long reload is what this class needs.

• Arbiter skins for gold. Yes, I understand that you added 4 paid skins to earn even more money on the introduction of the Arbiter, but a free alternative, albeit not so rich in details, should always be present, otherwise it looks like disrespect for the player, and players do not like that.

So far, this is all the feedback that I was able to collect in my head this morning, so if something else appears, I will definitely let you know. I hope that at least something from this will get into work and will be done in the near future. Thank you for the game and for your attention.
