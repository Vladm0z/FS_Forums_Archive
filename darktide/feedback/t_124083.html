20 Posts • 344 Views
W
Wherbanan • 2026-07-15 05:14:51

Expiditions are an incredible change of scenery, but once your grid map is unlocked and your penances are done, they become a complete content island. Tech-Remnants are a dead currency with nothing to spend them on. We need an endgame hook.

​Proposed Solutions for the Forum:

​The “Deadside Black Market” (Weekly Vendor):

Let us hoard Tech-Remnants and spend them at a sketchy, limited-time vendor on the Mourningstar once a week. He could sell exclusive, rusted, “scavenged” weapon skins, unique character titles, or even specific tier-IV blessings to bypass the standard Melk RNG.

​The Weekly “Spoils Casket” (Extraction Caches):

Extracting with a high threshold of Tech-Remnants on Auric/Damnation difficulties should award a weekly, high-tier loot cache. Give us a guaranteed 500 base rating weapon or a massive haul of crafting materials to make the “risk of losing everything” actually feel worth the gamble.

​Temporary Mechanicus Boons:

Let a successful high-tier Expedition run grant a temporary, 3-mission character buff (like +5\% Movement Speed or +10\% Sprint Efficiency). It would create a gameplay loop where you run an Expedition to “prep” your character before diving into a brutal Havoc 40 run.

​A “Prestige Glow” or Leaderboard:

Give us some visible swag on our character models—like an active hazard/radiation glow on our gear—for hitting certain extraction milestones on Auric.

Come on Fatshark!!! MOAR BANANS!!

Effigy • 2026-07-15 21:21:43

I agree that these side-game modes weren’t needed, but they already did the heavy lifting to create them, so they might as well spend a little more time improving them.

Mortis Trials probably needs an endless mode or something along those lines. Beating a certain numbers of waves could be counted as a victory, and then you just play to see how far you can go. Some of the power-ups need a balance pass too, because you can become functionally immortal and/or have absurd damage with some combos.

Expeditions has the fundamentals to be a fun game mode. The problem is, right now there’s no payoff for collecting the tech-remnants in this mode. It unlocks new zones to play, which are mostly inconsequential, and it progresses some penances. They need to give a real incentive for collecting this stuff.

To make unlocking new zones meaningful, there needs to be some sense of progression. Later zones should be more difficult than the starter zones. To help people succeed in the harder zones, they could make power-ups (which only affect Expeditions) purchasable with tech-remnants and you keep the power-ups until you fail an Expedition. That would create the gameplay loop of doing one or more runs in starter zones, buying power-ups, and progressing to harder unlockable zones. I never played Chaos Wastes, but this sounds like a similar concept. From what I understand, CW progressed through multiple areas with the same player party, but the system I’m proposing would store progression at an individual level, so it’s more friendly to quickplay and shorter gaming sessions.
