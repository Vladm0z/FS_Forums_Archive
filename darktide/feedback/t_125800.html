I’d reduce it to the core proposal:

Darktide Endgame Overhaul: Level 40, Havoc 5–50 and Better Progression

Proposed idea

This is a player-proposed concept, not an announced feature.

I’d like to see Darktide gradually expand its endgame rather than trying to add everything at once. The exact timing could depend on the developers’ development speed.

1. Level-cap increases

Gradually increase the character level cap:

Level 30 → 35 → 40

Use the additional levels for:

  • New talent choices

  • More build diversity

  • Better class/weapon interactions

  • Additional endgame progression

2. Havoc 5–50

Expand Havoc beyond the current ceiling toward:

Havoc 5 → 50

Every rank should give meaningful progression, with larger milestone rewards at 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, 35, 40, 45 and 50.

Higher Havoc should provide:

  • Better weapons

  • More crafting materials

  • More Havoc currency

  • Better blessing/perk rewards

  • Better weekly rewards

  • Exclusive cosmetics

The normal weekly rewards should remain alongside the milestone rewards.

3. Better high-end rewards

The highest Havoc levels should eventually provide another endgame gear tier, such as Red Gear, if Fatshark decides a new tier is appropriate.

For example:

41–44: Red Gear materials
45: major Red Gear milestone
46–49: better Red Gear rewards
50: top-end reward chest

4. Class and weapon balance

Before raising the endgame ceiling, review every class, weapon, talent, blessing and perk.

Some options currently feel weaker or less useful than alternatives.

The goal should be:

  • More viable talents

  • Better blessing/perk choices

  • More viable weapons

  • More competitive builds for high Havoc and Maelstrom

  • Fewer situations where only a small number of builds are worth using

New progression should increase build variety, not just enemy health and player stats.

5. Harder Maelstrom

Higher character levels should come with harder Maelstrom missions.

Prefer:

  • More dangerous enemy combinations

  • Stronger modifier combinations

  • More elite pressure

  • New challenges

rather than simply giving enemies much more health.

6. Seasonal rotation

Use seasonal rotations for:

  • Havoc modifiers

  • Maelstrom combinations

  • Cosmetics

  • Weapon skins

  • Seasonal rewards

Older seasonal rewards should return in future rotations, so players don’t permanently miss them.

7. Cosmetic shop improvements

Improve the cosmetic shop with:

  • Larger rotations

  • Wishlist/favourites

  • Full catalogue preview

  • Returning-items section

  • More individual cosmetic purchases

  • More earnable cosmetics

8. Stability and long-session performance

As Darktide receives more content, stability should improve as well.

Focus on:

  • Fewer crashes

  • Fewer disconnects

  • Less stuttering

  • Better performance during large hordes

  • Better long-session memory/performance stability

  • Fewer technical issues causing players to lose mission progress or rewards

The game should remain reliable after many consecutive Havoc or Maelstrom runs, not just during one mission.

Overall goal

Level 30 → 35 → 40
Havoc 5 → 50
More viable classes, weapons, talents, blessings and perks
Better milestone + weekly rewards
Harder Maelstrom
Seasonal content with returning rewards
Better cosmetic shop
Better long-session stability

The main goal is to give Darktide a stronger, longer-lasting endgame without requiring every change to arrive at once.

That version is much more likely to be skimmed because the entire proposal is structured around eight clear ideas instead of dozens of individual class changes.
