You’re not going mad and you’re certainly not alone. Many, many people have complained about broken disablers countless times on the forums for as long as I have been here (over 2.5 years).

Especially the sound cue, or lack thereof. With trappers, the only warning you often get is the firing of the gun (as in the actual net firing, no warm-up warning). Same with hounds…the snarling leap sound is the only thing you hear. Both only giving a second (probably less) for reaction time.

It even happens with bursters too. Goes from silent to an instant state of high pitch, fast pinging to indicate immediate detonation and where the burster is already mid-leap.

It’s so common that I think people have given up on reporting bugs for it now.

Silent crushers magically appearing behind you is another fun one - where the only sound you hear is the swoosh noise of their fully charged and released attack. Same for the chain sound on a mauler. No heavy footsteps or groans…just the final swing about to land on your head.

In all cases, you have a split second to react. Dodge and hope you dodged in the right direction. This is a big reason as to why mobility is king in DT. Because mobility helps the most against the sea of totally broken audio.
