I like the new mode, but I wish there was more to it. An example: Right now the points of interest are very similar to each other. Sure they may have different layouts, but they all ultimately are the same thing of “island amidst a sea of sand”

For the sake of improvement, I’d like to add some ideas.

Enemy Outpost:

Command Presence: A Moebian outpost that’s heavily defended and led by a captain, or even multiple captains, and that has as much tech yield as the rest of the zone combined. It’s a question of is your group tough enough? Is it even worth the risk?

It Will Not Die!: Admonition is performing a ritual in a ruined chapel nearby, you have to stop them, lest they taint the tech-remnants with their foul heresy. Kill the sorcerers and slay the abomination before it is too late! Fail and they summon an unkillable chaos spawn that retreats after taking enough damage, but never dies. Contains a trove of tech remnants should you succeed.

New POI events:

Splashdown: A valkyrie crashes down in the middle of the mission, retrieve a datapad with sensitive information on it before the enemy can. Failure to do so will increase enemy presence and prolong extraction.

Treasure Hunt: You find a piece of a map in a datacrypt, find the two remaining pieces at POI’s to reveal the datavault on the third map segment.

A Favor For Swagger: Swagger has his own objective in deadside, complete a small quest for him in lieu of using that time to gather tech remnants and he’ll let you buy something for free from the sanctuary.

These are just a few ideas I came up with, and I’m sure others may have ideas of their own. I’d be happy to hear them.

Commentor Concepts:

No_Ear932 - Pack It In!: Map segments that require the party to delve into mini-dungeons in closer quarters, more similar to the core gameplay of tight spaces and intense engagements. The result being a tense battle but for greater payoff.
