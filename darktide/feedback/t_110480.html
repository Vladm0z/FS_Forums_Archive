To clarify a lot of these thoughts are just a drop in the bucket to greater balance/design issues. Please at least read the whole thing. I will provide a tl;dr that will give you zero context but annotate most of the writing to some basic thoughts:

  • We are inundated with ‘free’ Attack Speed and Damage buffs that require very little input.
  • Difficulty balance around “Elite spam” is encouraging this and making it worse as balance changes keep compounding this game design approach.
  • A lot of these Talents and Blessings should be changed to require only slightly more effort to earn these buffs, with generous durations. See: Ogryn needing to attack a little bit to get +10% Attack Speed instead of just having it be a permanent passive buff.

Something I’ve been mulling over since the release of Ogryn’s rework is that there’s a lot of Attack Speed in this game that’s completely unconditional, necessary to have, and functionally ‘not fun’ for the game.

I saw quite a few people upset that Ogryn still didn’t gain an unconditional +10% Attack Speed “because everyone else has it.” At the time I was kind of annoyed by that, too, because it had become a subconscious standard of the game to have it. A few weapons don’t feel good without it across every Operative.

Fast forward to now where we have Arbites, and with the right build they can have a nearly +50% Attack Speed build:

  • Hammer of Judgement
  • Lone Wolf (Keystone)
  • Inspiring Recitation (Nuncio-Aquila Modifier)
  • Targets Acquired (Forceful Keystone Modifier)
  • Counterattack (Maul & Shield Blessing)

At least 40% of that Attack Speed has a significant uptime with no real condition to activate it other than attacking or spawning Nuncio-Aquila. One requires dogless. An additional 10% Attack Speed requires Perfect Blocking (I consider this to be conditional, because it requires you to do something other than attack an enemy which at that point is just free unconditional buffing).

Is it a good build or focus? I don’t know, probably not really. I imagine there’s quite a bit of diminishing returns in how much you gain out of just swinging faster. But they’re all essentially ‘free’ with class features or positions in the talent tree that are already really strong. It would be very difficult for your build to suffer picking these things at all.

Do I consider Perfect Block Blessings to be good? Not really. The duration for their buffs are insanely short and it’s not ideal to be constantly blocking in fights in this game. We’re at a point now with spawn rates where if you aren’t maintaining momentum with attack spam, you can get overwhelmed instantly. This is an entirely different issue that should be addressed at some point, but not something I want to talk about in this thread.

I also do not care about Havoc balance in my thoughts. Havoc is an entirely separate thing and should be treated independently from broader game balance/design, but should be balanced in accordance with itself as well. Havoc can still be unfun and has a lot of its own issues, but that would have been like saying Vermintide 2 should have been balanced around Weaves.

What do I consider “Unconditional” Damage/Attack Speed?

Essentially, any Blessing or Talent that allows you to have either of these things just by attacking or just by having it unlocked in your talent tree makes it what I consider to be unconditional. These buffs require no input from you other than to keep left-clicking, which you are already doing 24/7. For example:

  • +10% Attack Speed Talents are unconditional.
  • +10% Attack Speed when hitting 3 or more Enemies is unconditional.
  • +25% Damage when Staggered is unconditional.
  • +60% Rending on Critical Hit is unconditional.
  • +14 Bleed Stacks from Special Attacks is, instead, conditional.
  • +25% Damage when Electrocuted is, actually to me, conditional.
  • 3x stacks of +20% Strength when charging Heavy Attacks is also conditional.
  • Obstinate (Terminus Warrant Keystone Modifier) is very much conditional.

That’s establishing my baseline. In the case of Bloodletter and Thrust, these are Blessings that require you to do something other than left-click as fast as possible to get anything out of these Blessings.

Ogryn can have a potential +20% Attack Speed but it requires an effective uptime to reach that point. Does it take very long? Not really. Arguably this is unconditional because all you have to do is left-click. But at least there’s extra steps involved in earning it. They still only have to left-click, but it’s not an instantaneous reward, either. I consider this “conditional” and I think this is perfectly acceptable and preferred. I’m not asking for us to do 5 different mechanics just to earn +10% Attack Speed for 5 seconds.

“When hitting x or more” is pretty unconditional because that’s the nature of the game. There’s very rarely a situation where you aren’t getting cleave Blessings like this automatically. And if you aren’t, you probably don’t need those buffs. Probably.

These Blessings/Talents however are generally nice because it gives you a more horde-conditioned outset with little/no uptime against single-target. I simply don’t like the idea of it being used to gain Attack Speed. If anything it could be “gain 1 stack of +2.5% Attack Speed every time you hit 3 or more enemies with a single attack. Stacks 4 times.”

In the end I don’t think “When hitting x or more” needs to be changed or removed because they tend to have a comfortably varied amount of uptime. This is where my conception of “Unconditional” gets hazy.

Won’t this make a lot of things bad or unfun?

Yeah, definitely. I think Revolvers getting Rending on Critical Hits is a fun and dynamic thing, but it’s literally just free damage. Why would I ever pick anything else?

They could make it inherent to a much smaller degree by increasing the base armor damage multipliers of the Revolver so it’s always pretty decent at being anti-armor…

Or, they just make it a bit more conditional. This is a hypothetical change and I don’t think it would be a good one, to clarify, but something like, “Gain 1 Stacks of [Rending Blessing] every time you Critically Strike with this weapon. After 3 Stacks, you gain +60% Rending on Critical Hit for 10 seconds.”

10 seconds is a decent amount of time. Given the rate at which we currently clear rooms, that should last you most of the fight.

In the broader scope of things, there is a myriad of game balance issues that has pushed Darktide to a very unfun point where, as I have mentioned about difficulty design, “Elite spam” is the hallmark of what is making the game difficult anymore, and it’s genuinely not fun to me. This requires us to fulfill builds that focus entirely on as much damage output as possible and near-permanent buff uptime with as little conditional mechanisms as possible. Anything that can’t do either of these things are mediocre or not ideal as a result.

I think occasionally encountering entire rooms full of Gunners and getting lit up like it’s the 4th of July is awesome. It’s cinematic. It’s a room-specific challenge.

It’s not fun when this is almost every room or every other room.
