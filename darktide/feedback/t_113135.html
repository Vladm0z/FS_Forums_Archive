Most Havoc modifiers just make enemies tougher or more stagger resist. Stimms, rotten armor, rampaging, garden, pus-hardened etc.

It’s really just poor game design. Part of the fun of the game is getting the visual and auditory feedback from kills. No seriously I’d rather just that enemies keep spawning than having every 3rd elite be a bossfight in itself. Wailing non-stop with artillery and dark age of technology swords and cannons on the same 3 crushers or ragers for the better part of a minute isn’t fun for any of a very broad range of interpretations of that word.

Brainbursting the same stupid gunner four times without it resulting in a kill is ridiculous. Knowing there’s 8 more of them to go through even if you get it on the 5th slow-ass attack makes it feel like working in an Amazon warehouse in China. You’re not quite sure if you just want the day or your life to be over asap. There’s another work day tomorrow.

Stimms are objectively crap even all by themselves. Their interactions with other modifiers break the game completely.

Just stop with the whole more health more armor more stagger resist nonsense. Do something else for a change. The exploding head tentacle mutation was interesting as it was at least different. Adding another visual effect for the same basic “enemy is tougher” mechanic isn’t.
