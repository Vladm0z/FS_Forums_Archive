Hi Fatshark,

#1- Y’all cooked. I have been addicted and having a blast since I got back from europe, the class is really fun, extremely viable at all levels of play, the talent tree is a beautifully excruciating nightmare of “if only I had 3 more points”, and the personalities/voices and voice modulator combo was genius. Well. Karking. Done.#2 - Have some suggestions for consideration.

Talents - Blitzes

The abilities and keystones all feel like worthwhile options, it’s just the blitzes I have feedback on. Servo skull is the clear meta, some thoughts. (balance) is a placeholder for “use whatever makes sense, but please look at this”

1- Arc Grenades - These are actually quite strong, boombringer strong. They just take forever and a day to execute which feels awful and have additional targeting challenges (arcing, bouncing grenade). #1) Reduce throw/explode time by 75% (balance), #2) Consider adding extra grenade charge (balance).

2- Refraction Emitter - This is simply not worth a grenade charge. Also, as a defensive blitz, it’s extremely underwhelming - you could have 8-12s of what you could get out of … sliding. Why not turn this into a real survivability/tank blitz and tie it to the class mechanic? e.g. #1) No longer costs grenade charges, costs 50% capacitance (balance) with 45s cooldown (balance), and #2) Kinetic Repulsion - is now a passive similar to Arbites ‘True Grit’, as long as you have the node nothing can do >50 damage in a swing to you. This gives the class a very tanky option buried behind a situational defensive blitz, a worthy consideration for 2 points.

3- Servo Skull - Untalented is great, talented is great, med skull is great, noosphere is maybe a touch too good, purgator is bad. #1) Consider (balance) increasing cost (25%->33%) on Noosphere or adding a 3-5s (balance) cooldown, and #2) Make purgator differential / interesting by shortening the duration by 50-75% (balance - but key point, make it much shorter!), doubling or tripling the burn stacks (balance), and increasing the spin speed 50-100% (balance). Make it a high output, short duration option instead of a worse zealot flame grenade. Given the visual clutter it adds to the battlefield, I’d argue the shorter duration would be beneficial on several dimensions, just offset it with increased output.

Weapons

1- Transonics - Y’all cooked. I LOVE these things. My only feedback is that Deadly Frequencies is SUCH a cool concept because it gives the player a lot of control and lets skill expression shine, but in limited testing I get higher uptime on superiority in Auric/Havoc AND you can argue both are worse than just focusing on crit/finesse. Maybe up Deadly Frequencies to 50% (balance) but at the end of the day I love these things and have many worthwhile blessing options.

2- Power Sword - I gather it’s bugged on powered damage, and I gather it’s intended to be single-target focused. The weapon special as a new attack button threw me - interesting concept, but the attack chains suck so much for anything but single target I had no interest in learning it beyond the basic push-attack powered-attack for hordes (if we think about some of the Vet power sword attack chains, I’d actually be really interested in learning!). People can make it work (Reginald somehow posted 1.7m with the thing) but overall it just feels clunky, confusing, not fun, my time is better spent elsewhere. No real suggestions just a little exasperation.

3- Galvanic Rifle - It’s mid. Maybe that’s fine. It just feels not worth considering in high difficulties. Look & feel & sound are all great, but low max ammo + smallish clip hurts and it doesn’t feel like the damage output justifies them.

Maul, Phosphor Pistol, Transonics, and Arc Rifle are all really strong - tons of fun.
