The two Zealot gernades outside of Throwing Knife are pretty underwhelming in this day and age and need a slight buff.

Stun nade is basically a really terrible version of Arby shock mine. Buff it by adding one more, lower the throw time, and remove the fuse timer completely. This turns it into a quick flashbang-esque get-out-of-jail card.

The buffs to enemy HP over the years has really made the fire nade feel weak. The “burn” it applies doesn’t follow regular burn logic but instead randomly deals between 43.75 and 131.25 base damage per tick. Increase the damage and narrow the range to make it more consistent, add a slow to enemies within it, and don’t make enemies path around it so it’s actually usable outside of funnels. Also maybe have the initial explosion apply some stacks of actual burn. Also change the color to a transparent blue like IRL flames on a stove.
