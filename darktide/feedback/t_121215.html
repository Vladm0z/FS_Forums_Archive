The variety of blessings available to weapons will, imho, ever really be considered so long as things like Slaughterer, Headtaker, Blaze Away, Deathspitter, Superiority, Unstable Power etc., exist. It just seems to be one more large factor in how bad the overall balancing act is and warrants their removal. Strength blessings should still be around, but exist with much more stringent & gameplay altering requirements for the payoff. It’s not interesting or good balance when hitting, shooting and killing enemies can scale your character’s power the way some of these blessings do. Things like Thrust, Limbsplitter, Offensive Defence, Tenderizer, Full-Bore, Opening Salvo, and Pinpointing Target strike me as the much superior way of adding strength through blessings, as they require you to do something different than maintain a regular cadence of hitting, shooting or killing enemies. Just curious what other people think, as it strikes me as a relatively easy way to reduce player power before touching character trees, stats, and weapon stats, and introduce variety in some aspect of the game.

Effigy • 2026-04-30 23:47:04

Manpower is always an issue, but it doesn’t take that much effort to tweak some numbers here and there. One dev and one QAer could be working on balance adjustments at least part of the time. You don’t need artists, level designers, writers, etc. on this project, so I think it’s a cop out to say they can’t fit game balance on their plate.

The big thing is deciding what the “gold standard” of balance should be in the game and make a conscious effort to apply that standard any time they work on new or existing talents/weapons. Things that are clearly above or below standard should be incrementally adjusted to move them toward the standard. This should be an iterative process so that things don’t get dumpstered or massively buffed to OP status in any given patch. Mini-patches with balance adjustments should be coming out at least every other month. Since each change would be relatively small, they would be less risk of a massive backlash from players, especially if these patches became a regular occurrence. Include both buffs and nerfs in the same patch as well to make it more palatable.

I’m not confident that Fatshark would actually do this, but I think it’s pretty clear that this is needed for the health of the game. Putting off game balance is like putting off cleaning the dishes. The problem will get worse and worse the longer the solution is delayed.
