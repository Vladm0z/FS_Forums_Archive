Player revival system — like a form of elaborate cruelty

In some other game, not Darktide, the boss would spawn after the two players still standing made it to the two dead players at the respawn point. But not in Darktide, where the boss will be lying in ambush right in front of the respawn point

In some other game, not Darktide, the two players who fought their way through hell and the revived players would get a moment to catch their breath and a chance to refill health, ammo, grenades, stims, crates — but not in Darktide, where all four of them will immediately end up in an endless zone of toxic fire damage, from which only two will break out again: one of the players who was standing last time, and one of the players who was revived. This time, the two of them won’t make it, because why? That’s right! Because the boss is waiting for them!

So in practical terms, the game was over when two players died. That was it. After that, it was just elaborate cruelty, with false hope and its merciless, villainous, laughter-filled crushing

Anyway, one game like that was enough for me tonight. I’ve had my fill. I do not want any more. (I was the one who got rescued, only to be killed again a dozen meters from the spot where I had been rescued)

It would honestly be fairer this way: death is permanent. You die? Nice work — catapult back to the Mourningstar (with no bot for the team, of course). Sure, far more runs would fail, but there would not be this kind of elaborate torment
