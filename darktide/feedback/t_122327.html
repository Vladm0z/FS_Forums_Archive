There is little involvement in these points providing some semblance for discussion, so I will argue with some takes of my own:

Aradiel:
  • Trappers can not shoot trough the their own units

That’s not going to happen - it would make trappers largely harmless and dodgeable by backtracking constantly.

Aradiel:
  • Poxburster has less HP. This guy is literally made of bombs; he should explode in one hit. On Havoc, I couldn’t even one-shot him with the Brain Burst.

Good. They are supposed to be a threat if defused through means other than pushing it away/a commitment to dispose at range.

Aradiel:
  • Horde do not get a speed boost to reach players faster. It’s literally a cheat.

It’s not. One of the core ideas with a horde game is that you cannot/should not be able to ignore the mass of enemies the game sents at you. All units have defined walk and run speeds for this purpose - they already struggle with catching up to players by passing through teleporting doors; there’s no need to debilitate them more than they already are by the latter.

Aradiel:
  • Dreg Tox Bomber and Scab Bomber has a shorter throwing rang e

The entire identity of either of those is staying behind the lines and throwing grenades for crowd control; and they both have two sets of animations - long and short range - to accomodate for that. They should be threat to you positioning-wise, and having good sightlines for taking them out is a part of why they are engaging in the first place.

Aradiel:
  • Scab Bomber is not immune to his own fire.

It would screw up his routing way too much and he’d spend less time engaging the players, and be less of a threat. I wouldn’t like that.

Aradiel:
  • AI cannot spawn more than 2 bosses at a tim e.

And why would that be, especially considering Havoc has a system where it replaces some special enemies with small monsters, and has additional boss triggers? What would happen with boss triggers that are determined at the beginning of the map, if you cross them while 2 bosses are active?

Aradiel:
  • Breaking the captain’s shield will always interrupt any of his attac ks.

But it already does, except when he’s having the Final Toll buff in Havoc, and that’s the only exception, and a reason why you can’t just ignore his animations while they’re at half health.

Aradiel:
  • Reapercannot damage you when you are behind him .

There’s desync and it’s not like it will always be perfect.

Aradiel:
  • Reduce amount of Maulers

Why?

Aradiel:
  • AI ​​does not spawn bosses in certain small areas where players have no way back and little room to mane uver.

Why? Half the idea with Havoc’s monstrous specialists is that you will have a big disruptor thrown your way to make certain map segments less predictable in terms of outcomes for the player. Why would the tension stop ticking if you’re struggling? If you’re going to lose, you’re going to lose; and if you want to salvage, you really need to pull your weight.

Aradiel:
  • AI has a cooldown to spawn the Specialists (hello, 3-4 poxbursters running after each othe r)

This is called a surge and is intentionally designed, to flood the players with an unhinged wave of specialists throughout set amount of time, several times throughout the map.

Aradiel:
  • Remove “Power Supply Interruption”, “Hi-intensity Pox Gas Circumstance”, “Hunting Grounds (Power Supply Interruption)” from Havoc rotation

Environmental mods probably won’t ever be removed since it’s a content that was intentionally made, but, dogs are largely irrelevant for a mod, so I wouldn’t mind it being gone at all.

I prefer Trappers the way they behave now, where they run directly towards you, and always shoot once they get within the same range. It makes them easy to predict, and you can tell when you’ll need to spend a dodge.
