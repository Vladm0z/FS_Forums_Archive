In the recent announcement on Skitarii visuals, the Phosphor Blast Pistol is shown as a pepperbox revolver.

A pepperbox full of phosphor… hee hee.

image1804×2048 192 KB

I have a suggestion. Instead of regular bottlenecked autogun bullets, load them with these. These… are what real world blanks look like. They are crimped in the front like a shotgun shell, because they don’t contain a normal bullet, and they need to hold in the powder. They open when firing to ‘let the gases out.’

They are also used to run heavy equipment on occasion, such as the Russian version of the ‘jaws of life’ which is loaded with these and firing them closes the jaws. (Doesn’t require a hose to a machine but has less options. )

image1920×2560 1000 KB

image1920×2560 941 KB

This would effectively contain (and isolate from the air) any hyper sensitive compounds such as phosphorus, lithium, etc… and it would have a unique look that would stand out with minimal changes to the existing design, while also showing the phosphor pistol as loaded with something ‘weirder.’
