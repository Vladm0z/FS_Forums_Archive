Indeed it’s not perfect, siblings.
But honestly there are many things coming into place very nicely over time, DT slowly becoming the most engaging pve coop shooter experience right now.

There’s only one thing that is imo missing from the whole picture : once you have 500 hours in to the game, having cleared all levels, all difficulty levels, all classes, what is left to keep you engaged in playing ?
There is those good old talent trees infinite minmaxing yes, which feels very satisfying right now. But what else ?

The loot system. Arsenal progression. There is a boulevard here for so many ideas. Current loot progression system is not bad, but is tailored for leveling, not for endgame.

What if there was a new level quality, let’s say red color like in Vermintide, that is slightly above orange, only obtainable from missions, got some unique blessings, and has a durability score ?
For example you loot a red weapon after having completed some damnation, and becomes your new toy for XX games before wearing off. Maybe even letting us repair it with plasteel or diamantine.

edit for clarification : no new weapon, just a new quality tier with unique blessings.

The XX variable would be tweakable by FS gamedesigners to entice playing harder missions without turning the system into frustrating low possession times.

Anyway that’s just a suggestion, the bottom line is that we need an endgame loot progression system imo.
