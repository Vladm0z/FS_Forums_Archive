Some of the actives feels odd to use, and others feel not so good.

  1. Servoskulls
    Skulls are nice, but upgraded and with the tagging burst, it puts a strain on the fingers to keep tapping t to make it shoot. It also doesn’t seem to shoot much even with the upgrade if you don’t tag something. the Mediskull and Flameskull taking the same grenade charge also feels weird.

it also feels weird that its not flying about shooting things, like a version of the dog that doesnt incapacitate.

proposed solution;
Make las-skull go berserk for a longer amount of time on one tag(considering it eats a whopping 25% charge already), shooting first the tagged target then shoots anything else it can find for the longer duration.

Make either the Mediskull or Flameskull go on cooldown with charges, and the other can use grenade ammo. or at least separate uses of mediskull and flameskulls from each other so the limited grenade ammo doesn’t feel super restrictive.

Maybe make them actually fly about the place shooting things? Addendum; also a flashlight maybe. Could be the perks of having a servoskull that we dont need weapons for our flashlight.

  1. Personal shield.
    Not uh… particularly attractive?

With highly limited grenade ammo, this one isn’t really a high pick for me without any way to regen it, something to make it more interesting or even just have a longer duration. This is a skill that is mitigated the moment you walk behind a box to take cover, have a shield psyker, or even a smoke vet. Hivescum have the equivalent of this on a cooldown, with a higher duration and not on a grenade use too.

Its kinda hard to use aggressively, and defensively i have other options that doesn’t involve going in a place where everyone can shoot me.

not sure what to propose for fixes on this one, other than longer duration/regen/more charges.

3.arc grenades

so so I guess? Its usable.

It only shoots out limited arcs, so it only hits a limited amount of enemies yes? Not that compelling, but I guess if you need a cluster of heavy enemies removed this would be okay to use.
