I recently made a post regarding Desperado and Rampage incentivizing gameplay that’s overall too similar. In that post, I floated the idea of combining the two abilities and replacing the Left Side Tree ability with the Boom Bringer.

I’ve been thinking a lot about this idea since making that post, and decided to make a mock-up visual w/ ability explanations. Any and all feedback is appreciated, thank you! (Note: all numbers are up for tuning, mostly wanted to get across the general idea of how they’d feel)

And third, even though I didn’t bring up this ability in the previous post, I felt like it needed some touch ups to really help sell it conceptually: Stimm Supply

stimm4016×2480 294 KB

IMPORTANT NOTE: This Stimm Supply rework came alongside some mechanics changes, which are:

- No longer consumes Stimm upon use, max Stimm buffs receivable by the player are now capped (Specific % caps can be tuned by devs).

- Stimm buffs received from Stimm Supply linger for 5s by default.

Secondary Note: The Top and Bottom branches are locked off from each other upon selecting the first node on either side.

Lastly, of course, we’d need a new blitz! I think it would be easiest to just move over the Chem Knife special from the shivs over into the new blits slot. Plus, this would make the scum’s blitz have 3 unique ways of applying toxin: Small aoe + lower stacks (Blackout + Pocket Toxin), Single target + medium stacks (Chem Knife), and large area denial + high stacks (Chem Grenade). I think this feels very thematic!

Thank you for reading! Please let me know what you think!
