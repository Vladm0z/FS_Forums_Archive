MaxxJ • 2026-07-19 14:03:46

(tl;dr make maelstroms more of a deliberate choice to improve player experience)

Maelstroms in general are their own difficulty. I think they should get a mission assignment area sort of like the other two game modes.

Doing this would accomplish a few things.

  • Open the possibility to having multiple maelstrom modifier sets active at the same time.
  • Create an a soft barrier to this difficulty, to prevent people from joining it by mistake. If you’re going out of your way to join one of these, you know what you’re getting into and are probably actually ready for that challenge.
  • Decrease the amount of ragequitting due to two reasons; (1) realizing they’re not ready for that challenge and backing out (2) realizing the team is doomed because one or more of them wasn’t ready and giving up on them.
  • Increase the possibility of more content, like making these missions into a “we’re going back here again to deal with this situation that popped up” with more voice lines and possibilities for expanding lore.

In order to gain access to the heresy and auric maelstrom mission terminals, a person should have successfully unlocked the next difficulty on the standard board. Meaning, unlock damnation difficulty in the standard mission terminal to gain access to the heresy maelstrom missions, and put a mission completed counter on auric just like on lower difficulties so that when a person completes 5 auric missions on standard, they get access to auric maelstrom.
