New mission type (base game / expeditions): Data Collection

This mission’s idea came to me when I was thinking about possibilities of how to improve Expeditions’ experience. But it can actually be used in base game as yet another mission, just with randomly generated map.

  1. You need to go there and extract with 5-7 data pieces (amount may depend on difficulty level)
  2. Mission map is separated into several Areas, with safe zones placed between them (and at the start)
  3. Each area consists of three Paths one of which you must choose; each Path is an auto-generated map, but unlike the current Expeditions’ maps this is a classic DT linear map instead
  4. Before advancing into each area you are presented with a big screen on the wall giving you vague hints on what kind of challenge awaits you there; it may tell you that Path A will have extra armored troops, Path B will just have more elites in general, and Path C will have fewer elites, but much more trash shooters and specialists; it also may tell you that on Path B there will be no lights, and on Path A there will be Pox Gas environment condition
  5. It will also display how much Data Points you can collect from the facility on that Path; it’s a random number between 1 and 3 - and it’s totally unrelated to the challenge level there (completely randomly chosen); sometimes you may see an easy challenge with 3 Data Points locked behind it, sometimes the opposite; it’s up to you to decide what looks more like the best risk/reward option in there
  6. You choose the corresponding door and proceed to the Path you chose. You fight through the hoard as normal, reach the facility, solve the minigame, get the Points and go to the door leading to the next safe zone, rinse-repeat. You can extract at the next safe zone after you’ve collected the required amount of Data Points
  7. Additional incentive to be time-efficient could be placed by each subsequent zone past the 2nd one having enemy hoards’ intensity increased, ramping up exponentially with each new zone

So, it’s kind of “lowkey expeditions”, sort of - but without all the grind and need to carry some barrels, just a classical DT mayhem - still utilizing benefits of map generation and random modifiers you can’t predict, to spice it up.

EDIT:
I don’t see it as substitution for the current Expeditions’ missions. I don’t really hate them, they are okeish, and their biggest issue is low incentive for all that extra grind you do there. But I still think that kind of mode (which is designed to be replayable) could benefit from several different missions’ designs that could be mixed together on one map.
