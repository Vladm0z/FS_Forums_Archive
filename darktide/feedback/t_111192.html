CobaltNinja • 2025-07-22 02:26:18

Trauma gains stacks once per attack. Hammerblow gains stacks once per hit. Neither blessing loses stacks on miss, so Hammerblow is just indisputably better if you’re only taking one impact blessing.

For DC swords, take neither impact blessing. They’re more of direct damage weapons, and stagger bonuses would be better served on weapons like the shovels or shock mauls.
