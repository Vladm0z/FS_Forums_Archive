At short range regular gunners will require two shots as well - unless bleeding procs then one usually is enough (for plasma it’s usually isn’t though).

The gun also has a pretty irritating delay at the end of its reload sequence where it’s already seems like it’s almost cocked - but in fact it isn’t. So if you sprint at that moment, after stopping again to shoot you find out you can’t and need to wait a bit (sometimes that “a bit” turns fatal)

I think it needs its damage output somewhat increased, and reload sequence either sped up notably - or it should be allowed to reload it while sprinting natively (without spending any more bless slots, this gun needs all the help it can get).
