Simple as that. Sometimes it could be a life saver, especially on higher difficulties. Will work like this:

  1. You press 6 button on your keyboard (by default)
  2. Your character is shown holding an ammo satchel in their hands
  3. Hints are appearing on your screen, telling you to either press LMB or RMB to share your ammo:
    • LMB: you will throw/pass a small pouch of ammo holding 50 standard ammo units to the intended recipient you aim at (same way as you can pass other objects during a mission)
    • RMB: you’ll drop 200 standard units of ammo on the ground in a form of ammo crate anybody can take it from by 50 units per draw attempt
  4. In both cases, you lose corresponding amounts of ammo yourself (you should have at least that amount to begin with, for it to work ofc)

P.S.
I also thought it will be useful, while you are in this mode, if game would show current amount of ammo each of your teammates have above their heads (just some gauge) - so it would be easier to see who you need to give it to using LMB
