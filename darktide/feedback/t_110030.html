Now before anyone gets upset, I am going to point out that I am aware that likelyhood of getting an actual Beastman Archetype to use in-game is slim. But, until GW and Fatshark give us a definitive no, there is still a possibility of it happening. There are official imperial beastmen minis that exist, so GW could be talked into allowing it in the name of advertising.

As for how to justify them in the current story, well by now many of us have probably seen the new intro scene for the freshly released arbites class, during which Grendyl chews out Iven for failing to secure Atoma. Where the Beastmen could enter this story is as an attempt for Iven to bolster the efforts to reclaim Tertium, since the warband is suffering high attrition rates, therefor, desperate times call for desperate measures. Even using those you would rather shun.

Now the first question to address is what kind of unique weapons should this class get along side the general ones? Since Beastmen have a natural affinity for close quarters combat, it would seem fitting to me to grant them polearms and dual weapons, as currently no other class has such weapons with the exception of the Ogryn’s pickaxe.

As for unique abilities, I only have two that could readily implemented. One is basically the Darktide version of VT2 Bardin’s slayer. The other is a sort of berserker ability that forces you to put away gun, but grants higher damage, speed, stamina reduction loss, and damage resistance temporarily. It would of course come at a cost to balance it out, a debuff that results in slower movement and attack speed for a few seconds, as well as some small damage over time.

Last thing to address are what this character’s blitzes would be. First one I have are caltrops. Small, sharp caltrops that you can throw down just like the arbites shock mine, but instead zapping the target, it would cause them to stumble and inflict a temporary bleed effect. Second I have to suggest are throwing axes. We already have knives, so why stop there? Last idea I have is electro-bolas or net, similar to what the scab trapper uses. What would be the difference? Gameplay balancing, so maybe only 1 bolas or net max, and the enemy could eventually fight their way free of it (unlike our sorry rejects).

That’s all I got for now on this one. If you think you have some suggestions to improve on this idea, or fill any holes that you see here, please go right ahead.
