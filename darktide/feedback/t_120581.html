Since they have been introduced, both Mortis Trials and Expeditions have had concerns raised about their long-term viability, since, while they were fun at first, have quickly become very grindy game modes that the rewards are not worth the amount of effort put in versus the standard missions. What these game modes need to fix that is better rewards and more content for variety, but that sort of thing takes a good bit of time to implement, so what we need is something to tide us over in the short-term while the Devs get said content.

What do I propose? A new set of tasks, set by Hallowette on a bi-weekly or monthly basis that awards Aquilas for completion of a certain number of Mortis Trials and Expeditions in that time period.

Now before anyone starts griping, I know people have been asking for limited time special conditions events to give Aquilas for awhile, so what makes this request any different? One very simple thing, and that is you would only get the absolute MINIMUM amount of Aquilas from completing said bounties, so no more than five to fifteen total. I know that we would want it to be five hundred, but as Aquilas are purchased with real world currency, AND GW and Tencent have vested interests in this game, and they are not likely to allow major handouts of Aquilas outside major events, I.E. certain holidays, anniversary, etc.

zircuits • 2026-04-02 14:55:07

If you want to bring people back into those modes, you just make new content for them or expand them into an interesting way. Mortis Trials is like a proto-horde mode you’d see in gears of war or killing floor without any of the things that makes gears of war horde mode or killing floor interesting and fun. Expeditions is a basic helldivers formula with none of the character nor interesting enemies that makes that game fun.

Basically, complete overhaul of their designs with a more attentive design that tide games can allow. There’s a lot of maps in vermintide 2 that have hold out spots, and they each add some sort of flavor to them that make them feel cool to complete. Fortunes of war and righteous stand both end with a giant explosion that clears the map, buying you time to run to the portal and leave. The Pit has a wall that breaks down that, if you don’t start running through it, enemies start to pour out from, and can trap your team. Chaos wastes, even as a rather boring mode that gets very easy very fast, allows you to take on challenges to get more perks and resources that you can spend to upgrade your gear, and the final missions for each campaign is usually a rather big arena meant to push this stuff to its limits.

Why doesn’t Mortis Trials have special and unique challenges either in-between rounds or during with objectives where completing them allows you to gain more powerful and unique perks? The mode is practically begging for this kind of content, where the team can be absolutely crushing every single wave, why not just spawn a totem or something that the team breaks, spawning in several boss monsters and specials? Maybe it puts on a permanent modifier, spawning extra enemies or additional waves in each round. All in return for more disgustingly broken talents for your character.

Why does expeditions, a mode where radiation is crucial as a mechanic, not have a degradation system? Your gear could be made weaker as you stay for way too long in the area, and you could buy out ways to patch your gear using your points, or swap your gear out for custom gear from the locals. Change objectives so it’s less about avoiding enemy patrols, and moreso about assaulting enemy positions to make your final objectives in an area much easier to complete and access. Set up bases with mortar teams that constantly bombard you, power stations where if you choose to assault the objective first you now have to deal with constant turrets popping up around you trying to melt you, bases that produce specific types of specialists or monstrosities that go out to hunt you. Add in features where you can upgrade your navigation gear to show more points of interest or active patrols and their patrol routes, maybe going so far as to show cone of vision range. Make it a proper minimap and not a garbage compass. Make this unique to each player, so that the team as a whole can communicate and strategize on who should be a navigator, who’s going to be buying airstrikes, who’s going to be getting the biggest sticks to swing at enemies. Maybe even give patrols special items from time to time to make clearing them unique ventures that the team has to decide to invest time in doing.

You release a game mode that is a barebones version of some other game’s mode, remove the QoL features of said mode, and further reduce the amount of replayability by not even having a quarter the amount of content of those modes, and you wonder why people are uninterested in completing it beyond achievements.

It would probably, legitimately, take real world money to bring in enough players to these two game modes the way they are to make people want to play them for an extended period of time.
