Right, gave the game a run on SG Psyker. Here’s my thoughts.

  • The new trees look good. Very few dead nodes and a lot of freedom. Some talent placements don’t make sense (why do Zealots have their elite damage bonus locked behind a revive speed bonus, for example?) but overall massive improvement.
    • Veteran tree still feels messy and there were quite a few nodes I took that I didn’t really want to but they were blocking a more important node. Should give it another look.
  • Scrier’s Gaze now really easily has 100% uptime with Psykinetic’s Aura and is a frankly insane active ability. Only somewhat balanced out by the fact that it offers no stagger bonuses whatsoever and you have to rely on system mechanics to defend yourself. Needs a nerf.
  • Crushers feel really tanky now. I’m getting cornered by hordes of armor that I can no longer deal with solo. Not sure if I like it or not.
  • The first game I played I got 3 disconnects within 20 minutes:
    • Error 2019 early on: Appears to have affected everyone and was very sudden, like the server sh!t the bed. Two other players had disconnected and did not reconnect by the time I rejoined.
    • Error 2014: Lagged out.
    • Error 3013: Happened when attempting to reconnect.
      • In all cases it did not take very long for me to reconnect to the server thanks to auto-reconnect mods. Console log attached regardless if anyone wants to take a look.
      • Overall would say that connection to servers seems to be more stable than the last time I played… for now.
  • New stamina/dodge UI is good and essentially an official incorporation of the “show dodge count” mod into the game. Now go bug the VT2 devs to add it there!
  • Framerate is still poor even with DLSS on Performance mode. Please try to optimize the game.
  • Scab Plasma Gunners didn’t seem to do a lot and died pretty quickly. One hit me at full blue toughness and did no health damage. Friend of mine said they were annoying, though, so IDK.
  • Ain’t touched Mediocre Torture. Don’t plan on it.
