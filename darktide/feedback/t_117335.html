KJeraDRegular • 2025-12-30 11:26:34
BizarreSalp:

As a buff to Smokes and to give them a purpose outside of ranged enemies

They already do that - Poxhounds cannot pounce you in the smoke. Trappers and Flamers, depending on whetver you view them as ranged, also need to approach you more closely to perform their attacks.

Smokes don’t need a buff - they are basically an infinite resource with good enough uptime. They are a non-killing grenade with great, situational utility. Problem is, they don’t exactly outweight what other builds offer, but buffing them is not what should be done to make them more attractive.

Veteran in general has a crisis of sorts with its ranged playstyle - it’s non-existent, or at least not dominating, since Veteran has so many good things for melee that building “strictly” with ranged in mind is not the most effective way of playing.

Vror:

Demolition Stockpile needs to work like Arbites Lone Wolf talent.

It could - I wouldn’t mind that.

Vror:

Grenadier just needs to do something more for Smokes

I don’t think it does. Having a longer duration on the thing is an extended localized safety from dogs and a generally favourable moved margin of safety from trappers and flamers, on top of shutting up gunner/shooter squads to an extent. Advancing and moving position is also more consistent with longer duration.

If anything, I would move some of the power away from Grenade Tinkerer for Smokes and give its basic effect a longer duration, say from 15s to 20s, and adjust Tinkerer to instead provide 50% increased duration. That way Smoke users could allocate the point elsewhere, or enjoy a longer safety bubble if they want more supportive qualities from their blitz still.

Vror:

Demolition Team

I’d like to preface that I don’t cherish the skill being chance-based in general. It’s out of line with other chance-based effects in the game - Competitive Urge Vet skill notwithstanding, since that skill could also use this treatment.

Fatshark has - in Vermintide 2 - made Grail Knight Strength Potion quest proc scale with difficulty level, i.e., the higher the difficulty, the more kills were needed to gain a strength potion to use, since difficulty affected density on each level. Why not make that in Darktide? I.e., make the proc guaranteed on X elite/special kills in coherency?

That way not only we would get rid of redundant randomness to regain a grenade, but also mitigate spam in higher difficulties to an extent, where the Vet suddenly regains three of their grenades to make a drastic impact on the outcome of an encounter against a blob of mixed wave of enemies, or a boss.

Vror:

Keep Their Heads Down should also give Suppression Immunity to the Vet

Vets are already suppression immune or immune-adjacent since the most recent changes to skill trees of Vet/Psyker/Zealot, so this is redundant. Damage increase to suppressed enemies could be nice, though.

Vror:

I’m not sure if Marskman Focus itself needs a change, but feel free to make a suggestion if you feel it does

I think it does. As I have mentioned in the previous part of my post, ranged-centric Veteran is just not worthwhile in comparison to their other playstyles, and with Desperado HScum being what ranged-centric gameplay is, on top of Pickpocket giving Scum effectively infinite ammo, it’s ever more redundant a playstyle. Say:

  • Why use a keystone that requires a lot of effort/tempo to upkeep, when you can use Focus Target or Weapon Specialist, while also having access to best skills of both middle- and right-bottom trees, which are further reinforced by the keystones being just good, in general, regardless of playstyle?
  • It provides no feasible benefit if there is no ammo to spare - also it requires you to mostly focus on weakspot ranged kills, which most of the time you can afford against certain elites, and specials, and shooters/stalkers, so the pool of enemies this is handy for is limited. Like, yes, you probably can build for very obscure breakpoints on certain weapons or drastically improve DPS against monsters on weakspot hits, but you can just take a keystone that gives you that benefit basically all the time - you and/or your teammates - with no regard what weapon you have in hand.

I don’t know what to change in it, but it feels very dated with how HScum has had their skills designed. It’s a very Borderlands-like keystone/skill that belongs in a shooter game (something something Critical Ascension of BL2’s Zer0), but Darktide despite heavily marketing the term “hybrid combat” is primarily melee then a shooter, still. No matter how it’s changed, I’d:

  • Shift its focus from weakspots to allowing you to headshot with bodyshots,
  • Make Deadshot (stamina drain for crit chance skill) a part of it and make it integral part of how the skill grants weakspot hits on bodyshots,
    • Nobody realistically puts Deadshot in their builds outside of Helbore builds anyway and even then it’s not a great skill in general, especialy with Vet being suppression immune right now /semi-srs /sarcasm,
  • The idea is to enable weapons that are overly reliant on weakspot hits to rely on them less while also making weakspot hits stronger. This should give Infantry autoguns/non-laser auto weapons (or laspistols, to be frank) potentially a good niche in Vet’s build arsenal, while also implementing some gimmick that does something to stamina management. The keystone already has an upgrade node that improves stamina efficiency - it’s perfect!

Nobody takes these nodes up top basically, and moving them away from ranged tree will help streamline it in a way (replace their respective spots with Lock and Load, and Shocktrooper).
