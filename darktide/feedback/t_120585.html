Hello, I wish to explain a serious issue with the “pro-trader” penance that was recently added with the expedition update.

I think it needs to be said that the penance simply requires a ridiculous amount of salvage to complete, 100 000 to be exact, and does not scale with any of the other challenges on the expedition mode. This penance has multiple issues and one of the main problems is that it incentivizes toxic gameplay habits.

Currently, I have managed to acquire the last “Tech-Hunter” penance, which requires you to extract with a total of 250 000 tech-remnants. This by itself is not impossible since collecting tech-remnants is supposed to be the main drive in this new game mode. However, now is when the cracks start to show.

After acquiring “Tech-Hunter (5)”, tech-remnants become completely useless and the hunt for salvage begins. Because the penance amount is not scaled, you now often find players who simply ignore all tech-remnants on the map and who refuse to enter the gates upon closing time since the only thing lost on death is tech-remnants. Because the spending amount of salvage is so insane, players often spam grenades and ammo to buy new stock in the sanctuaries, and upon reaching the last scavenge zone, players simply leave the game because there is no point in helping others extract.

The main reason why this occurs is because the penances are not scaled. By the time you reach the end of “Tech-Hunter (5)”, you have barely completed a third of the amount needed in “Pro-Trader (5)”. By cutting the requirement of salvage in half, and by allowing you to find salvage inside points of interest, it would help slightly to deal with this issue.

Please take a look at this, it needs to be addressed.
