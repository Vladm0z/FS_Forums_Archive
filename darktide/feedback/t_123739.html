Warhammer 40k has always been just as silly as it is grim. Sometimes it gets so grim that it wraps all the way back around to being completely ridiculous, and honestly, that’s part of the charm.

I think Darktide already has the grim part nailed. We’re literally playing as rejects who earn the right to keep breathing by being thrown into one suicide mission after another. The atmosphere, the setting, and the constant sense of impending doom are all there.

Now I want a little more of the other side of 40k.

Give us a playable Ork.

Don’t spend twenty pages explaining why. Either don’t explain it at all, or just shrug and say he’s a Blood Axe mercenary working for the Inquisition because they pointed him toward the biggest fight in the sector. That’s honestly more than enough justification by 40k standards.

Let me charge into a horde with a proper Choppa in one hand, a ridiculous amount of Dakka in the other, and loudly insist that every plan would work better if it involved more explosions.

Darktide has the grim. Now let us have a bit more of the gloriously stupid side of Warhammer too.
