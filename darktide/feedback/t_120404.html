Let’s go a little further with this math.

You also have a 1.25x total ammo increase from Ammo Stash. From memory ammo you pick up is based on your reserve ammo max so this also means 1.25x ammo pickups.

Then you have BLO, which at 15% chance not to consume ammo is an effective ~1.18 ammo multiplier.

Then of course the already mentioned 2x multiplier from PBB.

I won’t break down my full attempt at modelling how all these interact (it was a reducing series infinite sum, for those curious) but I’m pretty sure these all functionally act as separate multipliers to your total ammo.

2 X 1.25 X 1.18 = 2.95x effective ammo. Basically on average, if only firing during PBB, you have about triple the effective ammo compared to that gun being used without right tree.

Now obviously only firing during PBB is unrealistic, but I do think it’s worth noting just how all these different ammo conservation effects combine into an extremely hefty effective ammo increase. They all also affect any ammo you pick up, which means ammo pickups also go up to 3x further than without right tree.

And that’s also not factoring in how the increased ranged damage from right tree also functions as ammo conservation by requiring less shots to kill per target.

TL;DR yeah the PBB change, combined with the ammo conservation/increases already present in right tree, combines into a pretty enormous increase in effective ammo. I don’t think we’re in an era anymore where Ogryn NEEDS a survivalist Vet in the party to play a ranged build.

Edit: It’s mentioned down below that you get the PBB refund even on BLO procs that didn’t actually spend ammo. IF this is true the math changes a bit and after remodeling with a new series I get a total effective ammo increase of 3.6x.
