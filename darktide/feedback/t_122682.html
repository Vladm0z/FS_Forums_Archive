Agayek • 2026-07-08 22:28:58
Metaphage:

As I said, make it so it drains capacitance. So you cant recharge at all while its active, this fixes the problem. It has a cost associated with it now. in those 12 seconds you have no way of recharging capacitance, which can cripple you in some scenarios. Might even be too steep of a price.

You’re just asking for IRE to work the same way as ACD, and uptime is not really a consideration or concern for any halfway competent player. It is, in fact, exactly why I’m saying this won’t work. A decent player just spits out so much capacitance just by being in combat that it’s just not a realistic concern; either the drain is reasonable and you can just regen all you need in the downtime where there’s minimal ranged threats, or the drain is unreasonable and people will still cry about it being useless because they run out of shield halfway through the gunner line. If IRE is just tied to capacitance drain, you get functionally permanent immunity to ranged damage as long as you pay even the slightest bit of attention to your capacitance management. That is not healthy for the game and should absolutely not be a thing.

The “cooldown to regen charges” works because it puts an absolute upper limit on the uptime you can achieve in any given period, and the “still uses blitz charges but drains capacitance for extra duration” also works because while you can get a lot more value out of any given use of it, using it is hard committing a finite resource, which means you need to make decisions and think about your play. But even then, both of these have their own issues where they are arguably too strong; hard to say for sure either way without playtesting though.

There are changes to Skitarii blitzes that I think are probably reasonable, such as increasing the arc grenade’s chain arc to 360* and maybe adding a short range burst of electrocute effect when it explodes so it’s more consistent with its damage, or giving the shield fewer charges but a timed regen, but “just make the shield work off of capacitance” is absolutely not one of them.
