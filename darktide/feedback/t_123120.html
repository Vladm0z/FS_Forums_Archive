After playing Skitarii and mastering the Galvanic Rifle, I tried to make a build around ACD (Advanced Combat Doctrines), FP (Flensing Protocols) and Galvanic but the performance felt lacking. The builds always felt like a melee build with a galvanic duct taped to it, and it performed worse than other abilities too. The Phosphor Blast Pistol is a better fit for ACD. It draws faster, crits better, and adds value with its unique status. I think it has better base cleave too. Even Vigilant Autoguns perform better, with lager mags and more ammo.

The Galvanic has an entry in 7th edition codex skitarii “These weapons fire specialised servitor-bullets over long distances that cause all potential energy of the target to burn out in a killing blast of electric force.” So basically, electrocute on hit. We still have trouble with reload and total ammo but now galvanic has some team value. I think more could be done but as a Helbore vet player, this would be a good start.
