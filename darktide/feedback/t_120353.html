dunnothisguy • 2026-03-28 16:13:20

Because vet’s stealth isn’t about getting some kind of insane damage benefits on one strike vs a priority target, like Zealot’s - it’s about getting to a better position, buying yourself some time to prepare counter-attack, assisting others and self-healing, mostly. So, a bunch of reasons that can easily coincide in time. Like, you were fighting, your toughness went down to a dangerous level / you got jumped by a pack of ragers / crushers. You stealthed and dropped a bunch of nades to stagger and kill them. But then 10s after that your teammate went down or fell over the edge - now you stealth again to assist them. Such situtations occur in almost every run for my stealth vet builds.

When you have one charge, you start to overthink it too much, like “hey, that’s a nice bunch of gunners ahead, may be I should stealth, run in the middle of it, drop frags and chop them down like I usually do? oh no, wait - what if I’ll need stealth later to heal myself or res somebody? nah, I better not do that”. Or you just use it at any opportunity - and then don’t have it when you urgently need it.

Taking stealth as an ability instead of VoC already handicaps you a lot. But by being too conservative with it or not ready to use your stealth when you need it, you handicap yourself even more. And game is too chaotic to properly plan when you will be using it, sometimes you don’t need it at all, then you need to burn all two charges in one minute. You may think about it like “VoC helps both me and the team at once; with two charges of Infiltrate I carry one charge to spend on myself, and the other one to spend on assisting somebody, thus making the whole tradeoff of switching from VoC to Infiltrate less wasteful”

PathOfLobo • 2026-03-28 19:35:40

How useful a second charge is compared to shorter cooldown often depends on the playstyle.

When you use the ability regularly, for example to drop aggro when sniping priority targets without being bothered by astray poxwalkers and such, shorter cooldown and an extra talent point might be a better choice. However, if you’re using Infiltrate more situationally, it’s better to have an extra charge in your pocket. For example, when you advance with a melee-oriented build to cause havoc behind enemy lines, it’s a good idea to have an option to disengage in case things go wrong.

In any case, I don’t think a second charge should be built in. The same arguments people are mentioning here could be applied to VoC or pretty much any ability. I think Infiltrate is actually in a pretty good spot. If anything, I would prefer the damage bonus to last 10 seconds to be consistent with other effects.
