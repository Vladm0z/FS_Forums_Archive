Dumbledood:

But.. I’m guessing that you don’t benefit from CDR during the length of the stimm supply ability, and only once its expired does the ability start to benefit from the CDR. Is that right?

You benefit from CDR when you use StimmSupp, its instantly reduced by your amount of CDR (40% if you have 4 stacks of chem dependency). So it will go on cooldown for 36sec. Which it does not right now cause it’s bugged as you say.

Dumbledood:

Also, I think you can go the stimm supply + practiced deployment route instead to lower the cooldown. Though I’m not sure if CDR impact your stimm cooldown.

CDR does not effect stimm cooldown, only Sample Collector and Viscosity of your stimm.

But yea, you can do without the 15s one, if you play a little careful. With Practiced Deployment and Sample Collector, you can in certain situation get very low cooldown without low Viscosity. It’s quite a flexible ability, depending on what you want.

Personally, I just use 15s by Viscosity, and then don’t take sample collector or Chem dependency.
