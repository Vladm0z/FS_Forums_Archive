First of all, thank you once more for posting the balance patch notes before they actually drop. Giving everyone some time to argue over provide constructive criticism on the changes means some bad ideas might be caught in the pipeline.

The change to Gold Toughness was a long time coming, but I’m also not sure if you really needed to up the max toughness bonus on VoC. Having that extra buffer of no chip damage taken is still fairly powerful. I also don’t really think the Sniper nerf was justified, but the Poxburster nerf is welcome.

I don’t really have takes on Ogryn, since I don’t play him much anymore, but they all seem fine.

Zealot “core keystone” thing seems like it could be dangerous, especially with how often Zealot gets to use their active ability, and Zealous Pilgrim seems like it could be an insane get out of jail free card. The rest of the addons seem fine at best. Chorus nerf was also a long time coming, and I’m pleased to see it dethroned from being the best defensive active ability in the game.

For Psyker, I don’t really like the removal of Tranquility Through Slaughter. It was a keystone I found a lot of use out of via IAGs and Recons, and I don’t see the use of Peril Equilibrium when SG builds are usually about keeping the buff ticking as long as possible (I guess to keep Warp Rider up after it ends? Genuinely what is this talent for). I also think their warp damage output was fine enough without the +15% bonus from Focused Warp that’ll be added.

Veteran changes seem… too minimal for me. Vet has a lot of dead nodes I never take (including keystones) and I don’t think any of these changes are going to affect that. At least their ammo economy will be a little bit better.

No notes on Cop. I’m still only rocking Charge if I do eventually decide to play them for a bit.

Hive Scum is a class I’ve played a lot of, and I’m nearing level 350 on them. Their hyper-aggressive playstyle suits mine perfectly, and they also happen to be one of the best classes in the game, tied with Psyker. To that end, the proposed changes (all of Two of them) are going to do absolutely nothing.
Rampage needs a way bigger nerf if it’s going to rock without Exhaustion in the future. I think you should also just cut the damage resistance and stun/suppression immunity, because its defensive bonuses are incredibly strong for what’s supposed to be a very fragile character.
Sample Collector has overall been buffed significantly, which I don’t think was the intent. Almost nobody uses high viscosity stimms anyways and the sweet spot is generally a 4/3 stimm tree with maybe an extra single point on damage resist for around a 45-50 second cooldown. This is going to let you use stimms a LOT more often, especially with explosive juicebox builds that already immediately put the juicebox on a 15 second cooldown.

For Skitarii, sweep claw was fine as is. I often found myself wanting for more when dealing with large groups of elites, and I think it should’ve been left alone. The thing you needed to nerf was the boss damage on the single-target attack. Everything else is okay, I guess. Nothing really worth writing about.

Weapon changes all seem fine, I guess.

Overall, these changes are decent, but they still don’t address the overwhelming dominance of firestaff and Smite, or a melee HS who knows how to cook.
