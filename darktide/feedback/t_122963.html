After doing almost all penances and leveling all weapons, I am confused how the Arc Rifle made it in the game in it’s current state. If you try this in Auric hi-shock troop, there is almost nothing besides bosses that you can’t just delete with it. This is worse than any Smite-Scriers Gaze or flame staff Psyker that already make me dodge the lobby.

The gun is good against specials, horde and Armour, both in point blank- and very long range without barely any weakness. Crusher waves are a complete joke, as you just keep shooting them until everything around them is dead. What I don’t understand is, why isn’t it at least balanced in low ammo efficiency or at least swap speed? You swap the gun faster than a Hellbore while it should be at least as slow as the Bolter or even slower imo. The ammo efficiency is also extremely good, as you can easily run the whole mission with it without taking too much ammo from the ground. The slow reload can easily be avoided by the self loading talent.

I call it now that in a couple of weeks, when people return to their most comfortable loadouts, we will see a rise in Arc Rifle users that just ruin the game for everyone that wants to use melee builds and wants to play an actual hard game. This is just power creep in a nutshell.
