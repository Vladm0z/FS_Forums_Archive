Poxbursters are enemy #1 right now, and I find they are the chief reason for most fails. When a wave of them spawns, it’s almost guaranteed that at least one player will die, due to how broken they are right now.

They’ve always been an issue when spawning in groups, as they can spawn staggered enough that pushing the first one will set off the second while it’s still in range. This has always been a frustrating issue, but now with their AoE effectively increased by poor optimization attempts, they are so much worse. The only damage I’m even taking in most missions is just bursters who refuse to be pushed, and especially on a glass cannon class like Hive Scum, it only takes this happening twice and you are simply dead.

There’s really no reason for something this dangerous to be this unavoidable. If you’re not going to revert your previous changes, then shrink their hitbox, or severely reduce the damage at the outermost distances. It never has made a lot of sense how they go from max to zero damage so sharply.
