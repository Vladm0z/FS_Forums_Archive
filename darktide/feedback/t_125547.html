a suggestion that I believe could greatly improve the experience for console players. would be to add a keyboard and mouse support for the console versions of the game.

some console players struggle with awareness in higher difficulty games because they can’t maneuver as well as pc players can. I myself am a pc player and I have console friends that struggle with movement and awareness because they can’t do as much as pc players can, such as getting out of a bad scenario.

For an example let’s say there is a pox burster behind you while you are getting rushed from the front. If some pc players are in this situation what they would do, is quickly look behind them, push the burster back and look forward at the horde or whatever is Infront of them before they get hit. But if console players were in the situation, they would most likely push the burster back but since it’s slower for them to move, they would probably get caught up by the horde or whatever is Infront of them.

This is one of many reasons why I think adding a kbm input for console players would help them and make the experience of the game much better for them.

Thanks for reading and please look forward to this.
