What does Redline Capacitors get?

%20 toughness reduction %20 capacitance generation and 1 ability charge.

An ability that gives %15 rending when we have 3 stacks of or above capacitance (in a spam ability keystone)

Make resource optimisation canticles basekit (the one where it gives +1 charge) and give the keystone another way to generate capacitance. This keystone should’ve been THE keystone to use for capacitance gain and ability usage. but literally every other one does it better.

You didn’t read the keystone. That’s why you think it sucks.

The strength buff maintains 5 stacks regardless of how many capacitance charges you had when you used your ability. So you use it at 5 stacks, then every ability use after that maintains the buff at 5 stacks.

The rending bonus is given based on how many redline stacks you have, not how many capacitance stacks you have.

So you have a lot of uptime on those buffs. It only goes down basically when you have a big gap between engagements, which means it gets better as you go up in difficulty.

What I will agree on tho is that it’s by far best when used with the claw, and the interaction with voltaic might need reconsidering.
