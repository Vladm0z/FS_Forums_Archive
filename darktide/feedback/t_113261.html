Adding utility to underused blessings

4 Posts • 40 Views
L
Leebee • 2025-10-04 17:59:09

I have no illusions the “meta” blessings can really be unseated by improvements to the lower tier ones so that’s not my goal, but adding some utility to them could go a long way in supporting new playstyles, rhythms, and textures of play.

It’s been on my mind with the recent chain weapon improvements (although Eviscerators still should be larger, have a bigger range and a slower swings to better differentiate from chainswords, just look at the size of them in the source material!) but for example, Rev It Up is a very fun but somewhat weak blessing (IJ Zealots have some synergy, which is cool), but a simple change like allowing you to cancel select attacks’ recovery into activation would go a long way to producing a new, novel playstyle with chain weapons.

It’s true this would have a direct improvement to overall DPS, but the bigger thing is that it would allow you to use activation to fit in a bigger cleaving attack you wouldn’t normally be able to, or disengage with a fast burst of movement to reposition, increase effectiveness against armored foes in a pinch… etc. To keep it balanced, you’d probably want to limit this to only the last few frames of animation and keep the cancel only for like the 2nd or 3rd attacks in a light string, push attacks, and heavy attacks so that you’re not just able to spam it.

I’m curious, are there other blessings that you love (or WANT to love), where making a change outside of a simple improvement to damage or whatever would go a long way to creating new dimensions of play?
