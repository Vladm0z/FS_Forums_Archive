To put it another way – the biggest problem now is that there are very few maps, and bugs keep popping up everywhere. For example, a blessing called ‘Relentless’ stopped working due to a bug a while ago, and I still don’t know if it’s been fixed. There’s also the veteran’s grenade regen delay bug. I feel like just listing all these issues could pressure FatShark into releasing a major update. Then there’s the lack of new maps – haven’t we gone a full quarter without a single new map? There are also other modes that need optimization, like updating Havoc with new buffs or an endless mode. And in Havoc mode, the map rotation means you can’t always play the map you want. Plus, weapon bugs – for instance, after the last update that buffed the lasgun, the muzzle flash and projectile path don’t align properly. And then there are the new paid cosmetic skins. Honestly, there’s so much that I feel like a single forum post wouldn’t be enough to cover it all. I notice that discussions on the forums about the lack of maps and gameplay experience optimization are relatively rare. I feel like we should come together as a community and push FatShark to fully optimize the game. What do you think?
