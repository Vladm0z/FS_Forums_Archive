If I may offer my general feedback on the psyker tree, I am really surprised at some of the game design decisions that were reached.

-- The two nodes that could actually boost non-warp damage have been removed, replaced by one node that boosts Warp Damage by more. Psyker’s non-warp focused builds were generally not considered the balance problem.

Indeed, this change pretty much benefits Inferno the most, because rapidly hitting relatively low damage attacks will have any damage buff translate more directly to DPS than for those attacks at the mercy of breakpoints.

-- Peril Equilibrium doesn’t really make up for this for gunpsyker builds, because even with Warp Rider, the damage lost compared to a Gaze is massive, as is the toughness regeneration from being able to churn Peril with Quietitude.

(And if we’re arguing “well, psyker isn’t reeeeeeally supposed to be a gun build”, then they should have more than four choices of staff).

-- Tranquillity through Slaughter has been removed, basically killing a lot of gun-psyker builds, because there’s now very poor capacity to sustain Scrier’s with any ranged weapon that hasn’t got its own quell. Which hits particularly hard considering the reduction in CDR available from Psykinetic’s Aura, so uptime will be horrible on Gaze for gunpsykers.

-- Perilous Combustion has had its stacks per proc reduced, but because of the non-linear damage of soulblaze, this hits non-Inferno builds (such as melee psykers who were using it to help with mixed mobs) a lot harder than it does the Inferno.

To me, the natural solution for solving the huge number of stacks that could be applied on top of Inferno was just capping Perilous Combustion’s stacks.

-- I can understand why Psykinetic’s Aura had its duration reduced, but we’ve entirely lost the “aura” part, and indeed the sense of team play by removing the “in coherency” part, making everyone have to compete for elite/specialist kills for their talents.
