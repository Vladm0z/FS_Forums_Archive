I’ll try and be brief: I enjoy DT a lot and I think havoc, in some ways, is the best the game has to offer.
But this entirely gratuitous reset is really breaking any goodwill I have.

What’s the point of resetting players in a co-op game? Sure, havoc 2.0 was a huge change and as such it made sense to pull players back given the entire reassessment in difficulty/how havoc works.
But this? This entirely unannounced reset is just weave “semi-competitive leaderboard mentality”: it brings nothing to the table and it’s done - I think - because it’s perceived as helping with player retention.
It did no such thing in Vermintide, and I don’t see it being a positive here either.

Some of us struggle with progressing, in big part because we don’t have a full premade that can dedicate 7 days a week to playing havoc. Still, we can - and want, and would - progress in our own time, because for the most part we enjoy the challenge. This kind of move denies us that. Worse, it makes me glad we didn’t try harder: I’d be really pissed if the reset happened when we happened to reach rank 38 or 39.

I’ll still play havoc for fun, but a lot more casually, and I probably will not be trying to reach rank 40 again. Why would I?
The main appeal of rank 40 is being free of the demotion system. But if we are still getting demoted for no reason whenever fatshark feels like it I will not be arsed.

Is it good for the mode? I doubt it. Newcomers will still want to get the penances I think, so it’s not like the mode will be free from players trying to play way above their skill level.
And any time-gate is discouraging players (like myself) that would try their best for the sake of it, reducing the active player population (which, I gather, isn’t great to begin with).

Look at cataclysm as an inspiration on how to foster a good community for the top content: the difficulty is there just for the added challenge: there’s no incentive whatsoever for playing it (I suppose technically some challenges are there too. But for the most part pretty basic ones, and cata was outside of the gameplay loop - giving exactly the same rewards as legend).

I’d have followed the same philosophy here. I dislike some of havoc’s workings, but at least I can see the reasoning behind its design. But I absolutely cannot see what good a “seasonal reset” can do: not to mention that, with all their merits, FS cannot pull off a seasonal system, even if such a thing was proven to be a positive: they are simply too erratic and piecemeal in their dev approach to pull something like that off. It’s a harsh criticism, but I think you’ll agree it’s a fair one.
