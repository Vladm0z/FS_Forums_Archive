17 Posts • 397 Views

Djaresh • 2025-09-25 21:12:52

After the last two patches it is save to say that the Heavy Sword is currently the worst melee weapon by far. While it’s definitely good at killing horde and the heavy attack has it’s unique property of dealing massive damage to the first two unarmoured targets, it absolutely lacks in everything else.

It’s slow movement speed, worst performance against carapace armour of all melee weapons and the fact that other than Headtaker all blessings are bad makes it such an awful weapon to use.

What I would suggest:

Buff the mobility. Having such high movement penalty during attacks makes it so horrible to wield and there are other weapons that have excellent horde clear that don’t suffer from this.

Add or rework some of the blessings. Other than Headtaker, all current blessings are mostly working against horde in which the weapon is already very good at. The only one that gives rending works when hitting multiple enemies, so you don’t have it against crushers where you need it.

Give us a way to deal with armour. What I think to be a good way would be giving the special attack the ability to apply brittleness similar like the Ogryns Ripper Gun with Can Opener, either as base ability or as a new blessing. This would still make it worse than other weapons against armour but at least you could fight crushers somewhat efficiently and it would make it unique to use.

I play all classes and use every weapon in Auric missions and in the current state I’d say the melee weapon balance is at a good point where you can make anything work on Havoc so far except the Heavy Sword. It is currently the most lacking weapon, even far behind the Devils Claw and it feels like a handicap to use.
