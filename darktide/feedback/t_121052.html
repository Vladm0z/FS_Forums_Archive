Rvl • 2026-04-23 13:41:21

There shouldn’t be situations where players literally do not have a means of dealing with something in the game.

There are multiple ways to counter DH final boss in Mortis.

Stagger explosion every 10 seconds or stagger explosion when your toughness breaks. This gives you breathing room to melee/shoot them, instead of being under constant pressure.

You can have insane damage to kill it in ~10 seconds with some luck, or survive them with grenades that heal boon, although relying on those might be the worst option, but heal grenades with some other buffs are really strong.

Stagger on each shot with infinite ammo builds (save ammo on crit with crit indulgence). There’s a boon that makes your ability give you infinite ammo pool and infinite clip, which allows you to shoot non-stop. Makes you shoot infinitely and as long as you keep hitting the target, anything gets perma staggered, even bosses. This build also can push the bosses slowly to the abyss and instantly kill them.

Stacking multiple usages of ability (+2 ability with low cooldown build is very doable, makes so that you can chain them into each other). Using stealth drops the aggro and lets you sneak in oneshots with Mortis talents like 350% more melee damage on a single hit every 10 seconds. Arbitrator with Castigator or Psyker with Scriers can have insane damage reduction in Mortis, because you can stack damage reduction on top of the ability buffs.

On Arbitrator and Vet, kraks and dogs can stagger DH infinitely. With a blitz build, as long as you keep landing the grenades on target, it won’t even be able to move.

One of the indulgences has a 5% chance to oneshot any enemy on melee hits, including DH and Twins. As long as you survive for at least a couple of minutes, it will eventually proc and kill anything.

There are indulgences like Colossus which dramatically increase your survivability. I know you might think they’re not worth using, but one of them has a boon that nullifies trapper nets and dogs if they hit you, it’s one of the strongest boons in the mode, despite not being DPS-related. They also have some nutty defense layers which can make you tank DH attacks with any class.
