I like plasma and don’t want it nerfed into the ground and call it a day. So here is a reasonable rework which addresses its overpowered status but will allow the weapon to remain fun and take on new aspects which are better for balance purposes.

Uncharged changes

  1. Reduce damage overall by 25%
  2. Remove cleave and replace with a reasonably size aoe effect (about twice the size of bolt explosion).
  3. Reduce suppression radius
  4. Reduce the amour pen by 15%
  5. Increase stagger a small amount
  6. Remove balwark shield pen

Charged changes

  1. Increase damage by 20%
  2. Reduce cleave buy half but give a krak sized explosion on full charge once the projectial stops moving
  3. Leave armour pen as is
  4. Increase Unyielding
  5. Increase stagger to be able to stagger bosses in 2 charged hits

Both uncharged and charged changes

  1. Remove cover cleave
  2. reduced clip size but not ammo overall

The issue with plasma is that it is a everything weapon with no real weakness and what this rework does is make the plasma more of a group and boss focused weapon than a quick sniper which could wipe out a hoard.

Reasoning behind changing plasma to an aoe weapon. Most other game depictions of the iconic weapon is one with aoe features and not a plasma bolt cleaving heaven and earth to delete the target. Also as an aoe based weapon with increasing aoe based on the charge the charged shot’s ammo increase would not feel as horrible as it does now with it requiring 3 times the ammo for about 2 times the damage.
