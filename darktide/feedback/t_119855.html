I’m also liking it so far. they could use more Tiles, but that’ll take time and i can wait. Though Though i feel it needs more polish. a few tweaks and features needed.

I’m still learning the ropes but here are a few ideas I wish to propose to Fat Shark if they read this. i might also make a full list later with my peers.

  1. Distance tracking - Currently all points of interest on the scanner are just ‘there’, but where is ‘there’? How far away is ‘there’ from your current location? I don’t need a map (yet) but I do want to know how far away I am to the nearest point of interest and the extraction zone. just some numbers to tell me how many meters or kilometers to tell players how far they got from point A to B.

  2. Sharing Scrap - This is a coop game and while scrap salvage seems to be split between players, it can’t be shared. you go to a sanctuary, buy ammo, meds, maybe a stim, but everyone is now low on scrap. one dude had 48, another has 25, a third has 13, last player has 5, and you all REALLY want that modified explosive for 75 scrap and the last player has a free slot open. I feel players should be able to share their scrap with others when inside the sanctuary to squeeze the most out of your resources to survive the next fight.

  3. Consensus on Sanctuary/evacuation - there needs to be 2-1, 3-1 vote on when the team should extract or enter a sanctuary. I’ve had a few times where one person would activate the sanctuary gates while me and another person were trying to unlock crates with tech in them and lost it all. So much tech and salvage lost just because someone decided to move on to the next section by mistake or intentional. This might sound convoluted, but depending how many active players are in a game (not including dead or tied up) there needs to be another person to actively agree on it.

Overtime people will learn from mistakes, break bad habits, and maybe try and communicate more with their team. Though these suggestions i feel should be looked into adding into the game mode to help polish it up.

Also we need a sniper rifle and scope/holosight attachments. I don’t care who is saying no on the subject, but right now I need something better then iron sights to hit targets that are mile away taking pot shots at me. I’m not asking for a Warhammer 40,000: Call of Duty, I just want a bayonet and a scope on my las rifle!
