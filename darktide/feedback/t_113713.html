Margeret-Dayhoff • 2025-10-18 22:17:01

Just to clarify something. Killing a mob that has soulblaze on it will not automatically credit the psyker with the kill. The person that “finishes off” the enemy gets the kill (unless there is some sort of bug I’m not aware of).

However, what you’re likely seeing instead is an escalating chainreaction from a psyker talent called Perilous Combustion that procs on elite kills.

If a psyker has put soulfire on an elite and it is killed (regardless of whether you or the psyker kills it), it will spread 3 stacks of soulblaze to all mobs close to it, including other elites.
This can quickly spiral out of control if a lot of elites are stacked together tight. If 1 or 2 elites die, suddenly surrounding mobs get huge numbers (3-6-9… etc.) of soulblaze stacks fast. If they’re already at high stacks, this will likely finish them all off very fast.

At very high stack numbers, the soulblaze dot ticks for a lot of damage. That’s where you see these huge bursts where psykers are credited with a bunch of elite kills. It’s because a soulblaze tick probably did indeed finish the mob off.
