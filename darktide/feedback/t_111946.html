As I have done before with my other posts suggesting new abhuman classes, I am going to state this right here: I am fully aware of just how unlikely it is for this kind of thing to implemented in-game, but sometimes we players can’t just leave an idea alone, no matter how stupid it may seem. And if you see any flaws in this post you are free to suggest ways to fix them.

Now, for those that are unfamiliar with what a scaly is, it is basically humanoid lizard. They hail from the Hiveworld of Necromunda (at least for now), and are found living in the most absolutely polluted and toxic environments there are, which is believed to the source of their mutation. They are strong, and even more resilient than ogyrns since they have regenerative abilities, but are not really social, since they are reviled for most part, and their mutation often makes them unable to speak normally.

As for what they would bring in terms of new content, we will start with new weapons. We already have some Necromundan weapons in-game, so we could use the scaly as an excuse to bring in more. As to what could be added, from what little lore that I have read on scalies, they are not quite as strong as ogryns, but they are perhaps more intelligent, so we could give them guns that might be considered too complicated for Ogryns to them.

As for abilities, I got one that could work canonically, but the other two I’m kind of grasping at straws here, so I will not be offended if you suggest something else. The ability that makes sense would be a regeneration ability, where activating it instantly cause your toughness to boost up for a time, reduce incoming damage and maybe remove some corruption, but it would come at the cost of almost being unable to move until the buff wears off, and you can’t toggle it on and off like the the arbites “castigator stance”, so got to be a little more strategic with it. The second ability is movement skill, basically it would be Darktide’s version of VT2’s Kerillian Handmaiden Dash ability. My excuse for having this? Life in the Underhive is absolutely ruthless, and you need to not only be strong but quick as well if you want to survive, thus you need to know when it’s time to run, as Zola can personally attest to since she was once a ganger. The third ability, poison spit. Have not read anything in the lore about whether or not scalies can actually do this or not, so my only justification is they gain this as a benefit from living in toxic shitholes.

As for Blitzes, the three I got are a jury-rigged tripwire, a molotov cocktail (that shatters on impact unlike the zealot’s flame grenades), and a sticky satchel charge that only goes off after you shoot it.
