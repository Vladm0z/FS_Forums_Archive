Make Arc Maul special work like Mechanicus Power Sword special

12 Posts • 154 Views
E
Effigy • 2026-07-22 03:08:47

This is a usability suggestion for the Arc Maul. Currently, the special action enables the powered attack mode on this weapon and has an activation animation. However, this weapon has limited charges for its empowered attacks, much like the Mechanicus Power Sword. Having an animation to enable the powered mode makes sense as a balancing factor for weapons that allow spamming the empowered attacks, like the Veteran’s Power Sword. For a weapon that only has ~6 charges for empowered attacks, I don’t think that mode-swap animation is needed for balance.

Mechanicus Power Sword special action performs one empowered attack without an activation delay, and works seamlessly in the attack chain with unpowered attacks. This makes it easier to ration out your power charges and doesn’t penalize players for switching frequently between powered and unpowered attacks. It seems natural to extend this same design to the Arc Maul, which uses power charges that are very similar to the MPS. I would enjoy using the Arc Maul a lot more if its special action worked like the MPS special.
