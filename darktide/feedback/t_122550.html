In a day and age where public opinion hard to actually follow with multiple groups of people on different forums, influencers influencing and everybody having the right of opinion on what should or could be added to the game, what do you want added? No wrong answers.

So I thought to myself, why not directly ask? Yes. It’s on a forum on a page for a game where you need to register to be able to post and reply, so it’s not the full on masses of people, but enough people to be heard.

(I do understand that these are simply wishes, Fatshark devs are in the end, Blessed servants of the machine god and emperor. They cannot simply add everything and anything they want. But hey, fun nonetheless to do some wishful thinking? Is it not?)

I can start with some things I have heard, and can relate to:

  • Weapons: Psyker staffs, how come there’s no new ones? Ogryn weapons? Arbites, hive scum and now skitarii releasing with many cool weapons being skipped? Veteran, the weapons-specialist having minimal amount of specialist weapons? And oh so many more. The 40k universe is massive when it comes to weaponry.

  • Story/Missions: With the abrupt (and in my opinion quite bad end) to the Dark Communion mission where you simply get a few voice lines and cut to black, it would be a delight to partake in missions with a narrative design. Or simply more missions. As cool as the new gamemodes being added are like Havoc, Expiditions and Mortis trials; They simply do not scratch that itch.

  • Cosmetics: Props to the team actually adding earnable cosmetics through penances but the armoury exchange remains untouched and new cosmetics being drip-fed through tedious long or difficult challenges and penances feel hardly satisfying at all. I myself can admit to buying a couple of items at the commodore’s vestures, but when most cool items are paid for with real money, it can leave a bit of sour taste in one’s mouth.

  • Balancing: Let’s face the truth, balancing a game is hard. When the meta is everchanging and the demands and needs of the playerbase shifts with it, it can be hard to find a good middlepoint where it can be seen as “Good Enough”. Some weapons go unloved by many and some might be lost in the flow of reworks that shifts through the immaterium into our game. But, balancing is a necessity for a good experience, not just for the top players that find the meta and min-max their builds but also for the newer or less experienced players that try to climb their ranks through the warband. Speaking only for myself, as someone who is not an influencer nor hold any sway over the devs, I feel powerless in the tides that change how I could play the game. A hard fulfilled wish from me would be more of an open dialogue for players to influence how the game is balanced. Like open streams where we would be allowed to submit questions about what the devs thoughts are, more direct responses on the forums or simply surveys where we could answer a simple 1-5/1-10 question on what we feel is powerful or not.

Let me hear your opinions, remember to be respectful. The devs are the not archenemy, they are our ally in the face of a much greater evil.

The emperor protects. Always.

Broadly, there’s 4 things I want for the game.

First, I want new weapons for the existing classes. Not that concerned about what they are or what class, though my preference would be an Ogryn “Fists” weapon. Preferably something very similar to what’s shown here:

Second, I want new maps for the core adventure game mode. Don’t really have any specific desires or goals in this regard though. More stuff like No Man’s Land would be baller as hell, but so would more stuff in Gloriana’s tileset, or maybe an adventure mode using the Expeditions tileset/aesthetic, etc. Anything would be good, just more and more varied full maps for the core game.

Third, I want new enemies. The dream would be to add a genestealer enemy faction akin to the way Beastmen were added to VT2, but I would perfectly happily settle for fleshing out the existing faction(s) and especially getting the enemies we logically should have but don’t. For example, adding a dreg mauler, ogryn rager, or dreg trapper (literally just a VT2 hookrat with a texture update), etc. That would do a lot to refresh the texture of the play experience.

Finally, and most importantly IMO, I want some dedicated time and attention from the devs toward QOL fixes and improving the balance situation. Rein in the ludicrous player power outliers so I can play auric with a competent psyker or scum or PBB ogryn on the team and actually interact with at least a certain minimum number of live enemies by the end of the mission. Fix the weird bugs and quirky nonsense that have been in the game since launch, like coherency regen being disabled by dead enemies in their death animation. Clean up and standardize tooltips and language with proper keywords, and add an in-game glossary for at least basic game mechanics for those interested to learn how the game operates without needing to consult the oracles. Etc, etc.

Any of these I’d be pretty happy with, but all of them would be fantastic. And if I had to only choose one, it’d definitely be #4.
