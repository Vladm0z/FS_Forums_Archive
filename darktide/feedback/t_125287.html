I’ve been playing a lot of arbites and I gotta say, I’m sad to see the state of dog in havocs, Perhaps its just power creep but it feels like you’re essentially pigeon holed into some very specific roles if you want to run dog - worse than that, aiming dog is much more difficult than aiming a grenade because it isn’t an instant gratification situation and you have to monitor to make sure the dog is in position for it to work correctly.

Unlike shockmine / arbites grenade where the effect is near instant, it is entirely possible to have the dog tagged to a mob, allow for travel time and for remote detonation to be a very bad call on timing to use.

Mobs like Crushers, reapers, daemon hosts and the Ogryn pack master cannot be latched onto by the dog - so timing requires you to be perfect - as once the dog does an attack it is bounced back and begins to circle the mob for its next pounce.

Mobs like Plague Ogryns, Beasts of Nurgle and Chaos Spawns can be latched onto - but the dog does not stay latched - this means you have a slightly larger window for when you can get a successful detonation off.

When you compare that and travel time to the point and click ease of voltiac shock mine/arbites grenade it just ends up leaving a bad taste in your mouth.

proposed solutions:
for monsters that can be latched onto - the dog shouldn’t let go. Pack master ogryn should likely be added to the list of monsters that the dog can latch onto in the name of consistency.

For mobs that the dog cannot latch onto the dog should definitely circle close - it might be worth adding some sort of leg biting animation - it would be cool to see that even if they cannot be fully stopped they could be slowed. That feels like a pipe dream though (with what budget?).
