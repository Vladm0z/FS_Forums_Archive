Suggestion: how to make Arbitrator's and Hive Scum's support abilities more fun to use

It always made me sad, seeing how plain and boring their support abilities (Cartel Special and Nuncio Aquilla) are. I saw a lot of missed potential in them, if only FS decided to invest a little bit more imagination when designing them. It’s basically “press F to buff when off-CD” kind of thing not involving much skill or strategy to use. So naturally I tried to think how to “gamify” them a bit, to make using them more entertaining.

Here are changes I would propose to FS design team to consider.

For Nuncio Aquilla:

The core is generally fine, so I would suggest to enhance its utility and versatility by allowing to use it offensively as well. Pressing ability button again while the currently activated one hasn’t yet expired triggers a stunning / shocking explosion in a surrounding area, heavily staggering / stunning mobs in vicinity. The effect is stronger if the explosion is triggered closer to its natural expiration time (starting with close to 0 stagger and reaching its full potential at about 50% of its duration passed - to emphasize its main usage as a support ability, first of all, not a cheap and quick AoE stagger attack). Triggering the explosion ends current effect in place immediately.

Thus you have both buffing and AoE stagger options in one Ability you can choose between on the go, adapting it to the situation at hand.

For Cartel Special:

Again, the core Ability is fine and even already can have certain tactical application options (like the sub-node that allows you apply toxin stacks to enemies). I still think it would be much cooler if you would be allowed to re-shuffle points for your stim crate on the go during the mission. That would allow for a kinda “field medic” style of play, with HS player adapting their drug concoction to the random team they got in that particular match, or to the situation at hand.

Designing a UI that would be convenient to use to quickly do it on the go may be a challenging task - but I’m pretty sure it’s doable, Stim’s tree has just 4 branches, so it’s about moving some points from one branch to another quickly, you can come up with some hotkeys allowing to do it quickly, may be even while dodging attacks during a fight (or certainly when in elevator / airlock).
