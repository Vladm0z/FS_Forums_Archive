I heard it was buffed - honestly, didn’t feel it at all. Still pretty horrible weapon, severely limited for no apparent reason.

A few improvements that imo are required to make it viable:

  1. Why on Earth it has just 2 dodges, while being a close range ranged weapon? A rule of thumb for other such weapons (combat shotguns, double barrel, shredder autopistol) is 3 dodges is minimal. Flamer being a notable exception, but it has benefits of its own (infinite cleave) so it makes sense to handicap it.

  2. The fact you can’t take out even “soft” specialists/elites enemies at even medium range already with it handicaps the weapon beyond any measures. I believe it needs a special attack which would switch it into “short bursts mode”, similar to how Agripina Combat Shotgun has alternative slug charge you can activate with alt attack. In that mode it will be firing 3-4 shots bursts with long pauses between them (so your DPS will plummet) - but it will be much more precise and will allow you to kill a sniper or a trapper at a notable distance.
