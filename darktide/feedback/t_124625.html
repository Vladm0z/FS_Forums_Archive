I started to play the Vigilante again a bit because I desperately needed some change from the Galvanic Rifle and imo, it’s currently still a bad weapon but it could be so good. With Fatshark even saying they want to buff some of the lesser performing weapons, let me give a couple of suggestions:

  • Change the recoil behaviour

The recoil is not only too strong on the burst variants, it’s also so inconsistent. I’d recommend to change the recoil for the two burst variants to be very little but with a stronger recoil modifier on the last shot of a burst. Basically a last shot multiplier like Battlefield 4 had on burst weapons. Burst rifles have to be able to hit the whole burst on target to be effective, which is currently not easy even up close as 20 meters. As for the single shot variant, the recoil should just be less random.

  • increase the burst RoF

The burst RoF on both burst variants is too slow. It should be around 800RPM minimum.

  • Add base-kit 10% rending on headshots

The only armor type it’s doing full damage is Unarmoured, which is bad because it means it looses a lot of damage against almost all enemies. This buff would make sense, as it would further make the weapon a skilled headshot weapon while preventing mag-dumping Specialists and Elites up close to be too effective.

  • Reduce Suppression effect

This one I would actually consider for all precision weapons that suffer from suppression. We don’t necessary need to nerf suppression into the ground, but a resistance when crouched would go a long way for the weapons not to be completely useless as soon as your getting shot by a single gunner. The Galvanic Rifle comes with almost complete suppression immunity too as a precision weapon. It’s just weird that weapons like shotguns and Recon Lasgun are more effective on long range than the precision weapons like Infantry Lasgun or Vigilante because they don’t suffer from suppression. Other classes than Veteran should at least have a way to work around it and I think a crouching reduction would be good.

  • Just add a sight pls

It’s rather ironic that the closest thing we have to a marksman/sniper rifle has a crappy iron sight.

Screenshot 2026-08-03 141947610×615 81.8 KB

This one is unused and would fit so great to the weapon. I do not wish for the zoom to be changed.

All in all I think the Vigilante Autogun has a lot of potential. I also disagree that people keep saying there is no place for sniper like weapons in the game. The Galvanic Rifle absolutely slaps if you have aim and the Vigilante already exists, so there is no reason to not show it a bit love and buff it to be usable. It has so much potential.
