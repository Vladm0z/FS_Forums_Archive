I think a spear-and-shield archetype would be a really fun addition to Darktide.

It would introduce a precision-focused melee weapon with excellent reach, playing similarly to Vermintide’s Spear & Shield rather than another crowd-clearing weapon.

I think the Skitarii would be the best fit for it because:

1. It’s already lore-friendly.

The Adeptus Mechanicus already fields Secutarii Hoplites, so the concept of spear-and-shield Skitarii isn’t new. Personally, I’d lean more toward a round Cog Mechanicum shield instead of the large ceremonial Secutarii shield, but the overall idea already exists in the lore.

2. It would have a unique combat identity.

Unlike the Arbites or Ogryn shield playstyles, I’d imagine the Skitarii version being less about crowd control and stagger, and more about fast, precise thrusts backed up by a defensive tool.

3. It complements the Skitarii’s gameplay.

The Skitarii already feels like a slower, more deliberate class. A precise, defensive melee weapon would fit that identity naturally without overlapping too much with existing options.

4. It fits the Skitarii better than the other classes.

I think it would feel a little too slow or awkward for the Veteran, not tanky enough for the Arbites, and aesthetically it doesn’t really fit the HS (assuming it would be a Mechanicus-style power spear).

Overall, it just feels like a natural fit for a close-range, defensive playstyle that emphasizes precision over brute force.

Thoughts?
