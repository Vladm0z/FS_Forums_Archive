dunnothisguy • 2025-09-30 23:17:40

With all readily-accessible talents (like, not hidden behind keystones) affecting their damage taken, including Grenade Tinkerer and Rending Strikes, throwing two frags one after another isn’t enough anymore to take out gunners reliably. Even if dropped rather close to them, even dreg machinegunners are not dying consistently, and scabs machinegunner don’t die almost always (tested in the simulation at Auric difficulty), they are left with a little bit HP left. Before the last rebalance update and under the same conditions, on a similar build, you were sure both will be dead, even if they were rather far away from the explosion’s center.

The only thing that consistently closes this gap is Skirmisher talent - but only if its stacks are up, and they are often tricky to keep up, in a fight, they will quickly go away when you go into melee. And not every build using frags takes this talent anyway, it’s too deep and too far away from the center.

And sure, Grenadier may proc and you’ll throw 3 grenades instead of 2 - but, again, not reliable and chance isn’t that high either.

That significantly reduces value of frag grenades as a mixed hoard clearing tool, their only purpose. Comparing to the now buffed Kraks which are now excellent tool in its niche, they look like not so good option.

I think increasing bleed stacks on them slightly, to 7-8 stacks, to make sure the gunners are still reliably killed, wouldn’t hurt balance that much? It was like that before, anyway.
