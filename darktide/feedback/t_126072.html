Psyker Boss is absolutely miserable

20 Posts • 331 Views
K
Kith • 2026-09-30 06:41:44

If I had to describe this fight in three words, I would say it’s overwhelming, frustrating, and exhausting. My complaints, in no particular order:

  • I’ve fought him multiple times, know his patterns, understand the indications of spawns, recognize what builds are most effective at dealing with him (ranged damage. the answer is ranged damage) and it STILL feels like there’s no time to breathe. At all. Ever. Even the Twins, for all their pressure and area denial, occasionally backed off and gave the players a chance to regroup.

  • I know “disgustingly resilient” is Nurgle’s thing, but we’re well into slog territory. He’s got a massive health pool, he’s frequently invulnerable, and his downtimes are short.

  • The spawns are either constant or feel constant, and they frequently spit out major threats and heavily armored enemies that are difficult to put down before the boss starts attacking again.

  • Ranged attack mechanics are not clearly communicated. You can shoot down the projectiles, but you can also smack them around? Can I send them into enemies? If so, I haven’t seen much value from doing so - the adds don’t seem to care much even when I’ve managed to send a projectile directly into them.

  • Ring attacks are very poorly telegraphed. It’s also really difficult to tell when the outermost ring is the safe spot, even knowing that it’s there. This isn’t FF14, it’s not easy to see the lines on the floor because we’re fighting from a first-person perspective and there’s Maulers and Crushers and other garbage constantly being flung into our faces. On top of that, the mounds of rotting flesh that slope up into the wall make it very difficult to tell where I can actually stand.

  • The radial attack with pillars is WAY too fast. Even as an Ogryn abusing Bull Charge and the Movement Speed bonus from Stuck In, I still frequently got hit by the detonation even when I was prioritizing movement above all else - and then the next safety pillar spawned far away from me, meaning that I couldn’t hope to get there in time and just got to eat the damage. I was often getting juggled or overtaken by specials, which felt awful.

  • Windmill/moving wall attack is good, except combined with everything else it’s just another layer of pressure on top of an already chaotic fight.

  • Flying around at the end of the fight instead of staying in the middle is adding insult to injury. It’s already tough enough to find the time to damage him between repelling waves of armor and managing the incapacitating specialists and avoiding the wall and finding the safe ring and shooting down projectiles, but now we can’t even melee him? And he’s so expertly camouflaged, too - a gray and brown outfit against a background of brown and gray makes it very difficult to actually pick him out among the chaos. Heck, that’s not even touching on how all of the particles and effects and combat and everything else going on goes straight into sensory overload territory.

  • There’s no indication of what the debuffs do. I tried to open the tab to learn and there just wasn’t anything there.

SOMETHING has to be toned down. Maybe restrict spawns to poxwalkers only so we’re not drowning in Maulers and Ragers and Crushers during the boss’s damage phases, or make the boss’s attacks slower so we have more time to react, or remove his invulnerability so we can preserve the intensity but make it faster to get through. Any one of those would go a very long way towards making the encounter actually enjoyable instead of what it is now.
