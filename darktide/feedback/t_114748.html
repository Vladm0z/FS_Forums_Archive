Please save the eviscerator

The eviscerator is such an iconic 40K weapon, and the current state of the weapon is so close to being decent. While eviscerator is definitely usable in any difficulty, it needs some help before it feels good to use in higher difficulties up to Havoc 40.

  1. The Evis Mk 3 moveset has glaring bugs or jank that need to be fixed. Evis Mk 3 requires drawing out H2 for the overhead heavy to maximize special attacks. Rev L1–>H2 or rev push–>H2 are the bread and butter combos for special attacks on Mk 3, and the rev push combo is especially important in situations when using an attack to draw out H2 might accidently waste your special attack on a stray enemy. Currently, sprinting after the rev push aborts your H2 overhead and forces you into the sweeping heavy for some inexplicable reason. Similarly, push attack–>heavy currently aborts your heavy attack if you sprint after the push attack which immediately breaks your combo flow and can be disastrous in game. These moveset bugs need to be fixed or changed.

  2. Special attack damage loading on armored targets needs to be changed. Shred damage was changed to be front-loaded on nonarmored targets which feels great. The same or similar changes need to be made for armored targets. In higher difficulties, fighting melee elite packs becomes a game of “dodge dancing,” and there is often not enough opportunity to wait for your special attack to fully finish unless your are willing to take significant damage. The shred damage is so heavily backloaded on armored targets that aborting your special attack often results you dealing next to no damage to a target even if you were 80-90% through the animation. This is frustrating and feels terrible when other special attack-focused weapons like the power swords, relic blade, or even Thunder hammer don’t have the problem of completely wasting you attack if you didn’t stay in dangerous melee range long enough. Front-loading or evenly-loading the shred damage on armored targets will reward players based on how long they hold the special attack without completely wasting aborted attacks.

TL;DR Evis Mk3 has issues with attacks aborting when sprinting that need to be fixed or changed. Shred damage backloading on armored is also a terrible way to reward the special attack. Currently, aborting a special attack on armored targets feels like your attack was wasted with next to no damage done. Shred damage should be front-loaded or at least evenly-loaded on armored targets so players are still rewarded when aborting the special attack based on how long the special attack was held.

I definitely agree the rev damage seems a bit odd on mk3. In terms of shred damage being backloaded, you are already making a commitment and taking risk by revving and staying still in melee range while waiting for the shred to finish. My point isn’t to load all the shred damage instantly, but to reward players for making the initial investment and risk. It is often impossible in higher difficulties to hold the full special attack when fighting multiple melee elites without taking significant damage; you often need to dodge and abort the special attack to survive an elite horde. Aborting the special attack is very necessary and a normal part of higher difficulty gameplay, and you should be rewarded with damage proportional to how long you stayed in the attack rather than only rewarding the full attack. If the special attack is only meant to be used when fighting a one or two lone armored elites, I really can’t see the point of the weapon since you can already kill lone armored elites with regular light heavy spam. Weapons like thunder hammer or relic blade have the empowered attacks specifically for situations that can’t be handled by regular unpowered attacks, and eviscerator should do the same rather than being stuck as a niche choice in Auric and below.
