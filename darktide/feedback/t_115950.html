I’ll clarify that yes if they fix the Hyper-Critical and Hyper-Violence interaction, Rampage will get better

But even then Rampage is so massively punishing for no reason. The fact that the timer to recharge the cooldown doesn’t begin UNTIL the ability is over is already punishing enough, then you add the fact that when the ability is over, you lose ALL your stamina AND it regenerates slower for 7 FULL SECONDS is just too much

The easiest solution for this would be making Exhausted take 50% of your stamina

Also, as a QoL change, make the ability start regenrating it’s cooldown after the diminishing returns begin (most important change btw)

Now you’re REWARDED for keeping it longer with your skill, and you can start blocking at least something if you can’t make it in time, but there’s still some punishment for failing to keep it up

Now for the rocket… It’s just Bad

I get it, kraks are OP and NEED to be reverted back to 2, but Rocket really needs help

The issue is that you can’t just make it mega good IMO and that’s it, so let’s go a little wild

When I first heard about rocket launcher, I assumed it was going to be an ultimate. You know, Bounty Hunter from VT2 firing a blessed shot dealing massive damage. I want THAT feeling on the rocket launcher

The solution is hilariously simple, buff the hell out of it (x2-2.5 dmg or any significant damage buff, buff the radius by x1.5-2 but give it harsher falloff to make sure it’s not as good as an ogryn frag to clear areas), but make it a 60-70s ult. You could split some or the buffs into subnodes, whatevz, end result should be rocket launcher being a combat ability and it being extremely awesome

And I guess this is a surprise 3rd thing because well if we’re making rocket launcher an ult, we’d need 1 ult to go away and 1 new Blitz

To which I say, just move the Stimm Crate into the Blitz spot!

Remove all subnodes from it, make it regenerate every 90s (obviously 1 max), but make it so that having a stimm available is required to deploy (not allowing you to deploy it if it’s empty or you have none)

Now we got the support blitz to help the team on hard stands by buffing the living out of everyone
