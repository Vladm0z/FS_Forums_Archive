This is only semi-serious in case it wasn’t obvious from the title.

I find the discrepancies between different descriptions for Brittleness-applying effects jarring, and with the recent introduction of Uncanny strike blessing rework, there are even more consistency gaps to be observed. Let’s take a look at them.

Uncanny strike

  • Applies to: Dueling sword, Combat blade, Force sword, Sapper shovel (1st picture values) and Shivs classes of weapons (2nd picture values).

obraz560×155 31.3 KB

obraz560×177 34.3 KB

Overview:

  • “Applies” a debuff. Reads scientific - I like it, but, it may collide with another good contender for phrasing used in certain other descriptions: “give”,
  • Shortens Duration and Max stacks references compared to other descriptions - it’s great.

This is the most recent version of any Brittleness-applying blessings by far, and I still find it quite imperfect. What can be done about it?

  • Append a colon to Duration - like so: “Duration:”,
  • Do the same for “Max stacks” and move it to the beginning of its comma-separated part, so “Max stacks: 16”,
  • The end effect: “Hitting an enemy’s weakspot applies [# of stacks] stacks of [Brttleness %] Brittleness. Duration: X seconds. Max stacks: Y.”. Chef’s kiss,
    • Optionally, add “to them” after “Brittleness”, so that it’s clear that a debuff is applied to the enemy hit [in their weakspot].

Thunderous

  • Applies to: Combat axe, Assault chainaxe, Arbites shock maul, Crusher, and Thunder hammer; Bully club, Latrine shovel and Delver’s pickaxe classes of weapons.

obraz556×176 33.6 KB

Overview:

  • Uses “give” instead of “apply”. Less scientific, more brute force-y - I like it just as well,
  • Uses a complex sentence to define Duration and Max stacks of the debuff - I think this is a good approach and either it or Uncanny strike’s version could work for making other blessings unilateral in the message conveyed - depends on the designer’s whim, really.

This is almost on the level of Uncanny strike, if not better in some ways. Had we simplified some information flows, we’d have to do the following:

  • Decide on whether the phrase for applying the debuff is “apply” or “give”. Both can be good,
  • Shorten the Duration and Max stacks into singular statements, like what’s currently the case with Uncanny strike; apply formatting changes suggested to its cousin blessing.

Shattering Impact

  • Applies to: Spearhead boltgun, Plasma gun; Rumbler and Grenadier gauntlet classes of weapons

obraz562×189 37.7 KB

Overview:

  • Stacks is capitalized. Why? Neither of the previous blessings capitalizes that,
  • Specifies a “target”, not an “enemy”. Why?
  • “Receives” is inconsistent with “gives”. We do not ask enemies if they like the debuff for them to “receive” it - they just get it, nasty or not,
  • Same descriptive approach for Duration and Max stacks as Thunderous.

What should be made more consistent:

  • Decapitalize Stacks; specify “enemy” instead of “target”,
  • Fix the information flow: “On direct projectile hit of an enemy, apply/give [# of stacks] stacks of [Brittleness %] Brittleness. Duration: Xs. Max stacks: Y.”, etc.
    • The further I debunk these descriptions, the more applicable “apply” is over “give”. Frankly, shame. Maybe we could embed some inconsistency here and there after all? More brute force (melee weapons?) = “give”. More “generic” application - “apply” /j

Rending Shockwave

  • Applies to: Voidblast Force staff class of weapon.

obraz560×224 43.4 KB

Overview:

  • Ditto from Shrimpact - why is Stacks capitalized? Why “target” over “enemy”? You can’t debilitate your teammates, as much as Male Loner Psyker would probably like to…
  • Features a charge time reliance on stacks applied. What bugs me about it is it doesn’t specify whether high charge = more Brittleness applied; figuratively it suggests that’s the case, but it’s nowhere to be found in the description. I don’t think this can be fixed without embedding a lot of information in the description though, so I’ll lay it to rest,
    • There is a minor inconsistency with this and the next blessing - Armourbane. Do both rely on minimal and maximal stacks based on charge time? If yes, Rending shockwave could also specify a minimal amount.

Solutions:

  • Normalise lower case “stacks”, and “enemy” over “target”,
  • Flow - again, any format we established for length or simplicity works: “Hitting enemies with Secondary attack applies up to [# of stacks] stacks of [Brittleness %] Brittleness, scaling with charge time. Duration: Xs. Max stacks: Y.”,
  • Optionally, if there is a min stack that this blessing can apply, it could be mentioned in the description.

Armourbane

  • Applies to: Helbore lasgun classes of weapons.

obraz556×140 25.6 KB

Overview:

  • “Adds” is inconsistent phrasing with previous blessings; we want “apply” or “give”,
  • Doesn’t specify duration of the debuff or max amount of stacks, unlike previous descriptions,
  • Provides a range of values of Brittleness it can apply (min and max), which is probably what makes it unique in comparison to other similar blessings,
  • Does not specify that it only works for Ranged attacks; why? This doesn’t work on melee - and that one is charge-able, too.

Solutions:

  • Similarise this format with what’s established for Rending shockwave; mention min and max stacks, and define the conditional that it’s only for ranged attacks: “Hitting an enemy with ranged attacks applies [# min stacks]-[# max stacks] stacks of [Brittleness %] Brittleness, based on charge level. Duration: Xs. Max stacks: Y.”, etc.

Brutish Strength

  • Applies to: Ogryn skill tree

obraz693×292 54.2 KB

Overview:

  • Look, I know Ogryns have to have some descriptions follow a rather rudimentary information flow to work, but not providing any information about what the stack value (2.5%) is, nor duration (which is 5s in this case, still, since it’s the same Brittleness template as weapon blessings), nor the fact that anything is applied (following phrasing consistency), is nothing short of laughable. Positively. We can do better, however,

The cure:

  • Follow some sort of information flow: “Hitting enemies with a push applies [# of stacks] stacks of [Brittleness %] Brittleness. Duration: X seconds. Max stacks: Y.”, etc.
    • This could probably follow a less gauche phrasing than what I assembled and some words could be rearranged, but I’m sure Ogryns can parse what’s consistent with weapon blessings like Thunderous /j

I think there are like 5 unique Brittleness sources in the game that can interact with one another in such a way - and each with their own Brittleness% applications and Stack amount caps. I’d only listed ones that pertain to weapon blessings - Penetrating Flame and non-Penetrating Flame blessings that is.

Other weapon-specific sources are like… Heavy sword special, Arbites Mk8 shotgun special shell, Hive Scum’s Bonesaw purple coating. And all of these are additive with one another, but since the main definition is occupied by the “generic” blessing-originating Brittleness profile, the game won’t tell you, because there’s no framework for explaining more complexities that pertain to that. Other than player-made resources that is, like Kuli’s guides on Steam or asking people that dig the game’s code for answers.

Problem is with complexity like that occupied by one term that does include a bunch of exclusions within its functionality to support its effect in the game, there’s just no way of elegantly embedding it in the game without working up a dictionary of some sort, and listing all the exceptions there.

And the list is already technical enough where it would be kind of useless to your average player/FS hasn’t had a history of explaining the technical intricacies of their games.

And then imagine also wrangling it into the in-game UI - it’s not like the “additional stats” screen wasn’t done and thus this isn’t feasible, but with FS it’s always either working on small things like that or working on new content, and frankly, new content always takes priority.

I would like to have a more solid in-game UI that would inform me as a player of all these fun interactions, so I decided to scream of it here - it’s the least I can do.
