Intro

Ever since datatide came out I really have wanted a official version. I think it would make learning the game way easier for people, help people with crafting builds, and would require very little dev time.

Basic Version

In Psychanium add “Meat Grinder Trials”

Take a small section from a few maps (Chandelier room from Archivum Sycorax and similar), wall it off with blue force barriers. Or could even be just a custom little arena reusing mortis trial assets.

Make a few “Trial Groups” could just copy paste the test groups from datatide or come up with new ones but there should be at least ones that contain Crushers/Maulers, Ragers, a Large Hoard, and each Boss. Maybe a large mixed group as a “expert” thing.

You can select your difficulty, all the standard difficulties including a havoc 40 difficulty.

Trial groups should ideally have a timer that shows how fast you clear it after you are done.

Optionally clearing every group at havoc 40 should give you a special title like “The Meat Grinder” or something.

Larger Version

Some other ideas that could be added to this.

  • Completing trials could give very small rewards (Like 20 diamantine and 100 plasteel or something. Just for new players who are a tiny bit short on upgrading their weapons or something, I remember this being a very big annoyance to me. Harder trials could also give XP to avoid the issue of experienced players speed running lower difficulties to level up their new characters.)
  • Scaling wave mode, something like having random waves that spawn based off an amount of spawn points that scales with the difficulty (same thing as the standard game director basically).
  • Group wave mode, same as prior but you can play as duo or full squad. Im sure people would love to try to grind to insane wave levels as a true test of skill.
  • Global leaderboard with highest wave reached and fastest clear times. Could also let people view the builds on the leaderboard.

Conclusion

This is basically what I thought mortis trials would be when I first heard of them. And mortis trials are neat but this would be way better. Being able to boot up the game and just go into raw combat would be very cool, and with any kind of leaderboard system would doubtlessly massively increase player retention, people LOVE to grind leaderboards. On top of letting people practice the game, get comfortable with havoc before going into playing it, let people better refine their builds, also technically being a “solo play mode” (so people can stop asking for it) and etc. There is literally no reason this shouldn’t be in the game.
