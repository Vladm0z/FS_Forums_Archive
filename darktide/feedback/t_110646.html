Before anyone gets confused, let me clarify I absolutely 100% WANT DS to get nerfed, considerably

However, I don’t like the way they proposed to do so, and the reason is quite simple:
It’s just numbers going slightly down, instead of guiding the weapon towards a specialization. I hate the idea that this weapon would retain the generalist aspect but do it worse, It’d be absolutely a lot more fun if it could still retain its current power against something, and sacrifice something else by lots

From these changes, I hate that they’re being done alltogether

  • Finesse mod: (1.5, 3) → (1.1, 2.2) (Thrusting Heavy Attack)
  • Uncanny Strikes Blessing: 12/16/20/24% → 6/8/10/12%
  • Special Changed to Parry-Counter
  • Armor mods:
    • Unyielding: 1 → 0.75 (Thrusting Heavy Attack)

These changes in conjunction will lead the weapon in a more boring state, because it can still deal with everything, it’ll just do it slightly slower and that’s all

The bandaid needs to be taken off, Uncanny Strikes needs to go for this weapon. Armor nerfs (Unyielding should probably be brought to 0.85-0.9 instead of 0.75 tbh) like Flak and Carapace were perfect (and I might even say to go a little bit further even, 0.65 flak and 0.35 carapace mayhaps). This weapon will be SO MUCH EASIER to balance if Uncanny Strikes was no longer available for it, instead we could replace it with Flesh Tearer, to help MK2 a little more and to give people the option to invest in better anti horde with this weapon at the cost of big dmg against single targets

Regarding the Finesse change, I’m perfectly fine with it being reduced, but from 3 to 2.2 is too low, a middle ground like 2.5 or 2.6 should be a better spot. (And regarding the overhead, it should defenitely be buffed to 2.3 or 2.4, 2.1 is too little of a buff)

I’d also like to add that Agile either needs nerfing (proc on weakspot kill instead of weakspot hit) or removed from DS in favour of Brutal Momentum

Now, regarding what I didn’t address yet from the list I presented, the new special. I already made another thread regarding this, but I’d like to explain the issue better

Because Fatshark would be nerfing both finesse and damage at the same time, I have a feeling that thrust heavies would no longer hit some stagger points, making the current special attack the only way to actually apply stagger to these enemies

I obviously agree that staggering Ogryn type enemies (and maulers charging overheads) is a very silly mechanic and should not be a thing, however I’d feel it pretty lackluster if I couldn’t stagger gunners/shotgunners/ragers even with a headshot special poke

Fatshark proposed a Parry, but I don’t see myself liking it for MK4 and MK2 (I think parry would be perfect for MK5 however, the defensive long dodging mark of this weapon), because with both MK4 and MK2 I feel I should be able to dodge gunner shots and inmediatly stagger them, it’s part of the reason why I think this weapon is so fun. Keeping this wouldn’t be an issue if we go by my armor nerfs (no uncanny + flak nerf even further)

These are my thoughts, TLDR: Remove Uncanny (And Agile) and replace it with Flesh Tearer (and Brutal Momentum), Nerf flak+cara modifier even further, Keep current special for MK2/4 but add new parry for MK5, Readjust the Finesse nerf for thrust and buff for Overhead to be more reasonable with the previously mentioned changes
