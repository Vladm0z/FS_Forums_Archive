Now we have people insta killing crowds of 20 ragers, 10 maulers, 3 crushers in seconds and a psyker isn’t even on the team.

Uncanny strike was not the biggest problem. Infinite crowd control would have been a way more important thing to target and fix, though even that wouldn’t solve anything. The powercreep is so insane and auric maelstrom is easy - havoc isn’t fun or fair, not that it could even be a solution to the core game being so easy. Nothing is a threat anymore unless you get surrounded. If there is even 1 spot of cover or a box on the ground, you can kill an infinite number of enemies with meta builds. If there’s a chokepoint you may as well just turn the screen off cuz all you need to do is dodge and spam attack in 1 direction.

If poxbursters made sound, if trappers couldn’t get you around impossible corners and through massive crowds - I would never lose. I have played many quickplay games over the months. What makes us lose? It’s consistently bursters making 0 sound and exploding right in our face. I understand dying to a burster is annoying, but it’s not the end of the world. The thing that bothers me is that I am only ever dying from this stupid bug - not only is that more proof the game is easy, cuz literally nothing else can kill me, but this game is in the worst state that it’s ever been in. Connection errors every play session, quickplay broken for months - I and many others already know not to expect to hear specials, and that fatshark will never bother to fix it.

The reason nobody plays Ogryn anymore is because what is the point of crowd control if everything is instakilled? You have all the classes running ahead because they’re way faster - instakilling everything and the ogryn is like - “huh, unless I use these 2-3 weapons I barely have any impact on the game at all.” All while moving slow, being a bigger target, doing less damage, attacking so slow like xanax is in the corpse starch.

I dont even use mods anymore, because I really don’t care enough about Darktide to update mods at this point, but every time I play the game - I remember how necessary mods are. It is truly a pain to not use mods, I feel bad for console players.

Clearly you can fix abilities, weapon changing, blitz, etc all failing to register - there are mods that fix all of these issues and more including the crosshair not working correctly. I could list many more bugs that are fixed by mods, but it isn’t just “bugs” that’re being fixed. These are huge friction points in the game that annoy everyone who actually loves this game.

Why do we need to download a mod to make the UI usable? Wow, it must be toxic if I know how much ammo my teammate has - so I can know if I can leave them ammo or not? For real though, what is the reason that you cannot see your teammates’ ammo after 3 years? Would it lead to “toxicity”? I seriously cannot think of any other reason to withhold this kind of info.

The four-color system for reading how much ammo your teammate has is incredibly stupid. someone can have almost no ammo and be yellow, shoot one shot and be orange. Why is white seemingly 100% - 80% ammo, while yellow is like 80%-15% ammo - and orange means you will probably run out of ammo in the next fight.

Only with mods can see what their special bar is at - and give them a stim at the right moment. That reminds me, having a readable UI for the hive scum stim so you don’t have to stare at the bottom corner of your screen and still not know if you have your stim or not? It makes no sense, you’ve literally patched the hive scum like 5 times at this point, and we still can’t tell if our stim is available. Apparently, it isn’t seen as an issue even though the stimcooldown mod has been downloaded over 36 thousand times. Wow, those 36 thousand cheaters - just can’t play fairly huh. It’s definitely cheating. Fatshark intended you shouldn’t be able to know when your ability is ready after all.

Fatshark - just blatantly copy the mod. I understand when the mods first came out like you can’t just do these things immediately. It’s been over 3 years. Just let me see how much ammo my teammate has - please. I’m begging you. You’ve literally patched the hive scum like 5 times at this point and we still can’t tell if our stim is available or not

I can mention solo and just say nothing else - but modders truly did it all. Not only can you go in solo, but you can equip your team with whatever loadout you want. The bots in this game are so hilariously bad by the way. Idk why fatshark even thinks it helps to add bots to your game. It is at the point now where they stand over your netted corpse running in circles - it has never been this bad.

Chat block? 3 years? Anyone? Must be a monumental - near Sisyphean task to implement into Darktide. Who knows what sort of chaos magic modders used - but they aren’t to be trusted. Also, ignore everyone who asked for chat block. I can’t even say no, and the help button is gone! or wait… it was never there to begin with!!!”

modders also did the impossible task of allowing you to have a loadout system that makes sense and look nice - not these silly icons that mean nothing.
