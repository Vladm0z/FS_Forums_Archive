Djaresh • 2026-07-30 11:47:16

As a chainsword main I know what you mean but it’s not really a bug but more an unfortunate side effect of how they work. The camera always centers to the point where your chainweapon is caught in the enemy’s body. If the enemy for instance charges a diagonal strike where he puts his right hand over his left shoulder and you hit that hand, it will shift your camera with that hand going down from his stagger animation.

The other thing where you may turn around completely all of the sudden is when an enemy you don’t even see is standing close to the side or behind you and one of their arms is going through your face just as you where striking your weapon and their arm got hit, causing you to turn completely around towards them. This however is rather rare and requires you to basically touch an enemy with your back or side.

The looking on the floor part is probably when you swing a vertical attack while missing the entire body of the enemy but hit it’s feet, or another enemy that is laying on the ground from a hard stagger, like explosions or Ogryn bull rush.

As for a solution, the only thing that would come to mind is that the attacks that get stuck only register towards the middle of the screen, which would probably cause more people to complain about chainweapons than the camera shift thing.
