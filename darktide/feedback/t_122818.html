I haven’t maxed out my Skitarius yet but I think I have a good enough impression of the Class and it’s stuff.

Overall I like the class, even if I’m not the best at it. I don’t know if I’m rusty or what but I’ve been going down more than usual. Could be I don’t have enough points to get all the Toughness nodes or I’m too much of a dingbat to get one of the Auras. The Abilities are pretty cool and the blitzes are good. From what I’ve seen the Artificer Servo-Skull gets glazed the most but the Arc Grenade cleans house and the Shield might come in handy, I haven’t used the Artificer Skull that much so I can’t comment on it much.

I like the weapons but a good chunk of them feel like they have a steeper learning curve than the other weapons, the Mechanicus Power Sword especially. I don’t know why but having the special action be the left-click combos just fries my brain. I also keep whiffing with the Phosphor Blast Pistol, which is either a problem of the shots sometimes phasing through enemies, that phantom shot thing that happens sometimes, or a skill issue so catastrophic that my mind refuses to cogitate it. The Arc Rifle might have an ammo issue.

Now for the chinks in the armor, starting with the armor the Skitarii starts with. Like the Hive Scum they get four sets of cosmetics for each Forge World when they start, but despite them being unlocked I can’t equip them. I can equip the sets unlocked from penances but the base cosmetics just make the load out of the yellow exclamation thing where it says “validate load out”. This feels like a bug, and if it is it needs patched.

The voice effect thing in the character creation section is a great addition but it’s unclear what the sliders do. I think I know what “binharic compatibility” does but everyone else confuses me. I could be stupid but I saw someone else share the sentiment.

Lastly is not really Skitarii related but it’s a long term problem I see on the horizon. DLC stuff is cool; albeit very close to PTW; but you have to cater to the people who don’t want to spend more money for one reason or another. We need free weapons, maps, and levels, otherwise we’ll reach critical mass and sink like Helldivers. And I mean new weapon families, marks are cool but that only lasts for so long. If need be angle it in a way so that Tencent starts drooling over the idea of selling more Commodore skins to us, assuming they are the ones stopping you from adding more free weapons. I really like this game and I don’t want it to buckle under it’s own weight.

Oh and the Vanguards are… enemies. I think they’re too tanky for common enemies but shield guys are decent enough enemies.
