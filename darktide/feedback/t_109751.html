RTO • 2025-06-26 07:45:27

It’s not something mysterious. Here is what happens in a programming sense from a software engineer perspective.
All numbers below are assumptions to demonstrate how it works.

  • Let’s say stagger resistance 100 = immune to stagger
  • Normal state (when they walk to the target): Poxbursters has 90 (leaving 10 for abilities that can almost always cc anything).
  • Before explode state: Roughly 2 seconds before explosion stagger resistance becomes 10 and most things will stagger or push it (eg. push action or attack or range weapon with stagger power over 10).

The problem is that Arbites dogs walk around with default stagger power of 11. So when Bursters are in “before explode” state, it will automatically bounce off the dog if they make a contact (whatever the hit box is).

I don’t know if the dogs have stagger power no matter their states but if they don’t, they sure become the state where they have stagger power super often.

And judging from the footage by @Harridas , it seems the dog has stagger power even in idle state. That’s messed up.
