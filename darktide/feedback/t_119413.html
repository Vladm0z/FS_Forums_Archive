I agree with the sentiment, but I may have misunderstood the first part. What shotgun shoots acid on Vermintide 2?

In any case, the Heroic Weapon variants in Space Marine 2 makes sense for the game story/lore since relic weapons and xenotech weapons are indeed used by some Space Marine chapters.

However, relic or xenos weapons would not make sense in the story of Darktide as we are rejects being thrown en masse at an Inquisition problem. We’re essentially expendable ground units.

Granted the player characters may be the strongest characters that can’t seem to die and keep surviving the most difficult missions Rannick sends us on (especially since Havoc missions are canon in-lore hard missions based on Morrow and Dukane’s Mortis Trials conversation), but we are still considered expendable fighters.

We canonically use refitted and jerry-rigged weapons from dead rejects that came before us. Hadron even says this in one of her weapon upgrade lines.

Both the Inquisition and the Adeptus Mechanicus representatives on the ship will never let us go down into Tertium to die and lose a relic/xeno weapon; much less even let us touch it in the first place.

The closest we could probably have are special mods added to the weapons that lets it have unique effects, but that’s already how the Marks system works.
