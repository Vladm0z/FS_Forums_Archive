This has been the biggest annoyance in the game for me since launch and only gets worse every time there is a big player spike. Why is this game server authoritative? Now I don’t actually know exactly how darktide servers work but it sure does feel server authoritative and I see absolutely no reason for this.

Ever get hit mid dodge? Ever get teleported back? Ever have your inputs just not happen? All of that is due to the game being server authoritative and there is absolutely no benefit to this. So what are we afraid that people are going to hack and have invincibility cheats or something? Who cares its a co-op game, its not like it will hurt the other player’s experience that much compared to the horrible server authoritative issues.

Just make it full client authoritative. If I don’t get hit on my screen I don’t get hit, if I shoot on my screen I shoot. Will this make hacking extremely easy? Yes. Will this make the combat feel about 10,000x better? YES. Sure the servers are often fine but why do I need to deal with stutters and teleporting and all of that at all? Server issues should only ever appear in minor desyncs that don’t actually matter at all where a enemy suddenly dies or a team mate teleports a bit or something, these wouldn’t seriously effect gameplay compared to the way bigger issues with the server authoritative system.
