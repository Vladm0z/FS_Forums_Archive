Yup. It’s slob and it clearly only targets new players, as long term players don’t need resources, and the cosmetic rewards are beyond pathetic. So they only seem to care about bringing new people in as of now, the ones that haven’t yet got tired of stale, static content not updated in months, and haven’t yet sunk their money in cosmetics shop.

Events normally are part of game expansion plan that both keep people engaged between major updates and improve immersion by creating an illusion of being part of the story. Here how it would work with events serving this goal in DT:

  1. There is some global campaign map with some community objectives, short term and long term. When they are reached (or not), a trigger is activated.

  2. Each trigger makes FS to reflect those results in one of their next events:

    • Short term goals events would be similar to what we have now, just themed accordingly (if we recaptured certain region on the map from some heretics regiment, the event is about clearing it from the rest of heretics of that regiment; so close to the fire or explosion events, i.e. fighting some unusual foes en masse etc)

    • Long term goals events also advance story significantly, they are more rare and more elaborate in design. For example, we need to do additional searches for samples (with sample scanners) during missions on the bodies of certain foes (quick enough or they will decompose), or use collective efforts to capture specific foes alive before they flee. At the end of the event story is progressed towards some culmination (with outro video or some sitrep issued by high command) that comes with next major update.

  3. ALL those events must still offer real rewards for all players, new and old. Not stupid frames or insignias, at least it must be new haircuts, faces, tattoos, titles, weapon and armor recolors etc - something that others can clearly see all the time, the rest has close to zero value

You can see for yourself how far FS are from that healthy model. It’s not even ground level, it’s a basement.

This model can further be expanded, to cover additional niches, like QA. Let’s suppose a new weapon or bless is released in a major update. Then event is started at the same time, with HQ assigning you task for field testing it, with objectives be various types of foes killed with it, number of attacks deflected, weak spot hits made, dps threshold reached etc. Thus players are incentivized to try all aspects of it, so that issues could be noticed faster and reported.

Or to provide new game modes even. Like, during a major event that is about capturing an enemy stronghold, temporarily and for this event’s missions only, instead of being captured upon death you will just select another your operative and deploy to the same ongoing mission immediately, spawning near your team (the number of deployment attempts would still be limited to 2-3). That would make it feel more like a large offensive operation where new troops are deployed to battlefield constantly.
