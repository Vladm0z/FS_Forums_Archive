I like what FS have done to it so far. Merging the +30% damage buff into the main node was a no-brainer, a niche underperforming Ability having 4 subnodes before that was just mental. But it still has 3 subnodes while VoC, the best Ability of the veteran out of them all, without doubt, has just 2. That’s a bit too much tax for what it does, don’t you think?

Suggestion: make Overwatch sub-talent part of the main node too. So, basically, from now on Infiltrate always has 60s CD, and 2 charges.

I mean, does anybody choose to run Infiltrate with only one charge willfully, and not because they can’t afford to spend a talent point there? IMO, having 2 charges trumps somewhat shorter CD by far, in this case.

This also would further refine its identity, in comparison to Zealot’s Shroud. Zealot’s shroud would be all about damage dealing, a single devastating hit. While veteran’s Infiltrate is more about utility, doing various things (assisting, ressing, saving teammates, pulling levers, pressing buttons, solving puzzles while stealthed, deploying grenades in a middle of a hoard etc) - thus it would benefit from having the extra charge all the time.

And it would free one talent point in many Infiltrate builds to spend on something usefull, those builds are far from being powerful, they could use a bit of help like that.
