Really pleased to see the upcoming changes post and I hope to see more of it. This kind of feedback post is something I want to make once a month. Don’t have a lot of notes on the balancing you’re doing itself other than “it’s good”, although I’m cautious about the Soulblaze compensation buff - don’t overdo it.

For this post, I want to think about some further Hive Scum nerfs to make. Reducing the capacity of Boom Bringer and Chem nades and reducing the crit mod of Shivs is a good start, but I think more needs to be done.

For starters, Rampage: either needs the strength of all of its bonuses halved, its cooldown doubled, or Exhaustion brought back. It’s just too active and free for the level of power you get during the install. Basically any enemy is a complete joke to tear through with Rampage on.

Secondly, keystones: any HS player worth their salt only ever takes Dependency. The other two give decent buffs (maybe not Vulture’s Mark) but extra CDR and crit on a class that revolves even more around both of those things is too good not to use. I don’t know what I’d do here. Maybe replace the CDR and reduce the bonus crit chance per stack to 2.5%?

Thirdly, a few outlier talents: Burst of Energy, Hive City Brawler, Hyperviolent, Jittery, and Street Tough are all absurdly strong defensive and offensive bonuses. HCB lets you survive crusher overheads from full health, I don’t even use Street Tough but it’s literally just a better Zealot (the melee TANK class?) talent, BoE is a get out of chip damage free card every 20 seconds, Hyperviolent can scale to the point you one shot bosses, and Jittery makes you functionally immortal with how good dodging is as your primary defensive mechanic in this game. I don’t know what I’d do to balance these. Maybe you lot in the comments can help out.

The rest of the tree I would argue is decently balanced overall. That’s all for this post.
