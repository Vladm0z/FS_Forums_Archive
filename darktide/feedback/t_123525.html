Dear Fatshark Team,

I am writing this feedback because I have a deep love for the Warhammer universe in general, and especially for Warhammer 40,000: Darktide. In my opinion, Darktide is one of the best horde-based action shooters I have ever played, and I have spent a considerable amount of time both playing Darktide and enjoying many other Warhammer titles over the years.

After countless hours in the game and many discussions with friends who also play it, there are two aspects that we consistently feel are missing.

The first is the absence of an offline mode.

The second is the inability to play entirely solo with AI bots. While the game does include bots under certain circumstances, there is currently no dedicated way to enjoy the full experience alone with a team of AI companions.

One of my biggest concerns regarding games that I truly love is the possibility that, one day, their servers may eventually be shut down. The thought of losing access to all the progress, characters, equipment, cosmetics, and memories I have accumulated over the years is honestly quite discouraging.

I have recommended Darktide to several friends because I genuinely believe it is an amazing game. When discussing this topic with them, we all came to a similar conclusion: one of the things that would make us significantly more willing to purchase cosmetics, skins, and other premium content would be knowing that the game could still be enjoyed in the future through an offline mode, even if online support were ever discontinued.

For example, I find myself more comfortable purchasing cosmetic content in other Warhammer titles (space marine 2) when I know that my investment will remain accessible regardless of the status of online servers. Many of my friends feel the same way.

I have played a large number of Warhammer games over the years, including first-person and third-person titles, as well as games such as Warhammer: Chaosbane and many others. However, Darktide is one of the games I care about the most, which is precisely why this issue matters so much to me.

I know that I am not the only player who feels this way. I have seen numerous discussions within the community where players express their desire for an offline mode and for the ability to launch missions entirely with bots. In addition to helping preserve the game for the future, such features would also allow players to avoid negative matchmaking experiences, such as teammates who intentionally disrupt runs or consume all available resources without cooperating with the rest of the team.

For me personally, and for several of my friends, the existence of an offline mode and a true solo-with-bots option would increase our confidence in supporting the game financially through cosmetic purchases, because we would know that the content we acquire will remain available to us in the long term.

For these reasons, I would like to respectfully ask you to consider implementing an offline mode in the future, as well as a fully supported solo experience with AI bots. Even a simple offline option would mean a great deal to players like me who care deeply about preserving access to a game they love.

Thank you very much for all the incredible work you have done on Warhammer 40,000: Darktide. It is clear how much passion has gone into creating this experience, and I sincerely hope you will consider this feedback for future updates.

With appreciation and best regards.
