Who are the Spire-Born? They are citizens of the Upper Hive-city areas and spires, the lesser nobles of Tertium. As to why they are now slumming it with us? By now many of us have progressed through the game enough to know that Rannick and Morrow have been wrangling quite a bit with the ruling families. So now they have decided to take a more direct approach to the heretic uprising since Rannick as refused (for now) to order entire districts purged, and thus are sending some of their lesser kin in to help influence the wars outcome. Why the sudden assistance? Perhaps they are merely impatient with waiting, perhaps they want to avert suspicion from their houses, or maybe they simply want to get rid of some their more “inconvinent” family members.

In any case, the role that this class would play would very much be the support role. The abilities and weapons that would available to them would of a more refined order, since after all, if got money, you can get yourself some of the good stuff. First ability that I have thought of for this class is the ability to deploy a Cyber hawk. The best way to describe its behavior would be to compare to Bloodwing from the first Borderlands game. Second ability would be to deploy your own personal forcefield, the DT version of Saltzpyre’s warrior priest shield of faith. And the third ability would be to deploy a Cybernetic cherub to provide a quick heal of a wound or even a revival of a teammate that has managed to get well beyond the reach of your standard med stims or abilities (we are looking at you speedrunners).

As for what unique weapons the Spire-Born would get, Plasma and Inferno pistols would be a good start, as well as that fancy revolver that Rannick has. Don’t know if we can have that double barrel that Commissar Yarrick is famous for. As for melee weapons, next to whatever is already in-game, battle claws and rapiers would seem the best bet. Not sure what to add for Blitzes, but I’m sure the Devs can handle it.

Now this just a rough outline of an idea, so apologies if any of this clashes with the official lore unacceptably. If I did, please feel free to point it out and perhaps suggest something better.

As to how these would function, either it could very briefly make all your range shots target the head, or just garunteed crits. One thing about the first two blitzes I listed, they would automatically cease to function if you exceed a certain distance from where you place them, to prevent abuse.
