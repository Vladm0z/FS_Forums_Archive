Is there ever going to be a gamepad support rework?

I’m going to preface this by saying I am well aware that mouse and keyboard is superior. But even still, the game is on Xbox, and for many personal reasons ranging from physical ability to preference, it can be played with a gamepad on PC. I’m in the latter camp, due to repeated wrist injuries, I am incapable of using a mouse in the manner Darktide expects, it is extremely painful.

To be blunt, and I’m not trying to sound harsh or facetious, but I genuinely believe Darktide has some of the worst gamepad support I’ve ever experienced in a modern video game. There are FPS that came out on console over two decades ago that feel far more fluid and responsive.

It’s fine to have preset controller layouts, but why is there no option to natively remap the controls without external software? Why not allow people to make their own custom bindings within the game so even if none of the presets are to their taste, they can make their own? PC players can get around this, but Xbox players can’t and would greatly benefit from this feature. The position of your Blitz, Ability, mark, dodge and crouch are all vital, and heavily opinionated on where they go.

Why doesn’t the aim acceleration activate on diagonal right stick inputs? I’ve seen reports on this as far back as release, and it’s never been addressed or fixed. You get full sensitivity when looking vertically or horizontally, but the moment you dare touch a diagonal input, your sensitivity plummets to snail speeds. I can’t recall ever playing another FPS game where this happens, it’s so alien and half the reason the game feels so awful on gamepad. Diagonal inputs happen a lot more than you think.

In regards to aim assist, why choose a sticky crosshair over bullet magnetism? Now, let’s be clear, aim assist of any kind exists to make up for the inherent deficiencies on the lack of precision inherent in a thumbstick, and the game already has this feature. The problem is I think the assist chosen is arguably the worst of the two for the kind of game that Darktide is. A sticky crosshair does not play nice when your number of targets is condensed into massive hordes and you’re expected to turn rapidly to precision-shoot a priority target, because the crosshair gets stuck on every single target it crosses paths with, dragging your turn speed down with it. Whereas bullet magnetism is the different kind, it instead applies a soft Matrix-tier curve correction so the projectiles will still hit the target even if the crosshair is skirting the silhouette of said target. Basically a little handicap because precision shooting on sticks is infinitely harder than dome clicking with a mouse. This point is going to be heated, I can already feel it, but I believe it would be a much better option for gamepad players over the sticky crosshair, a feature so bad that most people recommend to just turn it off and deal with the alternative.

So yeah, that’s my feedback. I wish something would happen for gamepads, because honestly, this isn’t even one of those games where I’d use a gamepad out of casual preference outside of my poor quality wrists, the base experience with them is just awful. It makes me hate playing this game sometimes.
