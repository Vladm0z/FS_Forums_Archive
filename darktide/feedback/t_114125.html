Went back to the Relic Blade & FGS recently and forgot how awesome they are, due in part because of their extended movesets with sliding & sprinting heavies, lights & combos. I figured the Power Falchion, being the latest addition to the arsenal, would’ve followed the same design trend started by Pickaxes, Relic Blades, and the FGS by embracing attacks from sprint, slide and hidden moves. It’s a pretty big bummer that FS has seemingly reneged on their weapon design trend that featured more expressive and fluid movesets in favor of the staler ones akin to those from the beginning of the game. I hope Fatshark will get more creative & add more options to upcoming weapons like they had been doing instead of reversing course like they’re seemingly doing. Shame Falchion missed out, as these moves really help the Relic Blade, FGS, and Pickaxes be some of the most enjoyable to wield. However long we waited wasn’t apparently low enough for FS to make a melee for Veteran that is half as fun or engaging to use as Pickaxes, Relic Blades or FGS.
