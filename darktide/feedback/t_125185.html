Hi, I have completed a run playing Auric Endless Hordes true solo while using a mod to become Blind & Deaf.
Content Warning: My voice + music (version w/o microphone audio & music or w/o mic but with music)

Additional links & info in video description

I believe this is something that should not even be possible (on any difficulty other than the beginner’s), however, this game has been terribly powercrept to an extreme degree. Most enemies in the game are unable to present a significant threat resembling anything that could force you to respect them (in the FG sense). In a 4 player setting, any challenge in the normal difficulties has been minimized so much as to make the game quite boring to play even though the game is intended to be a 4 player co-op game.

The game’s existing mechanics and player power have changed a lot since launch and even moreso compared to previous Tide games. And almost every other Darktide patch has been almost exclusively buffs to player power that over time has resulted in all enemy power being weakened and trivialized. I know it’s unlikely for Fatshark to even attempt to appeal to my desire for this game. At the very least I’d like for Darktide to have 2 additional difficulties added beyond Auric. Please let us have this at least, I want to the game to have difficulties that strongly enforce a 4 player group to work together to overcome the enemies they face instead of the current state where I can basically play a singleplayer game next to some strangers.

If I may write my ideal scenario: I would want to the game to drastically change so as to make every enemy type in the game a unique threat necessitating a different response for each enemy in moment-to-moment gameplay. I want for the presence of a single elite in a horde to be strong enough that I’m forced to respect them with the horde around them if I’m without team mates to help. With Darktide being a 4 player co-op game, it needs to be stated that player power increases exponentially with the number of players and that the danger of the enemies should be built with that in mind, too much of Darktide currently has accommodated the player’s ability to handle everything by themselves.

Some of my specific wants (I accidentally pressed enter and posted this too early before I finished my thoughts, why does pressing enter post?):

  • Getting hit should be important and carry consequence. This could be done by significantly nerfing different sources of toughness replenishment and increasing enemy damage.
  • Player mobility nerfed significantly, both dodging and sprinting. Disengaging enemies for long periods should be difficult and should necessitate use of other defensive mechanics such as stagger & block.
  • Enemy presence and armor should matter, players should not be able to just mash left-click through mixed hordes w/ elites. Addressing this is very complicated and would require at least nerfing player tools such as infinite cleave, rending/brittleness, blessings and adjusting cleave, mass & ADM values.
  • Nerf high damage player tools to require more investment to create a noticeable & felt opportunity cost, killing specific enemies fast should cost you. You shouldn’t be able to do everything by yourself.

But in the end, I’ll still be glad if Fatshark adds some new difficulties to the game instead of all this extra work making huge changes to the core game. I know this stuff is unrealistic.
