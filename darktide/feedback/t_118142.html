I think DS gets targeted a lot because it is the “whole package” so to speak. It’s not alone in damage output, but it does also get to be one of the most mobile weapons in the game AND have the possibility of uncanny dot abuse at the same time. Of course in a coordinated team you might prefer to just take power sword for better overall DPS but it’s hard to argue the use cases are as broad as DS.

Not trying to make any categorical claims here, that just seems to me likely why DS gets singled out a lot in these conversations.
