I’m the proud owner of the arbites dlc etc. etc. and, beyond just the insanely good weapons it has access to, I was starting to feel like, maybe Arbites are just fondamentally better than the other classes ?

How surprised was I when I realized this was arguably true !

Here are the Arbites base stats (no talents, no nodes) vs Veteran (green) and Zealot (orange).

image1039×1223 156 KB

The numbers don’t lie (if they’re correct. Which I must assume, it’s been some time since release of the new class). Among other things, baseline Arbites have better crit chance, the Vet’s base toughness, and the Zealot’s base health and base stamina. The rest of the stamina values are kind of in-between both classes.

With talents, Zealots can generally get +45 to +60 base toughness, using 3 to 4 nodes, putting them at 115-130.
Veterans can get up to +65, putting them at 165 base toughness.
Arbites get +50 base toughness, however they’re mandatory nodes, no diverting necessary, and each of those is worth +25, instead of +15 like most nodes the other classes get.

Zealots and Veterans get some more stuff, like +1 stam, or Toughness DMR, or 5% crit chance or mvmt speed. But all of these EACH cost one whole talent point.

Tl;dr : the Arbites’ stats are fundamentally better than the other classes’, not taking the talent tree into account. AND when taking the trees into account, the Zealot and Veteran do catch up to the Arbites in terms of raw numbers, but it costs them a lot of Talent points to do so.
