Havoc Improvement Proposal

This is based on the idea that Havoc should not be a mode meant only for a small group of ultra-skilled players, but one that includes the difficulty level of the old Auric and covers the broader group of high-level players.

1. Tiers

Havoc does not need 40 tiers. Especially tiers 1 to 15 are roughly the same or even easier than Auric or Maelstrom, making their existence meaningless.
Like standard missions, Havoc should be simplified to five clear difficulty levels. This would make the differences in challenge more understandable and make Party Finder usage much easier.
For example, something around Auric Maelstrom could be difficulty level 1, and the current Havoc 40 could be level 5.

2. Party Finder Matching

The current process of joining a Havoc party through the Party Finder takes too long and often ends without getting to play at all. This needs to be addressed.
Party leaders, especially at higher tiers, care a lot about party composition and aim to increase their chances of success by selecting strong players. In particular, key classes like bubble Psykers and Chorus Zealots are often in high demand.
When a leader has to manually screen out players who don’t meet the desired class or clearance level, it wastes time for both the leader and the applicants.

To solve this, party leaders should be able to specify the required class and clearance level (from 1 to 5) when recruiting for Havoc.
Then, players wishing to join would automatically be matched into a party that fits their current class and clearance level.

3. Combat Flow and Suppression

Some enemies at high Havoc levels, such as in Havoc 40, take too long to defeat, which slows down the game’s pacing.
With the reduced amount of available ammo, many ranged weapons like the Infantry Autogun or Braced Autogun become much less practical.
On top of that, suppression makes it nearly impossible to hit enemies even when aiming accurately at mid-range.
As a result, full-auto weapons that require sustained fire are clearly at a disadvantage, and players tend to choose high-damage-per-shot weapons like revolvers, boltguns, plasma rifles, or shotguns instead.

Suppression no longer adds meaningful value to the game.
At the very least, elite gunners and specialists that appear in large numbers should be easier to take down.
