Dear Fatshark Team,

I wanted to provide some feedback regarding the recent Skitarii DLC release and the current state of the content.

I purchased the DLC because I was excited to support Darktide and experience the new class and progression systems. However, I have been left frustrated by several issues that significantly affect the experience and, in my opinion, should be considered high priority.

The first issue concerns the Voltaic Emitter talent and its associated penance. The talent currently appears to behave inconsistently, resulting in the penance being extremely difficult or unreliable to complete. As many of the DLC cosmetics are tied to penance progression, this effectively leaves a large portion of the cosmetic rewards inaccessible through no fault of the player.

A balance issue is understandable, but progression blockers affecting paid content are particularly frustrating as they leave players feeling unable to access content they have already paid for.

The second issue concerns the new shield enemies introduced with the update. In their current form they feel excessively durable for what appears to be intended as a standard infantry or horde unit. Rather than creating interesting tactical choices, they often slow combat to a crawl and make horde engagements feel like a slog rather than the fast-paced, fluid combat experience that Darktide normally excels at.

Many players enjoy Darktide because of the intensity and flow of combat. Large numbers of highly durable shield enemies interrupt that flow and, in my experience, reduce the enjoyment of missions rather than adding challenge.

What has amplified frustration within the community is the perception that these issues were identified almost immediately after release, yet the update arrived immediately before a prolonged holiday period during which fixes and communication appear likely to be delayed.

I fully appreciate that developers deserve holidays and time away from work. However, releasing major paid content shortly before a period where rapid support and hotfixes may not be available risks leaving players feeling as though they are acting as unpaid testers for unfinished content.

Darktide remains one of my favourite cooperative games and I continue to support it because the core gameplay is exceptional. I hope these issues can be addressed quickly so that the Skitarii DLC can live up to its potential and players can enjoy the content as intended.

Thank you for your time and for continuing to support Darktide.

Kind regards,

Bozlightyear
