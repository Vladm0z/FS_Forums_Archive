lw88 • 2025-12-18 04:08:56

This isn’t gameplay feedback. It’s in the wrong category.

First thing - disable the animation events mod if you have it for scoreboard.

That said - what’s your specs? (cpu, gpu, ram?) and what resolution are you running the game at?

Set everything under advanced graphics to lowest or off.
All lighting off. All blood off. Depth of field off.
I mean it - everything off or lowest. Everything.

going up from advanced graphics -

Ray tracing and global illumination off.

sharpness filter 0.00

Then up to display for now - set your field of view where you like it, resolution to your monitor’s native resolution, screen mode to fullscreen, vsync off

If after this you’re still under 144 frames or so (an ok smooth amount) standing in the hub at mission selection, enable DLSS or FSR and set it to ultra performance.

It’s not as responsive as playing without that tech enabled, but I found moving from 1080p to 1440p on older hardware (13600kf + 2080 super) I had to enable DLSS or my game became a slideshow. I’m currently testing nvidia reflex low latency to see which is better in darktide, and at the moment I think I’m leaning toward off at the framerates I get (standing right up against the mission terminal with no players behind it looking at the green hologram of the hive city is like 160 fps) but at lower frame rates it could change.
