Regardless of what bots are doing, if the only player is 30 yards away, they should break and follow.

I KNOW this is a multiplayer game. This isn’t news to me. Sometimes quickplay doesn’t fill your team, particularly in Expeditions, where many players don’t have access to later grid points. The queue for later points is already thin at the best of times, now try in a weird difficulty or at a bad hour. The bots are in the game, they should be useful for something, anything.
