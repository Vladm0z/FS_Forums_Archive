Just like the title states, just a few ideas to further enhance the latest game mode that everyone is griping about right now.

First suggestion? More unique modifiers. Sandstorm and lightning storm are the two I got (not to be confused with the sand vortexes modifier).

Sandstorm modifier would essentially be the vent purge from standard mode but set up for expeditions. Bad news? It would even harder to see through than vent purge AND would increase background noise a bit, requiring you to pay attention for audio cues. The good news? It would act very similar to Vets smoke grenade and force most enemies to close to a certain distance before they can try to shoot you (beware those snipers), also you can walk right past patrols without them noticing you.

As for Lightning storm, basically it would be bolts of lightning randomly raining down out on any exposed part of the map. You get hit, you stunned hard and damaged if not outright downed. Any upsides? One, thanks to Hadron’s efforts, she has rigged a device that will predict where a lightning strike is about to hit, so that you can try to avoid it. And two, lightning bolts do not discriminate between friend or foe, so if an enemy just happens to lured right into a lightning strike zone, well…

Second suggestion for Expeditions: lore content. We know for a fact that all these tech remnants are a build up for the next class to be revealed, but it would also be fun if we got hidden pieces of Atoma’s history as we gather them up. After all, Hestia keeps going on about how Baross is a false hero, but we don’t get specific details. Could also add special relics for us to find that might be of interest to various members of the Mourningstar, such as an ancient lost astartes helmet for example, as well as other various trinkets.

Final suggestion I have to improve expeditions (for now) are dynamic events. Right now, all we really do is wander about from zone to zone collecting scraps and what not, but what if we added a 40% chance of some random temporary event occurring? It would not stay for very long, so it would be up to you to decide whether or not it would be worth taking the extra time to pursue it. What would these events be? Could be some arch-heretic crossing zone, could be one of the deadsiders needing a quick rescue. They would be risky, but rewarding.
