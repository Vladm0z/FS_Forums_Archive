i think this is an interesting question. do yall think dt would benefit from a kill-share system, where if you deal a significant chunk of an elite/specialist’s hp (ex; 40%) but don’t get the last hit, you still get credit & proc off it? or would that be too busted?

i feel like this could potentially be a good way to alleviate the problem with builds that are overly powerful that dominate lobbies (soulblaze stacking, arbites shotgun, scum dual-autos as examples) by still allowing other players to proc off certain talents if they’re contributing a large amount of dps i think weapon blessings should be exempt from procs, probably and not have their output be reduced, without absolutely nerfing the aforementioned builds straight into the dirt, but i can also see that it could very well skyrocket player power & make the game easier as a result, at least not without changing enemy spawns/stat values a lot to compensate.

on the other hand, this would need to exempt keystones like blazing piety where you get stacks just from enemy kills in coherency (even without damaging them) in order to prevent double procs.

as an ending note, this could probably help bring both veteran & zealot more up to snuff with the other classes by letting them proc their talents reliant on elite kills, who on average have easier access & less effort invested into putting out large amounts of dps in comparison while being able to retain less strict loadout variety, without sacrificing much in the way of viability.

edit: exclude abilities with timer extensions (ex; exe stance) & soulblaze/flamer, high damage DoT spreads from benefiting, that would be stupidly strong

any thoughts on this?
