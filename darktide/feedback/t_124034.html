Been a while since I saw any mention of Telekine Shield, so I want to revive the topic to hear from others about the current state of the ability and share my own idea.

Darktide has increasingly become a move or die type of game. Waves of armored enemies can push you back and split the team, while gunner packs can stop the team from moving forward. Shield is a static object and has a niche use VS gunners. You mostly won’t get the full benefit from it because your team gets pushed out of it or runs past it to deal with said gunners.

My question is, should both forms of Telekine Shield, travel with the Psyker (Dome shield moving around and covering the team as they advance on gunners) and fallow the aim reticle? (flat shield faces whatever the Psyker is looking at)

Would this change help more people to use this ability outside of certain builds / be more fun, or is this just power creep?
