While realizing that the Mourning Star is not a quest hub, I do realize an influx of new players (as with every major patch/promotion or new class release).

Would it be possible to introduce something akin to a “guide” flag ingame and or in the forums to people to show them around?

I am not talking late game builds etc. just “onboarding” to help digesting all the information the game throws at you. Darktide is alot deeper mechanics and character build wise than you expect as a new player and it can be pretty overwhelming.

Not talking any gm or moderator privileges just basic info you COULD research by yourself (weapon stats, masteries what the different stations do) … but sometimes you would rather not research by yourself because reasons… you get the idea.

The idea is to disarm potentially awkward situations were the party assumes every low level is a reroll of a vet and act snarky for a “not so great play” only to realize they are genuinly new and did not know better (you get the idea - why did you wake up the demon host? the what? or did you not know this mission is on a time? what timer? … situations).

Vice versa, maybe a flag for new players who would like to get more info as well (click here if you are a new player and would like assistance).

Maybe a cosmetic item or emote you can use on the mourning star that makes you actively stand out so people can interact with you prior to examing your build etc? My Ogryn usually wears his leopard print pants when assisting new players for instance
