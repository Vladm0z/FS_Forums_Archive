Simple Suggestion to Completely Fix Vanguards

11 Posts • 148 Views
J
JDLS • 2026-07-12 12:01:41

Vanguard are a enemy introduced to break up combat and add another factor to consider. However due to their un-interactive nature they completely fail at this and only server as a mild annoyance that eats slightly too many hits to be reasonable. There is no counterplay to them and I feel like this is a extremely missed opportunity.

The way they are designed punishes you for breaking their shield, if you break their shield they become effectively stagger immune. This would be fair if there was a way to deal with them without breaking their shield. I think the simplest solution to this is to make them take 4x damage and stagger in their animation after being blocked. Making most weapons able to one shot them in the head, and making it possible to push them over with all pushes. If we want to be more strict maybe this only happens after a perfect block.

Doing this allows players to actually play around them rather then just spamming light attacks until the problem goes away. You would be able to easily and effectively remove them as long as you engage in their counterplay. Either by blocking and then counter attacking for a instant kill with most weapons, or if they are in a larger hoard and you cannot get the last headshot on them, push the hoard after blocking their attack, knocking over the vanguards to temporarily remove them from play.
