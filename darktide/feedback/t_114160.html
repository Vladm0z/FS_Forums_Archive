Leebee • 2025-10-28 22:27:53

With the recent chain weapon improvements and the Chainsword buffs specifically, the Eviscerator feels somewhat better, but still kind of just like a slightly bigger Chainsword with some of the single target potential of the Chainaxe.

There have been multiple topics about the size of the Eviscerator, which absolutely should be increased (please refer to the weapon’s size in every other depiction of it:)

SisterRepentia1357×365 32 KB

im-the-only-one-who-cares-about-this-eviscerators-and-scale-v0-pp6z2qn9jn4c1669×691 127 KB

BUT, we should also be talking about gameplay with its visual design. That means:

  • Increasing the range of attacks
  • Decreasing swing speed
  • Adjust damage to keep DPS about the same, but maybe increase cleave a bit?

The Eviscerator should feel hefty and somewhat unwieldy to use, it’s one of the largest human-use melee weapons in the fiction. The Chainsword improvements have gone a long way to making it feel speedy, flexible, and practically a buzzsaw with the right builds. Let’s finally go in the opposite direction for its (much larger) counterpart. It doesn’t need to be a super meta weapon or anything, but getting the gamefeel right with it will make it feel more unique alongside the other chain weapons.
