The Purgator skull has an issue currently of feeling like griefing your teammates by wasting a grenade charge compared to the medicae skull or much stronger damage blitzes. A simple fix would be to buff the Purgator damage. An alternative, and IMO superior solution, would be for the ammo economy of purgator skull to behave like Zealot knives. That would make it worth taking as a talent point, and it would be more fun to use knowing you can still save your grenade charges for medicae, especially since it’s common for grenade charges to be reserved for stronger grenades like for Hive Scum’s RPG and toxic blitz.

The shield blitz also would feel much better to use if it was a recharge, instead of grenade pickup, like Arbitrator’s dog shock blitz. It could also have reduced quantity of charges to keep it balanced.

It’d also be a great change if the main servo-skull was baseline. When choosing a blitz other than skull, you should just lose out on Noospheric Command, Purgator, and Medicae skull. The main skull that hacks and does light consistent damage feels too important to the class identity, it feels bad to not have. A separate talent that removes the main skull in favor of grenade regen like Arbitrator would be perfect for anyone that wants to go completely skull-less.
