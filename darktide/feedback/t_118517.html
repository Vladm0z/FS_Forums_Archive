tl;dr: pounce via double-tag is bad due to being noisy and inconsistent, there must be an optional dedicated dog pounce button

Just one thing, for now. It’s your first pet class in game, and I would assume usage of the pet should be made as pleasant as possible. How comes it ended up with such god awful solution for the pounce - the action you are supposed to use dozens times a game - as double-tagging? Why isn’t there an optional dedicated pounce button as well? In many cases I don’t want to distract my allies when I know the target doesn’t threat anybody and it will be dead in a few seconds anyway when the dog will get there. And I hate myself when I have to do it dozens times a match - as I was on the other side of the situation and I hated my arbi teammates for how noisy they were. Why do you want your DLC premium class hated that much, is it so hard to add one more button definition?

Another issue with double-tag is that it’s cumbersome to execute during a heated combat. Yes you can automate it with some gaming mouse, I already do - result is still rather inconsistent, as if you try to tag it with quick mouse movement, the two tags will often land on different foes in the group, and NEITHER of them will be pounced actually, you’ll need to repeat the operation one or two more times.. being even more hated for how noisy you are. This happens even if you set delay between tags to rather low values, like 25ms, in your script. Still inconsistent at times.

And no, mods don’t solve it too, they use scripted double tagging approach as well, with the same outcomes. Not to mention nobody is supposed to rely on mods in such basic things.

Here how it should be implemented:

  1. A new dedicated button for dog pounce is added; the old double tagging pounce stays too for those who prefer it

  2. When you send a dog to a foe, new marker appears above their head, for others to see the dog is on the way. NO SOUND CUE is played when this happens, only visual indication

Thanks
