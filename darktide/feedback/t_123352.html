lMarcusl • 2026-06-30 19:31:54

Arc Grenades definitely should be consumable, those things pack a punch and have good utility outside of the damage as well (though they do tend to get blocked by terrain a lot I noticed).

For skull I’m of two minds. On one hand, yeah it’s not a great standalone, it helps, but kinda like the Zealot Immolation Grenade, it’s rarely a run saver. It’s another tool for your arsenal if you’re going for the artificer skull but depending on team, the pickup is most often better spent elsewhere so you’ll end up running out. The medicae can be a run saver, but it’s a really bad feel to be spending a grenade pickup for revives. On the other hand, it’s not really the flaming skull or the medicae skull that are the star of that particular show. It’s Noospheric Command. That thing is ridunculous. Admittedly, it eats your ability bar, so depending on build, it can have some severe cost. But if you commit to the skull, it’s insane and capacitance well spent. From that perspective, you’re getting essentially a free special/ranged elite sniper (and minigame solver) as your blitz, with some consumables as a side dish. Or to put it another way, if the flame skull becomes Chem Bomb levels of area clearing, it becomes the best blitz in the game cause it comes packaged with the option to take Noospheric Command and have a 2-in-1 blitz unlike any other. So flame skull probably should be more in the middling range. It just might be a tad too weak or too low in charges currently. Making it rechargeable would probably be too much, given how much Noospheric already gives you for “free”.

For the shield though, absolutely. Makes no sense for this thing to consume grenade pickups in the first place and it’s basically never worth it to dedicate the pickup to a gunner distraction tool when you can instead give it to something like an Ogryn to just wipe the gunner cluster with one box throw, while also reserving the option to use it on a boss or a clump of crushers, where the shield would do nothing except a bit of electrocution stun. For such a niche and selfish Blitz, it needs to have either way more charges so players can be more liberal with how many gunners need to be on the screen for it to be worth the consumable, or needs to have a cooldown, or needs to have a refill criterion similar to knives or blackouts. 3 charges is a farce.
