A few ideas on how a new gear tier could be implemented (with no powercreep)

dunnothisguy • 2026-03-14 06:25:28

As Expeditions are almost there, and with some new valuable resources we are supposed to be extracting there entering the scene, I think it’s a good opportunity to revisit the topic of new gear tier once again. That could ensure Expeditions will not become a deadend mode of play in a year, and people will see reason to go there on constant basis. It also will help to enjoy the game more to people who need a sense of “progress” and “achievement” more tangible than abstract “git gud” (which may never even come for them).

Without further ado, some ideas how to have it without introducing tons of powercreep and making it a mandatory gear treadmill you have to participate in, in order to play the game.

  1. Gear upgrade past 500lv (to the Red tier) is still linear, happens in Hadron and requires new unique resources that only can be acquired while playing expeditions and Auric/Havoc missions (with Expeditions being the fastest way to farm those)
  2. The new cap is set to 600lv. Each 20lv you add to the weapon add a new Insignia / Attachment slot to it, up to 5 of slots at 600lv
  3. In those slots you can put various Insignias enhancing certain properties of the weapon. Those properties are more detailed than the “umbrella” stats we deal with now, so, for example, you don’t increase Mobility anymore, you increase Sprint Cost and Sprint Speed properties separately.
  4. Aside from the usual properties we are familiar with, it also will allow to fine tune certain aspects of the weapon, like increasing or decreasing parry window on weapons that can parry, add parrying abilities to certain weapons that don’t have it, increasing speed of readying that particular weapon, speed of going into block with it, speed of activating its alt firing mode etc
  5. No properties directly increasing powercreep (that includes regular damage properties, as well as ones responsible for AoE radius, penetration, crit chance or crit damage; but also amount of stagger or supression it deals) can be increased, with the attack speed / rate of fire being the only exception
  6. For guns those slots can be used to install attachments, which could include flashlights, laser pointers, various kinds of advanced sights etc
  7. For melee weapons you could use those slots to install new visual customizations adding various halo and trail effects to it (purchased both for ingame and premium currencies); though it’s probably would make more sense to have separate slots for those - thus not forcing players to choose between utility and looks; could be 2 extra slots for those, granted at lvs 550 and 600.
  8. Changing contents of those slots will require spending the same unique resources as well (to enforce a constant demand for them)

So, as you can see by now, it’s mostly QoL improvements, having almost no effect on powercreep. It can be controlled how profound the most risky of them (like improvements to attack speed or sprint speed) will be, to ensure it won’t allow to create some cheesy builds breaking the balance. The improvements will be rather slight and will mostly serve the purpose of giving people some tangible, achievable grind goals, making playing the game to feel more rewarding for them.
