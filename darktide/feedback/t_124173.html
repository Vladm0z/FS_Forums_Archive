Right now, all classes plant breaching charges on the destructible brick walls during missions. To give the Ogryn a bit more unique class identity and compensate for their slower movement speed, Fatshark should add an Ogryn-only interaction prompt for these specific walls. Instead of planting a bomb, the Ogryn holds the interaction button to “Breach.”

How it works seamlessly behind the scenes without breaking code:

  • The Animation: It triggers the existing Bull Rush animation/movement script purely as a scripted interaction. No new animations needed. [1]
  • The Voice Lines: It triggers one of the Ogryn’s existing combat charge voice lines (e.g., “SMASH TIME!” or “Ere’ I come!”). No new voice acting required.
  • The Gameplay Triggers: To the AI director, the interaction works exactly like the breaching charge. It ticks down for 3 seconds of “charging up,” the wall shatters into the existing rubble assets, and the mission script continues normally. It does NOT use or require the player’s actual Bull Rush combat ability cooldown.

It’s a low-cost, high-reward flavor feature that fits the lore perfectly, reuses assets already in the game, and makes playing the big guy feel incredibly satisfying!
