It’s extremely underperforming. The only notable feature about it is that you become immune to damage while charging and even that doesn’t give you much in Havoc 40. It’s only useable in auric to speedrun the map while killing everything. I can see how its very powerful actually in auric however its completely useless in Havoc 40. Shroudfield + Thunderhammer has become somewhat viable in recent times if you run a tanky team like Ogryn, arbite, vet/psyker(for bubble/smoke), Zealot(shroud+TH) and that’s the only time because you arent really able to handle mixed hordes, you almost become just a boss-whacker and an occasional elite or stealth-revive. It takes a lot of quirky playing to make it work due to having to hit the boss in the back and all that but atleast you can become a vital contributing part of the team in all boss fights including twins, captains, not just monstrosities. Fury of the Faithful + Eviscerator on the other hand leaves you doing nothing:

-Its not realistic to use it against ranged because in Havoc 40 there are 10 more ranged units other than your target to which you charged to and you have only 2 charges so you can’t just zoom around between all of them.

-The only boss in which it outperforms Shroudfield is against the twins because you can break their shield quickly and 2-tap them both if you have 2 charges for each but even in that case it’s not something amazing. Not to mention that not every map has twins and Shroud+TH deals decent damage to them. It’s not even realistic for use because it’s not much better than just Relic Sword + Martyrdom(not even using Chorus). It’s not much better because your heavy attacks don’t really deal a life-changing about of damage considering that they leave you vulnerable due to sticking to your opponent. The special attack is even worse in terms of ratio between vulnerability and damage. Even when you use the 40%toughness damage reduction on crit talentpoint which is realistically almost the only way to use FoF-Evisc against a boss 1v1, it’s still not worth it compared to just whacking it safely with your relic sword martyrdom or 1hitKOing it with shroud+TH.

-Mixed horde fighting gets outperformed by a relic sword + chorus build and I am not talking about the extreme usefulness of Chorus itself. Here I am talking soley about the fact that the FoF+Evisc+martyrdom combination can easily get outperformed in mixed hordes by a relic sword martyrdom build without even using Chorus.

-It’s captain-shield damage isn’t better than the 1-handed assault chainsword. Shroud+TH can atleast 1 tap the captain. Evisc can’t even 1tap the shield regardless if you use FoF or not.

Shroud gives you big offensive damage + Aggro dump + Defenses after and during stealth. FoF gives you some toughness back, rending on 1 attack, 20% attack speed, 25% damage on 1 attack

Fury of the faithful + Eviscerator(the only notable interaction between weapon and ability) compared to Shroudfield + Thunderhammer is just terrible in Havoc 40 and keep in mind I am comparing it to Shroudfield + Thunderhammer which isn’t very popular nor considered useful enough in general. FoF + Eviscerator compared to just martyrdom Relic Sword is again underwhelming comparingly even if you play with +36% damage blessing and the 4% crit chance stacking buff(also why is it 4% and not 5%, it’s barely proccing)

Suggestions for buffs(I dont mean to apply these all at the same time but choose something):

  1. Increase charges from 2 to 4.
  2. Decrease cooldown to 15/20 so it’s like Arbites break the line.
  3. Add Armor-ignoring cleave lasting buff
  4. Add 100% normal cleave lasting buff
  5. Add impact lasting buff

Suggestions by fellow forum posters on this thread:

  1. Add crit damage% increase stat to it
