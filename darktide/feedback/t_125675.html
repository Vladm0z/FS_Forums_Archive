This talent has always felt a bit too conditional - not because bleed is hard to apply but because too many strike mates can apply it as well which negates its effectiveness. Some of the classes have AOE means to apply which just completely takes away the ability for zealot to take advantage of this talent because of the ownership of the bleed logic.

Some proposed changes to address the problem:

  • Change Blinded by Blood to:
    • When you damage a bleeding enemy you gain 15% damage for 5 seconds

OR

  • Add bleed to Purifying hatred so that you if something is bleeding burning or electrocuted you do 15% more damage.
  • Change Blinded by Blood to something more interesting more team utility orientated or defensive like:
    • more movement speed
    • a debuff to enemies where they do less damage when they are bleeding: if in your coherency OR if you hit them

im truly only posting this because hopefully a change like this could slide into the workload they got going on; i dont think its a massive deal but its at least a good opportunity to do something more interesting or add some QoL to a logically complex for no reason talent.
