WeNeedScoreboards • 2026-07-02 02:16:12

Fatshark’s reasoning for removing them was never valid or backed by anything objective. The amount of toxicity in VT2 to DT is exactly the same, and it boils down to one core reason:

Toxic players do not start being toxic at the scoreboard screen.

The scoreboard screen only provides a post hoc justification and something to point to as an outlet for toxic players.

That is not a valid argument for depriving players of an enjoyable feature.

Every one of dozens of friends I play with agree that this is a feature they’d like to see return to the game. It’s very fun to see the player who clutched things appear with a bunch of green circles. Especially it’d be appreciated in DT because of the build presets we could then see how well they perform against each other.

Even though we’ve established the toxicity angle is not a valid argument, there are ways to implement this even if you are stubbornly and incorrectly thinking scoreboards are a contributor to toxicity:

-They could be a post game screen accessible from the morningstar
-They could be opt in
-They could be only available to strike teams

There’s not a sufficient reason to deprive a large majority of players who enjoy this feature of it because you’ve a bad argument regarding what you think it results in.
