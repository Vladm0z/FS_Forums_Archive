Attention!!! Tempestus Scion Concept!!!

Dear reader, if you like this idea, please give it an upvote and leave a comment so that the developers can see it and consider it as a potential concept for a future class/archetype!

Reflecting on the experience of playing as the Outcast Engineer (Bardin) in Vermintide 2, I can’t shake the feeling that Darktide is missing a class with a dedicated, unique heavy weapon mechanical skill—something that operates similarly to a crank-gun/minigun with a heating and/or cooldown mechanic. The Militarum Tempestus Scion is absolutely perfect for this role!

Here are a few of my ideas for how this class could work:

1. Three Combat Ability Options (Heavy Weapon Focus):

Instead of a single ultimate, the talent tree could offer a choice of three heavy weapons carried on the back:

• Backpack-powered Hot-shot Volley Gun (Armor-piercing & Long-range): Has a base cooldown of around 75 seconds. If the ability is deactivated manually, the cooldown is calculated based only on the ammunition consumed, rather than instantly triggering the full timer. The cooldown can also be slightly reduced by scoring kills on Elites and Specials using your regular melee or ranged weapons.

• Backpack-powered Meltagun (Close-range, High-burst Damage): Deals massive damage at close quarters but has limited ammunition capacity. Has a base cooldown of around 90 seconds. Just like the Volley Gun, manually deactivating the ability calculates the cooldown based only on the ammunition consumed, and it features the same cooldown-reduction mechanic via Elite/Special kills.

• Semi-automatic Grenade Launcher (AoE Crowd Control): Designed for area-of-effect damage against hordes. Has a base cooldown of around 60 seconds. It shares the exact same mechanics: manual deactivation calculates the cooldown based only on consumed ammunition, and cooldown is reduced via Elite/Special kills.

2. Blitz Abilities (Tactical Grenades):

• Proximity Explosive Charge: A deployable explosive with a motion sensor. Players can configure it via a setting (or talent choice) to only trigger on specific enemy types (e.g., Elites/Monsters). This guarantees you detonate it exactly on the targets you want.

• Crowd Control Alternative: A second Blitz option could be a device that slows down approaching enemies to control chokepoints.

• Capacity: Base capacity is 1 charge, increasing to 2 if you pick the corresponding Blitz modifier talent.

3. Class-Specific Lore Weapons:

Since the Scion already brings a heavy weapon as a Combat Ability, their regular arsenal can be highly iconic and lore-accurate:

• Melee: Tempestus Combat Blade, single-handed or two-handed Power Axes.

• Ranged: Plasma Pistol, Hot-shot Laspistol, and other lore-friendly options.

Conclusion:

If you think this might break the game’s balance, I am confident it won’t. This concept fits perfectly into the existing balance ecosystem. We already see skilled players handling Havoc 40 modifiers incredibly well.

Dear community and developers, I hope you find these thoughts interesting and evaluate them on their merits. Thank you for your time and support!
