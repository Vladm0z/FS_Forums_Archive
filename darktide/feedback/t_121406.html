I understand the need of breaking synergies between Uncanny from melee weapons and things like SB / Inferno Staff, ok. But it also seems like it’s possible to limit Rending to only melee, at the same time? Then why weapons like Combat Blade, that are not that powerful as, say, DS, in the first place, get the same treatment, losing global Rending on weakspot hits? Brittleness isn’t exactly equal substitution, as you have to ramp up its stacks on every new foe you hit before you can deal proper damage to them.

Wouldn’t it be more reasonable to leave access to Rending on them - but just make it melee-exclusive Rending instead?

P.S.
It could even make more sense that way: DS will apply brittleness as it’s mostly single-target, long reach precise weapon, so it should incentivize you to select one big target and hit just it. While CB is short reach weapon you are expected to go right into hoard of enemies with, and hit many random enemies out of order with it. So it should have global melee Rending instead which you ramp up once and keep until you keep fighting, hitting heads.
