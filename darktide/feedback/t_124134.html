One thing that is somewhat minor but definitely confuses people without mods is the actual aura and awakening stage of Daemonhosts. The current visuals are very minimal and unless you are staring at it and are very aware of its animations you cant really tell if you are waking it or not. The radius is very ambiguous and there is no clear visual of you are about to wake it. This leads to people on certain maps trying to squeeze past a daemonhost and ending up waking it, or people not knowing you can squeeze past a daemonhost and then standing around for a few minutes trying to decide on if they should approach it or not.

My suggestion would be to have green flames start to flow out of the daemonhost as it awakens, starting small and slowly growing in brightness and speed as it gets closer to waking up. The flames should coil up and roll back at a set distance making a clear radius, giving the impression of the daemons magic trying to break free and pushing back on invisible wards that keep it contained. Could have a less intense version of this aura follow the daemon around to make its corruption radius more clear as well. Also it would just make the daemonhost feel more powerful and intimidating in general.
