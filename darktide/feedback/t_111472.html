It is an astounding accomplishment to create ELO hell in a cooperative PvE horde shooter. I commend the FS team responsible for this monumental achievement.

You have created an environment where only the sweatiest of the sweatlords can play, and if you want to join the elite cabal, well you better buckle up and get ready for a long trek of grinding to 40. Nobody wants to play with you either, because you don’t have 40.

Seriously, there is no benefit to doing Havoc like this. It’s optional super-endgame content only a few players are supposed to enjoy - let the natural difficulty weed out the weak.

I like lists, so the list of problems is as follows:

  • Early Havoc is mind-numbingly boring and easier than Aurics
  • Progression through Havoc is slow with nothing to show for it - there is very little meaningful difference in difficulty between one tier and the next
  • The friggin’ rank up system

  • The friggin’ derank system

  • Want to play an easier Havoc to warm up or cool off? Gotta grind back up to 40 buddy lol. Imagine freedom of choice.
  • It’s included in your weekly chores for some reason

My proposal is simple: get rid of the ranks entirely. Provide three or four difficulties for players to freely choose between. You can keep the weekly rewards if you want. Simple, clean, and doesn’t put everyone at each others’ throats.

Also, remove Dukane. She’s got less dimension than Paper Mario.
