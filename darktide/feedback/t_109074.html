(Havoc in mind)
Call it a hot take if you will, but Veteran is the weakest class in the game.
Voice of Command is the strongest ultimate in the game. Plasma Gun is top 3 best weapons in the game. Survivalist is awesome. But you take away Voice of Command and Plasma Gun, what does Veteran have left exactly?
The answer is that Veteran remains with nothing that other classes can’t offer to equal or greater amounts.
Veteran is the definition of a one-trick pony. Three qualities: Ammo regen, Plasma Gun, consistent golden health. Take any of these away and the class’ worth PLUMMETS, whether you’d like to believe it or not. I would much rather have a VoC Plasma Survivalist Veteran in my team than an Ogryn, but you take away any of these few qualities, and suddenly an Ogryn with literally ANY build at all is preferable. Hell I’d say Psyker with any build at all is preferable to a Veteran without his absolute best available equipment.
Veteran suffers from having a focus on ranged gameplay in a mode (Havoc) where ammo is very, very scarce. What teams usually do is that they leave all the ammo for the Veteran because of how much value the Plasma Gun gives. But you take away the Plasma Gun and suddenly people don’t care to give you ammo anymore, because they know that every class can do the same you can now.
Veteran needs a true 60:40 ranged to melee ratio, even in Havoc. He can only do this with the Plasma Gun. No other class relies on their gun so much. Veteran is supposed to be the firearms specialist, but in reality he’s the Plasma Gun specialist. Say what you want, but the value of being able to cleave through dozens of poxwalkers to hit a Dreg Gunner shooting at you from 50m away is a quality that close to no other gun has, and it’s absurdly invaluable. In fact, this is what Veteran was intended for. Plasma is the only weapon that can consistently do this without running dry after just a few engagements. Plasma Gun gives to Veteran the purest intended purpose of his class, to eliminate key high priority targets from afar and do it consistently. Most of his other guns somehow struggle at this to a similar extent as other classes.
Plasma Gun needs a nerf, Voice of Command needs a nerf, but not before Veteran can achieve his true purpose of being a ranged specialist.
