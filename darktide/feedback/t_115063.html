When I tried to complete with this map, I’ve failed at this same place, I think, 10 times with different players. The times when we clear this spot is when the game doesn’t spawn everything at the same time, making it basically a case of random roll of dice. You get the bosses and the elites together, you fail with almost a 100% certainty. Only one of those spawn, or none at all, you get to pass.

90% of weapons feel useless in these two modifiers specifiсally, even Zealot’s and Arbitrator’s Crusher weapon which was kinda underrated in the community, is now complete garbage in Havoc. Ogryn’s Pickaxe and Shovel got giga nerfed in Havoc due to HP changes, the shovel which could previously oneshot Crushers in Havoc 40 with specific setups now can’t even 2 shot them reliably with fully charged heavy attacks.

Find other ways to make the game difficult other than spamming 10+ tanky enemies every 10 meters of the mission.
