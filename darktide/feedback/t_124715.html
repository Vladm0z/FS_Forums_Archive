Buff to Rifleman and Gunners in Auric and suppression changes

Since release the enemy ranged units have only gotten weaker to a point where they barely even matter anymore even in Auric. One thing I like about Havoc is that those enemies become a great threat again and need to be paid attention to instead of just rushing the level.

I have some different things in mind:

Give all small rifleman a general 25% fire rate buff in Auric, similar to how “Emperors fading light” modifier works in Havoc.

Remove the Gunner resistance from Curios, as it is the best and most game changing resistance buff one could pick on them. If you pick it 3 times, you can just walk towards Reapers and Gunners to punch them in the face. I’d say maybe reduce their damage by around 20% but then remove the perk from curios. (Info: Gunner resistance only affects Scab/Dreg machine gunners and Reapers. No shotgunners, no small rifleman)

Back then, gunners where able to stagger the player, which was a horrible feature, but at the same time it caused them to be a serious threat and to hold their line, which was exactly what their job was. Instead of a horrible stagger, there could be a mechanic that makes players move slower by about 20% when moving towards a gunner they get shot from but ONLY when moving towards them. That way, players could still retreat into cover but would make rushing them harder on open fields. Reapers could even apply a stronger movement debuff because they are supposed to be a bigger threat.

Now, as a counter feature to gunners being a bigger threat that can’t be as easily rushed in melee, I would suggest a tweak to the received suppression system. I always thought it to be weird that the exact weapons that are supposed to deal with gunners and need precision, like the Infantry Lasgun and other precision weapons are completely useless once you get shot at, due to insane suppression and random bullet deviation. This current system only advocates rushing across the field to deal with them in melee.

My solution would be that if you crouch, you get a base-kit 90% suppression resistance so you can return fire when you’re getting shot at but not while running around on the open field. Also a general great suppression reduction for weapons that rely on precision like Infantry Lasgun and Vigilant Autogun, as it is shown with the Galvanic Rifle would be necessary for those weapons. The current suppression system feels really bad when not playing Veteran and makes pretty much all precision weapons unusable in situations where you need them most. This change would give you options in situations where neither rushing nor shooting them due suppression is possible.

Right now, even in Auric the best solution against many ranged enemies is to just rush them in melee, which completely takes out any threat from them and makes basically any countermeasure like bubble or smoke grenades against them redundant outside of Havoc. I don’t want them to delete players, but they should matter more and give ranged builds some value.
