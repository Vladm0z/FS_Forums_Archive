Okay, been complaining about the plasteel shortage since forever.

Many of us have.

The problems are multiple.

  1. We can’t trade mats. We can kind of recycle and buy things to recycle, but that’s meh and expensive.

  2. Gathering is hard. You are often teamed with player who only want to rush, never look in the crates, walk right past plasteel.

  3. Much is in crates which require even more breaking off to open.

  4. Crate take too long to open, although ‘autoloot’ mod helps by auto opening and taking mats in a chest. Everyone should download autoloot.

Putting a set amount of plasteel in mortis trials was a good idea, but overall the whole system of gathering as it relates to chests and going off the main route might need to be reconsidered around the way the teams we’re often matched with actually behave.

Perhaps every character could at a minimum have an pickup radius for materials?
