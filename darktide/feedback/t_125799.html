Vertraxxx • 2026-09-23 03:50:32

This is fundamentally an issue with the overall design philosophy of the game, but any system that effectively requires players to use something like a Party Finder just to be able to play is nothing but a hassle for 99.9% of the player base. It also creates opportunities for people to gatekeep certain builds and classes, which only serves to make the overall game experience worse. These kinds of requirements should simply be removed.

As for the difficulty of normal missions, the difficulty itself isn’t really the problem. The problem is that the difficulty can fluctuate wildly depending on things like class and weapon balance, meaning the intended difficulty isn’t actually guaranteed. The difference between a party that is completely optimized around the meta and a party where all four players are using off-meta setups is genuinely enormous.

I’d like to think that the removal of Gold Toughness this time will make things somewhat better, since it was one of the factors contributing to this problem. However, there are still plenty of things in the game that can break the balance, such as Smite, Inferno, and DoT damage. Unless those things are addressed, changing the difficulty of normal missions won’t really accomplish anything.

When it comes specifically to Auric and Maelstrom, I’d like to see the extremely boring event spawns improved. Some of them are so predictable and uneventful that they practically put me to sleep.

The assassination boss in Oubliette is another thing I’d like to see improved. It’s been a joke of a boss since release and feels like nothing more than a waste of time. Unfortunately, Fatshark doesn’t seem particularly interested in doing anything about it, so I don’t have very high expectations there either.
