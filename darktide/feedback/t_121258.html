Dear Fatshark,

I would like to propose an optional feature focused on progression integrity at the level of individual operatives.

Right now, all operatives created by a single player share access to penances, cosmetics, and certain progression elements. This creates situations where one character can wear or display rewards that were actually earned by a different character on the same account.

I am not referring to interactions between different players. The issue exists entirely within a single account: one operative can effectively present the accomplishments of another.

I would like to have the option to enable a mode where each operative is treated as a fully independent entity in terms of achievements and recognition.

In such a mode:

  • All penances, cosmetics, and Havoc rank would be tracked and unlocked per operative
  • An operative could only equip cosmetics they personally earned
  • Progress toward achievements would not be shared or combined across characters

For example, if one operative has completed 60 Auric missions and another has never progressed beyond Heresy, neither should be able to display rewards tied to 100 Auric missions unless that specific operative has individually reached that milestone.

The same logic should apply to all achievements, especially those that represent not just quantity, but also difficulty and player skill.

At the same time, not everything needs to be separated. Weapons and curios could remain shared across operatives for convenience. However, weapon mastery should remain personal. An operative who has not unlocked certain perks or blessings should not be able to freely apply them, even if another character on the account has already done so.

Additionally, it would greatly improve clarity if players could verify whether another operative has actually earned the cosmetics they are wearing. This is particularly important for Havoc ranks and other high-difficulty achievements. If I see an operative wearing rewards associated with a high tier of play, I should be able to trust that this specific character has reached that level.

This system would not replace the current one, but exist as an optional mode for players who value consistency between a character’s appearance and their actual experience.

The goal is simple: each operative represents their own history, not a shared pool of accomplishments.

Thank you for your time and consideration.
