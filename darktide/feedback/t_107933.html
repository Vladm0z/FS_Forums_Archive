TL;DR - This has got to be one of the least fun game modes of all the Tide games. The only even remotely redeeming quality is you can farm barrel kills for the penance lol. And have infinite ammo on a flamer and burn everything.

I know FS said it wasn’t going to be a copy of Chaos Wastes (I am not a fan of reused assets/ideas but I would have loved that), but I think myself and many others were still expecting something similar.

List of Issues:

  1. Difficulty is low. I’ve done 17/50 for the penance and only 1 time failed or even came close to failing, and that was because a silly heretic pushed a poxburster into the team and sent us to space.
  2. There are two static maps you play on. We thought normal map selection was lacking…
  3. The objectives/enemies rarely change, mainly because there are only like 2 different objectives. You get a DH, a Captain or the Twins. The only real variety is which waves of specials and elites spawn that game.
  4. The perks are extremely stale and boring.
  5. Player agency is almost entirely removed relative to Chaos Wastes. There you voted on which path to take to avoid or get certain modifiers and buffs. This lead to a lot more variety and choice in a playthrough. In Mortis Trials, you pick 1 of 6 initial buff types and a few minor ones as you progress. The rest are just given to you. ZZZ…
  6. True to form for Darktide, the cosmetic rewards suck lol.

Like I mentioned before, I am not a fan of reusing old content from previous games/events/ideas etc. However, the saying “don’t fix what isn’t broken” comes to mind. Chaos Wastes was a perfect framework to build off, instead it was gutted and stripped down to a sorry excuse of a poor replacement. Even calling it a roguelike game mode does a disservice to the entire concept of the genre.
