Need to confess from the start that whole idea of achievements was always pointless and of no interest to me, be it Steam’s or in this game. Yet I still can’t ignore how nonsensically they are implemented, it’s just bad game design, at this point.

If they are supposed to show your mastery of the game, then how is it not possible for others to see them? They must be a bragging matter, same as cosmetics, there should be plenty opportunities to show them to other players.

If they are supposed to be metric of how well you personally advanced your skill, then how is it not possible to see a percentage of players who have it? Otherwise you won’t know if you really that good, it’s only can be assessed in comparison to others.

Steam does it actually pretty well, it should be used as an example. They even allow you to compare your achievements to the select user’s. At least, next should be implemented in DT:

  1. Whenever your character is visible to other players (during Inspect on the station, or on the squad review screen), there must be, say, 5 new slots where you could display 5 Penances of your choice (configurable on the Loadout screen)

  2. Whenever you browse the Penances, it should show you what percentage of players have each of those.
