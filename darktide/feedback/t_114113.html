Relic Blade Perfect Block blessings have poor returns on activation cost

3 Posts • 156 Views
r
rally • 2025-10-27 19:39:23

Hello! I’m a huge fan of the Relic Blade, I run a mk. II and it’s easily my favorite weapon in the game. I am very happy with the recent changes to the Zealot talent tree, as most of them feel like they suit my playstyle as if they were made to match, and the talent changes have encouraged me to explore more of the Relic Blade’s blessing pool. My build feels stable enough that I can start trying things that I didn’t feel confident working into my play before, and I just wanted to pass along a bit of feedback from my experience.

The two Perfect Block blessings- Energy Transfer and Counterattack- both stood out as something that looked like they might offer a unique return for raising the skill ceiling of a build, giving something back for attempting something risky. Dodging is preferable to blocking, but if I could react to a back attack whoosh sound and activate a Blessing off of it, that might make for some interesting decisionmaking in timing the block instead of just getting out of the way and swinging back at them. I ran both of the Perfect Block blessings one at a time to give them a test ride in Aurics, and I felt disappointed by the returns.

My impression running Energy Transfer and Counterattack were that they didn’t do enough for long enough to justify attempting a Perfect Block instead of just dodging out of the way and swinging. Counterattack’s five-second uptime felt like it was much harder to take advantage of compared to the new Punish Impiety talent’s similar five-second window, since I wasn’t in control of when the activation window could start and portions of that uptime were lost to blockstun from activating the blessing, powering on if the opportunity hit while I was in cold state or powering down if the opportunity came up when I was near max heat. The window feels too small to take advantage of with the other time expenses needed, and while 12% attack speed is nice, it wasn’t enough to justify capitalizing on a Perfect Block opportunity to get three or four practical seconds out of it.

My trial of the Energy Transfer blessing was also lackluster. I’d been running Cranial Grounding and Heatsink to try and maximize my powered-on uptime and minimize my powered-off downtime, so I thought trying out a Perfect Block heat management ability might be a more fun and proactive way to extend my uptime. In practice this blessing didn’t really feel like it gave me much utility at all for the effort of blocking a melee attack amidst a hail of lasgun fire- for my troubles I maybe got one additional second of uptime off a Perfect Block, on an activation opportunity I can’t force to happen. Heatsink, by comparison, lets me manage my heat levels with Blades of Faith headshots. The big picture idea of Energy Transfer feels cool- I’d imagined Perfect Blocking monstrosity aggro to maintain my powered-on uptime for when I can get my shots in- but it didn’t produce any returns I felt like I could work with.

Overall I felt like these two blessings have a lot of potential, but their effects are too mild and they don’t last long enough to really justify deliberately taking an avoidable block instead of dodging out of the way. Counterattack’s speed boost lasting significantly longer and maybe being a small amount faster feels like it would be a competitive choice with Rampage’s free Strength boost for swinging into a crowd, and Energy Transfer having an immediate and significant reduction on your heat gauge would enable extended powered-on states at the effort cost of positioning yourself to hit a Perfect Block. My impression of the heat management blessings is that we’re not meant to reduce heat buildup into an active reduction, which I understand- the weapon is quite powerful- but I feel like in practice the opportunity cost of hitting a Perfect Block can’t compete with Cranial Grounding or Heat Sink if it can’t produce an actual reduction in an active heat bar, the margins on return are just too thin to justify the complexity of activation or the inability to choose when to activate it.

That’s my feedback. I’m a huge fan of the weapon and I think there’s a lot of potential for fun, complex play in these two blessings, but in trying them out I didn’t feel like I was able to get a big return for the effort I was going to, both for their short duration and mild effects. Thanks for all the work you guys put into this remarkable game, I’m really looking forward to the December update, and I’m gonna keep having fun tuning my builds in the wake of the new Zealot tree patch.
