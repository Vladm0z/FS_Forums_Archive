MadMartigan • 2026-01-29 14:30:37

OK so I have hated pox gas since it came out and have largely avoided it since getting any related penances etc.

But now I am being forced to play it again via the event (I am a completionist) so it has stirred my disappointment at the mechanic once more.

Even tox bombers are too much, let alone entire maps of the bl00dy stuff.

So a few simple fixes could be applied -

  • tox bomber distance reduced (their throw distance is insane)
  • tox bomber cooldown/reload increased
  • number of simultaneous bombers capped
  • tox gas cloud area greatly reduced but effects greatly increased (ie make the clouds much smaller but more potent)
  • Same rules for tox gas modifier - make the affected areas MUCH smaller but more damaging.

This would mean there would be small pockets of very toxic gas that turns the map more into a minefield to be navigated (ie a more fun mechanic that requires more skill) instead of “run blindly for 5 minutes until you reach the outside of the 1 mile wide gas cloud, then immediately do it again because the 1 mile gas cloud follows you within seconds”.

Make it more about strategy and choice in the immediate area/battle. Current mechanic means you just ditch the fun of combat because you have to get out of the stupidly large and opaque cloud.

Here are some arbitrary things I would do, just because it would at least be more fun (don’t care about “challenge” part, aka “kick me in the nuts”, sweats should go to Havoc for that).

  1. Make it a visibility problem for foes as well! So when gas is out, be it from vents or from the grenade, foes lose ability to attack you from range as well if you are in the cloud (except may be from really close distance, so shotgunners are still a threat). So everyone goes into melee. Otherwise it feels too unfair when enemy gunners and snipers still can hit your through the dense veil of pox gas.

  2. Make it to enhance you as well, not just foes! You inhale it and go berserk, your attacks are faster and stronger. So you may have toughness issues but at least you are having fun. May be take away some of stamina (total cap, or just reduce it over time), as a payback for that.

  3. Give players some extra (limited) ways to deal with smokes and fires. Like, make vet’s smoke grenades to extinguish fire and replace enemy smoke clouds. Make zealot’s fire and HS’s tox grenade to replace all harmful fire surfaces. Make all kinds of explosions to create small (temporary) pockets of clean air within smoke clouds of all kinds etc.
