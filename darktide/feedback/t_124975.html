Mech Power Sword Buffs!?

So, the Mechanicus Power Sword… It has been a little unpopular, probably because: You have to take some time to actually learn the moveset (tbf extra attack button makes it harder), actually aim (finesse weapon with bugged finesse stat.. oops), and you don’t actually have a perfectly horizontal sweep (except on stairs, lol). Actually, though!!!

Still this weapon is pure dopamine to me, and since I got my grubby robo claws on it I haven’t let it go. IMO the design is incredible, with the recent changes elevating it to an incredible peak!

And yet, there are certain aspects to this which bug me because we are yet again seeing unique and powerful weapon abilities that imo should be global game mechanics (simple QoL, even).

So, from the word of the prophet themselves:

Hotfix 1.12.3 - Patch Notes
Hotfix 1.12.3 - Patch Notes

We are also looking at the Skitarii Melee Weapons, and we are planning some fixes and changes to stat scaling and also a set of specific tweaks for the Mechanicus Power Sword.

I can’t wait for the Power Sword to be made obnoxiously op because some people couldn’t figure out how to use it correctly.

We got:

Patch 1.12.4 - Patch Notes

  • Mechanicus Power Sword

    • Fixed an issue where the Finesse and First Target stats would not correctly apply their scaling to Special activated attacks

    • Increased the Melee Strength bonus scaling on Special charges from +4% to +5% (max at 6 charges from +24% to +30%)

    • Added a new Light 5 diagonal Vanguard attack, chained from Light 4 and chaining back into Light 4 / Heavy 3

    • Changed the scaling of the Mobility stat to provide an additional effective Dodge when exactly at 80% stat.

    • Slight range and hitbox increase for all attacks

    • Slight movespeed curve increase for Heavy windups and attacks

(are we there yet???)

Easy wins (good job)

  • bug fix to finesse damage (duh)
  • Range/hitbox increase – this feels normal, the sword felt a lil’ short compared to how it looks/feels before

IMO warranted buffs and fixes to an already-solid weapon that just wasn’t gelling with many. Power Falchion vibes…

Special buff, not needed

  • Increased the Melee Strength bonus scaling on Special charges from +4% to +5% (max at 6 charges from +24% to +30%)

I’ll take it; didn’t need it though. It already had great breakpoints with charged attacks, and cleave if you charged it.

Even though it doesn’t break anything per se, changes like this are in danger of making the weapon overpowered the wrong way; it’s got so much going for it already and I would hate for the incredible versatility to lose out to pure numbers. IMO the best way to balance this weapon is to keep rewarding finesse (the stat and the playstyle) at a cost of raw power.

Players shouldn’t be able to just spam their way to victory, which brings me to:

The moveset – light attack spam addition

  • Added a new Light 5 diagonal Vanguard attack, chained from Light 4 and chaining back into Light 4 / Heavy 3

For those of you that haven’t tried it yet, this is basically tacking on a light-attack spam chain on the end of horde-clear openers (and more).

For instance, push → heavy → light now comboes into light spam.

Even easier, push-attack now straight-up comboes into light spam…

No push, no problem, you can light (poke) → heavy (sweep) → light spam!

So basically we now have a light-attack spam capable weapon (albeit diagonal slashes). FWIW I think this is a great addition and should help people deal with light hordes more intuitively. It hasn’t robbed the weapon of it’s identity or true power since you simply can’t beat the cleave of a charged heavy, especially since you can (and should) still combo charged heavies if you’re smart. And, the player’s ability to combo effectively will continue to separate the chaff from the wheat. Kudos!

That said, I’d hate for this newfound ability to compromise the weapon’s baseline damage. It’s already a potent weapon without having to use charge attacks (see Vet PS issues), but light-attack spam combined with blessings/talents runs the risk of turning this magnificent weapon into a foam paddle if unpowered attacks are deemed too strong down the line because they are now easily spammed.

Grudge 1: Mobility stat matters!?!?!?!?

  • Changed the scaling of the Mobility stat to provide an additional effective Dodge when exactly at 80% stat.

I know this is fresh out the oven but this change alone should be rolled out to every weapon in the game (and I’ve long thought this). Now mobility isn’t the most useless dump stat in the game, huzzah.

This would improve build variety and stat selection SO much that I hope to see it in my lifetime. Introduce it with the ability to tweak stat bars manually at Hadron, so we don’t have to gamble anymore. Amazing.

If this is dipping the proverbial toe to explore an eventual rollout, I am so here for it

Grudge 2: Thrust and thrust-likes

  • Slight movespeed curve increase for Heavy windups and attacks

IMO what people didn’t understand about this weapon on release is that it’s secretly a heavy weapon (another hint: 3 dodges). The charge wind-up slows you down, giving a real HEFT to the thing. Plus you can slide/dodge while doing it so you have tech. I’m gonna call this an unwarranted buff due to (imo unreasonable) player complaints. The real issue was people didn’t gel with the moveset and were disappointed that such a sexy weapon felt unintuitive and slow (it does, at first…).

Can’t complain that much though, since it does feel better (because it is better).

What I CAN complain about is Yet Another Innate Thrust Weapon. If Hive Scum crowbar can get it, more heavy weapons can get it. Innate Thrust is a great mechanic to give heavier weapons real oomph. And balance-wise, the drawback of hefting your weapon longer is a substantial con especially at higher difficulties – it practically balances itself!

Grudge 3: DLC Power weapons and their stupid sexy activations

Arbites Shock Maul.

Skitarii Power Sword.

FS did an incredible job making power weapon activation fluid, for the simple reason that you can combo them in at the press of a button. Power Sword elevates it to perfection with Thrust charge states. GIVE THIS TO THUNDER HAMMER. Paul and Vet PS, too. No more ““self-stun”” please!

Power weapons have a long history of issues, particularly around activation mechanics. And IMO, it’s basically been solved by DLC classes now. So let’s roll out some of this goodness to the old faithfuls why not

TL;DR

  • Mechanicus Power Sword is a DLC weapon which has mechanics that should be shared by many/most other weapons (Mobility matters, innate Thrust, Powered attack combo/stages)
  • Probably too much buffs since the weapon was kind of a sleeper before imo, losing identity as heavy weapon, can hardly complain since they do make my favourite weapon better so [BIAS]…
  • Light attack spam lol (it’s actually incredible)
  • Learn the damn weapon, it’s fantastic if you like fancy movesets! The surface-level drawbacks are there for a reason…
