That should be Feel No Pain. IMO, Feel No Pain should provide the best TDR and Stagger output, but at the cost of ammo efficiency and melee damage.

Heavy Hitter should be the best melee damage at cost of ammo, stagger and tankiness. So for that reason Impactful should probably go to Feel No Pain, replaced by something else for Heavy Hitter that allows Ogryn to close the gap for melee combat more effectively.

Burst Limiter Override should be the best at ammo efficiency, ranged armor pen but the “squishiest” Ogryn keystone by far. One way this could be done is by changing Back Off to something like this:

On Killing Melee Attack gain +10% chance to trigger Lucky Bullet for the next magazine. Stacks 5 times. At 5 stacks, magazine is loaded from reserves.

The next magazine coupled with chance reduction to 50% means Rumbler spam is no longer an issue, as Rumbler only has 1 shot in the magazine. It would however still be useful for Rumbler, as it would reload it from reserves and provide a 50% chance of free second shot.
