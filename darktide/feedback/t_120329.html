This sounds like unfamiliarity with how the caps actually work rather than a balance issue.

  • The shields have 70% ranged DR, melee it.
  • The shields have a cap on damage taken, 1300 per hit afair. Lightspam it, never heavy.
  • Shield recharge is based on damage taken, not a timer. Let the guy with the biggest burst land the first hit after it has been depleted.

Weapons with quick repeated damage application, tac axe lightspam, latch attacks from chain weaponry, shock maul special will obliterate the shield, whereas a thunder hammer or a relic sword can downright onetap their ass once shield is down.

You know you will run into them, plan your loadout accordingly. The game will not bend to your liking, that part is up to you.
