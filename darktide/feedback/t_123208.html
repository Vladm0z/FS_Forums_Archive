Advanced Combat Doctrine does nothing for the Galvanic Rifle and I’d like to change that.

To start, ACD has five supporting talents “Restoration Protocol”, “Writ of Ammunition Enumeration”, “Piercing Sight”, “Readiness Doctrines”, and “Calculated Priority”. Five points is too much when the other abilities cost two to three at most.

Take “Restoration Protocol”, and “Readiness Doctrines” (10% toughness regen each second, and 25% reload speed) Fold both of them into the base ability.

“Writ of Ammunition Enumeration”, and “Piercing Sight” Both remain unchanged.

“Calculated Priority” (5% damage for 10 seconds stacks 5 times) In addition to its current effect, It would give a bonus to Finesse damage but disable auto aim. All the other effects would work just not auto aim.

Two less talent points and an option to disable auto aim. Maybe then we can all have fun blasting enemies away with both Galvanic and Braced Auto Guns.
