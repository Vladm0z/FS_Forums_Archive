OK peeps, I’ve worn my shift and caps lock to nubbins spamming requests for CHAT BLOCK already. The new game mode, Expeditions, has cooperation at its core yet we are missing an extremely important feature to facilitate text chat (the most common of chat) in-game.

IT’S TIME!

For those unfamiliar, here’s for a rundown of what Chat Block is:

In-game, when a player opens a text chat window their player character automatically holds block – similar to the auto-block while reviving players. This small but impactful feature helps provide a small window of safety from stray groaners and the like while you converse politely with your team mates

To add insult to (literal!) injury, this feature was introduced into FatShark’s previous entry Vermintide 2 but neglected from Darktide. Yes, there is a mod that does this. No, I do not consider this to be sufficient – and so do this many people:
