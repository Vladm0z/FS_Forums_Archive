PLAGUE MARINE - 5th Monstrosity

Logline: A walking siege engine. The first Monstrosity that doesn’t chase you - you have to assault him.
The Concept: Fallen Brother

Lore Justification: The Moebian 6th and the Cult of Admonition are 99% mortal. But the Warp storm around Atoma has drawn a handful of true Death Guard. Wolfer would absolutely welcome them as advisors / enforcers for the 6th. They’re not leading the cult, they’re sent to make sure Tertium actually falls. That’s why there are so few - and why you only ever see ONE at a time.

In mission briefing, Morrow calls it a “Fallen Brother.” Hadron and Grendyl would be losing their minds. This is not a traitor guardsman. This is a 10,000 year old Heretic Astartes.

Visual & Audio Design

7’6", bloated beyond his power armor. XIVth Legion green, corroded and cracked with rust and ceramite blisters. Nurgling nests in the joints. His helmet lenses are dead, but a single red targeting lens flickers over one eye.

He doesn’t run. He advances. Heavy, inexorable stomps. Every step leaves a slick of bile that sizzles on the deck. He doesn’t run down his victims. He sieges them to death.

Health bar:

FALLEN BROTHER - [NAME]

Roster Context:

  1. Plague Ogryn - Melee chaser
  2. Beast of Nurgle - Disabler / Tank
  3. Chaos Spawn - Flurry grabber
  4. Ogryn Pack Master - Duelist / Leader
  5. Plague Marine - Ranged Anchor / Siege

Spawn & Presentation:
No ceiling drop. A bulkhead door at the end of the arena hisses open, flies pour out. Heavy boots, dragging ceramite, wet vox-breathing.

Stats - Damnation:

  • Health: 2,500 - Second-lowest of all generic Monstrosities
  • Armor: Unholy Carapace - Only Rending / Brittleness / Ogryn Power weapons do full damage. Lasguns tickle the chest.
  • Movement: 70% player speed. Never sprints. Unstaggerable except by Ogryn charge or backpack break.
  • Aura: Cloud of Flies - Within 8m, -10% stamina regen, -accuracy, screen flies. You can’t face-tank him.

Attack Pattern - No Channels, No Phases:

1. Blight Launcher [Primary - Siege]
Fires every 6-7 seconds. Loud THUNK with green laser sight.
Leaves a 7m slime pool for 12 seconds. Pool = corruption + slow + spawns 2-3 Nurglings. This IS his horde pressure. He denies cover, forces rotation.

2. Boltgun [Suppression - Mid Range]
Between launcher shots. Deliberate 3-round bursts. High toughness damage, massive suppression. He holds angles - he will not push you.

3. Plague Knife / Bubotic Axe [Melee Punish]
Only within 4m.

  • Unblockable overhead → Blockable sweep that corrupts.
  • Power Fist Shove - Huge knockback, throws you INTO his own blight pool.
  • No lunge. Dodge back once = safe, but he will launcher your landing spot.

4. Power Pack Vent [Defensive / Burst Window]
Auto-triggers at 50% and 15% HP.
He staggers himself for 2 sec, 360-degree pus explosion knocks back melee. After, his backpack smokes and takes +50% damage for 8 seconds. Your DPS window.

5. Death - Catastrophic Detonation
3-second whine, then 10m powerful green explosion leaves final blight pool. You must leave melee or you may go down with him.

Weaknesses:

  • **Bloated Power Pack ** - 3x damage. Break = 4 sec stun + loses Fly Aura.[Main]
  • **Head ** - 2x damage, but head is always tilted down.[Secondary]
  • Reload Window - 2.5 sec after launcher fire, chest exposed.

Gameplay Loop:
Veteran / Psyker peel the pack. Zealot waits for vent. Ogryn is the only stagger. Team must push, not kite.

VO - Plague Marine:

  • Entering: “Mortals… you trespass on Grandfather’s garden.” / “I have held this line for centuries. You will not pass it.”
  • Blight Launcher: “Blessed payload.” / “Bloom.” / “Grandfather… shares.” / “Fertilize them.”
  • Boltgun: “Hold still.” / “Die… upright.”
  • Taking Damage: “The pack… leaks…” / “Nngh… Grandfather… itches…”
  • Death: “Grandfather… I… return to the soil…” / “Even in death… I spread…” / “I… bloom… at last…” / “BLOOM!”
