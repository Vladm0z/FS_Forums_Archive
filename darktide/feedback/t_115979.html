Crazy idea, but hear me out

These 2 abilities are very similar when it comes down to their essentials: Damage stances that require damage to upkeep

The main thing seperating these 2 abilities is the damage type required for upkeep. To me, this makes the class feel very restricted in terms of its gameplay loop.

Get close, pop ability, kill.

I feel like this could be remedied in a number of ways, but theres one that I’d like to suggest given personal experience and recent player feedback.

Roll the abilies into one, make the base ability increase close damage on activation, and then give the ability 2 branches that buff their respective damage type, while being locked off from each other.

Then, replace the left side ability with the Boom Bringer. Currently, the boom bringer feels less than stellar. Sure it has a lot of flavor, but not enough oomf to really make it feel fleshed out conceptually.

Given how we had a big damage button ability in vt2 tied to bounty hunter, I feel like the boom bringer could function very similarly, which means allowing the numbers on this thing to be PUMPED UP, without overshadowing other blitzes.

Lastly, maybe they could fill up the last blitz spot with the chem knife currently tied to the shivs. Sure, it functions just like zealot knives, but lowering the number count and having it apply chem makes them feel diffrent enough as is.

Thanks for reading, sorry if its a little long winded. Would appreciate any feedback!
