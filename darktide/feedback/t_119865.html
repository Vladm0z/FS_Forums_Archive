I feel a sharp need to inform Fatshark about my very important opinion.

Did you ever ask yourself: why does anyone need to play this new game mode?
I spent 2000 hours in Darktide and I have no answer.

This is terribly useless content. It’s almost as pitiful as the countless recolors for cosmetic items sold at full price that you seem to love so much (since you keep adding more and more, I assume you really like it).

You had a good idea and a good implementation — operations.
Short missions, with limits. So many opportunities!

But there are only two of them… two!
There need to be twenty-two!!

What do I want to see in the game?

Stationary turrets, playing with bots on your side (there is a war, after all), active flights on Valkyries, I want to shoot from a Leman Russ, I want a Leman Russ to shoot at me, I want to ride in a Chimera, I want to kill at least one Chaos Space Marine — with a big boom at least.
New Chaos bosses (a lot of them), with interesting and new mechanics, new daemons, new human bosses with a variety of deadly weapons — flamers, volkites, heavy bolters, lascannons. Groups of enemies with all this stuff. NURGLINGS!

Asymmetric tasks that require cooperation, new class mechanics during missions — with dogs, with drugs, with prayers. A lot of new active items — dozens (you added only a couple in your boring expeditions). Maybe some acts of belief from zealots, some hint that the Emperor protects. Hostage rescue, escort missions, convoy missions, chase missions, missions with class restrictions, missions where we need specific builds, missions focused almost entirely on shooting… and more, and more.

Do you lack ideas? Easy!

Operation: Sewage
The group should go through very narrow sewage tunnels.
More complexity? Add labyrinth logic with random paths or minigames that define your route.
Need asymmetry and class limits? Turn the lights off — the group must include an Ogryn who carries a huge flashlight cannon.
New mechanic? The flashlight makes enemies stagger or flee.

Operation: To the Sky
The group should climb up using ladders and tight lifts to reach a high point.
There is a change in direction — you constantly move upward instead of forward.
New mechanic? You can push enemy packs off the edge.
More complexity? Pox gas rises from below continuously (classic, I know).

Operation: No One Step Behind
The group must cover the retreat of regular troops, falling back to prepared positions in several stages.
New mechanic? Add stationary turrets.
More complexity? Enemies attack not only you, but also the retreating NPCs — and they must survive.

It’s not that hard to create original and interesting content if you have some imagination.
It’s your job to have imagination, after all.

WH40K is probably the most popular techno-fantasy setting right now… There are so many opportunities.
And what do you offer?

“Wow man, we’re so glad to introduce a new super activity — so new and so active you’ve never experienced anything like it before.
Grind 250,000 pieces of very important stuff…
and you’ll be rewarded with some almost identical recolored (recolor is very important, remember!) cosmetic sets.”

What a shame…

Btw:
When you released Hivescum, you talked about “the first dual-wielding weapons in Darktide” — but there are no real dual-wielding mechanics. This is just an in-game model. You could add a random “di1do” model and call it “the first flexible weapon in the game”… but that’s literally nothing.

Your “new expeditions” are the same — nothing.
