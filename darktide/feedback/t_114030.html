To put this in a short TL;DR, the recycled use of the Inferno event’s visual modifier is horrible to deal with when you’re colourblind, and I would ask Fatshark to consider putting in an event mission which does not force you to deal with such visual modifiers, or find some other way to ease or ameliorate the burden of being forced to play through such visual modifiers because its part of an event.

To put it in a longer form, I am colourblind. Red-green, to be precise, and I am part of the 8.5-9% of people worldwide who have a form of colourblindess. For those whose eyes and brains can perceive colours correctly it might seem to be an unimportant thing but it has very real physical impacts.

The Inferno Event earlier this year was a nightmare to get through. Having to do missions which were lit about 40% by red-orange lighting was a horror story for me. It was a very real struggle and I was glad when it was done and I immediately didn’t play for some time because of it. At the time I thought it would be something that was used once and we wouldn’t see it again, so why comment about it?

In the circumstances of this visual modifier, what the brain is being asked to do is comprehend many multiple fast-moving shapes in a colour-spectrum the eyes/brain struggle with and to comprehend different enemy types, what they are doing, and to react to them. It is not that the brain cannot see the colours at all, but it might lag in realising they are there.[1] It leads me to play in an uncomfortable, anxious, and panicked manner, as though I were in a stressful situation - such as trying to rescue the team whilst being last man standing - which alone made me want to Alt+F4 out of the mission earlier, nevermind the pain of having a headache thrust upon me.

I’ve had difficulties already with the overzealous use of green at times in the game; getting the Pox Gas mission penance done was hard on me. I’ve had difficulties in other games too where visual choices which could not be changed at my end have been highly problematic.[2]Now, after playing just one mission and after a couple of days off from the game when the modifier wasn’t there, I have a headache that feels like a frozen pick axe is being sunk into my skull and moved around. It hurts. That was from one mission, and I can still feel the anxiety I mentioned above from going through that single mission. Normally, I could play to my heart’s content in Darktide, but now I’m spending my gaming time writing this instead.

I would implore you, Fatshark, to maybe include one event mission in future that does not have any of those visual effects during events, which would be clearly marked, so I don’t have to go through physical pain just because of your event. Or maybe allow me to change colours somehow so I am not reduced to being in pain because of what is probably a really awesome visual effect so long as you’re not colourblind. I know only so much can be done, but given the struggle I had with the Inferno event, I didn’t want to go through that again.

For an example of how horrible it is, looking at this screenshot I took midmission is super uncomfortable for me. As in it makes my head hurt more with this headache. Imagine how much worse it is with the flames flickering, sudden explosions, the vomit from Beasts of Nurgle, the shields of Captains, etc. It is really bad.
