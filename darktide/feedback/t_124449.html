Let’s say that your stimm has a 60 second cooldown. When activating the Stimm Supply ability the cooldown for your stimm begins immediately, so you have 60 seconds before it comes back assuming Sample Collector is ignored. However, when using the stimm on yourself the cooldown only activates once the duration of the stimm’s active effect ends, so if your stimm lasts the base 15 seconds you’re waiting 75 seconds from its activation or 80 seconds if it’s extended by Long Lasting.

Is this intended as an incentive for Stimm Supply over self-use? The discrepancy seems strange and it makes the Maxed Out Chems modifier particularly difficult to work with without Stimm Supply. Is it possible that the cooldown could be adjusted for self-use such that the cooldown begins when the stimm is applied?
