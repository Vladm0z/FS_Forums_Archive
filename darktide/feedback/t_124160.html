Feedback: Please give us a toggle to disable the jarring cinematic Valkyrie drop-in camera cuts

7 Posts • 128 Views
W
Wherbanan • 2026-07-17 18:20:30

I’m just going to be blunt: the jump-cuts added to the Valkyrie ride-in sequence are awful.

​What was supposed to feel “cinematic” completely misses the mark. Instead of building immersion before a drop, we are forced to stare at janky, poorly framed cuts of severed legs, half-rendered faces, and headless torso models. It looks incredibly unpolished and pulls players right out of the grimdark atmosphere before the mission even starts. Games like Space Marine 2 prove that keeping a steady, continuous camera angle creates a significantly more grounded and epic pre-mission vibe.

​Worse than the aesthetics, this change has actively hurt performance.

​Forcing the engine to rapidly cull, render, and reload assets from completely different angles while the game is already under heavy loading stress is causing noticeable frame drops, micro-stutters, and lag right as players control their operatives.

​Please, Fatshark, give us a native toggle in the settings to disable these cinematic camera cuts. Let us lock the camera to a single, stable angle. The community wants to look at the gear they worked hard to earn,not a broken, stuttering slideshow of random body parts.

Signed by: Mr. BigBanan
