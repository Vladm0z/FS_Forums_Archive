The Veteran’s Power Sword is in a strange spot balance-wise because it can be very strong, but its effectiveness is heavily reliant on the two extra energised swings provided by the Power Cycler blessing. As the weapon has only a single energised swing per activation by default, Power Cycler practically becomes a necessity on all builds using the weapon and leaves you with only one other blessing slot to tinker with.

I expect that taking one chained energised hit from tiers 3-4 of Power Cycler and moving it to the base weapon would allow other blessing combinations to become viable, giving the Power Sword a good amount of flexibility in how it can be built. One swing is rarely ever enough, but two should be plenty in at least a handful of situations.
