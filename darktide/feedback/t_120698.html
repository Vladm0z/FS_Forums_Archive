  1. Swap the Combat Abilities around
    image656×205 56.5 KB

Re-adapting the Combat Abilities to match the talent changes above, as well as the Aura change

  1. Create another either/or option on the tree
    image631×222 35.3 KB

  • Pickpocket
  • Long Lasting + Blessed Stimms hybrid node

What does this achieve?

  • It prevents Rampage from getting Pickpocket
  • It allows Stimm Crate to choose either better support (By giving teammates stimms for longer and better corruption clearing) or more toxins (pickpocket for needle pistol)
  • Desperado is no longer forced to pick Long Lasting
  1. Swap the bottom tree in the exact same way the Combat Abilities were moved around

Just for consistency of course, due to the combat abilities being moved
