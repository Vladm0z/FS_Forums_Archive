That hellscape of a salvage penance…I AM FREEEEEE!!!

and thanks alone to that singular penance:

  • I will likely never ever play expeditions again, despite actually quite liking it at launch
  • Many of my early games were ruined by people speed running scrap and then gate. I had to play POI alone and then often didn’t get to gate in time.
  • I then became one of those speed runners who ruined others’ games. I knew I was doing it but this penance was so painful I just wanted it over with and had to start blocking people who weren’t speed running scrap
  • It meant I missed most of the skulls event as I was doing nothing but scrap hunting for every second of playing
  • It meant I played nothing else in DT except a single 5 zone map in expeditions for weeks and weeks of playing.

Many of us who play DT are neurodivergent and we are compelled to finish penances. Heck, I’m sure many NT players also feel compelled, so this penance felt genuinely cruel.

It ruins the game mode for penance hunter and teammate alike and was totally unnecessary.

It has poisoned the well and could well have single handedly killed the game mode. I certainly no longer want to look at another piece of scrap ever again.

I absolutely love penances, as they bring genuine and meaningful dopamine-fuelled goals (and sometimes nice cosmetic rewards!!) but please consider the compulsion of many of your players when making them.

At least with poxbreaker, you could use any mission, mode and condition, but to limit such a monstrous penance to a single mode (and essentially a single map) was not fair.

I have now completed it but I want the team to consider the above as it can have a pretty serious knock on.

Anyone I have met who’s mining scrap have all said the same thing. They will not play expeditions again after this souring penance has ruined it for them. It’s single-handedly sickened them to the mode.
