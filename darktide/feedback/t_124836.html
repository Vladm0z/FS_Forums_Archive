I use two marks-- the oneshot one and the burst fire.

I don’t use deadshot, instead I use Marksman next to it. It helps with oneshots without crits for the first 10 seconds of activating exe stance. I just never, ever liked deadshot for some reason ever since they added it. I’m not saying it’s weak, I just don’t like it.

At the same time I always use 10% attack speed in all Vet builds because I like playing off-meta stuff like Falchion, Heavy Sword, etc. These melees feel awful without extra attack speed (to me).

I also use Longshot, it makes the gun more consistent against scab gunners specifically, since I often also use agripina vigilant which deals less damage to them, so it needs every damage buff it can get to still oneshot them. Without it, I see situations where they have like ~10% left after a headshot.

I also don’t use 25 toughness node in the middle. I ALWAYS take that one in Havoc, but in auric… there’s really no need. At least not for this build where you shoot from the backline.

Same for tunnel vision. I use it in hellbore builds, but not here. Exhilirating Takedown + Confirmed Kill + toughness regen from Exe Stance is enough in most scenarios.

Overall your build is very on-point. I also use Revive Speed on curios (on every class, actually).
