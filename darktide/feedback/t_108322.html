Just to start, I play almost exclusively thunder hammer on zealot and have been since the beta, so I have a lot of hours swinging it around.

I want to talk about the changes to the light attack pattern, its been a while since the changes and I’ve tried really hard to like the new pattern but it’s just objectively worse than the old one.

Previously, you were able to chain the overhead light attack after any heavy attack. But currently you can only chain the light overhead after heavy 1 (Right to left). If you try to light attack after heavy 2 (left to right), you get the new horizontal light attack.

Having access to the vertical attack from both sides of the heavy combo was extremely useful against bulwarks and mixed hordes. And since the light attacks have no cleave, there is almost no situation where I would choose the horizontal light attack over the heavy attack. If I want a right to left horizontal swing in that moment, I’ll just heavy attack instead.

Everything else about the crucis changes have been great, just please change that useless horizontal light attack back into the vertical one.
