Hello.

I’m sure this topic came up before, but I think it’s worth mentioning again. This is NOT about how viable they are. They could give +50% damage done and +50% damage reduction while in the cloud, and I would still have the same issue with them.

And that issue is that their downside affects team mates, as well. Taking away visibility in a game like Darktide is huge.

NO other ability can disrupt other players gameplay, or make that match less fun for other players like Smoke Grenade does.

And nor it should. I don’t know the best course of action to remedy this, but how it is now (and has been for years) is still not okay with me. The only upside is that.. almost no one seems to take them, in Auric at least.

Thanks for reading.
