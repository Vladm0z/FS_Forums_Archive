I know I’m really late to this kind of discussion but I’ve played Hive Scum a lot and I have Opinions® on their juices. Namely, how the pricing of juice works, and the opportunity cost from how the trees are structured.

Because each tree gets more expensive the deeper you go into it, and the fact that the buffs on the final tier aren’t really that big of standouts (the final nodes of the damage resist tree are especially mediocre for the point investment), it’s almost never worth it to go more than 4 nodes deep into a tree. Why would I take Wildfire V for another measly 4% power and 25% finesse when I could spend just one extra point and have 2 extra seconds of juice cooldown for 12% attack speed instead?

I think the HS juice tree needs to be reworked, because the buffs for the final tiers are cool, but I just never want to take them.

The strength branch of the stimm lab has good capstone nodes. The others are kind of lacking unless you need reload speed for a gun build. I recommend maxing strength with whatever works best for your build and then go 2-3 nodes deep in the attack speed and/or damage resist branches. The main exception is when playing spam crate for corruption immunity (where you want 15 CD stimm) or a Maxed Out Chems build without Stimm Supply (where you probably want <45 CD for Chem Dep uptime).

Cooldown branch isn’t that great unless you’re doing stimm share, and even then I consider it the weakest branch. For example, 2 nodes into CD branch gives 12.5% CDR and increases your stimm CD by 6 seconds. But 12.5% CDR (assuming no other CDR) over the 15 second duration of the stimm gives only (1-(1/1.125))*15 = 1.67 seconds reduction. And those first couple nodes are more efficient than the later ones, so the value proposition drops off pretty rapidly. If you have additional CDR in your build from Chem Dep or whatever, this is even less impactful.
