The bugs are the biggest reason the demotion system is just entirely anti-fun. It’d be one thing if you had three legit chances to clear a level before you get demoted, but in those three runs, any of a number of things can happen to just rob you of a chance. In every run I play, it usually goes like this:

First run; someone or someone’s are still warming up/getting used to this current set of conditions, we wipe.

Okay, not a big deal, we can try again. This time, we’re on point, going fine, and then some kind of buggy nonsense kills someone. The rest try valiantly, perhaps even clutch and rez the whole team, but if the game spawns twins or something, then that just isn’t possible because you can’t get space on the melee twin and the plasma twin can always interrupt your rezzes if he has LoS. Perhaps we even recover and THEN another bug happens. Perhaps some people crash at a critical moment, and in the time they’re gone things spiral out of control.

And now we’re all annoyed, feeling robbed, and try one more time - and the server crashes, kicking all of us and taking that last chance.
