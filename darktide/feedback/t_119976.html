So, after trying Hive Scum in the update in Auric, Expeditions (Auric) and Havoc 40, I think there’s 3 pain points that do need to be addressed (a buff, a rework, and a nerf)

The Buff

With Pickpocket getting nerfed (it’s a good nerf), it is objectively true that some ranged weapons became undesirable to run with Desperado builds

I always said that Desperado itself needed a buff, while Pickpocket deserved a nerf

So with this said, here’s my proposed change to Desperado:

Timer on kill is increased for slower firing weapons

Netting a kill on a highlighted target currently gives you 1s of extension regardless of your choice, you can clearly notice how this punishes slower firing weapons since they just can’t keep firing as fast and killing everything quickly

All weapons would remain 1s extension on kill, with the following exceptions:

2s per kill instead of 1s:
Combat Shotgun
Dual Pistols

3s per kill instead of 1s:
Revolver
Double Barrel Shotgun
Bolt Pistol

The Rework

It’s not a surprise that Rampage Scum is currently OP, and while yes exhaustion being completely removed was a bad idea and does help with Rampage being OP, there’s one talent that is flying under the radar which heavily contributes to this but it’s barely being mentioned

Hyper-Violence

Hyper-Violence is a talent essentially made to help you snowball, which is good for this kind of playstyle but it has a big flaw

It procs with trash mobs

Since you can deal a +3000 dmg stab on a Pox Walker, you get a HUGE damage boost on the following hits, essentially allowing you to stab a Poxwalkers, and then turn into 2 shotting a Crushers even in H40 because of this

So the rework would be simple

  • Increased overkill damage bonus from 25% to 30%
  • Now it can only trigger from overkill damage on Elites, Specials and Bosses

Snowballing is cool and fun, but make the ball start going once you start shredding elites

The Nerf

The new talent added is absolutely busted and degenerate, and I see absolutely no one talking about it

Street Tough

Street Tough is straight up Until Death levels of power, for being a talent that “you might take or not”

The cooldown is just 8s, it’s an extreme get out of jail free card and it can even free You in specific situations from dogs and such (literally better than the Zealot talent for that at this point)

The cooldown defenitely needs to be lowered down MASSIVELY to 60s (at the very least), but if you want to you can increase the movement speed to 20% for 5s instead of it being 10% for 3s
