There is no denying that the new arbites class is definitely fun addition. However, at the moment, he is also the most lacking in the weapons department compared to rest of the available classes. So here are a few suggestion for what kind of new toys we can give our favorite lawman.

Shield and Shredder pistol combo. We already have one gun and shield combo, so there is no real harm in adding another. Plus, the shredder pistol is aimed towards rapid fire mob control unlike the revolver and shield combo that we currently have, so it would be a nice variant.

The Whip. Given the utter brutality of the warhammer 40k universe, it would make complete sense for the arbites to wield whips in order to enforce the emperor’s will. I believe they are called Neuro and/or Power flails, and they would be exciting addition since we have never had whip style weapon in any of the tide games so far.
