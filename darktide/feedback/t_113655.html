Saw a post with feedback for havoc, i thought it would be good to do one of my own so that there is just more of it going around heres a link to the one in im talking about link

I got to assignment rank 40 after a little less than two weeks. I’ve been grinding h40s and helping others get their rank up too because I think it’s fun. I genuinely enjoy havoc and it has scratched the itch that the tide series has introduced to me.

There are three things I want to do here, comment about the current havoc mutators in their current state, suggest a change that would help them and suggest systems that would help havoc as a whole.

Rotten Armor:

I don’t mind this mutator, I think it’s good because it is a big threat that you know is coming and can adjust your group comp accordingly which is behavior i think is good for havoc.

If anything, I would slow down their movement speed slightly but honestly the only time a problem with this mutator comes in is when it’s paired with gardens.

Encroaching Gardens:

I think this mutator is good as a concept but it needs some adjusting. I like the idea of creating high value targets, this creates value for a sniping class/ high single target damage weapons.

I would change it such that the healing enemy stops healing when it takes damage. With a cooldown of 5 seconds (or something) after not taking direct damage (dots would not proc this) this punishes groups that disengage with enemies.

OR I would change it such that the healing enemy can only heal a fixed amount in total

OR the healing per second is split between some amount of enemies around it, so that its not 1200/s for all enemies, its more like 1200/s divided by 5 if there are 5 enemies around.

Any of these would be a huge nerf for gardens, I’m not usually a fan of nerfs but this mutator is a bit too oppressive and needs a change in some form.

Corrupted Stims:

I like this mutator, removing our ability to stagger enemies so easily is a good thing, it’s especially good when it’s not all enemies. I think this is a very good shake up for player to enemy interaction. The indicator needs to be more pronounced so you know exactly what you’re up against.

I would make it so that there are no unkillable enemies under any circumstance. Enemies being able to be healed is already a pretty sus mechanic in horde game but unkillable is just too much. If they are unkillable it needs to be abundantly obvious like with the captain shields or just not possible.

Moebian

This is a great mutator for a couple reasons. The biggest reason why I think it’s great is it’s something you can easily build for in your group composition which again, i think that should be the primary focus of all the mutators; to reward groups for building for the mutator rolls which makes each havoc game more unique. Another reason why I think it’s good is because it just increases the density of enemies.

I think something that would make this mutator even more interesting is if it modified the normal carapace enemies to also have flak armor instead but they’re given speed/ damage/ health bonuses to compensate. This would further incentivize players building for mutators by making this the “flak mutator”

Cranial Corruption

This is a good mutator, it might be too “easy” but again it adds variety to the player and enemy interaction which is a good thing to keep adding and making more prevalent to add uniqueness to the mutator roll for each havoc assignment.

I think seeing hordes of it might be funny, it’s a pretty “free” mutator so it might need a little buff. Not sure how I would do that though honestly.

Pox Gas

This is bad, bad bad bad. Zoning players and forcing them out of their favorite little spots is fine, I think it’s good to have mutators that change the map and player interaction (you see where im going here). This is just far too punishing, it needs to be toned downed greatly.

If I were to just change one thing about it, I would give the gas spewers a health pool that the player can destroy to make an area safe again but if that were the case then id probably increase the number of them and make them immune to damage over time or area of effect attacks.

Ventilation Purge (Sniper)

This is a fine mutator, i dont mind it. As far as the environment change mutators go, i like the lights out one much more as it changes the scenery and make its feel more like a horror game but i will say, aside from dark communion the other maps need more natural lighting and glow effects because with lights out a lot of area are just unplayable. This one could be adjusted some but i dont personally have much to say about it but I can understand the complaints others have. I would like to see more environmental mutators more often, the fire and flames one from that event is a good example of one that ought to be in havoc for the sake of variety.

I think those are most of the mutators this season. I may be missing some lemme know ty

System Recommendations

Havoc could use three system additions

-major/ minor categorization

-tier intensities

-reroll function

Each mutator should have two properties to them, a category and a tier. What this would do is allow the devs to better balance the mutators with each other. If there is a major/ minor system then it would be possible to disallow certain combinations of mutators like garden/ rotten which has a much higher difficulty demand than other combinations of mutators. What the current system does is make each havoc mission difficulty tied to the rolled mutators instead of the assignment rank which makes the ranks mean less.

On top of that each mutator should have a tier 1-5 lets say so that there can be a more observable difference in difficulty increases during the h40 grind. For example sometimes a havoc 32 is much harder than a havoc h40 simply because of the mutators rolled and the RNG you face during the mission. The tier would increase as you increase havoc assignment level furthering the player feeling the progression gain.

Currently there are 4 mutators at the most that can roll on a havoc assignment; two mutators, emperors fading light and a possible 4th environment mutator. I think this fine but it should instead be

-Major mutator

-Minor mutator

-Emperors fading light

-Environmental mutator

Major mutators would be the ones that have a higher impact on the mission like rotten armor or gardens. Minor mutators would be things like moebian or cranial which are easier to deal with. Deciding which mutators go where is definitely a nuanced thing and I think people will disagree but in turn it would be easier for the devs to design the difficulty intensities the mutator should bring based on the category it belongs to. There would need to be simple small changes to the properties of those mutators that the Tier would represent like more of that enemy or more health etc.

A havoc assignment reroll function would be the icing on the cake, but definitely not necessary to improve havoc in a meaningful way. There just isnt a good way to dump the currencies we get in game and this could be a place to do that.

I’ll add some bonus system changes. They ought to remove deranking, it’s overall bad for the community. We also could use a selection option to choose the havoc assignment rank we play, maybe as a reward for finishing 40 you can choose to do a h30 if you want.

Overall i enjoy havoc and think its been an excellent addition to the game. I think it needs some work but the foundation is there. As far as powercreep goes.. its kinda unavoidable and thats been the case in every game ive played that isnt a hyper competitive PVP lobby game. I dont know how people expect there not to be powercreep. Havoc is important to combat what the implications of powercreep brings, the game needs ridiculously challenging game modes.
