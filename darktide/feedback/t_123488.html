Integrated Refraction Emitter feels bad. Here are some suggestions

20 Posts • 355 Views
A
AllinForms • 2026-07-01 20:44:27

First thing’s first: No one is going to take Refraction Emitter (which will be referred to as bubble for the rest of this post) if it consumes grenades. Every other blitz in the game that uses grenades benefits the whole team in some capacity, while bubble is completely selfish.

Which is more valuable for the team: Blowing up every elite in the room three times, or being individually immune to ranged damage for 12 seconds 3 times? If I had to choose between the two, I know who I want the grenade to go to every time.

I really want to like bubble, i really really do. It’s super satisfying to use, but I can’t justify the cost of bringing it. Not when there’s Scum or Ogryn who can make much better use of the grenades. Which means that I’ll be harming the team by picking up grenades, or only get three uses of my blitz for the whole map. Both options feel awful.

So! Here’s some suggestions:

Make it cost capacitance AND also have a cooldown (30 sec, 45 sec, 60 sec, however long it needs to be). This way it can’t be spammed.

Alternatively, give it its own resource that behaves somewhat like a battery. When it’s turned on it activates and starts draining the battery, when its turned off the battery slowly charges over time.

Alternatively again, give the bubble hit points. When the bubble’s hitpoint threshold is exceeded it pops in an electric burst. Have the bubble cost capacitance with a cooldown like the first option.

Discussion and suggestions welcome.

I’m of several minds of how they can address the refraction bubble.

The easiest solution might be to just to fold some of the modifiers into the base blitz talent to make it cheap to spec into and then give it a hilarious number of charges. Like 8-10 uses.

On the other hand, they could go the capacitance route that is such a popular suggestion. And of course it’s popular, players don’t like being constrained by limited resources. (Even if resource management and usage is a significant part of the game play and game skill.) And noospheric command clearly demonstrates how good a capacitance powered blitz can be. There are lots of ways Fatshark could take this concept. I can easily imagine leaving it as is, but just implementing a mild capacitance cost + significant cooldown. But I can also imagine it having no modifier talents and a low cooldown, where the catch is that you gain the effects of the current modifier talents by letting it charge up more (similar to how voltaic emitter works.) Among other sundry mechanics.

And on the gripping hand, I doubt whether either of these routes can really get players to like the blitz. Most of the other defensive blitz options (like smokes, blinders, stunstorm) haven’t been widely enjoyed by players historically. And pretty much all of them, especially the ones that are more widely liked (smite & shock mines) have significant team utility baked in. In comparison the refraction field is purely selfish.

To redress this it might also be beneficial to let players keep their base servo-skull even if they pick the refraction emitter, and possibly the arc grenade too. That way the skitarii always has their hacking/debuffing utility tool available regardless of build choices. Or maybe they should just replace the personal bubble with another team psyker bubble that just happens to use grenades.
