This is what happens when you pile on new game “mechanics” on the top of poorly thought out core combat changes we have seen since VT2.

Unlike VT2 ragers, DT ragers do NOT run out of stamina, and can continue their combos forever. Also unlike VT2, in Darktide dodging stops stamina regen. Enter 20 strong Havoc ragers mixed in with some healers. Don’t forget our old friend Hyper-Density

Contaminated Stims is one of those poorly thought out Havoc additions. Probably more so than Rotten Armour and Garden.

In general too many things in Darktide are part of the core combat design, and then are allowed to by IGNORED either by player or “enemies” through blessings, talents and difficulty modifiers. This undermines the whole combat flow and leads to plenty of balancing issues.
