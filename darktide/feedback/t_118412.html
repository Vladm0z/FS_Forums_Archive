Not sticking with your team means teammates have more enemies on them. And when the Zealot is struggling with enemies aggroed on him, and he uses Shroudfield, the enemy’s AI immediately shifts its attention to whoever is still alive, in that case the teammates.

The loner aura only added onto it, causing some Zealots to create builds focused solely on being far away from your team and being ahead of everyone, aggroing enemies and using Shroudfield so they go to the teammates and not the Zealot.

That is why the Loner aura was removed for instance. Like Shroudfield, it promoted a toxic way of playing that goes against the game’s CO-OP design. Shroudfield however, still remains and while it is harder for people to make toxic builds and play like they used to, it’s still possible.

Stealth is generally a bad idea in CO-OP games like Darktide for the reasons I stated above (it’s also why I feel the same way about the Infiltrate ability of the Veteran, but it’s not nearly as busted as Shroudfield so almost no one uses it).
