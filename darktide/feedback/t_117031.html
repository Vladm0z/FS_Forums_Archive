17 Posts • 299 Views
j
junkoito • 2025-12-21 07:38:40

The ranged Keystone Vulture Mark, on the Hive Ganger, doesn’t feel like it was designed for the Ganger. Not only is the Vulture Mark feel like a sniper talent and feel out of place with the guns blazing Desperado ganger, it doesn’t really have enough uptime, benefits, or reliability the other three Keystones have.

The buff disappears completely instead of decaying one stack at a time, have short uptime of 8(12 with talent) seconds, and have a narrower condition for activating, which does not fit the feel of the all or nothing ganger imo.

The argument could be made that its stronger on higher difficulties where elites and specials are more numerous, but Adrenaline Frenzy and Chemical Dependency are both more reliable in its consistency, and doesn’t need specials or elites to activate.

As to why this is a problem, elites and specials are high priority targets usually killed first, and any kills your teammates make on them, is directly depleting your resources. This thus incentivises rushing, bolstering the feeling that getting the first kills on any elites and specials while running Vulture mark is severely important, even to the detriment of your other teammates.
