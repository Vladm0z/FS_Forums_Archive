Hello!

New to the forums here. I recently came back to Darktide about a month ago and have already dumped 100 hours into it and haven’t slowed. I love what Fatshark’s done to the game since I first played it. And recently I’ve been sucked into expeditions. With that said, there’s one elephant in the room with the gameplay loop that bugs me and I think could actually be solved in a very Darktide way: radiation.

As of now, radiation is a static timer before your squad gets one-shot - aside from skitari, of course. I believe a system where picking up or purchasing rad stims or there being rad stations and having radiation just be a constant, slow pressure would be a far more interesting mechanic than: “hurry before the clock stops or you’ll die!”

And these types of things can be more common/rare or adjusted based on difficulty. Being able to buy radiation stims or gas mask filters or something in deadzones would be sick. Or finding rad stations similar to med stations out in the wastes would be cool too. I just feel like there’s a lot of opportunity to grow the radiation mechanic from being an arbitrary timer to a mechanic exclusively interesting to the expedition game modes.

What are your thoughts on this? For me, it’s the only thing that sucks the fun after going 30 mins into an expedition to have your entire team become one-shot and wipe while extracting.
