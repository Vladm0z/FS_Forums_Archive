Put a few hundred hours into HS, here’s my thoughts: they’re too durable and deal too much damage.

Extra pouches just needs to go, you can’t rebalance grenade capacity while that exists. It’ll always make it so they either have too many rockets/chembombs or not enough. 2 is a perfectly fine value to settle on.

If exhaustion is gonna stay removed from Rampage then the buffs that active ability gives you needs to be halved, it’s just too strong for what it is. Desperado and Juicebox can GF themselves, I hate them and they’re bad and I don’t want to hear your opinion if you play H40 and they’re actually the greatest things ever. I don’t play H40, I play Auric, and yes I’m gonna be elitist about how I play an easier difficulty.

There needs to be CDR added to the other keystones to make them worth using. Darktide is balanced around cooldown and elite spam and only having access to CDR via a single keystone, pigeonholing every possible build you could make towards it, just doesn’t sit right with me. +20% additive critchance at 4 juiceboost stacks is also ludicrous. I’d drop it to 1.5% crit per stack, same as Flensing Protocols.

Dodging is your best defensive mechanic in DT and between this and the insane toughness regen HS have through melee it ends up in this really weird position where you’ve invincible until you take a crusher overhead and then you just explode. Pretty much the only advantage Zealot has over HS is that they have Until Death to save them when they inevitably eat said crusher overhead - HS has them beat in every other capacity through melee damage, speed, anti-armor, chip durability, crit, I could go on. Even the durability thing isn’t really true, with Hive City Brawler and Rampage you can tank a crusher overhead no problem.

Hyper Violent is fine in concept but in practice the way it scales infinitely up to and including oneshotting bosses with a light attack from shivs is just absurd. Calculate the damage before the HV buff and use that to reset the HV buff instead of scaling it upon itself.

The juice tree sucks and is inflexible. There’s never any reason to go down the CDR or TDR paths. The best option is to just go 4 deep into attack speed and power, giving you a 55 second cooldown, more than enough to allow Sample Collector to do its work if you’re awake and paying attention to your screen.

Overall they’re very exhilarating to play but their tree is incredibly inflexible with what works and what doesn’t, and of course they’re blatantly overtuned with how much damage they can output.
