Dueling Sword has Uncanny and Agile, an inherent Weakspot and Vs Carapace multiplier, good dodge distance, sprint speed and access to Movetech. Every Veteran that ticks Skirmisher and doesn’t use this weapon or a Knife is effectively handicapping himself.

There is 0 reason to take weaponry that doesn’t deal well with Carapace armor, especially in Havoc. Your ranged options are Bolter/Plasma & Flamethrower with Flamethrower being detrimental to what the Zealot should do.(Be a frontline and not a scaredy cat hiding behind the team)

Is it really that difficult to adress the armor problem carapace poses for loadouts that are just utterly useless against it?

If you can’t deal with Carapace your build isn’t viable and what makes your weapon viable is good Headshot Damage & Rending.

In VT2 the daggers on Shade or Billhook WHC excel for that very reason in Cataclysm + Lobbies, the weapons have good penetration on Heavies, good headshot damage & additional Scaling in their trees.

This balance philosophy is alot better in my opinion vastly superior to putting 90% of your builds strength into the melee weapon and it’s blessing as opposed to the passive tree.

Can we just accept that project Uncanny was a failure, go back to the drawing board and give people an actual reason to use weapons they like without them being terrible picks?

When it comes to parrying, the one area where Devil’s Claw surpasses DS is the duration of the parry window.

With DS, however, the parry window is shorter, so even if you still have stamina, you’ll end up taking damage if you’re attacked continuously.
