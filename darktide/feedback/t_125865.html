Note that this is based on H40, because I don’t think there’s much point in balancing the class to be only good enough for Auric.

Executioner’s Stance is the closest thing Veteran has to a truly ranged-focused ability, and I want it to succeed just as much as FS does. However, I think FS should change its approach slightly.

Most useful and successful abilities have some form of survivability built into them. For example:

  • Rampage: has knockback.
  • Voice of Command: has stagger.
  • Venting Shriek: has stagger.
  • Shroudfield: has crazy boss damage, but it can also be used defensively to revive teammates or get yourself out of trouble.
  • Chordclaw: has crazy boss damage, and if you don’t know, you can also use it to push armored enemies away in tight spaces, such as stairs, to give yourself room to reposition.

You get the idea.

Currently, Executioner’s Stance is basically a damage-boosting ability with little to no survivability. The small amount of toughness it gives means very little. Smart players play around their CDR, being more aggressive or passive depending on the availability of their career skill. Being aggressive on the highest difficulty is risky, and your career skill is often what saves you when you miscalculate that risk.

With that said, I think FS should change its approach to Executioner’s Stance and move away from the idea that Veteran will always have enough space to sit in the backline and snipe. Veteran’s melee is one of the weakest in the game, and we all know the game spawns enemies behind you almost as often as it sends them from the front. Because of that, Executioner’s Stance needs better survivability.

I would even be fine with lowering its damage bonus if that meant giving it better survivability.

  • The ability used to replenish significantly more toughness instantly, but that was removed.
  • The ability used to automatically reload your gun, but that was also removed.

A lot of people may not be familiar with this, but before the skill tree became a thing, Executioner’s Stance had these effects. We need to move away from the idea that Veteran always has free time to safely sit in the backline. I would even be fine with reducing the damage bonus from 25% to 15-20% if bringing these effects back would otherwise make the ability overtuned.

The auto-reload would allow for longer periods of continuous fire, which is also a form of survivability for many weapons. With a Bolter, for example, it could mean killing a few more Crushers or Maulers before having to reload. With an autogun, it means maintaining suppression for longer, which can help control incoming ranged fire.

Instant toughness replenishment would also be much more useful than replenishing toughness every second. I don’t really understand the reasoning behind the current approach. Are you expecting players to take a small amount of damage every second? That’s usually not how it happens. In a real situation, you mess up a defensive mechanic and lose 80% of your toughness almost instantly. A few more bullets hit you and you’re dead.

Other classes have abilities like Rampage, Voice of Command, Venting Shriek, and so on that provide defensive tools. Players can use those abilities without even caring about their damage output, simply to buy themselves another two or three seconds to survive, reposition, and recover. On H40, those few seconds can be absolutely critical.

That’s the kind of survivability I think Executioner’s Stance is missing.

Vertraxxx:

If you balance everything around H40, Auric will end up feeling like Uprising.

I’ve said this a thousand times, but all balance changes should be made around normal missions as the baseline, and then Havoc should be adjusted separately if its difficulty modifiers or scaling go too far.

The point I was making about balancing it around H40 is specifically about the amount of pressure and the constant ambushes you deal with there.

I’m not suggesting that Executioner’s Stance should simply be buffed across the board. I specifically said the damage bonus could be reduced to compensate for giving it better survivability.

That tradeoff would have very little impact on lower difficulties. Survivability is already much less of an issue there, while the lower damage bonus would actually make the ability weaker offensively.

H40 is useful as a stress test because it exposes weaknesses in an ability that may not be noticeable in normal missions. Executioner’s Stance works fine when you have enough space to shoot, but the problem becomes obvious when the game constantly spawns enemies behind you, collapses your position, and forces you into melee or immediate repositioning.

So I’m not saying normal missions should become as easy as Uprising because of H40 balancing. I’m saying that if an ability falls apart mechanically under heavy pressure, that weakness is worth addressing. Moving some of its power from raw damage into survivability seems like a reasonable way to do that without simply increasing its overall power.
