Most of the stub pistols blessings are pretty boring and none of them take advantage of their unique special attack, why not have some blessings that reward getting headshots with the special attack? For example:

  • special attack headshots gain increased crit chance

  • +attack speed for 5 seconds on special attack headshot

  • special headshots have a 10% chance of ricocheting into a nearby enemy’s head, this ricochet also has a 10% chance of ricocheting again

Crowbar should be able to hook open bulwark shields with its hook attacks and should have the stamina drain when dodging out of hook attacks removed and it would be perfect

Devil’s Claw isn’t a Hive Scum exclusive weapon, but should still have blessings for its unique mechanic. Things like attack speed for 3s on perfect parry or perfect parries have rending so you’re more incentivised to parry crusher overheads. These would be much more interesting than the boring blessings it already has.

I don’t really get why Fatshark introduces weapons with interesting, unique mechanics but doesnt add any blessings for them
