  4. You may cross invisible boss triggers (Havoc-only) and spawn bosses on your team.

Infiltrate is lacking, but it also resides within the Commando branch which imo has systemic issues. I also find that this branch is the most talent point starved due to the reliance on dipping into two other branches for Tactical Awareness and Precision Strikes+Fully Loaded.

Some of the more perplexing takes I’ve seen in a while. Weapon specialist is obviously useful on a number of weapons. The ranged crit bonus is very good for any high damage per shot weapon and can let you dump crit blessings on a number of guns for even more damage. The free reload talent is quite useful on any low magazine capacity gun (such as shotguns, bolt weapons, and yes, revolvers.) The melee attack speed is obviously good on any melee weapon. And, of course, the dodge bonuses are quite welcome on every bloody weapon in the game. The weaker parts of the keystone are largely confined to the other two modifiers that nobody ever uses. Both are nice in theory but more or less fall apart in the details. But weapon specialist is hardly the only keystone in that situation.

Onslaught is great on any volume of fire guns and fast melee weapons to boot. Reciprocity is still good even with stealth; it’s not like you can be invisible for the entire mission after all. Plus you have two other talent choices you can make to path through that area, so it’s not even a tax node. Precision strikes & fully loaded are in no way essential to every vet build. And it’s almost immaterial that the movement speed aura is subpar because Survivalist has been, and still is, the best aura in the game since launch. And these days it doesn’t even incur a build tax.

Yes, it is clear that Infiltrate is not that popular and for plenty of fair reasons. But being on the right ride of the vet tree is hardly one of them.
