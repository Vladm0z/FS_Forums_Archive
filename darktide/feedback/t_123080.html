I wish there was more variety in engagement ranges

9 Posts • 142 Views
Q
Quixotica • 2026-06-26 11:34:10

Played since Beta and I really feel like Dark Tide’s insistence that there always be enemies spawning in all directions 100% of the match and almost always have enemies in your face has pigeon holed its classes game play into one style. There is no such thing as clearing a room for even temporary safety as stuff just spawns constantly from any direction or can climb to any perch or balcony.

If there were segments of maps, or even just specific maps, where spawning direction was more restricted to create some engagements that are just at medium and even long range it would add much needed variety to the game. To clarify I’m not saying it shouldn’t emphasize melee as one priority, thats an important part of Dark Tides identity, what I am saying however is that medium and long range engagements shouldn’t always be “switch to ranged for two shots to kill a gunner or sniper while dodging the half dozen melee that are on top of you”. There should be segments of maps where you can clear a room, post up in a window or on a balcony and engage at range and cover fire for teammates in melee without the 100% guarantee something is going to hit you in the back if you don’t turn around after one or two shots.

When you’ve got thousands of hours in the game like I do you really start to feel how similar so many things actually play, even this brand new class feels little different in actual use, and that really comes down to how the way Dark Tide’s structured its encounter design. There should be maps that are just wall to wall enemies from every direction, there should also be maps that are almost exclusively head long pushes where enemies only really come from one direction, maps where defensive hold out positions can be cleared of enemies that they can’t spawn in and have to run to so that ranged focus classes can cover melee focused ones.
