So - oldies I’ve played with; get your digs in now
Yeah yeah. I was a nub beforehand. Whatever

But; I feel like coming back to this Arbites patch has really just re-invented the game for me. Not gonna lie; I 20% hate it, 80% love it.

The 20%: I moved in to the new crafting system way-back-when with everything unlocked. Spoilt. But I didn’t need to level or craft anything.

Now I’m having to try to remember what’s the most efficient way of levelling my perks and blessings on a weapon. And Brunt will only sell me greens (so far) in the bespoke weapon tree. It’s really weird.

Thinking about how this is for a new player: regardless of my skill; I’m being imposed a levelling barrier. Ok - guess that’s common for a lot of games. But I can just buy and sell weapons with some minor upgrades to “level up”. Why? Bit weird.

Then the tiering of maps; as a lvl 2000+ across multiple avatars that’s a bit jarring. I’m pretty sure I can do higher levels, even though I am rusty. I used to do tier 4 Heresy after reaching level 5 or 6 on a new avatar and hold my own.

;;

The other part of the 20%

Graphics for me have definitely changed. I’m not sure I like it. It’s harder. More maps are darker; with high contrast fire pits. Maybe this is down to improvements to the graphics engine? Maybe a graphics settings reset? IDK - but I do know I’ve not bought any new hardware, so this is on you FS!

wtf is this new map system? It’s boring as anything. Go find my old posts and make it exciting. Get weather on there! Make it so.

Then the 80%

I’m reminded how good the game is.
I’m reminded how playing with headphones for audio positioning is a 3-tier-advantage (vs without). I don’t use spidy sense.
HTF do I level weapons again? Oh yeah. Nice. Uh. Not nice. But yeah - great - I forgot about this.
Lots of returning players; all good.
I got a dog! Yaay! Someone to look after me when I get pinned and all my team-mates are… well, not present.

All in - I’m all in again. Hurrah!

Back to the 20%.

STILL not buying the extortionately expensive cosmetics guys. Jesus. C’mon. Get a grip.
