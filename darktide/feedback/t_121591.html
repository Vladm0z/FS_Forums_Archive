This thread reminds me when I was actually spitballing an idea shortly after Expeditions with a couple friends about a roguelike mode. The basic gist was that you’d queue up for the mode and go through an abbreviated character creator, then once a team is assembled, you get put on a valkyrie and dropped off in Tertium’s sister hive.

On arriving, the team gets out with white weapons in hand as fresh characters and then start what is effectively a Chaos Wastes session. You get a procedurally generated mission with upgrades sporadically scattered around to unlock talents, new and better weapons, unique perks, etc, and once you finish the mission, you then get a branching path with another procedurally generated mission at the other end. I’d probably combine all the game modes into this, so some of the generated missions will be Expeditions, some will be normal adventure maps, and some will be Mortis Trials-esque horde encounters.

After each mission, players get a choice between two options for the next mission, the team votes, and then they go do that mission, get another round of upgrades, and proceed to the next mission. Then you do it again. And again. And again. Each time you get a little bit stronger, but enemy resistance gets even more fierce.

Then you start wondering where the Valkyrie home is. But Rannick insists we need one more, so off you go, but once it’s done, there’s still no Valkyrie, just another mission. Again, and again, and again. Until finally, inevitably, the squad is wiped and you load back into the morningstar, and no sign of that character you were just playing.
