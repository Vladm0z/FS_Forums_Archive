Disclaimer; this is all my opinion as an average player, with only around 600h of play, and playing mostly on Auric, as I do not want to wait around for a party for doing Havoc. Feel free to correct me, or offer insight and opinions on this piece.

1) TALENT TREE

I will NOT be going over all nodes, only relevant mentions.

1a. Talent tree layout, purported to enhance freedom of build.

Generally true, but instead of one tree, the skills are laid into 4 smaller trees in a clover pattern, instead of a true free for all in a cloud shape. This essentially actually makes the choices narrower, as the only way to move around the tree is through the Ability Modifier nodes.

1b. Blitzes are anemic for the grenade ammo use, and in some cases, detrimental.

Servo skulls don’t actually shoot that much, which would be understandable if Noospheric Command lasted longer at a baseline of 5-10 seconds, instead of 2 seconds for a whopping 25% charge. A flashlight on the skull would also be a good qol for people who pick this blitz, as well as separating the uses of medskull into grenade use, and the flamerskull as cooldown blitz.

Shield blitz can be detrimental, as it loses you a blitz that can help in a pinch. It doesn’t clear chaff, nor does it help with heavies, being at most, electrocuting them. Lack of uses per grenade also hampers its usability, as players would be less likely to be using them aggressively due to it being a limited resource. This one would benefit most from being rechargable to help its lack of horde clearing potential.

Arc Grenades would be fine, if not for the very delayed explosion and the arcs apparently not killing everyone in the aoe. This is the only blitz that feels okay taking grenade ammo without being rechargable.

1c. Voltaic Emitters and Advanced Combat Docrines doesn’t work 100% of the time

Voltaic Emitters doesn’t work well, even when you think it should work. A small vehicle, a box, a railing, even sometimes differences in elevation, will cause it to not work. I have stared a gunner dead in the face as I activated it, and he simply shot me because a small knee high object was between us.

Advanced Combat Docrines however, works fine most of the time but doesn’t track moving targets very well or sometimes doesn’t target that well.

2) Skitarii Weapons

2a. Power sword feels… weak?

My favourite of the skitarii weapons because it looks like a Gladius. I’m not sure why, but it doesn’t feel as strong as it should despite charging up the powered swing.

2b. Transonic Swords feel a bit sluggish and weak

Could use a pass in readability like a different grip for different modes as well as the energy going up and down, a speedup in the secondary weapon action, and perhaps a parry during the secondary weapon action? A bit sluggish which is surprising, and doesnt do as much as I thought it would on any enemies despite switching to the recommended modes.

2c. Phosphor Blast Pistol’s illumination is too low duration.

In dark modifier maps, the pistol can make the map flash on and off. Please raise the bullet’s illumination to last longer. While preferably it would be a minute or so to mimic irl flares, even just 20 seconds of light will help from making the dark map blink on and off when shooting the pistol.

I thought I’d like the idea of modifier nodes for blitz going in but they’re all way taxing. Revive skull should be built into the enhanced servo, maybe noosphere too. Then we could leave the servo skull in tact when you use the other blitz, since not having it is just a terrible trade. The arc grenade ones should be built in and the shield should regen. Purgator skull should also have a recharge system, since its so skuffed vs comparable grenades.

In general they’re balanced for a darktide that doesn’t exist and stack up unfavorably to the current one. Their restore of toughness is extremely limited so chip damage is actually a danger. Also has pretty minor node buffs vs other DLC characters that usually get a fat number buff and additional effect from a named node. Despite having a lot of those, the ones with effects+ are limited to ones that are already fairly banal for combat (like the passive reloading).
