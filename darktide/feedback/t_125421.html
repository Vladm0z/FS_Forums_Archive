really my main complaints are the rotating board content (wow this is a darktide wide problem) and lack of a mid-mission merchant. the game was not really designed with certain actions being done in a combat situation, but the asset re-use has them present for gameplay flair and to make the objectives different from one another. constantly being harassed by gunfire and trickle adds makes a lot of them tough to even initiate without stealth; a detpack takes a little over 4 seconds to plant. there’s 0% damage reduction active for a lot of these animations, unlike being on a skull or carrying a rod. I really think there should be some kind of charm you can buy only here, on the very first sections, that enable 100% damage reduction when arming detpacks or launching/planting skulls. some objectives involve tons of those. really the only reason I play stealth. which is what the game plan was when you quit on me when we were fighting that captain, I am not that troll running around playing stealth to pressure my teammates…

there’s also the huge issue of the lack of supplies, which penalize the supply-user even more. grenades are ridiculously expensive vs. grabbing ammo (50 salvage vs 10) and they can be lost. you should really be able to buy grenades in a round currently because the rechargables (as always) brutally outclass them. it wouldn’t even make the game much easier for anyone but scum, considering I haven’t adjusted their cost at all (buying a mortar in the sanctuary and a grenade refill would be way more practical and cheaper than buying 4 ogryn frag bombs mid-mission).

but really if you approach it with the sole intention of nothing mattering (this should be your default state but still) you get a ton of control on how much or how little you want to be engaged. which was the pitch of the mode, and what I think they were going for trying to balance the need for shorter missions and longer plays in the same stroke (contrary to popular belief there was a 30m version on every single board, which also guaranteed timer spawns in the sanctuary and added 10 minutes, providing essentially the same experience to a board with no time restriction at all). engaging with it for any more than that is folly. I was really only grinding it to fight packmasters but in so doing discovered you can easily set up fights against multiple bosses at once here pretty often. the game could definitely have done a tutorial better, for some reason the fact that bosses drop remnants and some points of interest can appear (as many as 6 per board) can be found by exploring, but they don’t encourage you to do this at all. hadron even tells you to leave after doing the 4 objectives, when there’s often around 400+ of the sector’s remnants to find outside of those specific spots. its definitely poorly communicated what the hell the point of any of this even is, but not telling people how to have fun with it at the maximum engagement was a ‘why’ moment to me. there’s also the slow-rolling content issue, like I can see rotten armor and rotflies being 2 seperate updates and thus maybe even a year away…like why can’t we have them now? especially with how often the game seems to spam dregs at me when I play, it’d be nice to get followed by armies of scab bombers/flamers instead of dreg ones. and those trickle elites in the scab rager flavoring would be nice, right now they’re just mauler/dreg rager or bulwarks and crushers.
