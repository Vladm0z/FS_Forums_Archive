In the latest hot-fix 1.12.3, there was basically only one fix:

  • Fixed missing auto-aim on Advanced Combat Doctrines when shooting targets moving sideways.

I swear that is the biggest understatement I’ve seen in a patch, because it didn’t just fix the “Advanced Combat Doctrines” ability, it fixed the poorly hit-registration that caused ranged weapons to constantly get fake hitmarker when shooting enemies dead center in the head, completely aside from the ACD ability.

Forget about the aimbot ability, I’ve played Darktide since the Beta and the bad hit-registration was there since day one but in the last two days since the hot-fix, using precision weapons like the Vigilante Autogun has been a blast. I’ve played so many rounds and the hit-registration feels better than ever, especially on moving or idle enemies.

That being said, now that it works properly, can we please get a Sniper Rifle or at least a scoped Marksman Rifle, because I’m convinced the main reason we never got one is because of the constant fake hits that would have been so painfully obvious with such weapons. Please give us a long-las, I have so many Scab Snipers to pay some headshots back.
