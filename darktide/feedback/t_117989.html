I find the condition of this talent is quite problematic as it anti-synergises with more advanced movesets of some weapons like Relic Blade, Crusher, Heavy Sword, Devil’s Claw, etc. Usually a player generates max stacks with a high-cleave heavy attack and spends them on a low-cleave light attack to continue a combo. With the light attack a player usually doesn’t cleave many enemies so the heavy attack only benefits from 1-2 stacks. At its current state the talent is more of a tax node than something useful.

Screenshot 2026-01-21 105345404×232 54.1 KB

But it’s easy to fix changing how it works to something like Arbitrator’s Hammer of Judgement talent. +15 or 20% melee damage for 3 seconds on hitting 3 or more enemies with a melee attack. It would make the talent more consistent and equally beneficial for both simple and complex weapon movesets.
