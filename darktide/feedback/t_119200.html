Fundamental to the Tide games is the idea of the slot system. The short version of it is that the slot system is a game mechanic that only allows a certain number of enemies to attack you in melee range at any given time. Rip’s video here covers the slot system in Darktide and to see the debug information regarding slots you can use the Slot Zones mod to visualize enemies entering and waiting for slots to open.

And as far as I can tell, the slot system that is in Darktide is more or less the same as it is in Vermintide 2. However, I think there can be adjustments here to potentially make the game more engaging and challenging. My suggestion is that we increase the number of slots perhaps as a difficulty modifier for havoc or maelstroms or just auric+ such that it matches the amount of power the players currently have access to.

With the current slot system, there are 9 normal, 8 medium, and 5 large slots. In practice, that number slots filled usually about half that number as players generally keep threats in front of them and use parts of the map geometry to cut off access to the slots. As a quick example, I increased the number of slots by 50% and decreased the radius of the slots by about 20%. I aggroed the enemies and then went invisible for this example:

What this means is that melee combat is more difficult as more enemies can be attacking you at a single time while also being in front of you. In a couple practice runs against ragers and maulers with the increased slots, I found myself unable to deal with them the standard way I’ve been dealing with them, that is to say dodging backwards and throwing out attacks while only blocking occasionally, usually while waiting for dodges to reset. Blocks and pushes felt more impactful than they do in the current setup of the game.

That multiple enemies can hit you at once is what makes expanding the slot system even useful for affecting game balance. Currently, there is no point in running BCR or stamina perks because most players don’t even get to double digit blocks in a single run unless they’re running a shield weapon. You don’t really even need to engage with blocking and pushing outside really niche situations because you can kill things quicker than they can get into position to hit you and in some cases just regen the lost toughness if you do trade a hit. The slot system plays a part in that where if you’re in a position to just kite backwards continuously, you’re only actively pressured by like 3-4 enemies at a single time. The time between their attacks and distance between the enemies allow ample opportunity to thin out the horde without ever being in much danger.

idk at a certain point, deaths from melee combat are just not that common and usually a result of some situation where an altered slot system wouldn’t make a difference. It makes the most sense to me for it to be added as a variant of Emperor’s Fading Light that maybe replaces/reduces the ranged enemy buffs. Just a different form of pressure to change how the game is played in endgame content.

I think slots are a very easily mistuned mechanic as was apparent when they were bugged for ages in VT2. Enemies attack speeds, animations, and attack frequency are all tuned around slots, as are player defensive capabilities.

I think you could potentially achieve some sort of improvement with tuning slots but it’s more likely they’d just fatshark it up and it’s probably not the kind of fundamental change the game really needs. More melee slots and no other changes in the current meta would just make faster, more evasive weapons even more valuable and also raise the value of all gameplay avenues that avoid going into melee. While it would be an interesting thing to try, I feel like redoing the slot system would require an unrealistically huge amount of work. The entire game is built around this current number of slots and a huge number of unpredictable problems may crop up by changing it.

But also, if we weaken the players - well, maybe some people would like it, but the community would be up in arms because it would feel awful. People do not enjoy it when they become weaker and slower. The game has progressed along this idea of upping the ante - a common thing in a game, not just because you want to keep players engaged, but also because the general skill level of the community goes up. This is, however, bad in that it keeps compounding problems. Now that they started down this path, I don’t think they can stop.

My suggestion would be new enemies - perhaps Daemon enemies who, when they hit you, debuff you. Could even be in different ways, like some lower your toughness regen for a time or others make your abilities go on cooldown for a few seconds (or increase the timer for it if on cooldown already). If you wanted to be really mean, some could steal ammo on hit, and hey - give some a Cloud of Flies effect that reduces the effectiveness of incoming ranged attacks.

These daemonic enemies then, could be more threatening in smaller numbers, punish face-tanking with yellow toughness, and also have any other ability needed to help bring player power under control.

slots are a fine balance tool imo. I think there should be a similar “token” system to doom eternal has but specifically for ranged enemies, as I don’t find it fun when an entire room of shooters are just lighting you up with no limitations especially in havoc, but that could just be me. I honestly don’t know how ranged enemies “interact” with slots from a ranged perspective.

The large issue I personally have with this type of expansionism is that it will not affect the current gameplay experience of players except when you’re solo/clutching, where you’re already trying to keep your distance away from the horde to minimize your slots. There is a system in place where if you stand next to a player, each overlapping slot gets covered by said player. You effectively create defensive barriers with players which, assuming the game is functional, (it’s not, hyperdensity breaking the slot system is a well known issue that I’m pretty sure jsat had a video covering back in his vt2 days like 7 years ago) means that enemies should not be able to walk through those players and attack them from those sides.

So, in theory, increasing the number of slots means players have to play tighter to each other. In practice, this is something players already do because it maximizes dps and typically hordes only come from like one or two directions unless the team is really split, and you just have to 2 and 2 and have a wall nearby and this problem still disappears.

There’s a lot of problems with the melee combat in darktide, I don’t think any of them are resolved by expanding slots. No one wants to fight 1 havoc 40 final toll rager in green gas with combat stims in melee without a free parry button that plays the game for them, let alone a group of havoc 40 ragers. Ranged combat is far easier and safer to deal with any singular enemy in the game, and what isn’t beaten by ranged combat ends up getting beaten by overpowered blitz actions or dot effects.

I personally think they need to make ranged weapons far riskier than just “you will eventually run out of ammo”. Even the weapons that are supposed to be one-handed, better dodge weapons, end up just being outright better than their other two-handed, heavier dodge counterparts.
