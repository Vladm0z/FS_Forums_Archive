So im a massive fan of Lances and/or Lance and shield combos in general.
And the Power lance in 40k while a rare weapon to see is still no exception.

And I think I have a couple of cool ideas to make it work in Darktide.
So the whole thing of the power lance is that you jab it in to a enemy usually a big one.
Disconnect the head run away and explode it.

The idea I had was that its just a normal spear like weapon which we dont have yet in darktide but the weapon special is well special. Basicly you get the abilty to remove the head and let it explode.

Basicly giving you no longer the ability to use the weapon, or making it signifigantly worse for a period of time. (either on cooldown or pickup ill get back to that)

So i had several ideas for this,
First things first when you have the spear head you would have just a good weapon. Jabbing and allowing you to cleave really well in to the lenght rather then width. Similar to the Relic Blade jab but then more range. But ofcourse less well at cleaving horizontally.

Then the weapon special
1 Just the lance when you explode you lose the weapon entirely and are forced to ranged weapon.
2 Just the lance but when you explode the head you have still the hilt left and you can fight with that as its a long stick, which would be signifigantly worse then having a pointy tip on it.
3 Lance and small Shield and when the lance explodes you still have the shield to defend and fight. (this one is my personal favorite)
2 and 3 are basicly so that your not entirely f’d over when you get swarmed

Now my initial idea was that when the spear explodes it puts it on a cooldown and eventually recovers over time and you can use the spear like normal again.
But thats not really in the spirit of darktide since you lose the head/entire spear. and just recovering it over time doesnt make much sense. Most cooldown based abilities on weapons tend to make sense as in overheating or having to charge it back up by capturing the impact of the blow and storing that as energy and stuff like that.

It would be nice if the special packed quite a punch so my idea is that you recover it when picking up the Grendate crate that recover your blizzes. This makes it feel more realistic and in the spirit of darktide.
Because the punishment of having a bad melee weapon would be quite painful. But it could help take out monstrocities in a pinch.

Now my main idea is that this weapon would fit the Zealot, Arbites.
Or perhaps you guys have a new class in mind for this weapon?
Eitherway I’d love to see it!
