JanZamoysky • 2026-06-03 21:39:15

These are the bugs I can list from the top of my head related to that ONE difficulty modifier:

  • Rituals can be triggered way too early without any player aggro, and we are talking 100+ meters, often through walls, locked doors, or 100+ meters below players (one of the desert maps)
  • In other cases Rituals do NOT trigger until you are literally on the top of them
  • In some locations they can visually spawn very late, when a player gets close to their span location
  • Their progress bars often bug out and do NOT show for all or for some players
  • Multiple rituals can spawn in locations where usually there is only one (assumed bug)
  • Ogryn’s Bull Rush ability can prematurely wake up the Demon Host, with HP proportional to the Ritual progress when aggro’ed
  • Demon Host when aggo’ed often sinks under the ground making it hard to hit
  • Ritual can cause a visual bug with green lightnings blinding one of the players
  • Cultists can spawn in walls / ceilings making it in some situations impossible to kill, or killable to early
  • Rituals muffle audio around them making the audio even worse than usual
  • Ritual can continue at a very slow pace AFTER you kill all the cultists, which makes spawning a full HP Daemonhost unavoidable (thanks @Enforest )
  • Two rituals can spawn on the top of each other, at the same exact location.

So a question for @FatsharkJulia and @FatsharkQuickpaw from me is: how many more bugs related to ONE Havoc difficulty modifier do we have to discover, for it to be removed from the rotation and fixed? At this point this modifiers has more bugs than actual gameplay features.

Those issues have been known, and expanded since the modifier was introduced to the game. A sensible thing to do here would be remove it from the rotation, test and fix the issues, and then reintroduced to the game.
