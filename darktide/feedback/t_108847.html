So uh, I’d like to suggest something that I’d love to see implemented into Darktide.

Imagine yourself playing Ogryn, filling out the entire talent tree for your perfect™ build and playing a bunch. Suddenly, you now have filled out your loadout slots and everything.

Now imagine if you wanted to switch grenade type from Frag Bomb to Big Friendly Rock on one of your loadouts.
