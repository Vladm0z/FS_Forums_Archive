TL;DR - Goal: New player retention. Campaign is a vastly improved new player intro, but first 90m of new player experience is still quite bumpy and you likely lose a lot of potential players to frustration. Get them in game faster and pick a different first mission as the current end event is brutal.

Hey Fatshark,

Huge fan of your games - have had work/life events draining my energy so in recent months rather than climbing Havoc again I’ve been working on penances or on the couch watching streams, usually new player streams. I’ve helped probably ~50ish new players at this point either with advice or hopping in game, and a pair of issues are really obvious. Note: The new campaign is a great addition, thank you!

Problem Statements:

  1. It takes at least ~45m for a new player to be able to play with their friends / get in a real game, average more like 60m.
  2. The first mission’s end-event is really, really challenging for newbies - huge arena, boss, shield mechanics.

Time and attention is finite, you only have so long to ‘hook’ a new player with a positive experience.

When I enter a new player stream (see them in char creator), I 1) let them know that all their character creation choices can be redone at a barber later, so they don’t agonize and spend 30m of their 2h gaming window on char creation, and 2) explain that they have a prologue, a cutscene, a training event, and a cutscene to get through before they can play with their friends - which takes about 45m. I often see people not get to their first real mission until a solid 80-90 minutes into their stream.

  • Suggestion #1: Move training to a later level and/or make it optional. New players don’t retain it anyway, give it to them after they’ve been through 6-7 missions.
  • Suggestion #2: Move the ‘I am a warrior’ cutscene to after winning the first mission or two (or maybe after the 4th). It’s a speed bump to playing the game for an audience that’s in evaluation period and its not personal to the player’s character.

Now, for those that go into the game because they read about it online and don’t have a friend hand-holding them, the train station captain event is absolutely brutal. I’ve watched a number of true newbie fail attempts - the team scatters to the winds in a huge arena, doesn’t know to pick each other up, doesn’t know anything about the boss shield mechanics, gets distracted focusing on the boss and gets beaten down by adds they don’t see coming and are too new to expect. It happened as recently as yesterday, and then some L30’s watching popped in to carry the second attempt. This ~frequently happens in streams I watch - but players who are not streaming basically just spent 90m of their lives to fail their first mission and I would imagine are fairly frustrated.

  • Suggestion #3: Pick a different first mission with a tighter & simpler end event. You could swap the captain missions, but I’d argue you should pick something gentle like Enclavum Baross where the main goal is just to stay alive and its easy to see things coming - throwing captain mechanics / distraction at brand new players is a bit much!

My overall goal here is better new player retention, I’ve seen folks just hands up and walk away after losing to the captain nearly 2h into their stream and I can only imagine what the rate is for those that don’t have people helping them. I’m sure you have the data; would be super curious as to the retention rate after attempting first mission as a solo-queue player vs. non.
