Sharing some random persons first experience playing Spillway.

Doing the 3rd campaign mission to unlock spillway. Not a single person joins the whole time. I’m playing with level 1 bots at 7pm on a Sunday. And it’s a slog. Took me 45’ to get to the end event (I lost time running around in circles unsure where I was supposed to go until I go the bot respawn markers).

So, it’s a grind to get the end event at which point I do a double take because I feel like I just teleported into the end of Enchanter’s Lair (which is fine). I die in the first minute, I think to some AoE crap on the ground? Unclear. I was in the radius of a med kit so I just have no clue what took me down, which is not a great feeling since I’m not sure how to not have that happen next time. Any sort of indicator as to what is happening would be helpful. Obviously, the bots were instadowned (expected).

Anyway. I’m pretty shocked not a single person was playing in the early evening on the first Sunday since the patch came out. But I guess just not many people are playing this game anymore. But, playing a co-op game with bots was a pretty lame way to get introduced to the new map.

This was on Auric. I’m not particularly interested in dropping down difficulty just to unlock the map. I guess I’ll try again some other time and hope someone joins.

Otherwise, I thought the map looked cool. I liked seeing the glowing enemies from Havoc (would love to have regular Auric games get spicy modifiers sometimes), though I’m not entirely sure what their buff was. In general, it seemed like it would be a fun map to play a co-op match on. Just wish I could have.
