Spagett • 2026-07-13 14:53:39

Excessive quitters have been a big problem in Auric for a long time now. I really don’t think it’s about auric storm survivor anymore in most cases - I just think people are impulsive, impatient, and often playing above their skill/experience level. I agree that there should be some form of consequence for repeated quitting, but not for doing it once or twice. It’s extremely rare for me to play an auric mission with randoms and complete the mission with the same people that started it, or to complete the mission without any bots for that matter. The poor back-fill makes the problem feel a lot worse, as you end up with bots as the mission progresses and people quit. Here’s a few ideas I and others have had from previous threads:

  1. Improve back-fill, don’t stop back-filling once the mission goes off the board. Allow people to join from quickplay even if the mission is off the board.
  2. A warning pop-up that appears if someone has quit from 2 or 3 missions in a row, to let them know that they have been quitting repeatedly, suggest temporarily lowering the difficulty, and warn about a possible consequence if they continue.
  3. A short-term lockout from the difficulty they have been quitting from if they continue to quit missions after the warning. Maybe they have to complete 1 mission on a lower difficulty, or be locked out of that difficulty for 10 minutes, or something like that. Something to encourage them to at least try a lower difficulty.
  4. Some kind of tag or title on your character for repeated quitting mid-mission, which goes away after a certain amount of time without quitting or by completing a full mission without quitting. “Flight Risk” sounds good.
  5. Expand the block list or make it unlimited. We should be able to block quitters so that we don’t need to interact again if we don’t want to. Currently the block list limit is quite small and fills very fast if you do this.
