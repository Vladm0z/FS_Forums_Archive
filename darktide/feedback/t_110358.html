Pros/cons: dog kills some enemies faster just because that’s how pouncing works, you are less safe because of dog being animation locked, dog has easier time getting into optimal position for dognade but we want puncture and brittleness fixed so it won’t wipe an infinite number of enemies in a 5m circle.
