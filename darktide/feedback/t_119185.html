Overall i think its a great end game idea, and overall its fun. However atm imho there are several problems with it:

1- Whats alluring with a ladder like system like havoc is climbing, encountering bigger and bigger challenge, feeling yourself getting better. Atm Havoc has lost alot of this for me, because its diffculty levels seem very random.
One thing is spawns seems very random, one day you will get non stop crazy waves and bosses, another its much more quiet. Randomly you will get non stop bombers (yesterday i had such day on havoc level 22 everybody seemed to be shooting bombers and there were still alwys 2-4 alive the greanades exploding non stop, you couldnt move). On Havoc 22 we had i would guess like at least 12 bosses total, with twins + weak monstrosity at the same time.Some RNG is great to make it less reptitive but there should be limits so it maintaind its overall difficulty rating. Another is the random modifiers: a modfier like heinous rituals is 3 times as difficult as moebian 21. So in effect you can either wait for it to change/do other’s poeple havocs or do it, which will imho result in doing a havoc 4 levels more difficult (In practice from what i see poeple mostly look for the easier modifiers). Overall again random modifiers are great, but their diffculty should be comparable.

2-The bugs (its kinda for the overall game, but specially frustrating on Havoc). Things like disconnects,crashes and specially if you cant reconnect after crashing, getting thrown into the etherworld of buildings that you cant get out of, can be incredibly frustrating. Some amount of bugs can be overlooked in a single player game, unfortunately for Darktide its a skill based online game and here every such problem is felt tenfold.

3-The havocs below ~10 are easier then Auric missions. In effect poeple must slog through 10 missions ,which are for most who decide to try havoc, simply boring. And they must slog through them becasue nobody at havoc 15 will accept a havoc ranked 1 player. I from time to time try to help poeple advance from those low havocs, but its just so boring.
Imho the lowest level on diffculty on Havoc should be Auric without modifiers.
