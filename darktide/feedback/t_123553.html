Heinous Rituals is frustrating in a way that does not feel healthy for Havoc

The Heinous Rituals modifier in Havoc is one of the least enjoyable modifiers in Havoc mode.

The core issue is that it forces the team to advance regardless of the current state of play. In theory, I understand the intent: create pressure, prevent turtling, and force decisive movement. In practice, though, it often pushes the team into situations that feel less like a skill check and more like the game piling failure conditions on top of each other.

Trying to reach the ritual often means pushing into additional hordes, patrols, specials, or boss pressure while the team is already fighting to stabilize. The modifier’s intent seems to be “move with purpose,” but in practice it often becomes “advance regardless of the current state of play.”

That would be more acceptable if the failure state were manageable, but activating a daemonhost in Havoc is severe enough that the team has to treat the ritual as a disaster event. At the same time, reaching and killing it is frequently unrealistic. The timer speeds up as you get closer, leaving roughly 30-45 seconds to finish it once engaged, which is very little time when the team is already pinned down by high-Havoc pressure.

The result is that the modifier does not create an interesting decision. It uniformly forces a bad one: push forward at the wrong time and risk wiping now, or ignore it and risk wiping later when the daemonhost activates. That shifts success away from smart play and toward spawn luck, patrol timing, and whether the mission happens to give you a clean path.

Some possible ways to improve it:

  • Add more time to the “close proximity” speed up timer, or eliminate it entirely.

  • Slow the timer while the team is actively fighting near the ritual.

  • Reduce the amount and health of channelers on the ritual

  • Reduce triggers and spawn rates around ritual locations .

  • Make the modifier reward aggressive play without requiring suicidal advancement. Example, add a damage reduction field in the area around the ritual, one which the team can keep for a period of time if they defeat the ritual.

  • If the ritual does activate, make the health of the daemonhost relative to the number of channelers alive when it activates. This encourages good range play.

I do not mind difficult modifiers. Havoc should be hard. But there is a difference between challenging and frustrating, and Heinous Rituals crosses the line. Right now it feels less like a fair skill test and more like an RNG punishment that is neither fun nor engaging.

It undermines the part of Darktide that is most fun: reading the fight, stabilizing under pressure, making smart decisions, and then pushing when the team has earned the space to do so.

The idea has potential, but it is currently one of the few modifiers that makes me enjoy the mission less the moment I see it, and the only modifier I actively avoid.
