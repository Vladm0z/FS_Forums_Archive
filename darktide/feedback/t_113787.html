I’m a Veteran Sharpshooter who plays exclusively on Havoc 40, the highest tier of difficulty in Darktide.
Let me tell you something from the trenches — the Scavenger ammo returns are almost negligible at high intensity, but they still save lives.

At Havoc level, the Plasma Gun might give back only a few shots.
The Bolter — a single round.
Yet in dozens of near-wipe situations, those few bullets were the thin line between survival and total annihilation.

And ironically, the class that benefits the most from Scavenger isn’t even the Veteran — it’s the Ogryns.
Most Ogryns in Havoc carry Grenade Gauntlets, and when the Veteran is forced to burn through every cell and bolt to hold back waves of elite enemies, ammo crates are long gone.
Scavenger becomes their only lifeline.

In lower difficulties — yes, even the so-called Maelstrom missions (which in my circle we casually call “low difficulty”) — you’ll never face this density of specialists, elites, and ranged threats.
Ammo is everywhere. Mistakes are forgiven. You can reload without fear.

But on Havoc 40?
Every bullet is an act of faith in the God-Emperor.

If Scavenger were to be nerfed or removed because “it seems too good” on easier modes, then mark my words —
the Ogryns will be the first casualties,
and the countless times I’ve clawed victory from the jaws of death with those last pitiful few rounds would simply never happen again.
