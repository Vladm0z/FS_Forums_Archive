I don’t expect them to buff it to the level of Hive Scum’s chem nade, but at this point frags, flame servo skull, chem nade-- they all just do the same job 2x times better of securing a certain spot on the map.

I only use flame nade sometimes on Rotten Armor for additional damage source against rotties, since DOT damage doesn’t get penalized against them.

Stun nade is just super situational, since it offers really low value if used outside of downs. Most people who use it will never throw it inside a tight elite pack when nobody is downed so that people have easier time clearing it.

When it comes to stun nades main purpose, I sometimes go entire Havoc games without anyone really going down where it requires the nade. And if someone is down, they get revived with skull, voice of command revive talent, good Ogryn teammate with revive curios and For the Lil Uns talent. Not to mention shroudfield Zealot if he’s a good teammate and not a speedrunner.

I think overall that just using 3 revive curios + the talent that Zealot has with revive speed is better way to achieve the same result. Like, unless it’s 2-3 teammates down at the same time and you have crazy amount of elites, the situations where stun nade shines are limited. If you have gunners camping multiple spots, its value also drops dramatically, since its radius is not that big for a grenade that doesn’t apply any debuffs or deal any damage.

Yeah, it’s not completely useless and you can still make a decent play with it. But yeah I don’t like super specific and niche stuff that only shines in that one moment.
