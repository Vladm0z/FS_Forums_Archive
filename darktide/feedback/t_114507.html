So.. I’ve ran a few matches and noticed an uptick in players that use things like “scoreboards” and “true level” enemy “health bars” and mods of this sort.. This is not the sort of thread complaining about mods such as these. This is a thread with the intention to bring attention to mods / hacks or other exploits that provide increased awareness, map cues and what I suspect to be click-hacks or exploits.

Example: running with a psyker this afternoon in Havoc 30s that was running around with a staff and dueling sword combo as many a psyker would do. However he was killing everything (seemingly) in one hit or incredibly quickly - including rotten ogryns / maulers and the like. Something odd that I noticed about the character behavior when watching him run around doing so was that 1: He always knew where every special enemy and trigger was and was aware of them even off map - such as calling out twins / plague ogryns and chaos spawns before we reached the trigger point(s). 2: Something odd about his “weapon” behavior - I watched as his character was very quickly switching between staff and dueling sword continuously without stopping (auto-clicker?) both while attacking enemies and simply moving throughout the map. 3: He was applying soulblaze to everything WHILE he was attacking it withhis dueling sword (yes, I am fully aware of psyker talents that apply such things but this was.. .incredibly fast and even trash mobs) 4: killing everything in one hit (seemingly one hit, his character was switching weapons whilst he was attacking … everything … so it was very difficult to tell just how many hits enemies were taking).

Plague ogryns went down in no time at all and both twins went down within seconds far less time than most coordinated groups I’ve ever been part of have been able to accomplish such a feat.

I’m not sure if there is an exploit in the weapon switching time / animation for the character to swing a weapon and the weapon switching animation to increase attack speed - whilst taking advantage of staff casting at the same time … I don’t really know how to explain it. I do know that I am able to switch weapons mid swing and vice-versa to puff with the inferno staff then switch immediately to my melee weapon. I tested it in the Psykhanium where I landed a hit and immediately switched weapons so I’m quite sure it is possible to exploit the time it takes to swing / switch / puff / switch / swing through an auto-clicker.

Another of my concerns is the visibility some mods have brought forth in 1: highlighting ALL specials / monstrosities in a visible vicinity or radius around a character similar to those brought by psyker and arbitrator abilities and B: Event triggers (such as captain, twins or monstrosity spawnings) - I’ve seen the mods that allow this… Why does Fatshark allow something of this sort?

Yes, I get it, this is a coop game so who cares about advantages of this sort and I am also free to leave a match or stop playing with players that I suspect are … how do I put it eloquently … exploiting? Is it considered hacking? Which I do leave such matches - I’m not interested in playing with people that feel the need to exploit a game’s mechanics in such a manner.

I don’t feel true level mods, scoreboards or the like are exploiting or giving players unfair advantages - though I would argue true level mods cause players to be … discriminatory. While scoreboards can inflate some player’s egos - I’m all for letting you do all the work if you so desire. Knock yourself out. Jump in there and get it done!

(PS: I have over 1300 hours in the game and have been playing since launch and have purposefully created “new” characters and interacted with people that tell me my character level isn’t … up to their standard … which I find laughable and very silly.)
