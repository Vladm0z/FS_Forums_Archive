As I play more games of Auric I’ve seen Darktide evolve from a 20-30 minute game to a 10-15 minute one. It turns out that moving through missions faster conserves resources just as much as exploring for them does, and since health is a resource, the faster you progress, the less health you spend. For example, today I played a 12-minute game on the new event at an absolutely blistering pace.

Expeditions runs counter to this idea, despite the timer. The goal of expeditions is to explore, when most people are sick of exploring at this point. In a game where people are playing faster as their skill gets higher, Expeditions wants you to slow down, which makes it less fun.

Overall I’d say that this gamemode unfortunately just sucks as someone with ADHD who wants to fly at mach speed. Sorry FS.
