Visuals: Power and Force weapons

7 Posts • 140 Views

Margeret-Dayhoff • 2026-01-31 12:25:03

Even though power and force weapons in this game have an “activate” or “special action” button that appears to turn the power/force field on, I think visually the effects should be always on. Power weapons should have an always-on crackling powerfield playing about the blade/head. Be they mauls, swords, axes or what have you.

Part of the whole fun and power fantasy of using power and force weapons is that they should look cool all the time. It’s sort of like having a lightsaber in a Star Wars franchise game. It looks cool.

Interesting visual mechanics (tied to talents, heat, or weapon blessings) could be added to the weapons too. If you have more heat, or stacks from some weapon blessing or a talent, the field should crackle or glow more intensely.

Weapon cosmetics that alter the appearance of power fields and force/warp effects could also be added to the game. In addition to the typical remodel or recolor of skins, buying a new weapon skin for a power or force weapon could also add different field appearance. Instead of a pale blue crackling power field, perhaps it’s purple, red, green etc.

Same with force weapons. A pale blue frosty glow and fog (like dry ice) could play about force weapon blades, with small snowflakes quickly forming and dropping to the ground around them, intensity scales with peril. Similar to how there’s frost in the air and fog leaking from vents when we enter the Psykhanium. Occasional small sparks run along the blade’s length.
There could be cosmetics you could buy that could alter the visuals in some way, from recolors to entirely different field appearances. Pulsating pink waves ripple around the blade, or even geometic distortions (like space is bending) around the blade.

In this case, the “activate” and “special action” effects for force and power weapons should also be intensified to distinguish them from the normal always-on visual effects. I feel like this game is really missing out on upping the coolness factor of power and force weapon visuals.

Heretical_Cactus:

The current ones might be the least worse imo, and tbh, fit more toward the Dark Heresy rules (Unlike how SM have their power weapons)

The Dark Heresy rules merely say the power field can be activated and deactivated by choice. They do not say the power field automatically disables after a 1 or 2 swings of the weapon. The rules explicitly allow for the wielder to keep the power field on at all times.

Additionally, according to the Dark Heresy rules, Force weapons in particular don’t appear to be able to “deactivate” at all since they simply baseline conduct the psychic potential (psy rating) of the wielder into the head/blade of the weapon (it’s damage and penetration). Rather, upon striking an enemy the psyker can choose to offload additional psychic energy into the attack resulting in an opposed willpower test where if the psyker wins he does additional damage ignoring armor and toughness.

I like how SM deals with their power swords, which just allows low and high power modes as opposed a baseline mundane weapon that can only activate a powered attack for a single strike like most Darktide power weapons. The fact is that Darktide is the odd man out of all of 40K games and lore here.
