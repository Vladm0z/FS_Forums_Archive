Hammer_hold • 2026-09-19 11:51:50

Why does everyone hate the Inferno Staff? Let me break it for you:

Jokes aside, the reason why everyone hates this weapon is becasue of the devastating damage it can dish out at large with zero drawback. No ammo needed, Peril can be quelled in one or two seconds. Just hold down one button and you win.

And it’s so strong, it kills almost everything in a second or two. Hordes? Elites? Doesn’t matter. And because it’s also a ranged weapon, it kills stuff from a distance so all enemies die before they can even get to you. And that goes both ways: Enemies die before the players can get to them.

It sound great on paper. But here’s the thing:

People don’t play Darktide just to win. They play to fight heretics with melee and shoot them in the face.

If their ally is destroying everything before they even get a chance to do that, then they will become bored and pissed off at the player who’s essentially stealing engagement with the game from the team.

Knowing that it’s hard to blame people for hating on the Flame Staff. It’s just too damn powerful. It’s so powerful, in fact, that it’s the only weapon in the game capable of stealing fun from the rest of the team.

So if you nerf it, the problem vanishes for the most part. Of all OP weapons, I think only the Arc Rifle and Zealot’s Flamer are capable of doing the same thing, but worse - they don’t have infinite ammo and have to reload from time to time.
