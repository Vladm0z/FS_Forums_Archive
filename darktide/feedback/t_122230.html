i wanna add some insight in case nobody at Fatshark actually plays the game on a nerdy level (it does feel like it, or at least the dude in charge of taking decisions doesn’t have any clue themself):

for meleeing, regardless of your weapon, you can be perfectly fine without blocking and/or pushing, ever. for as long as you dodge reasonably, and don’t spam it, you are untouchable anyway.

exceptions exist ofc: when you are being cornered; or when you push+attack a rager (i.e. the heavy evisc mk15 has a stab as push attack, combined with shredder, it’s devastating against a rager and interrupts their combo) which isn’t even necessary, you can just dodge+attack ragers perfectly fine and not taking a hit either; pushing a pox burster; or similar exceptions.

in other words: yes even a dueling sword can handle an entire Ambush by themself without taking a single hit.
the difference lies in the space required to do so.
a good cleave weapon can do a rather tight orbit kite around the enemy pack (doesn’t necessarely have to be an actual circle, going left right (and repeat) in an arc is perfectly fine too). with some great cleave weapons you even can push into the enemy horde because you kill them faster than they can come close to you. meanwhile a bad cleave weapon (like the dueling sword) requires you to either have a much much larger circle radius, or you will have to retreat continuously (slowly but steadely).
in other words: even with a melee weapon that has garbage attack chain, anim cancle is not a “must do” under any circumstances. the only reason to do so is to make a bad weapon “more viable” which isn’t even true for all weapons that require “anim cancle” to begin with, but the game itself has to be tuned down (made more easy) to allow players to compensate for bad attack chain to begin with. this is the artificial difficulty i talked about.
i much rather have difficulty stemming from fighting my enemies, than artificial difficulty from fighting my own weapon so to speak. the former is pure chaos, allows fluid gameplay and high APM and is hella fun, while the latter is just clunkyness that requires a less difficult game.

so while “chain mastering” and “anim cancle” may sound like some high skill stuff, in reality it’s just a memory thing that quickly turns boring because again, the game has to be made easier to give us the time to handle a bad weapon.

and a last point:
for as long as we are attacking, we are projecting a zone of stagger and damage in front of us. a dueling sword does it in a rather narrow line, a cleave weapon like heavy evisc. mk15 generates a wide “safety zone” in front of you. but every time we must interrupt our attacks (i.e. for anim cancle) we literally give up our defenses (by stop attacking) just to compensate bad weapon design (hence the game must be easier to begin with).

edit: by “anim cancle” i mean both the actual anim cancle (aka chain reset) aswell as mixing lights and heavies into one chain. it’s just tedious to write and read both all the time.

but mixing heavies into a light chain equally serves no actual benefit over a weapon that can just use light chain for cleaving. the extra damage doesn’t matter for cleaving a horde since it’s mostly just overkill anyway → thus a waste of time spent not attacking in the first place.
