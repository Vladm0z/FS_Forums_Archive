JanZamoysky:

Yeah, I also observed that unlike other classes, on zealot I’m actually not getting more points to spend

I think this is more due to zealot getting additional talents added. When I compare my current vs future builds for zealot I end up spending most of the talents I saved in the new keystone and the new ranged DR talent. Instead of being able to parse them out to things I previously would skip.

I will say that I’m surprised that they expanded the number of dangly “talent testicles” in the reworked zealot tree. And I’m not a fan. Talents with absolutely zero pathing value are inherently less attractive in this talent system. Efficient pathing is how you compound the value of the talents you take. And when a talent cannot contribute to that it feels worse than its effect alone would imply.

Pathing is also influenced a fair bit by this clump of talents that are probably all going to be taken by like 80-90% of all zealot builds.

image250×300 61.4 KB

You don’t really want to skip more than 1, maybe 2, of these on any build. And that reduces how wide you can spread the rest of your talents later down the tree. Sort of the inverse of the old vet tree issue of wanting to spend the minimum number of talents at the top of the tree so you can cherry pick as much stuff at the bottom as possible.
