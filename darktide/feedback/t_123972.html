It is obvious that at this point, Captains from Assasination missions don’t bring anything to the table in Damnation and Auric other than dying instantly, so here I propose the changes that need to be done for them to represent a better boss fight

Damnation:
1- An Elite horde should spawn before the captain drops down / enters the arena
2- Captain can’t receive more damage than the minimum to reactivate the shield (so, he can’t lose more than 20% HP before he replenishes it)
3- After a shield regenerates, the Captain will win 10s of immunity (Blue shield, like Twins have on the Orthus Offensive)

Auric:
1- All Damnation changes will now also apply
2- When Below 75% HP, the Captain will win the Final Toll mutator, this means he’ll become immune to stagger of any source
3- Every time the shield breaks, an Elite horde will immediatly spawn

These changes should make Caps more of an actual boss fight and good end to the level for these 2 Difficulties

Heresy and below would not be changed at all

Havoc Captains would not be changed at all (only if the Cap is the last one from an Assasination mission)
