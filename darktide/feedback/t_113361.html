The peril animation imo is warranted because it does a little over a thousand damage in an aoe and knocks everything down with no cool down. I think the inherent risk of blowing yourself up shouldn’t be covered with immunity before hand because I can imagine this could be used to abuse invuln frames rather than the intention of the node (at least I know I would try to abuse a second invuln that has massive stagger afterwards). I don’t think having it require a bit of setup to make plays with this niche node kills all the usability. I suggest dropping a bubble and leaving a little distance from those ragers looking at you funny before the deed. It’s especially juicy in clutch situations with a bit of mobility since you don’t have to worry about elite killsteals and enemy pileups are going to eat all the aoe.

I used it in a very odd build where I would CW to purge all my peril. It instantly drops you to 0 peril, for whatever creativity that inspires.

While playing with this I’ve noticed there were some instances that I killed elites but still got the corruption, probably because the elites died closer to the edge of the aoe. Perhaps this is a bug?

The no-corruption proc should probably work on specials. Its odd hunting for opportunities to use this node looking for elites and having to count out all the specials that deserve to be next to me boiling over.
