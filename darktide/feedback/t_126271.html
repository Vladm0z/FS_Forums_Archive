Today? Take away the last two things I just mentioned. It’s an atrocious, pitiful experience now.

Psyker does more damage and is somehow less squishy. Ogryn and arbitrator does CC. Veteran does shooty-shooty things. Skitarius does it all.

Zealot is tied for dead-last in the tier list for me right now. But Zealot has so much more staying power and team-oriented buffs. Even though he does less damage that the Hive Scum, he’s still much more viable just because of Until Death, Chorus, Stealth, and Blazing Piety.

I used to love this game, and I used to main Hive Scum and I did H40 regularly, but now? I am done with the game until FS does something about this situation. I can not for the life of me understand why FS thinks this situation is acceptable.
