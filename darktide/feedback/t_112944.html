Remove random ammunition drops

14 Posts • 222 Views
C
Carny • 2025-09-26 16:56:45

Random ammunition spawns create uncertainty around ammunition consumption and efficiency.

Suppose a player spends a run mag dumping their bolter at random hordes and crushers. They might put up really good numbers and they’ll certainly have fun (bolter goes brrrrr), but in reality they’re being extremely inefficient with resources. So long as the rest of the team is efficient enough (psykers, melee focused builds, etc) magdump Marty will feel like they’re playing great and might be pumping big numbers on the scoreboard. But as soon as the rest of the team starts to use their ammunition at a standard rate, it starts to cause issues for M. Bolter. This can cause friction and feels bad for everyone involved. Do I give them ammunition in the knowledge they’re just going to dump it into a horde that we could kill for “free” using other means? Or do I deliberately let my team have a member who’s unable to contribute meaningfully?

If ammunition was a fixed recharge system based on class that could be further modified by talents, perks, weapon family, blessings, etc. then it becomes extremely easy to see when you are and aren’t making effective use of your resources. This also fixes the forever issues caused by the vet aura. Instead of adding ammunition based on kills, the aura can just be a modifier on the ammunition creation “stat” of other players.

The loss of a reason to “explore” maps is a potential pain point. But I think that can be solved via other means and that it’s more important to the player experience to be able to tell when you are and aren’t meeting the expectations the devs have for you. (ie; “playing wrong”)
