Now I’m going to state right here that I am well aware of just how small the chances of getting a class like this, since their even fewer official 40k models of nightsiders out there. But sometimes when get an idea, you just can’t leave it alone until you spill, and there is always a chance that it could be made to work if the Devs and, possibly more importantly GW, says maybe.

Now for those who are unaware of what a nightsider is, they are another type abhuman, you could basically think of them as human bat hybrids. They have exceptional sensory capabilities because of this and are well suited for the dark, although they are not really physically stronger than regular humans.

So what would the Nightsider bring to the table in terms of new weapons? Since it is a standard requirement that each current class have some unique weapons that only they can use, I think the best choice would be to give something like dual pistols and maybe combat knives and swords, since those require just a bit more awareness to use properly without running the risk of hurting yourself (heightened have to be good for something). There is also in the lore weapons that are dedicated for stealth, but those are really rare, and Darktide is a horde-shooter, and I don’t see us adding sneaky bits like they had in Payday 2.

Now for abilities, passively, you would be able to see much better in dark areas than your fellow rejects, and perhaps get earlier warnings when you are being targeted by a disabler (mutant, hound, trapper, etc.). As for active abilities, first one would obviously be a stealth ability, since running around in the dark is what nightsiders are best at, but what would make it different, is that you would be take a few shots before breaking stealth, so something similar to VT2’s Kruber Huntsman career. Second ability would be our own version of VT2’s Saltzpyre witch hunter shout. How would this work? Have you ever seen a snake unhinge their jaw or an animal puff out its fur before jumping at you? It will give you quite a start. And the nightsider is basically human-bat hybrid with pointy ears, glowing eyes, and sharp teeth, so I think you can see that would be more than enough to make you jump. Third and last ability, the nightsider would be able release a cybernetic falcon to attack enemies from afar, kind of like the first borderlands bloodwing. I know that this seems more of an arbiter ability, but they did not give them one so maybe someone else can have it.

As for blitzes, I do have three. First marker flare, lights up the area real nice and any enemy that is caught in the light takes additional ranged damage. Second, bouncing betty landmine. And third a remote detonation explosive.
