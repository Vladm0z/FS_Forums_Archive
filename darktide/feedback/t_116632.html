Scummers, let us be honest, how invaluable is Sample Collector?
I wager it to be basically an auto include in everybuild.
I do not know about you, but personally, the ability to get stims quicker is just too good to pas up on, and personally, i would prefer it to be, just like martyrdome on zealot, to be a node everyone will get. I would personally therefore suggest for it to be right after the blitz choice.

I understand that this could maybe clash with the idea that this section is for evasion, but i also notice that basically every build just rushes through that section as well, cause no one can be asked to really invest anything in there. I also think in general some talents need to just be straight up Removed cause they see barely any use.

Now, personally, that leaves us now with gang tough, so here is a list of movings i would suggest:
(“>” means it takes the spot of the talent it points to, while “<>” means they swap places)

Sample Collector > Gang Tough
Gang Tough > Ammo Jack
Ammo Jack > merged with pick pocket
Pick Pocket <> Moving target
Toxic Renewal > Old Sample Collector Spot
Stoked rage > Removed
Uncontrollable Aggression > Removed
Regained Posture <> Nimble

Balancing:

Ammo Jack: +15% > +25% Ammo
Pick Pocket: threshhold and repleneishment from 20% > 15%
Enhanced Desperado: Now refills 10% of your current magazine when killing a marked enemy.

Rampage
Grants +2.5% Melee damage for every second you spend in rampage, up to 10 stacks.

Pulverizing Strikes: Instead of damage, attacks apply 1 stack of brittleness for every elite enemy killed during Rampage
Forges Bellow: Now also regenrate 5% Toughness for every second in Rampage
Channeled Aggression: Removed the 50% time remaining condition

This ability always stood at odds with other combat enhancers, which had all very powerful bonuses, however Rampage uniquely had a great downtime and punishment unlike the others.
Instead of the boring route and just nerfing the downsides, i feel like putting more gas into the engine could proof more fun.

Vultures Mark
Gained an additional stack and every stack now also grants 5% reload speed.

Vultures mark always felt a little bit like it was fully geared for desperado, adding a reload speed buff and another stack to make it stick out just enough from Chemical dependency to be worth taking, as both have a 20% potential crit rate, though at the caviat that one is only for ranged.

Adrenaline Frenzy
Duration increases from 10s > 15s
Now also grants 20% Critical strike chance when active for melee only

Adrenaline Assassin: Additionally. weakspot hits deal +10% damage
Adrenaline Smite: Additionally, deal +15% Damage against Elites

Adrenaline frenzy felt always a bit odd, it felt strong but also weak at the same time, as the duration was short lived and it needed a bunch of tax nodes to run consistently, maybe these changes would proof sufficient?
