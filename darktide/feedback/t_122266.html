Expeditions Feel Dead Compared to the Main Game

9 Posts • 119 Views

sidimaki • 2026-06-09 05:10:46

I like Expeditions, but the mode feels almost disconnected from the rest of Darktide.

Because it has its own terminal and separate queue, it often feels like a side activity rather than part of the game. As a result, matchmaking can take longer and many players simply ignore the mode altogether.

One possible solution would be to merge Expeditions into the main mission board instead of keeping them on a separate terminal. Let Expedition missions appear alongside regular missions so players naturally see them when choosing what to play.

Maybe they could even be mixed into the regular event rotation and included in regular Quick Play matchmaking. That way, more players would encounter the mode naturally instead of having to actively seek it out.

This would increase visibility, improve matchmaking, and make Expeditions feel like a core part of Darktide rather than a forgotten side mode.

I would also be interested in seeing Havoc modifiers or experimental events tested through Expeditions to give players more reasons to return.

What do you think?
