The “Chemist” line of the Hive-Scum (the right side of the talent tree) is the first “archtype” in the game where it’s ability is incompatible with it’s key stone. If you use the Stimm to gain/maintaing the keystone buff, the stimm-pack is just an underwhelming buff (maybe it’s also a bomb if you got the upgrade, but it is still underwhelming) and if you drop the stimm-pack, consuming the stimm, you get the buff but you don’t receive the keystone buff (even though you are still being stimmed, just not through stabbing yourself with a needle).

I really like the “Chemist” idea, i also enjoy picking one thing and sticking with it, so i felt pretty bummed out when i discovered that if i wanted to use the stimm-pack i had to either go to another key stone or have an incredibly mediocre skill, so i propose two possible solutions to this.

1 - The Stimm-pack no longer consumes the stimm and every time you drop the Stimm-pack it always buff your team mates even if the stimm is on cooldown BUT you (the player who used the stimm-pack) can’t receive the stimm buff from it (the corruption cleanse effect you still receive, just not the equipped stimm buff). This would allow for the stimm-pack to be used apart from the stimm, allowing you to buff yourself and your team without making the keystone unusuable when using this ability, or…

2 - When you get Stimmed through the stimm-pack it also counts towards the keystone buff. This one is way more straight forward, this allows for the player to use the stimmpack as is intended without trivializing the keystone.

The stimm-pack right now is very underwhelming to use (at least in my opinion, if others enjoy it as it is i have no problem with that) and i think that a change similar to the ones i mentioned would make it way more fun to use it than it’s current state.
