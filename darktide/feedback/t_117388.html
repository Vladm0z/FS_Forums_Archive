I like necromunda and want to LARP as an Escher ganger. Their gangers all use basic lasguns (because they manufacture them).

I do like the hive scum’s identity in being able to make bullet weapons work. I would want the class to be encouraged to take the lasgun only on more melee focused/mixed builds. Otherwise the Infantry Lasgun outclasses the Infantry Autogun which would make the class less distinct.

I believe just one change would keep the Lasgun in check on this class:

Have the Pickpocket Passive not work on las-weapons This means a lasgun user can’t be shooting the whole match as easily as with other weapons. Pigeonholing the gun into being a support piece rather than a main gun for pure shooting builds.
