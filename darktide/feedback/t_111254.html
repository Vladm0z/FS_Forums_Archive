Synopsis (as it’s apparently too much to digest in one go ):

  • New resource called “Intel Points” is added
  • You farm them about the same as Plasteel/Diamantine (but there is one more game mechanic helping with that, which make it look like we fight for control for different regions of the map)
  • You use them to purchase new items called “Report Papers” at base, which exist in several types
  • Each such type you purchase is a vote you cast for a new feature of certain type you want FS to add to game asap (new maps/missions/classes/foes/items/weapons etc)
  • Global map shows the current voting progress, it resets once a few months to better reflect current needs

So it’s effectively a gamified voting/new feature requests on steroids

The detailed description:

So, as was suggested in another discussion, it would be nice for all us to see how our actions actually affect the course of the war effort. It may be not as easily achieved as it may appear, as development process takes time, and is planned many months in advance - so changing it according to player’s feedback here and now isn’t always possible. But I think I have an idea how we still can allow players to feel involved in the process, while at the same time helping FS to understand what playerbase wants. And we can turn it into an interesting game system that feels like your fighting during missions is actually what makes the change, thus enhancing the overall immersion.

  1. A new concept of “Intel” is added to the game. It’s pieces of data and various evidences all players can harvest and submit to their superiors

  2. Intel can be found during the missions as any other resources (it’s represented by stashes of paper, digital carriers, artifacts; scriptures in particular could cost a big chunk of intel on itself, finally giving people some reason to bother collecting them); it’s also handed out as rewards for missions, the more challenging they are (for any reason) - the more intel you can get.

  3. While at Mourningstar, you can access a new terminal which helps you to “analyze” all the collected raw intel data (represented as a separate new quantifiable resource; thus all your Intel rewards are translated into a generic total number of “Intel points”). The terminal just allows you to convert a predefined amount of your raw intel points into Report Paper(s) of several different kinds. Each kind of paper is effectively a vote you cast, telling Fatshark what you would like them to add to the game next or what to prioritize in the development of new features.

Here are some types of Report Papers, and what feature request they correspond to:

  • New weapons and Curios, higher tiers of gear etc: Counter-intelligence report, Equipment; it lists all the assessments of your organization’s combat capabilities done by the enemy’s spies, showing how they could exploit lack of proper equipment

  • New playable classes: Counter-intelligence report, Operatives; same as the previous one, but assess our force’s composition

  • New story chapters: Counter-intelligence report, Upcoming operations assessment

  • Rebalance request for certain class: Counter-intelligence report, Operative code CLASS_NAME threat level increase/decrease

  • New customizations/more customization options: Counter-intelligence report, Personal data leakage

  • New types of carriable items and power ups: Technoarcheology report; lists all the encounters of long forgotten and alien techs during your missions which we could put to use

  • New areas (maps) in the Tertium Hive: Urban Logistics report; assessment of all reports from scouting parties and interrogations of local populace on possible routes to currently off-limits sectors of the Hive

  • New areas beyond the borders of Tertium: Wilderness Reconnaissance report; same as the previous one, but for outlying regions of the Hive

  • New mission types on existing maps: Operations Scope Assessment report; lists evidence that our current scope of operations isn’t sufficient and we need to expand it

  • New types of foes: Threat Prognosis report, Enemy combatants; assembles all the clues we got on enemy soldiers present in the area, but not yet encountered by us

  • New bosses: Threat Prognosis report, Monstrosities and Generals; same as the previous one, but for monstrosities and enemy’s figures of power

  • New enemy factions: Threat Prognosis report, Enemy Factions; same as the previous one, but for enemy factions yet unmet

You can generate as many of those Reports as you want - until you still have enough Intel Points to do so. Every Report costs the same amount of Points and is considered a vote given by you in support of a certain new feature. It allows both community and FS to understand what is that people need most right now. Though the number of votes casted for a particular feature should give FS a hint it’s something they should prioritize, the fact that some other feature requests accumulated less votes doesn’t still mean they are not important - only that they don’t need to be on the top of their TODO list.

Fight for control over certain regions (maps) of Tertium to get more intel from it:

A new global map of War for Tertium is added (or the one that we see on mission selection terminal is appropriately redesigned). It shows you the current Intelligence Charts (effectively, the current results of on-going new features voting), but also allows you to select 2 regions (maps) you’ll be focusing at for the next Operation Cycle. When completing missions that happen on those maps, each mission you win will contribute to growth of Inquisition Presence gauge of this region; each mission you fail there will make it shrink.

If fully filled before the end of current Operation Cycle for some region, it grants instant Intel Points rewards to all players who were choosing this region and have won at least 5 missions in there. Then for a week the Gauge is locked in “Controlled by our forces” state and all those players who contributed to achieving it are granted additional Intel Points rewards for every mission they complete in that region (because while we control it, it’s much easier to gather intelligence). After that the Gauge resets and player are allowed to choose their regions again.

Please share your thoughts below.

P.S.
I think the feature request polls (the Reports’ charts) should also be reset once a few months, to better reflect the current needs of the community.
