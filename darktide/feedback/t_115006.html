honestly, the biggest issue i have with the character customization is that, when actually making your character, there isn’t actually any real reason to take any of the options aside from them being kinda neat, they don’t even come up in conversation! so, i’ve decided to suggest a system to fix this.

first, we’ll go over planet selection.

so, when selecting a planet, you’ll be given choices like Cadia and Branx Magna. the only real choice to pick is Cadia because it gives you more customization options and that’s it. so, to fix this, every planet will now give you a 1-3% buff to a specific stat, excluding Cadia. as an example, Mornax would give you faster heavy wind up and Pavane would give you a bit more health.

secondly, the childhood.

these will give you new voice lines, for instance, Hive Urchin would have you talk about how you lifted somebodies wallet and then found them hanging from a meat hook the next day and Saint-polisher would have you talk about this one stain that you couldn’t seem to get out and you were beaten for it until you finally ran away.

third, growing up.

these would give you a free cosmetic option with the rest being earned later, for instance, Enlisted would give you a Lazgun back cosmetic, Hive Ganger would give you a pair of goggles with a cracked lens, and Hive Guide would give you a map end screen pose.

fourth, Defining moment.

these would give you a 1-2% stat boost, for instance, Warp storm makes you take on less corruption, Elite Formation would make you flinch less, and Duel would make you better with 1-handed gun.

fifth, Personality. this would actually give you a buff to specific things, for instance, The Loose Cannon has much better stats with the shovels and the knives, The Professional has better all round stats, and The Cutthroat would have better stats with the plasma and power weapons, not by too much though.

after all of this, you have your character and your build will be effected, but minimally. but there will still be an actual POINT to thinking about your background and putting some actual time into it.
