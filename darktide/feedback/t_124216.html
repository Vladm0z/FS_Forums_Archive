hello everyone.
I apologize in advance for my English - I may make mistakes, as it is not my native language.

I have been playing Warhammer 40,000 tabletop since 1999, and since 2017, the Adeptus Mechanicus have been my main tabletop army. As I play as a Skitarii, I would like to express my gratitude for introducing this class to the game.

At the same time, I would like to leave a few comments about the implementation of this class in DarkTide.

  1. The most important thing is why doesn’t the galvanic rifle deal electricity damage? This is its basic feature in the lore, and it would make it relevant in the current gameplay.
  2. We have the Serberus Rider cosmetic, but we don’t have the saber in the weapon selection! The Cavalry Sabre is the primary weapon of the Mechanicus Cavalry, and it’s strange that it’s not available. However, we do have the chainsword, which is not present on any Skitarii miniature.
  3. The implementation of the arc rifle - in general, I really like this rapid-fire weapon against vehicles, and it would be great to see an alternative fire mode - a concentrated arc or a charge that hits a single target, dealing bonus damage to heavy armor.
  4. The absence of some unique weapons and the presence of unnecessary ones. I’m not asking to add a rad carbine (in fact, from a lore perspective, I’m glad it’s not there - because this weapon would kill the entire group with radiation), but!

Why is there a laser pistol but no arc pistol? Along with the phosphorus pistol, this is the primary weapon of squad leaders.
Why do we need a Shredder submachine gun when we have the excellent Flechette blaster, which essentially fills the same niche?
I agree that the Stab revolver is the same as the Archeo-revolver, and I eagerly await its cosmetics.
Finally, why was the Taser goad forgotten? It would have been an excellent alternative to the Power Mace.

Of course, I have a lot of suggestions for the Skitarii’s development tree (for example, making some talents more appropriate - for example, the force field would not consume grenade charges but would regenerate over time), but I just want this class to be more similar to a board game.

In any case, thank you very much for this class.

P.S. Make the Sicarians legs and helmets an additional cosmetic item!!! I’ll buy it right away)

Good luck!
