This is a good point. One of the things that stands out to me about Fatshark’s design is that it SHOULD be easily scalable, but they don’t realize that. Doom nailed this in 1993, yet it’s lost on so many game makers today. Early in the game, when you have a pistol, shotgun, and super shotgun, fighting smaller numbers of low- or mid-tier enemies is still a challenge. Having a couple rockets is your “oh crap” button. Then you get the plasma rifle and BFG and tons of rockets, and now you can face hundreds or thousands of higher-tier enemies at a time. There’s challenge at both tiers through controlling what tools are available to the player.

Any attempt at going “underarmed” in DT is going to be foiled by the game still trying to throw everything and the kitchen sink at players, and in addition to that you can’t control your team’s loadout in randoms (and can’t even solo play!).

The current event is so boring I was bringing intentionally gimped builds for some fun, but every other player was bringing Plasma-Krak-DS Vet or Rampage Scum with Boom Bringer and Needle Gun. It was ridiculous.
