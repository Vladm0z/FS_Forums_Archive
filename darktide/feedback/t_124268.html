Agayek • 2026-07-21 06:53:45

Twins and a monstrosity is semi-normal, yes. Twins triggers are separate from monstrosity triggers, and so it’s very, very possible to get both at the same time.

Multiple full monsters (aka, not weakened monstrous specials spawns) is also kinda a “normal” thing, but it’s very situational. There’s a few places in the game where there’s a boss spawn trigger, and then another boss spawn trigger 5 feet further down the path, and then another one 5 more feet down. And if you don’t know where they are and walk past all of them, it is very possible to get up to 2 full monster spawns at once (this is why it pays bigly to know where boss triggers are and control the pacing of the group to not cross multiple triggers without verifying if the first went off or not).

That said, there is a bug in the spawning system that’s tied to pacing changes (aka, when the spawning system shifts from normal “going along the path” mode to “event” mode or vice versa) that can remove all limits on boss spawns for a time, and when that happens, it is possible if your team is unlucky for the same boss trigger to go off 3-4 times and dump all of those monsters on you at once. The most I’ve ever seen at the same time is 4 full monsters and 2 weakened monsters, and it’s very not fun when it happens.
