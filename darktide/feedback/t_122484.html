#1 Martyrdom:

first off: Wounds by itself is a trap stat. it only makes it less worse IF you down, but it does not do anything for you to not down in the first place. removing Martyrdom entirely would also allow to remove the trap stat too. it’s a 3in1 (we’ll get to the 3rd part).

and the Issue with Martyrdom by itself is, so most folks running around in auric maelstrom don’t understand so it’s

a) get stronger the more dire the situation is

b) if there are only 2 charges on a medicaid-station for example, but 3 require healing, the Martyr-Zealot is the best choice to not take the heal.

so technically, it is not a bad idea on paper, the issue is so many players literally waste medicaid-station charges by deliberately not heal, which then also forces them to not be on the objective when a medikit-pack is dropped. and then when they down, and they always do, they down somewhere else, not close to the team. they are a liability and purposefully deny coherency. it’s just a bad concept to have in this game, even if on paper it may look fine.

in other words: Martyrdom as a concept causes multi-layered issues for the rest of the team. it’s quite funny actually so people nowadays justifiably are tired of seeing Arbites and Hive Scums in their teams, because they are almost never close to the team anyway, yet Martyrdom has this exact same issue since years. Arbites and Hive Scum also turn the game boring for everyone else in the team by turning the game into a Walking Simulator, while Martyrdom means we always have a dude running around that is going to down with a single mistake.

also: Heavy Eviscerator, and especially Thunder Hammer, kinda do not have a good Keystone except Martyrdom. Blazing Piety really only shines in combination with Scourge to stack even more crit-chance, which also helps building Blazing Piety faster when it’s down. on a fast attacking weapon, like a Tactical Axe, this combination is great. but on a weapon that kills squishy enemies in one hit, and also tries to kill Maulers, Ragers, Crushers and such in like 2 hits at most, it just doesn’t really work. you can’t stack Scourge with either Heavy Eviscerator or Thunder Hammer, and running Blazing Piety without Scourge is just meh, especially when you use Fury of the Faithful to get a guaranteed crit on i.e. Crushers and such to kill them in one hit anyway. also you’re just too slow to hit the same target several times if it were to survive long enough, because your team will already have killed it by that time.

And Inexorable Judgment is even worse for those two class specific weapons that lack a class-specific keystone outside of martyrdom.

thus: removing Martyrdom and adding something else technically would be a 3in1.

3in1…come on Fatshark, you can’t have it more easy, though some rework is required there’s so much potential to get more love to the Zealot-specific weapons. that would be great!

#2 Force Greatsword (Psyker): please rework the special to work like Illisis. not necessarely the same, but something we can use to manage Peril actively without being animation-locked or continuously required to special+weapon-swap while meleeing (Deimos and Obscuros).

additionally: it is anti-synergic with the Talent-Tree as a whole.

a) you cannot use it’s special to stack Malefic Momentum

b) if you do run Melee-Psyker (i.e. with a Quickdraw to kill those pesky Trappers, Snipers and such), you do want Immaterial Focus and One with the Warp, which both require manual Warp-Management, except they don’t with the Force Greatsword because:

it is just brokenly op if you do run a “high peril build” and don’t even try to manage your peril, at all, except using some Venting Shriek to regain Toughness+allow more dmg-soaking. you can soak up so much damage from ranged guys, while being completely safe in melee due to it’s insane cleave (especially when Warp Splitting kicks in), while still allowing you to kill Crushers faster than a Duelling Sword, even more so if you use Brain Ruptures on-hit effect Kinetic Flayer.

so it’s kinda hilarious actually. it is anti-synergic, but it’s power-creep nature makes it not even matter. and simply tuning down the damage numbers of the special also will not help to get it in line with other weapons that are not pay2win dlc bs.

so a rework of the Special itself would actually allow a much more fun playstyle, instead of a brain-dead boring af playstyle that does everything for you while you are incredibly safe in melee. keep in mind you also have Force-Push, and the Push-Attack is a great stab.

i do not want the Weapon itself to be changed, just it’s special (and some numbers nerfed to remove the power-creep crap).

Psyker is supposed to be “high risk high reward”, but the Force Greatsword flips this on it’s head.
