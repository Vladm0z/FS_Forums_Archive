Having to keep getting in and out of the inspect menu to see what each Marks do in terms of damage, attack pattern, any stats not shown in the Marks menu is very very annoying, especially when different marks are totally different weapons

Heavy Laspistol, Mk X compared to Mk II :
+60 DMG
+6 Shells in Mag

Weapons only have a couple marks, some weapons don’t even have one (bolt pistol) so i think removing the “marks” menu and adding a simple dropdown list IN the inspect menu would make it a lot easier to players, especially new ones to see which weapon marks they’ll prefer.

I hope that this get implemented because it would be really nice.
