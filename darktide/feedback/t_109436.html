I’ve been lurking on the forums for a little over a year now, but made an account just to talk about this because of how hard it affects my and probably many others enjoyment of the game. I’ve played a few matches since the update dropped and something is off, Auric now feels like normal Damnation pre update and I’ve talked to other players about this and they’ve noticed it too. I don’t know if this was an intentional reduction of difficulty they thought they could get away with or if something isn’t working correctly but it needs to be addressed quickly.

I shouldn’t have to explain why HISTG not working is bad for the game but I’m going to anyway. I don’t know how it is for everybody else but Auric HISTG is a very comfortable level of difficulty for me because I really don’t want to engage with Havoc’s annoying systems like the party finder and weekly rank systems just to play it (I’m also just probably not good enough to play it but that’s besides the point). My point is this difficulty is very important as it’s a good level of challenge for slightly above average players like me who are stuck in the middle of being bored on lower difficulties but also not wanting to play Havoc.

I’d like to know if anybody else feels like the difficulty of Auric missions isn’t the same as pre patch and I’d also like some Transparency from Fatshark on whether or not this decrease in difficulty is intentional or not, because if things are working as intended then this update may have killed the game for me. Or maybe this was a huge waste of time and Fatshark will ignore this like last time they accidentally made the game easier who knows.

PS: while we’re at it revert the “fix” that locked people out of using universal human cosmetics on Arbitrator, that is a completely horrible change that even if it was meant as a fix for something does nothing but make the game worse and anger players by removing options from people who paid for the cosmetics and also paid for the class.
