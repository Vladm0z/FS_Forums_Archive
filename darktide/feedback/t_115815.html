I know that we just got the Hive Scum class for Darktide, but I can’t help but want more weapons for our new Psychotic friends. So with that in mind, here are a some ideas for new toys to use one the heretics.

Nail/Rivet Gun: following along the theme of improvised weaponry, the Nail/Rivet gun is construction tool remade for combat in the deadly bowels of Tertiums underhive. Semi-automatic, it would shoot a solid rivet/nail spike at a target, crippling them with a debuff and massive damage, with kill shots potentially pinning enemy corpses to walls. Downsides? Large and clunky, it is not fast to reload, also the projectiles would have a falloff arc, making sniping long range enemies annoying. As for weapon special, super heats the next round so that it ignites the next target it hits.

Improvised Push Knives: another close quarters weapons, these would be small and fast, and also have semi-decent piercing. Not sure what the downsides would be, but here is an image of what a push knife looks like so you can get the general idea.

image225×225 28.7 KB

Lever-action shotgun: I know that lever-action weapons are almost non-existant in the 40k universe, but I feel that these would go along great with are new gang-bangers. Plus, someone did make an awesome custom design for lever action boltgun, so maybe we use that for inspiration?

image1920×1080 172 KB

Improvised Spear: how does a ganger make a spear? They take steel rod, chop it down to the correct size, then either sharpen one end or strap on blades top of it and voila, new spear!

These are just ideas that I have scrounged up based on what I think might work with our new class, so if you have of your own, or critique of what’s already listed, please, don’t be shy.
