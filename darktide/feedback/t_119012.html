Of the Veteran’s ranged weapons, only the plasma gun is unique. My proposal is aimed at diversifying the class specifically with ranged weapons.

We know that the Heretics have a machine gunner and the Scab has a hellgun. Why not add such a weapon with unique mechanics to the Veteran?
Let the machine gun and hellgun take away the ability to use melee combat, but offer their own unique gameplay specifically for ranged combat.

Machine Gun:
It will differ from all other weapons in its huge ammo capacity. When aiming, it will “stand up” like Ogryn does, increasing accuracy.
Since the machine gun takes away our melee capabilities, we need an equivalent. Let the alt attack be a bayonet strike.

Hellgun:
It’s exactly the same, but a more powerful option against armor with the same huge ammo capacity. In an alt attack, you can give him a knife, which he can use to fight back when pressed.

And of course, give him a bag/battery on his back; we already have a direct example with two Heretics.

I don’t argue that the Veteran is very flexible in itself, but it’s precisely this flexibility that sets it apart from other classes, without anything truly unique in terms of weapons. The Zealot, for example, has a flamethrower and a two-handed weapon, and I won’t even mention the Ogryn and Psyker. For me, the Veteran is someone who can potentially maximize ranged combat, and yes, that’s true, but it’s strange to see the absence of these two weapons; they seem to be begging to be added to his arsenal.
