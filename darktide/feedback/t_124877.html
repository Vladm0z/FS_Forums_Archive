The new updates to the Vanguard have proven somewhat divisive on the forums.

I for one appreciate the reduction of jank (nonsensical bloated HP even without shield, or weird invisible-zorb-like shield hitbox etc) BUT I would like to see them buffed in new ways to add genuine challenge.

Feel free to make suggestions here!

Some suggestions from myself:

  • shield is made larger (vertically, more like Roman wall shields).
  • shield can no longer be dropped/destroyed and can only be opened up temporality via an appropriate counter
  • shield enemies group together and move as a wall
  • shield enemies can guard bombers, gunners or reapers
  • some shield enemies have long spears
  • shield enemies stand ground together in pinchpoints (acting as barricade)

These are just loose ideas so don’t expect a fleshed out powerpoint presentation!

Just riff and give the Designers some ideas to play with.

Whether you agree with the recent changes or not is irrelevant to this thread so please spare us your venting. Just some positive suggestions to add new challenge please!

Dizzy721Regular • 2026-08-14 22:22:34

What are the design goals of the Vanguards? It’s a few parts:

First, is to replace the bulwark as a shielded enemy that is less visually and spatially obstructive. It does this by being human sized and carrying a much smaller shield. They do replace the Bulwark while being much less annoying to fight but how they occupy the space is something that could use some work that I’ll circle back to later on.

Second, is to stop certain sources of cleave from penetrating hordes. On the ranged cleave front, firing things like the plasma gun and manstopper shotgun blasts into hordes is just less good and builds around PBB and Desperado are also nerfed a bit. It does not block inferno staff like Bulwark shield which could be something to change but honestly it’d wouldn’t do much unless you’re point blank blasting into the Vanguard shield. This seems fine.

On melee cleave, unga bunga into the bodies of hordes is nerfed, this is good. Wide sweeping attacks will often get blocked by these dudes in actual combat due to animations even if you’re a headshot god. But on the Ogryn and Skitarii with their height advantage this enemy was a non-issue for many weapons even with unga bunga. One thing I would change is to make the enemy reactive to your attacks a bit. Like if they are in between attacks you can just hit them right in the head over their shield with a simple strikedown attack while they’re staring right at you holding the shield. You can also hit them frequently if you’re just standing as close as possible to them regardless of what animation they’re in. They should block attacks if you’re in standing right in front of them in this scenario, rewarding footwork if you don’t want to spend the minimal amount of stamina to do two pushes.

For explosive cleave, they do nothing but I think that whole mechanic needs some tweaks rather than some specific interaction with Vanguards.

Third, is to make melee combat more engaging. I found that I was swapping between single target combos and horde clear and moving around to get away from their shields more than previously. But also in most scenarios they were completely ignorable, just kill everything else and cleanup the Vanguards at the end. They attack slow, can be staggered out of all attacks by a simple push, and they don’t impede movement any more than a poxwalker would. There’s just not pressure on the player to actually engage with them. You can spawn 20 of these guys and just block and dodge through them while you hit actual priority targets. It’s even easier with double push to stagger. Having a shield bash/push that they can trigger that doesn’t delete stamina like Bulwark and just denies the player space/free movement would be helpful in making them more threatening.

Reducing Bulwarks and introducing Vanguards is generally good. Large groups of Bulwarks were incredibly annoying to fight. But the previous configuration did make the game harder: Bulwarks needed specific tools to deal with them that had drawbacks versus other enemies, required teammate cooperation (e.g. one aggros them to turn the Bulwark weakside to the rest of the team), blocked far more space and could actually pin players into nearly unwinnable situations. Vanguards don’t necessarily need to replace that challenge gap all by themselves. But they are the pretty much the only balance lever being pulled right now, and they keep getting made easier to deal with. What’s to come is still unsure. In the meantime, the game has lost such a degree of challenge that I’m just playing VT2 these days.

Other suggestions I have:

  • How are players to know if specific weapons interact with shields in unique ways? Is this mentioned in the UI anywhere? Kuli’s Guide and nowhere else? Something to address.
  • Add knockback to Vanguards when they get BTL’d, they don’t need to get knocked on their ass but they need to get knocked out of the way.
