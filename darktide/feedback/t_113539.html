This will be a longer post. If you want the TL;DR, look below. I want to clearly state at the start here, I don’t know what FS is doing or planning, but I imagine they do want to create as good of a game as possible. I don’t contribute the current state of the game to any kind of malice or ineptitude. Making and balancing games is hard. A lot of work goes into it. But I also want to be honest about my genuine feelings towards the current state of the game. To be blunt, shortly before Bound by Duty was announced, I had uninstalled the game for the first time since its launch. And now, after having a while to settle on my feelings towards the game post the BbD Update, i’m already back to debating whether or not I’m going to keep the game installed. So I want to talk about what’s leading me to that sentiment here.

Firstly, I want to talk about game modes; in part because I have the least to say about this aspect, but I imagine what I have to say might be a bit of a hot take. Personally, I do not come to Darktide for Havoc or Mortis. I personally prefer the base experience. I don’t dislike either secondary game mode, but Mortis can feel aimless to me, stuck on a little map and killing swarms of enemies. And Havoc is a whole lot of work to get into for an experience that is sweaty in a very unappealing way to me. I imagine, if I had peers of my skill level to play w/ consistently, I’d be a little warmer towards it, but if I want to play Havoc I have to look for a game w/ the party finder. And that’s a lot of work for a game mode that is a stagnant meta at the best of times and a miserable slog of modifiers at the worse. And I truly believe that FS shot themselves in the foot when introducing both game modes.

Vermintide 2 has Chaos Waste, which can be appealing to both sweaty players and casual, with cool modifiers that made enemies and players alike far stronger, wrapped up in a Roguelike game mode. Havoc and Mortis feels like Fatshark bifurcated Chaos Waste into two game modes that fail to reach the heights of the one. Havoc and Mortis already splits up the limited community into three separate game modes, and I worry that we’ll never get a proper Chaos Waste-like game mode because of it, or worse, if we do, it will further divide the community. Worse yet, I genuinely feel like Havoc is partially at fault for the overall problems of balance across the game.
To reiterate, I do not dislike Havoc on paper, or even in experience sometimes. But Havoc is the highest level of play in the game, which means the weapons and the classes that use them have to be balanced in a way that actually makes Havoc possible to beat.

It’s the nature of these kinds of games for the mathematically best builds to be found and shared. There’s not any way around that. But the issue of what works best in Havoc permeates the entire game. Weapons and Builds that excel in Havoc dominate everywhere else, or alternatively feel underpowered everywhere else. And so problems with the balance of Havoc spread to the rest of the game. An example of what I mean by this can be seen w/ Crystalline Will. W/ the use of a mod, you can better visualize the AoE of your Peril Overload. The node gets a lot of value from it’s main effect, which is that popping won’t drop you. But the secondary effect, in normal play, feels useless. I may not play w/ top players, but I do consider my peers competent, and in this small of a space, it’s not viable to expect to get an explosion off that has a 2 Second Start up before one of my allies kills this enemy or other viable targets in the area. Which means the second benefit of Crystalline Will only gets benefit in one of three situations: I’m not w/ my team for whatever reason, I accidently get a kill because of the size of the AoE, or I’m playing Havoc, where I’m more likely to be getting attacked by for more Elites and Specials.

image1522×860 369 KB

That’s well and fine for Havoc, but that means in normal play, you are only getting Crystalline Will’s first benefit unless your team all dies, you play away from your team, or as a mistake. And that feels…lame. It takes what could be a really cool node to build around, and reduces the use/value of it to either a negligible potential bonus or a decent tool that you can only use outside the main game mode. This is just one example w/ Psyker but the problem exist within Zealot and Veteran aswell.

I don’t think all balancing issues trace back to Havoc though. There’s a lot just wrong w/ the enemy roster at present. We’ve gained yet another Scab Elite Unit. People have already complained about how the Plasma Gunners are overbaked, so I won’t do that here. But in general, the factions feel lopsided. I’m convinced that Every Ogryn Unit in the game, including the PlOgryn, are related to the Scabs. But even if you don’t count them, Scabs have Maulers, Snipers, Trappers, and Plasma Gunners as unique Units, while Dregs only have Poxbursters, Dogs, and Mutants. That’s two disrupters and a disabler compared to a Heavy Melee Elite, a Heavy Ranged Elite, a Ranged Disrupter, and a Disabler. And this is leaving the Ogryns out, who constitute a Disrupter (Bulwark), a second Heavy Melee Elite, and a Second Heavy Ranged Elite.

I don’t expect perfect parody; in fact, I would prefer the factions have different niches. But the game is currently so heavily skewed towards Scabs, and Scabs are heavily skewed towards Armor. The units that do have some form of Parody are also all over the place. The biggest difference between Scab and Dreg mirrored units is the Ragers, with differences in effective health and attack speed. But Flamers, Bombers, Gunners, and Shocktroopers (Shotgunners) all act the same to each other, so they end up not feeling very different. I’d love to see Dreg units as a whole be a lot more aggressive and mobile to make up for their lack of armor. Gunners shooting in smaller burst before repositioning. Shocktroopers rushing into effective range of their shotgun, and being more nimble w/ their weapons. But right now, there’s nothing like that. This lack of a more impactful difference between factions has resulted in the primary gameplay loop growing incredibly stale, and one thing that doesn’t help is the stagnation of the weapon meta.

Because of how prevalent the Armored Unit spam is in the game, even now, if a weapon doesn’t have the innate capability to deal w/ armored foes in some way, it doesn’t get taken. And this means that a lot of the OG weapons are a tad undervalued. The Devil Claw, The Heavy Sword, and the Combat and Tactical Axe just to name a few. It’s not that these weapons are bad, per se, but when the game throws 5 Crushers, 5 Bulwarks, 10 Maulers, and 8 Scab Ragers at you, it’s hard to deny the appeal of the Dueling Sword. Anti-Armor meta has reigned supreme for over a year at this point, and having to Sweat or Risk losing a game just to run something besides Bolters and Dueling Sword isn’t especially fun. This stagnation of weapon meta even applies to Weapon Blessings and Perks. What is the point in having 8 Blessings if I’m always taking the same 2? Who is using the bonus damage against Unarmored perk? It feels like a false choice. It’s hard to justify using anything too far outside the meta, either weapon or blessing, because doing so is just giving yourself a harder time than necessary. But I want to feel powerful regardless of my weapon and blessing options, not because of them. I want to make interesting builds that utilize off-meta combinations, as specialize into certain perk benefits. Having a variety of builds doesn’t matter if they all feel similar due to weapon options. I mean, I have a Dueling Sword build competing w/ a Thunder Hammer build as my primary boss killing build, because the Thunder Hammer can deal more damage, but the DS isn’t far behind and is a safer option between strong enemies.

And speaking of class builds, I also have found the changes to the classes disappointing. Every class is enjoyable to play at high level now, but that’s really only getting them to the starting line. Arbites and Ogryn still feel far more varied in build options than Zealot, Vet, and Psyker to me. We have greater build variety, but not a greater variety of builds. Unlike Ogryn, who has a distinct variations of Melee, Ranged, and Tanking builds, it doesn’t feel especially different to play various Zealot builds. The ranged benefits you get on the class are too miniscule to really shake up what you’re doing. Every Zealot Build will devolve into running at enemies w/ melee. The idea of being a “Sainted Gunslinger” isn’t even really represented in the tree anymore, and using Blazing Piety over Inexorable Judgment doesn’t feel like an especially impactful decision aside from a few weapon options. Vet can choose between a Marksman, Commander, and Commando build, but in the game where melee is so emphasized, it still feels underpowered on vet. Melee Psyker is now Psykers best feeling build IMO (in large part because while staff is still prob the most powerful, its gameplay has become incredibly stale), but Gun Psyker is still something I wouldn’t take in anything higher than Heresy. All the classes are powerful enough to take into the highest level of play, but the three OG Human sized classes still feel poorly defined.

TL;DR: I personally feel like the extra game modes biggest contribution has been in dividing the player base more and further adding to balance issues throughout the game. The enemies of the game need a major overhaul to better differentiate the factions and their strategies in encounters, and the game needs to end its overreliance on armored enemies spawn for challenge. Until they do, weapon meta will continue being very stagnate, and weak blessing and perk options only further contribute to the meta feeling stale. And while all of the classes are finally more fun than frustrating to play into high level content, three of the five classes still suffer from problems in build distinction and variety, making them feel stale, basic, and without anything but class specific weapons to make them worthwhile to play over other classes. The game feels stuck; mired in balance issues and a boring gameplay loop that has plagued it for over a year. You’re mostly using the same builds, and the same weapons, with the same blessing and the same perks, for the same benefits, against the overabundance of the same enemies. I’m so bored w/ it all, and the extra game modes don’t help because the problems persist there too, assuming they aren’t worse. What I really want is the opportunity to play more distinctive builds, that use more interesting combinations of blessings and perks, for a more varied and defined roster of enemies spawning in to fight me. And I am hoping that something gives the next update, because I want to continue to love this game. I just don’t feel like I’ve been given enough to work w/.

I completely agree.
Darktide offers a wide range of options — skill trees, weapons, blessings, and more — allowing players to freely customize their playstyle.
However, no matter how many options there are, if only a small handful are actually viable, that diversity becomes meaningless.

Even with the new skill trees, most players end up using nearly identical builds, to the point where you have to wonder whether turning it into a tree system had any real purpose.
Ever since the major overhaul of the combat system, the game’s balance has gradually fallen apart, and leaving it unattended for so long has led us to the current state.

At this point, Darktide’s balance is in its worst condition yet. It’s far beyond the point of fixing individual issues — the entire system needs to be re-evaluated from its core design principles.
Of course, the more options there are, the more complex the interactions become, and balance inevitably becomes harder to maintain. But that doesn’t mean it can be ignored.

No matter how much content a game has, if only 20% of it is actually worth playing, then for the players, it’s effectively a game with only 20% of its content.
