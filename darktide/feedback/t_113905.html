Arbites and Zealot Classes: Poor Presentation and Pigeonholing

If you don’t want to read my preface, skip this paragraph. And as always, TL:DR at the end. This is a far more personal take on the balance of the game than I’m used to giving, and it’s on a subject matter that, through Fatsharks own expressed plans, we aren’t likely to see many major changes for a while. And I think that’s fair considering the biggest problem in the game is enemies.
Unfortunately, enemies are core to the experience of every class, and you can’t really address on without addressing the other. Though, based on Hotfix#80(1.9.4), it’s my hope that we will see more number tweaks to classes and enemies to try to keep everything in line for a major Enemy Rework. Something that updates the older maps to better benefit changes to Enemy AI and Director Spawning Logic, which in turn will facilitate changes to enemy health pools. While Darktide is still kind of a hot mess, and Bound by Duty didn’t address a lot of the problems, I want to tentatively have faith that FS is using the opportunity of all the classes being roughly at the same power level to tweak other aspects of the game. Onto the point of this post, though.

============================================================================

This post is inspired by yet another conversation with my own peers, specifically regarding the current weakness of the Zealot class (whom I main) and my own feelings towards the Arbites, a class that I practically don’t play at all. In a game where shooting and melee are both important, every class can melee well, but the Zealot can’t shoot well. And while there’s no longer a mono-build, the viability of certain nodes still lead you down a very narrow path. It’s not a mono-build, but that’s only because there’s a fork at the end. Meanwhile, w/ Arbites, to be blunt, I hated the class on release. It felt arbitrarily powerful in a way that specifically stepped on the toes of every other class. The Survivability of Ogryn, the Adaptability of Veteran, the Melee Prowess of Zealot, and the CC of Psyker. Now, the class has been tweaked, and it isn’t nearly so overbearing, but I still don’t play. And in this post, I wanted to try and define and share why these two classes in particular feel so bad to me.

===1===

The Arbites and Zealot both fail to be compelling classes, but for completely different reasons. The Arbites, to me, fails not due to mechanical issues but issues of presentation. To be clear, I am not a 40K Nerd. I am a 40K: Darktide Nerd. While I know a decent bit about 40K through osmosis, Darktide is my primary source of exposure (and therefore investment) to the universe. If we use Ogryn as an example, I don’t need to read any books or watch any videos to understand what an Ogryn is or what they do; their class tree makes it very clear. Ogryns are Bloody Brawlers, Loyal Bodyguards, and Walking Weapon Platforms. Arbites don’t posses nearly as much clarity of presented fantasy as Ogryns, and Zealots don’t posses as much mechanical variety as Ogryns. The Focus and Style of play, the Role of each branch of the Ogryn’s Class Tree feels distinct in how I choose to engage enemies. And w/ Zealot, regardless of build, every engagement boils down to charging at the enemies. No branch of the Zealot Class Tree feels mechanically distinct. *Arbites weakness lies in how the fantasy of the class is poorly presented, and Zealots weakness is in how Pigeonholing the class to Melee has resulted in it feeling one dimensional.
*
===2===

In order to make this post, I did a lot of research into what exactly Darktide’s Lead Designer, Victor Magnuson, might be looking at. And this feels weird to me because Arbites are an actual faction of Imperial Agents w/, like, Codex Rules, while Zealots aren’t. There are (at least from what I personally can find) no imperial units called Zealots. Some units are considered Zealots (such as the Adepta Sororitas), but Darktides’ Zealot Class is really a hodgepodge of elements from different Imperial Agents and, if I were to guess, the Sanctifier Kill Team. And my take away is that Arbites is one Unit that, across games and lore, do a lot of different things. They’re Riot Control, Manhunters, Lawyers/Legal Orators, Investigators, and more. Meanwhile, Zealots are a mix of about 10 different Units that all kind of do one thing together: hurt things that get close to them.

===3===

Knowing more about Arbites, I can say that I can see how their mechanics relate to their profession. The Nuncio-Auquila, for instance, feels fitting for both the Riot Suppression and Orator aspects of the profession. But other details really need you to squint. Execution Orders and Terminus Warrant both feel like different flavors of “Manhunter”. Even knowing more about what an Arbites is, I don’t feel like I’m investigating anything. And elements of the class that do feel like they present the fantasy well are brought down by a lack of tax. Arbites is the most free form structured class in the game and has a ton of powerful, no brainer must take nodes packed together. For instance, Forceful is my favorite Arbite Keystone Path. It gives Damage Resistance, Survivability, Extra Impact, and Auto-Staggering. I really feel like I can hold and even push back the line. I can control the riot. But then nodes like “Target the Weak”, “The Emperor’s Fist”, “Final Warning”, “Weight of the Lex”, “Strike Down”, and “Drive Them Back” add so much free damage that what should be a great Crowd Control build ends up becoming an immensely powerful Crowd Clear build. The fantasy becomes muddles and w/ it, so does the Role. As hard to take down as an Ogryn, as much Crowd Clear as Psyker, and greater melee effectiveness than Zealot. I’m not trying to suggest that Arbites shouldn’t be powerful enough to actually kill those enemies, but I don’t want the power of one branch to come at the cost of invalidating two entire classes while stepping on the toes of a third. What I’d really like to see is Terminus Warrant replaced w/ something similar to Grail Knight Duties from Vermintide 2, where you load into the mission w/ secondary objectives and once you complete them, you get a party wide buff. Let us go on Investigations, collect a certain amount of resources, or kill a certain number of a specific kind of enemy, and then get a party wide buff when we’ve “closed” our case. Without going out of my way to learn more about Arbites, the class felt like an awkward summoner, and after learning about it, it feels like a missed opportunity. I’d like the mechanical fantasy of the class to be more distinct and interesting. Buff the Vox( I’d personally like to see “Fear of Justice” folded into the base ability and replaced w/ something that would make the Vox follow us around), give us a Keystone that reinforces the idea of being an investigator, and let Forceful be a Crowd Control path instead of turning it into yet another flavor of mass execution by either giving less free damage or giving it specifically to your teammates instead of yourself. This is a cooperative game; I don’t want to feel like I’m competing w/ my allies.

===4===

Zealots are weird. Unlike Arbites or Ogryns, they aren’t representative of one easily definable group of units. I feel like there’s elements of the Death Cultist Assassin, Sanctifier, Confessor, Conflagrator, Ministorum Priest, and more. From a lore perspective, all of these units are at least loosely related to The Ecclesiarchy but not necessarily a part of it. For instance, the Confessor is a priest with the Eccesiarchy but a Death Cult Assassin is, well, a Death Cultist. The two might work together but they aren’t really a part of the same specific hierarchy within the Imperium itself. This gives FatShark a lot of freedom in how they Characterize the class, and the Characterization FS seems to have chosen is “The Melee Class”. This freedom is great, because FS isn’t beholden to any specific archetype/profession to base their mechanics off of, but it’s resulted in Zealot being too pigeonholed to the “Melee” Archetype. This is a problem that exist w/ the Psyker and Veteran as well, but as stated earlier, Darktide is a game where you need to be able to be competent in melee and shooting, and Zealot is only achieving one of those things. The class’ strongest options are nodes that give them very generalized increases to damage and survivability. The class’s weakest options are ranged related nodes and utility; anything that Strays too far away from that melee focus. It’s gotten to the point where it’s less like the Zealot is the class that’s good at melee and more like it’s the only thing the class can do. Especially when considering that the specific fantasy provided by the other classes’ melee builds feels distinct both from their other builds and anything the Zealot can do. Immolation and Stunstorm grenades are both good, but they aren’t reinforced by anything like how the Knives can leech of your weapons’ blessings, including Uncanny Strike. Chorus and Shroud, outside of the specific bonuses they give you, are mechanical cosplay more than functional utility, as both take you outside of the game in a way antithetical to how the class otherwise operates. Chorus stops you from attacking; the thing the class wants to do. Shroud gives you a massive bonus to movement speed and damage, but it also dumps all your agro onto your allies so you can pretend to be a single target assassin. In a horde shooter. The invisibility doesn’t even last long enough to be used for utility, unlike Veteran’s Infiltrator. The best power amping nodes in the class tree, such as “Disdain” or “Sustained Assault”, are generalized. The class’ best utility, such as “Holy Revenant” and “Shield of Contempt” are passive. None of them require you to do anything outside of what you were already going to do, and therefore don’t encourage any specific style of play. Meanwhile the worst power amping and utility in the class are tied to specific actions or circumstances. 25% Block Reduction from “Impassable” is nice, except for the part where you only get it while dodging. The 15% Extra Damage from “Unseen Blade” is entirely outside of my control unless i’m using Shroud. Any node that gets/gives power for Backstabs or Flanking Hits is crippled by the inconsistent hit registration for those locations. And of the Three Firearm related Nodes, none of them are supported by other node options nor do they offer enough impact on their own to really shape your play. Finally, Inexorable Judgment is the only Keystone that will facilitate a ranged weapon and also the weakest keystone by far. It gives a bonus that’s less consistent than Blazing Piety and far less impactful than Martyrdom. The other two zealot grenades need better support from the tree or built into them. You could tie the Pulse from Chorus to Heavy attacks while channeling to make using the ability more involved. Replace Shroud w/ something less focused on the Cosplay of being an Assassin and more focused the Movement Speed, Evasiveness, and Lethality, like a Zealot version of Scriers Gaze. Perhaps an Auto-Dodge/Bullet Deflection State? Give the class a node that lets Weakspot hits automatically count as Backstab/Flanking on a cooldown to make up for how janky the Hit Registration can be for those targets. Make the weaker utility options passive. Make the strong utility options active. Shield of Contempt, for example, could split a total of 60% Damage reduction between myself and all allies in coherency when I blocked (15% for each at 4, 20% at 3, 30% at 2, and so on) but for an extra stamina cost. If you’re going to only give Zealot 3 Firearm Specific Nodes, at least let them be impactful. Maybe Enemies Explode of Flanking Hit Kills. The Ability to ADS while Sprinting. Counting as Blocking when Reloading Weapons. Options that would at least enhance the mechanical usage of guns on the class. And if you want the Zealot to have an Assassination Archetype, replace Inexorable Judgment w/ a Keystone that allows us to suppress/stagger or otherwise stupefy enemies with our terrifying display of violence. Lean into the utility of fighting away from the team and debuffing enemies to compliment the way Blazing Piety can Buff our nearby allies or how Matyrdom buffs us.

===5===

I recognize that any major changes to classes aside from those explicitly mentioned by FS (Veteran and Psyker) are probably a long way out, assuming that we get them at all. But between my least played class who I struggling to get immersed in playing (Arbites) and my most played class that I struggle to find enough mechanical flair to play over the other classes, my hope is that changes to address these particular weaknesses of the classes happen eventually. It’s not that either class is weak or “bad”, but that neither fully live up to their potential in a way that stops their own defined experiences from shining. For the Arbites, this is due to the experience only feeling defined after researching the topic out of game. For zealot, this is due to the experience feeling like it’s only defined by half of your tools of engagement.
