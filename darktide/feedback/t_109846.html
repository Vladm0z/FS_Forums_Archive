Now that I’ve spent some quality time playing the Arbitrator, I’d like to offer some suggestions on balancing it to put it in line with the reject classes. While I absolutely appreciate the work put into it and the sake of just having something new, it could do with some tweaks.

In my current assessment: some key issues I’ve found in my days following release of the class:

  1. As it stands, the class breaks the flow of the game by way of survivability since I don’t need to care about being in coherency since I can generate all the toughness I could ever need. Since the toughness perks are so close together, they are very easy to point into.

  • “Man and Cyber Mastiff”: (Should be) Grants 5% toughness to any group member when within 8 meters of your mastiff
    I love the idea of this perk but add all squad members to get the perk bonus. (This should grant the same toughness bonus to ANY / ALL squad mates within 8m of the mastiff and should honestly be part of the unleashed brutality keystone - or a branch for it)
    Wherever your mastiff is - if there are allies close to it fighting - they should also benefit.

  • “Walk it Off” - Spending 1 stamina generates 15% toughness over 3 seconds

  • “Shield Plates” - On block replenish 15% toughness over 3 seconds, on perfect block replenish 10% immediately
    I created a build specifically with increased stamina + stamina regen + block efficiency. While I enjoy having a build that I can do this on, it made me nearly invulnerable in many situations as I can both block to gain toughness with Retaliatory Force and Shield Plates - I can also stand around and do push attacks all day long and regain toughness without breaking a sweat. As I’m already spending the stamina to block / dodge / push, I’m gaining toughness in multiple directions. While I enjoy this, that is a ton of toughness regen in a very short burst and could simply do with a slight decrease in toughness regen overall.
    I’d recommend dropping it to 10% for both and “maybe” to 5% for perfect blocks and see how that goes.

I was reviving someone and had a group of enemies hitting me at the same time and I was regaining more toughness and stamina than they could deal to break me since it reduces stamina first whilst reviving.

  • “Target Neutralized” - somewhat on par with what other classes have but I honestly think it is unnecessary when you have so much toughness regeneration already. Or you could simply merge with “Up Close” and remove one of the two. Arbitrator already has a ton of toughness regen.
    Or maybe move one / both of these further down in the tree to force a branch-off. If I’m being honest, Shield plates / Walk it off, and Target Neutralized / Up Close, should have been inside the ending tree branches in the last 6 or 8 tiers of a specific keystone path leading to more specialization (but that would have limited initial toughness regen perks - tradeoff - it would be more on par with the other classes)
  1. Weapon balance: Everyone hates when you toss in weapon balance but it is needed…
  • Shot pistol damage - 1000 - and this comes with a shield and the accuracy to kill specials from halfway across a map whilst blocking their attacks. Why is a shot pistol doing more damage than the bolter and Hellbore Lasgun and even the plasma gun (uncharged)? I can snipe snipers from across the map if not in one the second shot will finish it - increase spread and the damage should be closer in line with the revolvers and bolt pistol around 500 - 600 damage. (Granted of course, you CAN snipe with the revolvers)
  • Arb Shotgun - same as above - roughly 950 damage with the ability to clear waves of enemies in only a few shots. Granted shotguns were in a sorry state prior to the patch but it could do with a reduction in overall damage. (I haven’t tried the tweaks to the other shottys yet)
  • Damage reduction in Arb shock maul and shield and Arbitrator maul. Shock maul and shield does more damage than the Ogryn shield and club - which I find a bit odd since … well… Ogryns being Ogryns. More damage and it attacks faster vs .. well.. Ogryn hitting slower and somehow less damage than a wee human??? What now? (Granted Ogryns do have amazing stagger)
  1. Can’t argue with the Cyber Mastiff … however … with the included above of party members gaining toughness when close to the mastiff so it is of more benefit to all (not just the coherency buff). It could do with some increased time to kill against more heavily armored specials. Specifically Maulers, Scab Ragers, Scab Flamers , Scab Gunners and maybe even Trappers. I hate to say it, but I believe they should have a chance to be knocked off or “assisted”, especially if being run over by a Mutant(very similar to pox hounds). This would create more of a necessity to the Arb and teammates to assist in finishing off an easy target.
  • With the specialists and gunner damage perks - I’ve seen my mastiff tear through hordes of them, while I do appreciate this, I think they need an increased time to kill overall especially those aforementioned armored versions.
    That said…
  • I do absolutely love the aggressiveness of the mastiff and watching it leap from enemy to enemy and saving me from hounds and muties when the need arises. (cough the only class with such escapes cough). Of course, it does help others too. Not to mention it has an aggressiveness all to its own so I don’t need to micro manage it and the inclusion of priority perks further specializes this.

In Conclusion:
I understand arbitrators are … trained and geared … better than the rejects are supposed to be however for the sake of game flow and “balance” - they need to be a bit closer together. And let’s not be coy about it; you have to make the new class appealing to players to buy and have a good testing ground for its place in the group. But as it stands: the arbitrators can ignore coherency easily and not be a burden (looking at you stealth Zealots and Vets), face roll crowds of enemies (on par with if not better than shield Ogryns), snipe enemies with an unnecessarily accurate shotgun-style pistol (almost replacing the need for a Veteran at this point as the only good they provide now is ammo regen and maybe grenades) and clear out specialists all with ease and without breaking a sweat. Granted the right reject in the right player’s hands can do the same - the arbitrators just make it that much easier and a little balance could go a long way to really make it enjoyable for everyone.
I’d argue for some damage reductions in the talent trees across the board but would start with reducing the overall damage output of the weapons first and retain perks as they stand.

Getting paired up as a solo reject with 3 arbitrators will leave you playing catch up or getting overly defensive as most times they are quite capable of leaving you behind to do so with little regard for it.

The other side of this argument would be to give the rejects an overall buff in certain aspects (Veteran could definitely use an update akin to the Ogryn recent buffs and rework). Buffs are generally the way I’d prefer it be done and the above are more suggestions based on what I’m currently experiencing when playing as both an Aribtrator teamed in full or solo with rejects or teamed up as a solo reject with the rest arbs (this is generally the most noticeable situation).
