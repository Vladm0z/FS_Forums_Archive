A simple way to make the party finder useful

8 Posts • 138 Views
H
Hammer_hold • 2025-09-05 20:35:20

We are all aware (or are not aware at all, which only underlines the issue) that the party finder doesn’t play almost any role in Darktide, aside from Havoc runs. It’s almost non-existent. I think that the next update can make it more useful by making a simple addition to the game.

After each mission, the team of players doesn’t automatically disband but stays together. It’s more healthy for the game and for the players, who can then go with the same team if they liked the team dynamic.

It also could make queuing for any mission a lot better in my opinion since teams of 2 or 3 rejects could instantly fill missions, where not everyone joined at first and were replaced by bots. Furthermore, it can also go the other way around where instead of waiting for the game, a player could just join an already existing party if it’s aboard the Mourning Star. And since it would automatically would keep players together, finding a team to play with wouldn’t be an issue.

This small change could really help bring the playerbase a lot closer together. And from my knowledge, this isn’t really a super hard change in the code to implement so it just seems like an easy QOL addition for the Fall update.
