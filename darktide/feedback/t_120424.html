Weaving in pushes in-between the usual melee combos is sometimes encouraged or necessary, with Blessings or talents that activate on push or push-attacks, or when horde-clear combos start with the push-attack, for example.

But I’ve read quite a few posts mentioning that with a lot of weapons it’s slow, inefficient, and kinda irritating having to do that (for example, with the crowbar because of its blessings). And I’m in that same boat. I suspected a specific mechanical reason. So I did some testing in both DT and VT2. I did not record footage and count frames, but I tried my best to spam left-click as quick as possible and to get the rhythm right. I tested this on Hive Scum with Rampage (+20%) and Blue Stimm (+20%), and Zealot FotF + blue stimm, so we’re at +40% AS or close to it, doesn’t matter if it stacks additively or multiplicatively.

There seems to be 2 categories depending on the weapon itself :

=> At +40% AS, the Push animation is not affected at all by Attack speed bonuses with most weapons (Crowbar, Bone Saw, Catachan sword, Heavy Swords, Shivs, Tac Axe, Combat axe, Dueling sword, Relic Blade, Thunder Hammer, Shock Maul, Combat blade)

=> At +40% AS, the Push animation speed is very perceptibly affected (Chain axe, Chain Sword, Eviscerator)

In contrast, in Vermintide 2 the Push animation is always obviously affected by attack speed buffs. In VT2, a Handmaiden talent encourages you to push by giving you really high attack speed (+30%) for 2 follow-up attacks. With this buff active, you can clearly push 30% faster if you really hammer on left-click, at least it’s obvious with the spear&shield.

So, unless someone tells me I’m mistaken, I’ll be off to make a bug report because it looks really strange to me that only Chain weapons’ push benefit from attack speed. Seems like an oversight !
