firstly, Solo mode.

listen, i get it, some people don’t think it belongs, some people don’t care about it, but most people DO want it, somebody even went so far as to make an account on the forums with a name that was asking for solo mode, if not outright demanding it! solo mode was one of the first things put into the book of grudges, yet you have yet to add it. just remove the requirement of two people being in one private game, then you’ll have solo mode. we aren’t demanding it be fully offline with modding capability, just that it be available.

secondly, customizable bots.

we shouldn’t be stuck with just the same random vets for our bots, they never even use blitzes or skills! the least we should be able to do is customize their trees a bit so that they can actually be useful on higher difficulties, or at least let us put our own characters in their places. it’d be nice if we could have our vets, zealots, and our arbitrators all on the same team so while we play our psykers or the like, we can go up to auric maelstrom and not become mulched within 7 minutes because our bots aren’t completely worthless like usual. it would also make people try other classes because they’re forced to do it if they don’t want a team of nothing but ogryns or psykers or some other class.

thirdly, free coloring.

the basic thought is that we’d be able to get singular colors from the commissary and then we’d be able to use that color on our clothes, but not our weapons. this would mean that we can’t buy them in 3 parts anymore, but we CAN still make any cosmetic any color, so long as we bought said color from the commissary. this has been a request since day one. just add it.
