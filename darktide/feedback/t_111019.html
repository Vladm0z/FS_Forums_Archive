I don’t see any good use for the gun currently. And FS’s idea of “buffing” it without really changing how it works won’t solve this, imo, because of the bad design at its core.

A weapon with long reload sequence like that and such a low clip size OUGHT to add some tremendous utility and/or punch, to make sense. It doesn’t have both. Neither really have deep/wide cleave, nor that punch really, the revolver is way better hard punching all-rounder, or the bolter.

Here is redesign what could make it viable imo:

Mark Mk. I

It’s effectively a hand cannon, with both deep and wide cleave AND powerful punch

  1. Pointblank - the power is enough to instakill a Crusher on any difficulty, due to both high damage and high rending (when firing both barrels at once).
  2. At any range beyond pointblank the power and rending greatly diminish (because of the wide pattern of spread used for the pellets) - while the spread grows significantly. So anything carapace armored will survive a single shot, but anything unarmored or flak armored will probably die instantly, allowing you to clear hoards efficiently (on Veteran, when coupled with melee weapons and talents like Weapon Specialist that effectively reload one shot in your gun on every kill; by swapping from melee to Doulbe Barrel after a few kills you are able to make a shot, reducing chaff to shreds; then get back to melee)

Mark Mk . II

Works close to how it works today, with more decent range and still kinda powerful punch - but not as powerful as Mk.I’s point blank (may kill a crusher with 2 shots, but not always and everywhere). Doesn’t require precise aiming, but has way lower effective distance than the revolver. More powerful than bolt pistol, but has much shorter range. Has almost no spread or cleave, but good rending, also a higher ammo stash. So basically an easy-to-use short-range anti-armor modification for builds that rely on melee weapons to clear the hoard.
