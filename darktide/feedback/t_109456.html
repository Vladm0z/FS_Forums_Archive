This is the big disappointment of the update for me. The last derank was a bit annoying, but understandable given Havoc changed so drastically. It felt fair to earn our way back up to 40. This time? It really kills my enthusiasm to play the mode.

Havoc games are long and come with a decent chance of failure. Grinding from 30 back to 40 with randoms is at least a 10 hour grind for most of us. Not all of us have that much time. De-ranking us alongside an event, a new class, a new story, new Mortis Trials… and without a significant change to Havoc to go along with it? I feel demotivated to play the mode.

I don’t think the developers quite understand what makes Havoc fun. I’ve been 40 for months, but my friend group I play with once a week had just finally just finished working our way back to 40 and was looking forward to experimenting and messing around without having to worry about losing. Now it’s back to the super serious grind. It really takes the sense of achievement out of the whole thing when ranks reset with no warning, no rewards, and no significant changes.

Right now, the reward is being able to play 40’s with no penalty once you get there. If you’re going to keep up these resets, please remove the weekly de-rank for inactivity, please make it clear how long a season will be so we know what to expect, and please give some kind of appreciable reward for your participation and highest achieved rank during that time. Crafting materials are not enough.

Otherwise, just let us stay at 40 so we can enjoy the mode for what it is. Havoc is by far the most interesting game mode introduced thus far for many of us, so can we please just be allowed to enjoy the mode without the additional stress of constantly worrying about rank or if we have time to play it each week? Thank you.
