Agayek • 2026-07-05 07:39:17
LVSpooky:

a majority of it is toughness only.

No. No it is not.

In fact, most of Ogryn’s DR in the talent tree is health DR. In fact, the sum total of TDR in the Ogryn tree is a 10% and a 5% white node, 25% TDR for 10s on a 20s cooldown when an ally’s toughness breaks, and 15% TDR from reaching full stacks of allies in coherency taking damage, and up to 10% TDR for hitting things.

Literally every other source of DR on the tree is health DR.

The difference is that health DR applies to both health and toughness, while TDR only applies to toughness. So Ogryn does have a decent bit more TDR (~40% additional TDR, depending on build), but that’s not a bad thing and making all of his DR health is just a recipe for disaster, either by making Ogryn even more of an unkillable god or completely ruining his survivability, depending on how the transition is handled.

Really, you just need to learn how to leverage generating 20+% of your toughness bar literally just by swinging your weapon.
