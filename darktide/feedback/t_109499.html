SgtHavelock • 2025-06-24 15:41:07

To add my voice, the new mission board is egregious and repugnant, and I chose those words very carefully.

I am colourblind (red-green) and opening up that mission terminal is a horrid experience each time. It isn’t a ‘the new thing must be bad’ reaction to a change, but a real and physical problem. I have to focus on each of the small mission card images randomly placed on the screen to decipher what they are - the new styling of them is just atrocious - and as I am doing that there is a blaring green background behind them interfering and overwhelming my brain’s ability to comprehend what it is seeing. It is really uncomfortable, even for the very short periods I am interacting with it, and I absolutely loathe it.

I understand the aesthetical idea behind it, but in 2025 principles for Accessibility and UX design are not newfound concepts and those principles should not be eschewed simply because someone has a ‘great idea’. It is embarrassing that the mod ‘Sorted Mission Grid’ nailed it two years ago and that Fatshark haven’t gotten on board with a readily accessible and easy to visually comprehend mission selection screen.
