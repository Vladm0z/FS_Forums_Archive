I usually don’t really go in for the events that much. I suppose because a particular frame doesn’t appeal to me aesthetically, and I’d rather otherwise play havoc or in my pre-havoc days, just enjoy the regular game. But.. This is my favorite event so far.

Thumbs up from this bedraggled reject.
