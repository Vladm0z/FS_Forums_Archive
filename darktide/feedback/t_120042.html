The point being you WANT to run something specific if you expect to chew through them quickly, a bolter or pickaxe or crowbar or chainaxe or a thunder hammer or kraks/rockets (There’s options!). Having fewer spawn would still allow folks without strong Cara to whittle them down without being swarmed, so you don’t HAVE to, but having at least one player dedicated to heavy armor/boss damage should be an appreciated roll outside of raw DPS.
