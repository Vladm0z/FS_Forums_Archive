My weapon balance suggestions after 1400 hours

It’s no secret that a significant portion of the weapon roster is struggling to keep up with the rest in the higher difficulties of the game, mostly as a result of their anemic performance vs tougher enemies. I’d know, because I’ve been a part of the game since the closed beta (RIP Beta HUD). Rather than leaving these weapons to rot as they have for nearly 3 years, I’ve decided to brainstorm some changes that would make them relevant without destroying the balance of the game. I should preface this by saying that I know I’m not a game dev, I know it isn’t as simple as switching numbers around and copy-pasting “x” here. I am merely presenting my ideas that I believe would lead to a healthier roster than DS, DS, Knife, DS (soon to be quad knife post DS nerf) for the non-Ogryn/Arby classes. Regardless of how unbalanced the class itself is, the Arbites class brought with it some incredibly fun and versatile weapons that quite frankly blow most of the roster out of the water in terms of satisfaction to use. These suggestions I feel would bring some of these weapons up to parity (in terms of fun) or at the very least close to it. I don’t expect a single dev to acknowledge this post or take it seriously, and I’m sure they’ve already read a thousand like it. I’m mostly just venting about the lack of viable and fun late game options. PLEASE REWORK THE DEVIL CLAWS, thanks.

Devil Claw Swords

These changes are meant to transform the Devil Claws from the flimsy “noob” weapons they are right now to proper jungle fighter machetes that are easy to pick up and understand. There is no room in the higher difficulties for a weapon that specializes in killing the least dangerous enemies in the game, especially if it struggles to kill a single mauler, let alone a crusher. Every class has access to a better crowd control weapon or ability that doesn’t sacrifice all melee armor penetration.

Across the board changes –

  • ~100% increase to light attack vanguard damage vs Flak. There is no possible justification for this being so low. Up to 8 max damage stat lights to kill a single scab shooter in Auric? Really?
  • Minor increase to light attack vanguard damage vs Unyielding, on par with unarmored.
  • ~50% increase to heavy attack strikedown damage vs. Unyielding and Carapace. ~25% vs Flak.
  • Significant increase to heavy attack vanguard vs Flak, Carapace, and Unyielding. Once again, this damage is laughably bad vs incredibly common enemies beyond Malice.
  • ~100% damage increase to light attack strikedown vs Carapace and Unyielding.
  • Replace Trauma with Uncanny Strike (Nerfed to Max 50%?)
  • Replace Devastating Strike with up to 50% Strength for 3 seconds on special action hit.
  • Replace Vicious Slice with up to 100% damage to special hit

Catachan Mk.1 –

Replace its current light attack combo with looping downwards strikedown attacks using light attack 3 and the chainsword’s light attack 1 as a base.

Catachan Mk. 4 –

Replace the vanguard heavy with a strikedown thrust based on the Force Greatsword, using similar damage values.

Catachan Mk. 7 –

  • Reverse the order of the light attack chain (strikedown, vanguard, vanguard)
  • Replace the heavy strikedown attack with the aforementioned thrusting attack.
  • Light 1 (new) flows into heavy 2 (new) and vice versa.

Turtolsky Heavy Swords

The Heavy Swords are in a better position than the Devil Claws at the moment and need less fundamental changes to improve their usefulness in the late game, mostly needing better armor penetration and higher boss damage.

Across the board changes –

  • 100% damage increase to heavy attack strikedown vs Carapace. 50% damage increase to heavy attack strikedown vs Unyielding.
  • Combine Chained Deathblow and Deathblow.
  • Old Chained Deathblow is replaced with Can Opener (Yes, the signature blessing of the Ripper Gun).
  • Replace Bladed Momentum with up to +100% critical hit chance on next attack after elite kill.
  • Buff Perfect Strike to up to +50% melee critical damage.

Chainswords

At the moment, Chainswords are in a similar position to the Devil Claws and Heavy Swords, being mediocre non-mixed horde clearers with low stagger and armor pen. These changes are meant to bump up the armor pen a bit while also giving them above average boss DPS.

Across the board changes –

  • Significant increase to light attack strikedown and vanguard vs Carapace (the damage it currently achieves with its vanguard heavies should be at least how much the strikedown lights achieve)
  • Significant damage increase to heavy attack strikedown vs Carapace, Flak, and Maniac.
  • ~100% damage increase vs Unyielding (all attacks)
  • Special Action – Instead of giving the chainsword a single, powerful ripping attack like the other chain weapons, it instead “powers-up” the weapon for 5 seconds and makes its attacks “bite” the target like the chainaxes, giving a fair boost to single target damage and penetration at the expense of speed and cleave.
  • Bloodletter reduced to up to +4 bleed stacks per hit (for obvious reasons)

Tactical Axes

Good mobility, but completely outclassed by the knives even after the recent balance patch. Just increase their damage vs Carapace, Maniac, and Unyielding on all attacks, especially on the heavy attacks.

Force Swords

Overall in a significantly better position than the previous weapons, the Force Swords only really need some adjustments for improved flow.

Obscurus Mk. 2 –

  • Adjust light attack 1’s damage profile to match light attack 2.
  • Adjust the special activation to behave more like the Illsi, being repeatable and empowering the strike with increased damage and penetration. (In its current iteration, the move is too slow and too low damaging to ever be worth it)

Deimos Mk. 4 –

Most of its damage is achieved through looping the powerful heavy 2 with the low damage light 3, but due to the speed of light 3 it seems like more of a “tax” or annoyance than anything else. Due to this, I propose a move-set adjustment for better flow

  • Light 1 is now removed from the standard chain, with light 3 taking its place as the starting attack, chaining into light 2 (old) then light 4.
  • The order of heavy attacks is reversed, with heavy 2 (old) starting the combo and can flow into light 1 (old) in a loop.
  • Heavy 1 (old) still flows into light 2 (old)
  • Slight damage increase to lights 2 and 3 (old) vs Unarmored and Infested.
  • Special Activation is now a high stagger light or heavy thrust attack with an instant, rather than delayed, payload.

Force Staves

While most of the Force Staves are viable in all difficulties to varying degrees, there is one that struggles to fill its intended role. That being the Electrokinetic Staff, which despite being a single target focused staff, can struggle to kill tougher enemies with its secondary attack in a timely manner without repeated critical hits. Luckily a simple damage boost should do the trick.

Electrokinetic Staff –

Increased secondary attack damage vs Carapace and Unyielding by ~30%

Power Swords

Great, fun weapon with fantastic damage vs all targets with one Achilles’ heel; If you’re caught out by a group of Ragers or the melee Twin you’re pretty much helpless without VOC. Just increase the backwards dodge distance a bit to create enough space to fight back.
