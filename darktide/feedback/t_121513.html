I think Mortis trails can be improved considerably with minimal effort. First use the same tech that is used in expeditions. You play on all 3 maps(even if there are new maps keep it 3) in random order in 1 game. Every end of the 4th round you go through the door and go into a mini buy room where the next map will be loaded in.

Start:
Depending on the difficulty (Or it doesn’t have to) Spawn in with level 300 weapons without blessing. With the lower weapon level you can start off with lower amounts of enemies to offset that.

Buy Room:
In the buy room you can upgrade and roll for blessings for your weapons. Also have a cheaper option to get a randomly generated weapon with randomly generated blessings. Healing station(And a cheaper corruption clear kit), Stims, Ammo station and ammo crates etc. Maybe able to buy on display indulgence minoris for expensive price.

Objectives:
I have noticed that the most action usually happens close to spawn and the medstation. That discourges exploration of the map. I think each round without an objective there should be a random spot on the map you need to capture with enemies trying to push you out of that spot.

Economy:
I think you can litterly just copy and paste the scraps from expeditions. It’s a dream. It doesn’t have to make sense. I do think that the amount you can get should be random. It’s more engaging if 1 game you struggle financially even if you have all of the pickups and another game you get enough for a nice roll.

One more thing I think they should add an option at the main mission terminal to include quick play search for Mortis trails too. Similar to the campaign missions
