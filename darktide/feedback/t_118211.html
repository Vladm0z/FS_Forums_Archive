TLDR: The “Pox Gas” mutator makes it impossible for me to play the game and i don’t think i’m alone.

Full transparency - i’ve only played half a level with the “Cartel’s Favour” event mutator, after which i had to quit because my head and eyes hurt. Anecdotally it seems to me like most people playing the game at a high level are either on the autism spectrum or have ADD / ADHD. Due to the brain chemistry of autistic / ADD brains, we commonly experience sensory issues. In some cases, these can help. I for example can distinguish pretty much every spawn sound, no matter how distant or lost in the audio mix it is. While helpful, it can be overwhelming while trying to focus on moment to moment combat.

I already get overstimulated sometimes from the grating visual effects - when the screen is gray grom wounds, yellow from BoN vomit, broken from toughness breaking, pumping gas around the frame from a stim i took and shaking from recoil.

Pox gas (from any source) adds multiple visual and audio-effects:

  • health being corrupted / damaged
  • toughness breaking from damage
  • heavy breaking / caughing
  • pumping / swriling gas sound effect
  • limited visibility from clouds of gas
  • green screen effect

All these effects being added at the same time to all of the other effects happening during regular gameplay is the reason why i avoid pox gas as much as i can. I sometimes have to take a break from the game after playing a round where too many pox bombers have flooded the level with overstimulation-gas. It’s also the reason why i can’t play the pox gas mutator at all. My brain uses up it’s entire capacity twice or thrice as fast just to play at the same level i’m ususally playing at.

Due to this, i won’t be able to parttake in the event at all. Playing the game with the pox gas mutator is like having your desk/console placed in the middle of a mosh pit. Playing at lower difficulties won’t solve this problem either, since it’s not the difficulty that’s the problem, it’s the sensory experience of the game that become ubearable.

I know that most of my autistic / ADD / ADHD friends experience similar issues WITHOUT the pox gas mutator and that they would never post about it, so i wanted to share my experience and maybe it resonates with other people.
