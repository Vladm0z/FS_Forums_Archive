The idea I had was to make a Scab Commander or Sergeant or whatever. He’s a normal scab elite that has a relatively low spawn rate, and never spawns more than one in a given pack of gunners or shotgunners, and uses the Radio Operator model.

The Commander is a mixed melee/ranged enemy, in the same vein as shotgunners, where he’ll shoot at you then when you get close he’ll pull out a melee weapon and try to whack you, but his AI tries to stay at range and avoid letting you get that close, kinda like gunners. He should be reasonably tanky, but not quite Mauler tier. Somewhere between Plasma Gunner and Mauler, I feel is about right.

And his gun works a little differently than regular gunners. It charges up and fires a big burst, in the same way plasma gunners do, but it also does very light health damage with a high toughness multiplier (e.g., something like 5-10 damage to health, but 30-50 to toughness). Any player that gets hit by it is then debuffed with one stack of the Packmaster melee debuff, to a max of one stack, and at the same time, it taunts every (scab?) enemy near the targeted player to attack them.

So it’s a low volume elite that sorta bridges the gap between Elites and Specials. It doesn’t do a whole hell of a lot on its own and is fairly easy to kill, but any player that gets caught out and can’t avoid the shot has to play like a god for something like 8 seconds or really hope their team is competent and nearby to help get the enemies under control before they get swamped.
