first, i’ll go over the personal stuff, then the things that i know for a fact other people hate.

the mech-psword should have a second mark that can be used for clearing out swaths of pox walkers like the other ones instead of stealing things from the vet who already doesn’t have as much unique stuff as the other classes, this goes for the phos-pistol too, meaning it should have a second mark that actually deals heavy damage to single targets but doesn’t have the AoE effect of the normal mark. lastly for the personal stuff, the mechanicus should have had a shotgun modeled after the WW1 trench gun with a bayonet and slam fire, or at least the double barrel or combat shotguns. this would make everybody happy since they have the same type options as everybody else. lastly, why do i need to take an ammo regen passive for the marksman ability? it should just be an ability modifier.

now for the stuff that ISN’T personal gripes.

the theft of two weapons from vet is pretty bad since the vet now only has 4 unique weapons instead of the standard 6. i say standard because vet, arbi, and zealot only have 4 while everybody else has at least 6 and vet used to have 6 before skitarii arrived.

then we have the penances. two of them are just to master every skitarii exclusive weapon, which just shows that the penances were so VERY hard to think up, so much so, that there’s also penances to tell you to use every ability with every keystone and to use every aura, ability, and keystone, along with a penance to tell you to play expaditions and mortis trials as a skitariius. then we have curse of malfunction, which doesn’t seem bad until you realize that the voltaic emitter doesn’t work very well for the penance it’s near required for.

then there’s the flamer servo-skull penance. the entire problem there is that you need to light 80 enemies on fire in one use, but there’s no way to do this early on, so you need to go into high intensity damnation and auric missions, even then, it’ll take awhile since it’s likely that the spinning skull will only light up to 70 enemies on fire since it’s not an absolute burn, rather being a chance at burn while half the enemies just don’t worry about it since the skull isn’t facing that way. the forward spray skull isn’t very good either since it’s not shooting very far and it’s still got that same problem of not actually lighting enemies on fire half the time.

there are more general complaints, but i’ll just leave those for other people and move on to the vanguards.

vanguard is a great concept, but needs a rework and an upgrade to specialist and elite enemy. since they basically have two health bars and can’t be staggered once the shield is broken (even though you can stagger them when it’s not). they also have headshot resistance due to the helmet, which i haven’t actually noticed on any other common enemy that has a helmet. please, fix this, or at least make the shields break faster and the enemies staggerable after the shield is broken.
