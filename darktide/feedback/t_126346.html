Greetings Rejects,

Fatshark’s introduction of Havoc Mode successfully gave veteran Rejects the mechanical grind they wanted, culminating at the maximum ceiling of Havoc 40. However, climbing from Rank 1 to Rank 40 can feel like looking at a spreadsheet rather than staring into the horrifying abyss of the Warhammer 40,000 universe. Numbers don’t evoke fear; grimdark flavor does.

By mapping our standard mission terminal and the intense 40 Havoc tiers onto a clear 7-Tier Threat Escalation Matrix, we can ground the game’s brutal endgame scaling directly into the lore. It gives players distinct, mythic milestones to brag about beyond just a numerical rank and perfectly reflects the terrifying reality of an escalating galactic warzone.

Headline: Proposal: Expanding the Mission Terminal – A 7-Tier Threat Escalation Matrix to Bridge the Gap to Havoc

Right now, our standard mission selection loops comfortably up to Damnation and Auric Maelstrom before sending us off to tackle Commissar Dukane’s brutal Havoc Mode, capping out at the ultimate challenge of Havoc 40. However, the difficulty naming conventions on the main terminal feel disconnected from the extreme, mythic scale of Warhammer 40,000 lore as you push into that high-level endgame content.

To align our standard gameplay loop with the devastating reality of the Moebian Domain—and to contextualize the absolute meat-grinder of high-tier Havoc Assignments—I propose updating and expanding the mission terminal into a unified 7-Tier Threat Escalation Matrix.

This system reorganizes our existing base difficulties (Levels 1–5) while creating two final, lore-heavy tiers to represent the terrifying peak of the Havoc Mode ladder (Ranks 30 to 40+), where The Emperor’s Fading Light turns the game into a true fight for survival.

Here is how the tactical progression should look:

Level 1: Seditious (Easy)

  • Description: Low-intensity civilian unrest, minor logistical shortfalls, or localized worker dissent in the lower hives. Ideal for fresh Rejects learning the fundamentals of melee pacing and basic weapon handling. Enemies are sluggish, scattered, and easily contained.
  • Imperial Dictum:“Compliance is the first step toward purity. Cleanse the rot before it spreads.”

Level 2: Malice (Medium / Havoc Ranks 1–10)

  • Description: Entrenched heretical forces and organized cult raiding parties establishing a foothold. This tier demands proper squad cohesion, basic armor management, and spatial awareness. Elite specialists begin deploying in coordinated pairs to disrupt your formation.
  • Imperial Dictum:“By the blood of martyrs, the line holds. Steel your resolve against the shadows.”

Level 3: Heresy (Hard / Havoc Ranks 11–20)

  • Description: The conflict escalates beyond baseline human capacity. Sector-wide heretical corruption forces strike teams into relentless warfare. Special operatives must deal with punishing horde density, lethal specialist overlapping, and strict ammunition scarcity. Errors in positioning are met with swift execution.
  • Imperial Dictum:“Only in death does duty end. Purge the infection before it takes root in the hive’s heart.”

Level 4: Damnation (Very Hard / Havoc Ranks 21–30)

  • Description: Complete collapse of local planetary defense forces. The sky bleeds, monstrosities roam freely, and enemy horde density is at its absolute peak. Every single chamber of ammunition counts, and survival relies entirely on flawless mechanical synergy, optimal build paths, and split-second decision-making.
  • Imperial Dictum:“There is no time for peace. No respite. No forgiveness. Fight until the hive burns.”

Level 5: Auric (Extreme / Maelstrom Baseline / Havoc Entry)

  • Description: The ultimate standard “Gold-Tier” meat grinder reserved strictly for the Inquisition’s elite Kill Teams. You are dropped into high-intensity shock-troop gauntlets with stacked anomalies and impossible odds. Death is the statistical baseline.
  • Imperial Dictum:“May the Emperor have mercy on your soul, for the galaxy has none.”

Level 6: Primarch (Apocalyptic / Sector Crisis / Havoc Ranks 31–39)

  • Description: A threat tier named after the Emperor’s demigod sons, signifying a conflict of mythological proportions. This represents the punishing endgame tier of Havoc Mode where The Emperor’s Fading Light II disables enemy spawn dampening, drowning squads in relentless elites. Reality is actively tearing apart as heavy armor, super-heavy war machines, and endless waves of specials deploy simultaneously.
  • Imperial Dictum:“Let the stars bleed. Stand firm in the shadow of giants, for the Emperor watches your final stand.”

Level 7: Abyssus (Calamity / Extinction / The Havoc 40 Peak)

  • Description: The absolute terminal point of tactical computation, matching the peak of a Havoc 40 Assignment. The Eye of Terror opens directly beneath the warzone, or a Necron World Engine activates. Victory conditions do not exist. Operatives are dropped into a shifting, non-Euclidean nightmare where enemy spawns are infinite, negative modifiers stack to their absolute maximum limit, and warp corruption actively drains your life force. Success is defined simply by how many seconds you delay the inevitable.
  • Imperial Dictum:“The galaxy is a meat grinder, and you are the final tooth. Do not ask for mercy; ask only that your death chokes the machine.”

**

Let’s Make the Endgame Truly Grimdark**

Adding these two difficulty tiers doesn’t require Fatshark to build entirely new maps or assets from scratch. It simply takes the existing, brutal mechanics of high-tier Havoc 40 scaling and frames them within the legendary narrative weight that makes the Warhammer 40k universe so captivating. It gives the most dedicated players a true mountain to climb and a badge of honor worth fighting for.

What do you all think, Rejects? Would you dare step into a Level 7 Abyssus operation, or are you content staying in the lower hives?
