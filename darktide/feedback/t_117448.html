I’ve been off an on with Darktide for a long while and recently started binging the newest content, lv30 all classes and I hover in the 20s in Havoc. This is my personal thoughts but I don’t think the classes present interesting new gameplay experiences, excluding Psyker and Ogryn, who are both in my opinion the gold standard in moment-to-moment gameplay variety.

I was reading through recent reviews that put into words my feelings, classes in Vermintide II felt unique, whereas in Darktide, excluding blitzes, some of the classes are too similar imo. Veteran Arbitrator and Hive-Scum are a key example in my experience, although Scum is more interesting due to the stimm and toxin mechanic.

Please consider making future classes more unique, gameplay and visually. I don’t know if you have a restriction on xenos classes or a mechanicus class but as a player, variety is something I want to see much more of. The fact that only a Psyker and Orgyn could believably use their class exclusive weapons could be a good baseline, no reason a Veteran couldn’t use dual handguns for example.
