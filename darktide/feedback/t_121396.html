unleashzealot • 2026-05-09 06:10:06

The modification plan for Uncanny Strike is unreasonable. The damage amplification from Brittleness against other types of Armour (Maniac, Unyielding, Flak armour) for Duelling Swords and Combat Blade, as well as the extra damage beyond 100%, has been overlooked.

Tests were conducted on the heavy attack weak spot damage of Duelling Swords Mark IV, Duelling Swords Mark V, and Combat Blade Mark III under three conditions: 1. Old version of Uncanny Strike, 2. Hypothetical first and second hits of the new version of Uncanny Strike (0% Brittleness), 3. Hypothetical third and subsequent hits of the new version of Uncanny Strike (40% Brittleness). The results are as follows.

image1339×729 46.9 KB

For Duelling Swords and Combat Blade, the damage of the first and second hits against Flak armour, Unyielding, Maniac on weak spots has decreased by 20% or even 30% compared to the old version of Uncanny Strike hitting weak spots at full stacks. These weapons usually kill enemies with Flak armour or Maniac in one or two weak spot hits. This means the new version of Uncanny Strike not only reduces damage against Carapace armour as stated in the patch notes, but also reduces damage against other enemies. Meanwhile, Combat Blade has received no corresponding buffs, and Duelling Swords have once again had their weak spot and critical hit damage reduced. This is highly unreasonable.
The patch notes also mention: “Rending could be applied to any outgoing damage from the player, and not just to damage coming from their Melee weapon or towards the enemy that was being hit to trigger the Blessing.” I believe this is a reasonable use of game mechanic diversity. Making Rending effective against “damage not just from their Melee weapon” requires player skill, and this is not an easy maneuver.
