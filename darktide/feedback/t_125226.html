Just a couple things I’ve noticed since I’ve been back, I had all characters to level 30 in the first month of the game for note and I play every single character like I did in VT1 or VT2.

  1. Veteran still seems to be the red headed step child out of everyone assembled, there’s some good QoL changes and some bad ones in comparison to how the tree was 2 years ago, mainly it still feels like there’s genuinely no point to anything other than focus target keystone wise. Marksman’s focus is definitely improved but bouncing around between Psyker, Arbi, Zealot, Hive Scum, and Skitarii, basically any other buff that stacks up and gives any sort of damage bonus completely outdoes Veteran’s version. Arbi straight up gets a better version of weapon spec, too. Plus the lack of unique weapons (Power Sword should frankly work like power falchion/relic atp because I really don’t see a good argument for using it over the Falchion)
    Plus, no unique weapons anymore since Skitarii gets the plasma gun and their power sword is basically just an outright better version of the Vet’s (There’s very very few maps where you could probably reliably use it but a longlas or a sniper rifle of some kind would be nice since evidently asking for a hotshot lasgun would be a bridge too far even though we have, idk, Skitarii running around now with their shenanigans and chicanery)
    It’s also a bit shenanigans that there’s not one, but two classes who have the reload on ability use thing that vet use to have, that frankly do more damage than even bolter vet vs monsters and otherwise which, iirc, is the reason it was removed to begin with? I’ve watch Gungryns kill Daemonhosts and other bosses in legit under 30 seconds like bolter vet use to be able to… Hive Scum I don’t think I’ve seen that level of damage from as of yet but it wouldn’t surprise me if they could do the same but I honestly so rarely get them and haven’t messed around too much with mine other than getting them to 30.

  2. Psyker… I don’t remember if Scrier’s had 50% peril reduction before but it really probably shouldn’t, and the talent that just outright prevents you from blowing up after it’s done makes infinite smite and inferno staff like 10x more obnoxious, why soulblaze stacks up to 30something and then has talents that just lets you blow up stuff and apply all those stacks to everything around it is beyond me but the literal most braindead and boring way to play psyker is now the strongest for… W/e reason? The only saving grace is that most of the players using it do not have game fundamentals beyond mashing left click and if you don’t babysit like 9/10 of them they’ll get mobbed and die so you can actually play the game instead of sitting in the proverbial cuckchair. Also frankly think Force 1handed swords need a look at since there’s really no reason to not use the 2 hander and just become Ichigo Kurosaki. Force Axes/Maces would, frankly, be nice. Force weapons aren’t just swords, after all, you could do spears, duelling swords, etc. Would better fit the class fantasy of a psyker rather than having random normie weapons in hand.
    The Mark system should be utilized for staffs in order to give them alternate move sets, ik voidblast or w/e is basically just VT2’s fireball but there’s a few staff types from VT2 that could work as alternate marks for the electro/void/trauma/inferno still, I think. It would make them far more engaging/interesting I think for a lot of people. Beam or bolt staff could work as either electro or flame marks, but either way having the shotgunny/machinegunny stuff would be nice for a lot of builds and give people more options for if they wanted to play an actual psyker rather psychic guy with recon lasgun which seems to be the norm. Idk, it feels worse than when I quit around April 2024.

  3. Skitarii got completely overbuffed (I wasn’t around for initial release, just this recent buff patch) and it’s made worse by mods considering I just had a match recently where someone had some autotag BS wherein they were shooting stuff with their servoskull (that, for some reason, does more damage than a bolter, because mounting a laspistol to a servo skull = bolter damage evidently?) that was completely behind them. They were fighting mobs coming down from the other side of the room with their back turned but somehow tagging elites and specials completely out of their view.
    Damage/kills/etc don’t really matter but when the majority of the ones I am seeing are by default hitting 1mil damage with arc rifle/servo skull and then have some kind of AOE nuke ability that removes entire groups of crushers without needing to aim or really do much of anything it’s kind of ridiculous. It’s good to see people consistently calling out the inferno stuff but basically no one I’ve seen has pointed out Skitarii are the same level of oppressive of making the rest of the team basically obsolete esp since the Servoskull is basically just a 5th player with a super boosted laspistol that always has infernus and can basically fire as fast as an MG42 with infinite ways to just instantly restore the charges it uses. I didn’t even bother to finish the last 5 levels on my own Skitarii just because it was astounding how easy the class was.

  4. There seems to be some kind of bug rn that I don’t recall seeing before I stopped playing in 2024, wherein Poxbursters will straight up ignore pushes from people who are not their intended targets, a friend of mine who stuck with the game told me at one point there was a similar bug with dogs but I’m unsure of the validity of the claim. My memory may also be poor and this was always the case? Either way, incredibly frustrating and annoying + very much not latency or w/e.
