kqgee • 2026-06-13 22:11:54

If you are in front of the party finishing a fight and you turn around to see players “dealing” with enemies, in most cases, if its not a disabler the player in front will think “they got that” and push ahead to get engaged with the next group. The player “rushing” is creating safe space for players catching up. This can leave new players or players in the back to feel like they have to rush to catch up, instead of realizing the path from their position to the player in front is safe to traverse.

An experienced group, or player bringing up the rear, will use this space to funnel enemies while keeping up with the leader, this is called kiting.

The issue I see with this playstyle comes with stealth mechanics. If the player in front has the enemies engaged and goes invisible to save themselves this can create an “aggro-dump” on players left behind. The player who went invisible does not feel the side-effects from dumping and only benefits from freeing up space for themselves. They may or may not choose to help and kill the aggro they created. This can leave the players in the back see a sudden rush of enemies all clumped together. Getting dumped on feels bad.

I think running ahead is fine in most cases as the mission typically has checkpoints to punish the player too far ahead. Experienced players know the limits of when they are over extending and will rotate back to regroup with the squad. (This is why we need emotes so we can wait in style FS)

The issue to me starts when running ahead is combined with stealth mechanics. I only like Double doubles with fries, sah.
