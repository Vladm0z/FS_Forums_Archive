Especially on the DLC classes the single mark weapons often have some really simple movesets.

Don’t get me wrong, I think most, if not all weapons should have a mark with easy to use moveset, that allows players to worry less about the weapon combo, and more about threats on the screen.

However, I also think that having weapons only with really basic movesets is boring. To me, the experience of using a complicated weapon, that you have to invest time into learning how to master, is so much more rewarding than being ‘given’ the weapons combo’s.

I was especially dissapointed by the skitariis transonic blades, you end up just spamming heavies or lights depending on the situation & build, its such a cool weapon with cool animations (the second dual wield weapon in the game) but also with the most basic gameplay..

The old version of the Mechanicus Power Sword was more fun than the new version, currently its still a fun weapon but I would prefer if another mark was added with a simple moveset, over changing the fun one.

I love the crowbar but its also basic, to me its a weapon that really deserves having an engaging moveset with diverse options, it just screams multipurpose tool xD
The shield break is a good add, but having an attack to stop muties, maybe even one to open up bulwarks, hidden behind specific attacks in its stances and with difficult combo’s would be a dream to play with!

DLC class single mark weapons that has simple movesets:
Arbites Shock Maul (light heavy repeating, the only difference is what you start with)
Transonic Blades (Dual wield, stance weapon, so much potential but ends up being heavy spam)
Crowbar (The horde combo is ok but the latching stance is dissapointing)
Bone Saw (not as bad as the ones above but also not wildly engaging)
These weapons really deserve another mark with weird movesets.

Boring movesets:
Light spam
Heavy spam
Light, heavy repeating
Using one specific attack no matter the situation.

Examples of weapons with fun movesets:
Mk7 Dclaw
Mechanicus Power Sword
Mk2 Relic Blade
