Yes I’m going to ask for buffs to the strongest class in the game.

Burst Limiter Override needs another improvement.

Problem 1: it has wildly different impacts based on how much ammo one has, if one is using a burst fire mode, and if one is using the talent Back Off!

Problem 2: literally weapon balance. God help anyone who doesn’t get how strong Rumbler is.

Problem 3: resource requirement. Range weapons are stronger than melee by default so I get it, but we’re borderline too strict with it and it’s competing with the strongest keystones in game on the strongest character in game.

Goal 1: don’t make it OP

Goal 2: don’t enforce the meta even more

Suggestion 1: Lucky Bullet procs free bullets for X seconds or until next reload. Balance out its usability across all of Ogryn’s guns without throwing out game balance harder by giving unnecessary buffs to Rumbler.

Suggestion 2: give BLO additional interactions with ults (think Empowered Psyonics on Psyker, but for ults instead of blitzes). We can’t have “free ammo on ult” because then Rumbler would gain sentience and uninstall gameplay from Darktide entirely, but it’d be nice if BLO did something extra because, again, it needs ammo to work.
