By lobby I mean the screen where all four players are side by side, and you can see each-other’s loadouts.

I understand why, with havoc the way it is currently, random people can’t queue into your mission (although some kind of system for that would be nice), but while it’s really rare, I have encountered a few people trying to grief other players by clicking “Accept” when a havoc mission pops up then Alt+F4ing or something to leave the team to lose an attempt on the mission.

I just joined a low havoc (23 I think) to help out, and someone did that, the game said he left the strike team almost instantly after accepting it. Normally people rejoin so I give them the benefit of the doubt that it was a crash, but he never did and we ended up defying the odds and three-manning the mission. It’s totally possible that his power went out or something like that, so I could be wrong to be fair.

But, while it’s not really common for the unintentional three man to happen, it would be nice to be able to wait and see if they return before starting the mission. Probably save a few headaches.
