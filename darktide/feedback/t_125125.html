Dizzy721Regular • 2026-08-24 13:18:53

I don’t think Vet needs a rework. There have been some loud voices making similar claims and I just don’t agree. One thing I will say is that the tree rework that happened last year was maybe too well optimized and the critique that there isn’t a ton of build variety is valid. Like if you’re pathing down the tree and you’re given the option to take an S-tier node and an A or B-tier node, you’re going to take the S-tier node nearly every time. And then almost all of the most potent nodes are connected to each other.

Designing the tree this way makes sense! The days of trees having balancing in the form of point taxes is mostly over and if done, it’s usually only to specific nodes and not entire build playstyles. The players for the most part wanted this, especially after the Arbites tree showed what was possible. But the rework we received for Vet had two major flaws. First, is that in optimizing out all the tax nodes and streamlining pathing, the standard/meta builds from pre-rework just got stronger. The best build got more points to play with to buff up whatever fits your playstyle the most. Sure the off-meta builds are also stronger but they’re still not the “optimal” way to play. Second, is that the new talents they’ve added since are either poop from a butt or super niche and haven’t affected the meta in anyway. So if you’re a Vet player you’ve been playing like a 90% similar build since the patch they added his keystones.

Yet it’s possible to run every single weapon available (though maybe not every combination of melee and ranged) to the Vet in multiple different builds and do off-meta weird things and have great results. This is ultimately why a rework is not necessary; a few tweaks would be welcome for sure but the class doesn’t need heavy touch. The class can unlock a bit more build variety with a different layout for the section above MMF. The +Ranged Suppression node is just bad, the 10% TDR node is also not very good. Replace these nodes with some interesting talents and make Kill Zone easier to grab, e.g. in the slot where Ranged Suppression is right now, or just remove those talents and redo the layout. Is anyone taking Deadshot and not also MMF? That could be moved down and something like +Clip Size moved up, for example.

Some other talents that are underpowered are:

  • Competitive Urge: Either increase the uptime (higher proc chance or longer duration) or make it stronger (20% impact is nothing and suppression who cares)

  • Covert Operative: 30% multiplicative damage increase is not bad but the condition to trigger it is. The two ways that come to mind to reliably trigger it are: 1) dive in with Infiltrate and -Threat subnode and 2) Tauntgryn. With 1, you could just take the easily accessible weakspot damage talents and just tap Infiltrate and shoot people in the front of their face for more damage. With 2) you’re shooting enemies that can’t fight back and not every taunt is going to pull enemies in such a way where you’re going to get the flanking damage. Boost the damage, add some secondary effect like enemies killed by a flanking shot give DR for 5 seconds or something, idk. Or buff ways to make flanking more viable without team cooperation that isn’t cringe -Threat node.

  • Leave No One Behind: I do not think I’ve ever picked this node. From a pathing perspective, it’s competing with Demo Team which is just insanely good or Born Leader which is good in many builds as well. Also Only In Death Does Duty End exists. Just yell at your teammate and they get up with Gold Toughness which is much better than extra 33% DR on top of what you already get on being revived. Perhaps if this gave the DR bonus on saving teammates from incapacitated states as well or if it gave some toughness back to the saved teammate, maybe it might be pickable. The talent makes a ton of sense for the “Squad Leader” Vet, but players at higher diffs aren’t eating disables enough for that part to matter or you’re in a tight coherency so like you get 20% movespeed to move 5 meters to push a dog off or remove a net. A completely new talent would also be acceptable here. Something like Comrade in Arms from FK though not as much DR.

On the basis of balance, there isn’t any need for a rework for Vet. If the motivation is shaking up the meta or breathing new life in the class, then sure that’s something you can want. As far as criteria go, that’s quite subjective. I don’t need that personally, I’ve been playing Vet since beta and will continue to play this class more than any other until I stop playing this game altogether.
