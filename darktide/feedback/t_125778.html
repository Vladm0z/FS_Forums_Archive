Disclaimers first:

  • You don’t have to agree with all of them. This is not a package that you have to take as a whole or reject as a whole. We can implement those that make sense to be added to the upcoming Update and leave the rest out for further tweaking and tuning.
  • I originally posted those in a Zealot Rework video before the Update Preview was released. Since then we took a different direction. Thus, in this thread I’m only posting the ones that still make sense (in case you want to see my original ideas here they are: https://youtu.be/Kf96XJh4w58)

Disdain

  • +5% damage on your next melee attack for each enemy hit. Elites and specials count as 3. Max 5 stacks.
    (the change is meant to make this talent useful even when you pick weapons that cannot cleave. Especially considering that the other options are either for a ranged weapon or for a backstabbing playstyle)

Anoint in Blood

  • +25% Base Ranged Damage to enemies within 12.5m. Reduced the further you are from the target.10% Base Ranged Damage to enemies at 30m and beyond.
    (basically make it an inverse version of Veteran’s Longshot)

The Voice of Terra

  • Weakspot Kills Restore 3% Toughness. +7.5% Toughness per second while shooting.
    (basically a bit of both worlds; ranged and melee. There’s a reason to take it if u play a finesse melee build, there’s a reason to take it if you want to play a ranged/melee hybrid)

Enemies Within - Enemies Without

  • Basically the same as now (normal enemies count as 1 and Monstrosities count as 5) BUT Elites should also count as 2.
    (incentivizes your to put yourself in danger in order to reach max stacks faster, since you have to approach Elites in melee range instead of dealing with them from distance)

Got your back

  • 7.5% toughness to you and 5% to your ally (Swap the rewards)
    (In general people like to take talents that help their team if they get an equal or better bonus for themselves - otherwise, less so. The idea is simple; I need to be alive to “have your back”)

Retaliatory defence

  • On block break restore 50% stamina. On toughness break restore 30% toughness. When Block or Toughness breaks all stun/stagger effects are removed. 12s shared cooldown.
    (my guess is, if you keep it only as a block break talent no-one is ever going to spend 1 point to take it because Zealot is not a block based class - you can disagree all you want with me but in a month from now I guarantee you the data will prove my case to be correct)

Shield of Contempt

  • On top of what it does, it should also increase coherency radius by +X%
    (reason; something for my team and something for myself - also synergizes with unkillable buff if you are running a group of multiple zealots)

The Master’s Retribution

  • Knock back the Attacker on taking a Melee Hit. 8s Cooldown. 20% Stamina Regen if you have avoided melee attacks for 8s.
    (again, knocking an enemy back AFTER taking damage is not that interesting to spend 1 talent point. The point is to never get hit in the first place. So, give me a small buff if I haven’t taken any damage for 8s)

Dance of death

  • Increases weapon swap speed by 30%. Also, spending 1 stamina or successfully dodging reduced Spread by 75% and Recoil by 50% for 4s.
    (you said it yourself, Zealot is the MELEE FANTASY class and then you go ahead and give him ranged talents. Let’s at least make them worth our point investment)

Invocation of Death

  • can be activated by all critical hits (not just melee)

Pious Cut-Throat

  • can be activated by Melee Backstab hits and all Weakspot hits (not just melee)

No Respite

  • Replenish 10% Stamina on Critical Hit (not just Melee Critical Hits). 1s Cooldown.
    (opens an interesting synergy with autoguns blessing " Stripped Down")

Unfaltering

  • On top of what it already does, Wielding Two Handed Weapons grants you +15% Dodge Distance.
    (Zealot 2H weapons have 0.86-0.91 dodge distance and sometimes feel super clanky - see Thunderhammer - Since 2h weapons have limited dodges anyway, we should at least be able to remove their dodge penalties too // BTW if you do the math, 0.86 * 1.15 = 0.989 and 0.91 * 1.15 = 1.046)

Behind the lines

  • Elite Melee Weakspot Kills suppress enemies within 8m. 5s Cooldown.
    (again the idea here is; go get in danger and get rewarded - also HEAVY + MELEE + BACKSTAB is VERY situational. Not worth investing 1 talent point to this)

Fury of the Faithful - QoL changes

  • While Dashing you are immune to crowd control, Ranged Attacks, Slowdown effects and Disablers.
  • While Dashing any Disablers caught in your way are instantly killed (but your FotF is consumed)
    (this means, while dashing you cannot be netter or grabbed by mutants)

On the Brink

  • 10% Corruption Resistance per Martyrdom Stack. At max stacks you become immune to tox gas health damage. You still take Toughness damage.

Inebriate’s poise

  • 3 stacks of Momentum on Successful Dodge. 1 stack of Momentum on Weakspot Hit (or kill?).

Eternal

  • Momentum duration is increased to 12s. +1 Dodge Count.

(these changes make Inexorable differentiate more from Martyrdom and solidify a mobility & precision playstyle)

Unrelenting Fury

  • Killing an Elite or Specialist within 5s of activating FotF doesn’t spend your “next melee attack” bonuses (+25% Damage, +100% Rending, Guaranteed Crit). Maximum once per use.
    (instead of bloating the game with multiple CD regen talents give us a fun modifier that allows us to smack 2 elites back to back if we kill the first one)

edit:
Btw, some of those changes may appear as “buffs” on face value.
However, the caveat is that you only have 30 talent points to spend so you can NEVER take all of them - the talent point limit prevents power creep.

What these changes do is that, while you would always skip some of these talents before, you now have more options to choose from and you must think more carefully what you’re going to sacrifice to get what you see here.
