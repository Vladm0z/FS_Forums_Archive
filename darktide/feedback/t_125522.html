aroth68 • 2026-09-13 16:40:47

Ogryn’s got the lowest base critical chance (2.5%) of all the classes.

The Ogryn’s melee weapons have the lowest Crit damage multipliers, ranging from a measly x1.12, to a mediocre x1.33 (the Cleavers’ Strikedown attacks).

In general, Vanguard and Relentless attacks all reside within the range of x1.12 to x1.20 ; most Strikedown attacks peak at about x1.30 to x1.35.

There’s no Crit synergy for melee Ogryns in the talent tree ; Massacre and Lucky Streak, due to their placement, are clearly meant to improve ranged crits, although they coincidentally apply in melee.

So, wouldn’t you agree that all the Crit-related blessings on the Ogryn’s melee weapons are just pointless noob traps ? For example, why should anyone pick Sucker Punch over Skullcrusher or Bromentum on shovels ? Even on Cleavers, it seems preferable to use Tenderiser over Bash.
