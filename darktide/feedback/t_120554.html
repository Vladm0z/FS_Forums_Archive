It’s most likely part of the problem. I’m not a game dev or audio engineer, but from a podcast about another game I heard an interesting game dev fact. If you play a lot of sounds at once, game engines can max out the available audio channels, which results in some sounds not playing at all. There are ways around it, prioritising specific sounds and mixing them together.

I’m sure there is much more to it, and back in time I provided a suggestion to CMs that we would like to see a dev blog on the issue of “Missing Audio Cues” and related to this challenges.
