Sulphuric_Glue • 2026-08-11 10:56:00

The most recent patch removed the ability for the Puncture blessing to work on different sources. This removed the “Bleedwave” Psyker build, which was achieved by using the Force Greatsword with Bolt Pistol, sending out a FGS wave and then quickly swapping to the Bolt Pistol to let it apply bleed to the targets hit by the wave.

I understand this was unintended behaviour and ultimately stems from a bug, but it was a fun and unique way to play Psyker that was not overpowered at all. If you were good at it, you could come close to matching an Inferno Psyker’s average damage output, but the tradeoff was that being good at it was tough (definitely harder than using the Inferno staff) because it was pretty clunky to use.

It was seriously not hurting anyone by being present in the game and the only reason it was removed is because Fatshark cares more about things working “correctly” than they do about actually making the game fun to play. This isn’t the first time a unique Psyker build has been removed either, because the Charged Strike Empowered Psionics interaction was removed - another build that was the result of an unintended interaction but wasn’t at all overpowered, because the fact you had to effectively give up a keystone to make it work is what made it balanced.

It wasn’t always this way. I remember when Fatshark deliberately left the deed bug in VT2 because having fun was considered more important.

I can’t lie, this attitude is starting to make me more apathetic about Darktide than it is making me frustrated. Why should I bother experimenting with unusual playstyles if Fatshark is going to remove them, meanwhile “intended” systems remain sorely unbalanced?
