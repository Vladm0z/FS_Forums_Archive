Because shotgunners, gunners, ragers, and maulers all have unique voice lines and audio cues that signal what they’re doing and when they’re about to perform an action.
Shotgunners make a loud cocking sound when they’re preparing to shoot.
Gunners have distinct voice lines and extremely loud guns.
Ragers call out loud taunts when they rush you.
Maulers rev their chainaxes as they approach and prepare to attack.

Plasma Gunners reuse the captain charge sound, and, especially in the event, is very hard to hear when there’s a horde, and their red glow when they charge a shot is obscured by the forced Ventilation Purge/Power Supply Interruption, making it exceedingly hard to tell when they’re about to attack.
