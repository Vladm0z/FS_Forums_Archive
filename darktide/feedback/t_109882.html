Arbites build for Warhammer 40k: Darktide, using the Achlys Mk VIII Combat Axe and Zarona Mk VI Combat Shotgun. Created by Ralendil.

March is really great (+15% move on ranged hit)
Priority endowment (+15% clip size) is great on this shotgun, as it gives you more one or two shells.

Target priority is also a must have. +15% on enemies with full health is a game changer.

So in other word, you reload it entirely when there is nothing attacking you. It permits to clean a room with a clip. You reload 1 or 2 shells in combat and fire. Then you can benefit of a +15% bonus from reloading,. a +15% on full health enemies and several damage bonus.
