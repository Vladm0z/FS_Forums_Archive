Dear Mr. Martin Wahlund.

I fully acknowledge so you likely will never see this, and even if you do, it likely won’t change anything. I still am writing this however, because in my mind, someone should give you the PoV of an Everpresent. Those that do make up the valleys between the peaks on the monetization graph.

If we picture the graph, and divide the customer base into:

-Peakers: those that only play the game after a major update and only for a brief time at once.

-Everpresents: those that stick around for years and make up the valleys in the graph.

What your Company currently is doing with the “Quarterly Sales Revenue”-chasing, is alienating the core-fan-base, the Everpresents.

As your Lead-Designer and Community Managers can tell you, the gameplay-loop is broken in high difficulty (Auric Maelstrom) due to:

-systemic Power-creep as a whole

-DLC classes with overtuned stats, and overtuned kits

-Paid-Automation (i.e. the Skitarii Servitor Skull)

If the game at its most intense/highest difficulty looses its difficulty, aswell as the necessity for team-work in a co-op game, the incentive to be an Everpresent evaporates.

What this will cause over the long term, is so the number of the Everpresents keeps shrinking, the valleys become lower on the graph. However, the Everpresents are also around during the peaks, thus the entire average of the graph itself keeps shrinking over time.

Your Company will eventually run into the issue, where you will be forced to have more peaks during the same time-interval, further alienating the last remaining everpresents.

Your Company also is accumulating a bad rep in the process for players with the behaviour-type Everpresent.

i.e.: Why would someone that likes to enjoy one game for years on end, i.e. a nerd, loyal fan, or even just a content creator, start with your next game, and the game after, when the inevitable outcome is already well known before the game even exists? Why should I for example even buy Darktide2 in the future, when I already know so the game is running on a doom-timer.

This further accelerates the inevitable solution of having even more Peaks in the same time-frame. It is a self-induced downward spiral.

In the worst case scenario, as an extreme extrapolation into the future, you eventually will be left with a shallow game from the start, that can only survive due to highly aggressive Monetization, but this can and maybe will also backfire, because Peakers will not buy a new class every Month. Eventually it will come across as a cheap money-grab.

Thus, I plead to you Mr. Wahlund, to think about the PoV of someone like myself. We may not be those that fund your corp in the Peaks, but we would like to stick around and fund your corp for years to come. However, not if the current path of destroying the game for Quarterly Sales Revenues continues. This alienates us.

I also do want to emphasize, so I am writing this specific letter to you Mr. Wahlund, precisely because I think your Community Managers, Lead Designer, and all the other eployees are busy with their day-to-day business tasks of maintaining the game and the PR-stuff. They are managing the immediate fires, thus leaving the long-term economics on your desk.

Thus while I am fully aware so this letter will likely not change anything, you Mr. Wahlund at least should be made aware of it, so that it can be taken into consideration for future decisions.

Most Sincerely

a tryhard by heart
