The pinch point after the tree needs a redesign. Nothing major but it’s so stupid and has seen many missions failed where it’s really no fault of the players.

The section before you drop down (where 2 of the 3 bridges collapses) is just the width of 1 person, with no handrails on much of it and somewhat unbelievably has 2 enemy spawn doors just at the pinch before you drop down.

What happens is:

  • the area gets instantly flooded with enemies very fast
  • Players get stuck on the tiny gantry sandwiched in a horde
  • Every single playthough a netter is guaranteed to spawn behind the horde and shoots through the incredible mass and the player has nowhere to dodge…either off a ledge or not at all.
  • Flamers and bombers then carpet the area immediately from miles away and enemies are seemingly invulnerable to their fire for some reason.
  • Mission fails unless you have an ogryn to push through the crown on the 0.7m wide handrailess walkway

It’s just such a frustrating and unfair bit of the map after you’ve basically done the whole mission. Failing on that bit feels really really nasty.

My suggestion is simply to deactivate those 2 enemy doors.

Increase the spawns in the following section if you want but don’t shoot the players with trappers and flamers through a dense horde on a miniscule walkway at the very end of the mission. It’s really unsporting. Allow the players at least a tiny bit of time to drop down away from that nasty bottleneck.

If players were to retreat to defeat the horde first then it doesn’t really work as a strategy as the spawns just constantly increase from then on.

Allow the players into an area that actually permits combat.
