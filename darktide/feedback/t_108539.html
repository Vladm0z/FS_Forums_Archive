Hello there!

So I’ve taken a look at the icons in Darktide and I’d like some sort of addition and/or changes to them.

My main issue was from playing with a team that didn’t pick up an Ammo Crate when I pinged it, and then did not resupply when I placed it on the ground as not to waste it. I was carrying a Scripture at the time so I didn’t want to drop that.

So my idea was to make it more obvious that the Ammo Pack was on the ground, differentiating it from the box you can pick up.
While looking at all the icons I could find I saw that there were both frames and icons that you changed in order to show what was what.
That made me think about consistency, as just introducing something isn’t always a good thing.

The idea so far is to make changes to a number of pick ups.
Small ammo pick up
“Medium” ammo pick up (the bags).
Ammo Crate, placed Ammo Crate
*Medical Crate, placed Medical Crate

Possibly a seperate icon for Grimoires, but not really needed.
