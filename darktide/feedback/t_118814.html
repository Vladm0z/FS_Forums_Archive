Hammer_hold • 2026-02-22 23:29:04

Isn’t it funny that when it comes to hearing visceral screams of enemies dying, running at or fighting with you, Vermintide 2, a 7-year-old game, achieves this better than Darktide, a 3-year-old game, does? The Northlanders feels so much threatening because of the immaculate voice acting and when you hear a horde of them approaching you can feel the intensity in each voiceline.

Sometimes when I listen to Darktide’s enemy voicelines, I feel as if we’ve been robbed of something that could be a much more intense game, based on the sound design and voice acting alone. FatShark could have gone very hard on the voice acting of the enemies, but they didn’t.

A horde of groaners should be much louder, their steps making a loud rumbling sound, but not as loud as their screams and wails of agony should be. They are literaly trapped in their own bodies suffering and it should be communicated even more intensly than it is now. When they are lit on fire and burn to death, you should very clearly hear, and see in their death animation, just in how much pain they are. The cultist of the Admonition should be much louder and let out visceral screams of rage that blasphemers who deny their truth have dared to enter their sacred grounds. Their machine guns should be much louder and almost deafening when they shoot. Melee Scabs like Maulers and Ragers should also have much more intense screaming in their voicelines when they charge at you.

All of this to say that Darktide had the potential of a very intense game where every enemy feels like a threat based on sound design alone. A single groaner should be heard from very far away, it’s scream reverbing in the tunnels and corridors of the grimdark Hive City. And an entire horde of enemies would create a cacophony of rage, pain, suffering and hatred.

Shame we’ll never see that vision of Darktide come to life…
