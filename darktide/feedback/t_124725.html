It’s getting silly.

There are more people leaving the moment they go down or dead than people willing to wait for a res or while being hogtied. People also like leaving when they think the mission is lost, and then other players that use quickjoin load into a mission lost screen. They save 2 minutes of their time, just to screw over 3 minutes of someone else’s. These are my experiences on Auric.

It’s clear most of the community can’t handle this tiny bit of freedom and it’s hurting the game, so I would propose that if you disconnect or leave, for 5 minutes you can only rejoin the mission you left (and you would still be down/hogtied when doing so). Game will keep a spot open for 5 minutes. Left and the mission ended? Can’t join a new one for the remaining time.

Punishing players is in general not a fun thing to do, but I genuinely think this is good for the health of the game.

edit: I want to add that I do love the game and the community in general, otherwise I wouldn’t play this game as much as I do. Would just love it more if this was addressed. Also I use “you” a lot in the post, but I realize people here reading this (on the Fatshark forum) are probably not the ones quitting early.

edit2: Seems to be a bit of a controversial topic, but I think @Spagett has the best solution to this.

Spagett:

I think it’s important to recognize here that no one (reasonable) is saying that you shouldn’t be allowed to leave games whenever you want or for whatever reason you want. No one has suggested removing the “leave game” button. People are suggesting that there be a consequence for doing so. Freedom does not mean freedom from consequence. You have the freedom to do a lot of things in life that you still don’t do, or do sparingly, because of the consequences they entail.

Personally, I would focus any penalty specifically on players that leave games when they are downed. At least in my experience that constitutes the majority of leavers already, and I think there can be legitimate reasons for wanting to leave games when you are not downed, such as not wanting to play with people that use obnoxious meta builds or with rushers. Focusing on players that only leave when in a downed state would pretty much only capture those that are leaving because they don’t want to wait to be picked up, or people that rage quit. These are the antisocial, unsportsmanlike players that other players deserve some modicum of protection from, at least on Auric difficulty and above.

Furthermore, I would suggest that any theoretical penalty should be preceded by a warning, which would appear if someone has left more than a certain number of games in a row, or if they have left more than a certain number of games within a certain timeframe. The goal should be to penalize the worst offenders, people who serially leave games whenever they get downed, not just anyone who leaves a game once.

@FatsharkQuickpaw consider this please!
