The biggest issues that people are having with expeditions right now is how repetitive it is right now, so here are some ideas to help break up the monotone a bit.

First, two new enemies. Considering the open environment of expeditions, this would be the perfect chance to reintroduce the radio operator enemy that was present in the live event a while back. His abilities? He would either be able to call for reinforcements or call in some sort of artillery/air strike if you don’t kill him fast enough.
The other enemy that I can think of is some sort of variant of VT2s sackrat. Since extra supplies are only available at deadsider sanctuaries at the moment, this would provide something of relief for players in field, if you can kill him before runs away of course. What he drops would be randomized.

Second idea is to swap out the mid and/or end zones of each expedition run with either some sort of risk-reward puzzle/survival event, or with a boss fight. Not sure what kind of puzzles we could have, but on the boss fight, I think we can go for something extra. Since we are out in a open environment, it is only natural that we get bosses that suit this. Who’s up for fighting a traitor sentinel or valkery?
