I gave the MPS an earnest shot and I have to say it’s probably my new favorite Skitarii weapon. If it were on any other human class it would probably be one of the best weapons in the game. L1/L2 pokes are really powerful and the CC isn’t actually that bad once you get into the combo.

The problem is getting there in the first place. You HAVE to push attack to get into the CC chains or do three whole light attacks. It makes it really awkward to get into the swing of things.

I suggest removing L3 from the light chain, but it can stay after a push (not push attack). L1/L2 can stay but three lights is far too long to get into a crowd control chain, especially in a game where you’re often going to be changing target between elites and trash. Block canceling is a pretty big part of optimal MPS gameplay and that means occasionally taking a block while you’re in the middle of a cancelor not being able to quickly go into the horde clear while you’re surrounded, and it’s really holding it back from feeling great.
