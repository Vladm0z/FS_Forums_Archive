KayKay1212 • 2025-12-16 08:51:30

Darktide is loosely based on the Dark Heresy TTRPG game, which is about playing as an Inquisitor’s freshly-recruited Acolytes and investigating Chaos and other heresies.

Inquisitors have near uncontested power in the Imperium, and can recognize and recruit Acolytes from basically any walk of life, if it seems like the recruit would have useful skills to be the Inquisitor’s underling. That includes criminals like Hive Scum, if they’re skilled enough killers or have advanced street smarts which would make them useful investigators that can blend in. Scum is one of the default player classes in Dark Heresy:

Dark Heresy of the Secundus Sector Wiki

Dark Heresy Classes

Basically a catch-all term for criminals like gangers, bounty hunters, convicts, dashing rogues, basically anyone who’s been a criminal outsider who’s caught the eye of an Inquisitor as being a potentially useful operative, plus Inquisitors have the power to forcefully recruit anyone under pain of death upon refusal.

So yes, the Hive Scum are currently being used by Inquisitor Grendyl/Interrogator Rannick as a means to an end in the larger Tertium conflict right now and paying them, but the best of the best will probably be recruited as full-scale Acolytes once the conflict is over.
