Hey. just a suggestion to change the current penance to something less frustrating.

-Kill 150 specialists or elites by backstabbing them while using shroudfield-

This is very discouraging to grind, since by the time you position yourself behind the enemy, someone on your team has already nuked them from orbit, the only viable way is to run far ahead from your team and play selfishly, something that I hate doing in a coop game.

Furthermore, there are not a lot of weapons that can one shot elites and some specials, making the weapon choice very restrictive (dueling sword being the best weapon to do this, but I refuse to use that overpowered crutch anymore).

So my suggestion is, give a 5-10 secs window to kill an elite/special, after you have exited shroudfield, this could remedy situations where the target you were about to kill, gets wasted by someone else, and let us use more weapons that can’t one shot everything.
